Your existing taxonomy has 46 subject categories, but not every new niche requires a new subject category.

I recommend introducing these additional subject categories only where their scope is distinct enough:

| Proposed subject category          | Why                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Digital Business & Creator Economy | SaaS, monetization, creator business models             |
| Fitness & Exercise Science         | Training, physical performance, exercise                |
| Modern World History               | Major civilizations, wars and modern historical periods |
| Sports & Competitions              | Team sports, leagues, competitive disciplines           |
| Design & Creative Technology       | UI/UX, design systems, graphic and motion design        |
| Civic Society & Public Policy      | Democracy, elections, rights and public institutions    |

This would bring the category count from 46 to up to 52, subject to taxonomy approval.

More importantly, I recommend that each niche eventually have these attributes: canonical ID, subject category, parent niche, related niches, target audience, geographic scope, language, content format, primary editorial angle, audience demand, monetization suitability, research complexity, evidence requirements, production complexity and editorial status.
