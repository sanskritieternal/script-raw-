## Category decision

Retain the 46 current categories. No automatic expansion to 52. Digital business uses Commerce_Industry; major sports use Sports_Adventure; design/media use Visual_Arts_Crafts, Film_Production or Literature_Performance. Ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia belong to Ancient_Civilizations, not Modern World History. Further category changes require a separate coverage/routing case.
