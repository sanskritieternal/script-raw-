# Supporting characters

## Purpose and applicability

Record secondary cast and recurring background groups for this episode. An episode with no supporting cast may use a justified Not applicable entry. Keep every individual in exactly one of the main/supporting profile files.

## Character profiles

For each unique character ID record name/aliases, narrative purpose, relationship to the central cast, background, personality, introduction, recurring or one-time use and relevant scene numbers. Distinguish named individuals from group IDs such as GROUP_001. Group descriptions must not erase individual variation or rely on stereotypes.

## Appearance and voice

Record distinguishing appearance, gestures, voice and pronunciation, scaled to the character's story importance. Recurring visible characters need stable identity references and appropriate costume variants. Link approved designs rather than copying conflicting descriptions from another document.

## Evidence and depiction

Separate sourced statements, reconstruction choices and fiction. Link historical claims to Phase 1 evidence; invented crowd members or composite characters must be identified as such. Record relevant permissions and limitations honestly.

## Scene and reference links

Map actual appearances when scenes exist and identify pending mappings during planning. Link the [main cast](MAIN_CHARACTERS.md), [appearance lock](CHARACTER_APPEARANCE_LOCK.md), [relationships](RELATIONSHIPS_TIMELINE.md) and [voice guide](05_Voiceover_Guide.md). Do not create a second profile for aliases of the same person.

## Full supporting-entry worksheet

Give each relevant entity its own subsection, even when a cast table provides navigation. Explain narrative function, relationship to the central cast, evidence, actual scene mentions/appearances and whether it is depicted, voiced, composite or mentioned only. For depicted recurring characters, include distinguishing design, costume/props, expression/gesture, voice and reference/version requirements. For mention-only entities, explicitly state which visual/voice choices are intentionally not needed and how to prevent accidental cast expansion.

An entry should answer what this entity contributes to the episode and what the production must preserve. A bare name and role does not answer those questions. Do not invent a body, accent, psychology, age or historical claim to fill an inapplicable field. Keep aliases connected to the same authoritative profile.

## Required editorial depth

Before filling this document, read the shared source `CORE/PIPELINE/03_Phase2_PRODUCTION_BIBLE/PRODUCTION_DETAIL_STANDARD.md`. Write actual episode decisions, individual entries, representative scene use, examples, exceptions and acceptance checks relevant to this file. Do not submit a short summary or a list of instructions as completed production content. Keep genuinely inapplicable fields explicitly justified; do not invent factual detail, assets or approvals to increase length. Structural validation alone does not verify editorial depth.
