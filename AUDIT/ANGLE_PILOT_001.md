# Niche 001 angle pilot: AI + Technology Explainers

**Status:** Pilot for user review. This pilot curated only niche 001. Niche 004 was curated later; the remaining 498 retain their 15 base angles. At the time of this pilot the total was 7,505 angle files; niche 004 was curated later, bringing the current total to 7,550.

## Editorial boundary

Niche 001 explains AI concepts, systems and consequences for a general audience. Step-by-step tool tutorials belong primarily to niche 007; primary AI research to niche 337; developer implementation to niche 338; robotics and drones to their dedicated niches. A niche 001 episode may discuss those subjects when the story is about broader AI understanding.

## What changed

The original 15 angle slugs remain valid so existing references do not break. Their Q3 values now identify distinct AI-specific directions. Five new slugs were added: `data_provenance`, `evaluation_benchmarks`, `human_ai_collaboration`, `safety_tradeoffs`, and `infrastructure_compute`. Each angle file states a focused editorial scope and an example researchable question. The example is a starting point, not a required episode title or a verified claim.

The source is `REGISTRY/Niches/001_ai_technology_explainers/ANGLE_PLAN.json`. `populate_niches.py` and `validate_repository.py` read that plan for niche 001 while the master registry remains the base source for the other 499. See the [20-angle index](../REGISTRY/Niches/001_ai_technology_explainers/Angles/ALL_TOPICS_BY_NICHE.md).

## Review points before extending the method

1. Are the 20 directions useful and sufficiently distinct for this niche?
2. Does the boundary with AI tools, AI research and developer AI feel right?
3. Should future niche plans refine existing slugs, add new ones, or both?
