# Angle editorial review: first pass

## Scope and outcome

The companion [CSV](./ANGLE_EDITORIAL_REVIEW.csv) inventories every `ANGLE_PLAN.json`: 530 plans and 16,616 angles. It records plan status and simple wording signals. There are now 528 plans with reviewed question designs and two pilots, 001 AI and 004 Sanatan. The [expansion review](EXPANSION_EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md) records the source entry points, neighboring-niche boundaries and later refinements for IDs 501–530. Plan status does not establish verified episode facts.

## Completion verification

The later [scope review](ANGLE_SCOPE_REVIEW.md) rewrote 600 expansion scopes and completed evidence and limit guidance for the 80 pilot angles. Scope wording revisions retain the existing plan statuses. All angle files now present their focus, coverage, evidence and limits together, and new Phase 0 configurations snapshot that guidance.

The expanded inventory contains 528 `editorial_question_reviewed` plans and two pilots. All 16,616 angles have questions, scopes, evidence directions and boundaries; 16,536 belong to plans with reviewed question designs. The [completion checker](../verify_editorial_completion.py) now checks all registered niches by default, including source and overlap records for IDs 501–530. `--original-500` retains the original milestone check. A concrete episode still needs applicable local records, version checks, permissions and verified research claims.

## How to use the inventory

`template_scopes` counts angle scopes with repeated generator wording. It is a prioritization signal, not a defect count: some repeated wording is acceptable, but a niche with many such scopes deserves a manual fit check. `generic_questions` counts question text that repeats the registry display title in a generic phrase such as â€œin Banking.â€ `priority_score` adds 100 for a sensitive subject category, then adds the two wording counts; it is zero for already reviewed plans. These mechanical signals do not judge source quality, audience value, or factual accuracy. Sort the CSV by `priority_score` and inspect niche fit before editing. The next unreviewed high-priority group includes other health, government, and religious subjects. Their ranking does not imply all have equal editorial risk.

A profile-based generation pilot for niches 097 and 098 produced mismatched and ungrammatical question pairings. Its output was not applied. These two plans were revised directly, and future scaling methods must pass the same per-question fit check before any status is upgraded.

Review one plan at a time: preserve stable slugs, replace weak titles/questions/scopes with subject-specific ones, identify plausible primary source types, and keep boundaries suited to the niche. Then run:

```powershell
python populate_niches.py --id <ID> --sync-content
python populate_niches.py --id <ID> --verify-content
python validate_repository.py
python test_contract.py
python review_angle_plans.py
```

For a real episode, select a bounded case and verify its sources in Phase 1. A reviewed angle is a question route, not a claim of factual or source approval.
