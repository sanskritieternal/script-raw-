# Facts Documented

| Claim | Supported fact | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| C001 | The speed of light in vacuum has the exact SI value 299,792,458 m/s. | Say “in vacuum” and “in SI”; avoid implying the same speed in every medium. |
| C002 | The SI metre is defined using the fixed numerical value of the speed of light in vacuum, with the second defined separately. | The short narration may simplify the formal definition, but must not imply an independent measurement gives the exact value. |

Both claims are supported by NIST and the BIPM SI Brochure, with exact locations in `Claim_Ledger.md`.
