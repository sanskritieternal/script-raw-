# Research Summary — Physics pilot

This 16-second explainer answers why the SI speed of light in vacuum has an exact numerical value. The first scene states the value; the second connects it to the definition of the metre. Scope is limited to SI definitions, with no claim about light in materials or the history of measurements. The primary sources are NIST's SI definitions page and the BIPM SI Brochure, reviewed on 2026-10-15. Claims C001 and C002 are recorded in `Claim_Ledger.md`.
