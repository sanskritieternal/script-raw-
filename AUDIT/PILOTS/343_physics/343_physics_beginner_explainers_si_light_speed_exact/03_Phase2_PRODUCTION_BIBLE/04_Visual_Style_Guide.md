# Visual Style Guide

Locked Q15: Scientific Cinematic Explainer. Use a dark neutral field, a clear light pulse moving through empty space, and legible white numeric text. Show `299,792,458 m/s in vacuum` with units and qualifier together. Scene 2 uses a simple metre ruler and second clock; avoid imagery that suggests light has this exact speed in air or glass. The visuals are directions only; no asset is claimed to exist.
