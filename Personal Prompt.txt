### Setting Durations (10m, 20m, 30m)
Under our flexible duration standard, set `Duration` to:
- **10 Minutes:** `Duration: 600` (75 scenes @ 8s fixed)
- **20 Minutes:** `Duration: 1200` (150 scenes @ 8s fixed)
- **30 Minutes:** `Duration: 1800` (225 scenes @ 8s fixed)

### What the Agent Does Autonomously When You Send This:
1. **Resolves the Niche & Angle:** Matches the topic to the correct niche folder in `REGISTRY/Niches/` and `topics/`.
2. **Phase 1 (Research):** Builds the historical/technical research brief and facts matrix.
3. **Phase 2 (Script):** Writes the full scene-by-scene voiceover script adhering to 8-second timing constraints.
4. **Phase 3 (Visual & Audio Prompts):** Generates Midjourney/Runway image prompts, camera moves, and sound design.
5. **Phase 4 (Packaging):** Generates YouTube title, description, tags, chapters, and thumbnail concept.
6. **Phase 5 (Audit):** Runs the 70-point validation checklist and saves everything into `OUTPUT/<niche_id>_<slug>/<episode_slug>/`.
