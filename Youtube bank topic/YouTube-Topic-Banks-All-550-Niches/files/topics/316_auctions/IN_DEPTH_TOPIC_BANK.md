### Auction mechanisms

**Research standard:** Use current official terms, filings or macroeconomic data; state jurisdiction, date, fees, taxes, risk, units and assumptions. Use transparent illustrative calculations rather than personal recommendations or return promises.

**Source types to locate:** regulator or official statistical publications; actual contracts, fee schedules and audited filings; original economic studies and disclosed calculation inputs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
