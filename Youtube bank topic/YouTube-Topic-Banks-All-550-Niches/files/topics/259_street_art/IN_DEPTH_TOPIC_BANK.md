## Detailed proposals

### Urban art forms

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-259-001 — Graffiti writing histories

**Video question:** Graffiti writing histories: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Street Art; branch: Urban art forms. Address Graffiti writing histories through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Graffiti writing histories in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
- Analyze institutions, material conditions and overlooked participants rather than attributing everything to a single figure.
- Separate documented conclusions, interpretive debates and gaps that a reconstruction cannot resolve.

### Public-space politics

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Documentation

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-259-007 — Ephemeral art archives

**Video question:** Ephemeral art archives: which creative choices change what the audience understands?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Street Art; branch: Documentation. Address Ephemeral art archives through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Identify the audience, format and communication problem for Ephemeral art archives.
- Annotate a specific scene, design or draft to show how the creative choice works.
- Compare a revised version and explain what information, feeling or interpretation changes.
