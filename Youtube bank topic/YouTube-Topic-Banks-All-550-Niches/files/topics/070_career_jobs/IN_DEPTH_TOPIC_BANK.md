## 01. Mapping occupations and requirements

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-070-013 | How can someone distinguish a job title from the work actually performed? | Compare consented worker accounts, official occupational descriptions and real role tasks; separate employer-specific duties from assumptions attached to a fashionable title. |
| BANK-070-014 | What does a job advertisement reveal and omit about an ordinary workday? | Inspect responsibilities, reporting lines, schedule, performance measures and missing information across dated public ads; avoid inferring workplace culture from slogans alone. |
| BANK-070-015 | How can an occupational profile separate entry routes from the paths of already successful workers? | Compare formal requirements, apprenticeships, portfolios and career histories with selection bias explicit; do not present one prominent person's route as typical. |
| BANK-070-016 | Why should career exploration include undesirable tasks as well as attractive outcomes? | Map repetitive work, constraints, customer contact and responsibility through multiple consented accounts; avoid treating prestige or salary as a complete fit assessment. |

## 02. Skills, learning and credentials

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-070-017 | How can a skill gap be identified from actual job tasks rather than a long course catalogue? | Translate a bounded role into demonstrable tasks, prerequisite knowledge and evidence; distinguish mandatory requirements from preferences in current official role information. |
| BANK-070-018 | What makes a portfolio item useful evidence of a candidate's contribution? | Show problem, constraints, personal work, results and rights-cleared artifacts in an original example; disclose team contributions and exclude confidential employer material. |
| BANK-070-019 | When does a credential represent a legal requirement rather than a hiring preference? | Consult the regulator, awarding body and employer requirements for a named occupation and jurisdiction; avoid assuming international recognition or permanent validity. |
| BANK-070-020 | How can an apprenticeship be compared with a classroom-only training route? | Inspect supervised work, assessment, wages or fees, recognized outcomes and completion evidence; verify dated official terms rather than guaranteeing employment. |

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-070-021 | How can a resume make achievements assessable without inventing numerical impact? | Revise an original example to state work, context and attributable outcomes; keep estimates labeled and avoid copying fabricated accomplishments into an application. |
| BANK-070-022 | What changes when an application must be readable by both people and software? | Compare clear headings, accessible text and faithful role keywords with official submission requirements; exclude keyword stuffing and promises of beating every screening system. |
| BANK-070-023 | How can a cover letter explain a career transition with evidence? | Connect transferable tasks, relevant learning and a specific role using an invented candidate; avoid generic passion statements or concealing missing mandatory qualifications. |
| BANK-070-024 | What can a job-search record reveal beyond the number of applications sent? | Track role fit, referral source, stage, feedback and follow-up with privacy controls; avoid treating response rates from a small sample as a verdict on personal worth. |

| BANK-070-038 | What evidence helps assess whether an occupation's reported growth translates into accessible jobs? | Compare official projections, openings, region, entry barriers and experience requirements; distinguish forecasts from vacancies currently available to a particular candidate. |
| BANK-070-039 | How can a career break be explained without disclosing private health or family details? | Develop honest role-relevant examples of readiness and retained skills; check local rights and avoid implying an applicant must reveal sensitive circumstances. |
| BANK-070-040 | Why can automation forecasts tell different stories about the same occupation? | Compare task-based measures, adoption assumptions, time horizons and official labor data; separate technical capability from job-loss predictions. |
| BANK-070-041 | How should a redundancy or layoff episode distinguish an announcement from workers' actual outcomes? | Trace official notices, timelines, support and consented accounts for a defined case; avoid rumors and do not infer an individual's prospects from company headlines. |
