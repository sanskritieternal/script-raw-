## 01. Investigating a natural mystery

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-030-013 | What turns an unusual natural event into a testable question? | Define observations, conditions, measurement and alternative explanations rather than using unexplained as evidence for a preferred cause. |
| BANK-030-014 | How can a striking video misrepresent the frequency of a natural phenomenon? | Examine selection, location, timing and repeated events instead of inferring rarity from one viral clip. |
| BANK-030-015 | Why is a plausible physical explanation insufficient without evidence from the actual site? | Compare general mechanism, local conditions and observations that could distinguish alternatives. |
| BANK-030-016 | What should a field investigation record before trying to solve a mystery? | Preserve time, location, instruments, weather, context and uncertainty without disturbing the relevant evidence. |

## 07. Famous mystery narratives

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-030-037 | What does a fair investigation of Bermuda Triangle incidents need to count? | Define boundaries, time, traffic exposure, inclusion criteria and known causes rather than collecting only striking disappearances. |
| BANK-030-038 | How can an old natural-disaster account combine observation and later embellishment? | Trace contemporary witnesses, repeated claims and changing interpretations with a clear chronology. |
| BANK-030-039 | Why do natural alignments attract intentional-design claims? | Compare pattern selection, chance, geology and independent evidence of human intervention. |
| BANK-030-040 | What would justify saying an unusual event remains unexplained? | State which observations are reliable, which mechanisms were tested and which missing data prevent resolution. |

## 08. Visual explanation and correction

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-030-041 | How can an animation explain a natural mechanism without pretending to reproduce the exact event? | Label illustrative parameters, simplify transparently and distinguish observed data from reconstructed motion. |
| BANK-030-042 | What should change when a spectacular natural-mystery claim is debunked? | Correct the central claim, explain the evidence and update titles or images that still imply the false version. |
| BANK-030-043 | How can a field episode include local knowledge without treating all testimony as measurement? | Attribute accounts respectfully and compare them with recorded observations and uncertainty. |
| BANK-030-044 | Why should a science channel preserve wonder after offering an ordinary explanation? | Show the actual mechanism's complexity and evidence rather than treating understanding as the end of curiosity. |
