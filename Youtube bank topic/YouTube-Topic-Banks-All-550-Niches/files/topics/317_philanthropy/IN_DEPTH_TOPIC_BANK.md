## Detailed proposals

### Giving systems

**Research standard:** Use current official terms, filings or macroeconomic data; state jurisdiction, date, fees, taxes, risk, units and assumptions. Use transparent illustrative calculations rather than personal recommendations or return promises.

**Source types to locate:** regulator or official statistical publications; actual contracts, fee schedules and audited filings; original economic studies and disclosed calculation inputs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
