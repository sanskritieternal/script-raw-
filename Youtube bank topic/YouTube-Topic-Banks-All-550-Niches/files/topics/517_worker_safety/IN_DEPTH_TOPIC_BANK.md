## Detailed proposals

### Workplace hazards

**Research standard:** Inspect current professional guidance and original studies for the exact population, intervention or measurement. Report comparators, absolute outcomes, uncertainty, harms and limits. Do not diagnose, prescribe or imply that education replaces professional care.

**Source types to locate:** current official clinical or public-health guidance; original trials, cohort studies or diagnostic-validation reports; study registrations, protocols and adverse-event records. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Safety systems

**Research standard:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, tolerances, failure conditions and the limits of the example. Do not provide hazardous construction or unauthorized access procedures.

**Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-517-004 — Hierarchy of controls

**Video question:** Hierarchy of controls: how does it work, and where do its design assumptions fail?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Worker Safety; branch: Safety systems. Address Hierarchy of controls through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the components and operating conditions behind Hierarchy of controls.
- Trace a worked example using a diagram, calculation or observed system; state all numerical assumptions.
- Compare an alternative design against the same requirements; include cost, reliability and accessibility where relevant.
- Examine a failure mode, measurement error or edge case and show what evidence would change the explanation.

### Public accountability

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
