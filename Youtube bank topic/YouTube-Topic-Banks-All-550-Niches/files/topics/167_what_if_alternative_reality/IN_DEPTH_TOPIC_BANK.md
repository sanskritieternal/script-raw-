## Detailed proposals

### Physical counterfactuals

**Research standard:** Declare a conditional scenario, not a prediction. Anchor present-day capabilities in evidence, make physical and institutional assumptions explicit, and trace second-order consequences and failure conditions.

**Source types to locate:** current original research on the enabling technologies; physical and economic constraints with explicit inputs; published scenario methods and relevant legal frameworks. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-167-001 — Earth with a different axial tilt

**Video question:** Earth with a different axial tilt: what follows if the stated assumptions hold?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: What If? / Alternative Reality; branch: Physical counterfactuals. Address Earth with a different axial tilt through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- List the assumptions that make Earth with a different axial tilt possible and distinguish demonstrated capability from speculation.
- Build a resource, energy, time or governance budget appropriate to the scenario.
- Follow consequences for daily life, institutions and unequal access rather than only depicting impressive technology.
- Change a major assumption and show how the scenario branches; identify observable evidence that would alter confidence.

### Method

**Research standard:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
