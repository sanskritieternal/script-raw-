## Detailed proposals

### Hospital operations

**Research standard:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, tolerances, failure conditions and the limits of the example. Do not provide hazardous construction or unauthorized access procedures.

**Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-181-001 — Emergency department triage systems

**Video question:** Emergency department triage systems: how does it work, and where do its design assumptions fail?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Hospital Stories; branch: Hospital operations. Address Emergency department triage systems through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the components and operating conditions behind Emergency department triage systems.
- Trace a worked example using a diagram, calculation or observed system; state all numerical assumptions.
- Compare an alternative design against the same requirements; include cost, reliability and accessibility where relevant.

### Care teams

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-181-004 — Nursing handovers

**Video question:** Nursing handovers: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Hospital Stories; branch: Care teams. Address Nursing handovers through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Nursing handovers and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.

### Ethical storytelling

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-181-007 — Patient consent during illness

**Video question:** Patient consent during illness: how do the rules and institutions work in a named jurisdiction?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Hospital Stories; branch: Ethical storytelling. Address Patient consent during illness through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.
