## Detailed proposals

### Sacred landscapes

**Research standard:** Identify the text, edition, translator, commentary, community and ritual context as applicable. Attribute theological and spiritual claims, preserve regional variation and respect public versus restricted knowledge.

**Source types to locate:** primary texts in traceable editions; authorized public teaching and ritual descriptions; consented community accounts and critical historical studies. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Mapping belief

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-240-005 — Textual geography versus modern locations

**Video question:** Textual geography versus modern locations: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Sacred Geography; branch: Mapping belief. Address Textual geography versus modern locations through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

### Living landscapes

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-240-008 — Sacred groves

**Video question:** Sacred groves: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Sacred Geography; branch: Living landscapes. Address Sacred groves through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Sacred groves and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.
- Return to participants for context and consent checks; distinguish their views from the narrator's interpretation.
