## Detailed proposals

### Horror structures

**Research standard:** Create original fiction with explicit world rules, character goals and causal continuity. Label invented events and separate inspiration from claims about actual cultures or living people.

**Source types to locate:** original story notes and world rules; permission-cleared reference material; reader feedback and a continuity audit. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-375-001 — Isolation with limited information

**Video question:** How could a story about Isolation with limited information develop conflict, consequences and a satisfying resolution?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Horror Fiction; branch: Horror structures. Address Isolation with limited information through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Define the protagonist's goal and the central conflict in Isolation with limited information.
- Build three causally connected turning points with visible costs and character choices.
- Plant a detail that earns the ending; test whether a reader can follow the world's rules.
- Review cultural attribution, consent and sensitive portrayals; label the episode as original fiction.

### Atmosphere

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-375-004 — Unseen threat staging
