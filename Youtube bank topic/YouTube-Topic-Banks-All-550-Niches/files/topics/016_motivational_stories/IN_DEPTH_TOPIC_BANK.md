## 01. Documented achievement

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-016-013 | How can Helen Keller's education be told without erasing her teachers and collaborators? | Use traceable records, her own writing and disability context rather than reducing a life to a miraculous isolated breakthrough. |
| BANK-016-014 | What does Katherine Johnson's work reveal about skilled teamwork? | Locate documented tasks, institutions and collaborators; avoid invented dialogue or claims that one person alone enabled a program. |
| BANK-016-015 | How can Dashrath Manjhi's story include the community and infrastructure around it? | Compare reliable accounts, timelines, labor and local context without converting a simplified legend into a verified biography. |
| BANK-016-016 | How should a public account of Arunima Sinha's climbing journey be checked? | Trace firsthand statements, independent records, support and chronology; distinguish documented achievement from embellished inspirational details. |
