## Detailed proposals

### Digital persistence

**Research standard:** Declare a conditional scenario, not a prediction. Anchor present-day capabilities in evidence, make physical and institutional assumptions explicit, and trace second-order consequences and failure conditions.

**Source types to locate:** current original research on the enabling technologies; physical and economic constraints with explicit inputs; published scenario methods and relevant legal frameworks. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-482-001 — Personal archive avatars

**Video question:** Personal archive avatars: what follows if the stated assumptions hold?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Digital Immortality; branch: Digital persistence. Address Personal archive avatars through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- List the assumptions that make Personal archive avatars possible and distinguish demonstrated capability from speculation.
- Build a resource, energy, time or governance budget appropriate to the scenario.
- Follow consequences for daily life, institutions and unequal access rather than only depicting impressive technology.
- Change a major assumption and show how the scenario branches; identify observable evidence that would alter confidence.

### Governance

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
