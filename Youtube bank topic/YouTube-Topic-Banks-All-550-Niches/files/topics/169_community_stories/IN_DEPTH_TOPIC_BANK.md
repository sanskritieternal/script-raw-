## Detailed proposals

### Local institutions

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-169-001 — Neighborhood mutual aid

**Video question:** Neighborhood mutual aid: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Community Stories; branch: Local institutions. Address Neighborhood mutual aid through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Neighborhood mutual aid and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.
- Return to participants for context and consent checks; distinguish their views from the narrator's interpretation.

### Story methods

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-169-004 — Resident-led interviews

**Video question:** Resident-led interviews: which creative choices change what the audience understands?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Community Stories; branch: Story methods. Address Resident-led interviews through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Identify the audience, format and communication problem for Resident-led interviews.
- Annotate a specific scene, design or draft to show how the creative choice works.
- Compare a revised version and explain what information, feeling or interpretation changes.
- Check accessibility, rights, consent and possible misreadings before treating the example as a reusable method.

### Ethics

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-169-007 — Consent in small communities

**Video question:** Consent in small communities: how do the rules and institutions work in a named jurisdiction?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Community Stories; branch: Ethics. Address Consent in small communities through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the responsible institutions, applicable date and jurisdiction for Consent in small communities.
- Trace a documented case or service journey from entry conditions to outcome, distinguishing rules from actual implementation.
- Compare access, costs, incentives and accountability for different affected groups.
- Check amendments, exceptions and competing interpretations; identify where qualified local guidance is required.
