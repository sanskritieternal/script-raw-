## 01. Goals, priorities and realistic capacity

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-071-013 | How can a person distinguish a chosen goal from an obligation inherited from other people? | Use an original reflective planning example to separate preferences, duties and external pressure; avoid diagnosing motives or asserting one correct definition of success. |
| BANK-071-014 | What makes a weekly plan feasible when paid work and care responsibilities compete? | Map fixed commitments, transition time, variable demands and rest using an illustrative calendar; distinguish a capacity estimate from a moral measure of effort. |
| BANK-071-015 | How can a large ambition become a next action that actually reduces uncertainty? | Turn an invented learning project into a question, a small deliverable and a review point; avoid assuming that breaking work into more tasks always clarifies it. |
| BANK-071-016 | Why can prioritization fail even when every task has an urgency label? | Compare deadlines, consequences, dependencies and decision authority; use a bounded work scenario rather than treating one matrix as a universal planning solution. |

## 07. Rest, limits and unequal conditions

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Inspect current professional guidance and original studies for the exact population, intervention or measurement. **Source types to locate:** current official clinical or public-health guidance; original trials, cohort studies or diagnostic-validation reports; study registrations, protocols and adverse-event records. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-071-037 | How should a productivity episode discuss sleep without converting research into a personal treatment plan? | Use current health guidance and original studies on cognition, population and sleep conditions; exclude individualized sleep prescriptions or claims that a routine cures fatigue. |
| BANK-071-038 | When does workplace exhaustion require a discussion of job conditions rather than a new personal system? | Compare demands, control, staffing and support using official occupational-health definitions; avoid diagnosing burnout or claiming self-optimization resolves organizational harm. |
| BANK-071-039 | How can productivity methods be adapted with people who have fluctuating energy? | Use consented preferences, flexible commitments and access needs; distinguish accommodation ideas from clinical advice or assumptions about a diagnosis. |
| BANK-071-040 | What does a time-use diary reveal about unequal access to uninterrupted work? | Compare caregiving, paid hours, commute and household labor through official data and consented examples; do not treat time use as a universal personal choice. |

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

|---|---|---|
