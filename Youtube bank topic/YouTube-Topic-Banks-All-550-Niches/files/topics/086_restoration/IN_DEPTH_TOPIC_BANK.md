## 07. Treatment planning and professional ethics

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-086-037 | What does reversibility mean when a treatment cannot literally be undone without a trace? | Examine conservation standards, retreatability, documentation and material change through published cases; avoid using reversible as an unsupported marketing guarantee. |
| BANK-086-038 | How can the least-intervention principle conflict with an owner's wish to keep using an object? | Compare significance, wear, access and informed consent; distinguish ethical tradeoffs from a rule that all objects must be retired from use. |
| BANK-086-039 | What evidence should justify removing an earlier repair? | Inspect its history, stability, harm and effect on interpretation with professional assessment; do not assume an unattractive repair has no heritage value. |
| BANK-086-040 | How can restoration costs be discussed without promising an increase in resale price? | Separate labor, materials, specialist tests, risk and intended outcome using illustrative figures; distinguish treatment value from a speculative market valuation. |
