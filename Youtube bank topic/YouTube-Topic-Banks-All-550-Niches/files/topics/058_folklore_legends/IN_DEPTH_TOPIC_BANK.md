## 01. Folklore as living tradition

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Identify the text, edition, translator, commentary, community and ritual context as applicable. Attribute theological and spiritual claims, preserve regional variation and respect public versus restricted knowledge.

**Source types to locate:** primary texts in traceable editions; authorized public teaching and ritual descriptions; consented community accounts and critical historical studies. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.
