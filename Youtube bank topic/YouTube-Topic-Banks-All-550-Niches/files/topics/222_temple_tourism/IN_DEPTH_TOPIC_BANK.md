**Research standard:** Identify the text, edition, translator, commentary, community and ritual context as applicable. Attribute theological and spiritual claims, preserve regional variation and respect public versus restricted knowledge.

**Source types to locate:** primary texts in traceable editions; authorized public teaching and ritual descriptions; consented community accounts and critical historical studies. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Visitor systems

**Research standard:** Use dated official access rules, operator schedules and local information. Separate general planning from time-sensitive availability; include accessibility, local perspectives and changing environmental conditions.

**Source types to locate:** official transport operators and destination authorities; current permit, visa and access rules; consented local accounts and independently verified travel costs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
