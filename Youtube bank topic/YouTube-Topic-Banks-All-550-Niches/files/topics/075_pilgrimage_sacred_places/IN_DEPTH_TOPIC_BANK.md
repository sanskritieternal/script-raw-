## 08. History, conservation and contested evidence

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-075-042 | What can inscriptions establish about a shrine's patrons and repairs? | Use published readings, dates, provenance and archaeological context; distinguish a documented donation or repair from proof of the site's complete origin story. |
| BANK-075-043 | How can a pilgrimage town's history include labor and infrastructure as well as saints and rulers? | Trace transport, lodging, water, sanitation and service work through records and consented accounts; avoid making an institution's publicity the only source. |
| BANK-075-044 | Why can preserving a sacred monument require negotiation with its continuing ritual use? | Compare conservation records, material needs and authorized community perspectives at one site; do not assume either conservation or worship has a single uncontested voice. |
| BANK-075-045 | How should competing historical claims about a sacred site be presented without fueling hostility? | Build a dated evidence inventory from primary records and attributed scholarship; distinguish legal findings, archaeology and devotional claims and avoid unsupported allegations against communities. |
