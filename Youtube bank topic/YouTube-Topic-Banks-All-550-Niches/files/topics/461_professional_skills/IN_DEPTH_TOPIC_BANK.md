## Detailed proposals

### Workplace capability

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

**Video question:** Facilitating meetings: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Professional Skills; branch: Workplace capability. Address Facilitating meetings through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.
