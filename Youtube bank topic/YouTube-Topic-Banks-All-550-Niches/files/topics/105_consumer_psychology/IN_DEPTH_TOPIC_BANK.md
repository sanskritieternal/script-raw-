## 01. Consumer decisions and evidence standards

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-105-010 | How can a purchase decision be studied without assuming shoppers are either rational or foolish? | Compare goals, information, constraints and actual behavior in original research; distinguish a simplified economic model from a judgment of individuals. |
| BANK-105-011 | What is the difference between a stated purchase intention and an observed transaction? | Inspect survey design, context, follow-up and behavioral measures; avoid treating willingness to buy as verified demand. |
| BANK-105-012 | Why can the same product be evaluated differently when the comparison set changes? | Use original harmless examples and primary choice research; identify the alternatives and avoid universal predictions about a viewer. |
