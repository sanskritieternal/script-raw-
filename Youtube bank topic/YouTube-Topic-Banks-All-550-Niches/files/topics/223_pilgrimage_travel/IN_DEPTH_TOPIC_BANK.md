## Detailed proposals

### Journey planning

**Research standard:** Use dated official access rules, operator schedules and local information. Separate general planning from time-sensitive availability; include accessibility, local perspectives and changing environmental conditions.

**Source types to locate:** official transport operators and destination authorities; current permit, visa and access rules; consented local accounts and independently verified travel costs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-223-001 — Multi-stop pilgrimage logistics

**Video question:** Multi-stop pilgrimage logistics: what must a traveler verify for a specific route and date?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Pilgrimage Travel; branch: Journey planning. Address Multi-stop pilgrimage logistics through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose the route, dates, traveler requirements and budget assumptions for Multi-stop pilgrimage logistics.
- Verify access, transport connections, cancellation conditions and accessible facilities from official sources.
- Build a realistic plan with travel time and contingency, distinguishing verified prices from illustrative estimates.
- Examine local community impacts and list the time-sensitive details that must be checked again before travel.

### Community systems

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-223-004 — Langar and annadana services

**Video question:** Langar and annadana services: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Pilgrimage Travel; branch: Community systems. Address Langar and annadana services through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Langar and annadana services and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.
- Return to participants for context and consent checks; distinguish their views from the narrator's interpretation.

### Responsible coverage

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
