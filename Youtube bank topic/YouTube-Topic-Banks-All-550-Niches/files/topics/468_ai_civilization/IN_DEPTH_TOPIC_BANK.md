## Detailed proposals

### AI society scenarios

**Research standard:** Declare a conditional scenario, not a prediction. Anchor present-day capabilities in evidence, make physical and institutional assumptions explicit, and trace second-order consequences and failure conditions.

**Source types to locate:** current original research on the enabling technologies; physical and economic constraints with explicit inputs; published scenario methods and relevant legal frameworks. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

**Video question:** AI-mediated public services: what follows if the stated assumptions hold?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: AI Civilization; branch: AI society scenarios. Address AI-mediated public services through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- List the assumptions that make AI-mediated public services possible and distinguish demonstrated capability from speculation.
- Build a resource, energy, time or governance budget appropriate to the scenario.
