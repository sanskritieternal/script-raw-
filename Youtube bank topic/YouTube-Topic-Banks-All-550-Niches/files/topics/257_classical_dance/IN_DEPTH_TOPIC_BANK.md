### Indian forms

**Research standard:** Identify the text, edition, translator, commentary, community and ritual context as applicable. Attribute theological and spiritual claims, preserve regional variation and respect public versus restricted knowledge.

**Source types to locate:** primary texts in traceable editions; authorized public teaching and ritual descriptions; consented community accounts and critical historical studies. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Performance analysis

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
