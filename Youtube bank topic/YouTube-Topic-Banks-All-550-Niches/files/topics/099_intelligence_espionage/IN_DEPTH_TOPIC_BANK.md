## 04. Assessment, uncertainty and failure

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-099-024 | What does an intelligence estimate's confidence language mean in its own published methodology? | Read the actual official definitions and assessment context; do not convert qualitative wording into invented numeric probabilities. |
| BANK-099-025 | How can an intelligence failure be separated into collection, interpretation and communication questions? | Use a documented inquiry to identify supported findings and alternatives; avoid hindsight-based claims that the result was obvious. |
| BANK-099-026 | Why can the absence of a warning in released records fail to prove that no warning existed? | Examine archival coverage, retention, redaction and institutional boundaries; distinguish missing evidence from a theory of deliberate concealment. |
| BANK-099-027 | What can competing hypotheses contribute to analysis of incomplete information? | Use a harmless fictional historical-style dataset and published methods; avoid targeting real people or producing operational intelligence. |
