## 01. Describing a village through its residents

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-094-013 | How can a village documentary begin with residents' questions rather than an outsider's idea of simple living? | Use informed consent, varied households and local priorities to define the story; avoid presenting one family as the voice of an entire place. |
| BANK-094-014 | What does a day-in-the-life film omit if it records only visually attractive work? | Map planning, waiting, care, travel and seasonal demands alongside visible tasks; distinguish one filmed day from the village's ordinary annual rhythm. |
| BANK-094-015 | How can local place names and settlement histories be recorded responsibly? | Compare speakers, maps, official records and attributed memories; preserve disagreement and do not treat one oral account as the complete chronology. |

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-094-038 | How can compensation and consent be handled when filming residents' daily work? | Agree participation, payment, rights, withdrawal limits and intended distribution; distinguish voluntary cooperation from consent inferred through a local intermediary. |
| BANK-094-039 | What should a rural-life creator disclose about staging and repeated tasks? | Label recreated sequences, time compression and directing choices; avoid presenting an arranged activity as spontaneous evidence of everyday behavior. |
| BANK-094-040 | How can a village episode give viewers enough location context without exposing private homes or sensitive sites? | Use agreed geographic detail, anonymization and community review where appropriate; do not publish protected locations or assume filming access grants publication consent. |
