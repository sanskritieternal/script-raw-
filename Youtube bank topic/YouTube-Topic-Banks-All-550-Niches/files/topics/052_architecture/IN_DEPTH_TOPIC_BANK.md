## 01. Reading buildings

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-052-013 | How can a building be studied as evidence of daily life? | Compare plans, circulation, use, alterations and occupants' accounts rather than treating the facade as the whole story. |
| BANK-052-014 | What is the difference between a building's style and its structural system? | Use paired examples to separate visual conventions, load paths and construction materials. |
| BANK-052-015 | Why can an architect's original drawing differ from the completed building? | Trace revisions, budget, contractors, regulation and site discoveries through a documented project. |
| BANK-052-016 | How do buildings communicate status without explicit inscriptions? | Examine scale, access, materials, siting and audience while marking interpretation rather than assuming intention. |

## 04. Climate and environmental performance

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-052-025 | How do courtyards influence light, airflow and social space? | Compare measured performance, geometry, climate and actual occupation rather than assuming all courtyards cool buildings equally. |
| BANK-052-026 | When can passive design reduce dependence on mechanical conditioning? | State climate, occupancy, comfort criteria and limitations using a documented building study. |
| BANK-052-027 | Why does a highly glazed building require careful energy analysis? | Compare solar gain, daylight, orientation, shading and mechanical systems within explicit assumptions. |
| BANK-052-028 | How should embodied and operational carbon be compared in a building project? | Define lifecycle stages, service life, energy scenarios and uncertainty without treating one metric as a complete verdict. |
