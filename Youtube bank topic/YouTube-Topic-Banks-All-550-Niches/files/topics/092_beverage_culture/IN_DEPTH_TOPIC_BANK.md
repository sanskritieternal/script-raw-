## 01. Beverage identity and sensory language

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-013 | How can a beverage be described through aroma, texture and preparation rather than a prestige label? | Use ordinary permission-cleared examples with stated serving conditions; distinguish a taster's vocabulary from a universal quality ranking. |
| BANK-092-014 | Why can water composition change a beverage comparison? | Review documented mineral, temperature and extraction conditions in a bounded test; avoid equating taste preference with drinking-water safety. |
| BANK-092-015 | What should a beverage-tasting episode disclose about samples and commercial relationships? | Record origin, preparation, sponsorship and participant consent; avoid implying one tasting represents every batch or consumer. |

## 02. Tea and regional preparation

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-016 | How do processing choices distinguish green, black and oolong tea beyond the final color? | Use producer records and primary food-science sources for selected examples; distinguish oxidation, heating and handling without supplying a universal factory recipe. |
| BANK-092-017 | What changes when masala chai is discussed as a family practice rather than a standardized formula? | Use attributed household or vendor accounts, ingredient variation and preparation context; avoid declaring one community's proportions the authentic national rule. |
| BANK-092-018 | How can a gongfu-style tea episode preserve technique and social context without becoming an exotic spectacle? | Use authorized practitioners, identified tea and attributed histories; distinguish a filmed public demonstration from a claim about all Chinese tea culture. |
| BANK-092-019 | Why do tea origin names require attention to labeling and trade rules? | Read current official geographical-indication or appellation requirements for a named tea; distinguish geographic claims, blending and marketing language. |

## 03. Coffee from crop to cup

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-020 | How do coffee processing and roasting contribute different information about flavor? | Trace a documented lot through postharvest processing, roast records and sensory observations; avoid assigning every flavor note to one isolated cause. |
| BANK-092-021 | Why can grind distribution affect extraction even when the nominal setting is unchanged? | Inspect particle variation, equipment, brew method and controlled trials; distinguish a demonstrated effect from a universal grinder ranking. |
| BANK-092-022 | What does espresso crema reveal and fail to reveal about a shot? | Compare bean age, roast, gas and machine conditions using original tests and primary references; avoid treating crema alone as proof of quality. |
| BANK-092-023 | How can coffee-origin storytelling include growers without inventing a direct economic benefit? | Trace supply records, paid relationships and consented producer accounts; distinguish a named farm from evidence of fair compensation across the chain. |

## 05. Health claims, caffeine and alcohol literacy

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Inspect current professional guidance and original studies for the exact population, intervention or measurement. Report comparators, absolute outcomes, uncertainty, harms and limits. Do not diagnose, prescribe or imply that education replaces professional care.

**Source types to locate:** current official clinical or public-health guidance; original trials, cohort studies or diagnostic-validation reports; study registrations, protocols and adverse-event records. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-028 | How can caffeine content be discussed when serving size and preparation vary? | Use current official guidance and measured product or study data with units explicit; exclude individualized intake prescriptions and unsupported energy or focus promises. |
| BANK-092-029 | What evidence is needed before calling a drink detoxifying or immunity boosting? | Identify the precise outcome, tested formulation, population and original clinical evidence; distinguish nutrient content from demonstrated treatment or prevention. |
| BANK-092-030 | How should nonalcoholic and alcohol-free labels be checked in a specific market? | Consult current official definitions, actual product content and labeling rules; avoid assuming the same threshold or suitability in every jurisdiction. |
| BANK-092-031 | How can a beverage-culture episode discuss alcohol without equating cultural importance with health benefit? | Separate attributed social history from current official health evidence; exclude consumption encouragement, medical reassurance or advice for minors. |

## 06. Service, packaging and environmental evidence

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-032 | What does a bottled beverage's label reveal about concentration, added ingredients and serving assumptions? | Read the actual current label under the relevant authority's rules; distinguish juice, concentrate and flavor terms without diagnosing personal dietary needs. |
| BANK-092-033 | How can reusable-cup claims be assessed without assuming reuse has zero impact? | Define production, washing, transport, use count and end-of-life boundaries; use documented lifecycle evidence and label uncertain assumptions. |
| BANK-092-034 | Why can cold-chain and packaging requirements matter as much as a drink's recipe? | Trace official storage guidance, transport, tamper evidence and intended shelf life for a named product; avoid creating new storage-duration rules. |

## 07. Comparative tasting and honest beverage media

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-092-035 | How can a beverage comparison keep serving temperature from silently deciding the result? | Use coded samples, recorded temperature, timing and balanced order; distinguish a controlled small trial from a general consumer verdict. |
| BANK-092-036 | What should a cafe review separate into observation, preference and current service information? | Date the visit, describe tested items and disclose gifts; verify changing prices and access rather than presenting a past experience as current certainty. |
| BANK-092-037 | How can traditional drink names be translated without flattening distinct preparations? | Consult speakers, community-authorized accounts and actual ingredients; preserve local terminology where direct equivalence would mislead. |
