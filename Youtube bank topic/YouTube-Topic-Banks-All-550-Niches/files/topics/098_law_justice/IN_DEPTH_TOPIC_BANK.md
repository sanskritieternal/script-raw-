**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-098-042 | How can precedent be distinguished from a quotation taken out of a judgment? | Read facts, issue, reasoning, order and later treatment in official reports; separate a binding proposition from commentary or a dissent. |
| BANK-098-043 | What changes when a law is amended after a widely cited case? | Trace commencement, transitional provisions and later decisions; avoid applying the old rule to events outside its scope. |
| BANK-098-044 | How can a legal reform be evaluated beyond counting cases filed? | Compare access, processing, outcomes, costs and implementation using official data and original studies; distinguish association from causal evidence. |
| BANK-098-045 | How should a legal channel correct advice-like wording in an older explainer? | Identify the outdated or overbroad claim, update the visible material and define the current scope; avoid implying a correction resolves viewers' individual cases. |
