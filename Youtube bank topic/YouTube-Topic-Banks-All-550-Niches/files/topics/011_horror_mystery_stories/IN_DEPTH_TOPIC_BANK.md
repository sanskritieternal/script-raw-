**Source types to locate:** original story notes and world rules; permission-cleared reference material; reader feedback and a continuity audit. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| BANK-011-013 | How can a night-shift archive become frightening before anything supernatural appears? | Build ordinary routines, document inconsistencies and a protagonist's responsibility; establish what a changed record would cost. |
| BANK-011-014 | How can a village of disagreeing clocks sustain a mystery beyond its opening image? | Give different time discrepancies observable rules and consequences; avoid resolving every contradiction through an unexplained twist. |

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
