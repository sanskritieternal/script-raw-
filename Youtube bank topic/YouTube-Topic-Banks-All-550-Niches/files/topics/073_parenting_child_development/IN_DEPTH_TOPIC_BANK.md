## 01. Development, variation and observation

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-073-013 | What does a developmental milestone describe and what does it not decide about a child? | Consult current official age-range guidance and screening definitions; distinguish population expectations, individual observation and professional assessment without diagnosing from a checklist. |
| BANK-073-014 | Why can children develop different skills at different rates? | Review original developmental research on variation, experience and measurement; avoid ranking children or explaining a particular delay without clinical assessment. |
| BANK-073-015 | How can caregivers record a concern so a pediatric professional receives useful context? | Document onset, settings, routines and specific observable changes; protect the child's privacy and exclude self-administered diagnostic verdicts. |
| BANK-073-016 | What changes when child-development research follows children over time rather than comparing ages once? | Compare cohort design, attrition, contextual changes and measured outcomes; do not convert group patterns into an individual forecast. |
