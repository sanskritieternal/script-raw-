## 01. Story before generation

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Create original fiction with explicit world rules, character goals and causal continuity. Label invented events and separate inspiration from claims about actual cultures or living people.

**Source types to locate:** original story notes and world rules; permission-cleared reference material; reader feedback and a continuity audit. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-019-013 | How can a maintenance robot discovering a garden sustain a complete short film? | Define its task, unexpected discovery, decision and consequence before choosing impressive generated environments. |
| BANK-019-014 | What conflict emerges when a courier delivers letters after a city's evacuation? | Give the delivery a concrete recipient, obstacle and moral choice instead of relying on abandoned-city spectacle. |
| BANK-019-015 | How can two astronauts with a failing translation system remain emotionally understandable? | Build observable misunderstandings, shared tasks and a changed relationship rather than arbitrary mistranslation jokes. |
| BANK-019-016 | What rules would make a melody from a future recording a fair mystery? | Define what the signal can contain, how it is verified and the cost of acting on it. |
