## Detailed proposals

### Immersive interfaces

**Research standard:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, tolerances, failure conditions and the limits of the example. Do not provide hazardous construction or unauthorized access procedures.

**Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-128-001 — Inside-out tracking

**Video question:** Inside-out tracking: how does it work, and where do its design assumptions fail?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Virtual Reality / AR; branch: Immersive interfaces. Address Inside-out tracking through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the components and operating conditions behind Inside-out tracking.
- Trace a worked example using a diagram, calculation or observed system; state all numerical assumptions.
- Compare an alternative design against the same requirements; include cost, reliability and accessibility where relevant.
- Examine a failure mode, measurement error or edge case and show what evidence would change the explanation.

### Experience design

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-128-004 — Comfort and locomotion

**Video question:** Comfort and locomotion: which creative choices change what the audience understands?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Virtual Reality / AR; branch: Experience design. Address Comfort and locomotion through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Identify the audience, format and communication problem for Comfort and locomotion.
- Annotate a specific scene, design or draft to show how the creative choice works.
- Compare a revised version and explain what information, feeling or interpretation changes.
- Check accessibility, rights, consent and possible misreadings before treating the example as a reusable method.

### Evidence and privacy

**Research standard:** Inspect the actual product version, official documentation, terms and privacy policies. Test claims under stated conditions and distinguish account permissions, data retention, measured functionality and marketing.

**Source types to locate:** official versioned documentation and release notes; dated privacy policies and service terms; reproducible product tests and original validation reports. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-128-007 — Eye-tracking data retention

**Video question:** Eye-tracking data retention: what changes across products, versions and user settings?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Virtual Reality / AR; branch: Evidence and privacy. Address Eye-tracking data retention through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Identify the product, version, region and user settings relevant to Eye-tracking data retention.
- Trace an ordinary user task and observe what succeeds, fails or requires an undocumented assumption.
- Compare cost, accessibility, portability and data flows against an appropriate alternative.
- Check changes since the cited documentation and avoid presenting a single test as universal performance.
