## Detailed proposals

### Physical plausibility

**Research standard:** Declare a conditional scenario, not a prediction. Anchor present-day capabilities in evidence, make physical and institutional assumptions explicit, and trace second-order consequences and failure conditions.

**Source types to locate:** current original research on the enabling technologies; physical and economic constraints with explicit inputs; published scenario methods and relevant legal frameworks. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

**Research standard:** Create original fiction with explicit world rules, character goals and causal continuity. Label invented events and separate inspiration from claims about actual cultures or living people.

**Source types to locate:** original story notes and world rules; permission-cleared reference material; reader feedback and a continuity audit. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Cartography

**Research standard:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, tolerances, failure conditions and the limits of the example. Do not provide hazardous construction or unauthorized access procedures.

**Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.
