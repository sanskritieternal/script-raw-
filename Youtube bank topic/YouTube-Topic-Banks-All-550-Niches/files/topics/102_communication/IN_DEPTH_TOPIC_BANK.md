## 01. Meaning, audience and context

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-102-010 | Why can a technically accurate message still fail to answer its audience's question? | Compare assumed knowledge, purpose, vocabulary and missing context in original examples; distinguish accuracy from usefulness without prescribing one universal style. |
| BANK-102-011 | How can communication goals be separated into informing, requesting and persuading? | Rewrite one harmless message for each goal and inspect the changed evidence and action; do not equate clarity with agreement. |
| BANK-102-012 | What changes when a message moves from conversation to a permanent written record? | Compare context, ambiguity, audience expansion and correction options; avoid publishing real private exchanges as examples. |

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-102-013 | How can a clarifying question reduce uncertainty without making the speaker repeat everything? | Use original service or workplace exchanges to distinguish missing facts, assumptions and desired outcomes; avoid interrogation scripts or diagnosis of hidden motives. |
| BANK-102-014 | What does active listening require beyond repeating the other person's words? | Compare checking meaning, acknowledging uncertainty and allowing correction in fictional dialogue; do not promise a technique will resolve every disagreement. |

| BANK-102-016 | Why can silence mean different things across people and situations? | Use attributed cultural accounts and conversation studies with their limits; avoid treating pause length as proof of dishonesty or emotion. |

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

## 07. Disagreement, feedback and misinformation

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-102-033 | How can disagreement be made specific enough for evidence to matter? | Separate facts, definitions, values and proposed actions in original dialogue; avoid suggesting every conflict can or should be negotiated away. |
| BANK-102-034 | What makes feedback actionable without turning it into a verdict on a person's character? | Compare observable work, consequences, expectations and support in fictional examples; avoid disclosing private performance information. |
| BANK-102-035 | How should a correction connect the false claim with the evidence that replaces it? | Use original harmless misinformation and traceable primary sources; avoid repeating a harmful allegation unnecessarily or inventing audience-impact data. |
| BANK-102-036 | Why can certainty in delivery be mistaken for certainty in evidence? | Compare equivalent scripts with different confidence language and evidence quality; distinguish an illustrative contrast from proof about every audience. |

## 08. Digital channels and accountability

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Inspect the actual product version, official documentation, terms and privacy policies. Test claims under stated conditions and distinguish account permissions, data retention, measured functionality and marketing.

**Source types to locate:** official versioned documentation and release notes; dated privacy policies and service terms; reproducible product tests and original validation reports. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-102-037 | What should a team decide before moving every conversation into a group chat? | Compare urgency, searchability, access, retention and decision records using current tool documentation; avoid assuming a new platform resolves organizational ambiguity. |
| BANK-102-038 | How can an AI-assisted message preserve a human author's intended meaning? | Check factual claims, commitments, tone, privacy and actual recipient context; disclose assistance where required and do not send examples to real people. |
| BANK-102-039 | What does a communication audit need beyond counting messages sent? | Map purpose, audience, comprehension, action and unwanted burden with ethical measurements; avoid presenting output volume as communication success. |
