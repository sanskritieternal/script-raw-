## 03. Ecosystem processes and scales

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-067-020 | How can a decomposition sequence explain more than the disappearance of a leaf? | Plan evidence for organisms, moisture, material change and nutrient pathways; identify what a time-lapse cannot directly establish about chemistry or causation. |
| BANK-067-021 | What must a river documentary show between headwaters and estuary? | Compare flow, sediment, habitats, human use and seasonal change at selected reaches; avoid portraying a sampled route as a complete watershed inventory. |
| BANK-067-022 | How can a coral-reef episode distinguish bleaching, mortality and later recovery? | Require identified sites, dates, monitoring definitions and follow-up evidence; use marine research sources and avoid diagnosing reef condition solely from color grading. |
| BANK-067-023 | Why does filming a wildfire's aftermath require more than contrasting blackened ground with green shoots? | Investigate burn severity, prior ecology, regeneration and human impacts over a defined period; separate resilience evidence from claims that all fires are beneficial. |
| BANK-067-024 | How can soil life be made visible without turning magnified imagery into a misleading scale comparison? | Label imaging method, sample origin, magnification and representative limits; connect observed organisms to research rather than inferring ecosystem function from appearance. |

## 04. Camera, sound and time

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. **Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-067-025 | What does a macro lens change about the apparent world of a small animal? | Compare field of view, depth of field, scale cues and lighting using controlled non-disturbing subjects; avoid treating cinematic closeness as an animal's actual visual experience. |
| BANK-067-026 | How should a time-lapse disclose the relationship between screen time and real time? | Record frame intervals, omitted periods and environmental conditions; distinguish continuous observation from an assembled sequence of separate events. |
| BANK-067-027 | Why can slow-motion footage create a false impression of routine movement? | Identify capture rate, playback speed and the event context; compare real-time footage before drawing conclusions about speed or intention. |
| BANK-067-028 | How can location sound be distinguished from sound added during editing? | Plan field recordings, sound-library attribution and disclosures for recreated effects; do not imply that an enhanced soundtrack is an unaltered acoustic record. |

## 05. Evidence, narration and reconstruction

**Level:** intermediate. **Evidence direction:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-067-029 | What evidence is needed before narration says one environmental event caused another? | Build a claim map using field data, alternative explanations and relevant studies; separate visible sequence, correlation and causal inference. |
| BANK-067-030 | How can an animated reconstruction explain a hidden process without inventing certainty? | Annotate observed inputs, inferred mechanisms and unknown details using expert-reviewed references; label reconstruction and preserve uncertainty in narration. |
| BANK-067-031 | When should a documentary show a scientist's method rather than only their conclusion? | Choose an observable sampling or identification task, describe its limitations and retain conflicting findings; exclude staged laboratory results presented as original discoveries. |
| BANK-067-032 | How can climate trends be discussed in a film centered on a single unusual season? | Compare the local event with official long-term datasets and attribution evidence; avoid declaring one season proof of a trend or assigning causation without research. |

## 06. People, land and conservation stories

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-067-033 | How can a film include local ecological knowledge without extracting it from its owners? | Plan consent, attribution, review of sensitive knowledge and agreed uses with participants; distinguish public information from restricted locations or practices. |
| BANK-067-034 | What makes a protected-area story incomplete if it omits neighboring communities? | Investigate access, livelihoods, governance, conservation outcomes and differing accounts in one named place; avoid assuming protection has uniform costs or benefits. |
| BANK-067-035 | How can restoration be filmed without counting planted seedlings as completed recovery? | Define baseline, survival, habitat function and monitoring duration; compare stated targets with measured outcomes and include setbacks. |
| BANK-067-036 | What should a documentary reveal about a conservation project's funding and conflicts of interest? | Trace published sponsors, reported outcomes and decision roles; separate verified relationships from allegations and do not infer fraud from sponsorship alone. |

## 08. Advanced editorial comparisons

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Define the rival claims and their evidence standards. Compare sources on equal terms, separate empirical findings from values or interpretations and do not imply that all positions have equal evidential support.

**Source types to locate:** the original sources behind the compared claims; explicit definitions and measurement methods; independent critiques with traceable evidence. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-067-041 | How do presenter-led and observational films change what viewers can assess? | Compare rights-cleared scenes for explanation, access, pacing and visibility of evidence; separate formal differences from claims that one format is inherently truthful. |
| BANK-067-042 | What does an animal-centered episode omit that a habitat-centered episode can reveal? | Develop paired original treatments of the same wetland around relationships, background species and scale; avoid duplicating the wildlife bank's behavior questions. |
| BANK-067-043 | How can a series maintain continuity when filming spans different years and places? | Audit dates, sites, individual identity and seasonal matching across an episode timeline; disclose composite sequences instead of creating a false continuous journey. |
