## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Yoga across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Yoga Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Yoga.
- **Tier 2: Yoga Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Yoga.
- **Tier 3: Yoga Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Yoga.
- **Tier 4: Yoga Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Yoga.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Yoga: in a Named Source**
   - *Question:* What does one dated text mean by yoga in its own context?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish textual concept, later lineage, and modern class format. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
2. **Yoga: A Popular Yoga Style Examined**
   - *Question:* What practices and aims define one widely taught style?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare curriculum, teacher lineage, participants, and variations. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
3. **Yoga: Teachers and Communities Behind a Style**
   - *Question:* Whose work is missing from a founder-centered yoga history?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated materials and consented teacher and student accounts. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
4. **Yoga: as Philosophy and Practice**
   - *Question:* How do a named text and a present school use the word yoga?
   - *Key Focus:* Present their distinct aims without collapsing yoga into postures. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
5. **Yoga: Studying a Multi-Part Yoga Program**
   - *Question:* How can research separate posture, breath, attention, and group effects?
   - *Key Focus:* Define intervention, comparator, adherence, outcomes, and harms. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Yoga: A Historical Continuity Claim**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports a claimed direct line from an old text to a modern style?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace teachers, manuals, and changes; state gaps. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
7. **Yoga: Online Yoga Instruction**
   - *Question:* Does one digital format preserve accessibility and safety?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare supervision, feedback, privacy, and reported adverse events. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
8. **Yoga: A Teacher's Documented Contribution**
   - *Question:* What did one named teacher change in a yoga curriculum?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare earlier and later manuals, recordings, and accounts. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
9. **Yoga: Enters a New Institution**
   - *Question:* What changed when one practice entered a school, clinic, or public program?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare purpose, curriculum, instructor standards, and evaluation. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
10. **Yoga: What a Named Yoga Class Contains**
   - *Question:* Which components distinguish one program from another?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe study or curriculum design without giving exercise instructions. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Yoga: A Style's Spread and Revision**
   - *Question:* Why did one yoga style expand or change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use teaching records, publications, institutions, and participant demand. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
12. **Yoga: A Text and a Modern Studio**
   - *Question:* Which ideas persisted and which practices changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare named source, lineage history, and current curriculum. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
13. **Yoga: A Training or Access Proposal**
   - *Question:* What would one proposal need to improve safety and inclusion?
   - *Key Focus:* Assess teacher preparation, adaptations, cost, and evaluation. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
14. **Yoga: as a Cure?**
   - *Question:* Does a health claim fit trials of the exact style and condition?
   - *Key Focus:* Check comparator, population, benefit, injury, and current guidance. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
15. **Yoga: A Yoga Claim's Source Trail**
   - *Question:* How did one textual or health claim move into public marketing?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace source, teacher statement, study, and later correction. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Yoga: in a Specific Passage**
   - *Question:* What does one named text actually state about yoga?
   - *Key Focus:* Cite edition, chapter, translation, and surrounding argument. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
17. **Yoga: Two Philosophies of Yoga**
   - *Question:* How do named lineages differ on the purpose of practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Represent each from its own texts and teachers. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
18. **Yoga: A Participant's Reasons**
   - *Question:* What does one consented practitioner seek from a named yoga program?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate personal aims from evidence of clinical benefit. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
19. **Yoga: Within a Living Observance**
   - *Question:* How does one community integrate yoga into a public observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask practitioners and respect restricted parts. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
20. **Yoga: A Yoga Term in Context**
   - *Question:* How does translating one Sanskrit term change the perceived practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare linguistic and commentarial evidence. Evidence base: specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts.
