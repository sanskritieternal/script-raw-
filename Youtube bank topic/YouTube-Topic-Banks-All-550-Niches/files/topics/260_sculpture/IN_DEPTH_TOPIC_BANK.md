## Detailed proposals

### Sculptural materials

**Research standard:** Use the actual system documentation, a worked implementation and measurements. Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, tolerances, failure conditions and the limits of the example. Do not provide hazardous construction or unauthorized access procedures.

**Source types to locate:** original engineering papers; official versioned product or standards documentation; test reports and drawings with clear provenance. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-260-001 — Stone subtraction

**Video question:** Stone subtraction: how does it work, and where do its design assumptions fail?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Sculpture; branch: Sculptural materials. Address Stone subtraction through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the components and operating conditions behind Stone subtraction.
- Trace a worked example using a diagram, calculation or observed system; state all numerical assumptions.
- Compare an alternative design against the same requirements; include cost, reliability and accessibility where relevant.

### Historical contexts

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-260-004 — Chola bronze ritual use

**Video question:** Chola bronze ritual use: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Sculpture; branch: Historical contexts. Address Chola bronze ritual use through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Chola bronze ritual use in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
- Analyze institutions, material conditions and overlooked participants rather than attributing everything to a single figure.

### Interpretation

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-260-007 — Public monuments and contested memory

**Video question:** Public monuments and contested memory: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Sculpture; branch: Interpretation. Address Public monuments and contested memory through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Public monuments and contested memory and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
