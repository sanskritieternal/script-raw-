## Detailed proposals

### Village diversity

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-221-001 — Himalayan seasonal settlements

**Video question:** Himalayan seasonal settlements: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Indian Villages; branch: Village diversity. Address Himalayan seasonal settlements through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Himalayan seasonal settlements and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.
- Return to participants for context and consent checks; distinguish their views from the narrator's interpretation.

### Local systems

**Research standard:** Use the current official legal text, policy, procedure or public dataset for a specific jurisdiction and date. Distinguish written rules, implementation and lived access; give no personal legal or financial conclusion.

**Source types to locate:** official laws, rules and administrative guidance; audits, judgments and public service records; consented accounts from affected people and qualified specialists. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-221-004 — Panchayat service delivery

**Video question:** Panchayat service delivery: how do the rules and institutions work in a named jurisdiction?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Indian Villages; branch: Local systems. Address Panchayat service delivery through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Map the responsible institutions, applicable date and jurisdiction for Panchayat service delivery.
- Trace a documented case or service journey from entry conditions to outcome, distinguishing rules from actual implementation.

### Historical evidence

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-221-007 — Land settlement records

**Video question:** Land settlement records: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Indian Villages; branch: Historical evidence. Address Land settlement records through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Land settlement records in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
- Analyze institutions, material conditions and overlooked participants rather than attributing everything to a single figure.
- Separate documented conclusions, interpretive debates and gaps that a reconstruction cannot resolve.
