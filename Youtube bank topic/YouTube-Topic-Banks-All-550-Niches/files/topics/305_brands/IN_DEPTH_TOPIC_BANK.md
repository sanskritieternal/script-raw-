## Detailed proposals

### Brand systems

**Research standard:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Business strategy

**Research standard:** Use current official terms, filings or macroeconomic data; state jurisdiction, date, fees, taxes, risk, units and assumptions. Use transparent illustrative calculations rather than personal recommendations or return promises.

**Source types to locate:** regulator or official statistical publications; actual contracts, fee schedules and audited filings; original economic studies and disclosed calculation inputs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-305-004 — Brand extensions

**Video question:** Brand extensions: which assumptions change the costs, risks and outcomes?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Brands; branch: Business strategy. Address Brand extensions through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

### History and trust

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-305-007 — Trademark disputes

**Video question:** Trademark disputes: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Brands; branch: History and trust. Address Trademark disputes through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Trademark disputes in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
