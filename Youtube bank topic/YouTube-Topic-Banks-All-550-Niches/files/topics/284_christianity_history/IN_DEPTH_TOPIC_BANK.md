### Early history

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-284-001 — Early Christian communities

**Video question:** Early Christian communities: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Christianity History; branch: Early history. Address Early Christian communities through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Early Christian communities in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
- Analyze institutions, material conditions and overlooked participants rather than attributing everything to a single figure.
- Separate documented conclusions, interpretive debates and gaps that a reconstruction cannot resolve.
