### Aging research

**Research standard:** Use original observations or experiments, an appropriate scientific reference and the actual measurement method. Distinguish mechanisms, correlations, model assumptions, uncertainty and limits of generalization.

**Source types to locate:** original research reports and data; research-institution observation records; methods papers and current scientific reference works. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Translation

**Research standard:** Inspect current professional guidance and original studies for the exact population, intervention or measurement. Report comparators, absolute outcomes, uncertainty, harms and limits. Do not diagnose, prescribe or imply that education replaces professional care.

**Source types to locate:** current official clinical or public-health guidance; original trials, cohort studies or diagnostic-validation reports; study registrations, protocols and adverse-event records. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-472-004 — Biomarkers versus lifespan outcomes

**Video question:** Biomarkers versus lifespan outcomes: what does current evidence establish for a defined population?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Life Extension; branch: Translation. Address Biomarkers versus lifespan outcomes through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Define Biomarkers versus lifespan outcomes, the population and the outcome; distinguish normal variation, symptoms, screening and diagnosis where applicable.
- Explain the relevant mechanism without turning a plausible mechanism into proof of a clinical effect.
- Compare actual study designs, outcomes and follow-up; inspect harms, missing data and generalizability.
- State the evidence limits and the boundary between public education and an individual's clinical assessment.

### Social scenarios

**Research standard:** Declare a conditional scenario, not a prediction. Anchor present-day capabilities in evidence, make physical and institutional assumptions explicit, and trace second-order consequences and failure conditions.

**Source types to locate:** current original research on the enabling technologies; physical and economic constraints with explicit inputs; published scenario methods and relevant legal frameworks. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-472-007 — Pension systems under longer lives

**Video question:** Pension systems under longer lives: what follows if the stated assumptions hold?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Life Extension; branch: Social scenarios. Address Pension systems under longer lives through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- List the assumptions that make Pension systems under longer lives possible and distinguish demonstrated capability from speculation.
- Build a resource, energy, time or governance budget appropriate to the scenario.
- Follow consequences for daily life, institutions and unequal access rather than only depicting impressive technology.
- Change a major assumption and show how the scenario branches; identify observable evidence that would alter confidence.
