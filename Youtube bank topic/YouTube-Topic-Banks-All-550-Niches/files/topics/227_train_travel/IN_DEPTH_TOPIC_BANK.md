## Detailed proposals

### Rail journeys

**Research standard:** Use dated official access rules, operator schedules and local information. Separate general planning from time-sensitive availability; include accessibility, local perspectives and changing environmental conditions.

**Source types to locate:** official transport operators and destination authorities; current permit, visa and access rules; consented local accounts and independently verified travel costs. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

### Route interpretation

**Research standard:** Compare dated primary records, material evidence and historical scholarship. Distinguish event dates, the dates of surviving witnesses, later retellings and disagreement. Do not invent quotations, intentions or an unbroken lineage.

**Source types to locate:** dated archival records and original publications; inscriptions, artifacts or photographs with provenance; critical editions and historical scholarship. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-227-004 — Mountain railways and engineering

**Video question:** Mountain railways and engineering: what do contemporary records establish, and what remains uncertain?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Train Travel; branch: Route interpretation. Address Mountain railways and engineering through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Locate Mountain railways and engineering in a bounded time and place; build a chronology from identified records.
- Compare at least two independent witnesses or explain why only one survives.
- Analyze institutions, material conditions and overlooked participants rather than attributing everything to a single figure.
- Separate documented conclusions, interpretive debates and gaps that a reconstruction cannot resolve.

### Passenger experience

**Research standard:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These source types are not topic-specific sources already inspected.

#### BANK-227-007 — Accessible boarding

**Video question:** Accessible boarding: how do participants describe the practice and its differences?

**Coverage boundary:** Primary context: Train Travel; branch: Passenger experience. Address Accessible boarding through one bounded case, source set or experiment. Exclude an all-purpose survey of the whole niche; distinguish the case's findings from broader claims.

**Deep-dive research outline:**

- Choose a specific community and identify who participates in Accessible boarding and who may be excluded.
- Document the actual routines, resources and relationships involved through more than one perspective.
- Compare change across generations, locations or life circumstances without assuming uniformity.
- Return to participants for context and consent checks; distinguish their views from the narrator's interpretation.
