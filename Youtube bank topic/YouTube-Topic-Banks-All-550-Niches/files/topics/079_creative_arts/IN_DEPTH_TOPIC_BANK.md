## 01. Seeing and making decisions

**Level:** foundation. **Evidence direction:** Analyze an identifiable work or create a clearly labeled original example. Connect creative decisions to observable audience information; respect rights, attribution, consent and the difference between intent and reception.

**Source types to locate:** the actual work and authorized production materials; creator interviews or published craft explanations; permission-cleared examples and audience feedback. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-079-013 | How can a visual idea be developed before choosing an expensive medium? | Use original thumbnail sketches to explore focal point, scale and mood; distinguish a design exploration from a finished work or universal recipe. |
| BANK-079-014 | What changes when negative space becomes part of the composition? | Compare original arrangements for balance, grouping and movement; explain visible decisions without treating preference as an objective quality score. |
| BANK-079-015 | How can value contrast establish a focal point before color is added? | Build a rights-free grayscale study and compare local contrasts; distinguish luminance relationships from a claim that one palette fits every image. |
| BANK-079-016 | Why can two colors appear different when their surroundings change? | Use a labeled original swatch comparison and documented color-perception research; separate screen reproduction limits from a physical-pigment demonstration. |

**Level:** advanced. **Evidence direction:** Use consented participant accounts, local records and the community's own descriptions. Include differences of position, access and power; testimony is not a representative survey or clinical proof.

**Source types to locate:** consented interviews and oral histories; community publications and local records; field notes with method and consent documented. These are requirements, not sources already inspected.

| ID | Specific episode question | Individually written scope and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| BANK-079-037 | How can a critique describe what the work does before prescribing how to change it? | Use consented original work to compare observation, interpretation and suggestions; keep the maker's aims distinct from the critic's preferences. |
| BANK-079-038 | What does a useful art-study series hold constant and deliberately vary? | Plan original studies of light direction, material or composition with recorded choices; avoid equating repetition or volume with guaranteed improvement. |
| BANK-079-039 | How can an artist learn from a masterwork without presenting a study as an original invention? | Verify reference rights, label the study and distinguish copied structure from new decisions; avoid suggesting attribution alone permits every commercial use. |
| BANK-079-040 | Why can a portfolio benefit from process evidence rather than only polished final images? | Compare selected drafts, revisions and personal contributions for a defined audience; avoid exposing client material without authorization. |
