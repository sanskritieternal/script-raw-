# Delivery plan and execution checklist
## Deliverables and editorial specifications
Current deliverable is a source-backed text/scene/prompt package prepared for external AI generation. Proposed final video target: 600 seconds, 1920×1080, 16:9, 24 fps. Proposed review export: MP4/H.264 with stereo AAC at 48 kHz; preserve a high-quality master as chosen in the actual editing workflow. These are project choices; check the chosen generator/editor and platform before execution.

Supply a separate EN transcript, timed SRT captions after recording/edit, thumbnail, final metadata and source list. Optional short-form versions need their own truthful framing and reviewed captions.

## Pre-generation text checks
Configuration locks, full narration, synthetic quantities, source limitations, continuity, beat/chapter plan and planned asset identities are specified. Phase 3 still must instantiate scenes, cite actual factual wording, validate timing and supply concrete prompts. Phase 4 must compile a current package; Phase 5 must audit the resulting text.

## Final export checks — Pending actual media
- Measure narration fit for each scene; revise and log overruns.
- Inspect every generated visual for misleading evidence, identity/shape drift and incorrect text.
- Render exact overlays and verify fractions, order and source labels.
- Align captions to actual speech, review author names and mathematical phrasing, and check readability.
- Measure duration, frame rate, dimensions, audio loudness, clipping and final synchronization.
- Inspect thumbnail, cards, chapters and metadata against the actual edit.
- Confirm actual rights/provenance and platform disclosure decisions.

## Release gate
No export, upload, license clearance or publication has occurred. CONFIG human review stays Pending. Release requires the actual reviewers, their evidence and all applicable checks, including media checks after the user generates it. Planning completion cannot certify unseen media.
