# YouTube editorial strategy
Purpose: a standalone ten-minute educational episode in the proposed “Reading an AI claim” series. Use the specific Transformer mechanism and the labeled synthetic example as the viewer promise. Follow YOUTUBE_METADATA.md for proposed copy.

Avoid a universal “AI thinks” headline or a claim that research has settled every interpretation question. Introduce the question promptly, show the source and example boundaries, and place chapter changes at planned scene boundaries.

No reach, retention, ranking or monetization performance is predicted. The title, thumbnail and description should describe the actual episode. Tags are a small descriptive aid; [YouTube's tag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en), checked 2026-10-18, emphasizes the main metadata over tags.

Short-form adaptations remain optional and separate. Use a self-contained explanation of the synthetic example with its label and source context; do not clip away an interpretation caveat. No Shorts/Reels file or upload is part of Phase 2.
