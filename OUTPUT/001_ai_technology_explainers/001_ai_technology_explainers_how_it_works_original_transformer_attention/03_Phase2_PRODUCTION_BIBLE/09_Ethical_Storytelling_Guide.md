# Ethical storytelling decisions
Represent the original design as a specific historical architecture. Represent each paper's conclusions at the inspected scope. Attribution follows the research position throughout the narration and metadata.

Synthetic examples have persistent labels; authorial teaching choices are not observations. Never fabricate a learned attention heatmap, laboratory evidence, researcher quotation, interview, endorsement or archival scene.

The explanation debate is presented through C009 and C010 together. Use paired text cards with equivalent visual emphasis, while preserving their different evidence/inspection scope. This is a contextual comparison, not a staged adversarial encounter.

Educational questions in the narration invite inspection. They do not measure comprehension or promise that the audience now understands all Transformer systems. Content revisions that change the conclusion reopen the research gate.

After generation, inspect whether visual style implies claims absent from narration. Replace misleading footage rather than relying on a fine-print disclaimer. Record revisions and retain original evidence.
