# Visual style — Futuristic Tech Editorial
## Design system
Use a restrained dark editorial workspace, charcoal #111827 background, off-white #F8FAFC text and generous empty space. A is cyan #22D3EE with a square; B is amber #FBBF24 with a circle; C is magenta #E879F9 with a triangle. Shapes, labels and placement redundantly identify positions; color alone never carries a distinction.

Plan a 1920×1080, 16:9 composition. Keep important elements inside a 96-pixel margin. Main labels target 48 pixels or larger; citations target 30 pixels or larger, with an enlarged source card when needed. These are creative layout targets, subject to actual readability review.

## Layers
AI-generated material supplies abstract atmospheric surfaces or motion. Exact vector cards, diagram arrows, fractions and source labels are editorial layers. Use a clean, largely static board for arithmetic. Keep a persistent “Synthetic example” ribbon during cues 7–9 and 18–50.

Distinguish query card, keys, weights, values and combined vector through both position and labels. The score-to-weight transition changes the header explicitly. Maintain A/B/C order. An excluded C is hatched and marked “Excluded,” retaining its place.

Source cards use plain typographic attribution without logos, author portraits, fabricated paper screenshots or signatures. Avoid brain imagery, real-looking laboratories, science-fiction interfaces and invented empirical heatmaps.

## Continuity
Use WORLD-01 and BOARD-01 throughout. Only the active operation moves; hold other values stable. End the masked sequence with both results separately labeled. All artwork remains planned.
