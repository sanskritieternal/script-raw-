# Music, sound and silence
## Mix intent
Use clear speech as the foreground. A restrained non-vocal ambient bed may support section transitions. Prefer silence during the densest arithmetic. Minimal soft clicks can mark a reveal; use no dramatic alarms or “discovery” stingers implying empirical revelation.

## Targets and monitoring
Editorial mix targets: stereo master around -16 LUFS integrated and true peak no higher than -1 dBTP, subject to actual listening and loudness measurement after generation/edit. These are chosen production targets, not a statement of platform requirements. Duck the optional music under speech and check voice intelligibility on headphones, small speakers and mono playback.

No continuous music is required. Leave short silent holds for reading. Do not hide rushed speech under a louder mix.
