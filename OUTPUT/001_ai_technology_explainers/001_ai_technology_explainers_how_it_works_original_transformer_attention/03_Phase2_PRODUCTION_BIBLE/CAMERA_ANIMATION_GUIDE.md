# Camera and animation plan
## Framing
Use locked overhead BOARD-01 framing for numerical steps. Place cards on a stable left-to-right grid with one reserved lower band for captions and one source-label area. Architecture overviews may use slow controlled reframing; use a wide reset before each new section.

## Transition and continuity
Prefer cuts, short dissolves or single-element reveals. End each shot with a readable hold. During arithmetic, the camera stays fixed while only the active coefficient, vector or result changes. Keep prior contributions visible when a sum is being built.

Preserve A/B/C positions and exact order. For masking, hatch C before normalization updates and retain distinct unmasked/masked result labels. No flashes, rapid zooms, spinning equations or unlabeled scale changes.

## Generation instructions
Prompts specify a start state, one movement and end state for each scene. Generated backdrops remain text-free; precise overlays are handled separately. Reference WORLD-01/BOARD-01 and the asset IDs. Choose provider-specific syntax after inspecting that tool's current capabilities. No universal provider-duration or seed behavior is assumed.

Review actual generated continuity and motion before calling any asset reviewed. Phase 2 completes the plan, not the footage.
