## Gap Notes

- The transition from the single Devi narrative (Devi Mahatmya) to the nine-forms system is not precisely dated. Scholars suggest 7th-10th century CE but exact date is unknown.
- Animal sacrifice in Shakta traditions: difficult to date precisely; likely has ancient roots but the practice has diminished significantly in modern times.
- Exact origin date of Garba dance tradition is not precisely recorded; estimated at 500+ years based on folk history.
