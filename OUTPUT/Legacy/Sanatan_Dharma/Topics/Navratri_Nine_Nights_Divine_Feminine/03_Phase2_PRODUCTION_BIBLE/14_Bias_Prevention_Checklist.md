# Bias Prevention Checklist

## Fairness Verification Checks

### Check 1: Regional Representation

| Question | Standard | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Are all major Navratri traditions represented? | Bengal (Durga Puja), Gujarat (Garba), South (Golu), North (Ramlila/Fasting) | ✓ All covered |
| Is one tradition given more weight than others? | No single tradition should dominate | ✓ Equal coverage |
| Are regional practices described in their own terms? | Yes — no homogenization | ✓ Respectful framing |

### Check 2: Mythological Fairness

| Question | Standard | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Are the Devas portrayed as helpless? | Acknowledge their defeat but frame as boon-given, not incompetence | ✓ Balanced |

| Question | Standard | Result |
|---|---|---|

### Check 4: Historical Accuracy vs. Devotional Belief

| Question | Standard | Result |
|---|---|---|

| Question | Standard | Result |
