# Visual Style Guide

## Project: Navratri — The Nine Nights of the Divine Feminine

---

## Primary Visual Style

**Style:** Sacred Cinematic Devotional + Animation Hybrid
**Niche ID:** 275 — Shaktism
**Base:** Cinematic Editorial Documentary with Sacred/Epic overlay

---

## Lighting

- **Sacred sequences:** Soft sunrise/sunset glow, volumetric rays through temple architecture, warm divine light emanating from deity forms
- **Mythological animations:** Dramatic lighting — dark backgrounds with luminous deity figures, contrast between darkness and divine light
- **Real-world celebration footage:** Natural ambient light, warm practical lighting from lamps/diyas
- **Modern/diaspora sequences:** Soft cinematic contrast with motivated practicals

---

## Camera & Lens

| Sequence Type | Lens | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Temple/deity establishing | 24mm grandeur | Slow push-in |
| Devotional portraits (deity faces) | 50mm devotional portrait | Slow push-in, hold |
| Sacred object/mandala details | Macro | Static or very slow |
| Real celebration B-roll | 35mm storytelling | Handheld subtle movement |
| Animated sequences | Cinematic wide | Pan, parallax, motivated tracking |

## Scene-by-Scene Style Notes

### Act 1 (Scenes 1-20): Cosmic Battle
- Dark cosmic backgrounds with luminous Devi manifestation
- Mahishasura: shadowy, imposing figures
- Durga: radiant, multi-armed, riding lion
- Weapons from each god glow with their respective energies
- Transition: darkness → light (Devi's arrival)

### Act 2 (Scenes 21-60): Nine Nights
- Each of the 9 forms gets a dedicated color accent:
  1. Shailaputri — Green (nature, mountain)
  2. Brahmacharini — White/Silver (purity, asceticism)
  3. Chandraghanta — Red/Pink (warrior, moon)
  4. Kushmanda — Orange (cosmic warmth, sun)
  5. Skandamata — Blue/Green (motherhood, Kartikeya)
  6. Katyayani — Red/Orange (warrior energy)
  7. Kalaratri — Black/Dark (destruction, fearlessness)
  8. Mahagauri — White/Pure (purity after penance)
  9. Siddhidatri — Purple/Gold (enlightenment, perfection)
- Mandala patterns, yantra overlays, sacred geometry
- Each form's iconography accurately rendered (mudras, weapons, mount)

### Act 3 (Scenes 61-80): Regional Celebrations
- **Bengal (Durga Puja):** Grand pandals, ornate idols, community crowds, vermilion-red atmosphere
- **Gujarat (Garba/Dandiya):** Vibrant colors, circular dance formations, energy and movement
- **South India (Golu):** Tiered doll displays, colorful kolu steps, domestic warmth
- **North India (Ramlila/Fasting):** Temple interiors, fasting preparation, Jaagran atmosphere

### Act 4 (Scenes 81-100): Philosophy & Modern
- Kundalini imagery: chakra overlays, energy rising
- Mandala animations: geometric meditation patterns
- Modern celebration: professional event coverage aesthetic
- Diaspora: clean modern aesthetic with traditional elements

### Act 5 (Scenes 101-108): Closing
- Full Devi mandala reveal
- Aarti flame close-up (macro)
- Jai Mata Di text with saffron/gold palette
- Fade to warm divine light

## Transitions

- Between Navadurga forms: dissolve through matching color
- Between mythological and modern: ripple effect (sacred energy → modern world)
- Between regional sections: pan across India map
- Scene-to-scene: gentle dissolve (no hard cuts)

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Render deity forms with traditional iconography | Distort or disrespect deity representations |
| Use warm divine lighting | Use cold/clinical lighting for sacred sequences |
| Include authentic ritual elements | Show incorrect puja practices |
| Animate mythological battles with grandeur | Use cartoonish or comedic style |
| Show real Navratri celebrations with authenticity | Stage inauthentic celebrations |
| Use traditional Indian art influences | Use generic fantasy elements |
