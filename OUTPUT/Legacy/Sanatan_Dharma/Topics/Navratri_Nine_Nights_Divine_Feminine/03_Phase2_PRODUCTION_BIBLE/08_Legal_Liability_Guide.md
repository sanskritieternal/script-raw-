## Risk Areas

### 1. Religious Representation

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Misrepresentation of traditions | Different Shakta traditions may interpret narratives differently | Present the mainstream narrative; acknowledge regional variations; include disclaimer |
| Animal sacrifice reference | Some Shakta traditions historically practice animal sacrifice | Present historically and note that many modern temples have replaced this with symbolic offerings |
| Inter-religious sensitivity | Other faiths may have different views on Hindu deities | Maintain respectful, educational tone throughout |

### 2. Scriptural Accuracy

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Misquoting scriptures | Incorrect references could be seen as disrespectful | All scriptural references verified against primary texts and academic translations |
| Sanskrit errors | Incorrect transliteration or pronunciation | Verified against standard transliteration standards |
| Interpretation vs. fact | Presenting interpretation as scripture | Clearly distinguish between scriptural narrative and scholarly interpretation |
