# Monetization Strategy

## Project: Navratri — The Nine Nights of the Divine Feminine

---

## Revenue Model

**Primary:** AdSense (YouTube)
**Secondary:** Sponsorship
**Tertiary:** Channel growth (audience building for seasonal content)

---

## AdSense Strategy

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| Content type | Devotional/educational — high watch time potential |
| Audience | India-first (high CPM for Hindi devotional content on YouTube India) |
| Watch time target | 10-minute video = high average view duration |
| Optimization | Pre-roll ads at 0:00 and mid-roll at 5:00 |
| CPM estimate | $2-5 (India devotional niche) |

---

## Sponsorship

| Integration Point | Sponsor Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Before video | Intro sponsor read | Spiritual/wellness brand (yoga mat, meditation app) |
| No mid-video sponsorship | Preserves devotional tone | — |
| Description | Affiliate links (if applicable) | Religious books, spiritual products |

**Sponsorship Policy:** Minimal and carefully chosen. Only sponsors relevant to the devotional/spiritual space. No disruptive mid-video reads.

---

## Merchandise (Channel Level, Not Per-Video)

## Channel Growth Strategy

| Strategy | Detail |
|---|---|
| Seasonal content | New Navratri video each year (evergreen + timely) |
| Series potential | Expand to Dussehra, Diwali, other Hindu festivals |
| Shorts pipeline | 5 Shorts per video for algorithm reach |
