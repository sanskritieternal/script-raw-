## Consistency Rules

### Visual Consistency
- Color palette: Saffron, gold, vermilion, deep indigo
- Lighting: Soft sunrise glow, volumetric rays, divine warm lighting
- Camera: 24mm grandeur + 50mm devotional portrait + macro details
- Motion: Slow push-ins, parallax, motivated tracking
- Sacred sequences: Animated with reverent composition
- Modern sequences: Cinematic editorial documentary style
- **NO:** Disrespectful depictions, modern props, excessive neon, generic stock imagery

### Voiceover Consistency
- Narration always in Hindi (Devanagari)
- Sanskrit terms: transliterated and explained naturally
- Tone: Devotional reverence + narrative momentum
- Pacing: Deliberate for mythological passages, energetic for festival sequences
- **NO:** Casual slang, rushed delivery, disrespectful tone toward the subject matter

### Content Consistency
- All facts must trace back to Facts_Documented.md or Source_List_with_Links.md
- No placeholders (NO [TBD], [TODO], [PLACEHOLDER])
- All 9 forms of Durga presented in correct sequence
- Regional variations presented respectfully
- Both mythological narrative AND symbolic/philosophical interpretation included
