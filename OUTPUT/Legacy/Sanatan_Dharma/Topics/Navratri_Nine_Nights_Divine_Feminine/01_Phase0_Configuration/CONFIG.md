## Story Direction Notes

**Narrative Arc:**
1. Opening: The cosmic battle between Devī and Mahiṣāsura — why Navratri exists
2. Nine Nights: Each night honors one of the 9 forms of Durgā (Navadurga)
   - Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri, Siddhidatri
3. Regional Variations: Durga Puja (Bengal), Golu (South India), Dandiya/Garba (Gujarat)
4. Symbolism: Good vs Evil, inner transformation, Śakti worship
5. Closing: Jai Mātā Dī — the timeless message of the Divine Feminine

**Visual Direction:**
- Sacred cinematic: temple architecture, warm divine glow, saffron/gold/vermilion palette
- Animated sequences: Devi's battle with Mahishasura, each of the 9 forms
- B-roll: real Navratri celebrations, pandals, garba/dandiya, kumari puja
- Text overlays: Devi names, mantras, key dates

**Voiceover Tone:**
- Reflective yet engaging — devotional reverence with narrative momentum
- Mix of Sanskrit terms with accessible English/Hindi explanation
- Pacing: deliberate for mythological passages, energetic for festival sequences
