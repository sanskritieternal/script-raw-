# Visual Style Guide: Maharana Pratap

**Project:** Maharana Pratap Historical Documentary
**Selected Style:** Ultra Realistic Historical Documentary
**Base Style:** Rajput Warrior Epic + BBC Documentary + IMAX Documentary + Historical Reconstruction

---

## Rendering Quality

- **Resolution:** 8K Ultra HD, IMAX-compatible
- **Detail Level:** Hyper detailed, museum-grade historical reconstruction
- **Quality Standard:** Production-ready, documentary film quality
- **AI Style:** Photorealistic, physically accurate, no AI artifacts

## Material and Texture

- **Fabrics:** Authentic silk, cotton, wool of 16th century India — realistic drape and wear
- **Stone:** Weathered sandstone, granite — authentic to Mewar architecture
- **Metal:** Polished steel armor, worn leather, tarnished brass — realistic aging
- **Skin:** Natural skin texture, weathered from outdoor life, realistic pores and imperfections
- **Hair:** Individual strands visible, realistic texture, natural movement in wind
- **Weapons:** Polished steel blades, worn hilts, blood stains where appropriate

## Lighting Philosophy

- **Primary:** Natural sunlight — golden hour for emotional scenes, harsh midday for battle
- **Secondary:** Firelight — torches, lamps, cooking fires for night scenes
- **Mood Lighting:** Volumetric lighting for dramatic moments (sun through dust, light through fort windows)
- **Color Temperature:** Warm for emotional/royal scenes, cool for battle/mountain scenes
- **Shadows:** Deep, natural shadows from architecture and terrain
- **No artificial/modern lighting** — only fire, sunlight, moonlight

## Camera Philosophy

- **Style:** Handheld documentary feel mixed with cinematic compositions
- **Movement:** Slow push forward, slow pull back, slow pan, tracking shots, drone establishing shots
- **Pacing:** Camera supports story — slow for emotional moments, dynamic for battle
- **Composition:** Rule of thirds, natural framing through architecture and terrain
- **Shot Types:**
  - Establishing: Wide drone shots of forts, landscapes
  - Character: Medium shots, close-ups for emotional moments
  - Battle: Wide shots for scale, close-ups for intensity
  - Detail: Close-ups of weapons, jewelry, artifacts
- **No excessive camera movement** — documentary restraint

## Color Philosophy

- **Palette:** Warm earth tones (saffron, ochre, brown, gold)
- **Accents:** Saffron orange (Mewar's color), deep blue (royalty), red (passion/war)
- **Battle Scenes:** Desaturated, dusty, warm browns
- **Royal Scenes:** Rich, saturated golds, deep reds, royal blues
- **Night Scenes:** Cool blues, warm firelight orange
- **Forest Scenes:** Deep greens, earth browns, dappled light
- **Avoid:** Bright modern colors, oversaturation, neon tones

## Character Design Language

- **Style:** Hyper realistic, historically grounded, no exaggeration
- **Facial Features:** Authentic Rajput features — strong jawlines, high cheekbones, dark eyes
- **Body Types:** Varied but realistic — warriors athletic, royals imposing, common people varied
- **Aging:** Natural aging — wrinkles, gray hair, weathered skin
- **Battle Effects:** Realistic wounds, blood, dirt, exhaustion
- **No idealization** — real human faces with imperfections

## Environment Design Language

- **Architecture:** Authentic 16th century Rajput architecture — stone forts, palaces, temples
- **Landscape:** Aravalli mountains — rocky, ancient, green, realistic geology
- **Vegetation:** Native Rajasthan flora — acacia, cactus, dry forest, seasonal changes
- **Weather:** Realistic weather patterns — monsoon rains, dust storms, winter cold, summer heat
- **Animals:** Authentic Indian wildlife — Marwari horses, peacocks, eagles, monkeys
- **No anachronisms** — no modern plants, buildings, or landscape changes

## Costume Language

- **Royal:** Rich fabrics, gold embroidery, precious stones, heavy jewelry
- **Warrior:** Functional armor, leather, steel, practical for combat
- **Common People:** Simple cotton, wool, practical garments
- **Seasonal:** Summer — light cottons; Winter — woolen cloaks; Rainy — protection from monsoons
- **Battle:** Worn armor, dirt, blood stains, torn fabric
- **Consistency:** Exact same costumes throughout — Pratap's saffron angarkha always the same

## Visual Storytelling Style

- **Approach:** Show, don't tell — visual narrative carries the story
- **Emotional Language:** Close-ups for grief, wide shots for scale, slow motion for key moments
- **Symbolism:** Saffron flag, sun emblem, mountain landscapes, horses
- **Transitions:** Natural — scene flows to scene, no jarring cuts
- **Pacing:** Slow down for key moments (Haldighati, Chittorgarh fall), move faster through transitions

## Mood and Atmosphere

- **Heroic:** Warm golden light, proud compositions, powerful music
- **Tragic:** Cool blue tones, somber lighting, sparse composition
- **Battle:** Dusty, chaotic, warm browns, dynamic camera
- **Spiritual:** Soft light, temple architecture, meditative pace
- **Victory:** Bright sunlight, wide celebrations, golden hour
- **Loss:** Dark shadows, close-ups of faces, quiet moments
