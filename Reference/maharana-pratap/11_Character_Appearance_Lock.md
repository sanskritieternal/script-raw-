# Character Appearance Lock: Maharana Pratap

**Purpose:** This document locks character appearances. No changes allowed after approval except age progression, battle scars, and story-driven injuries.

---

## LOCKED CHARACTERS

### 1. Maharana Pratap Singh I — LOCKED

**Face:**
- Strong Rajput features: high cheekbones, defined jawline, strong chin
- Dark, intense eyes — piercing, determined gaze
- Dark eyebrows — thick, slightly furrowed
- Nose: Straight, prominent
- Ears: Standard, sometimes with small gold stud
- **Battle Scar:** Left cheek — diagonal scar from Haldighati battle (age 36 onward)

**Hair:**
- Long, dark, straight/curly
- Always tied in traditional Rajput sikha (top knot)
- Forehead exposed with chandbindi
- Never cut, always long

**Beard:**
- Thick, dark, well-groomed Rajput beard and mustache
- Slightly longer as he ages
- Never clean-shaven
- Graying slightly in final years

**Body:**
- Height: Tall (~5'10" to 6'0")
- Build: Athletic, muscular — warrior physique
- Posture: Upright, regal, always proud
- Hands: Calloused from weapons, strong grip

**Skin:**
- Sun-kissed complexion (outdoor life in Aravalli)
- Natural texture — pores, imperfections
- Weathering: More weathered in guerrilla phase, cleaner in royal settings
- Never unnaturally smooth or light

**Expression Defaults:**
- Neutral: Calm, composed, watchful
- Battle: Intense, fierce, focused
- Grief: Heavy eyes, set jaw, distant gaze
- Royal: Noble, proud, serene
- Happy: Rare — warm smile with family

**What CANNOT change:**
- Face shape
- Eye color/shape
- Nose shape
- Jawline
- Ear shape
- Hair texture (always long, dark)

**What CAN change:**
- Facial hair (graying with age)
- Skin (more weathered in guerrilla phase)
- Scars (battle damage accumulates)
- Body (ages naturally)

## CONSISTENCY NOTES

### Age Progression
- Pratap: Young warrior (20s) → Mature king (30s) → Experienced leader (40s-50s) → Elder statesman (50s+)
- Ajabde: Young bride (teens) → Queen (20s-40s) → Matriarch (50s)
- Amar Singh: Child (under 10) → Young prince (teens) → Warrior (20s+) → King (30s+)

### Battle Damage
- Pratap: Left cheek scar from Haldighati (age 36+) — NEVER disappears
- Armor: Shows wear, dents, bloodstains in battle sequences — resets for non-battle scenes
- Weapons: Polished in court, worn in battle

### Seasonal Changes
- Summer: Light cottons, exposed arms
- Winter: Woolen cloaks, covered
- Rainy: Protection from monsoon

### Costume Identity
- Pratap's saffron angarkha: SAME garment throughout — same shade, same cut, same gold armlets
- Royal turban with sarpech: Always present in formal/royal/battle scenes
- Chetak's saddle: Always the same royal saddle with sun emblem
