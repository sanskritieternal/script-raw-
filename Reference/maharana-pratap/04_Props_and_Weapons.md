# Props and Weapons: Maharana Pratap

---

## Weapons

**1. Rajput Talwar (Sword)**
- Description: Curved single-edged sword, steel blade, ornate hilt with Rajput motifs
- Length: ~75-85 cm blade
- Weight: ~1-1.2 kg
- Used by: All Rajput warriors, especially Pratap
- Scenes: Battle scenes, close combat, symbolic scenes
- Consistency: Always curved, always steel, ornate Rajput hilt

**2. Rajput Shield (Dhal)**
- Description: Circular or oval steel shield, painted with Mewar's sun emblem (Suryavansh symbol)
- Diameter: ~45-55 cm
- Weight: ~3-4 kg
- Used by: Pratap, Jhala Man, all Mewar warriors
- Scenes: Battle scenes, protective scenes
- Consistency: Always has sun emblem, always steel, circular shape

**3. Rajput Spear (Bhala)**
- Description: Long wooden shaft with steel spearhead
- Length: ~2.5-3 meters
- Used by: Mewar cavalry and infantry
- Scenes: Cavalry charges, infantry formations
- Consistency: Long shaft, steel head

**4. Composite Bow**
- Description: Rajput composite bow (made of horn, wood, sinew), shorter than traditional longbow
- Length: ~1.2-1.5 meters
- Used by: Mounted archers, Bhil warriors
- Scenes: Archery scenes, ambushes, battlefield
- Consistency: Compact, powerful, Rajput style

**5. Mughal Matchlock Musket (Banduq)**
- Description: Early firearm, used by Mughal forces at Haldighati
- Used by: Mughal army (not Mewar)
- Scenes: Battle of Haldighati, Mughal camp scenes
- Consistency: Old-style musket, bulky, with matchlock mechanism

**6. Mughal Cannons**
- Description: Heavy artillery pieces used by Mughal army
- Used by: Mughal forces at Haldighati
- Scenes: Battle of Haldighati — artillery barrage
- Consistency: Heavy, bronze/iron, 16th century style

**7. Chakram (Steel Disc)**
- Description: Circular steel throwing weapon
- Used by: Rajput warriors
- Scenes: Battle scenes, close combat
- Consistency: Circular, steel, thrown weapon

## Armor

**1. Mewar Steel Plate Armor**
- Description: Full or partial plate armor, steel construction, Rajput decorative motifs
- Components: Breastplate, backplate, arm guards, leg guards, chainmail underneath
- Used by: Pratap, royal family, high-ranking warriors
- Scenes: Battle scenes, royal appearances
- Consistency: Steel, ornate Rajput design, worn over chainmail

**2. Chainmail (Zirah)**
- Description: Interlocking steel rings, flexible armor
- Used by: Most warriors
- Scenes: Battle scenes, preparation scenes
- Consistency: Steel, interlocking rings

**3. Steel Helmet (Top)**
- Description: Conical steel helmet with Rajput crest
- Used by: All warriors
- Scenes: Battle scenes
- Consistency: Steel, conical shape, Rajput crest

**4. Mughal Turban Helmet**
- Description: Heavy turban with armor plates underneath
- Used by: Mughal forces
- Scenes: Battle of Haldighati, Mughal camp
- Consistency: Heavy turban with embedded armor

---

## Royal Regalia

**1. Royal Turban (Saffron)**
- Description: Saffron-colored turban with jeweled aigrette (sarpech) and kalangi
- Used by: Pratap, royal family
- Scenes: Coronation, royal audiences, battle (Pratap)
- Consistency: Saffron color, jeweled ornaments

**2. Royal Signet Ring**
- Description: Gold ring with Mewar's royal seal
- Used by: Pratap
- Scenes: Royal decrees, sealing documents
- Consistency: Gold, royal seal

**3. Royal Sword Sheath**
- Description: Ornate leather/metal sheath for Pratap's talwar
- Used by: Pratap
- Scenes: Drawing sword, battle preparation
- Consistency: Ornate, matching sword design

## Vehicles and Animals

**1. Chetak (Marwari Horse)**
- Description: White/gray Marwari war horse, ~14 hands high, lean muscular build
- Saddle: Royal saddle with Mewar sun emblem, saffron cloth
- Bridle: Traditional Rajput style with silver ornaments
- Significance: Pratap's legendary horse
- Scenes: Mounted scenes, Battle of Haldighati, chase scenes
- Consistency: Always white/gray, always with royal saddle, distinctive Marwari ears (slightly curved)

**2. War Elephants**
- Description: Large Asian elephants, armored with howdahs (platforms) on back
- Used by: Limited use in Mewar army, more common in Mughal army
- Scenes: Battle scenes, processions
- Consistency: Asian elephant, armor, howdah with archers

**3. Camels**
- Description: Transport animals for supplies
- Used by: Supply caravans, desert travel
- Scenes: Travel sequences, supply scenes
- Consistency: Single-hump camel, loaded with supplies

## Banners and Flags

**1. Mewar Royal Banner (Bhagwa Dhwaj)**
- Description: Saffron flag with sun emblem (Suryavansh symbol)
- Used by: Mewar army, forts, royal camp
- Scenes: Battle scenes, fort scenes, royal processions
- Consistency: Saffron color, sun emblem, fabric material

**2. Mughal Imperial Standard**
- Description: Mughal alam (standard) — black banner with Islamic inscriptions
- Used by: Mughal army
- Scenes: Battle of Haldighati, Mughal camp
- Consistency: Black color, Islamic inscriptions

**3. Clan Flags**
- Description: Various Rajput clan banners at Haldighati
- Used by: Different Rajput contingents in Mughal army
- Scenes: Battle formations
- Consistency: Different colors/symbols per clan

## Daily Life Props

**1. Earthen Pot (Matka)**
- Description: Clay water pot
- Used by: Pratap during guerrilla phase, villagers
- Scenes: Guerrilla camp, village scenes
- Consistency: Clay, simple, 16th century style

**2. Grass Bread (Bajra Roti)**
- Description: Flatbread made from millet (what Pratap reportedly ate during hard times)
- Used by: Pratap during guerrilla phase
- Scenes: Poverty/hardship scenes
- Consistency: Simple flatbread, brown color

**3. Royal Throne (Singhasan)**
- Description: Ornate royal seat, elevated platform with cushions
- Used by: Pratap, Udai Singh II for royal audiences
- Scenes: Coronation, court scenes, audiences
- Consistency: Elevated, ornate, royal materials

**4. Lamp (Diya)**
- Description: Oil lamp, clay or brass
- Used by: Temple scenes, palace scenes, night scenes
- Scenes: Temple prayers, night palace scenes
- Consistency: Oil-fed, flame, traditional design

**5. Bow and Arrow**
- Description: Traditional Rajput bow and arrow set
- Used by: Bhil warriors, Pratap in practice scenes
- Scenes: Archery practice, hunting, battle
- Consistency: Wooden bow, metal-tipped arrows

**1. Shiva Lingam**
- Description: Stone representation of Lord Shiva, at Eklingji temple
- Used by: Pratap's prayers, temple scenes
- Scenes: Spiritual moments, prayer scenes
- Consistency: Stone, traditional shape

**2. Temple Bell (Ghanti)**
- Description: Brass bell at temple entrance
- Used by: Temple scenes
- Scenes: Prayer, arrival at temple
- Consistency: Brass, traditional shape

**3. Puja Thali**
- Description: Brass plate with offerings
- Used by: Pratap during prayers
- Scenes: Spiritual moments
- Consistency: Brass, traditional offerings
