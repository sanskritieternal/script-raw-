# Master Memory - DO NOT FORGET

**Main Character:** Maharana Pratap Singh I
  - Current Age: 56 (at death), story spans 20 to 56
  - Current Kingdom/Location: Mewar → Chavand (final capital)
  - Current Allies: Bhil and Gaur tribes (most loyal), Afghan contingent (Hakim Khan Sur), family (Ajabde, Amar Singh, Bhamashah)
  - Current Enemies: Emperor Akbar, Mughal army (led by Man Singh I)
  - Current Emotional State: From determined youth → grieving crown prince → heroic warrior → resilient survivor → peaceful king → dying legacy
  - Current Costume: Saffron angarkha tunic, white dhoti, gold armlets, steel armor, royal saffron turban with jeweled sarpech
  - Current Weapon: Talwar (Rajput sword), steel shield with sun emblem, spear (bhala)
  - Current Horse: Chetak (died at Haldighati — subsequent horses not as legendary)

**Political Situation:**
- Mewar is the ONLY Rajput kingdom never formally submitted to the Mughals
- Akbar at height of his power — most Rajput kingdoms are Mughal allies
- Political isolation of Mewar — no major allies against Mughals
- Guerrilla warfare ongoing — Mewar survives through resistance, not pitched battles
- Mewar's identity built on freedom (swatantrata) above all

**Current Season:** Varies throughout story
- Early scenes: Summer/spring (birth, childhood, coronation)
- Haldighati: June (monsoon approaching)
- Guerrilla phase: Variable — monsoon (hardship), winter (cold), summer (heat)
- Final years: Peaceful seasons at Chavand

**Current Weather:** Varies by scene
- Mountain scenes: Cool morning light, mountain wind
- Battle scenes: Dust, heat, smoke
- Forest scenes: Dappled light, humidity, rain
- Palace scenes: Warm interior, natural window light

**Recent Events:**
- Birth at Kumbhalgarh
- Chittorgarh's fall — transformed Pratap's life
- Coronation at Gogunda
- Six rejected diplomatic missions (1573-1575)
- Battle of Haldighati (June 18, 1576) — Pratap wounded, Chetak dies
- Retreat to Aravalli forests — guerrilla warfare begins

**Upcoming Events:**
- Akbar's second invasion
- Battle of Dewair — 36 Mughal outposts captured
- Recovery of Mewar territory (1583-1587)
- Move to Chavand as capital
- Final years of rebuilding (1585-1597)
- Death at Chavand (January 19, 1597)
