# Character Relationships and Timeline: Maharana Pratap

---

## Family Relationships

**Maharana Pratap Singh I** (1540-1597)
  - Father: **Maharana Udai Singh II** (1522-1572) — Died when Pratap was 32
  - Mother: **Maharani Jaiwanta Bai** (Parmar princess)
  - Brother: **Jagmal Singh** — Joined Akbar, later fell out of favor
  - Sister: (Records limited)
  - Wife: **Maharani Ajabde Punwar** (married ~1555, age ~15) — Most beloved wife
  - Son: **Crown Prince Amar Singh I** (~1559-1620) — Successor
  - Son: **Prince Bhagwan Das** — Died in battle
  - Son: **Prince Shekha** — Died in battle
  - Son: Other sons — Several died young or in battle
  - Daughters: Married to Rajput nobles

**Maharana Udai Singh II**
  - Father: **Rana Sanga** (died 1528) — Legendary warrior
  - Mother: **Maharani Maharani**
  - Son: **Maharana Pratap** (1540-1597)
  - Son: **Jagmal Singh**
  - Founded Udaipur (~1559)

**Maharani Ajabde Punwar**
  - Father: **Rao Akheraj Punwar** of Peepalow
  - Mother: (Records limited)
  - Husband: Maharana Pratap
  - Children: Sons of Pratap (including Amar Singh)

**Crown Prince Amar Singh I**
  - Father: Maharana Pratap
  - Mother: Maharani Ajabde Punwar (likely)
  - Succeeded Pratap as Maharana (1597-1620)
  - Eventually accepted treaty with Jahangir

## Political Relationships

**Maharana Pratap ↔ Emperor Akbar**
- Relationship: Adversaries, mutual respect
- Nature: Akbar wanted Pratap's submission; Pratap refused
- Key Events: Six diplomatic missions, Battle of Haldighati, continued warfare
- Evolution: Akbar's frustration → grudging respect after Pratap's death

**Maharana Pratap ↔ Man Singh I**
- Relationship: Enemies (both Rajputs, fought at Haldighati)
- Nature: Political division — Man Singh chose Mughal alliance
- Key Event: Battle of Haldighati (Man Singh led Mughal army)
- Evolution: Personal respect despite opposing sides

**Maharana Pratap ↔ Jagmal Singh**
- Relationship: Brother, turned political enemy
- Nature: Jagmal joined Akbar after Udai Singh's death
- Key Event: Political split of the royal family
- Evolution: Reconciliation (traditional account) or permanent estrangement (academic view)

**Maharana Pratap ↔ Bhil Tribes**
- Relationship: Allies, deepest loyalty
- Nature: Pratap's most reliable fighters during guerrilla phase
- Key Events: Guerrilla warfare together, recovery of forts
- Evolution: Lifelong alliance

**Maharana Pratap ↔ Hakim Khan Sur**
- Relationship: Allies
- Nature: Afghan noble who fought against Mughals
- Key Event: Led Afghan contingent at Haldighati
- Evolution: Continued alliance during guerrilla war

## Chronological Timeline

### Phase 1: Birth and Childhood (1540-1556)
- May 9, 1540: Born at Kumbhalgarh Fort
- 1540-1556: Childhood at Kumbhalgarh, early education
- 1556: Udai Singh II crowned Maharana

### Phase 2: Crown Prince (1556-1572)
- 1556: Udai Singh II becomes Maharana
- 1560s: Pratap trained in warfare, horsemanship, administration
- 1567-1568: Akbar besieges Chittorgarh
- Feb 23, 1568: Fall of Chittorgarh — 30,000+ Rajputs killed
- 1568-1572: Pratap grows as leader, vows to never submit

### Phase 3: Coronation and Diplomacy (1572-1576)
- Feb 28, 1572: Udai Singh II dies — Pratap crowned
- 1572: Coronation at Gogunda
- 1573-1575: Six diplomatic missions from Akbar — all rejected
- 1573-1575: Preparation for war, army building
- 1576: Akbar marches on Mewar with Man Singh I

### Phase 4: Battle and Aftermath
- June 18, 1576: Battle of Haldighati
- Pratap wounded, escapes with Jhala Man's sacrifice
- Chetak carries Pratap to safety, dies shortly after
- Mughal forces occupy Gogunda but cannot hold Mewar

### Phase 5: Guerrilla Warfare (1576-1585)
- 1576-1577: Retreat to Aravalli forests, life in hiding
- Bhil allies join — guerrilla warfare begins
- 1577: Akbar's second invasion — Pratap retreats deeper
- 1578-1582: Hit-and-run tactics, cutting Mughal supply lines
- 1582: Battle of Dewair — 36 Mughal outposts captured
- 1583-1585: Gradual recovery of Mewar territory

### Phase 6: Recovery and Rebuilding (1585-1597)
- 1585-1587: Most Mewar territory recovered
- 1585: Capital moved to Chavand
- 1587: Administrative rebuilding begins
- 1588-1595: Sons grow into warriors, Mewar stabilizes
- 1596-1597: Final diplomatic negotiations

### Phase 7: Death and Legacy
- January 19, 1597: Maharana Pratap dies at Chavand, age 56
- Funeral rites performed with royal honors
- Crown Prince Amar Singh I succeeds
- Mewar continues resistance under Amar Singh
- 1615: Treaty with Jahangir — Mewar retains honor, Amar Singh accepted as vassal

## Political Alliance Map

**Akbar's Allies (at Haldighati):**
- Man Singh I (Raja of Amer) — Akbar's most trusted Rajput general
- Rai Singh (Raja of Bikaner) — Rajput in Mughal service
- Rajput forces from Marwar, Bikaner, Amer
- Mughal army: 5,000-10,000 soldiers

**Pratap's Allies:**
- Bhil tribal warriors — guerrilla support
- Hakim Khan Sur (Afghan contingent) — anti-Mughal
- Gaur tribe — forest support
- Rao Chandrasen Rathore (Marwar) — fellow resistor (indirect)
- Mewar's own army: 3,000-5,000

**Neutral/Other:**
- Most other Rajput kingdoms — accepted Mughal suzerainty
- Gujarat Sultanate — under Mughal control
- Deccan Sultanates — separate conflict with Mughals
