# Main Characters: Maharana Pratap

---

## Character 1: Maharana Pratap Singh I

**Role:** Protagonist — Maharana (King) of Mewar
**Age Range:** 20s to 50s (1540-1597 CE)
**Title:** Maharana of Mewar

**Physical Description:**
- Face: Strong Rajput features, high cheekbones, defined jawline, determined expression
- Eyes: Dark, intense, piercing gaze — conveys inner strength and resolve
- Hair: Long, dark, tied in traditional Rajput knot (sikha), with forehead band (chandbindi)
- Beard: Thick, dark, well-groomed Rajput beard and mustache
- Body Type: Tall, muscular, athletic build — trained warrior physique
- Skin Texture: Weathered skin from mountain life, sun-kissed complexion, battle scars
- Distinguishing Marks: Scar on cheek from Haldighati battle (age 36), calloused hands from weapons

**Clothing:**
- Royal attire: Deep saffron/ochre colored angarkha (tunic), white dhoti
- Armor: Steel plate armor with intricate Rajput designs, chainmail underneath, saffron turban with aigrette (sarpech/jeweled plume)
- Battle gear: Steel helmet (top), shield with sun emblem (Suryavansh symbol), talwar (sword) at waist
- Casual: Simple white/cream cotton garments during guerrilla phase
- Winter: Woolen shawl (chooli) over traditional garments

**Accessories:**
- Royal sword (talwar) — personal weapon, carried everywhere
- Shield — Mewar's sun emblem painted on it
- Royal signet ring
- Turban ornaments — jewelled aigrette and kalangi

**Weapons:**
- Primary: Two-handed Rajput talwar (sword) — named after the deity Ekling
- Secondary: Spear (bhala) for mounted combat
- Shield: Heavy steel shield with sun emblem
- Bows: Rajput composite bow for mounted archery

**Jewelry:**
- Heavy gold armlets (bajuband) — royal status symbol
- Necklace (haar) — traditional Rajput gold jewelry
- Earrings — gold jhumkas
- Turban ornaments — kalangi and sarpech with precious stones

**Personality:**
- Unyielding pride and honor — core identity
- Deeply spiritual — devoted to Eklingji
- Compassionate toward his people — shared their hardships
- Ruthless in war but respectful toward enemies
- Patient strategist — 11 years of guerrilla warfare proves long-term thinking
- Fiercely independent — refused all compromise on sovereignty

**Emotional Arc:**
- Phase 1 (Youth): Proud but idealistic — believes in Rajput glory
- Phase 2 (After Chittorgarh 1568): Grief and rage — Chittorgarh's fall transforms him
- Phase 3 (Coronation 1572): Determined resolve — commits to never submitting
- Phase 4 (Haldighati 1576): Heroic courage — leads from the front, wounded but unbowed
- Phase 5 (Guerrilla War): Resilient hope — endures hardship, slowly recovers territory
- Phase 6 (Final Years): Peaceful wisdom — achieves recovery, earns respect even from Mughals

**Expression Sheet:**
- Determined jaw, focused eyes — during war council
- Grief and anger — Chittorgarh aftermath
- Noble pride — addressing his people
- Quiet resolve — during long guerrilla campaigns
- Warm smile — with family and loyal followers
- Wrath — in battle
- Peaceful acceptance — final years at Chavand

## Character 2: Maharana Udai Singh II

**Role:** Supporting — Pratap's father, founder of Udaipur
**Age Range:** 40s-50s (at Pratap's birth in 1540, Udai Singh was 15; at death 1572, age 52)
**Appearance Age:** 40s-50s throughout relevant scenes (1540-1572)

**Physical Description:**
- Face: Softer features than Pratap, wise expression, aging gracefully
- Eyes: Calm, contemplative, political wisdom
- Hair: Graying, traditional Rajput style
- Beard: Full gray beard, well-maintained
- Body: Royal bearing, slightly heavier build — more administrator than warrior
- Skin: Weathered but less battle-scarred than Pratap

**Clothing:**
- Royal robes: Deep blue/maroon angarkha, white dhoti
- Crown: Rajput royal crown (mukut)
- Jewelry: Extensive gold and precious stone jewelry

**Personality:** Wise, diplomatic, visionary — founded Udaipur

**Emotional Arc:**
- Loves Pratap deeply but politically cautious
- Believes in peace with Mughals when possible
- His death creates the central conflict of the documentary

## Character 3: Maharani Ajabde Punwar

**Role:** Supporting — Pratap's first and most beloved wife
**Age Range:** 20s-40s (throughout Pratap's life)

**Physical Description:**
- Face: Beautiful Rajput features, strong jawline, intelligent eyes
- Hair: Long dark braided hair, traditional Rajput women's hairstyle
- Eyes: Dark, expressive, conveys both strength and grace
- Body: Graceful, regal bearing
- Skin: Fair, smooth complexion, traditional makeup (sindoor, kajal)

**Clothing:**
- Royal lehenga: Rich red/maroon with gold embroidery
- Jewelry: Heavy gold necklaces, armlets, earrings, nose ring (nath), maang tikka
- Veil (ghoonghat): Used in formal settings
- During war: Practical but regal, carries royal dignity even in hardship

**Personality:**
- Strong-willed, loyal, deeply devoted to Pratap
- Shares his hardships during guerrilla phase
- Political acumen — supports Pratap's decisions
- Mother figure to the people of Mewar

**Emotional Arc:**
- Young bride (married at ~16) — hopeful and romantic
- After Chittorgarh — grief and hardened resolve
- During guerrilla war — stoic endurance, pillar of strength
- Final years — respected matriarch of Mewar

## Character 4: Emperor Akbar

**Role:** Antagonist — Mughal Emperor, Pratap's adversary
**Age Range:** 30s-50s (1556-1605 CE)
**Appearance Age:** 30s-50s throughout (roughly same generation as Pratap)

**Physical Description:**
- Face: Broad, strong Central Asian features, fair complexion
- Eyes: Calculating, ambitious, observant
- Hair: Dark, styled in Central Asian fashion, sometimes covered with cap
- Beard: Full dark beard, well-groomed in Mughal style
- Body: Heavy-set, imposing presence
- Distinguishing: Often seen with turban jewels, imperial regalia

**Clothing:**
- Imperial robes: Rich fabrics — silk, brocade, jama (Mughal coat)
- Turban: Tall, wrapped in distinctive Mughal style with jeweled ornaments
- Jewelry: Extensive — necklaces, armlets, rings, turban jewels
- Weapons: Ceremonial sword at side

**Personality:**
- Brilliant strategist and administrator
- Pragmatic — genuinely wanted Rajput alliance
- Cannot tolerate defiance — Mewar's resistance challenges his authority
- Respects Pratap's courage even while opposing him

**Emotional Arc:**
- Confident emperor — initially dismissive of Pratap
- Frustrated by Mewar's persistent resistance
- Eventually develops grudging respect for Pratap's character
- After Pratap's death: recognizes his greatness

## Character 5: Chetak (Horse)

**Role:** Supporting — Pratap's legendary war horse
**Type:** Marwari breed horse
**Color:** White/gray with distinctive markings
**Build:** Strong, fast, loyal war horse — smaller than Mughal horses but more agile

**Physical Description:**
- Muscular, lean, high-spirited
- White/gray coat with dark markings
- Alert ears, intelligent eyes
- Wore royal saddle and bridle with Mewar's sun emblem

**Significance:**
- Legendary in Indian folklore
- Carried Pratap to safety at Haldighati despite wounds
- Died shortly after the battle — mourned by Pratap
- Symbol of loyalty and sacrifice
