## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** At what scale does evidence support an arachnid distribution claim?

**Coverage:** Define species, sites, dates, effort, and observation completeness.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered museum specimens, taxonomic revisions, repeatable field surveys, behavioral studies, dated range records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name species or lineage, geography, life stage, and date. Do not conflate all arachnids with spiders or infer human danger from a sighting; route any bite or sting guidance to current clinical and local public-health sources.
