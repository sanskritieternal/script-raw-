## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What path does energy take from source to wheels and brakes in one vehicle?

**Coverage:** Use a named model and distinguish powertrain, control, and braking systems.

**Evidence to seek:** manufacturer specifications and owner manuals, safety investigations and recalls, independent vehicle tests, emissions and fuel-economy protocols, repair records, and dated owner accounts

**Limits:** Name model, year, market, and test conditions. Distinguish lab ratings from real-world use, avoid unsafe driving or repair instructions, and check current recall and safety information.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
