## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular efficiency or perpetual-motion story fit measured inputs and outputs?

**Coverage:** Set system boundary and compare measurements with physical constraints.

**Evidence to seek:** dated design drawings and patents, manufacturer specifications, applicable standards, calibrated independent tests, public failure and service records, and user evidence

**Limits:** Name model, version, workload, and boundary. Use measured units and matched conditions; avoid hazardous operation, repair, or security-bypass instructions and verify current standards.
