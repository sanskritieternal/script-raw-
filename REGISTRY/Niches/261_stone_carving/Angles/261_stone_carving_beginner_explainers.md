## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do geology, tool marks, design, and setting connect?

**Coverage:** Use one named object and maker context.

**Evidence to seek:** dated carved objects with provenance, quarry and commission records, stone analysis, conservation reports, and consented maker accounts

**Limits:** Name stone, object, maker community, place, and period. Respect sacred uses and credit quarry and workshop labor; avoid unsafe process instruction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
