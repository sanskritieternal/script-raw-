## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** What Makes Footage Evidence?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can one recorded scene establish and what lies outside its frame?

**Coverage:** Identify date, location, recorder, edit, and corroboration.

**Evidence to seek:** original footage and metadata, transcripts, releases, primary records, archive provenance, production logs, participant responses, and documented corrections

**Limits:** A filmed scene is partial evidence, not full context. Verify factual assertions independently, document reconstruction and editing, and assess consent and participant risk throughout distribution.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
