## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How was a record tree density, biomass, or diversity claim verified?

**Coverage:** Check sampling area, taxonomy, units, method, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** official forest assessments and methods, validated imagery, permanent field plots, land-tenure records, biodiversity surveys, and consented local and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name forest type, boundary, jurisdiction, measure, and date. Distinguish tree cover from ecological integrity and reforestation from recovery; respect rights and protect sensitive locations.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
