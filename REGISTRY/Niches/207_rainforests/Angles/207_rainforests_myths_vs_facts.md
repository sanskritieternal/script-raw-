**Default:** Is Reforestation the Same as Recovery?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** When does tree regrowth restore rainforest functions?

**Coverage:** Compare species, age, soil, fauna, connectivity, and time.

**Evidence to seek:** official forest assessments and methods, validated imagery, permanent field plots, land-tenure records, biodiversity surveys, and consented local and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name forest type, boundary, jurisdiction, measure, and date. Distinguish tree cover from ecological integrity and reforestation from recovery; respect rights and protect sensitive locations.
