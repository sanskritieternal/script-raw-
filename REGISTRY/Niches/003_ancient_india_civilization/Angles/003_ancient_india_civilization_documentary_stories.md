## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did occupation, abandonment, excavation, and conservation unfold?

**Coverage:** Use chronological evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** Archaeological Survey of India excavation reports, UNESCO Dholavira and Nalanda site files, peer-reviewed archaeological analysis, inscriptions, and critical text editions

**Limits:** Name site, region, century, source, and interpretive uncertainty. Avoid treating diverse periods as one continuous polity or an undeciphered script as translated.
