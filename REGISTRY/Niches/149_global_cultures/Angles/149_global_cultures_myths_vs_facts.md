## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a broad claim about one group fit documented variation?

**Coverage:** Name claim, population, place, source, and counterexamples.

**Evidence to seek:** consented practitioner accounts and community records, dated objects and policy documents, appropriately contextualized surveys, and documentation chosen by community participants

**Limits:** Never generalize from one person to an entire culture. Name group, place, practice, and period; use preferred terms, secure free prior informed ongoing consent, and honor restricted knowledge and community review.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
