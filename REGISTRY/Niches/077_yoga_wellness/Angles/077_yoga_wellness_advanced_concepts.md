## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a yoga program add benefit beyond another appropriate activity?

**Coverage:** Compare time, attention, instructor support, and baseline differences.

**Evidence to seek:** registered intervention protocols and trials, systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event records, service data, and consented participant accounts

**Limits:** Name program, components, population, comparator, and outcome. Distinguish wellness from disease treatment; report harms and uncertainty and give no personal exercise or medical advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
