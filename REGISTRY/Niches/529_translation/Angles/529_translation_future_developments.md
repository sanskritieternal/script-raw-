## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What source access, expertise and review would a translation project require?

**Coverage:** Assess one proposed translation approach through source access, language expertise, review and rights; distinguish demonstration from publishable output.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: identified source editions, authorized parallel passages, drafts, translator notes and attributed reader studies. Starting framework: International Federation of Translators charter (https://library.fit-ift.org/public/Resources/Documents/FIT_Translators_Charter_1994.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name versions and permissions; no translation is culturally neutral. Niche boundary: Translation decisions, versions and reception; general linguistics, language instruction and criticism unrelated to translation remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
