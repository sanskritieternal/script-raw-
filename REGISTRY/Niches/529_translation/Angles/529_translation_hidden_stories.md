## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose editing, interpreting or language-consulting work is overlooked?

**Coverage:** Document overlooked translators, interpreters, editors or language consultants in one publication; credit their roles using attributable records.

**Evidence to seek:** Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: identified source editions, authorized parallel passages, drafts, translator notes and attributed reader studies. Starting framework: International Federation of Translators charter (https://library.fit-ift.org/public/Resources/Documents/FIT_Translators_Charter_1994.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name versions and permissions; no translation is culturally neutral. Niche boundary: Translation decisions, versions and reception; general linguistics, language instruction and criticism unrelated to translation remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
