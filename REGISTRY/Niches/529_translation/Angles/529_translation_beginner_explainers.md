## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do source, audience and revision shape meaning in a translated passage?

**Coverage:** Explain source text, translation choices, audience and revision through one short authorized passage; distinguish literal correspondence from conveyed meaning.

**Evidence to seek:** Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: identified source editions, authorized parallel passages, drafts, translator notes and attributed reader studies. Starting framework: International Federation of Translators charter (https://library.fit-ift.org/public/Resources/Documents/FIT_Translators_Charter_1994.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name versions and permissions; no translation is culturally neutral. Niche boundary: Translation decisions, versions and reception; general linguistics, language instruction and criticism unrelated to translation remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
