## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do two translations differ after controlling for their source editions?

**Coverage:** Compare two dated translations of the same source passage; control for source edition and explain differences in audience and conventions.

**Evidence to seek:** Align definitions, samples, versions and measurement conditions. Case records: identified source editions, authorized parallel passages, drafts, translator notes and attributed reader studies. Starting framework: International Federation of Translators charter (https://library.fit-ift.org/public/Resources/Documents/FIT_Translators_Charter_1994.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name versions and permissions; no translation is culturally neutral. Niche boundary: Translation decisions, versions and reception; general linguistics, language instruction and criticism unrelated to translation remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
