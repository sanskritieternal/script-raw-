## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do two product generations compare on the same user task?

**Coverage:** Compare two generations of a product on the same user task and support conditions; include compatibility and repair access.

**Evidence to seek:** Align definitions, samples, versions and measurement conditions. Case records: product evaluations, fitting and repair records, user-defined participation measures. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Global report on assistive technology (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240049451). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Center user choice and access; a prototype is not a proven benefit for everyone. Niche boundary: Assistive-product selection, provision and sustained participation; disability history, speculative enhancement and the full rehabilitation pathway are neighboring subjects.
