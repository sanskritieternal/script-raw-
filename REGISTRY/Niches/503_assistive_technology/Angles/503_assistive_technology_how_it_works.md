## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who supports an assistive product from assessment through replacement?

**Coverage:** Map assessment, product selection, fitting, training, repair and replacement for one service; identify responsibility and costs at each step.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the process, custody transfers and assigned responsibilities. Case records: product evaluations, fitting and repair records, user-defined participation measures. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Global report on assistive technology (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240049451). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Center user choice and access; a prototype is not a proven benefit for everyone. Niche boundary: Assistive-product selection, provision and sustained participation; disability history, speculative enhancement and the full rehabilitation pathway are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
