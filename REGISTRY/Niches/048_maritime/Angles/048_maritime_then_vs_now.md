## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a defined sea route change in vessel size, time, work, and impact?

**Coverage:** Use comparable route boundaries and measures for both periods.

**Evidence to seek:** vessel design and trial records, official marine casualty investigations, public survey and port records, voyage and cargo data, and attributed crew and community accounts

**Limits:** Name vessel, route, flag or jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish investigation findings from speculation; avoid navigational or emergency instructions, security vulnerabilities, and unsupported blame. Check current authority notices before production.
