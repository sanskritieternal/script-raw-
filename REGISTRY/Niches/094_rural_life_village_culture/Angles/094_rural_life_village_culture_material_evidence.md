## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can one tool, building, or household object show about village life?

**Coverage:** Document provenance, use, repair, and limits of inference.

**Evidence to seek:** dated local records and maps, census and service data, material provenance, and consented accounts from residents and community stewards

**Limits:** Name community, place, and period. Avoid a single romantic village portrait; protect private information and seek community participation and consent for living-heritage documentation.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
