## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Lesson Versus a Causal Claim

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one story establish about a strategy's effectiveness?

**Coverage:** Separate observed experience from evidence across people.

**Evidence to seek:** dated primary records, original interviews with informed consent, contemporaneous statements, outcome data, relevant research for generalized claims, and corrections

**Limits:** Verify chronology and quotations; protect privacy and dignity. Do not infer diagnosis, promise that adversity produces growth, glorify burnout, or universalize a single life story.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
