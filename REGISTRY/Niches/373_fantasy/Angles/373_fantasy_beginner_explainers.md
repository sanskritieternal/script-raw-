## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can and cannot happen under one story's stated rules?

**Coverage:** Read examples and exceptions before summarizing a system.

**Evidence to seek:** identified editions, author drafts, source traditions, production notes, archive records, attributed criticism, and situated reader accounts

**Limits:** Keep invented-world facts inside the specified work and edition. Attribute borrowed traditions and distinguish interpretation from author intent.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
