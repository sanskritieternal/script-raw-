**Coverage:** Distinguish the token, ledger, wallet keys, and intermediary claim.

**Evidence to seek:** protocol documents, dated transaction or market data, issuer terms, incident reports, and regulator publications

**Limits:** Date legal classifications and product terms; separate protocol claims from intermediary promises and avoid individualized financial advice.
