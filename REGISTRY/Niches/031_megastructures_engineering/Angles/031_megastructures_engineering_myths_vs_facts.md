## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a tallest, strongest, or most efficient claim use comparable definitions?

**Coverage:** Check dates, boundaries, measurements, and competing records.

**Evidence to seek:** approved designs and alternatives, public project records, inspection and load-rating reports, measured use, published investigations, budgets, impact assessments, and community accounts

**Limits:** Name structure, jurisdiction, date, and design basis. Distinguish modeled from observed performance; avoid unsafe operational details or declaring a facility safe from a single metric.
