## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do model and original dimensions relate?

**Coverage:** State ratio, units, and a measured example.

**Evidence to seek:** dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies

**Limits:** Name subject, scale, model version, and purpose. Distinguish a model's deliberate simplification from errors and check tool and material safety for the actual setting.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
