## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did one maker use casting, forming, assembly, or another method?

**Coverage:** Compare product requirements, yield, cost, and worker effects.

**Evidence to seek:** dated process and quality records, manufacturer specifications, independent tests, applicable product standards, recall and service records, and attributed worker accounts

**Limits:** Name product, plant or process, batch, and date. Separate demonstration from full-scale yield, account for upstream and downstream effects, and avoid hazardous factory instructions or live vulnerabilities.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
