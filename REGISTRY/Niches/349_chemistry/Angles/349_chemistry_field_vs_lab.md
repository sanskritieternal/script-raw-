## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a controlled result hold in environmental or industrial conditions?

**Coverage:** Check matrix effects and monitoring.

**Evidence to seek:** IUPAC terminology and metrology guidance, original experiments, analytical validation, lab notebooks, safety records, and independent replication

**Limits:** Name substance, conditions, units, analytical method, uncertainty, and exposure context. Distinguish measured products from proposed mechanisms and avoid procedural hazard instructions.
