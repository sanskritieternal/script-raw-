## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a familiar first, unbeaten, or impossible claim match records?

**Coverage:** Trace earliest claim, governing definitions, and missing evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** official rulebooks and results, event programs, federation archives, film and timing records, equipment specifications, medical or safety studies, and participant accounts

**Limits:** Name sport, event, date, and rule version. Compare eras on equal terms, protect participant privacy, and avoid unsafe training or injury advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
