## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What domain skill transferred and what was newly learned?

**Coverage:** Compare real pathways.

**Evidence to seek:** dated job descriptions and hiring records, credited project artifacts, BLS occupational profiles, NIST AI role and risk documentation, worker accounts, and training outcomes

**Limits:** Name role, sector, country, and date. Distinguish a job title, job posting, actual task, and forecast; avoid promising employment from a single tool skill.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
