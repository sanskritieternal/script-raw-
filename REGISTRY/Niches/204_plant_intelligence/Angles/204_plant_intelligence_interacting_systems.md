## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does one plant respond to cues from another organism?

**Coverage:** Separate emitted signal, reception, changed behavior, and adaptive outcome.

**Evidence to seek:** original experimental protocols and data, controlled replication studies, plant physiology and signaling research, field validation, and clearly attributed scientific critiques

**Limits:** Name species, cue, response measure, timescale, and operational definition. Do not equate signaling with consciousness, intention, brains, or sentience; distinguish measured behavior from interpretive labels.
