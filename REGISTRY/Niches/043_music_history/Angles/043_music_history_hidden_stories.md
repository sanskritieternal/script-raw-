## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose performance, arrangement, or technical labor shaped one well-known work?

**Coverage:** Trace session logs, contracts, oral histories, and credits.

**Evidence to seek:** dated recordings and scores, session logs and contracts, release credits, archival metadata, reviews, performer interviews with consent, and community histories

**Limits:** Distinguish composition, performance, recording, and later remastering. Credit collaborators, verify rights, respect restricted traditions, and avoid single-inventor or purity myths.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
