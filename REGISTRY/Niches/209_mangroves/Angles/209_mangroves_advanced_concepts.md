## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why can planting fail when tidal hydrology is wrong?

**Coverage:** Compare elevation, inundation, drainage barriers, species, and natural recruitment.

**Evidence to seek:** tidal and salinity records, elevation and sediment measures, validated imagery, repeat vegetation and fauna plots, tenure and project documents, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name coast, stand, tidal setting, species, and date. Do not equate seedlings planted or canopy cover with ecosystem recovery or guaranteed coastal protection; respect tenure and access rights.
