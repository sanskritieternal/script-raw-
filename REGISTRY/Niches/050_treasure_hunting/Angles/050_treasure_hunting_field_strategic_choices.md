## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did custodians document, leave, recover, or relocate one find?

**Coverage:** Compare risk assessment, legal authority, conservation capacity, and consultation.

**Evidence to seek:** original inventories and maps, permits and jurisdictional records, archaeological survey and context reports, conservation and custody records, and community accounts

**Limits:** Do not disclose vulnerable site coordinates or excavation instructions. Check current local law and permissions; protect human remains and archaeological context; do not assume the UNESCO convention applies in every jurisdiction.
