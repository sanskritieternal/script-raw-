## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence would justify XR for one training or learning task?

**Coverage:** Define comparator, user outcome, cost, access, and harms before deployment.

**Evidence to seek:** dated product specifications and release notes, standards drafts and implementation tests, independent usability and performance studies, consented user accounts, and privacy documentation

**Limits:** Name application, device, version, user group, and date. Separate demos from sustained outcomes, protect spatial and biometric data, and verify current comfort and accessibility guidance.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
