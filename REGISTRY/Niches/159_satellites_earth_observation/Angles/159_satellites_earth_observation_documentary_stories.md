## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did an Earth observation product inform one land or hazard choice?

**Coverage:** Trace data version, analysis, decision, ground checks, and outcome.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned mission specifications and data products, calibration and known-issue logs, independent ground reference, processing methods, and records of downstream decisions

**Limits:** Name mission, sensor, product version, scene date, resolution, and place. A satellite image is a measurement and interpretation, not direct proof of a cause; validate sensitive claims and assess privacy and security implications.
