## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a cartoon adjust narrative complexity for a specified age group?

**Coverage:** Compare vocabulary, pacing, implied knowledge, and independent audience evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** the finished cartoon and dated drafts, storyboards and credits, production interviews, age-specific audience evaluation with consent, and child-media guidance

**Limits:** Define the intended age group; avoid universal claims about children's learning or behavior. Check imitable harm, accessibility, rights, and consent in any audience research.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
