## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a road, dam, or protected-area project change a valley?

**Coverage:** Build a timeline of plans, consent, construction, impacts, and revisions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated geological and climate observations, glacier and hydrology measurements, land-use and hazard records, field ecology, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name range, valley, elevation band, season, and period. Distinguish physical hazard from human exposure, respect local knowledge and land rights, and avoid route or hazard advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
