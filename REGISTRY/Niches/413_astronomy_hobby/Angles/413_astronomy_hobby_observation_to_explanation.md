## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What alternatives besides the target could explain a brightness change?

**Coverage:** Check clouds, focus, calibration, and comparison stars.

**Evidence to seek:** timestamped observing logs, current ephemerides, raw and calibrated images, comparison catalogs, AAVSO observing protocols, and NASA skywatching and solar-viewing guidance

**Limits:** Name date, location, instrument, target, and uncertainty. Never point unfiltered optics at the Sun; distinguish observed light from inferred physical cause or discovery claim.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
