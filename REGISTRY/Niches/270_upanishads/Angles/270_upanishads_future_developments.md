**Focus / example question:** Which new manuscript or edition could clarify one disputed reading?

**Coverage:** Specify missing evidence without predicting theological resolution.

**Evidence to seek:** named Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, translations, commentaries, citation histories, and consented reader accounts

**Limits:** Specify the Upanishad, recension, passage, and interpreter. Do not collapse diverse texts or lineages into one doctrine; distinguish historical setting from later and present readings.
