## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which legal or political change ended its status?

**Coverage:** Compare dates and continuing unofficial use.

**Evidence to seek:** official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests

**Limits:** Name flag version, jurisdiction, authority, and date. Distinguish official symbolism from later interpretation and flag display from sovereignty recognition.
