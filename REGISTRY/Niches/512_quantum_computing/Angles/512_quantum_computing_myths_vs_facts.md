## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a quantum-advantage claim use a defensible classical comparison?

**Coverage:** Test one quantum-advantage claim against its original task and classical comparison; distinguish theoretical speedup from measured end-to-end usefulness.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: original experiments, benchmark definitions, error estimates and reproducible classical comparisons. Starting framework: NIST quantum information science (https://www.nist.gov/quantum-information-science). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Separate physical qubits, logical qubits and useful applications. Niche boundary: Quantum computing experiments, resources and verification; general quantum physics and classical programming remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
