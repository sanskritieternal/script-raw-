**Focus / example question:** What did a widely cited quantum demonstration actually establish?

**Coverage:** Examine one widely cited quantum demonstration using its task, device, comparison method and uncertainty; state what practical use it did not establish.

**Limits:** Separate physical qubits, logical qubits and useful applications. Niche boundary: Quantum computing experiments, resources and verification; general quantum physics and classical programming remain separate.
