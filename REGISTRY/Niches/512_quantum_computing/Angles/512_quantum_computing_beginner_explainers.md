## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do preparation, operations and measurement answer one quantum task?

**Coverage:** Explain qubits, operations, measurement and error through one defined experiment; distinguish quantum computing from ordinary parallel processing.

**Evidence to seek:** Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: original experiments, benchmark definitions, error estimates and reproducible classical comparisons. **Limits:** Separate physical qubits, logical qubits and useful applications. Niche boundary: Quantum computing experiments, resources and verification; general quantum physics and classical programming remain separate.
