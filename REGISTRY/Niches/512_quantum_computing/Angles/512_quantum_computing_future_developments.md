## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What assumptions stand between a quantum algorithm and its proposed application?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed quantum application through algorithm, error correction, hardware and input-output requirements; label untested assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: original experiments, benchmark definitions, error estimates and reproducible classical comparisons. Starting framework: NIST quantum information science (https://www.nist.gov/quantum-information-science). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Separate physical qubits, logical qubits and useful applications. Niche boundary: Quantum computing experiments, resources and verification; general quantum physics and classical programming remain separate.
