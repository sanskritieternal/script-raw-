## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can a dated artifact show about celebration?

**Coverage:** Check use, provenance, and whether it predates the named festival.

**Evidence to seek:** original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations

**Limits:** Define which beginning is being investigated. Attribute legends and contested accounts respectfully; do not call the earliest surviving record an absolute origin.
