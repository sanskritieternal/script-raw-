## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Are we dating a name, ritual, venue, or annual institution?

**Coverage:** Define the feature before comparing claims.

**Evidence to seek:** original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations

**Limits:** Define which beginning is being investigated. Attribute legends and contested accounts respectfully; do not call the earliest surviving record an absolute origin.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
