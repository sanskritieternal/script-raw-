## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do practitioners transmit and change one practice?

**Coverage:** Define community, setting, participants, and present variation.

**Evidence to seek:** community-approved accounts, dated archival and material records, oral histories with consent, and relevant heritage decisions

**Limits:** Name community, place, and period. Seek free, prior, sustained, informed consent; respect restricted knowledge and do not impose a single authentic form.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
