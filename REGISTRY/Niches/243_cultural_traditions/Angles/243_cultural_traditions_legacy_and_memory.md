## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does a present commemoration say about past practice?

**Coverage:** Separate memory work from direct evidence of earlier periods.

**Evidence to seek:** community-approved accounts, dated archival and material records, oral histories with consent, and relevant heritage decisions

**Limits:** Name community, place, and period. Seek free, prior, sustained, informed consent; respect restricted knowledge and do not impose a single authentic form.
