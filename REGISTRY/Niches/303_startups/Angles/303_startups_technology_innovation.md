## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a named feature create measurable advantage?

**Coverage:** Compare baseline, performance, reliability, cost, and user outcomes.

**Evidence to seek:** dated product versions and experiments, customer and financial cohorts, financing terms, incidents, and consented team and user accounts

**Limits:** Define what counts as a startup and name company, product, market, and period. Distinguish pitch claims from measured results and avoid investment advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
