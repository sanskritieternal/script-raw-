## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which government, court, or company claimed authority over one vessel?

**Coverage:** Compare legal documents, actual control, and rival claims at a fixed date.

**Evidence to seek:** dated letters of marque, Admiralty and piracy trial records, ship and port logs, correspondence, material provenance, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name period, jurisdiction, vessel, and source. Distinguish piracy from authorized privateering in context, separate legend from record, acknowledge coerced people, and avoid actionable raid or security details.
