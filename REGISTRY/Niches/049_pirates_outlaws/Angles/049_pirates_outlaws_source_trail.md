## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which original records underlie a repeated story about one person?

**Coverage:** Follow citations back to trial, correspondence, or ship records and note gaps.

**Evidence to seek:** dated letters of marque, Admiralty and piracy trial records, ship and port logs, correspondence, material provenance, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name period, jurisdiction, vessel, and source. Distinguish piracy from authorized privateering in context, separate legend from record, acknowledge coerced people, and avoid actionable raid or security details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
