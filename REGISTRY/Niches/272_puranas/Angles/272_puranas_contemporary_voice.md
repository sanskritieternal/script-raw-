## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one present storyteller emphasize and why?

**Coverage:** Use consented testimony and avoid making one account universal.

**Evidence to seek:** specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Name the Purāṇa, recension, passage, and edition; attribute local stories and devotional beliefs. Distinguish narrative claims from independently dated history and avoid treating all Purāṇas as one fixed text.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
