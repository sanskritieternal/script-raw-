## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which decisions belong to a manager, team, or institution?

**Coverage:** Map authority, delegation, accountability, and appeal in one workplace.

**Evidence to seek:** dated decision and policy records, relevant management research, worker surveys with response limits, and confidential attributed accounts

**Limits:** Name organization, role, jurisdiction, and period. Separate leader claims from measured outcomes, protect employee identity, and check applicable labor rules.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
