## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can another analyst rerun the analysis?

**Coverage:** Check data rights, code, environment, and limitations.

**Evidence to seek:** BLS occupational profiles, dated postings, reproducible project artifacts, data governance records, hiring assessments, worker diaries, and education outcomes

**Limits:** Name role, domain, location, and date. Distinguish observed tasks from marketing titles and projections; respect data privacy and use rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
