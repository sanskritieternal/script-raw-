## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do fiber supply and local teaching shape technique?

**Coverage:** Attribute makers and avoid an invented origin.

**Evidence to seek:** dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports

**Limits:** Name pattern, yarn, needle, gauge, and finishing conditions. Do not assign a single origin to a widespread motif or promise fit from an untested label gauge.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
