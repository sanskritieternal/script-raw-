## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do two savings or credit products compare for one use case?

**Coverage:** Use identical balances, dates, fees, liquidity, and risk criteria.

**Evidence to seek:** dated product disclosures, regulator education and complaint data, household surveys, and transparent hypothetical calculations

**Limits:** State jurisdiction and assumptions; avoid individualized financial advice or guarantees.
