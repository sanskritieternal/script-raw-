## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which traditions does a generated work draw on?

**Coverage:** Attribute sources and ask affected makers.

**Evidence to seek:** dated artworks and artist records, versioned tools, production files, exhibition materials, U.S. Copyright Office guidance, and audience research

**Limits:** Name creator, workflow, model version, source provenance, jurisdiction, and display context. Do not infer ownership or authorship from appearance alone.
