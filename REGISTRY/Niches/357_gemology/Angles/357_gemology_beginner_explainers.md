## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do mineral identity, color, cut, and treatment differ?

**Coverage:** Use a tested specimen and clear terminology.

**Evidence to seek:** GIA laboratory methods and reports, known-source reference stones, mining and trade records, field geology, and consented maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name specimen, laboratory, report date, test scope, and provenance gaps. Treat geographic origin as a bounded expert opinion and distinguish it from ownership history.
