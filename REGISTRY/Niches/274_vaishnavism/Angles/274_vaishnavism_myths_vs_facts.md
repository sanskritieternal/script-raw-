## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a broad claim about Vaiṣṇavism survive comparison of named lineages?

**Coverage:** Read their own sources and state differences fairly.

**Evidence to seek:** specified texts, editions, commentaries and songs, dated temple and institutional records, recordings with permission, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Name the lineage, text, language, and place; attribute theological claims and distinguish historical evidence from hagiography. Do not generalize one practice across all Vaiṣṇavas.
