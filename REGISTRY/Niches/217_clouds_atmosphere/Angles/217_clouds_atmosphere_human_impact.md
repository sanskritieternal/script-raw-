## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who uses cloud observations and with what limits?

**Coverage:** Examine aviation, solar energy, agriculture, and warning contexts.

**Evidence to seek:** WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies

**Limits:** Name cloud type, location, observation time, instrument, and classification standard. Do not turn a single sky photo into a weather forecast or climate trend; use current official forecasts for decisions.
