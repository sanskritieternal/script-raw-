## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What distinguishes a physical robot from software and remote control?

**Coverage:** Show sensors, actuation, and human input.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST AI human-interaction framework, deployment and incident records, independent task evaluations, procurement documents, and affected-person accounts

**Limits:** Name robot, task, operator, setting, and date. A demonstration does not establish autonomous or society-wide use.
