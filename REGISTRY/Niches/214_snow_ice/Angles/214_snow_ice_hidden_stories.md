## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose field and data work keeps a snow record reliable?

**Coverage:** Trace station crews, observers, calibrators, analysts, and local knowledge.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned snow and sea-ice datasets, station and snow-course records, satellite methods, weather observations, water gauges, and documented uncertainty

**Limits:** Name snow or ice type, measure, region, elevation, season, and date. Distinguish sea-ice extent from area and snow depth from SWE; use current official sources for travel or ice safety.
