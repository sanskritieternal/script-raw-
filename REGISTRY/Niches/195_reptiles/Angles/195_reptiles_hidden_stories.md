## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose knowledge and labor produced one population estimate?

**Coverage:** Trace local guides, survey teams, identifiers, and land stewards.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered specimens and taxonomic revisions, repeatable field surveys, habitat and threat records, dated conservation plans, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name species, place, life stage, season, and assessment date. Do not infer population change from sightings or generalize risk from one reptile to all; seek current local and clinical sources for encounter safety.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
