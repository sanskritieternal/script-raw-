## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which legibility and format needs guided a handwritten message?

**Coverage:** Compare intended and actual reading conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated original manuscripts, copybooks, archive provenance, transcription records, imaging methods, material analysis, and bounded legibility tests

**Limits:** Do not infer personality or diagnosis from handwriting. Mark uncertain letters and respect privacy, copyright, and collection handling rules.
