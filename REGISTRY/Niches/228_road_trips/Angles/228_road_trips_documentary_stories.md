## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What happened during a documented route interruption?

**Coverage:** Use time-stamped notices and consented accounts without inventing dialogue.

**Evidence to seek:** dated road authority notices, route maps, traffic and weather records, full-price offers, local planning documents, and consented traveler and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name route, jurisdiction, date, vehicle, and traveler needs. Verify live conditions with road authorities; never present a research question as current driving or safety advice.
