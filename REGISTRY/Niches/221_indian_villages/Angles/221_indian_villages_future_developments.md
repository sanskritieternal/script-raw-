## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which conditions could alter water, work, and access?

**Coverage:** State climate, migration, infrastructure, land, and governance assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated census village and amenity definitions, local government directory entries, gram panchayat and district records, land and service data, validated maps, and consented resident accounts

**Limits:** Name village code or unit, gram panchayat, district, state, source year, and season. Do not equate a listed amenity with usable access or present one resident as representative; protect privacy and sensitive social identities.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
