## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can a worker with defined needs enter and use it?

**Coverage:** Check route, restroom, power, and communication.

**Evidence to seek:** current official immigration and tax guidance, employer policies, local housing and service data, route and connectivity records, and resident testimony

**Limits:** Name nationality, destination, work arrangement, date, and source. Travel permissions and tax treatment vary; do not imply a tourist entry permits all remote work.
