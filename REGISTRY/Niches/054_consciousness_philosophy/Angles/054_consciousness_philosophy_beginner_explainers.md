## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do experience, wakefulness, attention, and self-awareness differ?

**Coverage:** Define terms with contrasting examples and avoid treating one definition as universal.

**Evidence to seek:** original philosophical texts and replies, translation and manuscript notes, published experiment methods and results, and attributed practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Distinguish experience, interpretation, argument, and empirical result. Attribute contested views; do not claim consensus, diagnose individuals, or infer subjective experience from a single measure.
