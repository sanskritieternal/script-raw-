## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What must a proposed housing intervention deliver beyond its unit target?

**Coverage:** Assess one proposed housing intervention through land, finance, delivery, maintenance and tenant access requirements; distinguish targets from completed homes.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and period; tenancy rights vary by place. Niche boundary: Household housing access, quality and tenancy; property investment, market forecasting and home decoration are neighboring subjects.
