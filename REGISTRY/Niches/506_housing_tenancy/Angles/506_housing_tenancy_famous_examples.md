## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which households benefited from a documented housing program?

**Coverage:** Examine one housing program using eligibility, delivered units, affordability and tenure outcomes; identify which households the evaluation represents.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and period; tenancy rights vary by place. Niche boundary: Household housing access, quality and tenancy; property investment, market forecasting and home decoration are neighboring subjects.
