## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed for households after a housing reform or development decision?

**Coverage:** Examine a dated housing reform, eviction shock or development decision in one place; compare affected households' conditions before and after.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and period; tenancy rights vary by place. Niche boundary: Household housing access, quality and tenancy; property investment, market forecasting and home decoration are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
