## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Reducing the Need for Rescue

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which evaluated safety measure relates to the documented hazard?

**Coverage:** Avoid implying people in distress were at fault.

**Evidence to seek:** official mission and after-action reports, dated public logs, local accounts, and consented survivor testimony

**Limits:** Protect survivors and responders; do not turn narrative questions into field instructions or speculate about blame.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
