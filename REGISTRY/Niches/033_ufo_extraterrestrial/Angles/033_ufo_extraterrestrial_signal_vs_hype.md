## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a claim about life beyond Earth match the original measurement?

**Coverage:** Separate molecule or pattern, abiotic alternatives, and required confirmation.

**Evidence to seek:** original sensor files and metadata, official case reports, weather and traffic records, calibrated astronomical observations, astrobiology research, and independent analyses

**Limits:** Unidentified is a statement about current evidence, not proof of extraterrestrial origin. Protect witnesses, verify media provenance, test ordinary alternatives, and clearly label fiction or speculation.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
