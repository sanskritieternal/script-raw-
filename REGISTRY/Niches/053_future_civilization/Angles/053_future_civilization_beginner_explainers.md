## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What assumptions turn current evidence into a particular possible future?

**Coverage:** Show a bounded scenario's drivers, dependencies, uncertainty, and alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** dated forecasts and proposals, measured current baselines, transparent scenario assumptions, physical resource estimates, governance documents, and affected-group perspectives

**Limits:** Treat scenarios as conditional, not predictions. State geography and time horizon, expose assumptions and distributional effects, and verify current data and rules before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
