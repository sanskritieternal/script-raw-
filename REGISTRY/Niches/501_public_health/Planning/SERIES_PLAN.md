## SER-501-001 — Reading a public-health evaluation

Entry requirement: No prior episode required; explain specialist terms on first use.

Learning outcome: Ask what was measured, who was included and how measurement limits were handled.

Ordering reason: First identify the question, then the sample, then the measurement assumptions.

1. **IDEA-501-0001** — New York smoke-exposure study: result and limits (ready_for_project). What did New York's 2003-2004 saliva study measure about nonsmokers' exposure, and what limits apply to its interpretation?
2. **IDEA-501-0002** — Who appears in an exposure study? (ready_for_project). How do recruitment and sample eligibility constrain the population represented by the New York saliva study?
3. **IDEA-501-0003** — When a measurement is below the detection limit (ready_for_project). How did the New York evaluation handle saliva values below the detection limit, and what interpretation limits follow?
