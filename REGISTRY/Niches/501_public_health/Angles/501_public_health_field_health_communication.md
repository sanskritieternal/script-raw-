## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a public-health message reach its audience with the intended meaning?

**Coverage:** Compare versions of one health message, its intended audience and comprehension evidence; examine language access and unintended interpretations.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define place, population and date; educational questions do not provide personal care advice. Niche boundary: Population-level prevention, surveillance and program evaluation; personal wellness advice and whole-system financing belong to neighboring niches.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
