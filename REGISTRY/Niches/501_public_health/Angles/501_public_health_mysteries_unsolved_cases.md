## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which competing explanations remain for a program's unexpected outcome?

**Coverage:** Investigate one unresolved program-outcome question using competing explanations and available surveillance records; identify what evidence would distinguish them.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define place, population and date; educational questions do not provide personal care advice. Niche boundary: Population-level prevention, surveillance and program evaluation; personal wellness advice and whole-system financing belong to neighboring niches.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
