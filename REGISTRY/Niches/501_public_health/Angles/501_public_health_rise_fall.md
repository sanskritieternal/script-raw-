## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did participation or support for a prevention program expand or decline?

**Coverage:** Explain the expansion or withdrawal of one program using funding, participation, delivery and evaluation records; consider competing causes.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define place, population and date; educational questions do not provide personal care advice. Niche boundary: Population-level prevention, surveillance and program evaluation; personal wellness advice and whole-system financing belong to neighboring niches.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
