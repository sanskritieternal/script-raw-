## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do contacts, units, and faults tell us?

**Coverage:** Use one map at its stated scale.

**Evidence to seek:** USGS geologic maps and methods, field logs, sample provenance, geochronology, geophysical surveys, and independent mapping

**Limits:** Name mapped area, map scale, unit, sample, dating method, and uncertainty. Distinguish observed contacts from inferred subsurface structure.
