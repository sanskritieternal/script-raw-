## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Revision in a Manuscript

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one scene or argument change from draft to print?

**Coverage:** Compare dated variants and avoid claiming motive without testimony.

**Evidence to seek:** dated manuscripts and editions, author and publisher correspondence, proofs and contracts, catalog and conservation records, contemporary reviews, and attributed reader accounts

**Limits:** Name work, edition, language, and date. Distinguish textual evidence from author intent, identify translation choices, check rights before reproduction, and avoid unsupported single-origin stories.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
