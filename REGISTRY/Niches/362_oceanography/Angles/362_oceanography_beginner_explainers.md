## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do temperature, salinity, and winds shape a named current?

**Coverage:** Use a bounded region and season.

**Evidence to seek:** NOAA ocean observing archives, Argo float metadata, cruise and sensor records, validation studies, model output, and coastal community evidence

**Limits:** Name basin or coast, depth, season, instrument, variable, and uncertainty. Do not infer deep or basin-wide conditions from one surface observation.
