## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which conditions produced a documented flood and its impacts?

**Coverage:** Compare rainfall, antecedent soil, land use, defenses, and exposure.

**Evidence to seek:** dated hydrologic and quality measurements, watershed and land-use maps, utility and allocation records, current health and environmental guidance, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name source, catchment, jurisdiction, season, and test method. Do not infer potability from appearance or one sample; verify current local health and allocation guidance.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
