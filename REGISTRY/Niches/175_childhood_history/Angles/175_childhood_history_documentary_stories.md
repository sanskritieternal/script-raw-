## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can sources support about one historical child's routine?

**Coverage:** Label documented facts, plausible inference, and unknowns.

**Evidence to seek:** dated school, family, legal, work, and health records with access limits, children's objects and writings, period maps, and voluntary consented recollections

**Limits:** Name place, era, age group, and source. Avoid universal or nostalgic childhood claims; protect children's records and living descendants, and distinguish direct evidence from reconstructed scenes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
