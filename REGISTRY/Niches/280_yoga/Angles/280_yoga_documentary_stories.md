## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one textual or health claim move into public marketing?

**Coverage:** Trace source, teacher statement, study, and later correction.

**Evidence to seek:** specified primary texts and translations, lineage and teaching records, registered trials and systematic reviews, current guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Name the style, components, instructor context, population, and outcome. Distinguish spiritual aims from clinical claims; give no exercise or treatment instructions and verify safety in current guidance.
