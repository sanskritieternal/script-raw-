## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What consent and rights attach to object and performance?

**Coverage:** Check maker, troupe, community, and jurisdiction.

**Evidence to seek:** dated puppets and scripts with provenance, troupe records, full performance documentation, and consented maker, performer, and audience accounts

**Limits:** Name puppet form, troupe, place, and date. Obtain recording and reuse permission; distinguish a display object from living performance.
