## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which institutions could make and enforce a particular international decision?

**Coverage:** Map domestic authority, treaty obligations, and practical limits in one case.

**Evidence to seek:** treaties, diplomatic archives, official statements, maps, legislative records, independent historical collections, and consented affected accounts

**Limits:** Date and attribute contested territorial or causal claims; separate official position, documented action, and scholarly interpretation. Avoid forecasts and current operational advice.
