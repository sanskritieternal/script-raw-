## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did two companies stage the same dramatic moment?

**Coverage:** Compare named dates, casts, spaces, and available records.

**Evidence to seek:** dated scripts and promptbooks, playbills and tickets, design and rehearsal records, performance recordings where licensed, company accounts, and attributed performer and audience testimony

**Limits:** Name production, venue, cast, language, and date. Distinguish script from staging and recording from live event; credit all contributors and verify performance and recording rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
