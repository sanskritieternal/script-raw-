## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence links a factor to illness?

**Coverage:** Compare confounding, dose, temporality, and biological tests.

**Evidence to seek:** WHO and CDC case-definition guidance, original clinical and epidemiologic studies, laboratory methods, systematic evidence, and consented patient accounts

**Limits:** Name condition, case definition, jurisdiction, population, and date. Distinguish surveillance from diagnosis and preclinical findings from proven patient benefit.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
