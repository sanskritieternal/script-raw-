## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a quoted passengers-per-hour figure match delivered service?

**Coverage:** Check train length, frequency, occupancy, and direction.

**Evidence to seek:** authority plans and timetables, observed headways and ridership, station access audits, maintenance records, safety-oversight findings, and community testimony

**Limits:** Name line, agency, city, and period. Separate forecast capacity from delivered service and document whose journeys are included.
