## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can the story avoid trivializing victims or erasing agency?

**Coverage:** Review framing with affected communities and label invention.

**Evidence to seek:** contemporaneous records of the real event and feasible alternatives, period resource and institutional data, relevant causal scholarship, and attributed perspectives from affected groups

**Limits:** Name the real branch point and time horizon. Keep established history visually and verbally distinct from speculation; avoid deterministic chains, invented private thoughts, and trivialization of suffering.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
