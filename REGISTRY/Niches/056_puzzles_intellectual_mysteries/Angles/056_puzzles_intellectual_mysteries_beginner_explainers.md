## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which facts are given, which are rules, and what counts as a solution?

**Coverage:** Work through one small example with a checkable answer.

**Evidence to seek:** original puzzle publications and editions, designer notes, formal proofs or reproducible solver checks, competition rules, playtest evidence, and accessibility feedback

**Limits:** State the exact rule set and edition. Verify uniqueness and open-problem status, credit creators and adaptations, and distinguish a solution, heuristic, and unsupported claim.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
