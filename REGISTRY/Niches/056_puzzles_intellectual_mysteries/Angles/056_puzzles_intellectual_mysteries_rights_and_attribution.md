## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who owns or is credited for one puzzle edition or adaptation?

**Coverage:** Check dates, licenses, contracts, and jurisdiction before reuse.

**Evidence to seek:** original puzzle publications and editions, designer notes, formal proofs or reproducible solver checks, competition rules, playtest evidence, and accessibility feedback

**Limits:** State the exact rule set and edition. Verify uniqueness and open-problem status, credit creators and adaptations, and distinguish a solution, heuristic, and unsupported claim.
