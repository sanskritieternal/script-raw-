## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does an unusual patient reveal a mechanism or merely suggest one?

**Coverage:** Protect privacy and compare with broader evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** original human studies and protocols, trial registries, study datasets where appropriate, clinical guidelines, laboratory and animal studies, ethics records, and patient-reported outcomes

**Limits:** Define population, outcome, comparator, and date. Distinguish association, model result, clinical effect, and personal advice; protect patient privacy and check current guidance and harms.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
