## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What recurring support would a proposed collection or access service require?

**Coverage:** Assess one proposed archive or library service through collection rights, staffing, preservation and user access requirements; identify recurring costs.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: collection policies, accession and custody records, catalogs, treatment reports and rights restrictions. Starting framework: Society of American Archivists core values and ethics (https://www2.archivists.org/statements/saa-core-values-statement-and-code-of-ethics). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** A catalog entry is not full provenance or unrestricted reuse. Niche boundary: Collection institutions, description, preservation and access; individual manuscript interpretation and literary criticism are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
