## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a collection or public service grow, shrink or disappear?

**Coverage:** Explain growth or loss of one service or collection using funding, policy, demand and preservation records; avoid equating digitization with improved access.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** A catalog entry is not full provenance or unrestricted reuse. Niche boundary: Collection institutions, description, preservation and access; individual manuscript interpretation and literary criticism are neighboring subjects.
