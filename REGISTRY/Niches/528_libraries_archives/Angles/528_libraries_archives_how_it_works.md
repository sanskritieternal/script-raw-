## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where do rights and stewardship decisions enter an item's institutional journey?

**Coverage:** Map one item from selection and acquisition through description, storage and user access; identify rights and stewardship decisions at each step.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the process, custody transfers and assigned responsibilities. Case records: collection policies, accession and custody records, catalogs, treatment reports and rights restrictions. Starting framework: Society of American Archivists core values and ethics (https://www2.archivists.org/statements/saa-core-values-statement-and-code-of-ethics). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** A catalog entry is not full provenance or unrestricted reuse. Niche boundary: Collection institutions, description, preservation and access; individual manuscript interpretation and literary criticism are neighboring subjects.
