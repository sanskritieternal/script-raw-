## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed and persisted in one family's or community's preparation?

**Coverage:** Use attributed accounts across dated periods without implying all families agree.

**Evidence to seek:** attributed recipes and oral histories with consent, local archives, agricultural and trade records, foodway inventories, producer accounts, and relevant food-safety guidance

**Limits:** Credit makers and custodians, respect restricted knowledge, and avoid one authentic recipe claim. Distinguish culinary tradition from health claims and follow applicable food-safety guidance.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
