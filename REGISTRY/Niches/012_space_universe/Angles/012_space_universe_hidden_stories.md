## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose observing, computing, or instrument work was omitted from one discovery narrative?

**Coverage:** Use dated team records and attributed accounts without inventing motives.

**Evidence to seek:** NASA and other observatory mission archives, calibrated data and instrument papers, primary research, dated model predictions, and independent follow-up observations

**Limits:** Distinguish observed signal, model-based inference, and speculation. State time, scale, detection bias, uncertainty, and alternatives; do not present an unconfirmed signal as discovery.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
