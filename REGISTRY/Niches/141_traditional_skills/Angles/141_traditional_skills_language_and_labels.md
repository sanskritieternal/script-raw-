**Focus / example question:** How do local names differ from a museum or market label?

**Coverage:** Document translations with practitioner review and historical context.

**Evidence to seek:** consented practitioner and apprentice accounts, dated objects and records, local terminology, workshop and material records, museum provenance, and community-led safeguarding documents
