## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Skill in Its Local History

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** When and why did one community begin practicing a named skill?

**Coverage:** Trace dated records and practitioner accounts; avoid an invented single origin.

**Evidence to seek:** consented practitioner and apprentice accounts, dated objects and records, local terminology, workshop and material records, museum provenance, and community-led safeguarding documents

**Limits:** Name practice, community, place, and period. Seek free, informed, ongoing consent; respect restricted knowledge and community control; avoid claiming a single origin or frozen authentic form.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
