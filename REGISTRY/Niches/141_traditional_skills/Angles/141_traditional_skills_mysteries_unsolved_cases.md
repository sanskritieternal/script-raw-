**Focus / example question:** What can establish who made one older object?

**Coverage:** Compare provenance, material, technique, testimony, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** consented practitioner and apprentice accounts, dated objects and records, local terminology, workshop and material records, museum provenance, and community-led safeguarding documents

**Limits:** Name practice, community, place, and period. Seek free, informed, ongoing consent; respect restricted knowledge and community control; avoid claiming a single origin or frozen authentic form.
