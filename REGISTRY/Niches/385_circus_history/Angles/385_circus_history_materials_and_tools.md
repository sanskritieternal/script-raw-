## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can posters, costumes, rigs, and tickets establish?

**Coverage:** Record provenance.

**Evidence to seek:** Library of Congress circus poster collection, programs, contracts, contemporary press, oral histories, and conservation records

**Limits:** Name troupe, place, and era. Posters are advertisements, not neutral proof of an act; handle exploitation and animal use with context.
