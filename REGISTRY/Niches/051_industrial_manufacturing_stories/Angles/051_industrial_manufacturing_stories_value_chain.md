## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which firms and workers contribute to one finished product?

**Coverage:** Map material, component, assembly, distribution, and service stages.

**Evidence to seek:** dated factory and company records, worker accounts, process and quality measures, public safety and environmental records, supply-chain data, and independent product evidence

**Limits:** Name product, plant, period, and jurisdiction. Separate company claims from measured outcomes; account for workers and neighbors; avoid operational safety instructions and undocumented confidential details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
