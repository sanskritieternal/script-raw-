## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a change target energy, material use, water, waste, or cost?

**Coverage:** Define metric and full life-cycle boundary.

**Evidence to seek:** EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs

**Limits:** Define baseline, functional unit, geography, time, and impact boundary. Avoid universal green labels or guaranteed savings; account for household constraints and shifted burdens.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
