## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who may reuse music, clips, performances, and scripts in a web episode?

**Coverage:** Check actual licenses and applicable credit rules.

**Evidence to seek:** identified episodes and upload dates, scripts, call sheets, platform metadata, credits and agreements, archive captures, and bounded audience evidence

**Limits:** Name platform, release, and version. Do not equate public view counters with unique viewers or assume one credit and rights system applies to every jurisdiction.
