## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does identification or taxonomic revision alter the claim?

**Coverage:** Use validated identification, locality, and current taxonomy.

**Evidence to seek:** dated production logs and source footage where available, location and permit records, original field research and population methods, current taxonomy and conservation assessments, and attributed crew and community accounts

**Limits:** Name species, population, filming date, and setting. Disclose captive, staged, or composite scenes; distinguish footage from inference; avoid disturbance and vulnerable-location details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
