## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Testing a Lost-Technology Claim

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular claim about an ancient artifact follow from its archaeological context?

**Coverage:** Trace the claim to evidence, check dates and provenance, and compare ordinary explanations.

**Evidence to seek:** excavation reports and context records, museum collections and conservation analyses, artifact measurements, dated texts where relevant, and published experimental archaeology

**Limits:** Ground interpretations in documented provenance and dated material evidence. Label replicas and modern substitutions, protect site and cultural heritage, and never treat an evidentiary gap as proof of a sensational lost technology.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
