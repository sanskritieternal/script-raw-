## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Is there evidence of repeatable dimensions or interchangeable components in one ancient production system?

**Coverage:** Measure a defined sample and separate deliberate standardization from normal variation.

**Evidence to seek:** excavation reports and context records, museum collections and conservation analyses, artifact measurements, dated texts where relevant, and published experimental archaeology

**Limits:** Ground interpretations in documented provenance and dated material evidence. Label replicas and modern substitutions, protect site and cultural heritage, and never treat an evidentiary gap as proof of a sensational lost technology.
