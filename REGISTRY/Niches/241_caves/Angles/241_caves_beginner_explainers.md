## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a named void form in its host rock?

**Coverage:** Define rock, water or lava process, scale, and evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** geological and hydrological studies, dated surveys, site management plans, monitoring, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name cave and land authority. Respect location-sensitive data, cultural permissions, and current closures; no question is current caving or safety advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
