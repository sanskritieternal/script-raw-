## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What test would demonstrate field reliability?

**Coverage:** Define task, variation, intervention, and failure threshold.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST robot test methods, applicable standards, dated design and service records, controlled trials, and consented worker accounts

**Limits:** Name robot, task, environment, software and hardware versions, and trial count. Separate demonstrations from routine performance and use safe, authorized tests.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
