## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which experts, assistants, and tradition holders shaped a demonstration?

**Coverage:** Record consent, work, pay, credit, and decision authority.

**Evidence to seek:** dated artifacts and archaeological context, documented experiments and material tests, transparent production logs, and consented practitioner and community accounts

**Limits:** Avoid a primitive-to-advanced hierarchy and claims that one reconstruction proves historical use. Attribute community knowledge and honor restrictions; keep hazardous fabrication at documentary level and verify site access and material collection rules.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
