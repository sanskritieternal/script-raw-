**Focus / example question:** How did a flow or flood prediction compare with later gauges?

**Coverage:** Match issue date, lead time, basin, observed value, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** calibrated streamgage records and methods, basin maps, land and water-use records, sediment and water-quality surveys, habitat monitoring, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name river reach, basin, gauge, jurisdiction, season, and date. Distinguish water level from discharge and a single event from a long-term trend; use current official alerts for flood safety.
