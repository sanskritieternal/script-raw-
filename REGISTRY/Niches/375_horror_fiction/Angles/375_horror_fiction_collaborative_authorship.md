## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did writer, performer, designer, editor, and composer contribute?

**Coverage:** Use credits and production records, not single-author myth.

**Evidence to seek:** identified works and versions, scripts, production and effects records, censorship documents, dated reviews, and bounded audience research

**Limits:** Do not claim a work frightens everyone or diagnose a viewer. Separate observable craft, documented response, and interpretation of social meaning.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
