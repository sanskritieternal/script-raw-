## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which elements derive from a source and which from adaptation?

**Coverage:** Trace credits, licenses, and specific designs.

**Evidence to seek:** identified works and versions, scripts, production and effects records, censorship documents, dated reviews, and bounded audience research

**Limits:** Do not claim a work frightens everyone or diagnose a viewer. Separate observable craft, documented response, and interpretation of social meaning.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
