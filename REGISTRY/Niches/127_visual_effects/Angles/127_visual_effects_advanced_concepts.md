## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which visual cues make one composite fit photographed action?

**Coverage:** Compare perspective, lighting, motion, grain, and viewer response.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving footage and authorized shot breakdowns, production notes and credits, artist interviews, version records, contracts, and contemporary reviews

**Limits:** Name film or program, shot, version, crew, and date. Distinguish publicity from production evidence, respect artist and performer rights, and avoid unsupported claims about methods.
