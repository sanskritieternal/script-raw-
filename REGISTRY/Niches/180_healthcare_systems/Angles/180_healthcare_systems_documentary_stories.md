## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where do handoffs help or hinder care?

**Coverage:** Use consented patient experience and anonymized service records.

**Evidence to seek:** current system policies and budgets, workforce and service-capacity data, validated quality and outcome measures, clinical guidance with version, and voluntary consented patient and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction, service, population, and date. Distinguish coverage from effective care and throughput from health outcomes; protect patient information and verify medical claims with current authoritative evidence.
