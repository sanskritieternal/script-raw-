## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a familiar story match contemporaneous records?

**Coverage:** Locate the earliest claim and compare orders, diaries, casualty data, and later retellings.

**Evidence to seek:** orders and unit diaries, maps and operational records, service and casualty records, artifacts, official investigations, local archives, and consented civilian or veteran accounts

**Limits:** Attribute claims by side and date, protect private people, avoid glorifying harm or giving actionable tactics, and include civilian effects and archival gaps.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
