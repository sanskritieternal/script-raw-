## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Is one fungus a decomposer, parasite, or partner in a defined setting?

**Coverage:** Use observed substrate use, host effect, and experimental evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered fungal specimens and cultures, taxonomic revisions, repeat field surveys, verified sequence databases, host records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name fungal species or group, host or substrate, site, season, and date. Do not equate mushroom sightings with abundance or infer edibility from appearance; avoid consumption, diagnosis, or treatment advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
