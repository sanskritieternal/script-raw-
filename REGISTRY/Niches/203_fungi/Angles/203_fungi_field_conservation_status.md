## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence supports a species assessment?

**Coverage:** Check range, substrate or host dependence, threats, survey coverage, and date.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered fungal specimens and cultures, taxonomic revisions, repeat field surveys, verified sequence databases, host records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name fungal species or group, host or substrate, site, season, and date. Do not equate mushroom sightings with abundance or infer edibility from appearance; avoid consumption, diagnosis, or treatment advice.
