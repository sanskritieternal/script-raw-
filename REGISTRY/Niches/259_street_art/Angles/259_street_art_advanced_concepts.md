## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who controls an image on a privately owned public-facing wall?

**Coverage:** Examine permission, rights, maintenance, and jurisdiction.

**Evidence to seek:** dated site photographs and proposals, artist and property permissions, municipal records, conservation reports, and consented resident accounts

**Limits:** Name work, site, date, and jurisdiction. Verify rights and access; avoid exposing private residents or giving directions for unauthorized activity.
