## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which physical and written tools shape sound?

**Coverage:** Compare instrument, tuning, score, and oral teaching.

**Evidence to seek:** dated scores and editions where applicable, oral lineage records, recordings, programs, contracts, and attributed artist and audience accounts

**Limits:** Name musical tradition and work. Distinguish source notation from live interpretation; avoid treating one canon or performance as universal.
