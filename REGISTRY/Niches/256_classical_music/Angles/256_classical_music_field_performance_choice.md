## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did one performer shape timing or ornament this way?

**Coverage:** Ask the artist and compare recorded alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** dated scores and editions where applicable, oral lineage records, recordings, programs, contracts, and attributed artist and audience accounts

**Limits:** Name musical tradition and work. Distinguish source notation from live interpretation; avoid treating one canon or performance as universal.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
