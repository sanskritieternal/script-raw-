## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What method and participant safeguards are needed?

**Coverage:** State hypothesis, validation, consent, and limits.

**Evidence to seek:** NIH BRAIN research frameworks, original recording and imaging studies, protocols, participant data protections, independent replications, and model comparisons

**Limits:** Name species or participant population, task, technique, and resolution. Do not infer mind reading or clinical benefit from a narrow neural correlation.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
