## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does a course's claimed outcome actually measure?

**Coverage:** State task, level, hours, and sample.

**Evidence to seek:** published proficiency descriptors, authentic language samples, classroom task and rubric records, learner feedback, delayed assessments, and independent studies

**Limits:** Name language, learner population, task, practice time, and assessment mode. A course completion badge or recall score is not proof of general fluency.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
