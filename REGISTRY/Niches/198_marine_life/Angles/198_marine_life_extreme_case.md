## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How was one exceptional marine record verified?

**Coverage:** Check depth or size calibration, species ID, specimen, and independent records.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered specimens, expedition and survey protocols, verified imagery, environmental DNA with reference data, long-term site monitoring, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name organism, habitat, depth, region, method, and date. Distinguish detection from abundance and avoid generalizing from a single transect or species to the whole ocean.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
