## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence links one ocean condition to organism survival?

**Coverage:** Trace exposure, physiology, reproduction, movement, and alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered specimens, expedition and survey protocols, verified imagery, environmental DNA with reference data, long-term site monitoring, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name organism, habitat, depth, region, method, and date. Distinguish detection from abundance and avoid generalizing from a single transect or species to the whole ocean.
