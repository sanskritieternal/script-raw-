## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where does one learner encounter Sanskrit in community life?

**Coverage:** Use consented accounts without assuming a religious use for all learners.

**Evidence to seek:** dated curricula and textbooks, source editions and dictionaries, assessment data, institutional records, and consented learner and teacher accounts

**Limits:** State learner level, language register, text edition, and assessment goal. Avoid promising fluency from a short course; distinguish language learning from ritual authority and respect learner privacy.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
