## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which edition and register make one passage suitable for a course level?

**Coverage:** Check provenance, script, vocabulary, and commentary.

**Evidence to seek:** dated curricula and textbooks, source editions and dictionaries, assessment data, institutional records, and consented learner and teacher accounts

**Limits:** State learner level, language register, text edition, and assessment goal. Avoid promising fluency from a short course; distinguish language learning from ritual authority and respect learner privacy.
