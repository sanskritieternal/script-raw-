## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do allegation, charge, verdict, and appeal differ?

**Coverage:** Apply terms to one closed case with dated documents.

**Evidence to seek:** court records, official investigations, dated reporting, relevant research, and consented survivor accounts

**Limits:** Protect victims and uncharged people; distinguish allegations from findings and avoid actionable crime details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
