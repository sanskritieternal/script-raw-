## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which specific evidence could responsibly change the public account?

**Coverage:** State uncertainty and avoid predictions or crowdsourced accusations.

**Evidence to seek:** court records, official investigations, dated reporting, relevant research, and consented survivor accounts

**Limits:** Protect victims and uncharged people; distinguish allegations from findings and avoid actionable crime details.
