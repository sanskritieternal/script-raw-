## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which checks establish whether reasons support a conclusion?

**Coverage:** Assess premise acceptability, relevance, sufficiency, and counterarguments.

**Evidence to seek:** original arguments and source documents, formal reconstructions, relevant empirical evidence, dated corrections, and anonymized learner work

**Limits:** Quote claims in context and distinguish validity from premise truth. Avoid fallacy-name shortcuts, motive speculation, and treating disagreement itself as proof of error.
