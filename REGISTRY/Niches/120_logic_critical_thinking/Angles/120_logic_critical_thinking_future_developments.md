## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which possible evidence would alter support for one conclusion?

**Coverage:** State a falsifiable threshold and plausible alternatives before outcome.

**Evidence to seek:** original arguments and source documents, formal reconstructions, relevant empirical evidence, dated corrections, and anonymized learner work

**Limits:** Quote claims in context and distinguish validity from premise truth. Avoid fallacy-name shortcuts, motive speculation, and treating disagreement itself as proof of error.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
