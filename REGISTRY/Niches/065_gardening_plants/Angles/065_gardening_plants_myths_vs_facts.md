## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a common plant-care rule hold for a named species and setting?

**Coverage:** Compare controlled evidence with local soil, climate, and cultivar limits.

**Evidence to seek:** current taxonomic and distribution records, plant trial methods and results, local climate and soil measurements, extension research, and documented gardener and community experience

**Limits:** Name accepted species or cultivar, place, soil, season, and management. Avoid universal care rules or remote diagnosis; check current local invasive, toxic-plant, pesticide, and conservation guidance before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
