## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What information does one product collect and transmit?

**Coverage:** Map data flow, access, updates, retention, and user controls.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned product specifications and standards, independent electrical tests, recalls and failure reports, repair documentation, manufacturing records, and consented user and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name model, version, use, jurisdiction, and date. Keep mains electricity, batteries, and circuit work at documentary level and refer hazardous testing or repair to qualified professionals.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
