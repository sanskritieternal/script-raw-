## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which official, local, and devotional names coexist?

**Coverage:** Record language, tradition, community use, and attribution.

**Evidence to seek:** current custodial and government route notices, dated maps and archival sources, visitor and service records, and consented pilgrim, worker, and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name tradition, route, custodian, jurisdiction, season, and date. Attribute devotional claims respectfully; do not generalize one faith's practice, publish private rituals without consent, or use historic route descriptions as current travel guidance.
