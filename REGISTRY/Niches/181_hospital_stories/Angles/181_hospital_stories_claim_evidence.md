## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a study support the way a hospital intervention is described?

**Coverage:** Compare population, comparator, outcomes, and evidence quality.

**Evidence to seek:** published clinical guidance, hospital service data, safety investigations, dated records, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Protect patient privacy and consent; define population and avoid diagnosis or individualized treatment advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
