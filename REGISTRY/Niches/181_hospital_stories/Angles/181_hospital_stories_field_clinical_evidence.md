## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which clinical evidence supports one local protocol?

**Coverage:** Check relevance, certainty, and exceptions.

**Evidence to seek:** published clinical guidance, hospital service data, safety investigations, dated records, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Protect patient privacy and consent; define population and avoid diagnosis or individualized treatment advice.
