## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where did sampled grade fail to predict recovered output?

**Coverage:** Check sampling, dilution, and processing data.

**Evidence to seek:** dated mine and permit records, assays and production balances, MSHA investigations, USGS environmental studies, water monitoring, and community accounts

**Limits:** Name commodity, mine, owner, jurisdiction, and period. Distinguish resources from recoverable reserves and measured impacts from forecasts; avoid operational extraction instructions.
