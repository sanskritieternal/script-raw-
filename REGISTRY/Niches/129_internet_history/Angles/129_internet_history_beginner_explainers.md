## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do interconnected networks differ from websites and browsers?

**Coverage:** Follow one web request through access network, DNS, routing, and server.

**Evidence to seek:** original RFCs and papers, archived code and network logs, standards discussions, governance agreements, infrastructure records, and participant accounts

**Limits:** Name network, protocol, institution, and date. Distinguish internet from web, proposal from deployment, and individual contribution from collaborative infrastructure; omit exploitable details.
