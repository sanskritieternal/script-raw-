## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What soil, light, space, and season does one plant need locally?

**Coverage:** Use region-specific extension advice and actual site measurements.

**Evidence to seek:** local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons

**Limits:** Specify region, season, site, plant, and test method. A single garden result does not establish a universal growing rule or guaranteed yield.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
