## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Did the New Firm Solve a Problem

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did customers receive at what total cost?

**Coverage:** Compare alternatives, quality, access, and repeat use.

**Evidence to seek:** business demography definitions and data, dated registrations and financial records, current local requirements, and consented founder, worker, and customer accounts

**Limits:** Name market, jurisdiction, cohort, and period. Avoid founder hero stories, survivor-only samples, and generic legal or financing advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
