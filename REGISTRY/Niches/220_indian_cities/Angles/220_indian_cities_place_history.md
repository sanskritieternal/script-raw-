## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose homes, work, and land uses preceded redevelopment?

**Coverage:** Use maps, land and court records, and consented community memory.

**Evidence to seek:** dated municipal and census records with definitions, city plans and budgets, service-performance data, validated maps, land records, and consented resident and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name city, ward or metro boundary, jurisdiction, population source, and date. Do not compare city rankings or growth with incompatible boundaries or years; protect residents' privacy and rights.
