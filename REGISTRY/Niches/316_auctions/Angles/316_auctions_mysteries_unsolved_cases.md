## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did similar lots sell differently?

**Coverage:** Check condition, provenance, timing, bidder count, and fees.

**Evidence to seek:** dated catalogues and terms, consignment and settlement records where available, provenance documentation, and consented buyer and seller accounts

**Limits:** Name auction, lot, venue, date, and jurisdiction. Distinguish hammer price from total paid and allegation from verified authenticity; do not give bidding advice.
