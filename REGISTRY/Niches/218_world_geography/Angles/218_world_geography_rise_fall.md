## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a mapped settlement or habitat expand or shrink?

**Coverage:** Compare classification, land use, population, imagery, and date.

**Evidence to seek:** dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name place, source, date, map scale, and boundary or naming authority. Attribute contested territorial claims and local names; do not treat one projection or official label as the only perspective.
