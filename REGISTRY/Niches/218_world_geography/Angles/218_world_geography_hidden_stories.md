## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose fieldwork and local knowledge appear or disappear in one map?

**Coverage:** Trace surveyors, Indigenous and local contributors, editors, and data sources.

**Evidence to seek:** dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name place, source, date, map scale, and boundary or naming authority. Attribute contested territorial claims and local names; do not treat one projection or official label as the only perspective.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
