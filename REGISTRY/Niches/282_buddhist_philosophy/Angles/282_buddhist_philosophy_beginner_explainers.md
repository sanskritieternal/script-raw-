## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do two named schools address a bounded philosophical question?

**Coverage:** Represent each from its own sources without a single Buddhist position.

**Evidence to seek:** specified Pali, Sanskrit, Chinese, Tibetan, or other source editions as relevant, parallel-text catalogues, manuscripts, translations, commentaries, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name the school, language, text, version, and interpreter. Distinguish a tradition's normative claim from historical analysis and avoid presenting one argument as universal Buddhism.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
