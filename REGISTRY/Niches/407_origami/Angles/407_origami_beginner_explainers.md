## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do mountain, valley, and orientation marks map to paper?

**Coverage:** Compare diagram and one sample without claiming one notation everywhere.

**Evidence to seek:** dated folded objects and diagrams, authorized design records, maker interviews, tested prototypes, mathematical proofs, and conservation records

**Limits:** Name the model, design version, paper, and tradition. Separate ideal geometry from physical performance and avoid a single-origin claim for shared folds.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
