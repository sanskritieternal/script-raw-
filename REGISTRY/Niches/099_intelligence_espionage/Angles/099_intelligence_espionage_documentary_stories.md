## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one case become knowable through release or inquiry?

**Coverage:** Trace original documents, declassification, redaction, and later correction.

**Evidence to seek:** declassified records, published laws and opinions, oversight and inspector-general reports, inquiry findings, and consented affected accounts

**Limits:** Use only public records; mark redactions and unknowns. Separate allegation, agency claim, and independent finding; protect private identities and omit operational tradecraft.
