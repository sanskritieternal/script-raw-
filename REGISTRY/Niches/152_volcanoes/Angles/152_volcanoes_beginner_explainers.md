## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one current alert level mean and not mean?

**Coverage:** Use the issuing agency's definitions, date, area, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** geologic maps and dated samples, observatory time series and archived bulletins, official hazard maps, meteorological records, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name volcano, area, event, and date. Use current official alerts for any present hazard, never imply exact prediction, and keep field access and sampling with qualified authorities and permissions.
