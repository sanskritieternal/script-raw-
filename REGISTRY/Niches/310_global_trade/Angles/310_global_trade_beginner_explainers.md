## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which documents, payments, and logistics cross a border?

**Coverage:** Map one product and pair of countries.

**Evidence to seek:** dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts

**Limits:** Name economies, product code, route, currency, and period. Check current tariffs with authorities; separate gross trade, reexports, and value added.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
