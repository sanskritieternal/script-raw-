## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** When do morphology, genetics, and reproduction suggest different classifications?

**Coverage:** Choose one disputed complex and compare the evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered herbarium specimens, taxonomic revisions, repeat plant plots, phenology observations, controlled studies, land-use records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name plant species, place, life stage, season, and date. Distinguish one collection or bloom from a population trend and attribute local and Indigenous knowledge with consent and context.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
