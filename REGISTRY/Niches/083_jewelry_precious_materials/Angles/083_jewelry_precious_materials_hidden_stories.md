## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose mining, cutting, setting, or repair labor is missing?

**Coverage:** Trace public supply evidence and attributed worker accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** identified objects and maker records, independent gem and metal reports, dated custody and trade documents, workshop and worker accounts, appraisal terms, and conservation records

**Limits:** Name object, material, report, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish grade from value and lab identification from proven origin; verify current disclosure and sourcing rules and avoid personal investment advice.
