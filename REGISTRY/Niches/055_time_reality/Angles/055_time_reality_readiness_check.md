## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence supports wider use of a new timekeeping technique?

**Coverage:** Compare repeatability, calibration, uncertainty, infrastructure, and independent labs.

**Evidence to seek:** primary physics papers and measurement records, BIPM and national-metrology standards, original philosophical texts, official civil-time decisions, and cited creative works

**Limits:** Distinguish measured time effects, philosophical interpretations, subjective reports, policy conventions, and fiction. State frame, definition, date, uncertainty, and source; never present speculative time travel as established physics.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
