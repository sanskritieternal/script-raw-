## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What happens when a laboratory result becomes a technology claim?

**Coverage:** Check validation, safety, access, and benefit.

**Evidence to seek:** original papers, experiment descriptions, calibration and uncertainty budgets, NIST metrology guidance, and independent replications

**Limits:** Name experiment, measurand, unit, model assumptions, and uncertainty. Separate measured result from broad theoretical interpretation.
