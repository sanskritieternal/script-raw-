## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do measurement, units, and a model answer one question?

**Coverage:** Use a bounded experiment and explain uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** original papers, experiment descriptions, calibration and uncertainty budgets, NIST metrology guidance, and independent replications

**Limits:** Name experiment, measurand, unit, model assumptions, and uncertainty. Separate measured result from broad theoretical interpretation.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
