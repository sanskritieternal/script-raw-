## SER-343-001 — From units to measurements

Entry requirement: No prior episode required; explain specialist terms on first use.

Learning outcome: Distinguish definitions, practical realization and measurement uncertainty.

Ordering reason: A definition establishes the reference; a method realizes it; uncertainty qualifies results.

1. **IDEA-343-0001** — What it means to define a unit (ready_for_project). In the current SI Brochure, what distinguishes a unit definition from a measurement made using that unit?
2. **IDEA-343-0002** — From a definition to a laboratory result (ready_for_project). For one selected SI unit, what additional steps connect its definition to a practical measurement?
3. **IDEA-343-0003** — Exact definition, uncertain measurement (ready_for_project). Why does an exact defining constant not remove uncertainty from a measurement made with a real instrument?
