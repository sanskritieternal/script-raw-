## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a public claim about distance, cost, or discovery match mission records?

**Coverage:** Read official data and distinguish milestone from scientific inference.

**Evidence to seek:** mission requirements and design reviews, telemetry and instrument calibration, official mission logs, archived data, primary research, and dated anomaly reports

**Limits:** Distinguish planned capabilities, telemetry, processed data, and scientific inference. Date mission status and launch claims; state operating limits and uncertainty.
