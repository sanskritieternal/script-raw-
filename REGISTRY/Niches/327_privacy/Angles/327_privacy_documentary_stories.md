## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can a user see, correct, or delete a record?

**Coverage:** Follow consented steps, response time, and limits.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST privacy frameworks, dated product disclosures, contracts, data-flow observations, audits, and consented user accounts

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction, system, version, data type, and affected people. Privacy risk is broader than a breach; do not expose real personal records or offer case-specific legal advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
