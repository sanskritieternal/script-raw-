## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a satisfaction score match residents' accounts of daily use?

**Coverage:** Compare survey design with interviews and observation.

**Evidence to seek:** dimensioned plans, dated briefs and revisions, product specifications, applicable access guidance, itemized costs, and consented occupant evidence

**Limits:** Name room, occupants, jurisdiction, and date. Do not infer usability from photographs or claim code compliance without the applicable rule and qualified review.
