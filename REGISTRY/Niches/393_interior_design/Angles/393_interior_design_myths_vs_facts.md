## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do photographs hide about clearances and actual capacity?

**Coverage:** Compare lens view with measured floor plan.

**Evidence to seek:** dimensioned plans, dated briefs and revisions, product specifications, applicable access guidance, itemized costs, and consented occupant evidence
