## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Reading a Room Plan

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do circulation, light, storage, and furniture fit a measured space?

**Coverage:** Show dimensions and intended users.

**Evidence to seek:** dimensioned plans, dated briefs and revisions, product specifications, applicable access guidance, itemized costs, and consented occupant evidence

**Limits:** Name room, occupants, jurisdiction, and date. Do not infer usability from photographs or claim code compliance without the applicable rule and qualified review.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
