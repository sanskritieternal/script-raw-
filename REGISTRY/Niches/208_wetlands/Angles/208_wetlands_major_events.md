## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed in one wetland after a documented event?

**Coverage:** Compare water levels, sediment, vegetation, species, and recovery.

**Evidence to seek:** site management and inventory records, measured water levels and flows, mapped soils and vegetation, repeat biological surveys, land-use records, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name wetland, boundary, jurisdiction, season, and measure. Distinguish a dry season from long-term loss and mapped area from ecological function; use current local sources for legal classifications.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
