## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How are hazards identified and prioritized for one workplace task?

**Coverage:** Examine how one published assessment identifies and prioritizes hazards for a defined task; discuss reasoning without giving unsafe operating instructions.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Starting framework: ILO occupational safety management guidelines (https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40ed_protect/%40protrav/%40safework/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_107727.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use current site rules; do not present an editorial angle as safety certification. Niche boundary: Occupational hazard control and prevention systems; general public safety and engineering failure are neighboring subjects.
