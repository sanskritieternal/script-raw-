**Focus / example question:** What evidence supports one protective design in its intended setting?

**Coverage:** Compare protective design options described in a published case using exposure, usability and maintenance evidence; defer implementation to qualified site review.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use current site rules; do not present an editorial angle as safety certification. Niche boundary: Occupational hazard control and prevention systems; general public safety and engineering failure are neighboring subjects.
