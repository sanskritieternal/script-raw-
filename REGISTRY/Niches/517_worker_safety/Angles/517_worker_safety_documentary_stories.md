## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** One Worker Safety Evidence Trail

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a worker concern move through investigation, prevention and follow-up?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one prevention effort from worker reports through investigation, action and follow-up; retain unresolved questions.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Starting framework: ILO occupational safety management guidelines (https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40ed_protect/%40protrav/%40safework/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_107727.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use current site rules; do not present an editorial angle as safety certification. Niche boundary: Occupational hazard control and prevention systems; general public safety and engineering failure are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
