## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** When a Worker Safety Approach Faltered

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a safety approach gain or lose practical support?

**Coverage:** Explain the adoption or withdrawal of one safety approach using incident, exposure, cost and implementation evidence; distinguish correlation from cause.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Starting framework: ILO occupational safety management guidelines (https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40ed_protect/%40protrav/%40safework/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_107727.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use current site rules; do not present an editorial angle as safety certification. Niche boundary: Occupational hazard control and prevention systems; general public safety and engineering failure are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
