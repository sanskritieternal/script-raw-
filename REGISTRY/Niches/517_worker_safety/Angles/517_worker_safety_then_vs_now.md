## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a safety trend persist under equivalent hours, tasks and reporting rules?

**Coverage:** Compare safety outcomes across two periods using equivalent work hours, tasks, exposure measures and reporting rules.

**Evidence to seek:** Align definitions, samples, versions and measurement conditions. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Starting framework: ILO occupational safety management guidelines (https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40ed_protect/%40protrav/%40safework/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_107727.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use current site rules; do not present an editorial angle as safety certification. Niche boundary: Occupational hazard control and prevention systems; general public safety and engineering failure are neighboring subjects.
