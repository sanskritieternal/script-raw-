## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** An Ecosystem After Disturbance

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after one fire, flood, drought, or spill?

**Coverage:** Compare baseline, exposure, recovery sequence, and unaffected references.

**Evidence to seek:** dated ecosystem maps and land-use records, repeat field protocols, sensor calibration, long-term monitoring, reference sites, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name place, ecosystem boundary, measure, season, and time span. Distinguish one indicator from overall system health and correlation from a demonstrated causal pathway.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
