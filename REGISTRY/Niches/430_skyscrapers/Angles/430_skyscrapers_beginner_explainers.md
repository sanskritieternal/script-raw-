## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do loads travel through floors, frame, and foundations?

**Coverage:** Use one documented structural scheme.

**Evidence to seek:** dated planning and as-built drawings, adopted codes and permits, wind and fire studies, commissioning and maintenance records, occupancy data, and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name building, jurisdiction, permit date, occupied use, and measurement boundary. Height rankings and energy claims depend on definitions; approval is not a universal safety guarantee.
