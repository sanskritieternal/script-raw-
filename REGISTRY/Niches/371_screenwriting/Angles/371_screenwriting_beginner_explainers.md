## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does action, dialogue, and silence deliver necessary information?

**Coverage:** Annotate one bounded script scene and its screen version.

**Evidence to seek:** dated outlines and screenplay drafts, production revision pages, completed films, archive catalogs, participant accounts, and applicable guild credit rules

**Limits:** Name the script version and jurisdiction. Distinguish contribution from formal credit, and written intention from decisions made in performance, camera, and edit.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
