## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How well does one assessment capture the knowledge it claims to test?

**Coverage:** Compare task, scoring, reliability, access, and later application.

**Evidence to seek:** current institutional policies and course materials, anonymized learner work and outcomes, evaluations with defined comparators, accessibility review, and voluntary consented staff and learner accounts

**Limits:** Name institution, course, cohort, jurisdiction, and date. Protect minors and student records, obtain proper consent, avoid identifying struggling learners, and do not claim a teaching method works universally from one classroom.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
