## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a prototype platform differ from a self-sufficient settlement?

**Coverage:** Define population and services.

**Evidence to seek:** IPCC coastal-settlements assessment, UN-Habitat prototype records, marine engineering studies, environmental reviews, and coastal-community accounts

**Limits:** Name location, hazard, project stage, and legal jurisdiction. A prototype proposal is not an inhabited self-sufficient city or established climate solution.
