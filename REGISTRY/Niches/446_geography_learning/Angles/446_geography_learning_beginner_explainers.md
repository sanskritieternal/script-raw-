## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do legend, scale, date, and projection alter meaning?

**Coverage:** Use a concrete map and ground example.

**Evidence to seek:** dated maps and metadata, Library of Congress map-teaching resources, field observations, official geographic datasets, community accounts, and learner responses

**Limits:** Name map author, date, scale, projection, and territorial status. A map is a situated source; avoid presenting contested borders or sensitive locations as settled.
