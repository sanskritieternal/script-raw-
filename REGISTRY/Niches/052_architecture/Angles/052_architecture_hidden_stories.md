## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Who Built the Building?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose craft, labor, or displaced community is absent from its architectural story?

**Coverage:** Use contracts, payroll, oral history, and land records where available.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and permits, building surveys, conservation and designation records, contracts, measured performance, and attributed user and community accounts

**Limits:** Name building, place, period, and users. Distinguish original fabric from alterations and design intent from observed performance; verify current access and preservation rules before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
