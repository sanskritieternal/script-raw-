## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did plot, street, climate, and neighboring uses shape its design?

**Coverage:** Compare period maps, planning records, and site conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and permits, building surveys, conservation and designation records, contracts, measured performance, and attributed user and community accounts

**Limits:** Name building, place, period, and users. Distinguish original fabric from alterations and design intent from observed performance; verify current access and preservation rules before production.
