## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a celebrated design anecdote match contemporary records?

**Coverage:** Trace its earliest source and compare drawings, letters, and fabric.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and permits, building surveys, conservation and designation records, contracts, measured performance, and attributed user and community accounts

**Limits:** Name building, place, period, and users. Distinguish original fabric from alterations and design intent from observed performance; verify current access and preservation rules before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
