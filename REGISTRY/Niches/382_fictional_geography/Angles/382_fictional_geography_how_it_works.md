## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can the sequence of travel fit the geography the work establishes?

**Coverage:** Trace locations, times, and stated transport.

**Evidence to seek:** identified texts and maps, dated sketches, publisher and production records, archive catalogs, credited cartographers, and situated reader responses

**Limits:** State the fictional work and edition. Treat maps as versioned creative artifacts rather than complete or physically exact surveys; attribute real-world references.
