## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a heat event affect different coral species at one site?

**Coverage:** Compare exposure, baseline cover, bleaching, mortality, and recovery.

**Evidence to seek:** dated reef monitoring protocols and surveys, temperature and water-quality records, verified imagery, controlled restoration studies, local management plans, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name reef, depth, species, metric, and survey date. Distinguish bleaching from mortality and coral cover from overall reef health; avoid extrapolating one image or restored plot to an entire reef.
