## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do lab notes, data, publications, and corrections reveal?

**Coverage:** Trace provenance and make gaps visible.

**Evidence to seek:** primary research papers and data, protocols, instrument calibration, independent replications, model documentation, and dated corrections

**Limits:** State the exact claim, scale, measurement, uncertainty, and competing explanation. Do not turn an open question into a solved one or generalize a result beyond its tested conditions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
