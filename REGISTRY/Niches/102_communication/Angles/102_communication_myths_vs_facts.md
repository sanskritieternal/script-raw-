**Focus / example question:** Does evidence support a claim that one device caused poorer family communication?

**Coverage:** Define outcome, comparator, and other changes before inferring cause.

**Evidence to seek:** consented and anonymized participant accounts, household time-use data, service communication records, and applicable accessibility and psychosocial guidance

**Limits:** Use a defined household setting and protect privacy. Do not diagnose relationships or offer personal counseling; seek current guidance for safety-sensitive cases.
