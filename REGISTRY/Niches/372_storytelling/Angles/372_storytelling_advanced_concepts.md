## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changes when the same events are told out of order or through another narrator?

**Coverage:** Compare bounded versions and information available to audiences.

**Evidence to seek:** dated story versions, recordings, oral-history and archive provenance, production notes, rights and consent records, and situated audience testimony

**Limits:** Name the teller, version, audience, and context. Avoid universal story formulas or claims of a single origin, and check attribution and community protocols before reuse.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
