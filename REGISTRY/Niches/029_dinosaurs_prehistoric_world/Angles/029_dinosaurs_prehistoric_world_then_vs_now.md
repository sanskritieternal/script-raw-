**Focus / example question:** Why does a current depiction differ from an older one?

**Coverage:** Compare available fossils, living relatives, methods, and artistic inference.

**Evidence to seek:** field notes and stratigraphic records, curated specimens and scans, dated paleontology papers, fossil measurements, comparative anatomy, and geological dating studies

**Limits:** Separate preserved evidence from inference and visual restoration. Verify specimen provenance and collection permissions, name geological age and uncertainty, and avoid treating one fossil as a complete species portrait.
