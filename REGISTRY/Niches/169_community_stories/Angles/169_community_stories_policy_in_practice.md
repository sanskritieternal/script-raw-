## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did one policy change the service residents receive?

**Coverage:** Compare text, budget, rollout, complaints, and outcomes.

**Evidence to seek:** community-held archives and meeting records, public budgets and service data, appropriately sampled surveys, and voluntary consented accounts from varied members

**Limits:** Name community, place, organization, and period. Do not present one spokesperson as consensus; protect small-group and third-party privacy, establish ongoing consent, and return context and review opportunities to participants.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
