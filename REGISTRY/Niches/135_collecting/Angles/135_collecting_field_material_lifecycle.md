**Focus / example question:** How does material degradation alter one item's care and use?

**Coverage:** Trace condition, treatment, storage, and documented change.

**Evidence to seek:** dated inventories and accession files, material and condition records, transfer documents, relevant collection policies and law, and attributed maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name object type, collection, jurisdiction, and date. Document provenance gaps, separate market price from cultural value, and respect source-community rights and privacy.
