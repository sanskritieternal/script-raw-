## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose sourcing, cataloguing, repair, or community labor is uncredited?

**Coverage:** Use accession records and attributed contributor accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated inventories and accession files, material and condition records, transfer documents, relevant collection policies and law, and attributed maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name object type, collection, jurisdiction, and date. Document provenance gaps, separate market price from cultural value, and respect source-community rights and privacy.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
