## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does one community draw on a school in a specific setting?

**Coverage:** Use consented voices and avoid generalizing to all adherents.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Sanskrit texts and manuscripts, critical editions and translations, named commentaries, institutional records, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Attribute each philosophical claim to its school, text, and commentator. Distinguish devotional affirmation from historical analysis and never present one position as universal Hindu doctrine.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
