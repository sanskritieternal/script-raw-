## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What role does a philosophical text play in one documented ritual setting?

**Coverage:** Ask participants and inspect local texts without assuming universal use.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Sanskrit texts and manuscripts, critical editions and translations, named commentaries, institutional records, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Attribute each philosophical claim to its school, text, and commentator. Distinguish devotional affirmation from historical analysis and never present one position as universal Hindu doctrine.
