## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which source discovery or translation choice could change one interpretation?

**Coverage:** State what evidence is missing without forecasting doctrinal change.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Sanskrit texts and manuscripts, critical editions and translations, named commentaries, institutional records, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Attribute each philosophical claim to its school, text, and commentator. Distinguish devotional affirmation from historical analysis and never present one position as universal Hindu doctrine.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
