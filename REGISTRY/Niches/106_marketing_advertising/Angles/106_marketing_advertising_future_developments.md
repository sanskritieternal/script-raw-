**Focus / example question:** What evidence would justify using a proposed automated ad tool?

**Coverage:** Define accuracy, bias, privacy, cost, and human review conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated campaign materials and contracts, claim-substantiation files where available, audience tests, independent sales measures, and current advertising guidance

**Limits:** Name campaign, platform, market, jurisdiction, and date. Separate exposure from incremental effect, disclose paid relationships, and verify applicable advertising and privacy rules.
