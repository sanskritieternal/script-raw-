**Focus / example question:** How should a damaged work be treated?

**Coverage:** Compare condition, artist intent, owner, community, and reversibility.

**Evidence to seek:** provenanced objects and models, studio and foundry records, commission contracts, site plans, conservation reports, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, maker and collaborators, site, and date. Distinguish intent from reception and obtain rights information before reproducing or relocating work.
