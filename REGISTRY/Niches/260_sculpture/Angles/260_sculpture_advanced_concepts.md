## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does relocating a sculpture change meaning?

**Coverage:** Compare original and current site, access, and interpretation.

**Evidence to seek:** provenanced objects and models, studio and foundry records, commission contracts, site plans, conservation reports, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, maker and collaborators, site, and date. Distinguish intent from reception and obtain rights information before reproducing or relocating work.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
