## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which monitoring and modeling choices change an exposure estimate?

**Coverage:** Compare methods for estimating one pollution exposure or effect; examine monitor placement, calibration, weather and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: calibrated monitor series, averaging definitions, emissions inventories and original exposure studies. Starting framework: WHO global air quality guidelines (https://www.who.int/Publications/i/Item/9789240034228). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State pollutant, monitor location and averaging period. Niche boundary: Air pollutants, monitoring, exposure and control; climate dynamics and whole public-health programs remain separate.
