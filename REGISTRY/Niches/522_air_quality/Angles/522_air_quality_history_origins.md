**Focus / example question:** Which pollution problem shaped an air-quality measure or policy?

**Coverage:** Trace one air-quality measure or policy through the pollution problem and monitoring methods that shaped it; name place and period.

**Limits:** State pollutant, monitor location and averaging period. Niche boundary: Air pollutants, monitoring, exposure and control; climate dynamics and whole public-health programs remain separate.
