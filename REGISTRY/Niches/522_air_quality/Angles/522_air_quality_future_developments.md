## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What implementation and monitoring would a proposed clean-air measure require?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed clean-air measure through source coverage, enforcement, monitoring and expected exposure pathways; label modeled outcomes.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: calibrated monitor series, averaging definitions, emissions inventories and original exposure studies. Starting framework: WHO global air quality guidelines (https://www.who.int/Publications/i/Item/9789240034228). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State pollutant, monitor location and averaging period. Niche boundary: Air pollutants, monitoring, exposure and control; climate dynamics and whole public-health programs remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
