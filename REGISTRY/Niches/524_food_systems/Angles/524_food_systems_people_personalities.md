## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose documented incentives shaped a food policy or supply-chain decision?

**Coverage:** Reconstruct one food policy or supply-chain decision through documented authority, incentives and affected participants' accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** Use decision records, assigned authority and attributed accounts. Case records: material and price flows, production and distribution records, worker accounts and household access measures. Starting framework: FAO agrifood systems resources (https://www.fao.org/food-systems/en). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Set system boundary and period; avoid single-cause food narratives. Niche boundary: Interactions across food production, processing, distribution and access; farm methods, food culture and individual nutrition are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
