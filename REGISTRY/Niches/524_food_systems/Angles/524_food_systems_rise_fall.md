## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a food-system practice expand or decline?

**Coverage:** Explain expansion or decline of one food-system practice using production, demand, policy and market evidence; avoid a single-cause account.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: material and price flows, production and distribution records, worker accounts and household access measures. Starting framework: FAO agrifood systems resources (https://www.fao.org/food-systems/en). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Set system boundary and period; avoid single-cause food narratives. Niche boundary: Interactions across food production, processing, distribution and access; farm methods, food culture and individual nutrition are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
