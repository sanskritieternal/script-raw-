## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which elements of a specific film were generated, recorded, or edited by people?

**Coverage:** Map source assets to final frames and sound, noting unknown provenance.

**Evidence to seek:** finished films, scripts and storyboards, asset and version ledgers, model documentation, production credits and agreements, performer consent, and dated creator accounts

**Limits:** Identify generated elements and their provenance. Obtain required rights and consent for people and source assets; disclose material synthetic content and verify current platform and contract terms.
