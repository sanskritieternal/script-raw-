## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How easily can parts be accessed, replaced, or reused?

**Coverage:** Compare actual repairs, part availability, fasteners, and costs.

**Evidence to seek:** dated maker plans and versions, manufacturer material data, prototype and independent test records, product safety guidance, licenses, and attributed craft-practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Bound projects to low-risk examples. Avoid hazardous tool, electrical, chemical, or structural instructions; check current manufacturer and local safety rules, and state when qualified work is required.
