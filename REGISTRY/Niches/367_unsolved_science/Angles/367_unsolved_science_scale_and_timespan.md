## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a laboratory effect persist across relevant distances or times?

**Coverage:** Check scaling assumptions and available measurements.

**Evidence to seek:** peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant

**Limits:** Date the question and its status. Separate observation from explanation, failed replication from disproof, and a partial result from a solved problem.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
