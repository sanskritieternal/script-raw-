## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which physical, financial, or social barriers constrain one action?

**Coverage:** Measure available options before attributing behavior to preference.

**Evidence to seek:** peer-reviewed and replicated studies, preregistered protocols where available, behavior measures with validation, comparable surveys, institutional records, and consented participant accounts

**Limits:** Name behavior, population, setting, method, and date. Avoid universal human-nature claims, mind reading, or diagnosis; distinguish association from causal evidence and protect participant privacy.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
