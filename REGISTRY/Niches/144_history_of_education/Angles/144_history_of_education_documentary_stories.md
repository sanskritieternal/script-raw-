## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can records reveal about a defined group of pupils?

**Coverage:** Follow enrollment, attendance, transition, and privacy limits.

**Evidence to seek:** dated laws and curricula, school archives and budgets, enrollment metadata, historical maps and objects, and consented former pupil, teacher, and community accounts

**Limits:** Name place, period, school type, and population. Distinguish enrollment, attendance, completion, and learning; protect personal and child records; do not infer one universal school experience from elite archives.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
