## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one scientist or community observer document ice change?

**Coverage:** Use dated logs, methods, collaboration, and credited knowledge.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned satellite and field datasets, ice-core and glacier records, documented methods and uncertainty, peer-reviewed process studies, and consented community-led observations

**Limits:** Name region, ice type, metric, period, and dataset version. Distinguish weather from climate and floating from land ice; honor Indigenous knowledge and consent; use current official safety information for travel.
