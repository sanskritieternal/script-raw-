## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose experiences are missing from the official account of a boundary shift?

**Coverage:** Use consented testimony and records of residence, services, and movement.

**Evidence to seek:** dated treaties and statutes, official boundary maps, court decisions, crossing rules, oversight reports, and consented resident accounts

**Limits:** Attribute competing territorial claims and date every map; distinguish legal delimitation, demarcation, and effective administration. Verify current rules and protect vulnerable interviewees.
