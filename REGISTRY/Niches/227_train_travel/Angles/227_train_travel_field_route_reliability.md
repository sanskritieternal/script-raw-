## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How often would a defined transfer work?

**Coverage:** Use observed arrival data, timetable buffer, station layout, and alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** current operator timetables and fare rules, dated punctuality and ridership data, station accessibility and assistance reports, infrastructure records, and consented passenger and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name route, operator, travel date, passenger needs, and currency. Verify current timetable and assistance with the operator; do not infer an accessible complete trip from one accessible train or station.
