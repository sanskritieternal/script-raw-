## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does an ad explicitly and implicitly promise?

**Coverage:** Identify audience, product, channel, evidence, and omission.

**Evidence to seek:** dated campaign materials and briefs, pre-claim substantiation, controlled lift studies, platform and spend data, and applicable authority records

**Limits:** Name ad, product, audience, channel, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish impressions and clicks from incremental outcomes and allegations from rulings.
