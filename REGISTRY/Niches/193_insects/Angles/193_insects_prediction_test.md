## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did one projected insect range shift match later observations?

**Coverage:** Compare model assumptions, survey effort, dates, and occurrence data.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered museum specimens, taxonomic revisions, repeatable field surveys, ecological studies, dated range records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name the species or group, location, life stage, season, and date. Do not generalize from one species to all insects or claim broad declines from non-comparable sightings; distinguish insects from other arthropods.
