## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who benefits and what else is affected by controlling one insect?

**Coverage:** Assess target reduction, non-target species, cost, and local needs.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered museum specimens, taxonomic revisions, repeatable field surveys, ecological studies, dated range records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name the species or group, location, life stage, season, and date. Do not generalize from one species to all insects or claim broad declines from non-comparable sightings; distinguish insects from other arthropods.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
