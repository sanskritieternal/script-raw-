## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Physical Change at a Sacred Site

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How has erosion, water, vegetation, or development changed one place?

**Coverage:** Use dated environmental records and attributed cultural meaning.

**Evidence to seek:** dated texts and maps, heritage and land records, environmental assessments, access rules, and consented custodian and resident accounts

**Limits:** Attribute sacred meanings and disputed place claims; date maps and distinguish official boundaries from lived geography. Respect restricted locations, custodial protocols, and current access rules.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
