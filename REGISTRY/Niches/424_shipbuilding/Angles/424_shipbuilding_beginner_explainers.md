## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do hull form and cargo placement affect a ship's behavior?

**Coverage:** Use one vessel type and loading condition.

**Evidence to seek:** dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations

**Limits:** Name ship type, build or refit date, flag, route, and loading condition. Approval and trials apply to defined conditions, not every voyage.
