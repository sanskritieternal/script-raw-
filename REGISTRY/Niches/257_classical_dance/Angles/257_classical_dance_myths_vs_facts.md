## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which features are documented earlier and which were standardized later?

**Coverage:** Use dated sources without dismissing living continuity.

**Evidence to seek:** dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts

**Limits:** Name dance form, school, production, and date. Respect artist recording rights; distinguish documented historical development from claims of an unchanged form.
