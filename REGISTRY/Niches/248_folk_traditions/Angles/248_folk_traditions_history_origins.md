## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can a dated source establish about one local practice?

**Coverage:** Name community and avoid a single timeless origin.

**Evidence to seek:** community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Name practitioners, place, and period. Seek consent for recording and reuse; do not equate a museum or festival version with all living practice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
