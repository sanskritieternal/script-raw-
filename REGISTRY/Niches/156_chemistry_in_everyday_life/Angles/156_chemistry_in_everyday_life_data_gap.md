## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What information is missing before a broad safety claim?

**Coverage:** Review exposure, long-term outcomes, vulnerable groups, and study quality.

**Evidence to seek:** current product labels and safety information, independent controlled tests, chemical measurements with method and uncertainty, dated regulatory records, and lifecycle data

**Limits:** Name product, formulation, use, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish hazard from exposure and mechanism from measured effect; avoid hazardous mixing, synthesis, or home-experiment instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
