## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What total removals result from one type of fishing?

**Coverage:** Count kept fish, discard mortality, unreported catch, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** current jurisdiction-specific fishing rules, stock assessments, catch and effort surveys, habitat and climate records, and consented fisher and community accounts

**Limits:** Name species or stock, waterbody, jurisdiction, season, and date. Check current local rules before any practical guidance; distinguish catch, effort, and stock size and avoid presenting release as automatically harmless.
