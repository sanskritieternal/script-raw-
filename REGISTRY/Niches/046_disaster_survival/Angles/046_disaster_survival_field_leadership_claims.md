## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular account of one leader match documented roles and decisions?

**Coverage:** Compare authority, team work, evidence, and hindsight limits.

**Evidence to seek:** official alerts and hazard data, incident logs, after-action reports, infrastructure and service records, longitudinal recovery data, and consented survivor accounts

**Limits:** Use trauma-aware consent and protect privacy. Avoid victim blame, sensational survival advice, operational vulnerabilities, and claims of exact prediction; check current local emergency guidance before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
