## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a viral claim about one disaster match official and local evidence?

**Coverage:** Check timestamp, location, source, and corrective reporting without amplifying harmful rumors.

**Evidence to seek:** official alerts and hazard data, incident logs, after-action reports, infrastructure and service records, longitudinal recovery data, and consented survivor accounts

**Limits:** Use trauma-aware consent and protect privacy. Avoid victim blame, sensational survival advice, operational vulnerabilities, and claims of exact prediction; check current local emergency guidance before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
