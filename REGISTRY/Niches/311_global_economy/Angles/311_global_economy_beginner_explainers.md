## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Reading a World-Economy Claim

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one indicator measure and omit?

**Coverage:** Define geography, year, currency, price basis, and source.

**Evidence to seek:** dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name countries, period, currency and price basis, aggregation, and forecast vintage. A global average does not describe every household; avoid personal financial advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
