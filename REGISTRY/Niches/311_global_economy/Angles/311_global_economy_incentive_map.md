## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do responses to a shock differ?

**Coverage:** Trace contracts, policy, and distributional effects.

**Evidence to seek:** dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name countries, period, currency and price basis, aggregation, and forecast vintage. A global average does not describe every household; avoid personal financial advice.
