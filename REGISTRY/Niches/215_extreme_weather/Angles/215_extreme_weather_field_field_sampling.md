## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which observations are needed for one extreme claim?

**Coverage:** Collect station metadata, radar or satellite, gauges, timing, and quality checks.

**Evidence to seek:** quality-controlled station metadata and measurements, official warnings and forecast archives, event investigations, peer-reviewed attribution analyses, and consented affected-community accounts

**Limits:** Name hazard, event, location, duration, baseline, and source date. Distinguish a verified record, forecast, trend, and attribution claim; use current official warnings for operational safety.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
