## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where does small population change the cost of providing a public function?

**Coverage:** Compare fixed costs, workforce, shared arrangements, and service quality.

**Evidence to seek:** constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts

**Limits:** Define microstate operationally for each comparison; distinguish sovereign states from dependent territories and small-island groupings. Avoid assuming all small states share one vulnerability.
