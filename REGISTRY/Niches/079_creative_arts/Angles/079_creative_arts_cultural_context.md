## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one work function in its maker's community?

**Coverage:** Use source-community accounts and period records, not only museum labels.

**Evidence to seek:** identified artwork and catalog record, maker drafts and contracts, technical and condition studies, acquisition and provenance documents, contemporary criticism, and source-community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, maker or attribution, place, date, and custody. Attribute contested meanings and ownership, distinguish observation from inference, and check rights and community restrictions before reuse.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
