## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do processor, memory, storage, firmware, and peripherals cooperate?

**Coverage:** Trace one ordinary task on a named configuration without repair instructions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated computer manuals and component specifications, firmware and interface standards, matched performance and power tests, service and support records, and owner and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name exact model, revision, configuration, workload, and date. Separate maker specifications from measured behavior; avoid unsafe repair or security bypass instructions and verify current support status.
