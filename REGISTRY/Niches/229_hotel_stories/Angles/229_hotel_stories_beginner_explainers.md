## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do booking, arrival, service, and checkout connect?

**Coverage:** Follow one guest journey and its staff handoffs.

**Evidence to seek:** property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name property, jurisdiction, date, and guest needs. Protect worker and guest privacy; verify current prices, features, and rules directly before any booking guidance.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
