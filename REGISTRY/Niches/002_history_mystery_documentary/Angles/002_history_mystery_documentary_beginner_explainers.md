## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do object, text, oral account, and later interpretation differ?

**Coverage:** Show provenance.

**Evidence to seek:** primary archives, excavation reports, dated maps and texts, object provenance, independent historical scholarship, and recorded corrections

**Limits:** Name event, place, period, and original source. Suspense should not turn a missing record or disputed interpretation into a proven conspiracy.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
