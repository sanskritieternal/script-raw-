## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does a well-documented pack show about architecture and performance?

**Coverage:** State model year, cell chemistry, and configuration.

**Evidence to seek:** DOE battery test guidance, cell and pack measurements, official recalls, service records, supply-chain documents, and peer-reviewed lifecycle analyses

**Limits:** State chemistry, vehicle and pack version, test protocol, temperature, and lifecycle boundary. Never infer vehicle range from cell capacity alone.
