## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did script and direction frame a role at a given stage?

**Coverage:** Compare brief, rehearsal notes, and finished work.

**Evidence to seek:** recorded performances, scripts, rehearsal and continuity notes, credits, contracts where available, actor and collaborator interviews, and dated criticism

**Limits:** Describe observable choices and documented decisions without diagnosing a performer or inferring private intent. Keep consent, credit, and working context specific to the production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
