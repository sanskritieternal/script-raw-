## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do take choice and reaction timing alter an actor's apparent intention?

**Coverage:** Compare available material and avoid attributing editor choices to actor.

**Evidence to seek:** recorded performances, scripts, rehearsal and continuity notes, credits, contracts where available, actor and collaborator interviews, and dated criticism

**Limits:** Describe observable choices and documented decisions without diagnosing a performer or inferring private intent. Keep consent, credit, and working context specific to the production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
