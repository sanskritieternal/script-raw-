## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who benefits and who remains exposed after an adaptation project?

**Coverage:** Compare adaptation benefits and costs between defined groups; examine who participates, who remains exposed and whether protection shifts risk elsewhere.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: local hazard assessments, project designs, maintenance records and observed event performance. Starting framework: IPCC AR6 urban adaptation chapter (https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGII_Chapter06.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State hazard, place and timescale; a plan is not delivered protection. Niche boundary: Urban adaptation measures and distributed protection; climate mechanisms and general city design have separate homes.
