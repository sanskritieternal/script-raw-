**Focus / example question:** Does an adaptation technology work under local operating and maintenance conditions?

**Coverage:** Evaluate one adaptation technology in its actual urban setting; compare operational performance, upkeep and who can use the protection.

**Limits:** State hazard, place and timescale; a plan is not delivered protection. Niche boundary: Urban adaptation measures and distributed protection; climate mechanisms and general city design have separate homes.
