## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do hazard, exposure and vulnerability interact in one neighborhood?

**Coverage:** Explain hazard, exposure, vulnerability and adaptation through one neighborhood; distinguish a planned intervention from demonstrated risk reduction.

**Evidence to seek:** Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: local hazard assessments, project designs, maintenance records and observed event performance. Starting framework: IPCC AR6 urban adaptation chapter (https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGII_Chapter06.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State hazard, place and timescale; a plan is not delivered protection. Niche boundary: Urban adaptation measures and distributed protection; climate mechanisms and general city design have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
