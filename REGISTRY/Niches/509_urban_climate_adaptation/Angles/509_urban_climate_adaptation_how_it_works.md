**Focus / example question:** Who designs, funds, maintains and evaluates one adaptation measure?

**Coverage:** Map how one adaptation measure is designed, funded, maintained and evaluated; identify responsibility for failures and follow-up.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the process, custody transfers and assigned responsibilities. Case records: local hazard assessments, project designs, maintenance records and observed event performance. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State hazard, place and timescale; a plan is not delivered protection. Niche boundary: Urban adaptation measures and distributed protection; climate mechanisms and general city design have separate homes.
