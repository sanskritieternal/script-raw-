## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose exposure or maintenance burden is missing from an adaptation account?

**Coverage:** Document overlooked residents or maintenance workers in one adaptation project; include consented accounts of burdens and uneven protection.

**Evidence to seek:** Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: local hazard assessments, project designs, maintenance records and observed event performance. Starting framework: IPCC AR6 urban adaptation chapter (https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGII_Chapter06.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** State hazard, place and timescale; a plan is not delivered protection. Niche boundary: Urban adaptation measures and distributed protection; climate mechanisms and general city design have separate homes.
