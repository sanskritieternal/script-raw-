## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which sources informed a fictional device or society?

**Coverage:** Verify cited research and date it to composition.

**Evidence to seek:** identified texts and editions, dated scientific context, author drafts, publication and adaptation records, and situated criticism

**Limits:** Separate fictional premise from current scientific fact and prediction. Define the work, edition, and publication context before comparing real technologies.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
