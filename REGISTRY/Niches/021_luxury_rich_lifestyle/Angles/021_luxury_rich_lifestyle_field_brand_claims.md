## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What exactly does one luxury brand's environmental claim cover?

**Coverage:** Check boundaries, measurements, verification, and excluded impacts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated company filings and product records, supply-chain disclosures and audits, labor accounts, auction and resale records, product testing, and applicable marketing rules

**Limits:** Treat prestige and value as claims to examine. Attribute prices and provenance, disclose estimates, check marketing assertions, and avoid glamorizing exploitation or implying an item is an investment.
