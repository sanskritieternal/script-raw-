## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which factors could explain a documented local absence?

**Coverage:** Test timing, observer effort, habitat, weather, movement, and identification.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered specimens, verified sightings, standardized surveys, tracking data, habitat records, taxonomic revisions, and current species assessments

**Limits:** Name species or population, geography, season, and date. Distinguish sightings and counts from modeled abundance; avoid disturbing nests and verify any management or feeding advice with current local sources.
