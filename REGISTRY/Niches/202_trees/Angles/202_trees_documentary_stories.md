## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can dated records reveal about a landmark tree?

**Coverage:** Use planting or discovery record, measurements, weather, management, and consent.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered identification, repeat forest plots and protocols, remote sensing with field calibration, climate and disturbance records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name species, forest type, plot scale, geography, and date. Distinguish tree cover from ecosystem health and one tree's condition from a stand or regional trend.
