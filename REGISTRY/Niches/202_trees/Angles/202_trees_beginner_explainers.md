## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do roots, trunk, canopy, and leaves serve one tree species?

**Coverage:** Use a named species and distinguish function across age and site.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered identification, repeat forest plots and protocols, remote sensing with field calibration, climate and disturbance records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name species, forest type, plot scale, geography, and date. Distinguish tree cover from ecosystem health and one tree's condition from a stand or regional trend.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
