## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can one interview preserve memory without pressure?

**Coverage:** Explain purpose, use, review options, and consent.

**Evidence to seek:** original civil, religious, land, work, and migration records with provenance, period maps, family objects, and voluntary consented oral histories

**Limits:** Name family, place, period, and each source. Do not treat a tree link or DNA match as proof without corroboration; protect living relatives, nonpaternity discoveries, adoption, and third-party information.
