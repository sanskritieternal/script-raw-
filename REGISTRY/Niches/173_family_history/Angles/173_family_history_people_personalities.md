## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can documents reveal about one person's choices and constraints?

**Coverage:** Use letters, work, land, and context without invented thoughts.

**Evidence to seek:** original civil, religious, land, work, and migration records with provenance, period maps, family objects, and voluntary consented oral histories

**Limits:** Name family, place, period, and each source. Do not treat a tree link or DNA match as proof without corroboration; protect living relatives, nonpaternity discoveries, adoption, and third-party information.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
