## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who shaped the final sound and design?

**Coverage:** Credit maker, player, teacher, and inherited methods.

**Evidence to seek:** dated instruments with provenance, maker and performance records, acoustic comparisons, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Name instrument variant, maker community, place, and period. Seek consent for recording and reuse; do not infer a fixed symbolic meaning or single authentic sound.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
