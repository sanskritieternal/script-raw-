## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can imaging or condition reports establish about one object?

**Coverage:** Distinguish physical observations from cultural interpretation and ownership.

**Evidence to seek:** museum acquisition and conservation files, dated labels, applicable law and ethics guidance, source-community records, and consented interviews

**Limits:** Name object, institution, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish legal title from ethical claim, document provenance gaps, and respect source-community restrictions and consent.
