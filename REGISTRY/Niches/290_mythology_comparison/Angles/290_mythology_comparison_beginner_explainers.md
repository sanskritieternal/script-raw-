**Default:** What Counts as a Fair Comparison?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which features can be compared without erasing each story's setting?

**Coverage:** Define source, version, genre, date, and community before motifs.

**Evidence to seek:** specified primary texts and editions, consented oral recordings and performer accounts, translation notes, manuscript provenance, and dated contact records

**Limits:** Name each source community, version, language, and date. Do not treat resemblance as proof of borrowing or sacred narrative as falsehood; respect custodial permission and restricted knowledge.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
