## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a current education guide grade a study strategy?

**Coverage:** Read evidence base, implementation conditions, and caveats.

**Evidence to seek:** registered education studies and replications, school assessments, practice guides, implementation records, and consented learner and teacher accounts

**Limits:** Specify age, subject, task, comparator, and retention interval. Do not infer broad intelligence from one test or diagnose learners; protect student privacy and identify limits to transfer.
