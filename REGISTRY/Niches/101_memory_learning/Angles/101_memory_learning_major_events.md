## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What happened after one school adopted a new learning routine?

**Coverage:** Compare implementation, teacher workload, retention, and equity.

**Evidence to seek:** registered education studies and replications, school assessments, practice guides, implementation records, and consented learner and teacher accounts

**Limits:** Specify age, subject, task, comparator, and retention interval. Do not infer broad intelligence from one test or diagnose learners; protect student privacy and identify limits to transfer.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
