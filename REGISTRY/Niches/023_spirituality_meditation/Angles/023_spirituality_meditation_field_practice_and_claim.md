## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does evidence support a stated benefit or safety claim for a defined meditation program?

**Coverage:** Check study design, comparator, population, adverse events, and current guidance.

**Evidence to seek:** dated source texts and translations, lineage teaching, community interviews with consent, program protocols, clinical studies, adverse-event reports, and current health guidance

**Limits:** Distinguish tradition, first-person experience, and empirical outcome. Do not prescribe practice or claim treatment; respect restricted teachings and check adverse effects and current evidence.
