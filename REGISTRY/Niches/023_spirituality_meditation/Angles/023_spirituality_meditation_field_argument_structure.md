## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What reasons does a named teacher or text give for a practice's aim?

**Coverage:** Reconstruct premises and conclusion without treating belief as empirical proof.

**Evidence to seek:** dated source texts and translations, lineage teaching, community interviews with consent, program protocols, clinical studies, adverse-event reports, and current health guidance

**Limits:** Distinguish tradition, first-person experience, and empirical outcome. Do not prescribe practice or claim treatment; respect restricted teachings and check adverse effects and current evidence.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
