**Focus / example question:** Does a popular account of why a capital was chosen match the contemporary record?

**Coverage:** Identify the earliest version of the story and compare it with decision documents.

**Evidence to seek:** dated government records, legal texts, historical maps, planning documents, and attributed local accounts as relevant

**Limits:** Date and attribute disputed status, borders, and sovereignty claims; verify current institutional locations before production.
