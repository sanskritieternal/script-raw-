## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular tale about one room, tunnel, or siege fit the evidence?

**Coverage:** Trace earliest telling and compare site records and archaeology.

**Evidence to seek:** building accounts and plans, fabric and archaeological surveys, inventories, household and military records, dated maps, conservation reports, and local accounts

**Limits:** Date each building phase and distinguish original fabric from restoration. Avoid unverified legends or sensitive access details; include builders, residents, and current custodians.
