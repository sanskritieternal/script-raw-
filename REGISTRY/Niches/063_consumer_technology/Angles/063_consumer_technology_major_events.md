## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed for owners after one recall or end-of-support notice?

**Coverage:** Use dated notices, remedies, user impact, and follow-up.

**Evidence to seek:** dated product manuals and support policies, standards and certification records, independent matched tests, recalls and advisories, and attributed owner and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name exact model, firmware, region, and test conditions. Separate marketing claims from measured outcomes, check current support and privacy terms, and avoid account or security bypass details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
