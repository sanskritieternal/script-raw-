## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a lab-measured advantage persist in natural settings?

**Coverage:** Compare environment, competitors, fitness measures, and replication.

**Evidence to seek:** primary genomic datasets and methods, validated variant calls, fossil and ecological records, population studies, experimental evolution, replication, and consent or collection records

**Limits:** Define population, time scale, and trait. Avoid genetic determinism and biological ranking of human groups; distinguish ancestry inference, association, function, and evolutionary explanation.
