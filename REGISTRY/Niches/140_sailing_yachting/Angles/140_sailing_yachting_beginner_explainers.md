## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which decisions belong in a plan for one short sail?

**Coverage:** Use a bounded route, current chart, weather, tides, crew, and alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** dated charts and port records, official marine forecasts and notices, vessel specifications, incident inquiries, current local rules, habitat surveys, and consented crew and waterfront accounts

**Limits:** Name vessel, crew, route, waters, jurisdiction, and date. Treat navigation and safety choices as documentary questions, never a live voyage plan; check current charts, weather, warnings, and local rules.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
