## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What independent evidence is needed before announcing a finding?

**Coverage:** Check calibration, replication, and alternative causes.

**Evidence to seek:** original project protocols, calibrated records and metadata, validation reports, published analyses, citizen-science data guidance, and participant agreements

**Limits:** Specify project, observation, units, sampling frame, and review status. Separate a volunteer report from verified discovery and data contribution from scientific conclusion.
