## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why can a new housing project have a delayed effect on availability?

**Coverage:** Distinguish stock, flow, vacancy, and household formation.

**Evidence to seek:** dated price and rent indices, permit and completion records, household surveys, planning rules, and documented study methods

**Limits:** Define geography, tenure, quality, and time period; distinguish correlations from causal effects and avoid price forecasts.
