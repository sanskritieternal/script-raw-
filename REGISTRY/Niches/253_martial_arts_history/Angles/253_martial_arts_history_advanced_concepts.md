## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does a claimed lineage establish or leave uncertain?

**Coverage:** Compare documents, oral testimony, dates, and breaks.

**Evidence to seek:** dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Separate documented chronology from lineage tradition and legend. Describe historical practice without teaching harmful techniques or giving personal injury advice.
