## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a school use one drill?

**Coverage:** Describe purpose and sources without teaching harmful techniques.

**Evidence to seek:** dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Separate documented chronology from lineage tradition and legend. Describe historical practice without teaching harmful techniques or giving personal injury advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
