## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which values and rules apply to one named park?

**Coverage:** Read its legal designation and current management plan.

**Evidence to seek:** legal designation and management plans, dated park notices, ecological and visitor data, land records, and attributed local and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name park, authority, date, and access need. Verify current rules and conditions; do not presume national-park status gives unrestricted access or erases prior rights.
