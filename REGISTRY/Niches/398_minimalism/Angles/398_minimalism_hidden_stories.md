## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who shops, repairs, rents, stores, or cleans for a minimalist household?

**Coverage:** Trace externalized work and cost.

**Evidence to seek:** dated inventories and spending logs, consented resident accounts, access and service records, life-cycle assessments, and original low-consumption research

**Limits:** Specify what is reduced and for whom. Do not assume fewer objects cause wellbeing or lower environmental impact without a matched comparison and full boundary.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
