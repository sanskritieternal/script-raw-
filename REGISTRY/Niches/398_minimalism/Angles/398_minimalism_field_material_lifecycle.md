## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do retained goods, services, and disposal have lower total impacts?

**Coverage:** Define functional unit and life-cycle data.

**Evidence to seek:** dated inventories and spending logs, consented resident accounts, access and service records, life-cycle assessments, and original low-consumption research

**Limits:** Specify what is reduced and for whom. Do not assume fewer objects cause wellbeing or lower environmental impact without a matched comparison and full boundary.
