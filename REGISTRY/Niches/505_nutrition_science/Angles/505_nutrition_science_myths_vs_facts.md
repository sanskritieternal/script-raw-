## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a food claim turn an association into an unsupported causal conclusion?

**Coverage:** Test one food or nutrient claim against the original study; distinguish association, causation, relative risk and practical effect size.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Separate association from causation and food claims from medical advice. Niche boundary: Nutrition measurement, study design and interpretation; cooking, food traditions and individualized dietary care are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
