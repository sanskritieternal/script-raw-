## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which evidence and methods shaped a nutrition recommendation?

**Coverage:** Trace one nutrition concept or recommendation through the studies and methods that shaped it; distinguish historical belief from later evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Separate association from causation and food claims from medical advice. Niche boundary: Nutrition measurement, study design and interpretation; cooking, food traditions and individualized dietary care are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
