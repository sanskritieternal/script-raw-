## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do deck, supports, and foundations pass loads to ground?

**Coverage:** Use one structural type and load case.

**Evidence to seek:** dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony

**Limits:** Name bridge, owner, inventory date, jurisdiction, and load case. A condition label is not by itself a collapse forecast or load rating.
