## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can an identified garment reveal beyond its famous image?

**Coverage:** Examine cut, fiber, maker, wearer, production, and later reuse.

**Evidence to seek:** identified garments and labels, dated patterns and design records, textile tests, supply-chain and worker evidence, consumer disclosures, conservation records, and attributed wearer accounts

**Limits:** Name garment, fiber, maker, market, and date. Separate brand claims from verified chain-of-custody and measured performance; credit source communities and workers and check current labeling rules.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
