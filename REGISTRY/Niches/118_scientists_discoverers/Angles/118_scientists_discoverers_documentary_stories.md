## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one claim move from first observation to independent acceptance?

**Coverage:** Build a dated, source-linked chain with disagreements and corrections.

**Evidence to seek:** original publications, laboratory and field records where available, correspondence and archives, independent replication reports, and credible science histories

**Limits:** Name scientist, exact claim, date, and field. Distinguish discovery, explanation, publication, and application; credit contributors and avoid unsafe experiment instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
