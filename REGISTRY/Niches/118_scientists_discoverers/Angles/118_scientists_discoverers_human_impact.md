## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one finding affect people after application?

**Coverage:** Separate original scientific claim from later products, policy, and harms.

**Evidence to seek:** original publications, laboratory and field records where available, correspondence and archives, independent replication reports, and credible science histories

**Limits:** Name scientist, exact claim, date, and field. Distinguish discovery, explanation, publication, and application; credit contributors and avoid unsafe experiment instructions.
