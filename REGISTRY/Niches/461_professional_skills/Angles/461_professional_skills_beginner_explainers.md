## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Define a Professional Skill

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What behavior shows communication or collaboration in one role?

**Coverage:** State task and observable criterion.

**Evidence to seek:** task analyses, work artifacts, assessment rubrics, OECD skills research, training evaluations, worker accounts, and promotion records

**Limits:** Name role, task, workplace, and criterion. Course badges and self-ratings are limited evidence; protect private performance records.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
