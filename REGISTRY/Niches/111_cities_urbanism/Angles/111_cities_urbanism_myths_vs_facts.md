## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Was a site truly unused before redevelopment?

**Coverage:** Check tenure, informal use, ecology, and community accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and maps, land and housing records, city budgets and service data, measured environmental outcomes, and consented resident and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name city, neighborhood, jurisdiction, and period. Include informal uses, affordability, accessibility, and maintenance; distinguish proposals from observed outcomes.
