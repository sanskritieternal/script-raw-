## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does more density improve access without displacing residents?

**Coverage:** Compare housing costs, travel times, services, and land values in one area.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and maps, land and housing records, city budgets and service data, measured environmental outcomes, and consented resident and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name city, neighborhood, jurisdiction, and period. Include informal uses, affordability, accessibility, and maintenance; distinguish proposals from observed outcomes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
