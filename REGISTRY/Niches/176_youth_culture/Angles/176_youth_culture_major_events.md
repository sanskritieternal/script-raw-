## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one policy or venue loss alter participation?

**Coverage:** Compare before and after activity, alternatives, and affected groups.

**Evidence to seek:** dated artifacts and media, venue and policy records, platform and contract terms, appropriately sampled data, and voluntary age-appropriate participant accounts

**Limits:** Name practice, age group, place, and period. Avoid generation-wide stereotypes and moral panic; for minors use safeguarding, age-appropriate assent, guardian consent where required, privacy protection, and meaningful refusal.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
