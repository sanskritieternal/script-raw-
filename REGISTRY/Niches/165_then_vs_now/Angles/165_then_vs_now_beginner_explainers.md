## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What must match before claiming a change?

**Coverage:** Specify place, measure, population, units, season, and method.

**Evidence to seek:** dated original images and records, comparable time series with metadata, policy and institutional histories, documented transformations, and consented firsthand accounts

**Limits:** Name place, population, metric, baseline, and end date. Do not infer causation from two snapshots; disclose breaks in definitions, inflation adjustments, archive gaps, and image edits.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
