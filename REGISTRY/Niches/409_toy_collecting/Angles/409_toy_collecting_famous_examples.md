## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can a named toy collection document about production and play?

**Coverage:** Inspect accession records, object dates, and use traces.

**Evidence to seek:** object markings and packaging, dated catalogs, accession and ownership records, condition reports, completed sale data where relevant, and consented collector accounts

**Limits:** Separate historical value, personal value, and market price. Mark provenance gaps, verify recalls before reuse, and avoid investment advice.
