## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence supports a named method for a defined task?

**Coverage:** Compare field studies and objective outcomes, not just testimonials.

**Evidence to seek:** original method descriptions, field evaluations, current occupational guidance, workflow and error data, and consented worker accounts

**Limits:** Specify role, task, comparator, time, and outcome. Distinguish anecdotes from evidence, include fatigue and structural constraints, protect worker privacy, and give no personal medical advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
