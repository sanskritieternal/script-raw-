## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can two work routines be compared without changing workload?

**Coverage:** Control task mix, staff, time, deadlines, and measurement.

**Evidence to seek:** original method descriptions, field evaluations, current occupational guidance, workflow and error data, and consented worker accounts

**Limits:** Specify role, task, comparator, time, and outcome. Distinguish anecdotes from evidence, include fatigue and structural constraints, protect worker privacy, and give no personal medical advice.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
