## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose labor or structural advantage is missing from a productivity story?

**Coverage:** Use records and consented accounts without assigning all outcomes to habits.

**Evidence to seek:** original method descriptions, field evaluations, current occupational guidance, workflow and error data, and consented worker accounts

**Limits:** Specify role, task, comparator, time, and outcome. Distinguish anecdotes from evidence, include fatigue and structural constraints, protect worker privacy, and give no personal medical advice.
