## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do tides, snow, erosion, or heat affect a travel corridor?

**Coverage:** Use current official hazard and closure data, not an assumed stable map.

**Evidence to seek:** current official operator schedules and terms, border and health authority notices, dated prices, accessibility audits, local capacity and environmental data, and attributed traveler and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name traveler needs, route, jurisdiction, and travel dates. Verify current entry, health, weather, and operator rules before production; avoid guaranteed prices or safety and respect local visitor restrictions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
