## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did editor, translator, or performer alter the work?

**Coverage:** Attribute changes from records.

**Evidence to seek:** dated editions and manuscripts, archival catalogues, author and editor correspondence, contemporary reviews, translation records, and rights documentation

**Limits:** Name work, version, date, language, and passage. Distinguish textual evidence from interpretation and avoid reproducing extensive copyrighted passages.
