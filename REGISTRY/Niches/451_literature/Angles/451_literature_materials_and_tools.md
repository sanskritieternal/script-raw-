## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did notebook, typewriter, or printing limits shape text?

**Coverage:** Use surviving artifacts and drafts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated editions and manuscripts, archival catalogues, author and editor correspondence, contemporary reviews, translation records, and rights documentation

**Limits:** Name work, version, date, language, and passage. Distinguish textual evidence from interpretation and avoid reproducing extensive copyrighted passages.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
