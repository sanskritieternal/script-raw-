## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one digital or laboratory tool reveal that earlier methods missed?

**Coverage:** Examine accuracy, sampling, privacy, and whether the measure changes behavior.

**Evidence to seek:** original psychological studies and protocols, registered hypotheses, data and instruments where available, replications, ethics records, and consented participant accounts

**Limits:** Define the behavior and population; distinguish association, causal evidence, and lived experience. Do not diagnose individuals, generalize from narrow samples, or treat group averages as fixed traits.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
