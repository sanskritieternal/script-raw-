## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How might ties between people affect one measured outcome?

**Coverage:** Map a defined network and distinguish association from contagion.

**Evidence to seek:** original psychological studies and protocols, registered hypotheses, data and instruments where available, replications, ethics records, and consented participant accounts

**Limits:** Define the behavior and population; distinguish association, causal evidence, and lived experience. Do not diagnose individuals, generalize from narrow samples, or treat group averages as fixed traits.
