## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why do community and official accounts of one place differ?

**Coverage:** Attribute claims, compare evidence, and distinguish law from memory.

**Evidence to seek:** community-approved oral histories, dated archaeological contexts and maps, ecological records, current and historical access rules, and records of trade, schooling, and state administration

**Limits:** Name community, route, period, and jurisdiction. Use preferred terms, avoid a settled-to-civilized hierarchy, respect sensitive locations and knowledge, and distinguish living practice from archaeological inference.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
