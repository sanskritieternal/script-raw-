## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a mapped cycling route match conditions on the ground?

**Coverage:** Check continuous access, surface, crossings, closures, and signage.

**Evidence to seek:** dated street plans and traffic counts, official crash and design records, equipment standards and tests, current local rules, and consented rider and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name place, route, rider, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish exposure from crash counts, avoid unsafe riding instruction, and verify current road rules and route conditions.
