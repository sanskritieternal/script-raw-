## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do slope, water, or wind shape one path's design and use?

**Coverage:** Relate mapped terrain to construction choices and documented conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated street plans and traffic counts, official crash and design records, equipment standards and tests, current local rules, and consented rider and resident accounts

**Limits:** Name place, route, rider, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish exposure from crash counts, avoid unsafe riding instruction, and verify current road rules and route conditions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
