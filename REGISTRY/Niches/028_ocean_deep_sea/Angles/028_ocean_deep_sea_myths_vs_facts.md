## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular image or headline represent what the expedition found?

**Coverage:** Verify location, depth, scale, species identification, and image provenance.

**Evidence to seek:** expedition logs and mission video, calibrated sonar and sensor data, georeferenced samples, primary ocean research, repeat surveys, and official management documents

**Limits:** State depth, location, instrument limits, sampling bias, and uncertainty. Protect sensitive habitats and do not present an unidentified signal or isolated dive as proof of a broad claim.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
