## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changes when a local genre term is translated as myth or legend?

**Coverage:** Compare original usage, translation choices, and community preference.

**Evidence to seek:** attributed recordings and oral histories, collector field notes, dated manuscripts and print versions, archive metadata, material provenance, and present tradition-bearer accounts

**Limits:** Name teller, language, place, date, and collection context where permitted. Respect consent, cultural restrictions, and differing versions; separate a story's meaning from testable historical claims.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
