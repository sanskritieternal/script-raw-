## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Did an Adaptation Change the Tradition?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence shows that a book or film changed later tellings?

**Coverage:** Compare dated versions and community accounts before and after.

**Evidence to seek:** attributed recordings and oral histories, collector field notes, dated manuscripts and print versions, archive metadata, material provenance, and present tradition-bearer accounts

**Limits:** Name teller, language, place, date, and collection context where permitted. Respect consent, cultural restrictions, and differing versions; separate a story's meaning from testable historical claims.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
