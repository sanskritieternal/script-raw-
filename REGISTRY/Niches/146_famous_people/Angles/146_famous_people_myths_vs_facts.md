## Q3: Primary Story Angle

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can one widely repeated quote be traced to a reliable source?

**Coverage:** Find earliest record, context, variant wording, and attribution gaps.

**Evidence to seek:** dated original work and records, archival correspondence and interviews, documented media reach, institutional and financial records, and consented accounts from collaborators

**Limits:** Name person, place, period, and claim. Separate public-interest facts from private life, avoid inferred motives or diagnoses, corroborate anecdotes, and document corrections.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
