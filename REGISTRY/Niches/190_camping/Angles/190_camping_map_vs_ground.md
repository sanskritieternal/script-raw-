## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which physical barriers or services are missing from a booking map?

**Coverage:** Check site grade, distance to facilities, shade, drainage, and actual access.

**Evidence to seek:** current campsite rules and alerts, booking and fee records, accessibility and facility information, land and ecological monitoring, and consented host and camper accounts

**Limits:** Name the site, jurisdiction, season, group needs, and date. Check current fire, food-storage, weather, and access rules locally; avoid implying historic conditions make a campsite safe today.
