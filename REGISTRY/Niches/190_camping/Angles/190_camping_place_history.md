## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose land and uses preceded a campsite's creation?

**Coverage:** Use land records and community sources, including Indigenous accounts where relevant.

**Evidence to seek:** current campsite rules and alerts, booking and fee records, accessibility and facility information, land and ecological monitoring, and consented host and camper accounts

**Limits:** Name the site, jurisdiction, season, group needs, and date. Check current fire, food-storage, weather, and access rules locally; avoid implying historic conditions make a campsite safe today.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
