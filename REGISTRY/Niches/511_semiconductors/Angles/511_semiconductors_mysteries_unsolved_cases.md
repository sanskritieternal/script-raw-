## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did measured device behavior differ from its published expectation?

**Coverage:** Investigate a documented gap between expected and measured device behavior; compare test conditions and published explanations without inventing proprietary details.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: original device papers, measurement conditions, process qualification and dated production disclosures. Starting framework: NIST semiconductor metrology program (https://www.nist.gov/chips/research-development-programs/metrology-program). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name process node, device and period; avoid universal performance claims. Niche boundary: Semiconductor device fabrication, qualification and supply dependencies; general materials and electronics remain neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
