**Focus / example question:** How did a chip move from design through qualification and deployment?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one chip's development, qualification and deployment; identify which production or performance claims remain unverified.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name process node, device and period; avoid universal performance claims. Niche boundary: Semiconductor device fabrication, qualification and supply dependencies; general materials and electronics remain neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
