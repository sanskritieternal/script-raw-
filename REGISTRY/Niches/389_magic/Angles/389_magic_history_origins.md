## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which dated performance or manual records a named effect?

**Coverage:** Compare primary texts and avoid a single invention claim.

**Evidence to seek:** dated programs and manuals, recorded performances, credited production artifacts, practitioner testimony, and bounded perception experiments

**Limits:** Distinguish effect from method and perception from inferred intent. Attribute creators, respect unpublished methods, and check participant consent and safety claims.
