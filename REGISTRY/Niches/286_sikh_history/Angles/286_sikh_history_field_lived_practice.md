## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does one community relate present seva or education to its records?

**Coverage:** Ask participants and avoid assuming unchanged practice.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Gurmukhi, Punjabi, Persian, or other relevant manuscripts and translations, hukamnamas, institutional records, material heritage, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name community, place, period, and source; distinguish contemporary documents, later tradition, and devotional commitment. Protect living voices and avoid one uniform Sikh history.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
