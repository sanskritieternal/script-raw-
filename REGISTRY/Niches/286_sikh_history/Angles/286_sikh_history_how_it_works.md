## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one gurdwara or association fund and organize its work?

**Coverage:** Read rules, ledgers, minutes, and service records.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Gurmukhi, Punjabi, Persian, or other relevant manuscripts and translations, hukamnamas, institutional records, material heritage, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name community, place, period, and source; distinguish contemporary documents, later tradition, and devotional commitment. Protect living voices and avoid one uniform Sikh history.
