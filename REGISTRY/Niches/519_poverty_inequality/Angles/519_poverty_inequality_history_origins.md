## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a poverty measure's purpose shape its original definition?

**Coverage:** Trace one poverty or inequality measure through its original policy purpose and methodological changes; distinguish changing definitions from changing living conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: household microdata documentation, thresholds, prices, survey weights and original policy evaluations. Starting framework: World Bank poverty measurement methodology (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099101424092027337/pdf/P500855154ddde0b31827a1e9377c43d15e.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define poverty measure and denominator; avoid stereotypes. Niche boundary: Measured deprivation, resource distribution and policy effects; broad social theory and whole-government operation are neighboring subjects.
