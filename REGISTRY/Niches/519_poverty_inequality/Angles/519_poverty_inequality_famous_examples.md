## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which households and outcomes does a poverty-reduction evaluation represent?

**Coverage:** Examine one documented poverty-reduction initiative using eligibility, comparison methods and household outcomes; identify whose circumstances the findings represent.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: household microdata documentation, thresholds, prices, survey weights and original policy evaluations. Starting framework: World Bank poverty measurement methodology (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099101424092027337/pdf/P500855154ddde0b31827a1e9377c43d15e.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define poverty measure and denominator; avoid stereotypes. Niche boundary: Measured deprivation, resource distribution and policy effects; broad social theory and whole-government operation are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
