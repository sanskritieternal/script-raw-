## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which analytical choices change the estimated distribution of resources?

**Coverage:** Compare analytical methods for one distributional question, including equivalence scales, price adjustment, sampling and treatment of missing wealth.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: household microdata documentation, thresholds, prices, survey weights and original policy evaluations. Starting framework: World Bank poverty measurement methodology (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099101424092027337/pdf/P500855154ddde0b31827a1e9377c43d15e.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define poverty measure and denominator; avoid stereotypes. Niche boundary: Measured deprivation, resource distribution and policy effects; broad social theory and whole-government operation are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
