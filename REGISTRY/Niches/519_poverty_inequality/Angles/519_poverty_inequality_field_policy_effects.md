## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who gained or lost under a documented policy change?

**Coverage:** Evaluate one policy's distributional effects using a stated comparator and follow-up period; include take-up, exclusions and uncertainty about attribution.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: household microdata documentation, thresholds, prices, survey weights and original policy evaluations. Starting framework: World Bank poverty measurement methodology (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099101424092027337/pdf/P500855154ddde0b31827a1e9377c43d15e.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Define poverty measure and denominator; avoid stereotypes. Niche boundary: Measured deprivation, resource distribution and policy effects; broad social theory and whole-government operation are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
