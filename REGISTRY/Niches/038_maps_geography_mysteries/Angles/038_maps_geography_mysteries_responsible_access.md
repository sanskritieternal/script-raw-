## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Should exact coordinates for a sacred or fragile place be published?

**Coverage:** Consult custodians and management rules before disclosure.

**Evidence to seek:** original map editions and metadata, survey methods and control points, official geospatial datasets, field observations, legal access records, and community place-name accounts

**Limits:** State map date, scale, projection, datum, and uncertainty. Do not infer legal access from a drawn line or disclose restricted or fragile locations without custodian consent.
