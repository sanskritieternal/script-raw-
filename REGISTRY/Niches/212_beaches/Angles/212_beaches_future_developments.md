## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which plausible factors could alter one beach?

**Coverage:** State sea level, storms, sediment budget, development, and management assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** repeat shoreline and beach-profile surveys, tide and wave records, sediment budgets, coastal imagery with datum, management plans, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name beach, shoreline definition, datum, survey season, and date. Distinguish a narrow beach at one tide from long-term erosion and verify current local hazard and access notices.
