## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which records could help viewers verify a future image's origin?

**Coverage:** Compare provenance metadata, edits, platform stripping, and limits.

**Evidence to seek:** original photographs, negatives and contact sheets, dated captions and publications, maker and subject accounts, archive records, and current rights guidance

**Limits:** Name image, maker where known, version, date, and context. Distinguish visible evidence from inference, protect subject consent and privacy, and verify rights by jurisdiction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
