## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did one widely reproduced image show at its original moment?

**Coverage:** Check original print or negative, caption, crop, sequence, and circulation.

**Evidence to seek:** original photographs, negatives and contact sheets, dated captions and publications, maker and subject accounts, archive records, and current rights guidance

**Limits:** Name image, maker where known, version, date, and context. Distinguish visible evidence from inference, protect subject consent and privacy, and verify rights by jurisdiction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
