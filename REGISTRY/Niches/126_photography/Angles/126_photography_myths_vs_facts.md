## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does an image's history support a claim of no alteration?

**Coverage:** Compare source file, darkroom or digital steps, crop, and publication version.

**Evidence to seek:** original photographs, negatives and contact sheets, dated captions and publications, maker and subject accounts, archive records, and current rights guidance

**Limits:** Name image, maker where known, version, date, and context. Distinguish visible evidence from inference, protect subject consent and privacy, and verify rights by jurisdiction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
