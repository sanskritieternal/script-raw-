## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How was an exceptional loss year verified?

**Coverage:** Check balance-year dates, method, units, sampling, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** standardized glacier mass-balance records, geodetic surveys, dated imagery, weather and runoff gauges, hazard assessments, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name glacier, balance year, measure, method, and uncertainty. Distinguish front retreat, area, and mass loss; avoid judging long-term change from one snowy year or photograph.
