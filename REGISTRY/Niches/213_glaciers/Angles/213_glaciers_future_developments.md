## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which conditions could alter its future size and runoff?

**Coverage:** State climate, snowfall, debris, geometry, and model assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** standardized glacier mass-balance records, geodetic surveys, dated imagery, weather and runoff gauges, hazard assessments, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name glacier, balance year, measure, method, and uncertainty. Distinguish front retreat, area, and mass loss; avoid judging long-term change from one snowy year or photograph.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
