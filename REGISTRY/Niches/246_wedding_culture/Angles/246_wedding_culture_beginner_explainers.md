## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which acts are legal, religious, familial, or celebratory?

**Coverage:** Define jurisdiction, community, and participants' own terms.

**Evidence to seek:** participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources

**Limits:** Name community, jurisdiction, and period. Respect couple and participant privacy, restricted rites, and legal variation; do not imply one family's practice is universal.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
