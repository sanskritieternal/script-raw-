## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does one population's record show about survival and health?

**Coverage:** Check records, migration, and selection.

**Evidence to seek:** WHO life tables and healthy-ageing resources, registered clinical trials, peer-reviewed outcomes, adverse-event records, and disaggregated public-health data

**Limits:** Name intervention, population, outcome, and follow-up. Animal lifespan and biomarker shifts do not prove extra healthy human years.
