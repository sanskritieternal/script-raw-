## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence would justify an extension beyond current trends?

**Coverage:** State biological and social assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** WHO life tables and healthy-ageing resources, registered clinical trials, peer-reviewed outcomes, adverse-event records, and disaggregated public-health data

**Limits:** Name intervention, population, outcome, and follow-up. Animal lifespan and biomarker shifts do not prove extra healthy human years.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
