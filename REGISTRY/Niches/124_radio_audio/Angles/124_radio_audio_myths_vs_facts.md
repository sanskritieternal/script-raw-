## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a famous claim about listener reaction match contemporary evidence?

**Coverage:** Check audience size, press accounts, letters, and later embellishment.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving audio and scripts, broadcaster schedules and production files, audience and coverage records, contemporary reviews, archive metadata, and attributed crew accounts

**Limits:** Name program, broadcaster, geography, date, and surviving version. Distinguish original transmission from archival copy, mark gaps, and verify current reuse rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
