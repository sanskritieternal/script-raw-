## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can be known when a broadcast itself did not survive?

**Coverage:** Compare scripts, listings, press accounts, and disputed recollections.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving audio and scripts, broadcaster schedules and production files, audience and coverage records, contemporary reviews, archive metadata, and attributed crew accounts

**Limits:** Name program, broadcaster, geography, date, and surviving version. Distinguish original transmission from archival copy, mark gaps, and verify current reuse rights.
