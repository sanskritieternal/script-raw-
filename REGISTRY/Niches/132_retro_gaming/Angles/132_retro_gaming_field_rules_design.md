## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do manual and playable version describe the same rules?

**Coverage:** Test a lawful copy and record build differences.

**Evidence to seek:** original media and hardware, dated manuals and builds, development archives, contemporary reviews, lawful play captures, and attributed developer and player accounts

**Limits:** Name game, edition or build, platform, region, and date. Distinguish original play from emulation or remaster, credit contributors, and verify rights before reuse.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
