## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did one game stand out to its original players?

**Coverage:** Compare contemporary reviews, sales evidence, competitors, and original hardware.

**Evidence to seek:** original media and hardware, dated manuals and builds, development archives, contemporary reviews, lawful play captures, and attributed developer and player accounts

**Limits:** Name game, edition or build, platform, region, and date. Distinguish original play from emulation or remaster, credit contributors, and verify rights before reuse.
