## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one event change the product?

**Coverage:** Trace official notice, affected models, remedy, and results.

**Evidence to seek:** dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts

**Limits:** Name product model, version, jurisdiction, and test conditions. Verify current recalls with the relevant authority; avoid treating specifications as real-use outcomes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
