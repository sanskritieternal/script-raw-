## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence explains its exit?

**Coverage:** Separate poor demand, safety, margin, and strategy.

**Evidence to seek:** dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts

**Limits:** Name product model, version, jurisdiction, and test conditions. Verify current recalls with the relevant authority; avoid treating specifications as real-use outcomes.
