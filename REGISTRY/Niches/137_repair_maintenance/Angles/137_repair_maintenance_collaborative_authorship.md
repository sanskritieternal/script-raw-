## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who contributed knowledge and decisions to one successful repair?

**Coverage:** Trace consent, design information, diagnosis, and final approval.

**Evidence to seek:** manufacturer and professional service records, parts and warranty terms, qualified test results, current repair and safety rules, and attributed repairer and owner accounts

**Limits:** Name product, model, fault, jurisdiction, and date. Keep hazardous work at documentary level, refer safety-critical tasks to qualified professionals, and verify current repair rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
