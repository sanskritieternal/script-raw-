## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did one digital tool improve a meaningful learning outcome?

**Coverage:** Compare matched learners, access, teacher workload, privacy, and cost.

**Evidence to seek:** original education studies and methods, dated curriculum and assessment materials, anonymized student work, implementation logs, teacher and learner accounts, and disaggregated outcomes

**Limits:** Name subject, learners, setting, age or stage, and date. Protect student privacy; distinguish association from causal effect, assess access and harms, and verify current local curriculum before production.
