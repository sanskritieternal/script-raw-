## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does the task measure and what does it not?

**Coverage:** Avoid reducing a person to one score.

**Evidence to seek:** original experimental protocols, registered replications, construct-validation studies, deidentified participant data, and naturalistic observations

**Limits:** Name task, construct, population, language, and setting. Separate a laboratory score from everyday ability or clinical diagnosis.
