## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after a closure, inspection reform or maintenance-funding decision?

**Coverage:** Examine a dated closure, inspection reform or funding decision and its effect on one asset's maintenance; use official reviews where available.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: authorized inspection reports, asset registers, work orders, budgets and service interruption records. Starting framework: FHWA transportation asset management guidance (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/asset/faq.cfm). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Do not infer safety from appearance or give repair instructions. Niche boundary: Institutional upkeep of public assets; household repair instructions, engineering-failure narratives and new construction are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
