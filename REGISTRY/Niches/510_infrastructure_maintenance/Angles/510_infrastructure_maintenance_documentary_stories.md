**Focus / example question:** How did one asset's defect move through investigation, repair and follow-up?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced timeline of one public asset's defect, investigation and authorized repair; distinguish established findings from unresolved causes.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. **Limits:** Do not infer safety from appearance or give repair instructions. Niche boundary: Institutional upkeep of public assets; household repair instructions, engineering-failure narratives and new construction are neighboring subjects.
