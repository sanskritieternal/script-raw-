## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a reported defect become a checked and completed repair?

**Coverage:** Map how a reported defect becomes an inspection, work order, repair and follow-up check; identify responsibility at every handoff.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the process, custody transfers and assigned responsibilities. Case records: authorized inspection reports, asset registers, work orders, budgets and service interruption records. Starting framework: FHWA transportation asset management guidance (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/asset/faq.cfm). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Do not infer safety from appearance or give repair instructions. Niche boundary: Institutional upkeep of public assets; household repair instructions, engineering-failure narratives and new construction are neighboring subjects.
