## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did a maintenance program improve the reliability measures it targeted?

**Coverage:** Examine one documented maintenance program using inspections, completed work and service reliability; compare its results with stated targets.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: authorized inspection reports, asset registers, work orders, budgets and service interruption records. Starting framework: FHWA transportation asset management guidance (https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/asset/faq.cfm). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Do not infer safety from appearance or give repair instructions. Niche boundary: Institutional upkeep of public assets; household repair instructions, engineering-failure narratives and new construction are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
