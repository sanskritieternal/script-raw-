## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What documented change brought visitors to one previously little-known place?

**Coverage:** Trace transport, promotion, local decisions, and earlier uses through dated records.

**Evidence to seek:** current official site and transport notices, management plans, maps, visitor and environmental data, local histories, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Verify access and conditions at production time. Respect local custodians, privacy, restricted or sacred sites, and fragile habitats; do not disclose sensitive coordinates or unsafe routes.
