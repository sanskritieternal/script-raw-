## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What should a visitor verify before planning access to a lesser-known site?

**Coverage:** Check official status, routes, permissions, season, safety notices, and local rules.

**Evidence to seek:** current official site and transport notices, management plans, maps, visitor and environmental data, local histories, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Verify access and conditions at production time. Respect local custodians, privacy, restricted or sacred sites, and fragile habitats; do not disclose sensitive coordinates or unsafe routes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
