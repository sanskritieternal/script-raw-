## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Singular Life and a Wider Pattern

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** When is one biography typical or unusual for its cohort?

**Coverage:** Use compatible population data without erasing individual experience.

**Evidence to seek:** dated primary records, archival provenance and access terms, versioned biographies, recorded interviews with use agreements, claim ledgers, and published corrections

**Limits:** Name subject, biographer, edition, and period. Label inference, do not invent thoughts or scenes as fact, verify consequential claims, and weigh privacy and consent before using intimate details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
