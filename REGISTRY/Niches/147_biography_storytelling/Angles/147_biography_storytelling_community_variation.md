**Default:** A Life in Several Communities

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do family, colleagues, and neighbors describe one person differently?

**Coverage:** Use attributed accounts and identify each vantage point.

**Evidence to seek:** dated primary records, archival provenance and access terms, versioned biographies, recorded interviews with use agreements, claim ledgers, and published corrections

**Limits:** Name subject, biographer, edition, and period. Label inference, do not invent thoughts or scenes as fact, verify consequential claims, and weigh privacy and consent before using intimate details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
