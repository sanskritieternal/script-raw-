## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a buyer and seller transfer a share in one period?

**Coverage:** Trace listing, broker, price, settlement, and ownership record.

**Evidence to seek:** dated exchange rules, prices, issuer filings, legal records, archival news, and scholarly analyses

**Limits:** Use consistent historical return methods and avoid treating market history as investment advice.
