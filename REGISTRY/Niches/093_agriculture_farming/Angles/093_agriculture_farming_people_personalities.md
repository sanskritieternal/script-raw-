## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which farmers, researchers, and workers shaped one farming method?

**Coverage:** Trace dated contributions and avoid a lone inventor story.

**Evidence to seek:** dated farm and weather records, field trials, land and labor records, current agricultural guidance, and attributed producer accounts

**Limits:** Name crop, farm system, place, season, and comparison. Distinguish trial results from local advice; respect land rights and local knowledge, and verify safety guidance before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
