## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which route facts must a traveler verify before departure?

**Coverage:** Check current permissions, terrain, elevation, weather, transport, and return options.

**Evidence to seek:** current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name country, valley, route, season, elevation, and date. Verify live local conditions before practical advice; distinguish hazard susceptibility from a forecast and avoid medical or rescue instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
