## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What histories do local and official place names reveal?

**Coverage:** Use language authorities and community-preferred names.

**Evidence to seek:** dated charts and maps, local records and consented resident accounts, transport and service data, ecological monitoring, current weather and access rules, and comparable shoreline surveys

**Limits:** Name island, jurisdiction, period, and community. Avoid treating islands as isolated or interchangeable, check current route and weather conditions, and separate measured environmental change from assumed causes.
