## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who may reproduce or sell a design?

**Coverage:** Check maker consent, community claims, and contracts.

**Evidence to seek:** provenanced embroidered objects, dated workshop and market records, material examination, and consented maker and wearer accounts

**Limits:** Name maker community, piece, and occasion. Respect restricted motifs, privacy, and reproduction rights; avoid claiming one fixed meaning for a design.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
