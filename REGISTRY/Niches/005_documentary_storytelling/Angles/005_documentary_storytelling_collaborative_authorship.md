## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Whose Story Is Being Told?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did participants, researchers, translators, and editors shape a film?

**Coverage:** Inspect credits, agreements, drafts, and consent.

**Evidence to seek:** original footage and archives, production notes and version history, source and rights ledgers, consent records, published corrections, and attributed participant accounts

**Limits:** Distinguish filmed evidence from staging and inference; document consent and rights, protect vulnerable participants, disclose material reconstruction, and keep a correction path.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
