**Focus / example question:** How does one group teach and enforce a norm?

**Coverage:** Use observation and interviews with consent and internal variation.

**Evidence to seek:** published studies with sampling and method details, disaggregated surveys, institutional records, ethical approvals where applicable, and voluntary consented interviews and observation

**Limits:** Name population, place, period, measure, and method. Avoid turning one story into a population claim, protect participants and third parties, and distinguish correlation, mechanism, and causal evidence.
