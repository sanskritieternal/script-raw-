## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Poll Against Field Observation

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why might reported attitudes and observed actions differ?

**Coverage:** Check wording, social desirability, sample, setting, and time.

**Evidence to seek:** published studies with sampling and method details, disaggregated surveys, institutional records, ethical approvals where applicable, and voluntary consented interviews and observation

**Limits:** Name population, place, period, measure, and method. Avoid turning one story into a population claim, protect participants and third parties, and distinguish correlation, mechanism, and causal evidence.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
