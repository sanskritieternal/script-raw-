## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did investigators preserve and interpret one failed component?

**Coverage:** Trace custody, inspection, testing, competing hypotheses, and correction.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned material specifications and test standards, independent laboratory results with conditions, manufacturing records, failure investigations, service data, and lifecycle assessments

**Limits:** Name material grade, form, product, environment, test method, and date. Avoid claiming one material is universally best; keep hazardous fabrication and safety-critical repair with qualified professionals.
