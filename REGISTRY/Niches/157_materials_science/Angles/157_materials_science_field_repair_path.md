## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Can This Material Be Repaired?

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which repair method is approved for one product and why?

**Coverage:** Check manufacturer guidance, qualified assessment, retesting, and cost.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned material specifications and test standards, independent laboratory results with conditions, manufacturing records, failure investigations, service data, and lifecycle assessments

**Limits:** Name material grade, form, product, environment, test method, and date. Avoid claiming one material is universally best; keep hazardous fabrication and safety-critical repair with qualified professionals.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
