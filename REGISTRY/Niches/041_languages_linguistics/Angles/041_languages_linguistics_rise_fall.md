## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where did use of one language expand or contract over a defined period?

**Coverage:** Measure contexts of use and intergenerational transmission, not just speaker counts.

**Evidence to seek:** consented recordings and transcripts, dated texts and corpora, community-approved dictionaries and grammars, language policies, speaker interviews, and archival metadata

**Limits:** Treat languages and varieties as systematic, not deficient. Obtain speaker and community consent, respect restricted knowledge and data rights, and distinguish attested forms from reconstruction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
