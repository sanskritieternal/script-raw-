## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How sensitive is a stock assessment to its data and assumptions?

**Coverage:** Examine one stock assessment's survey, catch and biological inputs; compare uncertainty, missing data and sensitivity to assumptions.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: dated stock assessments, effort and observer data, access rules, landing and custody records. Starting framework: FAO State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture 2024 landing record (https://www.fao.org/science-technology-and-innovation/resources/publications/the-state-of-world-fisheries-and-aquaculture-2024/fr). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name stock, gear, jurisdiction and assessment year. Niche boundary: Managed fish stocks, harvesting and livelihoods; recreational fishing, marine-life profiles and general conservation are neighboring subjects.
