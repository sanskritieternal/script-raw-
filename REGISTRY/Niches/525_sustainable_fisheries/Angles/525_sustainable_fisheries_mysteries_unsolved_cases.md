## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What competing explanations remain for a change in catch or stock estimates?

**Coverage:** Investigate an unexplained change in catch or stock estimates using effort, environment and reporting evidence; distinguish competing causes.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: dated stock assessments, effort and observer data, access rules, landing and custody records. Starting framework: FAO State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture 2024 landing record (https://www.fao.org/science-technology-and-innovation/resources/publications/the-state-of-world-fisheries-and-aquaculture-2024/fr). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name stock, gear, jurisdiction and assessment year. Niche boundary: Managed fish stocks, harvesting and livelihoods; recreational fishing, marine-life profiles and general conservation are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
