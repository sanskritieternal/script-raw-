## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do stock, catch, effort and management connect in one fishery?

**Coverage:** Explain stock, catch, fishing effort and management through one named fishery; distinguish species-wide labels from local stock conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: dated stock assessments, effort and observer data, access rules, landing and custody records. Starting framework: FAO State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture 2024 landing record (https://www.fao.org/science-technology-and-innovation/resources/publications/the-state-of-world-fisheries-and-aquaculture-2024/fr). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name stock, gear, jurisdiction and assessment year. Niche boundary: Managed fish stocks, harvesting and livelihoods; recreational fishing, marine-life profiles and general conservation are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
