## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did one mosque, school, court, or charitable foundation actually do?

**Coverage:** Read endowment deeds, accounts, legal records, and local context.

**Evidence to seek:** dated manuscripts, waqf deeds, court and civic records, inscriptions, material objects, archival provenance, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name community, place, and period; distinguish religious texts, juristic interpretation, administrative practice, and later memory. Avoid reducing Islamic history to one region, dynasty, or conflict.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
