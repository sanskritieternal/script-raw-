## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Community Across a Political Change

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after one dated conquest, reform, or migration?

**Coverage:** Compare institutions, taxes, language, and household evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** dated manuscripts, waqf deeds, court and civic records, inscriptions, material objects, archival provenance, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name community, place, and period; distinguish religious texts, juristic interpretation, administrative practice, and later memory. Avoid reducing Islamic history to one region, dynasty, or conflict.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
