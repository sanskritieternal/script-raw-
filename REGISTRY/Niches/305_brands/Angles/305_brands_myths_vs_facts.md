## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do measured outcomes justify a price difference?

**Coverage:** Compare matched products, use, warranty, and total cost.

**Evidence to seek:** dated trademarks and ownership records, packaging and advertising, product tests, market and warranty data, and consented customer and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name brand, owner, product, market, and period. Distinguish a mark from its legal owner and test claims against specific evidence; no legal advice.
