## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence is missing from a disputed causal explanation?

**Coverage:** Map rival hypotheses, their predictions, and the limits of existing observations.

**Evidence to seek:** primary research, dated teaching materials, evidence reviews, anonymized learner work, and documented assessment results

**Limits:** Name the phenomenon, learner group, date, and causal level. Separate explanation from correlation, purpose, and description; do not claim one teaching method works for every learner.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
