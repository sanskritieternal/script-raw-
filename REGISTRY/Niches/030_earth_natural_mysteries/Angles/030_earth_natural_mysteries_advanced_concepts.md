## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do interacting tectonic, climate, and erosion processes shape a site?

**Coverage:** Define timescale and compare evidence for each contributor.

**Evidence to seek:** geological surveys and maps, calibrated sensor records, field samples and lab analyses, remote sensing, dated hazard notices, and affected community accounts

**Limits:** Check location, time, calibration, and competing natural processes. Do not convert an unexplained observation into a supernatural conclusion or imply precise hazard prediction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
