## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which new observation ruled out an earlier account?

**Coverage:** Compare predictions and measurements at the same site.

**Evidence to seek:** geological surveys and maps, calibrated sensor records, field samples and lab analyses, remote sensing, dated hazard notices, and affected community accounts

**Limits:** Check location, time, calibration, and competing natural processes. Do not convert an unexplained observation into a supernatural conclusion or imply precise hazard prediction.
