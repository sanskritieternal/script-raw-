## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one idea develop from problem to public use?

**Coverage:** Build a dated sequence of notes, prototypes, claims, tests, and adoption.

**Evidence to seek:** dated inventor notebooks and drawings, patent filings and prior art, identified prototypes, independent tests, production and user records, and attributed collaborator accounts

**Limits:** Name invention version, claimed function, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish patent, working prototype, commercial adoption, and sole-inventor mythology; avoid hazardous replication instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
