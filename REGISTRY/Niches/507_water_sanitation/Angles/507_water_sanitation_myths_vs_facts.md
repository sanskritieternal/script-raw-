## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a safe-service claim represent the households and periods it describes?

**Coverage:** Test one safe-water or sanitation-coverage claim against sampling locations, service continuity and population definitions; identify unmeasured households.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: service definitions, utility monitoring, treatment records, household coverage and affordability data. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (https://washdata.org/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish design capacity from delivered safe service. Niche boundary: Delivered water and sanitation services, reliability and access; general water science and infrastructure maintenance have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
