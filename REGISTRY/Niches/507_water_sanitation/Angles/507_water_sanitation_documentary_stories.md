## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one utility project move from planning to monitored operation?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one service project from planning through operation; include household experience, monitoring results and unresolved problems.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: service definitions, utility monitoring, treatment records, household coverage and affordability data. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (https://washdata.org/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish design capacity from delivered safe service. Niche boundary: Delivered water and sanitation services, reliability and access; general water science and infrastructure maintenance have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
