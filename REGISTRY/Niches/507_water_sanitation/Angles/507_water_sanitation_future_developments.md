## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What operating and monitoring capacity would a proposed upgrade require?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed water or sanitation upgrade through source availability, treatment, operations, affordability and monitoring requirements.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: service definitions, utility monitoring, treatment records, household coverage and affordability data. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (https://washdata.org/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish design capacity from delivered safe service. Niche boundary: Delivered water and sanitation services, reliability and access; general water science and infrastructure maintenance have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
