## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after a water expansion, drought response or sanitation reform?

**Coverage:** Examine one service expansion, drought response or sanitation reform through dated records; compare actual coverage and continuity with stated goals.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: service definitions, utility monitoring, treatment records, household coverage and affordability data. Starting framework: WHO and UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (https://washdata.org/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish design capacity from delivered safe service. Niche boundary: Delivered water and sanitation services, reliability and access; general water science and infrastructure maintenance have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
