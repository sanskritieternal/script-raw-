## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do product features and labeling suit the stated age?

**Coverage:** Check current official age guidance and test records.

**Evidence to seek:** identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation

**Limits:** Name toy version, child age, setting, date, and jurisdiction. Do not certify safety from appearance or promise a developmental benefit from marketing or one play session.
