## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose navigation, translation, labor, or rescue work is missing from one adventure account?

**Coverage:** Use credited and consented accounts, not a lone-hero frame.

**Evidence to seek:** expedition logs and maps, permits and official advisories, weather and route records, community accounts with consent, budgets, and environmental observations

**Limits:** Verify conditions and permissions at production time, credit local guides and custodians, avoid colonial first-discovery framing, and withhold unsafe or restricted route details.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
