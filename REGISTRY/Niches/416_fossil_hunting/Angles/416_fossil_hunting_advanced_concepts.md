## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What information is lost when a specimen is removed without stratigraphy?

**Coverage:** Compare documented and unprovenanced finds.

**Evidence to seek:** current NPS and BLM collection rules, permits, original field notes, stratigraphic maps, accession records, specimen analysis, and peer-reviewed interpretation

**Limits:** Name land jurisdiction, fossil type, permit status, and geologic context. Do not encourage unauthorized collection or disclose sensitive locality details.
