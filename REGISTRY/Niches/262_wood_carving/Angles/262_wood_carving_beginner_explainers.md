## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do grain, joinery, motif, and use fit together?

**Coverage:** Use one named object and maker explanation.

**Evidence to seek:** provenanced objects, timber and workshop records, material analysis, conservation reports, and consented artisan and user accounts

**Limits:** Name object, wood source, workshop, and period. Verify material claims and permissions; do not equate machine assistance with absence of craft.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
