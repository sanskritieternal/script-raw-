## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why do similar-looking forms serve different purposes?

**Coverage:** Compare construction, wearers, context, and local names.

**Evidence to seek:** dated garments and photographs with provenance, material and process records, sales claims, and consented maker and wearer accounts

**Limits:** Name community, garment, place, and period. Respect restricted uses and maker rights; do not treat one outfit as a timeless uniform or infer meaning from appearance alone.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
