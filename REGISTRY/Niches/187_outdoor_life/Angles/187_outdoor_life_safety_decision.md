**Focus / example question:** What information should shape a go or turn-back decision?

**Coverage:** Use current local alerts, weather, route condition, daylight, and group capability.

**Evidence to seek:** site management plans, current closures and alerts, land records, visitor and ecological monitoring, accessibility details, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name the site, jurisdiction, season, and date. Check current local conditions before practical safety advice; distinguish recreation from land rights and avoid treating one visitor experience as universal.
