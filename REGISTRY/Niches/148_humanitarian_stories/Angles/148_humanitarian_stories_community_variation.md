## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do priorities differ among groups affected by the same crisis?

**Coverage:** Use safe, voluntary consultation without flattening internal differences.

**Evidence to seek:** dated needs and program records, independent evaluations, disaggregated outcome data, current standards and access rules, and voluntary consented accounts from affected people and local responders

**Limits:** Name crisis, place, program, and date. Never condition help on filming, protect identity and location where needed, assess downstream harm, seek ongoing informed consent, and distinguish delivery counts from benefit.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
