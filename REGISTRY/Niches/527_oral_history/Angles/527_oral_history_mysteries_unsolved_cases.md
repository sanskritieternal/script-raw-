## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What remains uncertain when a remembered event differs from other records?

**Coverage:** Investigate a discrepancy between a remembered event and other records; preserve the narrator's perspective while identifying what remains uncertain.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: authorized recordings, consent records, interview guides, transcripts and corroborating primary records. Starting framework: Oral History Association core principles (https://oralhistory.org/oha-core-principles/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish memory from contemporaneous record; honor access conditions. Niche boundary: Interview creation, memory, interpretation and consent; biography, family reconstruction and a community's whole history are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
