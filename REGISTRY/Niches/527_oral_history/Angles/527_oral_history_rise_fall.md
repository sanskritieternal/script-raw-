## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did an oral-history project expand, close or change stewardship?

**Coverage:** Explain expansion or closure of one oral-history project using funding, participation, stewardship and access records; avoid judging value by interview count.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: authorized recordings, consent records, interview guides, transcripts and corroborating primary records. Starting framework: Oral History Association core principles (https://oralhistory.org/oha-core-principles/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish memory from contemporaneous record; honor access conditions. Niche boundary: Interview creation, memory, interpretation and consent; biography, family reconstruction and a community's whole history are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
