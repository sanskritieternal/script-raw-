## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who may authorize public use of a culturally specific creature story?

**Coverage:** Check living community protocols, copyright, and attribution.

**Evidence to seek:** attributed oral histories and recordings, dated manuscripts and editions, object provenance, collection metadata, and current tradition-bearer accounts

**Limits:** Name tradition, teller or maker, place, date, and source where permitted. Respect community restrictions and differing tellings; separate narrative meaning from testable historical or biological claims.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
