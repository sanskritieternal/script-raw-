## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose growing, spinning, dyeing, weaving, and selling is uncredited?

**Coverage:** Use archival and consented maker accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated textiles with ownership records, material analysis, trade and labor archives, and attributed maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name textile, place, period, and source. Do not infer production place from motif alone or treat current makers as unchanged evidence of the past.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
