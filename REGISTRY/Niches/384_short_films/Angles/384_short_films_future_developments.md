## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can a finished short demonstrate about a proposed longer work?

**Coverage:** Separate documented production capacity from promised financing.

**Evidence to seek:** identified films and cuts, scripts, production and sound records, festival or broadcast programs, rights documents, archives, and situated criticism

**Limits:** Name film, cut, runtime, and exhibition context. Avoid treating a short as automatically simple or a festival appearance as proof of broad impact.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
