## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do two dating methods support the same sequence?

**Coverage:** Compare sample selection, calibration, stratigraphy, and uncertainty.

**Evidence to seek:** survey and excavation records, context sheets and stratigraphy, laboratory analyses and dating reports, collections catalogues, permits, conservation records, and consented descendant or local accounts

**Limits:** Protect sites and restricted locations, verify lawful provenance and consultation, distinguish observed context from interpretation, and do not encourage unauthorized digging or collecting.
