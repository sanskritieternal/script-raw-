## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which evidence explains one show's cancellation or revival?

**Coverage:** Compare cost, schedule, audience measurement, contracts, and executive records.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving broadcasts and scripts, broadcaster schedules and production files, contemporary reviews, archive metadata, rights records, and attributed crew accounts

**Limits:** Name program, broadcaster, country, episode, and date. Distinguish contemporary reception from later reputation, mark missing recordings, and verify reuse rights.
