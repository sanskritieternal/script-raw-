## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** How a TV Episode Was Made

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which stages took one program from script to broadcast?

**Coverage:** Follow a dated production with budget, roles, and recording format.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving broadcasts and scripts, broadcaster schedules and production files, contemporary reviews, archive metadata, rights records, and attributed crew accounts

**Limits:** Name program, broadcaster, country, episode, and date. Distinguish contemporary reception from later reputation, mark missing recordings, and verify reuse rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
