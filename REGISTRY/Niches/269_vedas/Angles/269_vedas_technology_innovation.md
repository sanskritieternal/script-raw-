## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Can audio archives preserve a named chant without replacing living transmission?

**Coverage:** Ask practitioners about consent, context, access, and technical fidelity.

**Evidence to seek:** named recensions and editions, oral recordings with permission, manuscript catalogues, ritual texts, heritage records, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Specify Veda, branch, passage, and source edition; distinguish composition, oral transmission, and manuscript dates. Attribute practice and respect community access rules.
