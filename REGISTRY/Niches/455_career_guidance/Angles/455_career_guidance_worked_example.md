## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can a student identify duties, entry path, pay, and outlook?

**Coverage:** Use a dated BLS page and limitations.

**Evidence to seek:** current BLS occupation and wage data, local labor-market and credential rules, job advertisements, worker interviews, and guidance evaluations

**Limits:** Name occupation, country, region, and date. National projections and median wages are population statistics, not individual offers or guaranteed careers.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
