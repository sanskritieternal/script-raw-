## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence supports a widely repeated lightning story?

**Coverage:** Verify event time, location, strike detection, damage, and alternative causes.

**Evidence to seek:** calibrated ground and satellite lightning observations, radar and weather archives, official incident investigations, instrument metadata, and verified event records

**Limits:** Name event, place, time, flash definition, and detection method. Do not infer individual strike risk from historical counts or use these research questions as current safety guidance; cite official live alerts when needed.
