## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which records and metadata should be improved next?

**Coverage:** Set provenance, community input, privacy, and measurable discoverability.

**Evidence to seek:** original institutional, wage, legal, family, and movement records, object provenance, community archives, and voluntary consented oral histories
