## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** When and how did women enter a named field or organization?

**Coverage:** Trace rules, actual participation, roles, and barriers.

**Evidence to seek:** original institutional, wage, legal, family, and movement records, object provenance, community archives, and voluntary consented oral histories

**Limits:** Name place, period, institution, and population. Do not generalize one woman's experience to all women; distinguish law from actual access, check first claims carefully, and credit collaborators and unpaid labor.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
