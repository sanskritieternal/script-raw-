## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a waste-management approach gain or lose viability?

**Coverage:** Explain the growth or withdrawal of one waste-management approach using contracts, markets, contamination and operating results.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: weighed inputs and outputs, contracts, destination records and waste-worker accounts. Starting framework: US EPA materials, waste and recycling data (https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Track the full destination of waste; recycling claims need evidence. Niche boundary: Municipal material flows, collection, treatment and final destinations; household lifestyle choices and general ecology are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
