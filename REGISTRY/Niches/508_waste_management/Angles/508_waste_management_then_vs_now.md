## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do waste-performance trends survive consistent material and coverage definitions?

**Coverage:** Compare waste performance across two periods using consistent material definitions, population coverage and destination accounting.

**Evidence to seek:** Align definitions, samples, versions and measurement conditions. Case records: weighed inputs and outputs, contracts, destination records and waste-worker accounts. Starting framework: US EPA materials, waste and recycling data (https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Track the full destination of waste; recycling claims need evidence. Niche boundary: Municipal material flows, collection, treatment and final destinations; household lifestyle choices and general ecology are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
