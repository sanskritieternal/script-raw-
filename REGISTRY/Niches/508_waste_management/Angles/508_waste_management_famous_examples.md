## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do verified material destinations reveal about a waste program's results?

**Coverage:** Examine one waste-management program through collection coverage, material destinations and verified recovery; include operating costs and data limits.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: weighed inputs and outputs, contracts, destination records and waste-worker accounts. Starting framework: US EPA materials, waste and recycling data (https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Track the full destination of waste; recycling claims need evidence. Niche boundary: Municipal material flows, collection, treatment and final destinations; household lifestyle choices and general ecology are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
