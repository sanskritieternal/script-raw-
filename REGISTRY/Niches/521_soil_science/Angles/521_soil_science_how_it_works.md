## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where does uncertainty enter between selecting a soil sample and interpreting it?

**Coverage:** Map how one soil sample is selected, collected, analyzed and interpreted; identify uncertainty between field conditions and laboratory results.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the process, custody transfers and assigned responsibilities. Case records: soil surveys, site histories, depth and season metadata, laboratory methods and repeated field measurements. Starting framework: FAO Global Soil Partnership (https://www.fao.org/land-water/soils/global-soil-partnership/en). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name depth, location and method; avoid universal soil prescriptions. Niche boundary: Soil profiles, properties, processes and field measurement; farming instructions and general materials or biology are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
