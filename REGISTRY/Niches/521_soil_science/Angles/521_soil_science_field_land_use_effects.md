## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How much of a soil difference can be attributed to land use?

**Coverage:** Compare soil properties under defined land uses using matched sites or repeated observations; examine confounding and the time required for change.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: soil surveys, site histories, depth and season metadata, laboratory methods and repeated field measurements. Starting framework: FAO Global Soil Partnership (https://www.fao.org/land-water/soils/global-soil-partnership/en). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name depth, location and method; avoid universal soil prescriptions. Niche boundary: Soil profiles, properties, processes and field measurement; farming instructions and general materials or biology are neighboring subjects.
