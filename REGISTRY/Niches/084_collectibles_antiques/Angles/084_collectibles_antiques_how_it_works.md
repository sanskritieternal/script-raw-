## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How is one antique identified, documented, conserved, and displayed?

**Coverage:** Follow actual institutional records and distinguish catalog opinion from proof.

**Evidence to seek:** identified object catalogs, inventories and sale records, technical and condition reports, custody and export documents, maker records, and attributed source-community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, date, material, jurisdiction, and custody. Separate market value from historical significance, attribute contested ownership, and verify current trade and export rules before production.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
