## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence documents adoption or adaptation of one motif?

**Coverage:** Trace contact, chronology, and local reinterpretation without equating similarity with influence.

**Evidence to seek:** dated artifacts and inscriptions, original texts and translations, maker or patron records, collection provenance, and attributed practitioner and community accounts

**Limits:** Interpret a sign in its specific place, time, material, and use. Do not infer a universal or secret meaning from visual resemblance; respect restrictions and attribute contested readings.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
