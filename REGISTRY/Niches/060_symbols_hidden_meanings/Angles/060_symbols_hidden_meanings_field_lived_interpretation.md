## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does one wearer or viewer interpret a sign?

**Coverage:** Respect testimony while avoiding universal claims from one account.

**Evidence to seek:** dated artifacts and inscriptions, original texts and translations, maker or patron records, collection provenance, and attributed practitioner and community accounts

**Limits:** Interpret a sign in its specific place, time, material, and use. Do not infer a universal or secret meaning from visual resemblance; respect restrictions and attribute contested readings.
