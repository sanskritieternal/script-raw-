## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do head, flow, turbine, and generator interact?

**Coverage:** Use one project and season.

**Evidence to seek:** FERC project licenses and inspections, USGS river data, metered generation, flow and sediment records, environmental monitoring, and affected-community testimony

**Limits:** Name project, regulator, river reach, water year, and license condition. A renewable energy label does not establish ecological neutrality or dam safety.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
