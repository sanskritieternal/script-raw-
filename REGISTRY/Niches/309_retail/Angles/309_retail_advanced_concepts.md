## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why is a product stocked or removed?

**Coverage:** Compare margin, supplier terms, demand, and inventory risk.

**Evidence to seek:** dated store and firm records, retail statistical definitions, supplier terms, local planning data, and consented worker and shopper accounts

**Limits:** Name store or chain, product market, place, and period. Distinguish nominal sales from volume and individual store closure from company failure.
