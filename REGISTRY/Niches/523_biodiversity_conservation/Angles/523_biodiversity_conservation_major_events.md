## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after a dated protection measure or habitat disturbance?

**Coverage:** Examine a dated protection measure, habitat disturbance or species assessment through its implementation and later monitoring.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: dated species assessments, survey effort, site baselines, intervention records and local stewardship accounts. Starting framework: IUCN Red List categories and criteria (https://nrl.iucnredlist.org/resources/categories-and-criteria). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name species, site and baseline; designation is not proof of recovery. Niche boundary: Conservation interventions and ecological outcomes; species profiles and general ecosystem explanation have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
