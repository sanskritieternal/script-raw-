**Focus / example question:** Why did a conservation approach gain or lose support?

**Coverage:** Explain the expansion or withdrawal of one conservation approach using measured outcomes, funding and local effects rather than designation counts alone.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: dated species assessments, survey effort, site baselines, intervention records and local stewardship accounts. **Limits:** Name species, site and baseline; designation is not proof of recovery. Niche boundary: Conservation interventions and ecological outcomes; species profiles and general ecosystem explanation have separate homes.
