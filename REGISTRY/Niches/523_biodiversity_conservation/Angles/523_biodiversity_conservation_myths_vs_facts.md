## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** Testing a Biodiversity Conservation Claim

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a biodiversity success claim state its baseline and remaining threats?

**Coverage:** Test one biodiversity success claim against the species, site, baseline and monitoring evidence; examine displacement of threats or burdens.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: dated species assessments, survey effort, site baselines, intervention records and local stewardship accounts. Starting framework: IUCN Red List categories and criteria (https://nrl.iucnredlist.org/resources/categories-and-criteria). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name species, site and baseline; designation is not proof of recovery. Niche boundary: Conservation interventions and ecological outcomes; species profiles and general ecosystem explanation have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
