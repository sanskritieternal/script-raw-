## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What would a proposed conservation action need to show measurable progress?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed conservation action through threat reduction, land access, ecological timescale and monitoring requirements; distinguish forecasts from observed recovery.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: dated species assessments, survey effort, site baselines, intervention records and local stewardship accounts. Starting framework: IUCN Red List categories and criteria (https://nrl.iucnredlist.org/resources/categories-and-criteria). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name species, site and baseline; designation is not proof of recovery. Niche boundary: Conservation interventions and ecological outcomes; species profiles and general ecosystem explanation have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
