## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose labor supports a managed bee operation?

**Coverage:** Trace seasonal workers, queen breeders, transport crews, inspectors, and growers.

**Evidence to seek:** current local apiary rules, agriculture surveys, colony inspections, pest and pathogen studies, forage records, contracts, and consented beekeeper and grower accounts

**Limits:** Name species, region, operation type, season, and date. Distinguish managed honey bees from wild pollinators and colony counts from health; verify treatment and safety guidance with current local authorities.
