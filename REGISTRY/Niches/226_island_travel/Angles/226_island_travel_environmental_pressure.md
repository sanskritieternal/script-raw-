## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which impacts are measurable at one destination?

**Coverage:** Set boundaries for flights or ferries, water, waste, habitat, and energy.

**Evidence to seek:** current ferry and flight and local access notices, service-capacity records, dated visitor counts, shoreline and ecological monitoring, land records, and consented resident accounts

**Limits:** Name island, jurisdiction, season, operator, and date. Check current transport and weather before practical advice; do not label lived-in or traditionally used land empty or assume visitor spending benefits residents equally.
