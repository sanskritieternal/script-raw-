## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose access or interoperability changed after a governance decision?

**Coverage:** Examine one documented governance decision through procedures, participants and implementation; assess whose access or interoperability was affected.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: dated drafts and RFCs, public decisions, implementation records and jurisdiction-specific policies. Starting framework: IETF standards process (https://www.ietf.org/process/). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Separate standards bodies, states and platform power. Niche boundary: Internet coordination, standards and policy authority; internet history, general connectivity and speculative future networks are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
