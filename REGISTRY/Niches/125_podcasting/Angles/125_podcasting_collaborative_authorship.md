## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How are research, reporting, sound design, and hosting credited?

**Coverage:** Compare actual work, agreements, bylines, and listener-facing credits.

**Evidence to seek:** original episodes and transcripts, production and fact-check records, feed and hosting metadata, consent and rights agreements, audience methods, and listener feedback

**Limits:** Name show, episode, platform, release date, and metric definition. Protect interviewees, verify claims and rights, and distinguish downloads from people or comprehension.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
