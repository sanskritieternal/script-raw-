## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can authorized evidence establish about one knowledge practice or technology?

**Coverage:** Examine one technology or knowledge practice through community-authorized accounts and dated evidence; avoid extraction, novelty claims or restricted detail.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare validation, deployment and follow-up against a stated baseline. Case records: community-authorized historical sources, attributed oral accounts, provenance and dated external records. Starting framework: Smithsonian Native Knowledge 360 (https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use community names and permissions; avoid treating one account as universal. Niche boundary: Community-grounded Indigenous histories and authority over accounts; general culture surveys and universal historical narratives are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
