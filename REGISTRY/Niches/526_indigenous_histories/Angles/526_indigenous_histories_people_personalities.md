## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one person's actions relate to collective relationships and institutions?

**Coverage:** Reconstruct one person's documented role using appropriate community sources; situate individual action within collective relationships and historical conditions.

**Evidence to seek:** Use decision records, assigned authority and attributed accounts. Case records: community-authorized historical sources, attributed oral accounts, provenance and dated external records. Starting framework: Smithsonian Native Knowledge 360 (https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use community names and permissions; avoid treating one account as universal. Niche boundary: Community-grounded Indigenous histories and authority over accounts; general culture surveys and universal historical narratives are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
