## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do land records preserve or omit about a community's occupation?

**Coverage:** Examine one place's land records alongside community accounts and changing legal categories; distinguish recorded title from complete historical occupation.

**Evidence to seek:** Use the selected field lens's underlying records, measurement definitions and attributed accounts. Case records: community-authorized historical sources, attributed oral accounts, provenance and dated external records. Starting framework: Smithsonian Native Knowledge 360 (https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use community names and permissions; avoid treating one account as universal. Niche boundary: Community-grounded Indigenous histories and authority over accounts; general culture surveys and universal historical narratives are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
