## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a community's authorized account explain a historical development?

**Coverage:** Trace one community's account of a historical development using community-authorized sources and dated records; avoid imposing a single origin narrative.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: community-authorized historical sources, attributed oral accounts, provenance and dated external records. Starting framework: Smithsonian Native Knowledge 360 (https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Use community names and permissions; avoid treating one account as universal. Niche boundary: Community-grounded Indigenous histories and authority over accounts; general culture surveys and universal historical narratives are outside this plan.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
