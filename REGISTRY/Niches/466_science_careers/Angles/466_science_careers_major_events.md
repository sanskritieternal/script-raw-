## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Funding Change

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a documented grant or policy shift affect work?

**Coverage:** Compare positions and research output.

**Evidence to seek:** NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts

**Limits:** Name occupation, discipline, region, and year. A degree field is not an occupation, one scientist's path is not typical, and a projection is not a job offer.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
