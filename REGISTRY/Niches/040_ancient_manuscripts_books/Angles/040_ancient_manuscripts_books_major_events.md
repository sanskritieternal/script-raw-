## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What event moved or divided one manuscript collection?

**Coverage:** Trace inventories, sales, confiscations, transfers, and missing volumes.

**Evidence to seek:** collection catalogues and shelfmarks, high-resolution images, material and conservation reports, dated textual witnesses, colophons, inventories, and custodian accounts

**Limits:** Distinguish physical copy from text composition, and catalogue claim from verified provenance. Respect custodian access rules and conservation limits; label uncertain readings and translations.
