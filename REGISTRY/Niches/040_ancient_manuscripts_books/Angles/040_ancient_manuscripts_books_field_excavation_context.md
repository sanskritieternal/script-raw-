## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** If excavated, what do its layer and associated objects establish?

**Coverage:** Check site records, contamination, and custody; do not assume all manuscripts were excavated.

**Evidence to seek:** collection catalogues and shelfmarks, high-resolution images, material and conservation reports, dated textual witnesses, colophons, inventories, and custodian accounts

**Limits:** Distinguish physical copy from text composition, and catalogue claim from verified provenance. Respect custodian access rules and conservation limits; label uncertain readings and translations.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
