## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who or what does a specific joke invite the audience to criticize?

**Coverage:** Compare wording, context, and documented readings.

**Evidence to seek:** identified scripts and recordings, drafts, rehearsal and production records, dated reviews, and bounded audience evidence

**Limits:** Describe observable comic design and documented reception. Do not claim universal laughter, invent an origin for a joke, or overlook local context and attribution.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
