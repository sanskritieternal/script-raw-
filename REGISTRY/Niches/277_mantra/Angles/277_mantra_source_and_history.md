## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can recordings and manuscripts each establish about one formula?

**Coverage:** Date the witnesses without conflating their age with the practice's origin.

**Evidence to seek:** specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts

**Limits:** Name the source and lineage; respect restricted or initiated material and publication permissions. Attribute spiritual meanings and avoid presenting recitation as a proven medical treatment.
