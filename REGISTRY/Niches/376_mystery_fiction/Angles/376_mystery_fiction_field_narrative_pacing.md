## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does spacing evidence alter suspense without breaking logic?

**Coverage:** Map clue intervals and reader knowledge.

**Evidence to seek:** identified editions, dated drafts and outlines, clue inventories, publication and adaptation records, relevant historical sources, and situated reader responses

**Limits:** Keep the mystery and its solution specific to a work and edition. Distinguish literary clue logic from real forensic reliability and documented authorship from later lore.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
