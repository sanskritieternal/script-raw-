## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can a film show unequal power and diverse lives?

**Coverage:** Check class, race, sexuality, age, relationships, and evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** dated institutional, legal, wage, family, military, and migration records, material objects, scholarly gender history, and voluntary consented oral accounts

**Limits:** Name place, period, group, and institution. Do not treat men as an unmarked universal norm or one homogeneous group; distinguish prescribed masculinity from actual life and include people affected by men's power.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
