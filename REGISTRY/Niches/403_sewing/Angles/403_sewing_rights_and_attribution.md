## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who authored a pattern and what reuse terms apply?

**Coverage:** Check publisher, designer, and license.

**Evidence to seek:** surviving garments, dated patterns and size charts, maker and fitting records, textile tests, conservation reports, and consented wearer accounts

**Limits:** Name garment, fabric, pattern version, and wearer needs. Avoid a universal body standard or credit claim unsupported by maker records.
