## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a popular benefit claim match evidence for the actual beverage and dose?

**Coverage:** Check study design, population, and harms; seek current health guidance.

**Evidence to seek:** dated recipes and labels, food standards and health guidance, producer and worker records, supply-chain data, and attributed community accounts

**Limits:** Name beverage, place, age group where relevant, and date. Check health claims against current guidance, avoid glamorizing alcohol, and do not provide unsafe fermentation or preservation steps.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
