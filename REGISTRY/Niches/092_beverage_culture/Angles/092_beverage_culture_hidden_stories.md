**Focus / example question:** Whose cultivation, processing, service, or water work is left out?

**Coverage:** Follow a defined beverage chain with attributed worker and producer accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated recipes and labels, food standards and health guidance, producer and worker records, supply-chain data, and attributed community accounts
