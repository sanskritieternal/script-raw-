## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** How Suspense Works on the Page

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does delayed information make a reader anticipate danger?

**Coverage:** Analyze a bounded passage and show exactly what the narrator withholds.

**Evidence to seek:** published works and scripts, dated drafts and production records, credited creator interviews, audience classification guidance, and attributed criticism

**Limits:** Label fiction and invented reconstructions clearly. Respect source cultures and creators, use age-appropriate content framing, and avoid gratuitous or instructional depictions of harm.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
