## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can one news-trust survey establish about a community?

**Coverage:** Check sampling, wording, outlet specificity, and qualitative accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated original stories and editions, newsroom records, primary source documents, ownership and audience data, correction archives, and consented reporter and community accounts

**Limits:** Name outlet, story, jurisdiction, audience, and date. Verify exact claims against original records, protect vulnerable sources, and distinguish reach from trust or public effect.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
