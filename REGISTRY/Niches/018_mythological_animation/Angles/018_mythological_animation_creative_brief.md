## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which version and viewpoint will one short animated retelling present?

**Coverage:** Name tradition, source, target audience, creative license, and consultation plan.

**Evidence to seek:** named source texts or oral accounts with language and version, production scripts and credits, creator interviews, and attributed community interpretations

**Limits:** Name the source version and distinguish adaptation from tradition. Respect living communities, consultation, consent, restricted material, and differing interpretations.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
