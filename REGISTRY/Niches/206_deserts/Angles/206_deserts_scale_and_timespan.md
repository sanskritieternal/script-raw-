## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** At what scale is a dryland change claim valid?

**Coverage:** Define landform, watershed, region, years, and sampling coverage.

**Evidence to seek:** long-term precipitation and temperature records, repeat vegetation and soil protocols, calibrated imagery, land-use records, restoration monitoring, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name desert, landform, season, measure, and time span. Distinguish a brief green-up from lasting recovery and sparse visible cover from low biodiversity; respect land rights and fragile soil crusts.
