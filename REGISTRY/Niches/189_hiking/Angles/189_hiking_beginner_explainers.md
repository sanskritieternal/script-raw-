## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which route details matter before a newcomer starts?

**Coverage:** Define distance, ascent, surface, weather, daylight, facilities, and return options.

**Evidence to seek:** current land-manager notices, trail maps and inspections, visitor counts, weather records, accessibility details, ecological monitoring, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name the trail, jurisdiction, season, visitor profile, and date. Verify current conditions with the responsible land manager before practical route or safety guidance; avoid declaring any trail safe for every hiker.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
