## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose sketches, costume, rig, or paint choices shaped the final figure?

**Coverage:** Use dated asset records and credits.

**Evidence to seek:** dated briefs and sketches, approved model sheets, rigs or costume records, test shots, credits, production notes, and bounded audience feedback

**Limits:** Name the character and production version. Do not infer a designer's intent or assign one artist every production stage; attribute visual references.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
