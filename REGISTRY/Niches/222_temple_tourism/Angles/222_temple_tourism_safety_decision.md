## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which official information should a visitor verify before travel?

**Coverage:** Check opening, closures, weather, crowd measures, transport, and site guidance.

**Evidence to seek:** current site and custodial rules, archaeological and conservation records where relevant, dated visitor and service data, maps, and consented worshipper, worker, and neighbor accounts

**Limits:** Name temple, tradition, custodian, jurisdiction, and date. Attribute legends and contested histories; verify current entry and photography rules and do not assume a living temple functions only as a monument.
