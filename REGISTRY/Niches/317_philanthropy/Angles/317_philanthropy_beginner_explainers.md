## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does money move from donor to program and recipient?

**Coverage:** Map restrictions, administration, partners, and reporting.

**Evidence to seek:** dated grants and nonprofit filings where applicable, program and outcome data, governance records, and consented recipient and staff accounts

**Limits:** Name institution, program, jurisdiction, and period. Protect recipients' privacy and distinguish pledged, disbursed, and measured benefit; no tax advice.
