## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did a permit or license authorize at that date?

**Coverage:** Distinguish design approval, construction, and operation.

**Evidence to seek:** NRC licensing dockets and inspection findings, plant performance indicators, EIA net generation, environmental reviews, public incident records, and operator filings

**Limits:** Name reactor unit, license basis, authority, and date. Treat approvals and oversight as bounded evidence; avoid operational or security-sensitive instructions.
