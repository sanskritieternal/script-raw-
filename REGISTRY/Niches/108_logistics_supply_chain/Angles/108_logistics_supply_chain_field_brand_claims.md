## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a carrier's sustainability claim cover the full relevant route?

**Coverage:** Check mode, load factor, boundaries, and independent method.

**Evidence to seek:** shipment and custody records, contracts, route and capacity data, relevant trade rules, worker accounts, and measured service outcomes

**Limits:** Name product, route, dates, and chain boundary. Distinguish promised from observed delivery, avoid exposing security-sensitive routes, and check worker and environmental effects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
