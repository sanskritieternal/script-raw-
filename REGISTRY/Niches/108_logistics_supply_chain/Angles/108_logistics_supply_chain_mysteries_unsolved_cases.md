## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which evidence locates the cause of one delayed shipment?

**Coverage:** Check timestamps, border processing, capacity, weather, and missing scans.

**Evidence to seek:** shipment and custody records, contracts, route and capacity data, relevant trade rules, worker accounts, and measured service outcomes

**Limits:** Name product, route, dates, and chain boundary. Distinguish promised from observed delivery, avoid exposing security-sensitive routes, and check worker and environmental effects.
