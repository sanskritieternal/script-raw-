## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do trees, soil, fungi, water, animals, and people interact in one place?

**Coverage:** Use a bounded site and distinguish observations from models.

**Evidence to seek:** dated field plots and species surveys, remote-sensing products with validation, land and management records, ecological studies, and consented local knowledge

**Limits:** Name forest type, site, tenure context, time span, and definition. Distinguish tree cover from ecological condition and respect Indigenous and local knowledge and land rights.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
