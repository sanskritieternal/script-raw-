## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which study would most reduce uncertainty about one Ayurvedic claim?

**Coverage:** State comparator, sample, safety monitoring, and prerequisites without predicting success.

**Evidence to seek:** dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Attribute traditional explanations; distinguish clinical evidence from belief, laboratory findings, and anecdotes. Give no diagnosis or treatment advice; verify product-specific safety and current guidance.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
