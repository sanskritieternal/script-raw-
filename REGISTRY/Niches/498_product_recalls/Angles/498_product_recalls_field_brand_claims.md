## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does it match the regulator notice and observed hazard?

**Coverage:** Compare dated claims.

**Evidence to seek:** current CPSC, NHTSA, FDA, or local regulator recall notices, serial or lot identifiers, company remedy records, and completion data

**Limits:** Name product, model, serial or lot, jurisdiction, and notice date. Recall status and remedies can change; verify against the live regulator notice.
