## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did pay, tasks, security, and entry change?

**Coverage:** Adjust pay for inflation and compare the same geography and occupational definition.

**Evidence to seek:** current occupational classifications and labor surveys, dated job postings and licensing rules, training outcome data, employer policies, and consented worker and applicant accounts

**Limits:** Name occupation, location, career stage, and date. Avoid guaranteed job or salary claims and personal legal or financial advice; verify current licensing and labor rules and protect worker privacy.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
