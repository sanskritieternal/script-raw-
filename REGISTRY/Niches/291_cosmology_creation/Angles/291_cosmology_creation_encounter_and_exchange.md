## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence shows one community responding to another's account?

**Coverage:** Trace dated texts and real contact rather than resemblance alone.

**Evidence to seek:** specified sacred-text editions and translations, consented community accounts, dated commentaries, and current NASA or ESA mission observations for empirical claims

**Limits:** Attribute sacred narratives and interpretive claims to their communities. Keep empirical cosmology tied to observations and model uncertainty; do not claim an ancient text predicted a modern result from a loose translation.
