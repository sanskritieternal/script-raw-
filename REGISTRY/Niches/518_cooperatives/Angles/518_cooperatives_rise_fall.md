## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did a cooperative grow or decline under its market and financing conditions?

**Coverage:** Explain growth or decline of one cooperative using market, financing, governance and membership records; avoid assuming ownership form determines success.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.
