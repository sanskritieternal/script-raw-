## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose voluntary or administrative labor is omitted from a cooperative's account?

**Coverage:** Document overlooked member, administrative or community labor in one cooperative; obtain consent and distinguish paid from voluntary work.

**Evidence to seek:** Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
