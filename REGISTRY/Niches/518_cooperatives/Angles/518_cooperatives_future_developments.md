## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What enforceable rights and resources would a proposed cooperative need?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed cooperative model through capital, membership, governance and market requirements; distinguish aspirations from enforceable rights.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
