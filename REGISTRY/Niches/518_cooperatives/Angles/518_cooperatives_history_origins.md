## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a cooperative's founding need shape its ownership and trading rules?

**Coverage:** Trace one cooperative from its founding need through changes in membership, ownership and trading activity; use bylaws and dated business records.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.
