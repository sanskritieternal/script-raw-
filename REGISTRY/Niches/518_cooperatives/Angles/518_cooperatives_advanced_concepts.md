## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which objectives conflict when judging a cooperative's success?

**Coverage:** Compare methods of measuring cooperative success using financial resilience, member participation and distribution; identify conflicting objectives.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.
