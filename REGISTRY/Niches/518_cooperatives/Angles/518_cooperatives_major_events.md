## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed in control after a cooperative merger, conversion or crisis?

**Coverage:** Examine a dated merger, conversion or crisis through its effects on membership, finances and control; compare original aims with outcomes.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
