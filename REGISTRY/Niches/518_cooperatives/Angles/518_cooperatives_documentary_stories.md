## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a member need become a cooperative decision and observed outcome?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one cooperative decision from member need through negotiation, implementation and outcome; include dissenting member perspectives.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check actual voting and surplus rules; the label alone proves little. Niche boundary: Member ownership, cooperative governance and distribution; corporate empires and general workplace management have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
