## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one idea move through testing, review, and real use?

**Coverage:** Follow published records, independent checks, and patient or user experience.

**Evidence to seek:** original research and independent replication, public regulatory assessments, validated product specifications, quality and adverse-event records, and consented user accounts

**Limits:** Name product, intended use, population, jurisdiction, and date. Keep biological procedures at a high documentary level, defer hazardous work to qualified oversight, and separate tested outcomes from promotional claims.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
