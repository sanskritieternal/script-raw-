**Focus / example question:** What supports the latest status designation of one stock?

**Coverage:** Check assessment date, abundance, trend, mortality, threats, and review process.

**Evidence to seek:** current stock assessment reports, peer-reviewed surveys and methods, verified incident records, authorized tracking studies, and consented coastal and Indigenous accounts

**Limits:** Name species, stock, jurisdiction, season, and assessment year. Do not infer stock growth from sightings or one tracked animal; protect sensitive locations and follow current wildlife-viewing and research rules.
