## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How much of a present show is documented in earlier versions?

**Coverage:** Separate longstanding motifs from later additions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated programs and recordings, scripts where available, troupe records, object provenance, and consented performer and audience accounts

**Limits:** Name form, troupe, venue, and date. Respect performance and recording rights; distinguish live practice from edited tourist or archival excerpts.
