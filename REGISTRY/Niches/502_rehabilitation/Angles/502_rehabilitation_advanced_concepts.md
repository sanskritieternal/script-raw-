## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do alternative functional measures tell the same rehabilitation story?

**Coverage:** Compare functional outcome measures used in one rehabilitation study; examine baseline differences, follow-up duration and clinically meaningful change.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name condition, setting and outcome; do not promise individual recovery. Niche boundary: Rehabilitation pathways and patient-defined function; general medicine, health-system overviews and assistive-product engineering have separate homes.
