## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a rehabilitation claim describe the population and outcome actually studied?

**Coverage:** Test one rehabilitation claim against the population, comparator and outcome actually studied; separate measured function from promotional testimonials.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name condition, setting and outcome; do not promise individual recovery. Niche boundary: Rehabilitation pathways and patient-defined function; general medicine, health-system overviews and assistive-product engineering have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
