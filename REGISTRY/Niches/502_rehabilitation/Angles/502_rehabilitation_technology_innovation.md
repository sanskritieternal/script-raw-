## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did a rehabilitation technology's laboratory performance carry into everyday use?

**Coverage:** Evaluate a rehabilitation technology in a defined task and patient group; distinguish laboratory performance, clinical implementation and everyday use.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare validation, deployment and follow-up against a stated baseline. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name condition, setting and outcome; do not promise individual recovery. Niche boundary: Rehabilitation pathways and patient-defined function; general medicine, health-system overviews and assistive-product engineering have separate homes.
