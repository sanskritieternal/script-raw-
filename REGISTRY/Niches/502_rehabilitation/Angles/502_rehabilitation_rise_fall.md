**Focus / example question:** Why did a rehabilitation approach gain or lose institutional support?

**Coverage:** Explain why one rehabilitation approach expanded or declined using evidence, reimbursement, staffing and patient access records.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare a dated series with competing explanations for change. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name condition, setting and outcome; do not promise individual recovery. Niche boundary: Rehabilitation pathways and patient-defined function; general medicine, health-system overviews and assistive-product engineering have separate homes.
