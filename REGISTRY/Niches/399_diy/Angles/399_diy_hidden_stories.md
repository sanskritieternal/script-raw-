## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose trade knowledge, help, or inspection is omitted from a solo-build story?

**Coverage:** Use credits, invoices, and participant accounts.

**Evidence to seek:** dated plans and receipts, product data, manufacturer instructions, CPSC safety guidance, current local permit information, inspections, and consented household feedback

**Limits:** Name project, site, jurisdiction, and skill boundary. Do not turn an editorial question into unsupervised instructions for hazardous or licensed work.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
