## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who benefits and who bears costs when visitor access expands?

**Coverage:** Compare rights, ecological limits, infrastructure, and local views.

**Evidence to seek:** dated Survey of India maps and metadata, relevant state and district records, georeferenced imagery with field checks, physical studies, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name region, jurisdiction, map edition, scale, measure, and date. Attribute disputed boundaries and local names; avoid treating one region or season as representative of all India.
