## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did a defined audience take from a text?

**Coverage:** Use task-based feedback, not likes alone.

**Evidence to seek:** consented versioned drafts, editorial notes, source and rights records, published text, reader feedback, and writing-instruction evidence

**Limits:** Name genre, audience, brief, and version. A creator's workflow is a case, not a universal prescription; preserve credit and private drafts.
