## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did two named churches develop differently in comparable periods?

**Coverage:** Define doctrine, governance, language, and local context separately.

**Evidence to seek:** dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** Name church, place, and period; distinguish normative theology from documented practice and later memory. Avoid a single Christian trajectory or collective blame; protect private and affected voices.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
