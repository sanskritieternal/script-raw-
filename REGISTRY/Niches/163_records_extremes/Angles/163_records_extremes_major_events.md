## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What conditions produced one documented extreme?

**Coverage:** Compare contemporaneous observations and avoid single-cause claims.

**Evidence to seek:** original observations and instrument metadata, authority rules and review decisions, calibration and quality-control records, comparable historical datasets, and documented impacts

**Limits:** Name record category, geographic scope, interval, authority, and date. Do not use an unqualified ever claim, distinguish measured from modeled extremes, and verify that the cited record remains current.
