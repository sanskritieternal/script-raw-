## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can a field observation establish about a species?

**Coverage:** Separate occurrence, abundance, behavior, and causal explanation.

**Evidence to seek:** peer-reviewed field studies and raw survey methods, taxonomic records, dated conservation assessments, habitat data, and attributed local and Indigenous knowledge where permission is granted

**Limits:** Name species, population, place, method, and date. Account for detection and sampling bias; do not publish vulnerable locations or wildlife-handling instructions, and verify current status and local permits.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
