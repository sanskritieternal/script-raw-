## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did one transfer, loss, or return change for a work and its communities?

**Coverage:** Trace provenance, law, conservation, and public access.

**Evidence to seek:** collection catalogues and ownership records, object and conservation reports, maker documents, technical imaging, dated criticism, and attributed community perspectives

**Limits:** Verify provenance and attribution, distinguish observation from interpretation, respect cultural protocols and return claims, and label restored or reconstructed elements.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
