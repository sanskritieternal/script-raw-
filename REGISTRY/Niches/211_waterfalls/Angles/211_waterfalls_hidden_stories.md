## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose labor and land knowledge shape access and stewardship?

**Coverage:** Trace Indigenous and local knowledge, site workers, guides, and managers with consent.

**Evidence to seek:** geologic and topographic surveys, calibrated upstream gauges, repeat imagery with dates, site management records, habitat observations, and consented local accounts

**Limits:** Name waterfall, watershed, season, drop definition, and date. Do not equate photograph appearance with measured flow or infer access safety from historic descriptions; verify current closures and alerts.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
