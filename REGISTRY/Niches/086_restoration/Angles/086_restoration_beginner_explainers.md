## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do stabilization, repair, reconstruction, and interpretation differ?

**Coverage:** Use one identified object and documented treatment goals.

**Evidence to seek:** identified object and custody records, before-and-after condition reports, technical studies, treatment plans and logs, conservation guidance, and attributed custodian and source-community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, material, place, and treatment date. Distinguish original from reconstructed material, document uncertainty and rights, and avoid unqualified repair instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
