**Focus / example question:** Does a viral before-and-after image accurately represent the treatment?

**Coverage:** Check object identity, lighting, image processing, intervention, and condition.

**Evidence to seek:** identified object and custody records, before-and-after condition reports, technical studies, treatment plans and logs, conservation guidance, and attributed custodian and source-community accounts
