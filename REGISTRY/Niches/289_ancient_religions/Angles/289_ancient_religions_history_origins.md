## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What do the earliest surviving sources show about one named tradition?

**Coverage:** Date sources and distinguish practice from later reconstruction.

**Evidence to seek:** dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** State gaps and disputed interpretations; avoid presenting ancient traditions as uniform or automatically extinct. Let living communities describe their own continuity.
