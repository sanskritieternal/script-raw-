## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which new evidence could resolve a defined historical disagreement?

**Coverage:** Specify needed excavation, edition, or provenance without predicting discovery.

**Evidence to seek:** dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts

**Limits:** State gaps and disputed interpretations; avoid presenting ancient traditions as uniform or automatically extinct. Let living communities describe their own continuity.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
