## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which operating conditions did a certification or flight test cover, and which were outside it?

**Coverage:** Read dated test and approval records before comparing with service experience.

**Evidence to seek:** aircraft design and certification records, official occurrence investigations, public maintenance directives, flight and airport performance data, route records, and attributed worker and passenger accounts

**Limits:** Name aircraft variant, route, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish official findings from speculation; avoid pilot or maintenance instructions, security vulnerabilities, and unsupported blame. Check current authority records before production.
