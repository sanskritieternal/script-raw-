## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence shows change in context rather than toward perfection?

**Coverage:** Use an observed population and tradeoffs.

**Evidence to seek:** original field and laboratory studies, dated fossils and geologic context, sequence datasets, phylogenetic methods, and conservation assessments

**Limits:** Name species or lineage, trait, population, time span, and uncertainty. Avoid teleology and unsupported direct-ancestor claims.
