## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which material traces reveal how a craft object was made or used?

**Coverage:** Compare tool marks, wear, repairs, and non-destructive analysis limits.

**Evidence to seek:** dated objects and workshop records, material studies, market and training records, and consented maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name craft, community, object, and period. Seek maker permission for filming or sharing knowledge, protect restricted practices, and avoid a single authentic version.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
