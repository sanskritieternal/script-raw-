## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why do makers, dealers, and collectors describe one object differently?

**Coverage:** Attribute each account and test against provenance and material evidence.

**Evidence to seek:** dated objects and workshop records, material studies, market and training records, and consented maker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name craft, community, object, and period. Seek maker permission for filming or sharing knowledge, protect restricted practices, and avoid a single authentic version.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
