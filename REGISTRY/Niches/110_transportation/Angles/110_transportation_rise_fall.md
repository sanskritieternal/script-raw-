## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did one route or mode lose service?

**Coverage:** Compare ridership, funding, land use, regulation, and alternatives.

**Evidence to seek:** dated travel surveys and trip observations, planning and budget records, safety data, current accessibility standards, and attributed traveler and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name route, traveler group, jurisdiction, and date. Compare door-to-door journeys and safety exposure on equal terms; verify current rules and avoid sensitive vulnerabilities.
