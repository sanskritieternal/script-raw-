## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a verification effort document evidence, corrections and remaining uncertainty?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one verification effort from initial claim through evidence checks, publication and correction; document unresolved uncertainties.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: original claims and records, dated redistribution traces, lesson designs and original learning evaluations. Starting framework: UNESCO media and information literacy curriculum (https://www.unesco.org/mil4teachers/en/introduction). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check original evidence and assess learning, not just confidence. Niche boundary: Claim checking, media interpretation and evaluated learning; media history, account security and general education have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
