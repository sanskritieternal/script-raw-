## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What audience and evaluation evidence would a proposed literacy program require?

**Coverage:** Assess a proposed media-literacy intervention through audience needs, teaching design, evaluation and access requirements; frame benefits as testable expectations.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the actual proposal and distinguish required conditions from achieved results. Case records: original claims and records, dated redistribution traces, lesson designs and original learning evaluations. Starting framework: UNESCO media and information literacy curriculum (https://www.unesco.org/mil4teachers/en/introduction). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check original evidence and assess learning, not just confidence. Niche boundary: Claim checking, media interpretation and evaluated learning; media history, account security and general education have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
