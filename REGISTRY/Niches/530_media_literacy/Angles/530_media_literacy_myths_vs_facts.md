## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does original evidence establish about a viral claim's accuracy?

**Coverage:** Test one viral or widely repeated claim against the earliest accessible source and supporting evidence; distinguish unsupported, misleading and demonstrably false statements.

**Evidence to seek:** Trace the public claim to original evidence and retain its qualifications. Case records: original claims and records, dated redistribution traces, lesson designs and original learning evaluations. Starting framework: UNESCO media and information literacy curriculum (https://www.unesco.org/mil4teachers/en/introduction). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Check original evidence and assess learning, not just confidence. Niche boundary: Claim checking, media interpretation and evaluated learning; media history, account security and general education have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
