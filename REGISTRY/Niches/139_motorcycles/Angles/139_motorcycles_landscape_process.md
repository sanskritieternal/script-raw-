## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do terrain and weather shape road design and motorcycle use?

**Coverage:** Study mapped gradient, drainage, maintenance, and incident records.

**Evidence to seek:** dated vehicle and service specifications, registration and traffic exposure data, official crash and road records, current local law, safety standards, and consented rider and worker accounts

**Limits:** Name vehicle class, model, place, jurisdiction, and date. Keep riding and mechanical hazards at documentary level, use qualified safety sources, and distinguish exposure-adjusted risk from raw crash totals.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
