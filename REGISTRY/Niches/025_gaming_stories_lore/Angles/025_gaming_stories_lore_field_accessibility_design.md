## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which audio, text, control, or timing barriers keep a defined group from narrative content?

**Coverage:** Test available options and describe remaining gaps.

**Evidence to seek:** released versioned games and patch notes, design documents and credits, creator interviews, consented playtests, accessibility testing, and documented fan interpretations

**Limits:** Distinguish released canon, creator comment, and fan theory by version. Credit creators and communities, respect rights, and test whether accessibility options expose the story.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
