## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Whose evidence was left out of a standard overview?

**Coverage:** Compare source selection and contributors.

**Evidence to seek:** primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides

**Limits:** Define terms, region, date, and measurement. A broad fact without scope or source is not ready for production; avoid implying one learner response proves mastery.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
