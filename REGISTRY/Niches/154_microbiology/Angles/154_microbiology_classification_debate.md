## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why do taxonomic schemes place one organism differently?

**Coverage:** Compare phylogeny, naming conventions, data quality, and version.

**Evidence to seek:** peer-reviewed observational and experimental studies, versioned sequence and taxonomy databases, transparent sampling and controls, environmental measurements, and qualified biosafety assessments

**Limits:** Name organism or community, strain where relevant, habitat, method, and date. Avoid universal claims from one sample, distinguish association from causation, and keep culturing or handling details with qualified biosafety professionals.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
