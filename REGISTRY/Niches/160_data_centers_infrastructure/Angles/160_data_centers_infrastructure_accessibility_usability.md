## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does nearby infrastructure improve service for local users?

**Coverage:** Compare latency, price, access, reliability, and digital inclusion.

**Evidence to seek:** facility permits and grid records, metered energy and water with boundary definitions, public incident reports, operational and workload data, lifecycle studies, and consented worker and community accounts

**Limits:** Name site, operator, workload, metric boundary, jurisdiction, and date. Avoid exposing operational security details; distinguish facility efficiency from total resource demand and verify promotional claims independently.
