## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Who owns a reproduction and who authored the written work?

**Coverage:** Separate object custody, copyright, and creator credit.

**Evidence to seek:** identified works and inscriptions, language-specific scholarship, collection provenance, maker records, material analysis, and conservation reports

**Limits:** Name script, language, place, date, and object. Distinguish reading from visual interpretation, and attribution from stylistic resemblance.
