## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How is one study reviewed, run, analyzed, and reported?

**Coverage:** Follow registration, consent, safeguards, outcome, and correction.

**Evidence to seek:** original protocols and registrations, ethics-review and consent records, deidentified outcome and attrition data, replications, complaints and corrections, and voluntary participant accounts

**Limits:** Name study, population, setting, jurisdiction, and date. Do not stage deceptive public tests for content; evaluate live research with qualified oversight, informed consent, privacy safeguards, and risk review.
