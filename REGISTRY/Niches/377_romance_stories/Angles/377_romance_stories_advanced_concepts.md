**Focus / example question:** How do asymmetries of status or resources shape consent and agency?

**Coverage:** Trace character options without diagnosing people.

**Limits:** State whether the work is genre romance or an adjacent love story. Describe fictional relationships and agency without applying one ending rule to every cultural tradition.
