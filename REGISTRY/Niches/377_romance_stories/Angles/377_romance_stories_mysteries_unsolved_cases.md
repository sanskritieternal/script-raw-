## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** An Ambiguous Ending

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a story establish a continuing relationship or leave it open?

**Coverage:** Read ending, earlier promises, and alternate editions.

**Evidence to seek:** identified texts and editions, creator drafts, publisher framing, period context, adaptation records, and situated reader criticism

**Limits:** State whether the work is genre romance or an adjacent love story. Describe fictional relationships and agency without applying one ending rule to every cultural tradition.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
