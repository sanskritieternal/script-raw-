## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does a romance keep the relationship central alongside other plots?

**Coverage:** Map scenes and consequences in one work.

**Evidence to seek:** identified texts and editions, creator drafts, publisher framing, period context, adaptation records, and situated reader criticism

**Limits:** State whether the work is genre romance or an adjacent love story. Describe fictional relationships and agency without applying one ending rule to every cultural tradition.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
