**Coverage:** Verify source, influence, and justified treatment.

**Evidence to seek:** original datasets and code where available, survey and measurement methods, official revisions, independent reanalyses, and data documentation

**Limits:** Define population, denominator, period, and estimand. Distinguish association from causation and statistical significance from practical importance; protect private records.
