## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What information would most reduce uncertainty on one question?

**Coverage:** Specify target, frame, sample, measurement, privacy, and cost.

**Evidence to seek:** original datasets and code where available, survey and measurement methods, official revisions, independent reanalyses, and data documentation

**Limits:** Define population, denominator, period, and estimand. Distinguish association from causation and statistical significance from practical importance; protect private records.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
