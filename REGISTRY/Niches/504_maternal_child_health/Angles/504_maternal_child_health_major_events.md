## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What changed after a maternal or child service opened, closed or reorganized?

**Coverage:** Follow a dated service expansion, closure or policy change; compare access and continuity records for the same population.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated before-and-after record without assuming causation. Case records: age-specific service indicators, referral and follow-up records, original program evaluations. Starting framework: WHO maternal and child health monitoring resources (https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/epidemiology-monitoring-and-evaluation). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Specify population and jurisdiction; no individualized medical instruction. Niche boundary: Maternal and child service pathways and access; broad wellness and general health-system subjects remain separate.
