## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do access measures and health-outcome measures describe the same service performance?

**Coverage:** Compare measures used in one maternal or child health evaluation; examine denominators, case mix, follow-up and differences between access and outcomes.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: age-specific service indicators, referral and follow-up records, original program evaluations. Starting framework: WHO maternal and child health monitoring resources (https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/epidemiology-monitoring-and-evaluation). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Specify population and jurisdiction; no individualized medical instruction. Niche boundary: Maternal and child service pathways and access; broad wellness and general health-system subjects remain separate.
