## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What does a program evaluation establish for its defined families and setting?

**Coverage:** Examine one documented maternal or child health program using eligibility, uptake and outcomes; separate evaluation findings from individual care advice.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: age-specific service indicators, referral and follow-up records, original program evaluations. Starting framework: WHO maternal and child health monitoring resources (https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/epidemiology-monitoring-and-evaluation). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Specify population and jurisdiction; no individualized medical instruction. Niche boundary: Maternal and child service pathways and access; broad wellness and general health-system subjects remain separate.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
