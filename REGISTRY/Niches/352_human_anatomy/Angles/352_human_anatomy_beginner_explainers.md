## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Where is one organ and how does its shape support a task?

**Coverage:** Use accepted terminology and a bounded example.

**Evidence to seek:** NIH anatomical reference resources, consented specimen and imaging studies, terminology standards, atlas provenance, and population variation research

**Limits:** Name structure, orientation, imaging method, population, and age range. Treat normal variation neutrally and do not diagnose a person from a diagram.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
