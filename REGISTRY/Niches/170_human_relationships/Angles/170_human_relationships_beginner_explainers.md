## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can surveys and interviews reveal about a named relationship?

**Coverage:** Define population, measure, context, and privacy boundary.

**Evidence to seek:** peer-reviewed studies with defined populations, comparable surveys and time-use data, relevant policies, and separate voluntary consented accounts from participants

**Limits:** Name relationship type, population, place, and period. Avoid universal relationship advice or diagnosis, protect third-party privacy, and account for power or dependency in consent.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
