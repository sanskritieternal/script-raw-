## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can a team test whether one enrichment change helps?

**Coverage:** Use veterinary or welfare oversight, baseline, behavior measures, and animal choice.

**Evidence to seek:** current veterinary and welfare guidance, original clinical and behavior studies, shelter and household outcome data, product tests, and consented owner and caregiver accounts

**Limits:** Name species, life stage, population, jurisdiction, and date. Do not diagnose or prescribe for an individual animal; verify current veterinary and local legal guidance and protect owner privacy.
