## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why do sources disagree on a ruler's start or end date?

**Coverage:** Compare accession, coronation, effective control, calendar, and record survival.

**Evidence to seek:** charters and legal acts, chronicles, royal and state archives, correspondence, household accounts, coins and seals, local tax or court records, and critical translations

**Limits:** Distinguish legal claim, ceremony, and effective control. Treat genealogies and court chronicles as situated sources, protect living people, and seek voices beyond the palace.
