## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a claim to rule proceed from assertion to recognition or failure?

**Coverage:** Use dated correspondence, legal acts, and responses beyond the court.

**Evidence to seek:** charters and legal acts, chronicles, royal and state archives, correspondence, household accounts, coins and seals, local tax or court records, and critical translations

**Limits:** Distinguish legal claim, ceremony, and effective control. Treat genealogies and court chronicles as situated sources, protect living people, and seek voices beyond the palace.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
