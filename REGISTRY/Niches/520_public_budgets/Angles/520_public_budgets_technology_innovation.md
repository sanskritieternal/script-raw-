## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did a digital budget system improve usable transparency and accountability?

**Coverage:** Evaluate one digital budget or procurement system through transparency, access, data quality and actual decision use; avoid equating publication with accountability.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare validation, deployment and follow-up against a stated baseline. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish appropriation, expenditure and outcome; name fiscal year. Niche boundary: Public revenue, spending execution and accountability; general government administration and geopolitical narratives have separate homes.
