**Focus / example question:** What financing and implementation assumptions support a proposed budget measure?

**Coverage:** Assess one proposed budget measure through financing, legal authority, implementation capacity and monitoring; distinguish forecasts from committed funds.

**Limits:** Distinguish appropriation, expenditure and outcome; name fiscal year. Niche boundary: Public revenue, spending execution and accountability; general government administration and geopolitical narratives have separate homes.
