## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Why did actual services differ from a budget promise?

**Coverage:** Investigate one discrepancy between budget promises and service delivery using execution and audit records; identify missing or incomparable data.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish appropriation, expenditure and outcome; name fiscal year. Niche boundary: Public revenue, spending execution and accountability; general government administration and geopolitical narratives have separate homes.
