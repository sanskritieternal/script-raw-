## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a spending promise proceed through delivery and audit?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced timeline for one public expenditure from promise through approval, delivery and audit; document unresolved findings.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish appropriation, expenditure and outcome; name fiscal year. Niche boundary: Public revenue, spending execution and accountability; general government administration and geopolitical narratives have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
