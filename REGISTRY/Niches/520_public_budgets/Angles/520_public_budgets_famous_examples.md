## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What did a public spending initiative deliver beyond its appropriation?

**Coverage:** Examine one public budget initiative using appropriations, actual spending and service outcomes; state fiscal year, government level and accounting basis.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Distinguish appropriation, expenditure and outcome; name fiscal year. Niche boundary: Public revenue, spending execution and accountability; general government administration and geopolitical narratives have separate homes.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
