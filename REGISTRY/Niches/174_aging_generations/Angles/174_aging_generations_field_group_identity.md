## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do people identify with a named generation?

**Coverage:** Compare self-description, birth years, shared events, and internal difference.

**Evidence to seek:** age-disaggregated population and service data, longitudinal research, current policies, accessibility assessments, and independent voluntary accounts from older and younger people

**Limits:** Name age measure, cohort, place, and period. Avoid generational stereotypes and treating chronological age as ability; protect privacy and autonomy where care relationships affect consent.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
