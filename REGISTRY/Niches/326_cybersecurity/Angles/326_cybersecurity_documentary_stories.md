## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did an organization restore trusted service?

**Coverage:** Use public timeline, impacted users, and documented lessons.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST frameworks, public incident reports, system inventories, control tests, measurements, and consented user accounts

**Limits:** Describe defenses and evidence at a safe level. Do not provide operational intrusion instructions or treat a framework as proof of security.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
