## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What can anatomy and context reveal about behavior?

**Coverage:** Separate direct evidence, comparison, and speculation.

**Evidence to seek:** museum specimen and collection records, field notes, stratigraphic maps, dating studies, comparative anatomy, and Smithsonian paleobiology guidance

**Limits:** Name specimen catalog, locality, layer, age method, and preservation. Separate observed anatomy from reconstructed behavior and direct-ancestor claims.
