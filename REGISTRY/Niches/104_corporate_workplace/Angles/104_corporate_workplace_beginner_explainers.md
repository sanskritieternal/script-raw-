## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do employer, contractor, manager, and team roles differ?

**Coverage:** Map one organization's actual decision and pay relationships.

**Evidence to seek:** employment and policy records, representative workforce data, labor standards, workplace surveys, and consented worker accounts

**Limits:** Name workplace, jurisdiction, worker group, and period. Check labor law and safety obligations, protect vulnerable workers, and separate company claims from observed conditions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
