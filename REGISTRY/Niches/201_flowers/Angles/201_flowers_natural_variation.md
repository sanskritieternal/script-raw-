## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How much timing variation occurs among individuals and years?

**Coverage:** Use multi-year records and local weather context.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered plant identity, repeated phenology observations, visitor and pollen-transfer studies, seed-set measurements, weather records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name plant species, place, phenophase, observation interval, and date. Distinguish a flower visitor from an effective pollinator and one early bloom from a population or climate trend.
