## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How does one flowering event affect people and place?

**Coverage:** Compare cultural use, tourism, growing practices, access, and habitat effects.

**Evidence to seek:** vouchered plant identity, repeated phenology observations, visitor and pollen-transfer studies, seed-set measurements, weather records, and species assessments

**Limits:** Name plant species, place, phenophase, observation interval, and date. Distinguish a flower visitor from an effective pollinator and one early bloom from a population or climate trend.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
