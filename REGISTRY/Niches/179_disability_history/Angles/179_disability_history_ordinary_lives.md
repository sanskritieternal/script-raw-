## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What records show a disabled person's ordinary routines?

**Coverage:** Use consented family, work, community, and object histories.

**Evidence to seek:** self-authored disability movement records, dated policy and implementation files, building and transport plans, object provenance, and accessible consented oral histories

**Limits:** Name place, period, institution, and group. Use participant-preferred language, avoid tragedy or inspiration framing, distinguish legal rights from practical access, and provide accessible participation and review.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
