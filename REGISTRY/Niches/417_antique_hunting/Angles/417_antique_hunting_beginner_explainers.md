## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence distinguishes age, maker, and legal category?

**Coverage:** Define item and place.

**Evidence to seek:** ICOM museum ethics and provenance guidance, UNESCO cultural-property convention, object records, conservation reports, and source-community accounts

**Limits:** Name object, place, date, and ownership evidence. A seller's story, mark, or test alone does not prove lawful provenance or authenticity.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
