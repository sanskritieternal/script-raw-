## Q3: Primary Story Angle
**Default:** A Safety Measure After Failure

## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did one new design or monitoring method reduce the identified risk?

**Coverage:** Check validation, adoption, later incidents, and unintended effects.

**Evidence to seek:** official inquiry reports and exhibits, dated design and maintenance records, physical evidence and qualified tests, standards in force at the time, and documented recommendation follow-up

**Limits:** Name incident, system, jurisdiction, and date. Avoid blame without evidence, preserve inquiry uncertainty and affected people's dignity, and keep hazardous repair or forensic work with qualified investigators.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
