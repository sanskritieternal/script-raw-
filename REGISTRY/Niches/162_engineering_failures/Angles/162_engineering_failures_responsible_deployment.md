## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did an organization translate findings into safer practice?

**Coverage:** Review transparent reporting, worker participation, remedy, and follow-up.

**Evidence to seek:** official inquiry reports and exhibits, dated design and maintenance records, physical evidence and qualified tests, standards in force at the time, and documented recommendation follow-up

**Limits:** Name incident, system, jurisdiction, and date. Avoid blame without evidence, preserve inquiry uncertainty and affected people's dignity, and keep hazardous repair or forensic work with qualified investigators.
