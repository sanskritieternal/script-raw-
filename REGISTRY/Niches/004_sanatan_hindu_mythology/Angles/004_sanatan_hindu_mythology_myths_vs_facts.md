## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which parts of a popular claim come from text, belief, or historical evidence?

**Coverage:** Separate what a source narrates, what practitioners believe, and what independent historical evidence can establish about one claim.

**Evidence to seek:** identified texts and editions, attributed translations or commentaries, dated performance records and consented community accounts appropriate to the selected narrative

**Limits:** Attribute the text, version, period and community; distinguish sacred narrative, interpretation and historical evidence, and honor restrictions on living or restricted traditions.
