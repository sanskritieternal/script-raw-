## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What happens when a player acts in one game?

**Coverage:** Trace input, rules, state change, feedback, and save behavior.

**Evidence to seek:** dated design and production records, playable builds and patch notes, authorized code or documentation, playtest methods, player feedback, and attributed developer accounts

**Limits:** Name game, build, platform, team, and date. Distinguish design goals from tested player outcomes, credit contributors, and avoid exploit or cheat instructions.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
