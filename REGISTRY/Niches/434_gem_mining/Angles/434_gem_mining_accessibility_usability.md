**Focus / example question:** Can affected residents understand permits and monitoring?

**Coverage:** Check language and disclosure.

**Evidence to seek:** USGS gem geology, site and labor records, laboratory reports with reference sets, parcel custody records, certificate terms, and environmental monitoring

**Limits:** Name gem material, deposit, parcel, place, and date. A rough-diamond trade certificate does not certify every gem or all labor and environmental conditions.
