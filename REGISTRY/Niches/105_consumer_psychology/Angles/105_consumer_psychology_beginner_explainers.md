## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How can the same offer appear different under two presentations?

**Coverage:** Compare matched price, terms, and wording with a defined consumer group.

**Evidence to seek:** original behavioral studies, representative consumer research, actual offers and contract terms, transaction data, and current consumer-protection guidance

**Limits:** Name product, market, consumer group, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish observed behavior from motives; avoid manipulative shopping advice or universal psychological labels.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
