## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did one researcher define and measure a consumer behavior?

**Coverage:** Read methods and collaborators rather than popularized anecdotes.

**Evidence to seek:** original behavioral studies, representative consumer research, actual offers and contract terms, transaction data, and current consumer-protection guidance

**Limits:** Name product, market, consumer group, jurisdiction, and date. Distinguish observed behavior from motives; avoid manipulative shopping advice or universal psychological labels.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
