## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do image and sound place an audience inside or outside a character's knowledge?

**Coverage:** Compare scene choices and plausible interpretations in one film.

**Evidence to seek:** identified film versions, dated scripts and production files, trade and release records, contemporary reviews, crew interviews, and film archive and restoration documentation

**Limits:** Name title, release version, language, territory, and date. Distinguish evidence on screen from claimed creator intent, credit collaborators, and verify clip rights before reproduction.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
