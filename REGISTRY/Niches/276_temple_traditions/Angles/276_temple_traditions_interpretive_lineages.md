## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How do two lineages explain the same temple practice differently?

**Coverage:** Represent each view in its own terms with attributed sources.

**Evidence to seek:** original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts

**Limits:** Attribute beliefs and local protocols; distinguish source tradition from independently dated history. Respect restricted practice and avoid treating one lineage as universal.
