## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which findings from one model hold in another species?

**Coverage:** Compare homologous mechanisms and direct tests.

**Evidence to seek:** original biological studies, specimen and protocol records, independent replication, comparative data, field observations, and official species assessments

**Limits:** Name organism, population, condition, endpoint, and time. State limits of model-organism generalization and separate basic findings from applied claims.
