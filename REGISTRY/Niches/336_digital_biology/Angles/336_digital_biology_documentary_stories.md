## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a specimen and its records reach a published result?

**Coverage:** Follow consent, metadata, analysis, and reanalysis.

**Evidence to seek:** versioned biological repositories, sample metadata, published methods, computational pipelines, independent experiments, and official species assessments

**Limits:** Name organism, population, dataset accession, reference version, and biological question. Distinguish modeled predictions from experimental or field evidence.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
