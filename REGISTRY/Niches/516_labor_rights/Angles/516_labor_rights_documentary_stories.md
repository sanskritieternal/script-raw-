## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How did a workplace-rights issue proceed from complaint to documented remedy?

**Coverage:** Build a sourced account of one workplace-rights case from the original issue through review and remedy; include consented affected voices.

**Evidence to seek:** Build a dated evidence trail including dissent and unresolved findings. Case records: applicable local laws, collective agreements, enforcement decisions and consented worker accounts. Starting framework: ILO conventions, protocols and recommendations (https://www.ilo.org/international-labour-standards/conventions-protocols-and-recommendations). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and employment status; prompts are not legal advice. Niche boundary: Workplace protections, coverage and enforcement; career coaching, general management and consumer transactions are neighboring subjects.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
