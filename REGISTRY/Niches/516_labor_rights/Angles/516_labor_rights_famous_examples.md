## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did a labor-rights intervention improve workers' usable access to protection?

**Coverage:** Examine one documented labor-rights intervention through coverage, enforcement and worker outcomes; distinguish legal entitlement from practical access.

**Evidence to seek:** Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: applicable local laws, collective agreements, enforcement decisions and consented worker accounts. Starting framework: ILO conventions, protocols and recommendations (https://www.ilo.org/international-labour-standards/conventions-protocols-and-recommendations). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and employment status; prompts are not legal advice. Niche boundary: Workplace protections, coverage and enforcement; career coaching, general management and consumer transactions are neighboring subjects.
