## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Did a payroll or reporting tool make a labor protection more usable?

**Coverage:** Evaluate one payroll, scheduling or reporting technology against a defined labor-rights problem; examine transparency, access and dispute records.

**Evidence to seek:** Compare validation, deployment and follow-up against a stated baseline. Case records: applicable local laws, collective agreements, enforcement decisions and consented worker accounts. Starting framework: ILO conventions, protocols and recommendations (https://www.ilo.org/international-labour-standards/conventions-protocols-and-recommendations). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and employment status; prompts are not legal advice. Niche boundary: Workplace protections, coverage and enforcement; career coaching, general management and consumer transactions are neighboring subjects.
