**Focus / example question:** Who is covered by one workplace right and how is it enforced?

**Coverage:** Explain one workplace right through published rules and a documented process; distinguish employees, contractors and jurisdiction-specific eligibility.

**Evidence to seek:** Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: applicable local laws, collective agreements, enforcement decisions and consented worker accounts. Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting.

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction and employment status; prompts are not legal advice. Niche boundary: Workplace protections, coverage and enforcement; career coaching, general management and consumer transactions are neighboring subjects.
