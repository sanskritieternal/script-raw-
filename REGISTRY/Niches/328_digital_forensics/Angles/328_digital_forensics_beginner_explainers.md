## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How is digital material preserved and interpreted?

**Coverage:** Explain authorization, acquisition, integrity, analysis, and reporting.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST evidence-preservation guidance, public case records, tool validation studies, custody logs, and controlled test data

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction, device, software version, acquisition method, and authority. Separate artifact observation from attribution; protect unrelated private material.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
