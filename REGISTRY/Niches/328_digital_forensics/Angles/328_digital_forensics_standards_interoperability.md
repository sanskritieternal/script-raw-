## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Do two tools interpret the same artifact consistently?

**Coverage:** Compare versions, schemas, discrepancies, and manual checks.

**Evidence to seek:** NIST evidence-preservation guidance, public case records, tool validation studies, custody logs, and controlled test data

**Limits:** Name jurisdiction, device, software version, acquisition method, and authority. Separate artifact observation from attribution; protect unrelated private material.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
