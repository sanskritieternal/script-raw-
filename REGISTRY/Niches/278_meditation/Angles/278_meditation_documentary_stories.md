**Focus / example question:** How did a meditation program move from teaching to public health claims?

**Coverage:** Follow manuals, trials, reviews, marketing, and corrections.

**Evidence to seek:** specified primary texts and translations, teaching manuals, registered trials and systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, adverse-event reports, and consented accounts

**Limits:** Name the exact method, lineage or program, population, and outcome. Attribute spiritual claims; do not present an anecdote as clinical proof or provide therapeutic instructions. Include possible harms and current guidance.
