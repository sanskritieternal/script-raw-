## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Does a study claim reflect the population and task?

**Coverage:** Read original research and evidence quality.

**Evidence to seek:** IES learning practice guides, original intervention studies, learner task data, delayed assessments, accessible materials, and implementation records

**Limits:** Name subject, learner group, comparator, and outcome. A short-term score or subjective familiarity does not establish durable learning for everyone.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
