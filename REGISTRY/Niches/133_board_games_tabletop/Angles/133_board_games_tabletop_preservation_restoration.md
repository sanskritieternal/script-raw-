## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which materials and instructions must an archive keep?

**Coverage:** Preserve board, pieces, rules, edition, box, and play documentation.

**Evidence to seek:** dated rulebooks and editions, physical game sets, prototypes and playtest records, publisher archives, and attributed player and maker accounts

**Limits:** Name game, edition, language, and date. Test rules against actual components, credit collaborators, and avoid treating a single group's house rules as universal.
