## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** What evidence would justify a proposed rule revision?

**Coverage:** Define player group, balance measure, access, costs, and playtest plan.

**Evidence to seek:** dated rulebooks and editions, physical game sets, prototypes and playtest records, publisher archives, and attributed player and maker accounts

**Limits:** Name game, edition, language, and date. Test rules against actual components, credit collaborators, and avoid treating a single group's house rules as universal.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
