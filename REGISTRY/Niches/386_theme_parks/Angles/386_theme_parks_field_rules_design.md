## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** How are access, queue, and safety instructions communicated?

**Coverage:** Review actual guest materials and relevant jurisdiction.

**Evidence to seek:** dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research

**Limits:** Name park, attraction, jurisdiction, and date. Do not infer safety from a promotional description or universal guest experience from selected reviews.
