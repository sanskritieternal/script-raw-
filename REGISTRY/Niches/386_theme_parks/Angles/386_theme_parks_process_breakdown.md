## Editorial scope

**Focus / example question:** Which handoffs move an attraction from concept to safe use?

**Coverage:** Trace approvals, tests, training, and operating revisions.

**Evidence to seek:** dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research

**Limits:** Name park, attraction, jurisdiction, and date. Do not infer safety from a promotional description or universal guest experience from selected reviews.

Before Phase 1, narrow this guidance to a specific case, source or work, and the relevant place, population, version or period. Record that choice in the episode's story question and research summary.
