### Global Production Rules

- Preserve one visual identity throughout a video unless the story intentionally changes era, location, or emotional state.
- Treat the **Primary Visual Style** as the default; use the **Visual Language** as the shot-design rule.
- Palette is a guidance system, not a requirement to force exact colors into every frame.
- For AI video, keep character identity, wardrobe, architecture, props, period, and lighting consistent across scenes.
- For documentary niches, prioritize believable environments, motivated camera movement, period-appropriate materials, and source-faithful visual evidence.
- For educational niches, visual clarity beats spectacle; diagrams and labels should remain readable and sparse.
- For devotional/mythological niches, distinguish traditional/scriptural representation from imaginative cinematic interpretation when accuracy matters.

## Relationship to the 500-Niche Content Database

- The supplied niche database is the **content taxonomy**: 500 major niche families × 15 content directions = 7,500 opportunities. fileciteturn1file0L11-L27
- This file is the **visual taxonomy**: one default visual-style profile for every major niche, while allowing the 15 content directions to inherit or override the niche-level style.
- Recommended inheritance: `Niche Style → Sub-Niche Override → Episode Style → Scene Style → Shot Override`.
- This prevents every video from looking random while still allowing different stories, eras, emotions, and locations.
