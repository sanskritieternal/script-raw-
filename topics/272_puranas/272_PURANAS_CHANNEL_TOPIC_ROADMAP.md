## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Puranas across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Puranas Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Puranas.
- **Tier 2: Puranas Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Puranas.
- **Tier 3: Puranas Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Puranas.
- **Tier 4: Puranas Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Puranas.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Puranas: Formation of a Named Purāṇa**
   - *Question:* What can manuscript and citation evidence show about one Purāṇic text's development?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish layers, recensions, and much later printed editions. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
2. **Puranas: A Familiar Purāṇic Story**
   - *Question:* How does one famous narrative differ across named sources?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare passages, versions, and local retellings with attribution. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
3. **Puranas: Local Custodians of a Purāṇic Narrative**
   - *Question:* Whose telling or performance is absent from a standard textual account?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented community accounts and dated records. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
4. **Puranas: What Is a Purāṇa?**
   - *Question:* How does one named Purāṇa combine narrative, genealogy, place, and practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Show its particular structure without imposing one template on all texts. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
5. **Puranas: Textual Layers and Recensions**
   - *Question:* How can researchers identify changes within a named Purāṇa?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare manuscripts, citations, language, and regional editions. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Puranas: A Passage of Uncertain Origin**
   - *Question:* Why do witnesses disagree over one Purāṇic section?
   - *Key Focus:* Assess provenance, incorporation, and editorial uncertainty. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
7. **Puranas: Digitizing Purāṇic Witnesses**
   - *Question:* Can a searchable corpus clarify where one story appears?
   - *Key Focus:* Check transcription, manuscript base, omissions, and search limits. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
8. **Puranas: A Purāṇic Figure Across Versions**
   - *Question:* How is one figure portrayed differently in two named texts?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare narrative function and later devotional reception. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
9. **Puranas: A Text Enters a Regional Tradition**
   - *Question:* What documented change made one Purāṇa important in a place?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated manuscripts, patronage, performance, and local voices. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
10. **Puranas: Following a Purāṇic Narrative**
   - *Question:* How is one story framed, told, and interpreted within its source?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify narrator, audience, surrounding chapters, and edition. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Puranas: A Text's Changing Circulation**
   - *Question:* Why did one recension become more visible than another?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare copying, printing, translation, and institutional use. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
12. **Puranas: One Story in Text and Living Practice**
   - *Question:* How does a community tell a Purāṇic story today?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish textual passage from local adaptation and meaning. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
13. **Puranas: A Manuscript Research Question**
   - *Question:* Which missing witness could clarify a contested Purāṇic passage?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify needed source evidence without predicting discovery. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
14. **Puranas: Narrative and Historical Evidence**
   - *Question:* How should a Purāṇic account be presented beside independent historical records?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute devotional narrative and date external evidence. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
15. **Puranas: A Story's Version History**
   - *Question:* How did one Purāṇic episode travel through copies, translations, and performances?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace named witnesses, editions, and community adaptations. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Puranas: Finding a Purāṇic Passage**
   - *Question:* Which text, recension, chapter, and edition contain a claimed story?
   - *Key Focus:* Check source numbering and variant locations. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
17. **Puranas: Different Readings of One Story**
   - *Question:* How do two named lineages interpret the same narrative?
   - *Key Focus:* Represent each reading in its textual and practice context. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
18. **Puranas: A Purāṇa in Community Life**
   - *Question:* How is one text read, heard, or performed in a named community?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and identify when and where. Evidence base: specified manuscript witnesses and editions, citations, translations, performance records, art and site archives, and consented practitioner accounts.
19. **Puranas: A Story in an Observance**
