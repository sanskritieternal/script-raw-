#### BANK-127-042 — Why do some blur, glow or transparency operations look different when computed in linear light?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare encoded display values, scene-referred values, compositing operations and view transforms on a controlled image; separate calculation space from final display appearance.

#### BANK-127-043 — How does a particle system create a repeatable effect without simulating every physical detail?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace emission, lifetime, forces, seeds, collision approximations and artistic controls in a benign dust or sparkle effect; distinguish controlled appearance from validated physical prediction.

#### BANK-127-044 — Why can a simulated stack of rigid objects behave differently when solver settings change?
