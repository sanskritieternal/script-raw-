# Coral-reef case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected dated primary records anchor limited questions. These notes do not establish production readiness.

| Case | Primary record and inspection | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Fourth global bleaching event | [NOAA Coral Reef Watch update, 2 June 2026](https://www.coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/satellite/research/coral_bleaching_report.php) says the event likely ended in 2025, reports satellite bleaching-level heat stress over about 84.4% of reef area and field-documented mass bleaching in at least 83 countries and territories. | Heat stress is not a count of dead coral; figures and event classification are dated and subject to NOAA revision. The archive's “50 nations” is stale. |
| Hawaii sunscreen law | [Hawaii Act 104 (2018)](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/sessionlaws/Years/SLH2018/SLH2018_Act104.pdf) restricts sale, offer and distribution of products with oxybenzone or octinoxate from 1 January 2021, subject to its exception. | This is a Hawaii sales rule, not a universal use ban or proof that sunscreen caused a particular bleaching event. Palau requires a separate legal record. |
| Hong Kong tiles | [Hong Kong Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department commissioned Hoi Ha study](https://bih.gov.hk/en/feature-studies/index-id-14.html) describes 3D-printed terracotta reef tiles. | Project description is not proof of durable reef recovery, and does not establish Caribbean outcomes. |
| Gulf of Aqaba | [Grottoli and colleagues 2017 original thermal-stress experiment](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2017.00215/full) studied northern Gulf of Aqaba corals. | Sampled species and experimental exposure cannot justify “never bleach” or generalize to all Red Sea reefs. |

The linked pages were accessible on 2026-10-24. Long-term restoration outcomes and complete legal comparisons remain unverified.
