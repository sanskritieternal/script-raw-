#### BANK-199-010 — The Rainforest of the Sea: How Tiny Polyps Build Living Structures Visible from Space

**Video question:** How do soft, centimeter-sized colonial animals build 1,400-mile long limestone ramparts that support 25% of all marine species?

**Individual coverage scope:** Cnidarian colonial morphology, shared gastrovascular networks connecting thousands of polyps, symbiotic energy transfer, and why coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor yet harbor a quarter of all marine life.

**Video question:** What mutualistic biochemical contract allows plant-like microalgae living inside animal cells to trade glucose for shelter and nitrogen?

**Individual coverage scope:** Symbiodiniaceae dinoflagellates residing within coral gastrodermal cells, transferring up to 90% of photosynthetic sugars to the host, coral waste recycling (ammonia and CO2), and vibrant coral pigments.
