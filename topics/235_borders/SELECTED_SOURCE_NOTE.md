# Selected primary-source checks - Borders
Checked 2026-10-24. These are bounded historical and institutional questions, not legal advice or current crossing instructions.

- BANK-235-039: US-Mexico International Boundary and Water Commission text of the Chamizal Convention of 29 August 1963, https://www.ibwc.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ChamizalConvention1963.pdf , and IBWC treaty index, https://www.ibwc.gov/treaties/ . The convention relocated a river channel and treated its centerline as the international boundary for the defined tract. Its 1963 land and channel provisions do not state current visitor crossing rules or describe every river-border segment.
- BANK-235-040: International Court of Justice 16 April 2013 Burkina Faso/Niger frontier judgment, https://www.icj-cij.org/node/103272 . The Court discusses the 1927 colonial instrument, erratum and the role and limits of a 1960 IGN map for the submitted segment. This judicial holding is bounded to the parties, instruments and segment, not a universal rule for disputed borders or a statement of present local access. Read the judgment and map references, not only its headline.
- BANK-235-041: UK Windsor Framework Retail Movement Scheme Regulations 2023, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/896/made , and Northern Ireland Assembly institutional explanation, https://www.niassembly.gov.uk/assembly-business/brexit-and-beyond/the-windsor-framework/ . These describe specified goods movements and simplified procedures under conditions. The 2023 as-made text may have been amended; verify current consolidated law and EU/UK implementation before any trade guidance. Do not equate a goods regulatory boundary with a personal passport border.
- Overlap: BANK-038-057 already covers residents of India-Bangladesh enclave exchange; BANK-239-033 covers enclave-map change; BANK-218-078 covers atlas depictions of disputed lines. New cases here focus on a specific river treaty, a court-evidence trail and a particular goods regulatory scheme. No attributed resident or negotiator voice was invented.
