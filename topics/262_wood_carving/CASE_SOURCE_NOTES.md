# Selected case-source note — wood carving

Inspected 2026-10-24. This is a bounded premise check for `BANK-262-042`, not full episode verification.

It says no documented mention survives from Gibbons' lifetime and frames demonstration for prospective clients as likely, not established.
- [V&A, Grinling Gibbons collections page](https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections/grinling-gibbons): collection context for the cravat and Gibbons' work in multiple media. A museum attribution and explanatory reconstruction do not identify every assisting hand or prove original use.

The V&A pages were readable as institutional object evidence. Their approximate dating and inferred purpose require more archival and conservation checking before a script. The case concerns wood carving and material illusion; stone carving in niche 261 and general sculpture in niche 260 retain their separate questions.
