# Selected manufacturing source checks — 24 October 2026

These checks bound two proposed production cases and one routed claim. Producer records can document a producer’s own process and dates but do not establish every factory’s practice, third-party performance or secret yield data. Other archived technical and safety claims require process-specific research.

## BANK-088-063 — TSMC N2

- [TSMC’s 2025 annual report](https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2025/english/index.html) says its N2 technology entered high-volume manufacturing in the fourth quarter of 2025, with a faster ramp expected in 2026. This is a dated company disclosure, not an independent comparison of all semiconductor factories.
- [TSMC’s N2 technology page](https://www.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/logic/l_2nm) describes its first-generation nanosheet transistor technology and production status. The “2nm” designation is a process-node name; it should not be narrated as a universal measured transistor dimension.
- **Editorial bound:** Date production claims and distinguish N2 from later N2P/A16 variants, wafer fabrication from advanced packaging, and published process features from confidential yield or customer details.

## BANK-088-064 — Chicken Ramen

- [Nissin’s corporate history](https://www.nissin.com/en_jp/about/history/) records the 25 August 1958 launch of *Chicken Ramen*. [Nissin’s founder account](https://www.nissin.com/en_jp/about/founder/) attributes the shelf-stable instant-noodle method to Ando’s flash-frying discovery.
- [Nissin’s earlier annual-report account](https://media.www.nissin.com/jp/company/ir/library/annual/pdf/anr_1803_01.pdf) says Ando experimented for about a year. This duration is the company’s retrospective account, not an independently timed laboratory record.
- **Editorial bound:** Distinguish invention history from a current factory route; no consumer deep-frying instructions or universal claim that every instant noodle is fried.

## Routed LEGO pitch — BANK-051-064

- [The LEGO Group’s 2025 annual report](https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/aboutus/assets/blt94a9daa1bc646283/2025_Annual_Report_-_The_LEGO_Group.pdf) identifies precision moulding, later processing and packing as separate production stages. Its [published Fair Play sheet](https://www.lego.com/cdn/cs/legal/assets/blt1a4c9a959ce8e1cb/LEGO_Fairplay_Nov2018.pdf?pubDate=20250703) gives a 0.004 mm moulding accuracy claim, whereas the archived pitch asserts 0.002 mm. These are company statements in different contexts, so a tolerance specification needs definition of measured feature, date and instrument.
- **Editorial bound:** The existing industrial-manufacturing proposal already covers LEGO brick dimensions across tools and runs; do not add a duplicate active case or repeat the archived figure as verified.
