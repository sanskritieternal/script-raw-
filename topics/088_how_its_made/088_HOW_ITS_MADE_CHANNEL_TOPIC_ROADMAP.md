## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of How It's Made across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: High-Volume Consumer Goods & Packaging Metallurgy (Scratch / Beginner)** — Aluminum beverage cans (cupping, ironing, double-seaming), corrugated cardboard fluting, glass bottle blow-and-blow molding, and injection molded plastic Lego bricks.
- **Tier 2: Heavy Industry: Steel, Float Glass & Continuous Processing (Intermediate)** — Blast furnaces and electric arc steel forging, Pilkington float glass bath on molten tin, paper making Fourdrinier machines, and tire vulcanization with sulfur and carbon black.
- **Tier 3: High-Precision Components: Bearings, Microchips & Carbon Fiber (Upper Intermediate)** — Silicon ingot Czochralski crystal pulling and photolithography, precision steel ball bearing centerless grinding, carbon fiber pre-preg autoclaving, and optical lens grinding and anti-reflective coating.
- **Tier 4: Ultra-Cleanroom Manufacturing, Aerospace Alloys & Modern Mega-Factories (Advanced / Expert)** — Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) semiconductor wafer fabrication, single-crystal nickel turbine blade investment casting, automated robotic car assembly lines, and lithium-ion battery cell winding and electrolyte filling.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Consumer Goods & Packaging Metallurgy
1. **The Aluminum Can: The World's Most Engineered Mass-Produced Object**
   - *Question:* How do factories stamp and stretch a flat disc of aluminum into an ultra-thin 0.08mm walled pressure vessel capable of holding 90 PSI at 2,000 cans per minute?
   - *Key Focus:* Cupping press stamping, draw-and-ironing (D&I) thinning the walls while keeping the base thick, the arch-dome bottom resisting internal carbonation pressure, internal water-based polymer spray lining, and the double-seam lid crimp.
2. **How Glass Bottles Are Made: Molten Gobs, Blow-and-Blow Molds, and Annealing Lehrs**
   - *Question:* How do industrial glass factories shear molten 1,500°C orange glass gobs and blow them into beer bottles in under 3 seconds without exploding from thermal shock?
   - *Key Focus:* Raw materials (silica sand, soda ash, limestone, cullet recycled glass), the Individual Section (IS) machine, blank mold parison formation, blow-and-blow vs. press-and-blow shaping, and the 60-meter annealing lehr slowly cooling glass to eliminate internal stress.
3. **How Corrugated Cardboard Is Made: Fluting Rolls, Starch Glue, and Crush Resistance**
   - *Question:* How does gluing a wavy piece of recycled paper between two flat sheets of Kraft paper create a lightweight packaging box that can support 1,000 lbs of weight?
   - *Key Focus:* The corrugator machine running at 1,000 feet per minute, heated fluting rolls forming architectural arches, cornstarch adhesive gelatinization, Edge Crush Test (ECT) ratings, and rotary die-cutting.
4. **How Lego Bricks Are Made: Extreme Injection Molding and 0.002mm Tolerances**
   - *Question:* Why does a Lego brick molded in 1958 snap perfectly onto a brick manufactured today with zero wobble, zero glue, and unmatched 'clutch power'?
   - *Key Focus:* Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS) polymer granules, automated robotic molding halls in Billund, multi-million dollar hardened steel molds with 2-micron tolerances, and only 18 defective bricks per million produced.
5. **How Ballpoint Pens Are Made: Tungsten Carbide Micro-Spheres and Thixotropic Ink**
   - *Question:* How does Bic manufacture a 1-millimeter tungsten carbide ball ground smoother than a mirror that can roll out 2 miles of continuous ink without leaking or jamming?
   - *Key Focus:* Tungsten carbide sintered ball grinding and diamond polishing, brass tip extrusion and internal fluid channels, thixotropic ink chemistry (viscous at rest, liquid under rolling shear), and vacuum centrifuging.
### Tier 2: Heavy Industry & Continuous Processing
6. **How Steel Is Made: Blast Furnaces, Basic Oxygen Converters, and Continuous Casting**
   - *Question:* How do steel mills take red iron ore dirt and coal coke and transform them into thousands of tons of high-strength structural steel I-beams?
   - *Key Focus:* Blast furnace reduction of iron oxide, pig iron tapped at 1,500°C, Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF) supersonic oxygen lances blowing out excess carbon, continuous strand casting into slabs, and hot rolling mills.
7. **How Float Glass Is Made: The Pilkington Tin Bath That Flattened the Modern City**
   - *Question:* How did Alastair Pilkington's 1952 breakthrough of floating liquid glass across a pool of molten tin eliminate grinding and polishing to give every skyscraper optical clarity?
   - *Key Focus:* The continuous float glass ribbon, specific gravity mechanics (glass floating on heavier liquid tin), edge knurling top rollers controlling glass thickness from 2mm to 25mm, protective nitrogen-hydrogen atmosphere, and laser cutting.
8. **How Paper Is Made: Pine Forests to Pulp Slurry on Giant Fourdrinier Machines**
   - *Question:* How does a modern paper mill turn whole tree logs into a 99% water pulp slurry and dry it into rolls of printer paper moving at 60 miles per hour?
   - *Key Focus:* Debarking and wood chipping, Kraft chemical pulping with white liquor digesting lignin, bleaching, the Fourdrinier wire mesh draining water by gravity and vacuum, steam-heated drying cylinders, and calendar smoothing rolls.
9. **How Car Tires Are Made: Vulcanization, Steel Belts, and 30 Ingredients in One Dough**
   - *Question:* Why is an automotive tire made from 30 different rubber compounds, nylon plies, and brass-plated steel cords rather than a single piece of molded black rubber?
   - *Key Focus:* The Banbury internal mixer compounding natural rubber, synthetic polybutadiene, carbon black, and silica; building the multi-layered 'green tire' on a drum; and curing presses at 170°C where sulfur bridges cross-link polymers (vulcanization).
10. **How Mirrors Are Made: From Silver Nitrate Chemical Baths to Magnetron Sputtering**
   - *Question:* How do factories coat high-speed glass sheets with an atomic layer of pure liquid silver in 10 seconds without leaving a single streak or cloud?
   - *Key Focus:* Cerium oxide glass polishing, tin(II) chloride sensitizer preparing the glass surface, twin-head spray reducing silver nitrate with sugar/formaldehyde, protective copper backing layer, and dual-coat waterproof baking paints.
### Tier 3: Precision Components & Micro-Fabrication
11. **How Ball Bearings Are Made: Wire Forging to Grade 3 Micro-Inch Spheres**
   - *Question:* How do you manufacture millions of chromium steel balls that are perfectly spherical to within 3 millionths of an inch (0.000075 mm)?
   - *Key Focus:* Cold heading wire slugs between hemispherical dies, flash grinding equatorial bands off, heat-treat quenching and tempering, rill grinding between grooved cast iron plates, and final lapping paste polishing.
12. **How Carbon Fiber Is Made: From Acrylic Plastic Threads to Aerospace Super-Weaves**
   - *Question:* How does baking ordinary yellow sweater plastic (PAN) in 3,000°C oxygen-free ovens drive off every atom except pure graphite carbon lattices stronger than steel?
   - *Key Focus:* Polyacrylonitrile (PAN) precursor filament spinning, thermal stabilization at 300°C, high-temperature carbonization in pure nitrogen driving off hydrogen and nitrogen, surface sizing treatment, and weaving tows into carbon cloth.
13. **How Optical Lenses Are Made: Diamond Curve Generating, Pitch Polishing, and PVD Coatings**
   - *Question:* How does a cinema lens manufacturer grind exotic crown and flint glass blanks to nanometer surface tolerances without a single wave aberration?
   - *Key Focus:* Abbe number and chromatic dispersion, CNC diamond curve generating, pitch polishing with cerium oxide slurry, laser interferometric curvature verification, and vacuum Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) multi-layer anti-reflective coatings.
14. **How Copper Pipes Are Made: Piercing Solid Billet Plugs to Seamless Drawn Tubing**
   - *Question:* How does a rolling mill pierce a hole straight through the center of a red-hot solid copper bar without drilling or cutting any metal away?
   - *Key Focus:* The Mannesmann cross-roll piercing mill creating internal tensile tearing at the core, floating mandrels smoothing the bore, cold drawing through progressive tungsten carbide dies, and bright annealing in vacuum furnaces.
15. **How Wire Rope and Cables Are Made: Stranding Millions of Feet of Steel into Golden Gate Cables**
   - *Question:* How do spinning planetary stranding machines twist hundreds of individual high-tensile steel wires together around a lubricated core without breaking or untwisting?
   - *Key Focus:* High-carbon steel wire patenting, cold drawing through diamond dies, planetary stranding bobbins rotating in epicyclic motion, regular lay vs. Lang lay flexibility, and aerial cable spinning for suspension bridges.
### Tier 4: Ultra-Cleanrooms & Aerospace Mega-Factories
16. **How Microchips Are Made: Silicon Ingot Pulling to 3-Nanometer Photolithography**
   - *Question:* How does TSMC grow a 99.9999999% pure electronic-grade single-crystal silicon boule and etch 50 billion transistors into a chip using Extreme Ultraviolet light?
   - *Key Focus:* The Czochralski crystal pulling furnace, diamond wire wafer slicing, Class 1 cleanroom filtration, photoresist spin coating, ASML Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) photolithography at 13.5nm wavelength, chemical vapor deposition, and copper chemical-mechanical polishing (CMP).
17. **How Jet Engine Turbine Blades Are Made: Single-Crystal Nickel Investment Casting**
   - *Question:* How do foundry engineers grow an entire jet engine turbine blade out of a single continuous metallic crystal with zero grain boundaries to withstand 1,700°C centrifugal loads?
   - *Key Focus:* Lost wax investment casting, ceramic shell molds, directional solidification in vacuum induction furnaces, spiral grain selector pigtails filtering out all crystal orientations except one, and micro-EDM laser-drilled cooling holes.
18. **How Lithium-Ion Batteries Are Made: Slurry Coating, Calendering, and Automated Jelly Rolls**
   - *Question:* How do Tesla and CATL gigafactories coat foil moving at 100 meters per minute with microscopic cathode slurries, dry it without pinholes, and roll it into cylinders?
   - *Key Focus:* NMC/LFP cathode and graphite anode chemical mixing, precision slot-die coating onto aluminum and copper foils, massive heated calendering roll presses compacting electrode density, automated cylindrical jelly-roll winding, and dry-room electrolyte injection.
19. **How Automated Robotic Car Assembly Lines Work: Stamping to The Sacred 'Marriage'**
   - *Question:* How do 1,000 orange industrial KUKA robots weld 5,000 sheet metal stampings into a rigid car body every 60 seconds with sub-millimeter repeatable precision?
   - *Key Focus:* Transfer press tandem stamping lines, Body-in-White (BIW) laser and spot welding cells, cathodic dip painting (E-coat) rust immersion, the 'marriage' station where the pre-assembled chassis and engine bolt into the painted unibody, and end-of-line dyno inspection.
20. **The Future of Manufacturing: Metal 3D Printing, Lights-Out Factories, and AI Inspection**
   - *Question:* What happens when dark, unheated 'lights-out' factories run 24/7 without a single human on the floor, using lasers to weld titanium rocket parts from digital CAD models?
   - *Key Focus:* Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS) powder bed fusion, topology optimization generating organic bionic structures impossible to machine or cast, machine vision neural networks catching micro-flaws at conveyor speeds, and localized digital fabrication.
