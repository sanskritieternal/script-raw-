## Bounded premise

NPS describes the Unigrid as a shared design and production system developed in the late 1970s. Standardized folds, grid and typography supported recognizable park publications while the program varied maps, images and page organization to fit each site. The named first brochure and later examples permit a concrete comparison of consistent structure with distinct content.

## Rights, access and inference limits

These NPS records are program self-description, not a controlled study of comprehension, visit behavior or accessibility. Do not claim that a grid caused better reader outcomes without user research. Some park brochure components may contain third-party maps, photographs, typography or credited artwork; check asset-level reuse rights before reproducing pages. The shared print brochure does not satisfy every reader's access need; describe Braille/audio/large-print availability by dated source and individual park, not as a universal service. Avoid treating a 1978 design or 1998 handbook as unchanged current specification.
