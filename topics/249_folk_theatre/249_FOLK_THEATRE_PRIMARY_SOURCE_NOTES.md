# Selected institutional source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-249-041: Ramlila

- UNESCO intangible heritage element page, India, inscribed 2008 (original proclamation 2005): https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ramlila-the-traditional-performance-of-the-ramayana-00110
- UNESCO describes scenes with song, narration, dialogue and audience participation during Dussehra, with localities and performance durations varying. The page does not document every local production or guarantee a fixed ancient script. Select a named troupe/locality/year, obtain stage and performer consent, and compare a versioned recording or program before scripting. Avoid treating caste participation claims in a generalized page as the current situation everywhere.

## BANK-249-042: Ramman

- UNESCO intangible heritage element page, 2009, with linked nomination and community-consent materials: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ramman-religious-festival-and-ritual-theatre-of-the-garhwal-himalayas-india-00281
- The record identifies Saloor-Dungra, masked dances, songs, ritual and differentiated village roles. Its 2009 inscription account is not a live roster, permission for filming, or proof that roles have not changed. Consult local custodians and participants for contemporary descriptions and consent, especially for sacred masks and ritual access.
