# 074 selected source inspection — 2026-10-24

These are bounded research leads for proposed travel decisions. Prices, access, schedules, entry rules and warnings change; recheck the official source for the travel date, passport and itinerary before production or booking.

## Flight search and fare terms — BANK-074-049 and BANK-074-056

[Google Flights' price-tracking help](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/6235879?hl=en) documents alerts for specified flights, routes and dates; it does not support a guaranteed 70% saving. [American Airlines' current conditions of carriage](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/customer-service/support/conditions-of-carriage.html) identifies hidden-city ticketing as purchasing without intending to fly all segments and lists possible ticket consequences. The rule is carrier-specific. Compare total fare, bags, connection risk and the actual contract before portraying a lower displayed fare as a usable ticket.

## California Zephyr — BANK-074-050

Amtrak's [timetable dated 11 April 2026](https://content.amtrak.com/content/timetable/California%20Zephyr.pdf) lists daily service and a journey of roughly 52–53 hours, depending on direction. This is a specific timetable snapshot; service, connections, accommodation and price must be checked again for a proposed departure. The archived pitch conflates this route with the Trans-Siberian; do not treat them as one six-day journey.

## Croatia remote-work stay — BANK-074-052

Croatia's [Ministry of Interior digital-nomad stay page](https://mup.gov.hr/aliens-281621/stay-and-work-281644/temporary-stay-of-digital-nomads-286833/286833) defines eligible third-country remote work and states conditions and possible duration. This is a Croatian immigration category, not a worldwide digital-nomad visa. A travel episode should identify passport, employer or self-employment structure, application date and separately check tax and social-security questions with current authorities.

## Annapurna and emergency communications — BANK-074-053 and BANK-074-054

Nepal's [Department of Immigration trekking-route and permit page](https://immigration.gov.np/trekking-route-and-permit-fee) shows that restricted-area permit rules depend on location; it is not a blanket Annapurna Circuit permit answer. The [U.S. State Department's Nepal advisory dated 4 September 2026](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/nepal.html) flags disaster and altitude risks for U.S. travelers. [Garmin's inReach service terms](https://subscriptions.garmin.com/en-MY/legal/TC_GARMIN_SUBSCRIPTION_SERVICES_INREACH_PERSONAL) require active service and describe satellite line-of-sight limits. An SOS request does not guarantee immediate rescue or replace acclimatization, local emergency planning or current route checks.

## Venice access fee — BANK-074-055

The [official Venice access-fee portal](https://cda.veneziaunica.it/en/choose-the-date/) says the 2026 application period has ended. The [Municipality's 2026 announcement](https://live.comune.venezia.it/it/node/150848) described a €5 early price and €10 later price during the applicable dates. Therefore the archive's perpetual “$5 entry fee” is wrong as a current blanket statement. Verify dates, exemptions, currency, amount and whether a new municipal measure applies for the visit year.

## ATM conversion — BANK-074-057

[Visa's dynamic-currency-conversion guidance](https://www.visa.com/en-gb/personal/travel/dynamic-currency-conversion) explains the offered home-currency conversion and the user's choice. [Visa's travel-card guide](https://www.visa.com/en-us/personal/travel/travel-cards-and-foreign-transactions) notes ATM and issuer fees can still apply. Declining DCC may avoid one conversion markup, but does not make withdrawals fee-free. Compare the shown rate and both fee schedules.
