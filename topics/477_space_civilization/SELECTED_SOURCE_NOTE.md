# Selected source note — ISS water recovery demonstration

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-477-044. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA or self-sustaining settlement claim.

## Primary agency records

- NASA, *NASA Achieves Water Recovery Milestone on International Space Station* (June 20, 2023): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/nasa-achieves-water-recovery-milestone-on-international-space-station/ . NASA reports a demonstration of 98% total water recovery with the Brine Processor Assembly added to the U.S. segment Environmental Control and Life Support System. It distinguishes the earlier 93–94% recovery, urine processing, brine processing, atmospheric moisture capture and water purification.
- NASA, *NASA's 2023 Space Station Achievements*: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/nasas-2023-space-station-achievements/ . It describes the 98% water-recovery milestone as a test relevant to distant missions.
- NASA Technical Reports Server, 2023 water recovery paper: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230008504/downloads/ICES-2023-312%20Final.pdf . This technical discussion describes processor operation and maintenance issues; consult its exact test conditions before any sustained-rate claim.

## Flight versus settlement limits

This was a subsystem demonstration aboard an operating, Earth-supported research station. The 98% figure concerns collected water in the tested U.S. segment chain and should not be stated as 98% recycling of every material or proof of complete life-support closure. It does not establish independence from resupply, failure-free operation, permanent habitation or local manufacturing. NASA's public summary and a technical paper have different measurement contexts; retain dates and denominators. Paraphrase NASA material and check image rights.
