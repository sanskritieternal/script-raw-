# Selected source and boundary note — 444 Trivia

Reviewed 2026-10-24. Proposed **BANK-444-043** audits a specific recurring trivia claim: “the Great Wall of China is visible from the Moon.”

## Primary agency records

- NASA, [Great Wall image article](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/great-wall/), identifies an ISS photograph by Expedition 10 Commander Leroy Chiao and says the wall is **not visible from the Moon** and is difficult or impossible to see unaided from Earth orbit; the cited ISS photo used a high-powered lens.
- NASA Johnson Space Center, [Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit FAQ](https://esrs.jsc.nasa.gov/FAQ/), distinguishes naked-eye orbit visibility from recent high-resolution astronaut photographs. It also states the wall is not visible from the Moon. NASA's [2005 explainer archived as a PDF](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/PDFs/great_wall_less_great_from_space.pdf) gives more historical context but is still an agency account.

## Clue repair, rights and overlap

A fair item must specify viewpoint (lunar surface versus low Earth orbit), unaided eye versus optical/camera assistance, and whether “visible” means detected in processed imagery. “Only human-made object” adds a separate false exclusivity claim. Do not replace one vague true/false answer with another. NASA images have mission/image-specific credits and reuse terms. This is a trivia-source and clue-wording case rather than a study of the Wall's architectural history or optics. **BANK-443-046** is the UN-membership counting example in general knowledge; **BANK-444-034** handles stale trivia answers broadly.
