## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Trivia across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Trivia Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Trivia.
- **Tier 2: Trivia Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Trivia.
- **Tier 3: Trivia Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Trivia.
- **Tier 4: Trivia Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Trivia.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

   - *Key Focus:* Trace original wording and answer changes. Evidence base: dated primary references, question drafts, answer-key reviews, player response samples, corrections, and accessible-format tests.
2. **Trivia: A Classic Question Audited**
   - *Question:* Is its familiar answer uniquely defensible?
   - *Key Focus:* Define period, region, and category. Evidence base: dated primary references, question drafts, answer-key reviews, player response samples, corrections, and accessible-format tests.
3. **Trivia: Whose Knowledge Is Called Trivial?**
   - *Question:* Which traditions or communities are missing from common trivia sets?
   - *Key Focus:* Audit source and topic distribution. Evidence base: dated primary references, question drafts, answer-key reviews, player response samples, corrections, and accessible-format tests.
4. **Trivia: Building a Fair Trivia Question**

5. **Trivia: Ambiguity in a Superlative**
