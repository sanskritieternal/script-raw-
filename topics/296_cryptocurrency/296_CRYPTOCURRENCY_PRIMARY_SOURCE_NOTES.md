## BANK-296-043: USDC and Silicon Valley Bank, March 2023

- Circle, [$3.3 Billion of USDC Reserve Risk Removed, Dollar De-peg Closes](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/3-3-billion-of-usdc-reserve-risk-removed-dollar-de-peg-closes), dated 12-13 March 2023, inspected 2026-10-24. The issuer said $3.3 billion of USDC reserves, about 8% then, had been deposited at SVB and described expected access and redemption arrangements.
- FDIC/Treasury/Federal Reserve, [joint statement of 12 March 2023](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23017.html), inspected 2026-10-24, announced depositor access under the U.S. systemic risk exception from 13 March. This is a U.S. bank-resolution record, not a stablecoin guarantee.
- Limit: Circle's statement is an issuer account, not an independent reserve audit or complete market-price series. The federal announcement concerns depositors of the failed bank, not a standing insurance promise to token holders. Do not generalize the March 2023 outcome to later USDC reserves, other stablecoins, or all jurisdictions. Check contemporaneous trading and final bank records before claiming a precise depeg path.

## BANK-296-044: U.S. spot Bitcoin ETP listing order, January 2024

- U.S. SEC, [Exchange Act release 34-99306](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nysearca/2024/34-99306.pdf), 10 January 2024, identified in the SEC's [rulemaking index](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/national-securities-exchanges/all-years?file_number=&order=field_publish_date&page=33&release_number=&sort=desc&sro_organization=192811); inspected 2026-10-24. It approved specified U.S. exchange rule changes for listing and trading shares of spot Bitcoin ETPs.
- SEC Chair's [contemporaneous statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023), inspected 2026-10-24, explicitly distinguishes approval of the products' listing and trading from approval or endorsement of Bitcoin itself.
- Limit: order scope is the specified January 2024 U.S. ETP filings. It does not certify Bitcoin's safety, a token price, every custodian, investor outcome, or regulatory status in another country or later date. The chair's statement explains but is not a substitute for the order; review the order's detailed conditions before an episode.
