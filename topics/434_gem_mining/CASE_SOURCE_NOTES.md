# Selected primary-source note — gem mining

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-434-043`.

- [GIA *Gems & Gemology*, Renfro, Palke and Berg, “Gemological Characterization of Sapphires from Yogo Gulch, Montana” (Summer 2018)](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/summer-2018-gemological-characterization-of-sapphires-from-yogo-gulch-montana): original gemological study with field observations, inclusions and trace-element analyses of Yogo reference material. It identifies a sapphire-bearing lamprophyre dike and explains why combinations of inclusion and chemistry features can support geographic-origin comparison. The paper notes some individual spectral features are shared with other Montana deposits and that its worldwide comparison sample is limited.

The studied reference suite does not create an unbroken chain of custody for any unexamined retail stone, verify a seller's mine-to-market story, guarantee treatment status or prove labor/environmental claims. Historical production figures in the article cite earlier literature rather than a new independent audit. Published figures and photographs have GIA or credited third-party rights; check reuse permission. Mine work and gem testing require appropriate professional controls; this note is not a sorting or excavation guide.
