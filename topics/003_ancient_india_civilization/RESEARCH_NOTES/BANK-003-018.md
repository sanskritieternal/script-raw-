## Editorial decisions and open checks

- The preserved local queue item asks about rock-cut reservoirs and also mentions a giant signboard. Link the water part to this proposal and the signboard's writing question to the Indus-script proposal. The queue entry is archival wording, not a second active BANK topic.
- The queue's “16 massive reservoirs” and “5,000-year-old” phrases are not established by the inspected source passages as exact episode claims. Before using either number, identify what was counted or dated, from which site stage and publication.
- The inspected ASI nomination site plan names Castle tanks C3/C4 and South Reservoir features SR1–SR5, but those labels cannot be summed into the queue's “16” figure. It includes a legend typo for the fifth SR feature and does not date each feature. A script can use the plan's relative positions with attribution while keeping count and construction sequence open.
- The ICOMOS account places major reservoir construction within its Stage III description and notes later damage and unrepaired water structures in Stage V. A future video should attribute this sequence to the evaluation and check it against excavation plans; do not imply that every reservoir belongs to one construction phase.
- Open: inspect the ASI excavation review's full pages and drawings for measurements and phases. Its NMMA PDF timed out during this follow-up, so search-visible excerpts were not treated as inspected pages. Compare conservation interventions and an alternative archaeological interpretation if a causal claim about settlement decline is proposed.
