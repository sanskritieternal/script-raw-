## The hard problem and Mary's room — BANK-054-014 and BANK-054-049

In [Chalmers's 1995 paper](https://consc.net/papers/facing.html), the hard problem asks why physical and functional processes are accompanied by experience. That is the author's philosophical framing, not a finding that science can never explain experience. [Jackson's 1982 *Epiphenomenal Qualia*](https://doi.org/10.2307/2960077) presents Mary's room as a knowledge argument with stipulated complete physical information. The episode should state the stipulation, what conclusion follows if it is granted, and at least one physicalist response. It should not present Mary as a real experiment.

## Libet timing — BANK-054-051

The [original 1983 Libet and colleagues study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6640273/) measured readiness potentials and reported timing of conscious intention for simple voluntary movements. [Schurger, Sitt and Dehaene's 2012 model](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869750/) provides a competing interpretation of the averaged pre-movement signal. Neither study measures all deliberative decisions or proves that free will is an illusion. Specify movement task, timing method and inference before linking results to agency.

## Rubber-hand illusion — BANK-054-052

The [original Botvinick and Cohen report](https://www.nature.com/articles/35784) describes touch felt as if referred to an artificial limb under coordinated visual and tactile stimulation. It supports a test of body ownership under those conditions; the archive's fixed “60 seconds” and claim that the brain adopts a limb in general require study-specific measurement. An ownership illusion does not by itself establish a metaphysical theory of self.

## Integrated Information Theory — BANK-054-053

[Oizumi, Albantakis and Tononi's IIT 3.0 paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588) develops axioms and postulates linking phenomenology to proposed mechanisms. Phi is a theoretical measure within that model. The episode should distinguish a formal proposal and its predictions from a validated universal consciousness meter, and compare how alternative theories would interpret the same experiment.
