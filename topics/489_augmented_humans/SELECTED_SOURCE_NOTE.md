# Selected source note — FDA ReWalk De Novo review

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-489-043. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA, clinical recommendation or current-device endorsement.

## Primary regulatory records

- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, De Novo DEN130034 device record, decision 2014-06-26: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm?ID=DEN130034 . FDA lists ReWalk as a powered exoskeleton and records the decision date and classification.
- FDA De Novo review summary, K131798/DEN130034: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/den130034.pdf . It states indications for adults with specified spinal-cord injury levels: T7–L5 ambulatory functions with a specially trained companion; T4–T6 in rehabilitation institutions under training and assessment. It says the device is not intended for sports or stair climbing and specifies candidate, crutch, surface, fitting, training and battery constraints.

## Medical, technology and access limits

The FDA record establishes a 2014 U.S. device-specific regulatory decision, not restored unassisted walking, strength beyond ordinary ability, universal suitability, or long-term quality-of-life benefit. It is not personal medical advice; user evaluation and qualified training are part of the reviewed use. Later ReWalk versions and indications may differ, so date the device/model and recheck current labeling. Reported engineering figures are not individual outcomes. Paraphrase FDA review and check permissions for photos or schematics.
