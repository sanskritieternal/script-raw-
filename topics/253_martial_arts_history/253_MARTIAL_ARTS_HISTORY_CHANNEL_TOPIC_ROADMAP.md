## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Martial Arts History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Martial Arts History Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Martial Arts History.
- **Tier 2: Martial Arts History Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Martial Arts History.
- **Tier 3: Martial Arts History Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Martial Arts History.
- **Tier 4: Martial Arts History Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Martial Arts History.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Martial Arts History: An Art's Earliest Evidence**
   - *Question:* What dated records describe one named practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish techniques, school, and later origin story. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
2. **Martial Arts History: A Famous Demonstration Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What can a documented performance actually show?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare purpose, rules, editing, and practitioner context. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
3. **Martial Arts History: Teachers Beyond the Founder Myth**
   - *Question:* Who transmitted a school's methods?
   - *Key Focus:* Use lineages, archives, and consented practitioner accounts. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
4. **Martial Arts History: History of One Training System**
   - *Question:* What are its named methods and institutional setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Define place, period, sources, and current variants. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
5. **Martial Arts History: Lineage and Historical Proof**
   - *Question:* What does a claimed lineage establish or leave uncertain?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare documents, oral testimony, dates, and breaks. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Martial Arts History: A Contested Founding Tale**
   - *Question:* Which rival accounts survive for one school?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace earliest versions and missing evidence. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
7. **Martial Arts History: Weapons and Protective Gear**
   - *Question:* How did one material change training?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare design, rule changes, access, and documented use. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
8. **Martial Arts History: A Teacher's Actual Influence**
   - *Question:* Which students, texts, or institutions carried one person's methods?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate documented transmission from later legend. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
9. **Martial Arts History: A Reform That Changed Training**
   - *Question:* How did one ban, school policy, or tournament alter practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated rules and practitioner accounts. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
10. **Martial Arts History: Reconstructing a Training History**
   - *Question:* How can texts, images, objects, and oral accounts be combined?
   - *Key Focus:* State what each source can and cannot prove. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Martial Arts History: School Membership Over Time**
   - *Question:* What explains a reported expansion or decline?
   - *Key Focus:* Check registries, counting, migration, and commercial schools. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
12. **Martial Arts History: A Training Method Across Eras**
   - *Question:* What changed in goals, rules, and pedagogy?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated manuals and consented current observation. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
13. **Martial Arts History: A School's Historical Archive**
   - *Question:* Which records could clarify unresolved claims?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify provenance, permissions, preservation, and research questions. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
14. **Martial Arts History: One Inventor of Every Technique?**
   - *Question:* Do contemporary sources support a sole-founder story?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace diffusion and collective contribution. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
15. **Martial Arts History: One School Through Its Records**
   - *Question:* How did a documented training institution evolve?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a timeline with dissent and archival gaps. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Martial Arts History: The Earliest Manual Checked**
   - *Question:* What does its original wording actually describe?
   - *Key Focus:* Check edition, translation, audience, and later citation. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
17. **Martial Arts History: A Weapon or Training Tool**
   - *Question:* What does an object reveal about practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Check date, wear, provenance, and context; avoid instruction. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
18. **Martial Arts History: Dating a Technique**
   - *Question:* Why do schools give different dates?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate first depiction, written name, and later standardized form. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
19. **Martial Arts History: Training Alongside Work**
   - *Question:* Who could afford time and access to lessons?
   - *Key Focus:* Use fees, schedules, class, gender, and community records. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
20. **Martial Arts History: Who Standardized the Art**
   - *Question:* How did federations, states, or schools set rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare authority, exclusions, and practitioner response. Evidence base: dated manuals in original language, school and federation records, material provenance, and attributed practitioner accounts.
