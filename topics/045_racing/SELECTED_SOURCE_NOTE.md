## Group B — BANK-045-057

The [FIA historic entry for the Ford RS200](https://historicdb.fia.com/range/rs) records its Group B homologation context, a competition power figure above 420 hp, and the FIA's 1986 rally ban on safety grounds. The [FIA account of rally safety after Portugal 1986](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/auto3.pdf) describes spectator exposure and later changes in tracking and medical response. This supports a regulation and safety case, not the archive pitch's universal 600 hp claim or its claim about human reaction time. Compare contemporaneous homologation and incident records before selecting vehicles.

## Imola 1994 and safety — BANK-045-059

The [FIA's thirty-year safety history](https://www.fia.com/news/legacy-safety-how-fia-responded-tragedy-imola-1994-and-launched-drive-safer-racing) identifies the deaths of Roland Ratzenberger and Ayrton Senna and dates later steps including wheel tethers and mandatory HANS use in F1 in 2003. The episode should separate immediate race-weekend action from developments that took years, and test each design change against its own adoption record.

## Brawn GP diffuser ruling — BANK-045-060

The [FIA International Court of Appeal's 14 April 2009 decision](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/judgements/ICA-14-04-2009-a.pdf) is the primary adjudication for the disputed diffuser interpretation. Compare the exact rule language, objections and ruling with dated car designs. The archive's “secret loophole gave the championship” wording compresses legality, aerodynamic benefit, rivals' development and an entire season into one causal claim.
