## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Board Games & Tabletop across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Ancient Origins, Probability Dice & The German Eurogame Revolution (Beginner / Scratch)** — The Royal Game of Ur (2500 BCE), Senet, normal distribution curves of 2d6 dice, Klaus Teuber's Settlers of Catan, and the elimination of player elimination.
- **Tier 2: Worker Placement, Deck Building & Drafting Mechanics (Intermediate)** — Agricola worker placement action drafting, Dominion deck-building engine loops, 7 Wonders card drafting, tile laying in Carcassonne, and area majority voting.
- **Tier 3: Asymmetric Game Theory, Social Deduction & Campaign Legacy Systems (Upper Intermediate)** — Root faction asymmetry, Secret Hitler social deduction psychological tells, Rob Daviau's Legacy irreversible board destruction, and Gloomhaven tactical hand management.
- **Tier 4: Tabletop RPG Worldbuilding, Miniature Sculpting & Master Board Game Architecture (Advanced / Masterclass)** — D&D Dungeon Master narrative improvisation, Warhammer 40k miniature army balance math, Kickstarter crowdfunding economies, and the master tabletop design document.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Settlers of Catan: How Klaus Teuber Sparked the Modern Board Game Renaissance**
   - *Question:* How did a German dental technician build a game in his basement that saved board gaming from Monopoly and roll-to-move boredom?
   - *Key Focus:* Modular hexagonal board, 2d6 dice probability bell curves, trading resource friction, zero player elimination, and the birth of the Eurogame.
2. **The Royal Game of Ur: Deciphering the Oldest Playable Board Game on Earth**
   - *Question:* How did British Museum curator Irving Finkel translate a 4,000-year-old cuneiform clay tablet to resurrect ancient Sumerian gaming?
   - *Key Focus:* 2500 BCE Royal Tombs of Ur excavation, tetrahedral four-sided dice, race game track mechanics, betting stakes, and gaming as universal human anthropology.
3. **The Mathematics of Dice: Bell Curves, Variance, and Mitigating Bad Luck**
   - *Question:* Why does rolling 2d6 create a smooth probability pyramid centered on 7 while rolling 1d20 is pure flat chaos?
   - *Key Focus:* Discrete uniform distributions vs triangular/bell-shaped probability, expected value, input randomness vs output randomness, and luck mitigation mechanics.
4. **Monopoly's Anti-Capitalist Secret: The Stolen Landlord's Game**
   - *Question:* How did Lizzie Magie design a 1903 board game to warn the world about greedy landlords, only to have Parker Brothers steal it to celebrate greed?
   - *Key Focus:* The Landlord's Game Georgist tax critique, dual rulesets (monopolist vs anti-monopolist cooperative), Charles Darrow's stolen patent, and why Monopoly ruins friendships.
5. **The Eurogame Philosophy: Why German Games Banned Violence and Player Elimination**
   - *Question:* Why do European board games focus on farming sheep and building medieval cathedrals rather than blowing up opposing armies?
   - *Key Focus:* Post-WWII German pacifism cultural roots, Spiel des Jahres cultural influence, indirect player interaction, victory point scoring, and 'Ameritrash' thematic contrast.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Worker Placement Mechanics: How Agricola Turned Starvation into a High-Stress Strategy**
   - *Question:* How does Uwe Rosenberg's masterwork turn draftable action spaces into an intense game of resource scarcity and feeding your family?
   - *Key Focus:* Action space blocking, action-efficiency optimization, engine building, tension curves, and the evolution of worker placement from Caylus to Agricola.
7. **Deck Building Engines: How Dominion Invented a Whole New Tabletop Genre**
   - *Question:* How did Donald X. Vaccarino eliminate the need for expensive collectible booster packs by making the deck-building process the game itself?
   - *Key Focus:* Trash mechanics for deck thinning, engine building synergy, draw/action economy balance, Kingdom card variance, and spawning games like Clank! and Star Realms.
8. **The Art of the Tile: Carcassonne and the Spatial Puzzle of Medieval France**
   - *Question:* How did Klaus-Jürgen Wrede invent the 'Meeple' and turn laying cardboard road tiles into a vicious tactical area-control war?
   - *Key Focus:* Tile draw luck vs strategic placement, farmer field scoring disputes, meeple placement commitment, city trapping aggression, and spatial puzzle mechanics.
9. **Card Drafting and Simultaneous Play: The Genius of 7 Wonders**
   - *Question:* How does Antoine Bauza's pick-and-pass drafting system keep a 7-player game running in exactly 30 minutes with zero downtime?
   - *Key Focus:* Simultaneous play resolution, neighbor interaction economy, military science culture scoring tracks, and eliminating waiting between turns.
10. **Social Deduction and Hidden Roles: The Psychology of The Resistance and Secret Hitler**
   - *Question:* How do social deduction games use imperfect information, voting patterns, and eye contact to turn friends into paranoid liars?
   - *Key Focus:* Information asymmetry, logical deduction vs emotional bluffing, the Mafia/Werewolf heritage, legislative card tracking, and gaslighting psychology.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Root and Asymmetric Design: When 4 Players Are Playing 4 Completely Different Games**
   - *Question:* How did Cole Wehrle balance industrial cats, woodland guerilla mice, aristocratic birds, and a wandering vagabond raccoon on one map?
   - *Key Focus:* Radical asymmetry, shared ecosystem balance, COIN (counter-insurgency) military mechanics disguised as cute woodland animals, and emergent policing.
12. **Legacy Games: The Shocking Day Rob Daviau Told Players to Rip Up Their Cards**
   - *Question:* How did Risk Legacy and Pandemic Legacy invent permanent irreversible board changes, sealed envelopes, and destroying game components?
   - *Key Focus:* Component destruction taboos, persistent narrative consequences across 12-session campaigns, emotional stakes, and the #1 BoardGameGeek rating peak.
13. **Gloomhaven: How a 20-Pound Box of Cardboard Smashed D&D with Pure Math**
   - *Question:* How did Isaac Childres replace chaotic 20-sided combat dice with deterministic dual-action card mechanics to conquer the hobby?
   - *Key Focus:* Dual-action card initiative system, resting hand exhaustion limits, modifier decks instead of dice, modular campaign progression, and Kickstarter crowdfunding.
14. **Warhammer 40k: The Grimdark Miniatures Empire and Competitive Math**
   - *Question:* Why do millions of hobbyists spend thousands of hours gluing, painting, and measuring line-of-sight for 28mm plastic space soldiers?
   - *Key Focus:* Games Workshop plastic sprue injection molding, Codex balance cycles, armor save probabilities, tournament tournament meta math, and grimdark lore.
15. **The Kickstarter Board Game Gold Rush: $100 Plastic Minis vs Cardboard Gameplay**
   - *Question:* How did crowdfunding platforms turn board game publishing into high-margin luxury toy campaigns with 50-pound boxes of plastic figures?
   - *Key Focus:* Stretch goal psychology, FOMO exclusive promos, CMON and Kingdom Death: Monster economics, container shipping freight crises, and publisher bankruptcies.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Dungeons & Dragons and the Tabletop RPG Renaissance: Critical Role and 5th Edition**
   - *Question:* How did D&D 5th Edition strip away 40 years of rulebook bloat to become a streaming entertainment juggernaut?
   - *Key Focus:* Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson origins, advantage/disadvantage simplification, actual play streaming culture (Matt Mercer), and collaborative improv theater.
17. **Solo Tabletop Gaming: The Explosive Rise of 1-Player Board Games**
   - *Question:* Why are busy adults buying complex tabletop games specifically to play alone against automated cardboard AI decks?
   - *Key Focus:* The Automa Factory algorithmic card decks (Scythe/Wingspan), analog tactile escape from computer screens, puzzle optimization, and Mage Knight mastery.
18. **The Dark Side of Tabletop: The 2023 D&D Open Game License (OGL) Revolt**
   - *Question:* How did Hasbro's attempt to revoke the 20-year-old Open Game License trigger a massive boycott and birth competitor RPG engines (ORC)?
   - *Key Focus:* Open-source tabletop commons, Ryan Dancey's 2000 OGL vision, Hasbro monetization corporate leaked memos, Paizo Pathfinder backlash, and Creative Commons surrender.
19. **The Art of Blind Playtesting: Why Your Game Sucks Until Strangers Break It**
   - *Question:* What happens when you give your game and rulebook to four strangers, sit in the corner, and are legally forbidden from saying a single word?
   - *Key Focus:* Rulebook cognitive load, ambiguity failure modes, dominant strategy exploits, emotional table telemetry, and iterative post-it note prototyping.
20. **The Master Tabletop Game Architecture: The Complete Designer's Blueprint**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive methodology unites theme-mechanic marriage, mathematical pacing curves, cognitive ergonomics, and manufacturing capex?
   - *Key Focus:* Component cost punchboard sheets, cardboard thickness vs plastic molding, player decision density per minute, visual graphic design hierarchy, and pitching to publishers.
