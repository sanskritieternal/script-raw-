# Selected primary-source research: tabletop rule cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These source checks bound two proposals only. They do not establish the historical, psychological or sales claims in the other archived pitches.

## Monopoly Free Parking — BANK-133-043

- Primary publisher source: Hasbro/Parker Brothers, [Monopoly Real Estate Trading Game rules](https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/monins.pdf). In this rulebook, Free Parking gives no money, property or reward; it is a resting space.
- Related publisher context: Hasbro’s [2014 house-rules announcement](https://corp.hasbro.com/node/15036) distinguishes player house rules, including Free Parking cash, from the official rules it then discussed adding to future games.
- Limit: The PDF is a particular printed rule set, and Hasbro has published multiple editions and variants. Do not generalize that every Monopoly edition handles the square identically. Neither source measures how much a cash-pot house rule changes game duration or who wins; that would need a specified ruleset, assumptions and simulation or observed play data.

## D&D SRD 5.1 licensing — BANK-133-044

- Primary publisher statement: Wizards of the Coast/D&D Beyond, [OGL 1.0a & Creative Commons, 27 January 2023](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1439-ogl-1-0a-creative-commons). Wizards said it would leave OGL 1.0a in place and make the entire SRD 5.1 available under Creative Commons.
- Primary current document index: D&D Beyond, [System Reference Document releases](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd/), which distinguishes SRD 5.1 under OGL 1.0a and CC BY 4.0 from later SRD versions.
- Limit: This is the publisher’s dated declaration and document availability, not a full independent legal analysis. It does not license D&D trademarks, every setting or every rulebook; creators must inspect the actual version and terms relevant to their use. The statement does not itself prove a boycott’s size, speed or sole causal role, nor that a proposed OGL revocation legally took effect. Avoid individualized licensing advice.

## Semantic boundaries

The tabletop bank handles the printed rule and game-use question. [Chess accessibility](../134_chess/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-134-042) has a game-specific home; [collection provenance](../135_collecting/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-135-031) addresses an object’s ownership history rather than reconstruction of Royal Game of Ur play. Game crowdfunding and the making of collectible objects can be compared without merging their episode questions.
