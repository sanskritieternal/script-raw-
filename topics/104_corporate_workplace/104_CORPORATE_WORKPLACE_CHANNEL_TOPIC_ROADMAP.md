## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Corporate & Workplace across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Workplace Dynamics, Onboarding & Corporate Communication Etiquette (Scratch / Beginner)** — Slack/email asynchronous vs. synchronous norms, navigating matrix hierarchies, managing up, meeting discipline (no-agenda no-meeting), and professional boundaries.
- **Tier 2: Performance Reviews, Career Trajectories & Compensation Negotiation (Intermediate)** — Calibrating performance review rubrics, 360-degree feedback loops, promotional packet documentation (the 'Brag Sheet'), and equity/RSU vs. base salary negotiation.
- **Tier 3: Workplace Politics, Power Mapping & Burnout Prevention (Upper Intermediate)** — Jeffrey Pfeffer's Power dynamics, stakeholder coalition mapping, surviving corporate restructuring and layoffs, and Maslach Burnout Inventory recovery protocols.
- **Tier 4: Future of Work: Remote Architecture, AI Workplace Integration & The Hybrid Office (Advanced / Expert)** — Fully remote vs. hybrid productivity metrics, asynchronous documentation culture (GitLab handbook model), AI agent integration into knowledge workflows, and employee surveillance ethics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Workplace Dynamics & Professional Etiquette
1. **Managing Up: How to Build an Unbreakable Strategic Partnership with Your Boss**
   - *Question:* How do top performers make themselves indispensable to their manager without ever kissing up or engaging in toxic office politics?
   - *Key Focus:* Understanding your boss's real OKRs and stressors (what keeps them awake at night), adapting to their preferred communication style (Slack vs. bulleted email vs. face-to-face), never presenting a problem without two actionable solutions, and making your manager look brilliant to their own leadership.
2. **Corporate Communication Etiquette: Asynchronous vs. Synchronous Slack and Email Hygiene**
   - *Question:* Why does sending a message that just says 'Hi' on Slack waste collective time, and how do top professionals write emails that get replies in 5 minutes?
   - *Key Focus:* The 'No Hello' rule (including the question in the initial ping), clear subject lines with action tags ([ACTION REQUIRED], [INFO], [DECISION]), public channels vs. DMs for institutional knowledge retention, and setting healthy boundaries against late-night notifications.
3. **Navigating Matrix Organizations: Multiple Bosses, Dotted Lines, and Cross-Functional Chaos**
   - *Question:* How do you survive and thrive when you report to a functional manager for reviews but three different project leads for daily work who all think their task is priority #1?
   - *Key Focus:* Solid line vs. dotted line reporting structures, transparent capacity signaling, bringing competing managers together to force priority arbitration ('I can do Project A or Project B by Friday, which would you like me to deprioritize?'), and cross-functional diplomacy.
4. **The No-Agenda, No-Meeting Rule: How to Protect Your Calendar from Corporate Black Holes**
   - *Question:* How do you politely decline useless meetings called by senior colleagues without seeming uncooperative or damaging your career?
   - *Key Focus:* Defending calendar time blocks for deep work, the standard meeting contract (every calendar invite must have a 3-bullet agenda and desired outcome), delegating async status updates, and professional decline scripts ('I'm heads-down on X deliverable, can I review the notes afterwards?').
5. **Corporate Onboarding: The 30-60-90 Day Plan for Hitting the Ground Running**
   - *Question:* What is the strategic difference between Month 1 (absorbing context), Month 2 (identifying bottlenecks), and Month 3 (executing changes) in a new corporate job?
   - *Key Focus:* The 30-day listening tour (interviewing 20 stakeholders with identical diagnostic questions), understanding unwritten corporate norms, avoiding the trap of criticizing previous systems, securing low-hanging fruit early wins, and establishing your professional brand.
### Tier 2: Performance Reviews & Compensation
6. **The 'Brag Sheet': How to Document Your Impact So Promotions Become Inevitable**
   - *Question:* Why will working hard in silence NEVER get you promoted, and how do elite performers keep a running 'Brag Sheet' that writes their own promotion packets?
   - *Key Focus:* The psychological recency bias of managers during annual reviews, keeping a weekly 5-minute achievement log, translating daily tasks into business impact metrics (revenue generated, hours saved, processes automated), collecting praise emails, and presenting undeniable data during calibration cycles.
7. **Deciphering Performance Review Calibrations: How Ratings and Bell Curves Really Work**
   - *Question:* What actually happens behind closed doors when directors and HR sit in calibration meetings to fight over who gets the top 'Exceeds Expectations' rating?
   - *Key Focus:* How forced distribution bell curves cap top ratings, manager advocacy capital (why your boss's reputation dictates your rating), 360-degree peer feedback strategy, reading between the lines of performance rubrics, and challenging unfair performance reviews.
8. **Compensation Negotiation: Base Salary, RSUs, Signing Bonuses, and Comp Ratios**
   - *Question:* How do you negotiate an extra $30,000 in equity and signing bonuses on a job offer without risking the company rescinding the offer?
   - *Key Focus:* Understanding corporate compensation bands (comp-ratios), Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) vs. stock options, 4-year vesting schedules with 1-year cliffs, the power of competing offers, counter-offering with data (Levels.fyi), and negotiating non-salary perks (extra PTO, remote flexibility).
9. **PIP (Performance Improvement Plan): The 30-Day Countdown and How to Navigate It**
   - *Question:* When your manager places you on a PIP, is it ever a genuine attempt to help you improve, or is it simply HR building a paper trail to fire you legally?
   - *Key Focus:* The reality of corporate PIPs as legal liability shields against wrongful termination lawsuits, assessing whether the goals are achievable or engineered for failure, negotiating a voluntary exit package (severance) in exchange for signing liability waivers, and updating your resume on day one.
10. **The Career Matrix: Moving from Individual Contributor (IC) to People Manager**
   - *Question:* Why is the transition from star engineer/designer to first-time manager a complete career change where your old technical skills become irrelevant?
   - *Key Focus:* The loss of immediate tangible dopamine (writing code/closing sales vs. ambiguous people coaching), learning to celebrate indirect output through others, managing former peers, avoiding the urge to jump in and do the work yourself, and evaluating if management is truly your calling.
### Tier 3: Workplace Politics & Burnout Defense
11. **Jeffrey Pfeffer's 'Power': The Real Rules of Corporate Politics That Nobody Admits**
   - *Question:* Why do naive believers in pure meritocracy get passed over while politically savvy colleagues who understand power dynamics rise to the top?
   - *Key Focus:* Stanford Professor Jeffrey Pfeffer's seminal book 'Power', the 'Just World' fallacy (believing good work speaks for itself), building visibility with key decision-makers, controlling resource gateways, strategic networking across departments, and accumulating social capital ethically.
12. **Stakeholder Mapping: The RACI Matrix and Power-Interest Grids**
   - *Question:* How do you launch a major corporate initiative without getting blindsided by a quiet veto from a senior director you forgot to consult?
   - *Key Focus:* The Power-Interest Grid (High Power/High Interest 'Manage Closely', High Power/Low Interest 'Keep Satisfied'), the RACI Matrix (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed), avoiding too many cooks in the kitchen, and holding pre-alignment meetings before formal steering committees.
13. **Surviving Corporate Layoffs: WARN Act Notices, Severance Agreements, and Financial Triage**
   - *Question:* When mass corporate layoffs strike and your Slack access is suddenly cut off at 8:00 AM, what are your immediate legal rights and financial action steps?
   - *Key Focus:* The federal WARN Act (60-day notice rules for mass layoffs), severance agreement review (never sign on day one), negotiating extended health insurance (COBRA) subsidies and unvested equity, filing for state unemployment immediately, and emotional shock recovery.
14. **The Maslach Burnout Inventory: Exhaustion, Cynicism, and The Architecture of Recovery**
   - *Question:* Why is true clinical burnout NOT just feeling tired from a long week, but a serious neuro-endocrine breakdown of your adrenal system?
   - *Key Focus:* Christina Maslach's 3 dimensions of burnout: Emotional Exhaustion, Depersonalization (cynicism and treating coworkers like obstacles), and Reduced Personal Efficacy; workplace causes (lack of autonomy, unfair rewards, values mismatch), and medical recovery protocols requiring systemic boundary resets.
15. **Imposter Syndrome in Corporate Spaces: Why Top Performers Feel Like Frauds**
   - *Question:* Why does getting promoted to Director or VP make high achievers feel terrified that everyone is about to discover they are completely unqualified?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. Pauline Clance's 1978 Impostor Phenomenon research, the spotlight effect, attributing success to 'luck' or 'flukes' while attributing mistakes to permanent incompetence, distinguishing normal learning curves from fraud feelings, and adopting the growth mindset.
### Tier 4: Future of Work & Remote Architecture
16. **Asynchronous Remote Work Architecture: The GitLab Handbook Model That Ditched the Office**
   - *Question:* How does an enterprise software company with 2,000 employees across 65 countries operate with zero physical offices and almost zero live meetings?
   - *Key Focus:* GitLab's 2,000-page public handbook, 'Documentation-First' culture (if it's not written down, it doesn't exist), asynchronous project tracking on merge requests, respecting global time zones, eliminating the 'tap on the shoulder' culture, and output-based compensation.
17. **Employee Surveillance Tech: Bossware, Keystroke Logging, and Mouse Jigglers**
   - *Question:* Why are corporate IT departments installing software that takes webcam photos every 10 minutes and logs keystrokes, and why does it incentivize fake 'productivity theatre'?
   - *Key Focus:* The explosion of 'bossware' monitoring software, keystroke logging and idle-time tracking, the underground market for USB hardware mouse jigglers, the destruction of employee trust, and why measuring activity inputs instead of business outcomes is a managerial catastrophe.
18. **AI in the Corporate Workflow: How LLMs and Automation Are Rewriting the White-Collar Job**
   - *Question:* Will generative AI eliminate entry-level corporate analysts, or will professionals who master AI copilots simply replace those who don't?
   - *Key Focus:* Automating routine document synthesis, financial modeling code generation, customer service triaging, the shifting definition of corporate competence toward curation, verification, and critical strategic synthesis, and enterprise AI compliance policies.
19. **The Fractional Executive Economy: Why Top Leaders Are Working for 5 Startups at Once**
   - *Question:* Why are senior corporate VPs leaving $400k corporate jobs to work 10 hours a week for four different companies as 'Fractional' CMOs and CFOs?
   - *Key Focus:* The rise of fractional leadership (startups getting elite executive strategy for $8k/month without full-time benefits or equity dilution), high hourly leverage, portfolio careers diversifying career risk, and the unbundling of traditional full-time corporate employment.
20. **The Future of the Workplace: The 2035 Office, Virtual HQs, and The Search for Meaning**
   - *Question:* What will the corporate office look like in 2035, and how will humanity decouple personal identity and life meaning from corporate job titles?
   - *Key Focus:* The reimagining of physical offices as hospitality social clubs for collaboration rather than computer rows, spatial VR presence, asynchronous global contracts, and the cultural movement decoupling self-worth from corporate ladders toward holistic life design.
