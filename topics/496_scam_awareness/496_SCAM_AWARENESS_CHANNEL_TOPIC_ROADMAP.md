## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Scam Awareness: How a Scam Pattern Emerged**
   - *Question:* Which records show the development of one fraud pattern?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated consumer alerts and enforcement cases, not scripts. Evidence base: dated regulator alerts, enforcement filings, complaint-data methodology, and consented victim accounts.
2. **Scam Awareness: A High-Profile Scam Reexamined**
   - *Question:* What do official findings establish about a widely reported scheme?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare charges, judgments, losses, and restitution. Evidence base: dated regulator alerts, enforcement filings, complaint-data methodology, and consented victim accounts.
3. **Scam Awareness: The People Behind Reported Losses**
   - *Question:* Whose experience is missing from aggregate fraud statistics?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and reporting-coverage limits. Evidence base: dated regulator alerts, enforcement filings, complaint-data methodology, and consented victim accounts.
4. **Scam Awareness: Checking a Suspicious Claim**
   - *Question:* Which independent sources can verify a claim of authority or payment?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain safe verification channels without repeating the scam's steps. Evidence base: dated regulator alerts, enforcement filings, complaint-data methodology, and consented victim accounts.
5. **Scam Awareness: Why Fraud Reports Understate Harm**
   - *Question:* How do reporting and classification affect measured fraud losses?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare survey, complaint, and enforcement datasets. Evidence base: dated regulator alerts, enforcement filings, complaint-data methodology, and consented victim accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Scam Awareness: An Unresolved Fraud Investigation**
