## Berkeley Free Speech Movement and Academic Senate records — BANK-145-046

- Primary institutional and participant records: UC Berkeley Academic Senate’s [history of the 1964 free-speech vote](https://academic-senate.berkeley.edu/about/berkeleys-academic-senate-history), and the [8 December 1964 Academic Senate resolution and debate transcript](https://www.fsm-a.org/stacks/AP_files/ACSenate.html) reproduced by the Free Speech Movement Archives. Berkeley Library’s [collection guide](https://guides.lib.berkeley.edu/BancroftPoliticsProtestCollections/ucb_collections) identifies additional contemporary student and faculty records.
- These records permit a dated question about how faculty governance responded to student protest; they do not make the faculty resolution identical to the students’ demands or to final campus policy.
- Limit: The institutional history is a retrospective summary, and the participant archive is a transcription/reproduction whose wording should be checked against an archival image before close quotation. Compare implementation chronology before asserting an outcome. Public archival availability does not remove privacy, publicity or reuse restrictions for student material.

## Harvard fiscal 2025 endowment — BANK-145-047

- Primary institutional accounts: Harvard University, [FY2025 Financial Report](https://finance.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum12671/files/2025-10/fy25-financial-report.pdf) and [institutional endowment fact book](https://oira.harvard.edu/factbook/fact-book-endowment/).
- The FY2025 report states endowment value of $56.9 billion at June 30, 2025, an 11.9% investment return, and $2.5 billion made available for operations. The fact book reports endowment support as 40% of revenue for that fiscal year.
- Limit: The endowment is an asset stock made up of many funds, some with donor restrictions; the annual distribution is a separate flow. These sources are Harvard’s own audited or institutional accounts and do not by themselves establish teaching quality, investment ethics, affordability or a comparison with a hedge fund. Fiscal years and accounting definitions matter; do not splice figures from different years.
