## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of School & University Stories across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Campus Traditions, Secret Societies & Historic Student Pranks (Beginner / Scratch)** — Yale's Skull and Bones rituals, Oxford college dining hall traditions, the legendary MIT and Caltech hack wars, Harvard-Yale game pranks, and student folklore.
- **Tier 2: Elite Admissions, The Ivy League Mystique & Fraternal Subcultures (Intermediate)** — Legacy admissions mechanics, Operation Varsity Blues celebrity scandal, Greek life hazing tragedies and legal liability, and campus architectural symbolism.
- **Tier 3: Student Revolts, Academic Freedom & Endowment Empires (Upper Intermediate)** — The 1968 Columbia University student occupation, Berkeley Free Speech Movement, Harvard's $50 billion hedge fund with a university attached, and tenure civil wars.
- **Tier 4: The Trillion-Dollar Student Debt Crisis, Remote Campuses & The Higher Ed Reckoning (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Tuition hyperinflation dynamics, administrative bloat, predatory for-profit college collapses, the decline of humanities, and the future of the decentralized campus.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Great Caltech Rose Bowl Hack: How Pranksters Hijacked the Scoreboard**
   - *Question:* How did 14 Caltech engineering students secretly alter 2,232 flip-cards at the 1961 Rose Bowl so Washington fans spelled 'CALTECH' on live national TV?
   - *Key Focus:* The Great Rose Bowl Hoax, reverse-engineering instruction sheets, swapping master mimeograph templates, student technical ingenuity, and non-destructive prank ethics.
2. **Skull and Bones: Inside Yale's Secret Society of Presidents and Spies**
   - *Question:* What happens inside the windowless brownstone 'Tomb' where George W. Bush, John Kerry, and CIA founders swore lifelong secrecy?
   - *Key Focus:* William Huntington Russell 1832 origins, Eulogian rituals, tap day selection, stolen Geronimo skull rumors, alumni networking power, and conspiracy lore.
3. **Operation Varsity Blues: How Rick Singer Sold the 'Side Door' to Stanford and USC**
   - *Question:* How did Hollywood celebrities and CEOs pay $500,000 to photoshop their kids onto water polo teams and bribe corrupt college coaches?
   - *Key Focus:* The 'side door' vs 'back door' donation strategy, bribing SAT proctors to fix test answers, fake athletic resumes, FBI wiretap stings, and prison sentences for Lori Loughlin.
4. **The MIT Hacks: A Police Car on the Great Dome and Disappearing Subways**
   - *Question:* How did MIT students place a fully assembled campus police cruiser with flashing lights on top of a 150-foot dome overnight?
   - *Key Focus:* Strict MIT hacking code (leave no damage, do not get caught, be funny), disassembling car bodies, night-time stealth rigging, and the MIT Museum hack archives.
5. **Oxford vs Cambridge: The 800-Year Rivalry of The Other Place**
   - *Question:* Why did scholars flee Oxford in 1209 after townsfolk hung two clerks, founding Cambridge and sparking an 800-year feud?
   - *Key Focus:* The University Boat Race on the Thames, collegiate tutorial systems, Latin subfusc examination robes, high table dining rituals, and prime minister production lines.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Berkeley Free Speech Movement: Mario Savio and the Gears of the Machine**
   - *Question:* How did a ban on civil rights fundraising on UC Berkeley sidewalks ignite the student protest movement that defined the 1960s?
   - *Key Focus:* Jack Weinberg's police car sit-in standoff, Mario Savio's 'Put your bodies upon the gears' speech, Sproul Hall occupation, and the birth of modern campus activism.
7. **The Dark Reality of Greek Life: Hazing Tragedies and the Machine Behind Fraternities**
   - *Question:* Why do fraternity pledge rituals repeatedly end in fatal alcohol poisoning despite decades of university bans and criminal laws?
   - *Key Focus:* Psychological bonding through shared trauma, bystander paralysis, national fraternity liability insurance pools, the University of Alabama 'Machine' political ring, and reform battles.
8. **The Harvard Endowment: How a University Became a $50 Billion Hedge Fund**
   - *Question:* Why do critics say Harvard is a private equity fund that happens to offer undergraduate classes on the side?
   - *Key Focus:* Jack Meyer's alternative asset allocation strategy (timberland, private equity, hedge funds), tax-exempt educational status, donor gift restrictions, and endowment payout spending rules.
9. **The 1968 Columbia University Occupation: When Students Took Over the President's Office**
   - *Question:* How did anger over a segregated gymnasium in Harlem and Vietnam weapons research lead students to barricade themselves in Low Library?
   - *Key Focus:* Students for a Democratic Society (SDS), Student Afro-American Society (SAS), barricading Dean Coleman, NYPD tactical raid brutality, and the resignation of President Grayson Kirk.
10. **Legacy Admissions: How Aristocratic Bloodlines Still Beat Perfect SAT Scores**
   - *Question:* Why do children of wealthy alumni have a 400% higher acceptance rate at elite private colleges than first-generation applicants?
   - *Key Focus:* 1920s quotas against Jewish immigrants, donor retention economics, 'Z-list' delayed admissions, Supreme Court affirmative action fallout, and states banning legacy preferences.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Student Debt Trap: How Higher Education Became a $1.7 Trillion Crisis**
   - *Question:* Why has college tuition inflated by 1,200% since 1980, outpacing healthcare, food, and housing inflation?
   - *Key Focus:* The Bennett Hypothesis (unlimited federal student loan guarantees driving tuition hikes), administrative bloat (3 administrators per faculty member), luxury campus amenities arms races, and debt forgiveness politics.
12. **The Tenure Track Death Match: Why 75% of College Professors Are Now Adjuncts**
   - *Question:* Why are PhD scholars earning minimum wage on food stamps while universities hire armies of vice-provosts?
   - *Key Focus:* Casualization of academic labor, publish-or-perish neurosis, tenure as a shrinking golden ticket, adjunct unionization strikes, and the corporate gigification of higher ed.
13. **The Great Harvard-Yale Prank of 2004: 'WE SUCK' in Crimson and Blue**
   - *Question:* How did two Yale students disguise themselves as Harvard cheerleaders and trick the entire Harvard alumni section into holding up placards reading 'WE SUCK'?
   - *Key Focus:* Card stunt psychology, fake student organization paperwork, red and white construction paper placement, live TV broadcast humiliation, and viral internet fame.
14. **Predatory For-Profit Colleges: The Rise and Fall of Corinthian Colleges and ITT Tech**
   - *Question:* How did private-equity-backed vocational schools drain billions in GI Bill and federal loan money before being shut down for systemic fraud?
   - *Key Focus:* The 90/10 federal revenue loophole, high-pressure 'pain funnels' call center recruiting, fabricated 95% job placement statistics, worthless unaccredited degrees, and federal borrower defense discharges.
15. **The Heist of the Century: The Transylvania University Rare Book Robbery**
   - *Question:* How did four bored college students try to steal John James Audubon's Birds of America and a 1459 Gutenberg Bible from their library?
   - *Key Focus:* Tasing the librarian, escaping in a minivan, Christie's auction house sting in New York, FBI arrests, and the feature film American Animals.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Town vs Gown Wars: The 1355 St. Scholastica Day Massacre in Oxford**
   - *Question:* How did an argument over sour wine at the Swindlestock Tavern turn into a 3-day bloody street war that killed 60 Oxford students?
   - *Key Focus:* Town and gown economic tensions, academic special legal immunity, bow-and-arrow civilian mobs, royal retribution favoring the university, and centuries of ritual apology.
17. **The Death of the Humanities: Why English and History Majors Are Vanishing**
   - *Question:* Why did enrollment in English, history, and philosophy collapse by 40% over a decade as students fled to Computer Science and Finance?
   - *Key Focus:* Post-2008 financial trauma, ROI transactional mindset toward degrees, corporate hiring requirements, cuts to university arts departments, and the cultural cost of losing humanistic literacy.
18. **University Land Empires: How Columbia and Yale Swallowed Entire Neighborhoods**
   - *Question:* How did private universities use eminent domain and tax-exempt property status to become the largest real estate landlords in New York and New Haven?
   - *Key Focus:* Columbia's Manhattanville expansion battle, eviction of minority industrial businesses, PILOT (Payments in Lieu of Taxes) disputes, and campus borders policing.
19. **The Fall of the College Degree: Silicon Valley Hiring Without Diplomas**
   - *Question:* Why are Google, Apple, and major state governments stripping four-year college degree requirements from job descriptions?
   - *Key Focus:* Skills-based portfolio hiring, coding bootcamps, apprentice models, the paper ceiling, and the challenge to higher ed's credentialing monopoly.
20. **The Master Higher Education Architecture: The Future of the University**
   - *Question:* What sustainable institutional blueprint balances affordable tuition, genuine tenure-protected research, digital access, and civic community roots?
   - *Key Focus:* Endowment reform, decoupling credentials from credentialism, hybrid physical-digital campuses, lifelong subscription learning models, and restoring higher education as a public good.
