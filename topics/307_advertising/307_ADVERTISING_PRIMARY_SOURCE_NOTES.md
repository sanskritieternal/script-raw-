## BANK-307-042: Lord & Taylor, 2015 campaign

- U.S. FTC, [2016 administrative complaint](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/cases/160523lordtaylorcmpt.pdf), inspected 2026-10-24. It alleges undisclosed paid Instagram posts for the Design Lab paisley dress and sponsored Nylon content around a March 2015 campaign.
- FTC staff, [Lord & Taylor native advertising case explanation](https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2016/03/ftcs-lord-taylor-case-native-advertising-clear-disclosure-always-style), inspected 2026-10-24. It describes 50 paid influencers and the distinction between the campaign's measured reach/engagement and its disclosure problem.
- Limits: complaint allegations and settlement language should be attributed to the FTC; do not invent motives or infer that views or dress sellout establish incremental sales. This is a dated U.S. regulatory case, not a universal rule for every format or jurisdiction. Verify final order wording if discussing obligations.

## BANK-307-043: Volkswagen clean-diesel claims

- U.S. FTC, [29 March 2016 complaint announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/03/ftc-charges-volkswagen-deceived-consumers-its-clean-diesel-campaign), inspected 2026-10-24. The agency alleged false environmental and emissions representations for named VW/Audi TDI models marketed in the U.S.
- FTC, [July 2020 final court-summary report](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2020/07/final-court-summary-ftc-reports-volkswagen-repaid-more-95-billion-car-buyers-who-were-deceived-clean), inspected 2026-10-24. It reports the settlement/redress program for affected buyers and lessees.
- Limits: allegations, settlement outcome, emissions-test evidence and actual advertising examples are distinct record types. Do not extrapolate to all Volkswagen models, current products, non-U.S. legal findings or each buyer's measured reliance. This bank concerns the challenged advertising claim; product performance test design belongs with BANK-306-040 and wider brand repair with BANK-305-040.
