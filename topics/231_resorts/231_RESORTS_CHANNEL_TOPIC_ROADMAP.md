## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Resorts across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Resorts Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Resorts.
- **Tier 2: Resorts Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Resorts.
- **Tier 3: Resorts Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Resorts.
- **Tier 4: Resorts Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Resorts.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Resorts: How a Resort Site Was Chosen**
   - *Question:* Which land, transport, and financing decisions created one resort?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated land, planning, and ownership records. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
2. **Resorts: A Celebrated Resort Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What does the property actually offer in a specified season?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare current services, total price, access, and promotional claims. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
3. **Resorts: The Workforce Behind Leisure**
   - *Question:* Whose labor keeps one resort running?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace housekeeping, grounds, food, transport, maintenance, and subcontractors. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
4. **Resorts: Reading a Resort Offer**
   - *Question:* What is included in the quoted stay and what costs extra?
   - *Key Focus:* Check property, dates, room, meals, activities, fees, and cancellation terms. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
5. **Resorts: Capacity and Service Trade-offs**
   - *Question:* How do occupancy peaks affect staffing and guest experience?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare room nights, queues, service hours, and worker schedules. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Resorts: Why a Resort Failed**
   - *Question:* Which documented factors explain a stalled or closed development?
   - *Key Focus:* Test finance, demand, access, water, regulation, and incomplete records. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
7. **Resorts: A Resort Service Tool Tested**
   - *Question:* Did one booking or room system improve service?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare errors, staff workload, accessibility, privacy, and recovery. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
8. **Resorts: A Resort Development Decision**
   - *Question:* Who authorized a consequential land or design choice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use proposals, consent records, objections, and outcomes. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
9. **Resorts: A Resort During Disruption**
   - *Question:* How did one event alter guest and worker arrangements?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated property notices, payroll evidence, and local records. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
10. **Resorts: From Reservation to Departure**
   - *Question:* Which handoffs produce a complete resort stay?
   - *Key Focus:* Map booking, transfer, check-in, meals, activities, and checkout. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Resorts: A Resort's Changing Occupancy**
   - *Question:* What explains a documented rise or fall in use?
   - *Key Focus:* Check seasonality, prices, transport, room supply, and counting. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
12. **Resorts: One Resort Across Two Periods**
   - *Question:* How did its scale, labor, amenities, and local effects change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable plans, rates, employment, and resource measures. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
13. **Resorts: A Resort's Conditional Future**
   - *Question:* Which factors could alter its operating model?
   - *Key Focus:* State climate, water, labor, financing, and demand assumptions. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
14. **Resorts: Is All-Inclusive All-In?**
   - *Question:* Which exclusions materially change the guest's cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Check dated contract for taxes, activities, drinks, transfers, and tips. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
15. **Resorts: A Day at One Resort**
   - *Question:* What work and decisions shape a guest day?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented observation without exposing private guest or worker data. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Resorts: Beyond the Resort Map**
   - *Question:* Which paths, barriers, or nearby places does the site map omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Check actual mobility routes, shore or land access, and season. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
17. **Resorts: Reaching a Remote Resort**
   - *Question:* Which transfer option works for a defined traveler?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare timetable, full cost, reliability, luggage, and accessibility. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
18. **Resorts: The Resort in Two Seasons**
   - *Question:* What changes in services, price, staffing, and risk?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated offers, weather data, and operating schedules. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
19. **Resorts: Neighbors of the Resort**
   - *Question:* How do residents describe benefits and burdens?
   - *Key Focus:* Seek varied accounts and data on jobs, water, roads, and land. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
20. **Resorts: Why This Resort Sits Here**
   - *Question:* Which coast, slope, water, or habitat conditions shaped its site?
   - *Key Focus:* Use physical maps, planning assessments, and dated observations. Evidence base: dated property offers and contracts, land and planning records, measured resource use, and consented guest, worker, and resident accounts.
