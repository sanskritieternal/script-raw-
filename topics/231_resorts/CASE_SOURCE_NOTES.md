# Resort case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected operator and regulator records are primary to their proposals and notices; neither proves a resort’s actual outcomes.

| Case | Primary record inspected | Claim and access limit |
|---|---|---|

Public English rendering was available; authoritative Dhivehi wording merits later cross-check. |
| Anantara Dhigu terms | [Operator’s February 2025 beverage package terms](https://www.anantara.com/uploads/minor/anantara/documents/anantara-dhigu-maldives-resort/offers/all-inclusive/amd_all_inclusive_package_en_feb2025.pdf) identify exclusions and time/party limits. | A commercial offer is not a neutral comparison or current price. It does not define what every all-inclusive resort must provide. |

No claim about local livelihoods, wastewater quality, beach access or seasonal operations is treated as verified by these selected records.
