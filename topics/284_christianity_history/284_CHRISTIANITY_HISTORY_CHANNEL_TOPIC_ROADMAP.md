## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Christianity History: A Christian Community in the Record**
   - *Question:* Which dated sources establish one community's early presence in a place?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inscriptions, manuscripts, archaeology, and later origin traditions. Evidence base: dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts.
2. **Christianity History: A Council or Church in Context**
   - *Question:* What did one well-known council or institution actually decide or do?
   - *Key Focus:* Read surviving acts, participants, later reception, and omitted voices. Evidence base: dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts.
3. **Christianity History: Everyday Work of a Christian Community**
   - *Question:* Whose teaching, care, craft, or household labor is missing from standard histories?
   - *Key Focus:* Use local archives, material evidence, and consented accounts. Evidence base: dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts.
4. **Christianity History: Many Christian Histories**
   - *Question:* How did two named churches develop differently in comparable periods?
   - *Key Focus:* Define doctrine, governance, language, and local context separately. Evidence base: dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts.
5. **Christianity History: Authority and Church Governance**
   - *Question:* How did a named community determine authority over one issue?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare canons, councils, local practice, and later interpretation. Evidence base: dated church and civic archives, manuscripts, council and legal records, inscriptions, material objects, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Christianity History: An Uncertain Early Text**

8. **Christianity History: A Leader in Contemporary Sources**

11. **Christianity History: A Congregation's Changing Size**
