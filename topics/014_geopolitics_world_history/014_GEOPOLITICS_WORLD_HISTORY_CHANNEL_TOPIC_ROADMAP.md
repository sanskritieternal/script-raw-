## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Geopolitics / World History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Geography, Treaties & Strategic Chokepoints (Scratch / Beginner)** — Physical topography as foreign policy driver, maritime chokepoints, sovereignty origins, and imperial transition crises.
- **Tier 2: Cold War Alliances, Deterrence & Proxy Warfare (Intermediate)** — Nuclear brinkmanship, global reserve currency architecture, humanitarian airlifts, and ideological alliance splits.
- **Tier 3: Post-Cold War Unipolarity, Resource Wars & Trade Blocs (Upper Intermediate)** — Soviet collapse archives, petroleum corridor security, arbitrary colonial borders, and interoceanic canal expansions.
- **Tier 4: Multipolar Realignment, Cyber Sovereignty & Global Supply Chains (Advanced / Expert)** — Semiconductor silicon shields, Arctic navigation races, critical mineral dependencies, and SWIFT sanctions bypass mechanics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Geography, Treaties & Strategic Chokepoints
1. **The Prisoners of Geography: How Mountain Ranges, Rivers, and Coasts Dictate Foreign Policy**
   - *Question:* Why do nation-states repeatedly pursue the same military buffers and naval ports regardless of ideology or leader?
   - *Key Focus:* North European Plain vulnerability, warm-water port imperatives, navigable river networks, Halford Mackinder's Heartland.
2. **The Strait of Malacca: The Chokepoint Carrying 25% of Global Maritime Trade**
   - *Question:* What happens to the global economy and Asian energy supplies if a 1.7-mile shipping funnel is blocked?
   - *Key Focus:* Phillips Channel bottleneck, Middle East crude transport, Malacca dilemma, Kra Isthmus canal proposals.
3. **The Peace of Westphalia (1648): The Birth of Modern National Sovereignty**
   - *Question:* How did the devastation of the Thirty Years' War create the concepts of non-intervention, borders, and nation-states?
   - *Key Focus:* Treaties of Münster and Osnabrück, cuius regio eius religio, end of universal papal authority, balance of power.
4. **The Suez Canal Crisis of 1956: The Day the British Empire Lost Hegemony**
   - *Question:* How did Gamal Abdel Nasser's nationalization of a canal expose the shift from European empires to US-Soviet superpower dominance?
   - *Key Focus:* Sèvres Protocol secret collusion, Eisenhower's financial threat to the British pound, UNEF peacekeepers.
### Tier 2: Cold War Alliances & Deterrence
5. **The Cuban Missile Crisis: 13 Days of Brinkmanship, U-2 Flights, and Vasili Arkhipov**
   - *Question:* How close did nuclear war actually come in October 1962, and whose single dissenting vote saved the world?
   - *Key Focus:* ExComm deliberations, naval blockade quarantine, Jupiter missiles in Turkey trade, Soviet B-59 submarine officer.
6. **The Bretton Woods System: How the US Dollar Became the World's Reserve Currency**
   - *Question:* How did 44 allied nations in New Hampshire in 1944 peg global finance to gold-backed dollars, and what followed the 1971 Nixon Shock?
   - *Key Focus:* Keynes vs White bancor debate, IMF and World Bank genesis, petrodollar agreement with Saudi Arabia.
7. **The Berlin Airlift: Operation Vittles and the Logistics of Feeding 2 Million Citizens**
   - *Question:* How did British and American air crews land a cargo plane every 30 seconds for 15 months to break Stalin's blockade?
   - *Key Focus:* Tempelhof and Gatow air corridors, coal and powdered milk tonnage logistics, Gail Halvorsen 'Candy Bomber'.
8. **The Sino-Soviet Split: How Border Skirmishes Divided the Communist Bloc**
   - *Question:* Why did ideological allies sharing a 4,000-mile border engage in artillery battles on Zhenbao Island in 1969?
   - *Key Focus:* De-Stalinization tensions, nuclear sharing refusal, 1969 Ussuri River clashes, Nixon's 1972 opening to Beijing.
### Tier 3: Post-Cold War Unipolarity & Trade Blocs
9. **The Fall of the Berlin Wall & The Soviet Collapse: 1989–1991 Declassified**
   - *Question:* How did economic stagnation, Chernobyl, Glasnost, and the Afghan war cause an empire of 290 million people to dissolve in days?
   - *Key Focus:* Belovezha Accords, August 1991 coup attempt, non-intervention in Eastern Europe, archival Kremlin minutes.
10. **The Strait of Hormuz: Oil Tankers, Sea Mines, and Persian Gulf Escalation**
   - *Question:* Why does a 21-mile-wide strait between Oman and Iran dictate global crude oil prices and maritime insurance rates?
   - *Key Focus:* Tanker War (1984–1988), fast attack craft swarms, anti-ship cruise missiles, maritime insurance war-risk premiums.
11. **The Sykes-Picot Agreement: How Lines in the Sand Carved the Modern Middle East**
   - *Question:* How did secret 1916 British and French diplomatic maps partition the Ottoman Empire, ignoring ethnic and sectarian realities?
   - *Key Focus:* Mark Sykes and François Georges-Picot, Balfour Declaration contradictions, Arab Revolt betrayals, artificial frontiers.
12. **The Panama Canal Expansion: Post-Panamax Freighters and Central American Geography**
   - *Question:* How did a $5 billion engineering mega-project double the throughput of world trade, and why are droughts now threatening it?
   - *Key Focus:* Neopanamax lock basins, water-saving recycling basins, Gatun Lake freshwater shortages, global container rerouting.
### Tier 4: Multipolar Realignment & Global Supply Chains
13. **The Taiwan Strait Dilemma: Silicon Shield, Island Chains, and Maritime Law**
   - *Question:* How does an island producing over 60% of the world's advanced semiconductors form the fulcrum of 21st-century geopolitics?
   - *Key Focus:* TSMC foundry dependence, First Island Chain naval geography, Taiwan Relations Act, air-defense identification zones.
14. **The Arctic Geopolitical Race: Melting Ice, Northern Sea Route, and Subsea Resources**
   - *Question:* Who controls the top of the world as disappearing ice opens shipping lanes 40% faster than the Suez Canal?
   - *Key Focus:* UNCLOS continental shelf claims (Lomonosov Ridge), nuclear icebreaker fleets, Arctic Council tensions, rare minerals.
15. **Rare Earth Elements & The Critical Minerals Chessboard: Lithium, Gallium, and Neodymium**
   - *Question:* Why is the green energy and defense transition entirely dependent on refining capacity concentrated in a single nation?
   - *Key Focus:* Processing monopolies, neodymium magnets for EV motors and F-35s, export controls, DRC cobalt corridor.
16. **The Thucydides Trap: Can a Rising Power and an Established Power Avoid War?**
   - *Question:* Does Graham Allison's historical audit of 16 great-power rivalries mean war between the US and China is structurally inevitable?
   - *Key Focus:* Peloponnesian War precedent (Sparta vs Athens), peaceful transitions (Britain to US), nuclear deterrence offsets.
17. **The SWIFT Banking Network: Financial Sanctions, Dollar Weaponization, and De-Dollarization**
   - *Question:* How did a cooperative messaging system in Belgium become the world's most devastating financial weapon?
   - *Key Focus:* Cross-border financial messaging, freezing central bank reserves, CIPS and SPFS alternatives, gold settlement.
18. **The Belt and Road Initiative: Ports, Railroads, and Debt Geoeconomics**
   - *Question:* Ten years and $1 trillion later, what did the modern revival of the Silk Road achieve across Eurasia and Africa?
   - *Key Focus:* Gwadar and Piraeus port concessions, China-Europe Railway Express, debt distress renegotiations, BRI 2.0.
19. **Undersea Internet Cables: The Hidden Fiber-Optic Grid Carrying 99% of Global Data**
   - *Question:* What happens if the thin glass lines crossing the seabed are severed by anchors, seismic shifts, or submarine sabotage?
   - *Key Focus:* Red Sea subsea cuts, cable repair ship scarcity, NSA and Russian spy ship intercepts, landing station choke points.
20. **The Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT): The Delicate Balance of Deterrence**
   - *Question:* How has the 1968 treaty prevented dozens of nations from acquiring atomic arsenals, and is it now crumbling?
   - *Key Focus:* IAEA safeguards inspections, Article VI disarmament commitments, breakout timelines, North Korea withdrawal precedent.
