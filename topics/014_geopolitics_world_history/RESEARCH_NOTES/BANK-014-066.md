# BANK-014-066 — NPT history and obligation boundaries

Status: bounded UN institutional-history review for a topic proposal, not a treaty-law opinion or verified script. Inspected 2026-10-24.

## Primary institutional source inspected

- [United Nations Audiovisual Library of International Law, NPT historical overview](https://legal.un.org/avl/ha/tnpt/tnpt.html). The UN history records the 1968 opening for signature, the 5 March 1970 entry into force, periodic review conferences and the 1995 indefinite extension. It describes distinct aims of preventing proliferation, peaceful nuclear cooperation and pursuing disarmament.

## Editorial boundary

The archived pitch calls the NPT a balance of “deterrence.” The inspected UN overview supports a treaty-history question, not a claim that the treaty settles deterrence strategy or that every state has identical obligations. The full treaty text and current party-specific safeguards and compliance records require separate inspection before quoting articles, judging implementation, or giving current state-by-state status. A future episode should distinguish treaty text, review-conference commitments, IAEA safeguards findings, each state's position and outside analysis, with dates on all status claims.
