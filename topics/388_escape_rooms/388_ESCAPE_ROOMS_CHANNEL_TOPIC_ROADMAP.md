## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Escape Rooms across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Escape Rooms Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Escape Rooms.
- **Tier 2: Escape Rooms Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Escape Rooms.
- **Tier 3: Escape Rooms Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Escape Rooms.
- **Tier 4: Escape Rooms Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Escape Rooms.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Escape Rooms: An Escape Room Format Emerges**
   - *Question:* Which documented venue or game practice shaped a room format?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated designs and avoid a single-origin claim. Evidence base: design documents, dependency diagrams, prototypes, playtest observations, operator protocols, accessibility information, and identified room versions.
2. **Escape Rooms: A Room's Puzzle Path**
   - *Question:* How does one named room guide teams from first clue to final task?
   - *Key Focus:* Use permitted design material and avoid unapproved spoilers. Evidence base: design documents, dependency diagrams, prototypes, playtest observations, operator protocols, accessibility information, and identified room versions.
3. **Escape Rooms: Designers and Game Masters**
   - *Question:* Whose writing, fabrication, testing, and live operation make a room work?
   - *Key Focus:* Check credits and operator testimony. Evidence base: design documents, dependency diagrams, prototypes, playtest observations, operator protocols, accessibility information, and identified room versions.
4. **Escape Rooms: A Puzzle Chain**
   - *Question:* How does solving one task reveal the next without arbitrary guessing?
   - *Key Focus:* Diagram dependencies and intended clues. Evidence base: design documents, dependency diagrams, prototypes, playtest observations, operator protocols, accessibility information, and identified room versions.
5. **Escape Rooms: Parallel versus Linear Puzzles**
   - *Question:* How do two dependency structures affect group participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare playtest observations for a specified room. Evidence base: design documents, dependency diagrams, prototypes, playtest observations, operator protocols, accessibility information, and identified room versions.
