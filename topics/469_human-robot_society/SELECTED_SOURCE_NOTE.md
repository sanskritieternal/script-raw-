# Selected source note — Toronto sidewalk delivery devices

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-469-043. Proposed question only; no IDEA approval or script readiness is claimed.

## Primary municipal records

- City of Toronto Council agenda item IE26.12, *Automated Micro-Utility Devices — Accessibility Feedback*, adopted 2021-12-15 to 2021-12-17: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2021.IE26.12 . The recorded decision prohibited use of automated micro-utility devices on sidewalks and cycle tracks and related stopping or storage while the proposed Ontario pilot and a subsequent city opt-in decision remained pending.
- Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee agenda item DI18.4, adopted 2021-11-15: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2021.DI18.4 . It recommended the restrictions and records stakeholder accessibility concerns and the committee vote.

## Claim, time and access limits

These records establish a 2021 policy decision and reasons raised in deliberation. They do not measure collision risk, prove that every device is unsafe, or establish the current rule. Ontario's proposed pilot and Toronto's later choices may have changed the regulatory setting; verify current city by-laws separately before any present-tense claim. The record covers automated micro-utility devices, including remotely controlled ones, not only fully autonomous AI robots.
