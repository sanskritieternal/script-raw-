## Scope and reporting review

Existing 098 topics cover proof standards, IP categories, public-domain checks, police safeguards, trial and appeal, precedent, jurisdiction and legal information versus advice. The repeated proof and IP pitches route there, and the James Duane lecture pitch routes to the bounded Miranda and police-safeguards cases. Neighboring true-crime, forensics, government and public-safety banks were checked; legal rules and court reasons stay in 098, while forensic or narrative methods remain in their banks. All new cases specify a court, statute, jurisdiction or documented program to prevent conflating U.S. state, U.S. federal and international law. Quoting a decision does not establish the present rule unless subsequent authority is checked. Do not identify protected complainants or infer guilt from allegations, pleas in other cases, or a commentator's claim. The bank records research questions, not production readiness.
