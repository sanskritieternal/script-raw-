# Selected source note — BLS wind turbine technician profile

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-455-043. This is a proposed question, not an approved idea or completed script.

## Primary evidence

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, *Occupational Outlook Handbook: Wind Turbine Technicians*, last modified 2026-08-27: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/wind-turbine-technicians.htm . It reports about 11,800 U.S. jobs in 2025, projects about 15,300 in 2035 (30% rounded growth), and estimates about 1,500 annual openings over 2025–35. It reports a May 2025 median annual wage of $64,120 and describes typical postsecondary nondegree education, on-the-job training, work at heights, travel and on-call time.

## Claim and access limits

The 2025–35 figures are projections, not a promise of vacancies, growth in any particular locality, or personal career outcomes. Annual openings include replacement needs and cannot be equated with the net 3,500 projected employment increase. A median wage across incumbent workers is not starting pay; U.S. conditions cannot be generalized to other countries. Recheck the live BLS edition and state/area figures when scripting because projections and wages are revised. Paraphrase BLS content; do not imply a training provider is endorsed.
