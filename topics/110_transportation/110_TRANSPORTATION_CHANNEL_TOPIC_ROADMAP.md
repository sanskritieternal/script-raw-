## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Transportation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Mobility Anthropometrics, Marchetti's Constant & Flow Physics (Beginner / Scratch)** — From ancient Roman walking budgets to Marchetti's 60-minute travel law, modal capacity hierarchies (PPHPD), phantom jam shockwaves, and Braess's paradox of induced demand.
- **Tier 2: Surface Transit Innovations, Congestion Pricing & Aviation Hubs (Intermediate)** — Curitiba Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) mechanics, London/Singapore congestion toll zones, 15-minute city micromobility, airline hub-and-spoke geometry, and passenger ferry transit.
- **Tier 3: Mega-Fleet Electrification, Mode Competition & Autonomous Taxis (Upper Intermediate)** — Megawatt depot charging for municipal bus fleets, 4-hour HSR vs short-haul aviation tipping points, Waymo/Cruise robotaxi algorithms, runway separation safety, and transit desert equity.
- **Tier 4: Exotic Topographies, Speculative Transit & The Unified Metropolis (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Medellin urban aerial cable cars, vacuum tube hyperloop thermodynamic limits, eVTOL urban air mobility reality check, suborbital rocketry, and intermodal MaaS metropolitan architecture.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Evolution of Human Mobility: From Walking Paths to Hypersonic Transit**
   - *Question:* How did human civilization transition from domestic animal pack trains to integrated global transit grids?
   - *Key Focus:* Zahavi's Marchetti's constant, Roman roads, canal eras, motorized mobility, and intermodal transport.
2. **Marchetti's Constant: Why Humans Spend Exactly One Hour Traveling Every Day**
   - *Question:* Why has the average human daily commute remained roughly 60 minutes from ancient Rome to modern Tokyo?
   - *Key Focus:* Travel time budget theory, urban sprawl dynamics, speed vs distance trade-offs, and behavioral economics.
3. **The Hierarchy of Transit Modes: Speed, Capacity, Energy, and Infrastructure Cost**
   - *Question:* When should an urban planner choose heavy metro rail over Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) or light rail?
   - *Key Focus:* Passengers per hour per direction (PPHPD), capital expenditure per kilometer, energy efficiency, and lifecycle costs.
4. **Traffic Flow Dynamics: The Phantom Traffic Jam and Wave Mechanics**
   - *Question:* Why do highways grind to a complete halt even when there is no accident or lane closure ahead?
   - *Key Focus:* Shockwave propagation, driver reaction latency, Nagel-Schreckenberg cellular automata, and variable speed limits.
5. **Induced Demand: Why Adding More Highway Lanes Never Solves Traffic Congestion**
   - *Question:* How did the 26-lane Katy Freeway in Houston end up with longer commute times after a multi-billion dollar expansion?
   - *Key Focus:* Braess's paradox, latent travel demand, triple convergence, and the futility of highway widening.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Bus Rapid Transit (BRT): The High-Capacity Subway on Wheels**
   - *Question:* How did Curitiba, Brazil and Bogota's TransMilenio achieve subway-level passenger volumes at 10% of the cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Dedicated median busways, off-board fare collection, level platform boarding, and signal priority.
7. **Congestion Pricing: How London, Singapore, and Stockholm Tamed City Traffic**
   - *Question:* How does charging drivers a dynamic toll to enter downtown zones revitalize public transit and reduce smog?
   - *Key Focus:* Pigouvian taxes, license plate recognition gantries, revenue reinvestment, and political resistance.
8. **Micromobility and the 15-Minute City: E-Bikes, Scooters, and Walkable Infrastructure**
   - *Question:* Can lightweight electric bikes and protected bike lanes replace cars for short urban trips under 3 miles?
   - *Key Focus:* First-mile/last-mile solutions, dockless fleet unit economics, street redesign, and Paris's 15-minute city model.
9. **The Airline Hub-and-Spoke System: How Global Flight Networks Are Built**
   - *Question:* Why do airlines route millions of passengers through Atlanta, Dubai, or Doha instead of flying point-to-point?
   - *Key Focus:* Network connectivity math, bank scheduling, fleet utilization, point-to-point budget carriers (Southwest), and alliance codeshares.
10. **Maritime Ferries and Water Transits: Moving Millions Across Oceans and Rivers**
   - *Question:* How do commuter ferry networks like Hong Kong's Star Ferry and Staten Island Ferry integrate with city transit?
   - *Key Focus:* Ro-Pax vessel design, pier boarding ergonomics, electric hydrofoil technology, and weather resilience.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Fleet Electrification Challenge: Charging Megawatts, Battery Degradation, and Grid Strain**
   - *Question:* What happens to municipal power grids when 5,000 city buses and garbage trucks plug in simultaneously at night?
   - *Key Focus:* Depot megawatt charging infrastructure, smart load balancing, vehicle-to-grid (V2G), and battery chemistry lifecycles.
12. **High-Speed Rail vs Short-Haul Flights: The Continental Mobility Duel**
   - *Question:* At what travel distance does high-speed rail decisively destroy airline market share?
   - *Key Focus:* The 4-hour door-to-door tipping point, airport security delays, carbon intensity comparisons, and French short-haul flight bans.
13. **Autonomous Robotaxis: Waymo, Cruise, and the Battle for Driverless Mobility**
   - *Question:* Will fleets of self-driving robotaxis eliminate personal car ownership, or will they flood streets with empty vehicles?
   - *Key Focus:* LiDAR sensor suites, edge-case remote assistance, cost per mile economics, and municipal regulatory battles in San Francisco and Phoenix.
14. **Airport Design and Airspace Operations: Managing 2,000 Flights a Day**
   - *Question:* How do air traffic controllers and runway dispatchers orchestrate aircraft landings every 45 seconds?
   - *Key Focus:* Runway separation minima, wake turbulence categories, NextGen satellite navigation, and airport terminal layouts.
15. **Transit Deserts and Mobility Equity: Who Gets Left Behind Without a Car?**
   - *Question:* How does car-centric urban design trap low-income households in cycles of poverty and lengthy multi-bus commutes?
   - *Key Focus:* Spatial mismatch hypothesis, transit accessibility mapping, fare-capping policies, and paratransit services.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Urban Cable Cars and Aerial Gondolas: Transit Over Steep Mountains and Slums**
   - *Question:* How did Medellin's Metrocable transform isolated, crime-ridden hillside barrios into thriving connected communities?
   - *Key Focus:* Aerial ropeway engineering, top-station integration, low capex construction, and social urbanism impact.
17. **Hyperloop and Evacuated Tube Transit: Physics Triumph or Expensive Pipe Dream?**
   - *Question:* Why did Elon Musk's 700 mph vacuum-tube train collapse under the harsh realities of thermal expansion and safety vacuums?
   - *Key Focus:* Kantrowitz limit, tube expansion joint leaks, emergency decompression risks, and astronomical tunneling capital costs.
18. **Electric Vertical Takeoff and Landing (eVTOL): The Flying Car Reality Check**
   - *Question:* Will urban air taxis ferry executives across congested cities, or are they unviable noise hazards for the ultra-rich?
   - *Key Focus:* Distributed electric propulsion, acoustic noise signatures, vertiport airspace integration, and FAA certification hurdles.
19. **Spaceports and Suborbital Transit: 90 Minutes from London to Sydney?**
   - *Question:* Can Starship-style rocket point-to-point travel ever become commercially viable for regular human transportation?
   - *Key Focus:* Atmospheric re-entry thermal loads, g-force physiological limits, sonic boom overland bans, and launch infrastructure economics.
20. **The Unified Intermodal Metropolis: The Master Urban Mobility Architecture**
   - *Question:* How do world-class cities like Tokyo, Zurich, and Singapore weave rail, bus, walking, and shared micro-mobility into a seamless web?
   - *Key Focus:* Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS), single-ticket integration, transit-oriented development (TOD), and zero-emission transit networks.
