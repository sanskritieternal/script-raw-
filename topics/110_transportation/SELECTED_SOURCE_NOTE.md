## Semantic and reporting review

Existing 110 questions already cover induced-demand measurement, access, affordability, suppressed trips, hub transfers, safety exposure and announcements versus operating projects. Repeated archived pitches route there or to verified neighboring BANK-090-066 (controlled traffic wave), BANK-109-036 (rail-versus-air corridor) and BANK-047-024/-070 (airport capacity and eVTOL readiness). The new questions name an agency, route, corridor, place or dated project and separate plans, pilots and operator claims from outcomes. Traffic counts need segment, direction, period and comparator. Rates, tolls, permits, legal authority, fares, capacity and emissions must be refreshed by jurisdiction and date. Include riders who cannot make a trip and groups paying costs without gaining access.
