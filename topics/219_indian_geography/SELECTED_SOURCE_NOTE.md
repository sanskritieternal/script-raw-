# Selected primary-source checks - Indian geography
Checked 2026-10-24. Selected sources bound proposed questions; no map, access or voice is production-ready.

- BANK-219-053: Original Kosi avulsion GIS study by Sinha and colleagues, https://home.iitk.ac.in/~rsinha/Publication/2014_Kosi%20avulsion_Geomorphology.pdf . It examines avulsion threshold and channel planform using mapped evidence around the August 2008 event. A pre/post map cannot by itself assign singular fault or predict the next avulsion; satellite dates, resolution and embankment records matter. The study is a river-landform case, distinct from BANK-210-034 general braided-channel behavior.
- BANK-219-054: Original 1973-2019 Landsat channel and land-cover analysis, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34786632/ , and Assam Majuli district disaster-management record, https://majuli.assam.gov.in/departments/district-disaster-management-department . The satellite study spans selected image dates and classification choices; district area figures may use different boundaries and dates. Do not treat a largest-river-island label, area figure or erosion rate as timeless. The district's disaster plan documents Brahmaputra bank-line movement, https://majuli.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/public_utility/DDMA%20Plan_compressed.pdf .
- Cross-bank scope: BANK-220-033 addresses Mumbai city shoreline and flood pathways; BANK-221-030/-038 address village water and land-use records; BANK-114-042 addresses catchment runoff; BANK-205-010/-039 ecosystem effects. This bank asks how named Indian landforms appear across maps and measurements, without substituting for resident testimony or visitor access advice.
