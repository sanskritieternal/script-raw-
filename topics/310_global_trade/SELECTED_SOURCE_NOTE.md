# Selected primary-source note: global trade

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed cases BANK-310-041 and BANK-310-042.

The US Trade Representative's 2018 Section 301 List 1 case page links proposal, hearing, action notice and exclusion procedure: https://ustr.gov/issue-areas/enforcement/section-301-investigations/section-301-china/34-billion-trade-action . The June 2018 fact sheet describes the proposed-line revision and comment process: https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/fact-sheets/2018/june/section-301-product-list-fact-sheet . These primary agency records support a dated US policy timeline. They do not establish pass-through to a specific household, seller's final landed cost or present tariff applicability; verify the precise HTS line, effective date and later modifications before such claims. Do not present trade-policy analysis as importing advice.

The enacted UK Electronic Trade Documents Act 2023 lists bills of lading and specifies reliable-system conditions for identifying, protecting and exclusively controlling an electronic trade document: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/38/2023-09-20?view=plain . This is direct evidence of UK legal recognition from the displayed commencement date, not evidence that all countries, banks or carriers adopted electronic bills. The legal effect depends on the Act's scope and other applicable law; use a dated version and seek a real transaction record before claiming operational savings.
