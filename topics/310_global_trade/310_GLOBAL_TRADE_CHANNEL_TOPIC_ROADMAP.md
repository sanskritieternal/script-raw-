## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Global Trade across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Global Trade Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Global Trade.
- **Tier 2: Global Trade Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Global Trade.
- **Tier 3: Global Trade Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Global Trade.
- **Tier 4: Global Trade Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Global Trade.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Global Trade: How a Trade Route Formed**
   - *Question:* Which transport, policy, and demand changes connected two markets?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated shipping and customs records. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
2. **Global Trade: A Widely Traded Product**
   - *Question:* What does one product's route reveal?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace origin, processing, borders, value, and labor. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
3. **Global Trade: Workers Across a Trade Chain**
   - *Question:* Whose production, port, customs, and transport work is overlooked?
   - *Key Focus:* Use attributed accounts and labor records. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
4. **Global Trade: From Exporter to Importer**
   - *Question:* Which documents, payments, and logistics cross a border?
   - *Key Focus:* Map one product and pair of countries. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
5. **Global Trade: Gross Flows Versus Value Added**
   - *Question:* How can a product cross borders repeatedly?
   - *Key Focus:* Use input-output evidence and define counting. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Global Trade: A Trade Data Mismatch**
   - *Question:* Why do exporter and importer reports disagree?
   - *Key Focus:* Check valuation, timing, transshipment, classification, and errors. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
7. **Global Trade: A Digital Trade Document**
   - *Question:* Did one system reduce delay or cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare actual clearance time, adoption, trust, and exclusions. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
8. **Global Trade: A Negotiation Decision**
   - *Question:* Who shaped a tariff or agreement term?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated negotiation and stakeholder records. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
9. **Global Trade: A Tariff or Port Shock**
   - *Question:* How did one event redirect trade?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare quantities, prices, routes, and timing. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
10. **Global Trade: One Shipment Across Borders**
   - *Question:* How do producer, carrier, insurer, customs, and buyer coordinate?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace a defined commodity and date. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Global Trade: A Bilateral Trade Trend**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured rise or fall?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate price from volume and check reexports. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
12. **Global Trade: One Trade Corridor Across Eras**
   - *Question:* How did transport cost, rules, and sourcing change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable categories and currency context. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
13. **Global Trade: Conditional Trade Reconfiguration**
   - *Question:* Which costs or policies could reroute supply?
   - *Key Focus:* State tariffs, transport, demand, and capacity assumptions. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
14. **Global Trade: A Deficit Means One Country Loses?**
   - *Question:* What can and cannot be inferred from bilateral balances?
   - *Key Focus:* Define measure and avoid welfare shortcut. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
15. **Global Trade: One Shipment's Recorded Journey**
   - *Question:* What happened from order to delivery?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated documents and protect commercial secrets. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Global Trade: Where Value Accrues**
   - *Question:* How are stages and profits distributed across countries?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace processing, brands, logistics, and retail. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
17. **Global Trade: Exporter, Importer, Carrier, State**
   - *Question:* How do costs and timing incentives differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Read contracts and applicable rules. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
18. **Global Trade: Landed Cost of One Unit**
   - *Question:* What remains after production, freight, duty, insurance, and loss?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify currency, quantity, date, and terms. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
19. **Global Trade: A Trade Change at Checkout**
   - *Question:* Did buyers see price, quality, or availability change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare matched goods and alternative causes. Evidence base: dated customs and trade statistics with classifications, contracts, shipping records, policy texts, and attributed producer and buyer accounts.
20. **Global Trade: Who Can Switch Suppliers**
   - *Question:* Which producers and ports are real substitutes?
