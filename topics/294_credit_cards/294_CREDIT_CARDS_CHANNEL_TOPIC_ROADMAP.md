## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Credit Cards across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Credit Cards Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Credit Cards.
- **Tier 2: Credit Cards Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Credit Cards.
- **Tier 3: Credit Cards Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Credit Cards.
- **Tier 4: Credit Cards Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Credit Cards.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Credit Cards: How Credit Cards Became Common**
   - *Question:* Which rule or network change widened card use in one market?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated issuer, network, and regulatory records. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
2. **Credit Cards: A Card Offer Under the Microscope**
   - *Question:* What does a widely promoted card offer actually cost for a defined use?
   - *Key Focus:* Read its dated agreement and model transparent scenarios. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
3. **Credit Cards: The People Behind Card Payments**
   - *Question:* Whose labor and decisions are invisible in a card transaction?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace issuer, merchant, network, and service roles. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
4. **Credit Cards: Reading a Card Statement**
   - *Question:* What do balance, minimum payment, due date, and interest line mean?
   - *Key Focus:* Walk through an illustrative statement under stated terms. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
5. **Credit Cards: Interest Across Billing Cycles**
   - *Question:* How can payment timing alter interest on a revolving balance?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a dated agreement and transparent arithmetic. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Credit Cards: Why a Card Charge Appeared**
   - *Question:* Which records can resolve a disputed charge and which cannot?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace transaction, authorization, posting, and dispute evidence. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
7. **Credit Cards: A New Payment Authentication Method**
   - *Question:* Did one authentication change reduce a measured fraud pattern?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare a documented rollout with before-and-after data and limits. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
8. **Credit Cards: Who Sets a Card's Terms?**
   - *Question:* Which issuer or regulator decision changed a cardholder's costs?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated agreement notices and policy records. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
9. **Credit Cards: A Credit-Card Rule Changed**
   - *Question:* What changed for cardholders after one dated rule took effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare legal requirements with issuer practice and outcomes. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
10. **Credit Cards: A Card Purchase End to End**
   - *Question:* What happens between a card tap and merchant settlement?
   - *Key Focus:* Map authorization, clearing, settlement, fees, and possible reversals. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Credit Cards: A Balance That Grew**
   - *Question:* How can a revolving balance grow even when payments continue?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a hypothetical schedule with explicit rates, fees, and purchases. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
12. **Credit Cards: Card Terms in Two Periods**
   - *Question:* How did one common card term change over time?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated agreements using the same use pattern. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
13. **Credit Cards: A Conditional Card Payment Change**
   - *Question:* Which constraint would shape adoption of a new card feature?
   - *Key Focus:* Assess security, network support, cost, and access without prediction. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
14. **Credit Cards: Testing a Card Score Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a common claim about card use and credit scores match documented rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify jurisdiction and distinguish lender policy from scoring model. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
15. **Credit Cards: One Billing Dispute's Record**
   - *Question:* How did a cardholder's dispute proceed under the applicable rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented or anonymized records and dated deadlines. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Credit Cards: The Clause Behind the APR**
   - *Question:* Which agreement term controls interest for one type of balance?
   - *Key Focus:* Read purchase, transfer, and cash-advance provisions separately. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
17. **Credit Cards: Card Due Dates and Pay Dates**
   - *Question:* How does a due date affect a household's cash timing?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a fictional, labeled pay calendar and agreement terms. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
18. **Credit Cards: Promotional Rate Expiry**
   - *Question:* What happens when a promotional rate ends with a balance remaining?
   - *Key Focus:* Model one disclosed offer with explicit dates and conditions. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
19. **Credit Cards: Annual, Late, and Transaction Fees**
   - *Question:* Which charges apply to a defined pattern of card use?
   - *Key Focus:* Read a dated fee table and avoid treating all cardholders alike. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
20. **Credit Cards: What a Billing Dispute Right Covers**
   - *Question:* Which transactions and deadlines are covered in one jurisdiction?
   - *Key Focus:* Use regulator guidance and a dated agreement. Evidence base: dated card agreements, regulator rules and data, issuer disclosures, and transparent billing calculations.
