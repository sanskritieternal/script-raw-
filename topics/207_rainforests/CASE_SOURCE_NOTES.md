# Rainforest case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary records define researchable questions, not proof of the archived trend descriptions.

| Case | Primary record inspected | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Southern Amazon moisture | [Wright and colleagues 2017 satellite study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28729375/) reports transpiration and shallow-convection observations during southern Amazon wet-season onset. | Regional seasonal analysis does not show one literal flying river or a quantified planet-wide cooling effect. Abstract accessible; full data were not reanalysed. |
| Walking palm | [Goldsmith and Zahawi 2007 field comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19086384/) measured 80 *Socratea* trees across two sites and tested stilt-root architecture. | Root architecture and stability do not establish whole-tree walking toward light. Abstract accessible; full data were not reanalysed. |
| Madre de Dios land use | [MapBiomas Perú Collection 3 launch, 5 June 2025](https://peru.mapbiomas.org/2025/06/05/lanzamiento-coleccion-3-mapbiomas-peru/) reports mapped mining growth concentrated in Madre de Dios. | Remote land-cover classification is not a mercury or cyanide exposure assay; chemistry and health outcomes need separate primary measurements. The source was read through its public Spanish summary. |
| Atlantic Forest corridor | [Brazilian Environment Ministry corridor project record](https://www.gov.br/mma/pt-br/assuntos/biodiversidade-e-biomas/gestao-integrada-de-paisagem/corredores-ecologicos/projeto-corredores-ecologicos) describes the Central Atlantic Forest Corridor program and 2002–2015 period. | Program existence does not establish corridor function or long-term restored community; site outcome records remain needed. |

Isolated Indigenous peoples' locations and images are not reproduced. River-dolphin observations and restoration outcomes require case-specific source review before production.
