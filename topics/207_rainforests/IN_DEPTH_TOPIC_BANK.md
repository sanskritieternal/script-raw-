#### BANK-207-010 — The Layered Fortress: How the Four Tiers of the Rainforest Harbor 50% of Earth's Species

**Video question:** How does vertical stratification divide a 150-foot forest into four completely separate ecological worlds with their own microclimates and wildlife?

**Individual coverage scope:** The Forest Floor (dark, humid, fungal breakdown), Understory (broad leaves, insect ambushers), Continuous Canopy (90% of all rainforest life), and Emergent Layer (isolated giants braving gales).

#### BANK-207-011 — Rapid Nutrient Cycling: How Poor, Weathered Oxisol Soils Support Lush Mega-Forests via Fungi

**Video question:** Why is the soil beneath a towering Amazonian rainforest biologically impoverished and sterile once the trees are cleared?

**Individual coverage scope:** Ancient weathered red oxisols leaching minerals under torrential rains, 95% of ecosystem nutrients held in living biomass, rapid 24-hour fungal decomposition of leaf litter, and mycorrhizal direct root recycling.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

**Individual coverage scope:** Massive plank buttress roots anchoring against wind shear, small aerodynamic compound leaves reducing drag, deciduous dry season leaf-dropping for wind resistance, and wind-dispersed silk cotton seeds.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

**Individual coverage scope:** Liana flexible vascular wood resisting hurricane twisting, competing with host crowns, tank bromeliads holding gallons of water in overlapping leaf rosettes, and housing endemic tree frogs and crabs.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-207-014 — The 2% Light Threshold: How Understory Plants Capture Scattered Photons with Broad Leaves

**Video question:** How do ground seedlings and understory shrubs survive when 98% of all sunlight is stolen by the dense green canopy ceiling above?

**Individual coverage scope:** Extreme shade tolerance, giant dark green leaves with high chlorophyll b ratios, purple anthocyanin under-leaf reflective layers bouncing photons back through chloroplasts, and sunfleck photosynthesis.

#### BANK-207-015 — Poison Dart Frogs and Batrachotoxin: How Ingesting Ants Yields Deadly Alkaloids

**Video question:** Why are poison dart frogs raised in captivity completely harmless, while wild frogs in Colombia carry enough poison to kill 10 humans?

**Individual coverage scope:** Dendrobatid dietary sequestration of lipophilic batrachotoxins and pumiliotoxins from formicine ants and mites, aposematic warning coloration, modified sodium channels preventing self-poisoning, and blowgun darts.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

**Individual coverage scope:** Hollow internode stem chambers (domatia), specialized trichilia pads producing glycogen-rich Müllerian bodies as ant food, aggressive ant swarming biting encroaching lianas, and mutualistic defense.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.
