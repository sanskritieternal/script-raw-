## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Aircraft Engineering across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Aircraft Engineering Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Aircraft Engineering.
- **Tier 2: Aircraft Engineering Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Aircraft Engineering.
- **Tier 3: Aircraft Engineering Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Aircraft Engineering.
- **Tier 4: Aircraft Engineering Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Aircraft Engineering.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Aircraft Engineering: A Design Requirement Takes Shape**
   - *Question:* Which route, payload, or safety requirement shaped one aircraft?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated design and certification records. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
2. **Aircraft Engineering: A Famous Aircraft Reassessed**
   - *Question:* Which design choices explain a named aircraft's role?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare production versions and operating evidence. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
3. **Aircraft Engineering: Workers behind Flight Test**
   - *Question:* Which test, maintenance, or manufacturing contribution is overlooked?
   - *Key Focus:* Use documented personnel and program records. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
4. **Aircraft Engineering: Lift, Thrust, and Control**
   - *Question:* How do these systems interact in one phase of flight?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a specified aircraft and conditions. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
5. **Aircraft Engineering: Aeroelastic Design Limit**
   - *Question:* What evidence sets an aircraft's safe operating envelope?
   - *Key Focus:* Read analysis, ground tests, and flight tests. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Aircraft Engineering: An Unresolved Technical Event**

10. **Aircraft Engineering: Flight Control Chain**

   - *Key Focus:* Follow sensor, control law, actuator, and feedback. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Aircraft Engineering: An Aircraft Type Retired**
   - *Question:* Which economics, maintenance, or rule changes explain withdrawal?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish fleet and model-specific evidence. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
12. **Aircraft Engineering: Two Aircraft Generations**
   - *Question:* Which efficiency and safety measures can be fairly compared?
   - *Key Focus:* Align payload, route, test basis, and period. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
13. **Aircraft Engineering: A Proposed Aircraft Design**
   - *Question:* What ground and flight evidence would support deployment?

14. **Aircraft Engineering: A Claimed Fuel Saving**
   - *Question:* Does a published percentage hold for an actual mission?
   - *Key Focus:* State load, distance, weather, and baseline. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
15. **Aircraft Engineering: Prototype to Service**
   - *Question:* How did test findings change one aircraft before delivery?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated test, redesign, approval, and service records. Evidence base: FAA certification and airworthiness records, dated design documents, ground and flight tests, accident investigations, operator manuals, and service records.
