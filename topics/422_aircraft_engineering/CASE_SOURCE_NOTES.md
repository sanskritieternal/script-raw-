# Selected primary-source note — aircraft engineering

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-422-047`.

- [NASA, *X-29 Advanced Technology Demonstrator Aircraft*](https://www.nasa.gov/aeronautics/nasa-aircraft/x-29-demonstrator/): NASA program history describes the forward-swept, aeroelastically tailored composite wing, variable-camber surfaces and triple-redundant digital fly-by-wire system. It reports Phase 1 observations of no wing structural divergence within the flight envelope and Phase 2 high-angle-of-attack research.
- [NASA Technical Reports Server, *Flight tests confirm X-29 technologies* (1988)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19890061478): indexed NASA technical report record states test flights evaluated forward sweep, digital controls, composite wing skin and three-surface pitch control. The short abstract was inspected; full report figures and detailed data were not reviewed here.

These NASA accounts concern a research demonstrator and particular flight phases, not type certification, routine fleet use, all forward-swept wings or guaranteed efficiency. Program-page narrative needs report-level data checking for quantitative claims. This is documentary engineering analysis, not flight-control design or operating guidance.
