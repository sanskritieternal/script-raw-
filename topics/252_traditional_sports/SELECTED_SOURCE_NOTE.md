# Selected primary-source note: traditional sports

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed case BANK-252-040.

The International Federation of Basque Pelota's documentation page lists general game rules, Frontball rules, equipment-approval regulations, approved makes/models, and installation regulations: https://www.fipv.net/en/documentacion . This is direct evidence of the federation's own sanctioned rule framework and a tractable comparison of equipment and venue standards. A script should open and date the specific rule/model documents and identify the modality and revision before asserting an actual change. The landing page alone does not establish when a local game changed or that every regional pelota form follows FIPV rules. Do not reproduce full rulebooks or imagery without checking rights.
