## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Traditional Sports across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Traditional Sports Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Traditional Sports.
- **Tier 2: Traditional Sports Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Traditional Sports.
- **Tier 3: Traditional Sports Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Traditional Sports.
- **Tier 4: Traditional Sports Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Traditional Sports.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Traditional Sports: The Early Record of a Sport**
   - *Question:* What dated evidence describes one community sport?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate first surviving account from invention. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
2. **Traditional Sports: A Celebrated Match in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one well-known event reveal about actual play?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare rules, competitors, setting, and later retellings. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
3. **Traditional Sports: Who Sustains the Sport**
   - *Question:* Which coaches, makers, officials, and volunteers are overlooked?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit work and community organizing with consent. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
4. **Traditional Sports: How One Local Sport Works**
   - *Question:* What rules and aims must a newcomer understand?
   - *Key Focus:* Name place, governing body, equipment, and variant. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
5. **Traditional Sports: Tradition and Standard Rules**
   - *Question:* What changes when local play becomes a formal competition?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare rule sets, access, training, and community meaning. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Traditional Sports: A Disputed Sport Origin**
   - *Question:* Which routes of exchange are supported by evidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated descriptions and avoid origin by resemblance. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
7. **Traditional Sports: Equipment Change Tested**
   - *Question:* Did a new material alter performance or access?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare specifications, cost, safety, and player accounts. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
8. **Traditional Sports: An Athlete-Teacher's Contribution**
   - *Question:* How did one practitioner transmit skills?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated records and credit a wider teaching network. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
9. **Traditional Sports: A Rule Change or Venue Loss**
   - *Question:* How did one event alter participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dates, membership, access, and unintended effects. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
10. **Traditional Sports: A Match from Start to Finish**
   - *Question:* How are turns, scoring, officials, and disputes handled?
   - *Key Focus:* Document a named version and current rule source. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Traditional Sports: Participation Over Time**
   - *Question:* What explains a claimed revival or decline?
   - *Key Focus:* Check counting, informal play, facilities, and funding. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
12. **Traditional Sports: The Sport Across Two Eras**
   - *Question:* How did rules, equipment, and participation change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable sources and distinguish practice from promotion. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
13. **Traditional Sports: Paths for Continued Play**
   - *Question:* Which resources do participants prioritize?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare space, coaching, equipment, safety, and governance. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
14. **Traditional Sports: An Unchanged Ancient Sport?**
   - *Question:* Which features are documented early and which came later?
   - *Key Focus:* Date rules, objects, and records without authenticity ranking. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
15. **Traditional Sports: One Competition Day**
   - *Question:* How do players and organizers make an event work?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented observation and sourced event records. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Traditional Sports: Where Play Actually Happens**
   - *Question:* Does the official venue map reflect usable access?
   - *Key Focus:* Check grounds, transport, facilities, and current permissions. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
17. **Traditional Sports: Reaching Practice**
   - *Question:* How does travel affect who can participate?

   - *Question:* How do weather and calendars shape play?
   - *Key Focus:* Use local schedules and dated conditions. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
19. **Traditional Sports: Players and Neighbors**
   - *Question:* How do communities value or contest a play space?
   - *Key Focus:* Seek varied consented views and land-use context. Evidence base: dated rulebooks and event records, venue documents, participation data, equipment provenance, and consented player and organizer accounts.
