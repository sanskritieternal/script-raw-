## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Personal Security across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Personal Security Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Personal Security.
- **Tier 2: Personal Security Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Personal Security.
- **Tier 3: Personal Security Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Personal Security.
- **Tier 4: Personal Security Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Personal Security.
