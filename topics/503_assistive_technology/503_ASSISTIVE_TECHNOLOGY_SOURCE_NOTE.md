# Assistive technology source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-503-043 - NLS Braille eReader rollout

- Primary operator record: U.S. Library of Congress, National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled, *News*, October-December 2024, https://www.loc.gov/nls/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/News-October-December-2024-web.pdf (inspected 2026-10-24). NLS reports completing nationwide Braille eReader rollout, distributing refreshable displays through network libraries in every state and territory, training staff for patron support, and approximately 8,500 devices with patrons and libraries by the end of FY2024. This is an operator-reported implementation count, not an independent reading-outcome study.
- Primary service and device documentation: Library of Congress NLS, *Equipment for NLS Materials*, https://www.loc.gov/nls/services-and-resources/equipment-for-nls-materials/ (inspected 2026-10-24). It lists eReader equipment and user guides; NLS describes loans and network-library support. The HumanWare guide https://www.loc.gov/nls/services-and-resources/equipment-for-nls-materials/nls-braille-ereader-humanware-user-guide/ describes one model's features and workflow.
- Access and outcome limits: This is a U.S. eligibility-based library service, not evidence that every blind or print-disabled reader can receive a device in another jurisdiction. Counts combine patrons and network libraries and do not establish independent use, comprehension, literacy, satisfaction or repair continuity. Device models and distribution status can change; verify current NLS program details before a script. Do not present a product feature as proof of personal benefit or clinical rehabilitation.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-503-043 covers a named assistive-service rollout and support chain. BANK-502-038 concerns rehabilitation equipment access and durable function; BANK-489-037 concerns augmentation cost and repair generally.
