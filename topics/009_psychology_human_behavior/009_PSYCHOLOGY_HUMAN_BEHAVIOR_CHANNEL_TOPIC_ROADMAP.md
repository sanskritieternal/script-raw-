## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes cognitive biases, neurobiology, social conformity, emotional resilience, relationship dynamics, and behavioral economics across four progressive learning tiers:
- **Tier 1: Foundations of the Mind & Cognitive Biases (Scratch / Beginner)** — System 1 vs System 2 thinking, confirmation bias, fundamental attribution error, the replication crisis in psychology, and neuroplasticity basics.
- **Tier 2: Social Psychology, Influence & Dark Patterns (Intermediate)** — Group conformity (Asch experiments), obedience to authority (Milgram), bystander apathy, social proof, and manipulative digital persuasion.
- **Tier 3: Emotions, Trauma & Mental Health Science (Upper Intermediate)** — The neuroscience of anxiety and the amygdala, attachment styles in relationships, habit formation loops (dopamine circuits), and cognitive reframing (CBT).
- **Tier 4: Advanced Behavioral Economics, Culture & Evolution (Advanced / Expert)** — Dual-process decision architecture, behavioral nudges, evolutionary psychology trade-offs, cross-cultural WEIRD bias, and the psychology of extreme belief systems.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundations of the Mind & Cognitive Biases
1. **Thinking, Fast and Slow: How Your Two Brain Systems Battle Every Day**
   - *Question:* Why does System 1 make instant impulsive errors while System 2 acts lazy and distracted?
   - *Key Focus:* Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky, cognitive heuristics, availability bias, mental energy conservation.
2. **The Confirmation Bias Trap: Why Smart People Believe Stupid Things**
   - *Question:* How does the brain actively discard contradictory facts to protect existing beliefs and ego?
   - *Key Focus:* Identity-protective cognition, echo chambers, motivated reasoning, scientific falsification habits.
3. **The Dunning-Kruger Effect: Why the Least Competent Are the Most Confident**
   - *Question:* How does a lack of skill rob someone of the exact meta-cognitive ability to judge their own skill?
   - *Key Focus:* The original 1999 Cornell study, the peak of 'Mount Stupid', the slope of enlightenment, intellectual humility.
4. **The Replication Crisis: Why Half of Psychology Textbooks Might Be Wrong**
   - *Question:* How did p-hacking, publication bias, and small sample sizes corrupt decades of popular psychology?
   - *Key Focus:* Open Science Collaboration, failed replications of social priming, power posing, preregistered studies.

### Tier 2: Social Psychology, Influence & Dark Patterns
5. **The Milgram Experiment Revisited: Would Normal People Shock a Stranger to Death?**
   - *Question:* What do the original Yale audio recordings reveal about obedience to institutional authority?
   - *Key Focus:* The agentic state, gradual escalation (the foot-in-the-door), ethical fallout, modern replications.
6. **The Asch Conformity Experiments: Why We Deny the Evidence of Our Own Eyes**
   - *Question:* Why will 75% of people deliberately give an obviously wrong answer just to fit in with the group?
   - *Key Focus:* Normative vs informational conformity, the power of a single dissenter, peer pressure biology.
7. **The Bystander Effect: Why Crowds Freeze During Emergencies**
   - *Question:* Why are you mathematically safer collapsing in front of one person than in front of a crowd of 30?
   - *Key Focus:* The Kitty Genovese reality vs myth, diffusion of responsibility, pluralistic ignorance, active intervention training.
8. **Dark Patterns & Social Engineering: How Tech Apps Hijack Your Dopamine Circuits**
   - *Question:* How do casino slot machine mechanics (variable ratio schedules) keep users scrolling for hours?
   - *Key Focus:* B.F. Skinner operant conditioning, infinite scroll, red notification dots, friction design.

### Tier 3: Emotions, Trauma & Mental Health Science
9. **Attachment Theory: How Childhood Bonds Dictate Your Adult Relationships**
   - *Question:* How do secure, anxious, avoidant, and disorganized styles shape relationship communication?
   - *Key Focus:* John Bowlby & Mary Ainsworth strange situation, adult attachment dynamics, earned security.
10. **The Amygdala Hijack: The Neuroscience of Anxiety, Panic, and Rage**
    - *Question:* Why does your brain's alarm system shut down logical prefrontal thinking in fractions of a second?
    - *Key Focus:* Sympathetic nervous system, cortisol and adrenaline spikes, vagal nerve stimulation, physiological sighs.
11. **Habit Loops: The Neuroscience of Making and Breaking Any Behavior**
    - *Question:* How do cue, craving, response, and reward hardwire automatic basal ganglia pathways in the brain?
    - *Key Focus:* Charles Duhigg & James Clear frameworks, habit stacking, friction reduction, replacement strategies.
12. **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): How to Rewire Negative Automatic Thoughts**
    - *Question:* How does challenging catastrophic thinking and cognitive distortions break chronic depression cycles?
    - *Key Focus:* Aaron Beck framework, catastrophizing, mind reading, thought records, behavioral activation.

### Tier 4: Advanced Behavioral Economics, Culture & Evolution
13. **Loss Aversion: Why Losing $100 Hurts Twice as Much as Gaining $100 Feels Good**
    - *Question:* How does evolutionary survival program humans to avoid downside risks at all costs?
    - *Key Focus:* Prospect Theory, sunk cost fallacy, endowment effect, status quo bias in investing and politics.
14. **The WEIRD Bias: Why Modern Psychology Doesn't Apply to Most of the World**
    - *Question:* Why do Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, Democratic subjects perceive optical illusions differently?
    - *Key Focus:* Joseph Henrich's research, Mueller-Lyer illusion cross-cultural tests, relational vs analytic thinking.
15. **The Lucifer Effect: Philip Zimbardo & The Truth About the Stanford Prison Experiment**
    - *Question:* Was the 1971 basement experiment a revelation of human evil or a staged theatrical fraud?
    - *Key Focus:* Thibault Le Texier's archival investigation, demand characteristics, experimenter coaching, guard coaching.
16. **The Psychology of Charisma & Cult Leadership: How Demagogues Control Minds**
    - *Question:* What psychological vulnerabilities make intelligent individuals surrender their critical thinking?
    - *Key Focus:* Love bombing, isolation tactics, cognitive dissonance resolution, us-vs-them tribalism.
17. **The Neuroscience of Empathy: Mirror Neurons, Compassion Fatigue, and Psychopathy**
    - *Question:* How do mirror neuron networks allow us to feel another person's physical and emotional pain?
    - *Key Focus:* Cognitive vs affective empathy, dark triad traits, burnout in healthcare workers, neuroplastic training.
18. **The Hedonic Treadmill: Why Winning the Lottery Doesn't Make You Permanently Happy**
    - *Question:* How does human psychological adaptation drag our baseline happiness back to normal after life events?
    - *Key Focus:* Brickman lottery study, hedonic adaptation, synthetic happiness (Dan Gilbert), daily gratitude practices.
19. **Nudge Theory: How Governments Silently Steer Citizen Behavior Without Mandates**
    - *Question:* How did Richard Thaler & Cass Sunstein prove that choice architecture changes public decisions?
    - *Key Focus:* Opt-in vs opt-out defaults, social proof nudges, cafeteria layout choices, ethical boundaries.
20. **The Psychology of Loneliness: The Hidden Biological Epidemic of Modern Life**
    - *Question:* Why does chronic social isolation trigger the same physiological alarm states as physical starvation?
    - *Key Focus:* John Cacioppo evolutionary theory of loneliness, systemic inflammation, neurobiology of connection.
