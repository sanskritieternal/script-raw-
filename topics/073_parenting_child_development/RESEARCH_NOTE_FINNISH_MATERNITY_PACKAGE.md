# Finnish maternity package: bounded policy and safety note

Linked proposal: [BANK-073-059](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-073-059). Research checked 2026-10-24. This is a historical and policy source trail, not infant-care advice or evidence that a cardboard box caused Finland's mortality decline.

## Primary records

Finland's social-insurance institution Kela says in its [maternity-grant history](https://www.kela.fi/maternity-grant-history) that a 1937 law preceded grants in 1938, initially for lower-income families; eligibility broadened in 1949. Kela's [maternity-package FAQ](https://www.kela.fi/maternitypackage-faq) explains the choice between a package and a cash benefit and describes the health-examination condition. [Statistics Finland's historical infant-mortality series](https://stat.fi/til/ksyyt/2010/ksyyt_2010_2011-12-16_kat_007_en.html) documents a long-run decline. These sources establish program history and national outcomes separately; they do not identify the box's causal share in that decline.

## Episode checks

1. Distinguish the **maternity grant**, the optional package, the cardboard container and contact with antenatal services. They are not one exposure.
2. Compare the year, eligible population and infant-mortality definition before plotting trends. Other maternal and child-health changes need consideration before attributing mortality change.
3. Do not present the package as a proven safe-sleep device. The [American Academy of Pediatrics' evidence review](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/150/1/e2022057991/188305/Evidence-Base-for-2022-Updated-Recommendations-for) says the evidence about baby boxes does not isolate box effects from accompanying education and discusses product-standard issues. [CDC's infant-sleep guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/sudden-infant-death/sleep-safely/) applies to US safety advice; local clinical and product guidance must be checked for any other jurisdiction.

## Limits

The note supports a bounded public-policy episode, not a claim that families should use a box for sleep or change an infant's care. It does not isolate a treatment effect or address individual medical needs. The active BANK proposal remains proposed and source-unverified beyond this selected source access.
