## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Bhagavad Gita: The Gita in Its Epic Setting**
   - *Question:* How does the Bhagavad Gita function within the Bhīṣma Parvan of the Mahābhārata?
   - *Key Focus:* Read the surrounding narrative and distinguish textual setting from dating claims. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
2. **Bhagavad Gita: A Famous Verse in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one widely quoted Gita verse say in its full exchange?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare Sanskrit, surrounding verses, and named commentaries. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
3. **Bhagavad Gita: A Reader Beyond the Canonical Commentators**
   - *Question:* Whose interpretation of a Gita passage is missing from a familiar account?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated translations, teaching records, and consented contemporary voices. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
4. **Bhagavad Gita: Arjuna's Question and Krishna's Reply**
   - *Question:* What problem does one chapter address, and how does the dialogue develop?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow the named chapter without reducing the whole text to one instruction. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
5. **Bhagavad Gita: Action, Knowledge, and Devotion**
   - *Question:* How do named commentators relate two paths described in the Gita?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare precise passages and attribute philosophical differences. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Bhagavad Gita: A Disputed Verse Reading**
   - *Question:* Which textual or translation choice remains contested in one verse?
   - *Key Focus:* Check editions, manuscripts, grammar, and commentarial history. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
7. **Bhagavad Gita: Digital Comparison of Gita Commentaries**
   - *Question:* Can aligned texts reveal how readings diverge?
   - *Key Focus:* Verify edition provenance, verse numbering, and limits of automated matching. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
8. **Bhagavad Gita: A Commentator's Gita**
   - *Question:* What did one named commentator argue about a specific passage?
   - *Key Focus:* Read primary commentary and later reception separately. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
9. **Bhagavad Gita: A Translation Shapes Reception**
   - *Question:* How did one dated translation change public use of the Gita?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace publication, audience, criticism, and later editions. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
10. **Bhagavad Gita: Reading a Gita Argument**
   - *Question:* How does one exchange move from objection to answer?
   - *Key Focus:* Map speaker turns, key Sanskrit terms, and rhetorical setting. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Bhagavad Gita: A Reading's Changing Influence**
   - *Question:* Why did one interpretation gain or lose prominence in a named setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare institutions, editions, teaching, and historical context. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
12. **Bhagavad Gita: One Verse Across Centuries**
   - *Question:* How did a classical commentary and a present community read the same verse?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute both readings and avoid assuming uninterrupted sameness. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
13. **Bhagavad Gita: An Open Translation Question**
   - *Question:* Which source or study could clarify a disputed Gita rendering?
   - *Key Focus:* State needed evidence without predicting doctrinal agreement. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
14. **Bhagavad Gita: A Single Message of the Gita?**
   - *Question:* Does a popular slogan fit the dialogue and its diverse commentaries?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare bounded passages, not an isolated line. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
15. **Bhagavad Gita: A Gita Passage Through Editions**
   - *Question:* How did one verse move through manuscript, print, and translation?
   - *Key Focus:* Track edition choices, numbering, and reception. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Bhagavad Gita: Locating a Gita Claim**
   - *Question:* Where exactly does one claimed teaching appear in the text?
   - *Key Focus:* Cite chapter, verse, edition, speaker, and neighboring passage. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
17. **Bhagavad Gita: Two Lineages, One Verse**
   - *Question:* How do two named traditions disagree over a verse?
   - *Key Focus:* Present each argument from its own commentary. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
18. **Bhagavad Gita: The Gita in a Community's Life**
   - *Question:* How is the text studied or recited in a specific setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented participant accounts and identify local variation. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
19. **Bhagavad Gita: The Gita in an Observance**
   - *Question:* Where does a selected passage enter one documented observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe local practice with permission; do not prescribe it. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
20. **Bhagavad Gita: A Term with Several Readings**
   - *Question:* How does translating one Sanskrit word alter a Gita argument?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare syntax, context, and major commentarial choices. Evidence base: specified Sanskrit editions and manuscript witnesses, named commentaries and translations, publication records, and consented community accounts.
