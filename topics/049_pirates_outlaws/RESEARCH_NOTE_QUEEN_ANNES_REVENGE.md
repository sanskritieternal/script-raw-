# Beaufort Inlet wreck: bounded source-access note

Linked proposal: [BANK-049-066](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-049-066). Research checked 2026-10-24. This is a traceable source trail for one case, not an episode claim ledger or a bank-wide source-verification claim.

## Archaeological record

North Carolina's [Queen Anne's Revenge Project discovery account](https://www.qaronline.org/history/discovery-shipwreck) identifies the Beaufort Inlet site as **31CR314**. It reports cannon, anchors and a bronze bell dated 1705 among the evidence considered in identifying the wreck. The state's [site history](https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2026/04/15/queen-annes-revenge-c-86) dates the grounding of Blackbeard's ship to June 1718 and describes the later archaeological investigation. The project's [management summary](https://www.qaronline.org/html/management-plan-for-north-carolina-shipwreck-31cr314-queen-anne-s-revenge) explains why location, assemblage and lack of a better candidate support the identification.

## Episode checks

1. Compare the project's artifact inventory and excavation context with the contemporary account of the grounding. Give the site number and recovery context for any artifact shown on screen.
2. Distinguish evidence for the **ship's identity** from evidence for Blackbeard's biography or behavior. Artifacts at the wreck cannot independently prove the smoking-match legend.
3. Include the ship's prior identity as the French slave ship *La Concorde* when narrating its history; the project's [ship-journey record](https://www.qaronline.org/history/ships-journey) traces that earlier use.
4. Treat “sunken treasure” as a separate claim requiring documented cargo and custody evidence. The neighboring [treasure-legends question](../050_treasure_hunting/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-050-031) is its primary home.

## Limits

The project and state records support case selection, site identification and a research path. A full episode still needs the detailed excavation reports, primary eighteenth-century documents, artifact-image permissions and provenance checks. The site does not establish an undiscovered treasure cache.
