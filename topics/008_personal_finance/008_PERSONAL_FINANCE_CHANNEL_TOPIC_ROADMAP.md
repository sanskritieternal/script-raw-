## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes practical cash flow management, debt elimination, index fund investing, tax efficiency, and long-term retirement planning across four sequential learning tiers:
- **Tier 1: Money Foundations & Cash Flow Mastery (Scratch / Beginner)** — Budgeting systems (50/30/20 vs zero-based), high-yield savings accounts, building an emergency cushion, credit card reward mechanics, and credit score psychology.
- **Tier 2: Debt Elimination, Banking & Protection (Intermediate)** — Snowball vs Avalanche debt strategies, student loans, mortgage interest math, avoiding predatory hidden fees, and insurance essentials.
- **Tier 3: Wealth Building, Investing & Tax Optimization (Upper Intermediate)** — Index funds (S&P 500, total world), tax-advantaged accounts (Roth IRA, 401k, HSA), dollar-cost averaging, asset allocation, and fee drag.
- **Tier 4: Financial Independence, Real Estate & Legacy (Advanced / Expert)** — The FIRE movement (Financial Independence Retire Early), 4% safe withdrawal rate, rental property cash flow analysis, trust/estate planning, and market crash survival.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Money Foundations & Cash Flow Mastery
1. **The Step-by-Step Money Map: How to Organize Your Finances from Scratch**
   - *Question:* How can anyone take control of their money with a 4-account financial automation system?
   - *Key Focus:* Checking, high-yield savings (HYSA), investing, sinking funds, paycheck automation.
2. **The 50/30/20 Rule Tested: Does It Still Work with Today's Housing Prices?**
   - *Question:* How can households adapt the famous needs/wants/savings split when rent takes 45% of income?
   - *Key Focus:* Adjusting ratio tiers, fixed cost audits, trimming subscriptions, guilt-free spending money.
3. **Emergency Funds: Exactly How Much Cash to Keep and Where to Store It**
   - *Question:* Why keeping 3–6 months of expenses in a checking account loses money to inflation?
   - *Key Focus:* High-yield savings accounts, money market funds, Treasury bills (T-bills), liquidity vs return.
4. **Credit Cards for Beginners: How to Build an 800+ Credit Score Without Paying Interest**
   - *Question:* How do credit utilization, on-time payments, and credit age combine to determine your FICO score?
   - *Key Focus:* Statement balance vs current balance, autopay rules, avoiding interest fees, credit line increases.

### Tier 2: Debt Elimination, Banking & Protection
5. **Debt Avalanche vs Debt Snowball: The Math vs The Psychology**
   - *Question:* Is it better to pay highest interest first or knock out smallest balances for quick emotional wins?
   - *Key Focus:* Mathematical interest savings vs behavioral momentum, negotiating lower credit APRs.
6. **Rent vs Buy: The Honest Math Nobody Tells You About Homeownership**
   - *Question:* Does renting really 'throw money away' when property taxes, maintenance, and insurance are counted?
   - *Key Focus:* 5% rule of thumb, opportunity cost of down payments, unrecoverable costs of owning.
7. **The Silent Killer: How Hidden Banking & Investment Fees Steal Your Wealth**
   - *Question:* How do 401k administrative fees, mutual fund expense ratios, and ATM charges compound over time?
   - *Key Focus:* Expense ratios (0.03% vs 1.2%), fee audits, low-cost index funds, fee-only fiduciary advisors.
8. **Insurance You Actually Need (And 5 Policies That Are a Total Waste of Money)**
   - *Question:* Why is term life insurance 10x better than whole life insurance for 99% of families?
   - *Key Focus:* Term life vs whole life, disability insurance, umbrella insurance, avoiding gimmick policies.

### Tier 3: Wealth Building, Investing & Tax Optimization
9. **Index Funds Explained: How to Own the Entire Global Economy for $10**
   - *Question:* Why do 90% of professional Wall Street stock pickers fail to beat a simple S&P 500 index fund?
   - *Key Focus:* S&P 500 (VOO/SPY), Total Stock Market (VTI), international diversification (VXUS), compounding math.
10. **The Roth IRA Masterclass: The Ultimate Tax-Free Wealth Building Machine**
    - *Question:* How can tax-free growth and tax-free withdrawals create millions in retirement?
    - *Key Focus:* Contribution limits, income caps, Backdoor Roth IRA mechanics, penalty-free principal withdrawals.
11. **The 401(k) Blueprint: How to Maximize the Employer Match and Optimize Investments**
    - *Question:* Why is skipping an employer 401k match the equivalent of turning down a 100% immediate raise?
    - *Key Focus:* Traditional vs Roth 401k, vesting schedules, target-date retirement funds, rolling over old 401ks.
12. **The HSA (Health Savings Account): The Triple-Tax-Advantaged Secret Weapon**
    - *Question:* How does an HSA provide tax deduction on entry, tax-free growth, and tax-free medical withdrawals?
    - *Key Focus:* High-deductible health plans (HDHP), investing HSA funds in index funds, the receipt reimbursement hack.

### Tier 4: Financial Independence, Real Estate & Legacy
13. **The FIRE Movement: How to Retire in Your 30s or 40s (Financial Independence Retire Early)**
    - *Question:* How do LeanFIRE, BaristaFIRE, and FatFIRE calculate target net worth based on annual spending?
    - *Key Focus:* 25x annual spending rule, high savings rates (50%+), lifestyle design, post-retirement healthcare.
14. **The 4% Rule: Can You Really Live Off Your Portfolio Forever Without Running Out?**
    - *Question:* What did the landmark Trinity Study prove about safe withdrawal rates through market crashes?
    - *Key Focus:* Sequence of returns risk, bond tents, dynamic withdrawal strategies, historical failure rates.
15. **Real Estate Investing 101: Cash Flow, Cap Rates, and the 1% Rule**
    - *Question:* How can individual investors analyze rental property cash flow without getting trapped by repairs?
    - *Key Focus:* Gross vs net yield, property management costs, mortgage amortization, depreciation tax shields.
16. **How to Survive a 40% Stock Market Crash: The Psychology of Bear Markets**
    - *Question:* Why is panic-selling during a recession the single greatest destroyer of middle-class wealth?
    - *Key Focus:* Dollar-cost averaging through dips, historical recovery durations, staying invested, asset rebalancing.
17. **Taxes in Retirement: How to Withdraw Money Strategically to Pay 0% Taxes**
    - *Question:* How do savvy retirees sequence taxable, tax-deferred, and tax-free accounts to minimize bracket jumps?
    - *Key Focus:* Bracket filling, Roth conversions in low-income years, required minimum distributions (RMDs).
18. **Estate Planning for Normal People: Wills, Trusts, and Power of Attorney**
    - *Question:* What happens to your bank accounts, home, and children if you pass away without estate paperwork?
    - *Key Focus:* Avoiding probate court, revocable living trusts, beneficiary designations, healthcare directives.
19. **How to Talk About Money with Your Partner: Ending Financial Fights Forever**
    - *Question:* How can couples combine or separate finances fairly when one earns significantly more?
    - *Key Focus:* 'Yours, Mine, and Ours' joint account system, monthly money dates, aligning financial values.
20. **The Psychology of Money: Why Wealth Is What You Don't See**
    - *Question:* How do status signaling, lifestyle creep, and fear dictate human financial happiness?
    - *Key Focus:* Morgan Housel principles, spending money to buy freedom/time, redefining true wealth.
