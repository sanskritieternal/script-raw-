## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Genetics / Evolution across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Mendelian Inheritance & Molecular Genetics (Scratch / Beginner)** — DNA base pairing, transcription/translation, Mendelian Punnett squares, and the architecture of the human genome.
- **Tier 2: Natural Selection, Speciation & Deep Evolutionary Time (Intermediate)** — Darwinian fitness, genetic drift vs gene flow, allopatric speciation, and the Cambrian Explosion fossil record.
- **Tier 3: Human Origins, Neanderthal Introgression & Epigenetics (Upper Intermediate)** — Out-of-Africa hominin migrations, Svante Pääbo ancient DNA paleogenomics, Neanderthal/Denisovan heritage, and histones.
- **Tier 4: CRISPR Gene Editing, Synthetic Genomes & Future Human Evolution (Advanced / Expert)** — Base editing vs prime editing, de-extinction genetics (woolly mammoth/dodo), synthetic life (JCVI-syn3.0), and germline ethics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Mendelian Inheritance & Molecular Genetics
1. **The Double Helix Revolution: How Watson, Crick, and Rosalind Franklin Cracked the Code of Life**
   - *Question:* How did X-ray diffraction Photo 51 reveal the antiparallel sugar-phosphate backbone and base-pairing that underpins all life?
   - *Key Focus:* Adenine-Thymine and Guanine-Cytosine hydrogen bonding, Chargaff's rules, copying mechanism of inheritance.
2. **From Gene to Protein: The Central Dogma of Molecular Biology**
   - *Question:* How does RNA polymerase read 3 billion base pairs to synthesize proteins folding into functional molecular machines?
   - *Key Focus:* Introns and exons splicing, codon triplets, tRNA anticodons, Christian Anfinsen protein folding thermodynamics.
3. **Gregor Mendel's Pea Plants: How an Austrian Monk Discovered Heredity in a Garden**
   - *Question:* Why did Mendel's 29,000 pea plant crosses disprove 'blended inheritance' and sit ignored for 35 years before revolutionizing science?
   - *Key Focus:* Dominant vs recessive alleles, Law of Segregation, Punnett squares, rediscovery by De Vries and Correns in 1900.
4. **Mutations: The Raw Fuel of Evolution and the Engine of Diversity**
   - *Question:* How do cosmic rays, replication copy errors, and chemical mutagens create both fatal diseases and revolutionary adaptations?
   - *Key Focus:* Silent vs missense vs nonsense mutations, frame-shifts, sickle cell resistance to malaria as heterozygote advantage.
### Tier 2: Natural Selection & Evolutionary Time
5. **The Galápagos Finches: How Beak Geometry Proved Natural Selection in Real Time**
   - *Question:* How did Peter and Rosemary Grant spend 40 years measuring millimeters on Daphne Major to prove evolution occurs before our eyes?
   - *Key Focus:* Drought selecting for heavy seeds and deep beaks, adaptive radiation from a single ancestor, Daphne Major field studies.
6. **The Cambrian Explosion: When Animals Invented Skeletons, Eyes, and Predators**
   - *Question:* What triggered the sudden appearance of nearly all major animal body plans (phyla) in the fossil record 541 million years ago?
   - *Key Focus:* Burgess Shale fossils, rise of atmospheric oxygen, invention of compound vision (trilobites), evolutionary arms race.
7. **Genetic Drift vs Natural Selection: The Power of Random Luck in Evolution**
   - *Question:* Why does random chance sometimes matter more than survival of the fittest in small isolated populations?
   - *Key Focus:* Sewall Wright's genetic drift, Founder Effect, cheetah population bottleneck leading to near-identical skin grafts.
8. **The Selfish Gene: How Richard Dawkins Inverted Our View of Altruism**
   - *Question:* Why are organisms merely temporary survival machines built by immortal genes to ensure their own replication?
   - *Key Focus:* W.D. Hamilton's rule (rB > C), kin selection in ant colonies, evolutionary game theory, the origin of the word 'meme'.
### Tier 3: Human Origins, Neanderthals & Epigenetics
9. **Neanderthals Within Us: Svante Pääbo and the Ancient Genome Revolution**
   - *Question:* How did recovering fragmented DNA from a 40,000-year-old bone prove that modern non-African humans carry 1–2% Neanderthal DNA?
   - *Key Focus:* 2022 Nobel Prize, contamination elimination protocols, high-altitude adaptation from Denisovans (EPAS1 in Tibetans).
10. **The Mitochondrial Eve: Tracing All Living Humans to an African Mother 150,000 Years Ago**
   - *Question:* How does maternally inherited mitochondrial DNA act as a steady molecular clock tracing all 8 billion humans to a single maternal lineage?
   - *Key Focus:* Allan Wilson and Rebecca Cann (1987), Y-chromosomal Adam, Out-of-Africa migration waves, debunking race as biological taxon.
11. **Epigenetics: How Trauma, Diet, and Environment Leave Chemical Tags on DNA**
   - *Question:* Can your grandmother's starvation permanently alter your metabolic health without changing a single letter of your DNA code?
   - *Key Focus:* The Dutch Hunger Winter (1944–45), CpG island methylation, agouti mice coat color experiments, transgenerational inheritance.
12. **Junk DNA Deconstructed: What 98% of the Non-Coding Genome Actually Does**
   - *Question:* Why does only 1.5% of human DNA code for proteins, and what are the remaining 3 billion letters doing?
   - *Key Focus:* ENCODE project controversy, Barbara McClintock's jumping genes (transposons), long non-coding RNAs, ancient viral retro-elements.
### Tier 4: CRISPR, Synthetic Genomes & Future Evolution
13. **CRISPR-Cas9: How a Bacterial Immune System Became a Molecular Word Processor**
   - *Question:* How did Jennifer Doudna and Emmanuelle Charpentier harness bacterial scissors to cut and edit any DNA sequence in any living species?
   - *Key Focus:* Guide RNA design, PAM sequence recognition, double-strand breaks, non-homologous end joining vs HDR, 2020 Nobel Prize.
14. **Base and Prime Editing: The Surgery That Fixes Single Letters Without DNA Double Breaks**
   - *Question:* How did David Liu's lab create 'search-and-replace' gene editors that convert C-to-T or A-to-G without breaking chromosomes?
   - *Key Focus:* Deaminase fusions, prime editing guide RNAs (pegRNAs), reverse transcriptase fusions, eliminating off-target insertions.
15. **De-Extinction: Can We Bring Back the Woolly Mammoth and the Passenger Pigeon?**
   - *Question:* Is Colossal Biosciences truly resurrecting the woolly mammoth, or are they genetically engineering cold-tolerant Asian elephants?
   - *Key Focus:* Permafrost ancient DNA extraction, elephant artificial wombs, tundra Pleistocene park re-wilding, ethical ecological concerns.
16. **Synthetic Life: Craig Venter and the Creation of the First Cell with a Synthetic Genome**
   - *Question:* How did scientists synthesize 1.08 million base pairs of chemical DNA from bottles of reagents and boot up a living bacterium?
   - *Key Focus:* Mycoplasma mycoides JCVI-syn1.0, watermarking poetry in the genetic code, stripped-down minimal genome JCVI-syn3.0 (473 genes).
17. **The He Jiankui Scandal: The First CRISPR Gene-Edited Human Babies**
   - *Question:* How did a Chinese biophysicist shock the global scientific community by editing the CCR5 gene in human embryos, and what happened to Lulu and Nana?
   - *Key Focus:* Germline editing ban violation, mosaicism dangers, off-target mutations, prison sentence, international moratorium on germline engineering.
18. **Gene Drives: The Technology That Could Eradicate Malaria or Wipe Out Entire Species**
   - *Question:* How can a genetically engineered mosquito ensure that 100% of its offspring inherit infertility genes, defying Mendelian 50% inheritance?
   - *Key Focus:* Super-Mendelian inheritance, Anopheles gambiae population suppression, ecological food-web risks, containment protocols.
19. **Are Humans Still Evolving?: Lactase Persistence, High-Altitude Living, and Wisdom Teeth**
   - *Question:* Did modern medicine stop human evolution, or are our genomes changing faster now than at any point in prehistoric history?
   - *Key Focus:* Lactase persistence in pastoralists, Bajau 'sea nomads' enlarged spleens for diving, disappearing wisdom teeth, median artery persistence.
20. **The Future of Human Speciation: Colonizing Mars and the Splitting of Humanity**
   - *Question:* What happens when isolated populations live under low gravity, high cosmic radiation, and artificial biospheres for 1,000 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Allopatric speciation mechanics, radiation-resistant radiotrophic fungi DNA, epigenetic adaptation, the emergence of Homo martis.
