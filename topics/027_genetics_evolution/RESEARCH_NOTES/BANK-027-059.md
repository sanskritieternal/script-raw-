# BANK-027-059 — Kap København sediment DNA

## Decision and question

The archived trending pitch calls the Greenland result a two-million-year-old genetic breakthrough and says it doubled a previous survival limit. The bounded episode question is what ancient environmental DNA recovered from dated sediment can reconstruct about an ecosystem, and what it cannot show about individual organisms. This is a **research note**, not production approval; `BANK-027-059` remains proposed with no verified-source flag in the catalogue.

## Inspected primary source

- Kjær et al., [“A 2-million-year-old ecosystem in Greenland uncovered by environmental DNA,” *Nature* 612, 283–291 (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05453-y), DOI 10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y. Inspected the article's abstract, site/sample methods, assignment discussion and limitations on 2026-10-24.

## Claim boundaries

- The study describes Kap København Formation deposits dated to **around** two million years ago. The team extracted DNA from 41 organic-rich sediment samples at five sites and made 65 dual-indexed libraries. This is sediment-derived environmental DNA, not a complete genome or a recovered two-million-year-old animal body.
- The authors report a mixed boreal and Arctic plant assemblage and animal assignments including mastodon-related mitochondrial reads. They say the mastodon placement depends on few informative SNPs; the local DNA signal supports a revised ecosystem reconstruction, not a filmed animal or an exact census.
- The article contrasts its result with a then-known ancient mammoth molar result, but the archived “doubled the previous age limit” line treats an empirical record as a fixed physical survival limit. Do not state a permanent age record or universal DNA preservation ceiling without a current, like-for-like review of specimen and sediment dating.
- The team's sampling and laboratory controls address contamination. Before a script claims a particular species, read the corresponding reference placement, blank controls and supplementary assignments; the paper itself notes reference and dating uncertainty.
