## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Construction across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Construction Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Construction.
- **Tier 2: Construction Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Construction.
- **Tier 3: Construction Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Construction.
- **Tier 4: Construction Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Construction.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Construction: A Building Method Adopted**
   - *Question:* Which site and labor constraints led to a method's use?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated projects and technical records. Evidence base: dated contracts and change orders, approved plans, site and inspection logs, OSHA materials, commissioning records, incident investigations, and user accounts.
2. **Construction: A Project beyond Its Image**
   - *Question:* What do contracts, inspections, and occupancy show?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare design intent with as-built records. Evidence base: dated contracts and change orders, approved plans, site and inspection logs, OSHA materials, commissioning records, incident investigations, and user accounts.
3. **Construction: Workers and Subcontractors**
   - *Question:* Whose tasks and exposure are missing from a project's story?
   - *Key Focus:* Use contracts, site logs, and worker accounts. Evidence base: dated contracts and change orders, approved plans, site and inspection logs, OSHA materials, commissioning records, incident investigations, and user accounts.
4. **Construction: Project from Ground to Handover**
   - *Question:* How do design, permits, procurement, build, and inspection connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one bounded project. Evidence base: dated contracts and change orders, approved plans, site and inspection logs, OSHA materials, commissioning records, incident investigations, and user accounts.
5. **Construction: Managing Interface Risk**
