# Selected primary-source note — construction

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-429-044`.

- [US Government Accountability Office, *Inquiry Into Change Orders and Claims for Construction of the Richard B. Russell Federal Building*, LCD-79-313 (1979)](https://www.gao.gov/assets/lcd-79-313.pdf): the federal oversight report records a 1966 prospectus, 1975 revision, 1976 contract award and an April 1979 review. It states GSA had issued 167 non-cancelled change orders by 3 April 1979; many concerned design deficiencies or changed agency needs. It describes disputed contractor claims and says delay responsibility was unresolved at the report date.

The inquiry is an interim government oversight snapshot, not the completed building’s final cost, contract liability decision or a verdict that all contractor claims were valid. GAO reports agency and contractor positions separately; no adverse claim should be collapsed into a finding of fraud or individual blame. PDF text is OCR-imperfect, so exact figures and quotations should be checked visually in the report. This is documentary project-control analysis, not contract or construction advice.
