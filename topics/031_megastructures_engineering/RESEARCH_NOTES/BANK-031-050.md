# BANK-031-050 — Three Gorges and Earth's rotation

Reviewed 2026-10-24. The preserved `pending_topics/1` pitch describes the Three Gorges Dam as a concrete titan that “literally slowed Earth's rotation.”

## Inspected primary/institutional sources

- [NASA JPL, “NASA Details Earthquake Effects on the Earth”](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-details-earthquake-effects-on-the-earth/), 10 January 2005. In an account centered on the 2004 Sumatra earthquake, NASA scientist Benjamin Fong Chao compares its mass redistribution with a **hypothetically filled** Three Gorges reservoir holding 40 cubic kilometers of water. The estimate is that such a shift would **increase the length of day by only 0.06 microseconds**. The statement is a model comparison, not a direct measurement of the dam's effect after completion.
- [USGS Earthshots, Three Gorges Dam](https://eros.usgs.gov/earthshots/three-gorges-dam-china) documents the reservoir and changing landscape using dated satellite views. It does not itself measure a change in the planet's rotation.

## Claim disposition

The archived sentence collapses a conditional calculation into a measured project consequence. A responsible episode can explain angular momentum, the model assumptions and scale of 0.06 microseconds while making clear that atmospheric, oceanic and other mass shifts also change rotation. For the engineering case, use dated dam design, operation, sediment, resettlement and hazard records separately. Do not imply the rotation estimate is a significant environmental or safety impact.
