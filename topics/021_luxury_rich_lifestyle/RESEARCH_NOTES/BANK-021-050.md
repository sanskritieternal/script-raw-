## Editorial finding

The preserved pitch asks why a Patek Philippe Grandmaster Chime “costs $31 million.” The inspected sources support a narrower statement: a **unique reference 6300A-010** sold at a **2019 charity auction for CHF 31 million**. They do not establish that the model's ordinary retail price, manufacture cost or future market value is $31 million. A script should identify the object, currency, date and auction setting in the same claim, then examine mechanism, one-off production and auction demand as separate explanations rather than assign the entire price to craftsmanship.

The existing [watch-complication question](../IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-021-025) explains what a mechanism does; this case asks how a documented **transaction** was produced and described. It also borders the [resale-price question](../IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-021-037), but one auction cannot establish a broad investment return. A Phase 1 claim review would need the actual result record, sale terms and dated currency conversion for every numerical statement. No IDEA status or production file was changed.
