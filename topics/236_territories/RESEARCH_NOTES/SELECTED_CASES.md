## BANK-236-039 — New Caledonia referendum electorate

- The [UN 2022 working paper](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3970499/files/A_AC.109_2022_11-EN.pdf) describes the Noumea Accord's one-to-three referendum framework, the question agreed in March 2018, the French organic act and special electoral-roll measures, and the dated 2018 vote. This supports a case about how eligibility and administration shape a status vote.
- [UN General Assembly resolution A/RES/80/98](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4097588/files/A_RES_80_98-EN.pdf) records continuing attention to electoral-roll questions and the three referendum dates. It is a diplomatic resolution, not an adjudication of every party's interpretation.
- Before scripting, examine the Accord and French organic law in the original language, electoral commission records, Indigenous and other local perspectives, and subsequent legal developments. Do not turn the 2018–2021 record into a claim about current or final status.

## BANK-236-040 — Puerto Rico fiscal authority

- [48 U.S.C. § 2121](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title48-chapter20) establishes the Financial Oversight and Management Board for Puerto Rico and describes its purpose. The statutory text supports institutional authority, not a measured economic result.
- [GAO-18-387](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-18-387.pdf) describes how the board certified fiscal plans in 2017 and 2018, including plans it developed itself in April 2018. This is a dated implementation example, not a full history through 2026.
- Before scripting, read the precise fiscal-plan and budget provisions, Puerto Rico government records, board certifications and court decisions for the selected period. Keep the question of elected versus board authority distinct from the broader territory-status and local-service questions in BANK-236-030/031/033/035. No claim about residents' welfare follows from board certification alone.
