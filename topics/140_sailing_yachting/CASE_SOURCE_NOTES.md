# Selected sailing case-source notes

Inspected 2026-10-24. These are bounded premise checks, not sailing instructions, live hazard notices or a complete accident review. Proposed topics keep `verified_sources` empty until episode research.

## Iberian-orca interactions — BANK-140-045

- The [Spanish and Portuguese government-sponsored February 2024 workshop report](https://www.miteco.gob.es/content/dam/miteco/es/biodiversidad/temas/biodiversidad-marina/orcas/informe-taller-orcas-miteco-en.pdf) brings together killer-whale scientists and management authorities and describes interactions in which orcas contact or damage rudders. It sets out research needs and management recommendations. The [Spanish ministry's May 2024 notice](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2024/mayo/miteco-y-mitma-ofrecen-recomendaciones-a-los-navegantes-en-caso-.html) dates one set of advice.
- These sources do not establish a single motive such as aggression, revenge or deliberate sinking. Incidents reported to a network are not a denominator for encounter risk without traffic exposure and reporting-rate analysis. The 2024 advice is dated and jurisdiction-specific; it should not be repackaged as current passage instructions. Wildlife welfare and crew safety both require consultation of current local authorities.

## AC75 class design in 2024 — BANK-140-046

- The [37th America's Cup organizer's AC75 boat overview](https://37th.americascup.com/the-boats) identifies the updated Version 2 class rule and describes renewed allowance for cycle power. [Persico Marine's March 2024 supplier statement](https://www.persicomarine.com/news-official-supplier-AC75S.html) identifies its official foil-arm-supplier role. The [organizer's 2024 press kit](https://www.americascup.com/files/m15492_AC-PressKit_231024.pdf) supplies period-specific class context.
- Organizer and supplier material is primary but promotional. A script needs the exact 37th Cup rule text and dated design disclosures to distinguish one-design arms from team-designed foils or hulls. Do not infer every yacht reached 55 knots, or import [2025 rules for the 2027 Cup](https://www.americascup.com/news/3839_AC75-CLASS-RULES-AND-TECHNICAL-REGULATIONS-PUBLISHED) into a 2024 design claim.
