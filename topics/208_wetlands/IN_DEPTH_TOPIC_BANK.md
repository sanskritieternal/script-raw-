Subcategory `CATALOG-208-R01` · foundation.

<a id="bank-208-010"></a>
#### BANK-208-010 — The Kidneys of the Earth: How Wetlands Filter Toxins, Stop Floods, and Store Carbon

**Video question:** How do shallow, muddy wetlands perform trillions of dollars in water purification, flood protection, and carbon storage for human civilization?

**Individual coverage scope:** Denitrification by anaerobic microbes converting agricultural nitrate runoff into harmless nitrogen gas, settling toxic sediments, slowing peak floodwaters, and global wetland ecology.

**Neighboring scope:** [BANK-114-037](../114_water/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-114-037) — Wetland service overview versus season-specific flood function.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-208-011"></a>
#### BANK-208-011 — The Four Wetland Types: Distinguishing Marshes, Swamps, Bogs, and Fens by pH

**Video question:** What is the precise botanical and hydrological difference between a woody swamp, a grassy marsh, an acidic bog, and an alkaline fen?

**Individual coverage scope:** Marshes (herbaceous emergent grasses, neutral/alkaline water), Swamps (dominated by woody trees and shrubs), Bogs (ombrotrophic, fed only by rain, highly acidic Sphagnum peat), and Fens (minerotrophic groundwater fed).

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-208-012"></a>
#### BANK-208-012 — Hydric Soils and Mottling: Gleyed Soils, Iron Reduction, and Anaerobic Redox Chemistry

**Video question:** How does standing water force soil microbes to use ferric iron (Fe3+) instead of oxygen for respiration, turning dirt bluish-grey?

**Individual coverage scope:** Redox potential (Eh) dropping below zero, anaerobic soil microbes reducing iron and manganese, bright orange redox concentrations (mottles) where oxygen pockets remain, and hydrogen sulfide 'rotten egg' odors.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-208-013"></a>
#### BANK-208-013 — Hydrophytic Plant Adaptations: Aerenchyma Air Channels Delivering Oxygen to Roots

**Video question:** Why don't cattails, water lilies, and reeds suffocate when their root systems are buried in oxygen-free anaerobic mud?

**Individual coverage scope:** Aerenchyma spongy tissue with continuous gas-filled lacunae channels piping atmospheric oxygen down stems directly to root tips, radial oxygen loss oxidizing toxic sulfides around roots, and adventitious water roots.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.
