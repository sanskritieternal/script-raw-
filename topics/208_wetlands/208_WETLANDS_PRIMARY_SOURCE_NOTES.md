# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-208-036: 2020 Pantanal fires

- NASA Earth Observatory, 8 March 2020 Terra MODIS observation: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/146409/fire-in-the-pantanal
- NASA Earth Observatory, 2020 dry-season satellite review: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/147269/fires-char-the-pantanal
- Brazil Ministry of Environment Pantanal action plan (published 2025, retrospective INPE metrics): https://www.gov.br/mma/pt-br/assuntos/controle-ao-desmatamento-queimadas-e-ordenamento-ambiental-territorial/controle-do-desmatamento-1/pppantanal/pppantanal_2025_versao-ingles_internet.pdf/@@download/file
- These records establish dated satellite observations and agency accounting, not a cause for each ignition, animal mortality count, or uniform wetland condition. Compare time windows and area definitions before quoting percentages.

## BANK-208-037: CERP

- Federal-state Everglades Restoration Initiatives program page: https://www.evergladesrestoration.gov/comprehensive-everglades-restoration-plan
- The official program identifies CERP authorization in the Water Resources Development Act of 2000 and water quantity, quality, timing and distribution as its targets. Its live project list changes; authorization is not completion, and the archived $21 billion figure must not be used without a dated budget basis.

## BANK-208-038: Baltimore Harbor Wetland

- National Aquarium, Harbor Wetland exhibit history: https://aqua.org/explore/exhibits/harbor-wetland
- National Aquarium, 2024 opening announcement: https://aqua.org/contact-us/newsroom/press-releases/2024-08-08-national-aquarium-unveils-harbor-wetland-exhibit-recalling-chesapeake-bay-habitat-to-inner-harbor
- The operator documents a 2010 prototype and 2024 public installation. An exhibit and observed habitat do not independently prove pollutant removal at harbor scale. Water quality needs separate site-level measurements and comparison periods.
