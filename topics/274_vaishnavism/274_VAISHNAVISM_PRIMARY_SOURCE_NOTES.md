## BANK-274-047: a Bengali Vaishnava poem manuscript

- British Library Archives and Manuscripts Catalogue, [EAP781/1/1/570: Vaisnava-padavali](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003395842), inspected 2026-10-24. The institutional record identifies ten folios, Bengali language and script, a 16th-17th century catalog date, attribution to Govindadasa, no colophon, and apparent completeness. It places the witness in the Santipur collection and gives a [digitized-content link](https://eap.bl.uk/archive-file/EAP781-1-1-570).
- Limit: catalog attribution and dating are not independently demonstrated by a colophon. The manuscript images and Bengali text were not transcribed or collated in this check. Do not assign a precise composition date, trace an entire transmission network, translate a poem, or claim uniform Vaishnava usage from this witness. Obtain a qualified edition and identify a specific poem before production. This is Bengali Vaishnava song transmission, not Vedic recitation or Bhagavata Purana exegesis.

## BANK-274-048: Manipur Sankirtana

- UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, [Sankirtana, ritual singing, drumming and dancing of Manipur](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/sankirtana-ritual-singing-drumming-and-dancing-of-manipur-00843), inscribed 2013; institution-indexed text inspected 2026-10-24. The listing identifies Krishna-centered song and dance, temple and domestic settings, drummers and singer-dancers, and life-cycle functions within a Vaishnava community of the Manipur plains.
- UNESCO [2013 nomination evaluation](https://ich.unesco.org/en/8-representative-list-00665?select_country=00103) lists the nomination form and community-consent evidence. The [2011 referral decision](https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/6.COM/13.20) documents an earlier procedural stage, so avoid presenting inscription as a single uncontested event.
- Limit: the main listing returned a JavaScript/captcha challenge on direct open, so the indexed institutional text and accessible decision pages supplied this check. Nomination descriptions reflect a dated institutional process, not observation of all current gatherings or consent for filming individuals. Specify the Manipur community and seek contemporary practitioners' perspectives; distinguish performance history from the dance bank's choreography scope.
