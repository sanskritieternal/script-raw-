This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Travel Systems across four progressive tiers:

- **Tier 3: Travel Systems Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Travel Systems.
- **Tier 4: Travel Systems Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Travel Systems.
