# Selected primary and institutional checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-233-042: Southwest 2022 disruption

- US DOT Consent Order 2023-12-11, 18 December 2023: https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer/southwest-order-2023-12-11
- Primary order PDF: https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2023-12/Southwest%20Order%202023-12-11.pdf
- DOT found failures in prompt/proper refunds, flight-status notification and customer service during and after the December 2022 disruption; it assessed a $140 million civil penalty. This US enforcement order is not a full independent technical root-cause analysis of every cancellation. Passenger-rights rules may have changed since 2023.

## BANK-233-043: NDC standard

- IATA NDC institutional page: https://www.iata.org/en/programs/airline-distribution/retailing/ndc
- IATA NDC factsheet dated December 2025: https://www.iata.org/en/iata-repository/pressroom/fact-sheets/fact-sheet-ndc/
- IATA describes an airline-agent data exchange for shopping, orders, servicing, payment and refunds. These are standard capabilities and stated aims, not proof that every carrier, agent or platform implements the same version or that multi-provider journeys are seamless. Recheck schema version and participant implementation before production.
