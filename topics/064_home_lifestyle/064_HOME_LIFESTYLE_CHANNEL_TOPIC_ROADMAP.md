## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Home & Lifestyle across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Spatial Ergonomics, Lighting Psychology & Decluttering Systems (Scratch / Beginner)** — Circadian rhythm Kelvin lighting color temperatures, the KonMari category sorting system, corridor traffic flow clearance, and VOC indoor air toxins.
- **Tier 2: Smart Home Mesh, Passive Thermal Design & Soundproofing (Intermediate)** — Zigbee/Thread mesh networks, cross-ventilation passive airflow, double-pane Low-E window coatings, and acoustic mass-loaded vinyl decoupling.
- **Tier 3: Heat Pumps, Home Battery Storage & Non-Toxic Materials (Upper Intermediate)** — Heat pump coefficient of performance (COP) thermodynamics, home solar battery time-of-use peak shaving, lime wash mineral paints, and greywater recycling.
- **Tier 4: Prefab Modular Living, Biophilic Architecture & Off-Grid Autonomy (Advanced / Expert)** — Cross-laminated timber prefab construction, living indoor moss particulate air scrubbers, off-grid rainwater micro-filtration engineering, and hyper-compact micro-apartments.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Ergonomics, Lighting & Decluttering
1. **Why Your House Feels Depressing: The Science of 2700K vs 5000K Lighting Psychology**
   - *Question:* Why does installing 5000K cold blue-white LED bulbs in your living room spike cortisol levels and ruin sleep, while 2700K warm layered lighting creates biological relaxation?
   - *Key Focus:* Melanopsin retinal ganglion cells sensing blue light wavelengths, Kelvin color temperature scale, layered lighting triad (ambient, task, accent), Color Rendering Index (CRI 90+).
2. **The Kitchen Work Triangle: The 1940s Ergonomic Rule That Still Rules Every Modern Kitchen**
   - *Question:* How did University of Illinois time-motion researchers in the 1940s calculate that connecting the sink, refrigerator, and cooktop in an unobstructed triangle saves 100 miles of walking a year?
   - *Key Focus:* The 4-to-9-foot leg distance rule, kitchen island traffic bottlenecks, prep-cooking-cleaning functional zones, modern kitchen ergonomics.
3. **The KonMari Method: Why Organizing by Category Instead of Location Stops Rebound Clutter**
   - *Question:* Why does tidying room-by-room doom homeowners to endless clutter cycles, and how does gathering all your clothes onto one giant bed shock the brain into ruthless curation?
   - *Key Focus:* The 5 KonMari categories (Clothes, Books, Papers, Komono, Sentimental), tactile decision-making ('does it spark joy?'), the endowment effect cognitive bias, vertical folding physics.
4. **Indoor Air Quality: The Invisible Chemical Smog Inside Your Sealed Modern Home**
   - *Question:* Why is the air inside a tightly insulated modern apartment often 5 times more toxic than outdoor street air due to off-gassing furniture and carbon dioxide spikes?
   - *Key Focus:* Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) from pressed wood and paints, CO2 levels exceeding 1,500 PPM causing cognitive fatigue, HEPA vs activated carbon filters, ventilation air-exchange rates.
### Tier 2: Smart Mesh, Thermal Design & Sound
5. **The Science of Soundproofing: Why Egg Cartons on Walls Are an Absolute Lie**
   - *Question:* Why do acoustic foam wedges only stop high-frequency echoes inside a room, while blocking your noisy neighbor requires physical mechanical decoupling and mass?
   - *Key Focus:* Acoustic absorption vs sound transmission loss, the four elements of soundproofing (Decoupling, Absorption, Mass, Damping/Green Glue), Mass Loaded Vinyl (MLV) physics.
6. **Passive Thermal Cooling: How Ancient Courtyards and Cross-Breezes Beat Air Conditioning**
   - *Question:* How can opening low windows on the shady side of a house and high transom windows on the warm side create a natural chimney breeze that cools rooms by 8°F for zero electricity?
   - *Key Focus:* Thermal stack effect buoyancy, prevailing wind cross-ventilation, thermal mass diurnal damping (stone/adobe cooling at night), exterior window shading eaves.
7. **Low-E Windows: The Microscopic Silver Coating That Traps Heat in Winter and Blocks It in Summer**
   - *Question:* How does a 10-nanometer transparent silver layer sputtered onto glass let visible sunlight in while reflecting invisible infrared radiant heat back to where it came from?
   - *Key Focus:* Low-Emissivity (Low-E) pyrolytic vs magnetron sputtered coatings, Argon gas thermal conductivity dampening between double panes, Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC) ratings.
8. **Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Thread: Building a Smart Home That Still Works When the Wi-Fi Dies**
   - *Question:* Why does putting 50 smart light bulbs on standard home Wi-Fi crash your router, and how do low-power mesh radios pass signals from bulb to bulb autonomously?
   - *Key Focus:* Wi-Fi channel congestion vs 802.15.4 low-power radio mesh, self-healing mesh topology (every mains-powered plug acts as a repeater), local hub processing vs cloud server lag.
### Tier 3: Heat Pumps, Battery Storage & Non-Toxic Living
9. **Heat Pumps: The Thermodynamic Miracle That Delivers 300% Heating Efficiency**
   - *Question:* How can an outdoor unit pull heat out of freezing 10°F air to warm your radiators, achieving 3 to 4 times the efficiency of electric baseboards or gas furnaces?
   - *Key Focus:* Carnot refrigeration cycle reversed, R32 and R454B eco-refrigerant phase changes, Coefficient of Performance (COP = 3.5), cold-climate inverter compressor breakthroughs.
10. **Home Battery Storage: How Peak Shaving and Solar Self-Consumption Beat the Electric Utility**
   - *Question:* How does storing cheap afternoon solar or off-peak grid electricity to power your home during 4 PM - 9 PM surge hours pay for an $8,000 battery system?
   - *Key Focus:* Time-of-Use (TOU) utility rate arbitrage, round-trip efficiency (90% AC-to-DC-to-AC), critical loads panel backup during grid blackouts, virtual power plant (VPP) grid revenue.
11. **Limewash and Mineral Paints: Why Breathable Walls Prevent Black Mold Forever**
   - *Question:* Why do modern acrylic plastic latex paints trap moisture inside drywall causing hidden toxic black mold, while 2,000-year-old calcium hydroxide limewash lets walls breathe?
   - *Key Focus:* High alkaline pH 12 natural mold resistance, carbonation process (calcium hydroxide absorbing CO2 to turn back into solid limestone), micro-crystalline vapor permeability.
12. **Greywater Systems: Reusing Shower and Sink Water to Water Your Entire Garden**
   - *Question:* Why do households flush gallons of pristine drinking water down toilets and onto lawns, and how does simple 'Laundry-to-Landscape' gravity plumbing cut water bills by 40%?
   - *Key Focus:* Blackwater vs greywater legal plumbing definitions, diverter valves, subsurface mulch basin filtration preventing standing water and mosquitoes, non-toxic biocompatible soaps.
13. **The Swedish Death Cleaning: The Practical Nordic Art of Decluttering Before You Die**
   - *Question:* Why do Swedes over 50 spend years systematically giving away, donating, and shredding possessions so their grieving children aren't burdened with cleaning out their hoard?
   - *Key Focus:* Margareta Magnusson's 'Döstädning' philosophy, psychological honesty about material impermanence, the 'throw-away' private box, emotional liberation for aging adults.
### Tier 4: Prefab Modular, Biophilic & Off-Grid
14. **Biophilic Interior Design: Why Adding Living Plants to Rooms Increases Cognitive Function by 15%**
   - *Question:* How does integrating natural fractals, living plants, wood grain textures, and natural daylight into interior spaces measurably reduce blood pressure and mental fatigue?
   - *Key Focus:* Edward O. Wilson's Biophilia Hypothesis, Attention Restoration Theory (ART), natural fractal patterns (Statistical physics), acoustic masking with indoor water features.
15. **Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT): The Engineered Wood Slabs Replacing Steel and Concrete**
   - *Question:* How do alternating perpendicular layers of kiln-dried lumber glued under massive hydraulic pressure create building panels stronger than concrete that sequester carbon?
   - *Key Focus:* Orthogonal cross-lamination tensile strength, charring layer fire resistance (wood chars at predictable rates insulating inner core), precision CNC factory prefabrication, erecting a home in 3 days.
16. **Off-Grid Rainwater Harvesting: From Roof Catchment to 0.2-Micron Potable Tap Water**
   - *Question:* How does an off-grid home turn rain falling off a metal roof into sterile, crystal-clear drinking water without chlorine or municipal connections?
   - *Key Focus:* First-flush diverter physics (washing initial bird droppings away), food-grade poly tanks, 5-micron sediment pre-filters, activated carbon blocks, and ultraviolet (UV) germicidal sterilization.
17. **Micro-Apartments: How Tokyo Architects Turn 150 Square Feet into a Luxurious Palace**
   - *Question:* How do Japanese architects use transformer furniture, vertical ceiling lofting, and hidden sliding partitions to make a 14-square-meter apartment feel spacious and organized?
   - *Key Focus:* Unit-bath modular Japanese bathrooms, Genkan entryway dirt boundaries, sliding Shoji partitions concealing storage, multi-functional furniture engineering.
18. **Earthships: The Radical Off-Grid Homes Built from Recycled Car Tires and Beer Cans**
   - *Question:* How did architect Michael Reynolds build self-heating desert homes in Taos, New Mexico that require zero heating or cooling bills in sub-zero winters?
   - *Key Focus:* Pounded-earth automotive tire load-bearing walls, greenhouse solar heating envelope, integrated botanical greywater planter cells, complete off-grid autonomy philosophy.
19. **Smart Thermostats: How Machine Learning Learns Your Schedule and Cuts Heating Bills by 15%**
   - *Question:* How do smart thermostats calculate your home's unique thermal envelope delay (how many hours it takes to gain or lose 1°F) to pre-cool before energy peak prices hit?
   - *Key Focus:* Thermal decay time-constant algorithms, geofencing phone proximity detection, remote room temperature/occupancy sensors, automated demand-response utility rebates.
20. **The Autonomous Home in 2040: Closed-Loop Energy, Water, and Robotic Maintenance**
   - *Question:* Will future homes generate 100% of their power with transparent perovskite solar window coatings, recycle 95% of water infinitely, and clean themselves with humanoid domestic robots?
   - *Key Focus:* Perovskite building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV), closed-loop vacuum distillation water recycling, domestic robotic tidying models, resilient distributed neighborhood microgrids.
