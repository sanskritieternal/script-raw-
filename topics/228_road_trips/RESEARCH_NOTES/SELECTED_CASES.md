All linked questions remain **proposed**. Historic road records cannot establish today's closure, weather or charging conditions.

## BANK-228-039 — Williams and Route 66

The [National Park Service Arizona Route 66 account](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/route-66-arizona.htm) identifies Williams, Arizona, as the location of the last U.S. Route 66 segment bypassed by the interstate in 1984. The [NPS historic registration](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64500038_text) supplies alignment and preservation context. Neither is a measured before/after revenue study for Williams. Existing BANK-228-033 owns the general bypass mechanism; this case owns a dated route and town record.

## BANK-228-040 — Regent's Slide and Highway 1

[Caltrans' Regent's Slide project page](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-5/district-5-current-projects/05-1r640) documents the February 2024 closure and repair project. A [January 14, 2026 reopening notice](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-5/d5-news/d5-news-1-14-26) records access at that date, while a [February 17, 2026 notice](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-5/d5-news/d5-news-2-17-2026) records a later closure after debris buildup. The changing status demonstrates why a script cannot use either notice as current driving guidance. Existing BANK-228-031 covers mountain-road alignment broadly and BANK-228-035 covers navigation redundancy; this case owns the dated disruption sequence.
