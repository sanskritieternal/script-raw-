# Selected cultural-tradition cases — source review, 24 October 2026

These are proposed episode questions. The bank JSON routes every archived pending and trending format to an active question; its original status snapshots and IDs remain preserved. ## BANK-243-042 — Vanuatu sand drawings

- The [UNESCO inscription](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/vanuatu-sand-drawings-00073) describes sand drawings as mnemonic and communicative practice, with stories, knowledge and sometimes sacred meaning. The [UNESCO safeguarding project](https://ich.unesco.org/en/projects/safeguarding-of-vanuatu-sand-drawings-00022) states goals for transmission, curricula and distinguishing designs available for public use.
- The inscription and project page support the question of what the project *intended* to transmit. They do not show that all goals were achieved, that all language communities share one practice, or that restricted designs and stories may be copied. Read project evaluations and consult authorized practitioners before any example is represented. BANK-243-033 addresses consent as a general method; this case tests it in a named tradition.

## BANK-243-043 — Kyrgyz, Kazakh and Karakalpak yurt craft

- [UNESCO's 2025 shared inscription](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-knowledge-and-skills-in-making-kyrgyz-kazakh-and-karakalpak-yurts-turkic-nomadic-dwellings-02284) describes distributed frame, felt and decorative work and transmission through families, apprenticeship and events.
- The inscription supports a comparison of the roles and routes *described in the nomination*. It cannot establish that all makers use the same division of labor, that craft is unchanged, or that inscription improved livelihoods. Read the multinational nomination and each community's own accounts, seek permission for personal practice, and compare distinct local settings before scripting. BANK-243-040 covers ceremonial textiles broadly; this case follows a named dwelling-making practice with shared inscription rather than repeating textile symbolism.
