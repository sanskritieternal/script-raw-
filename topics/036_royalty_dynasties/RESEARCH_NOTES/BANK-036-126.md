# BANK-036-126 — Sovereign Grant and UK royal finance

**Question:** What do the Sovereign Grant accounts show about UK royal public expenditure versus private income?  
**Review date:** 2026-10-24. **Status:** Bounded source and premise review. The BANK topic remains `proposed`; no legal or financial advice, script approval, or production claim.

## Primary sources inspected

1. [Sovereign Grant Act 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/15/contents), UK Parliament legislation portal. This establishes the statutory public funding framework. Later amendments and annual determinations must be read at their own dates.
2. HM Treasury, [Sovereign Grant Act guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sovereign-grant-act-2011-guidance), updated 5 August 2025. It describes the grant as support for the King's official duties. Its 2025 update predates the 2025–26 annual accounts.
3. Royal Household, [financial reports for 2025–26](https://www.royal.uk/financial-reports-256), published 25 June 2026. The Household reports a **£132.1 million Sovereign Grant for 2025–26**, of which **£67.5 million** was allocated to preservation and protection of Occupied Royal Palaces. It says the Royal Trustees set **£137.9 million for 2026–27** and describes a planned lower grant from 2027–28. These are year-specific public funding figures, not any individual's salary or net worth.
4. Royal Household, [Royal Finances overview](https://www.royal.uk/royal-finances). It distinguishes the Treasury-funded Sovereign Grant, Duchy of Lancaster income used for official and some private expenditure, and the King's personal income and estates. The Duchy of Cornwall funds the Prince of Wales separately. This is the Household's own categorization; independent audited accounts and parliamentary review should be compared before making accountability claims.

## Claim boundary

- The archived “How does the British Royal Family actually make money?” pitch combines public spending, institutional estate income and private wealth. No single Sovereign Grant amount answers all three.
- Crown Estate revenue is used in the statutory grant calculation; it is not automatically personal income to the monarch. Occupied palaces and most Royal Collection objects must not be counted as freely saleable private assets on the basis of a celebrity net-worth article.
- The 2025–26 and 2026–27 numbers are not timeless. Report the fiscal year, source, gross/net definition and whether a value is allocation, expense or projected grant. The 2027–32 reset described by the Royal Household should be checked against the final legal and Treasury record before stating it as operative law.
- This case is royal institutional finance. It does not replace the broader comparative-dynasty question in `BANK-036-113` or the legal succession questions in this niche.
