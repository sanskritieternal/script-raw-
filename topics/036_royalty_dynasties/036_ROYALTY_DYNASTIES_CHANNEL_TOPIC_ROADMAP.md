## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Royalty / Dynasties across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Divine Right, Succession Laws & Crown Foundations (Scratch / Beginner)** — Salic law succession disputes, coronation sacramental rituals, dynastic heraldry, and royal cousin marriages.
- **Tier 2: The Great Dynastic Houses & Imperial Clashes (Intermediate)** — The Plantagenets vs Valois, the Habsburg universal empire, the Ottoman fratricide law, and the Qing Dynasty banner nobility.
- **Tier 3: Regency Scandals, Abdications & Court Intrigues (Upper Intermediate)** — The Madness of King George, Edward VIII's abdication crisis, the Prince in the Tower murder mystery, and Rasputin's influence.
- **Tier 4: Fall of Monarchy, Modern Constitutional Royalty & Dynastic Aftermath (Advanced / Expert)** — The 1918 royal collapse across Europe, crown jewel repatriation disputes, civil list sovereign grants, and modern monarchical survival.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Divine Right & Crown Foundations
1. **The Romanovs: The 300-Year Dynasty and the Final Night in the Ipatiev House**
   - *Question:* How did a dynasty that governed one-sixth of the Earth's surface collapse into an Ekaterinburg basement firing squad in 1918?
   - *Key Focus:* 300-year tercentenary hubris, Tsarevich Alexei's hemophilia crisis, Yakov Yurovsky execution command, forensic DNA identification.
2. **Salic Law: How One Ancient Rule Barred Women from European Thrones for Centuries**
   - *Question:* Why did an obscure 6th-century Frankish agricultural code ignite the Hundred Years' War and bar women from the French crown?
   - *Key Focus:* Male-preference vs absolute agnatic primogeniture, Edward III's claim through Isabella of France, the Pragmatic Sanction of 1713.
3. **The Coronation of Charlemagne: Christmas Day 800 and the Holy Roman Empire**
   - *Question:* How did Pope Leo III placing a golden crown on the King of the Franks create a 1,000-year rivalry between Popes and Emperors?
   - *Key Focus:* Translatio Imperii, surprise coronation debate, the Carolingian renaissance, foundation of the Holy Roman Empire.
4. **The Habsburg Jaw: The Terrifying Genetic Cost of 200 Years of Inbreeding**
   - *Question:* How did the family motto 'Let others wage war; thou, happy Austria, marry' end with a Spanish king who could barely chew his food?
   - *Key Focus:* Mandibular prognathism, avunculate uncle-niece marriages, Charles II's autopsy records, extinction of the Spanish Habsburgs in 1700.
### Tier 2: The Great Dynastic Houses & Imperial Clashes
5. **The Wars of the Roses: Lancaster, York, and the Bloody Birth of the Tudors**
   - *Question:* How did 30 years of cousin slaughter over the English throne end with a Welsh nobleman picking a crown out of a hawthorn bush?
   - *Key Focus:* Henry VI's catatonic mental illness, Margaret of Anjou, the Kingmaker Earl of Warwick, Battle of Bosworth Field 1485, Henry VII's union.
6. **The Ottoman Law of Fratricide: Legalized Brother Execution for the Stability of the Realm**
   - *Question:* Why did Sultan Mehmed II make it legal for every new sultan to execute all his brothers with a silk bowstring upon ascension?
   - *Key Focus:* Kanunname decree, preventing civil wars and Mongol-style nomadic succession disputes, the transition to the Kafes (gilded cage).
7. **The Sun King (Louis XIV): How Versailles Tamed the French Nobility with Etiquette**
   - *Question:* How did Louis XIV turn dressing him in the morning into a cutthroat privilege to prevent the nobility from ever rebelling again?
   - *Key Focus:* The Fronde trauma, the Lever and Coucher royal ceremonies, domesticating the aristocracy in a gilded surveillance palace.
8. **The Qing Dynasty: How Manchu Nomads Ruled 400 Million Han Chinese for 268 Years**
   - *Question:* How did an invading nomadic force enforce the queue hairstyle under pain of death while mastering Confucian imperial governance?
   - *Key Focus:* The Eight Banners military system, Kangxi Emperor's administrative tours, the civil service examination integration, Qianlong's zenith.
### Tier 3: Regency Scandals & Court Intrigues
9. **The Princes in the Tower: Did Richard III Murder His Own Nephews?**
   - *Question:* What happened to 12-year-old Edward V and his 9-year-old brother inside the Tower of London in the bloody summer of 1483?
   - *Key Focus:* Titulus Regius illegitimacy decree, 1674 bone discovery under the White Tower stairs, Shakespeare's Tudor propaganda vs modern revisionism.
10. **The Madness of King George: Porphyria, Bipolar Mania, and the Regency Crisis of 1788**
   - *Question:* Was King George III suffering from an inherited metabolic blood disorder, or was he battling acute bipolar mania during the American Revolution?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. Francis Willis harsh behavioral restraints, royal arsenic hair analysis tests, Charles James Fox vs William Pitt the Younger regency debate.
11. **Edward VIII & Wallis Simpson: The 1936 Abdication Crisis That Threatened the Empire**
   - *Question:* How did a king choose marriage to a twice-divorced American woman over the British Empire, and what were his secret Nazi sympathies?
   - *Key Focus:* Church of England moral blockade, Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin's ultimatum, December 1936 radio broadcast, Marburg Files declassified secrets.
12. **Rasputin: The Siberian Peasant Who Brought Down the Russian Imperial Crown**
   - *Question:* How did a barefoot holy wanderer control ministerial appointments in the Russian Empire by stopping the Tsarevich's internal bleeding?
   - *Key Focus:* Aspirin avoidance hypothesis, Tsarina Alexandra's letters, Prince Felix Yusupov's poison and bullet assassination at Moika Palace.
### Tier 4: Fall of Monarchy & Modern Constitutional Royalty
13. **1918: The Year Four European Empires Collapsed in the Ashes of World War I**
   - *Question:* How did four centuries-old royal dynasties (Romanov, Hohenzollern, Habsburg, Ottoman) completely vanish within 18 months?
   - *Key Focus:* Kaiser Wilhelm II exile to Doorn, Emperor Karl I renunciation in Austria-Hungary, Sultan Mehmed VI flight, treaty redrawing world borders.
14. **The Koh-i-Noor Diamond: The Cursed Mountain of Light and Imperial Repatriation**
   - *Question:* How did a 105-carat gem pass from the Peacock Throne to Sikh Maharajas and into Queen Victoria's crown, and who owns it today?
   - *Key Focus:* 1849 Treaty of Lahore and 10-year-old Duleep Singh, the curse on male wearers, British diplomatic refusal to return to India/Pakistan.
15. **The Sovereign Grant: How Does the British Royal Family Actually Make Money?**
   - *Question:* Do British taxpayers fund the monarchy, or does the Crown Estate surrender 75% of its billions in offshore wind profits to Parliament?
   - *Key Focus:* George III 1760 civil list agreement, Crown Estate £16B asset valuation, Duchy of Lancaster and Duchy of Cornwall private hereditary income.
16. **The Last Emperor of China: Puyi's Journey from the Forbidden City to Communist Gardener**
   - *Question:* How did a 3-year-old boy carried onto the Dragon Throne end up a Japanese puppet emperor, Soviet prisoner, and finally an ordinary citizen?
   - *Key Focus:* 1912 abdication agreement, expulsion from Forbidden City in 1924, Manchukuo puppet trial at Tokyo War Crimes Tribunal, Fushun re-education.
17. **The Japanese Imperial House: The 2,600-Year Chrysanthemum Throne and the Succession Crisis**
   - *Question:* How has the oldest continuous hereditary monarchy in human history survived, and why does a lack of male heirs threaten its extinction?
   - *Key Focus:* Emperor Jimmu 660 BCE mythology, 1947 Imperial Household Law male-only restrictions, Prince Hisahito as the sole young heir, public opinion polls.
18. **The Borgias: Papal Royalty, Poison, and Machiavelli's Prince**
   - *Question:* How did a Spanish family buy the papacy and rule Rome like an absolute Renaissance monarchy using assassination and dynastic weddings?
   - *Key Focus:* Rodrigo Borgia (Alexander VI) simony, Cesare Borgia ruthless military campaign in Romagna, Lucrezia Borgia diplomatic marriages.
19. **Queen Victoria: The 'Grandmother of Europe' Whose Grandchildren Fought World War I**
   - *Question:* How were Kaiser Wilhelm II, Tsar Nicholas II, and King George V all first cousins writing personal letters while their armies clashed?
   - *Key Focus:* Albert and Victoria's diplomatic dynastic marriage master plan, the 'Willy-Nicky' telegrams on the eve of war, hemophilia gene transmission.
20. **The Survival of Royalty: Why 43 Nations Still Have a Monarch in 2026**
   - *Question:* In a democratic age, why do the Netherlands, Norway, the UK, and Japan maintain hereditary heads of state with overwhelming popularity?
   - *Key Focus:* Bagehot's English Constitution (dignified vs efficient parts of government), neutral political arbiter roles, Scandinavian 'bicycle monarchies'.
