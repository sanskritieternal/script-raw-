# Biodiversity conservation source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-523-043 - Kirtland's warbler delisting and continued stewardship

- Primary regulator decision: U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, *Removing the Kirtland's Warbler from the Federal List of Endangered and Threatened Wildlife*, final rule published October 9, 2019, effective November 8, 2019, https://www.fws.gov/species-publication-action/removing-kirtlands-warbler-federal-list-endangered-and-threatened-0 (inspected 2026-10-24). FWS concluded that the species met its Endangered Species Act recovery standard under its then-available evidence and announced a post-delisting monitoring plan. The original rule text is https://www.fws.gov/sites/default/files/federal_register_document/2019-22096.pdf .
- Primary management context: FWS, *Kirtland's Warbler Wildlife Management Area: About Us*, https://www.fws.gov/refuge/kirtlands-warbler-wildlife-management-area/about-us (inspected 2026-10-24). FWS reports an estimated more than 2,300 pairs in 2019 and describes continued young-jack-pine habitat management and monitoring. Its 2021 census release https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2021-10/kirtlands-warbler-census-shows-once-endangered-songbird-continues-thrive reports a dated singing-male survey, not a census of every bird across its range.
- Limits: Delisting is a U.S. legal and scientific decision at a time, not proof of permanent security, restored biodiversity generally or independence from continued habitat/cowbird management. Survey counts, estimated pairs and breeding-range measures have different definitions; do not join them into a seamless population trend without method review. The ruling is species-specific and subsequent status may change. Avoid operational wildlife handling instructions; include partner and local stewardship context rather than crediting one institution alone.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-523-043 is the named recovery/delisting evidence case. BANK-523-041 covers recovery plans generally; BANK-208-038 covers an urban wetland demonstration, not this bird's habitat management.
