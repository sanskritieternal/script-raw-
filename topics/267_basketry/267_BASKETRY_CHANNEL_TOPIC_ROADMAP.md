## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Basketry across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Basketry Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Basketry.
- **Tier 2: Basketry Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Basketry.
- **Tier 3: Basketry Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Basketry.
- **Tier 4: Basketry Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Basketry.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Basketry: Early Evidence for a Basket Form**
   - *Question:* What dated object or account documents one local basket?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish survival from absolute origin. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
2. **Basketry: A Celebrated Basket in Context**
   - *Question:* What do material, maker, and use reveal?
   - *Key Focus:* Check provenance and local variants. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
3. **Basketry: Gatherers and Preparers**
   - *Question:* Whose harvesting, drying, splitting, dyeing, and teaching is omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit labor and custodians with consent. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
4. **Basketry: How Basket Structure Works**
   - *Question:* How do material, weave, rim, and base support its use?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one named form and maker explanation. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
5. **Basketry: Material Ecology and Craft**
   - *Question:* How do plant cycles shape design and production?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare harvesting rules, properties, and maker decisions. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Basketry: An Unattributed Basket**
   - *Question:* Which evidence could locate its maker or age?
   - *Key Focus:* Check fiber, technique, labels, provenance, and gaps. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
7. **Basketry: New Material in Basketry**
   - *Question:* How did a substitute fiber change work or function?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare durability, price, supply, and maker views. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
8. **Basketry: A Basket Maker's Teaching**
   - *Question:* How did one artisan transmit knowledge?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace apprentices and community context. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
9. **Basketry: A Harvesting Rule Change**
   - *Question:* How did one land decision affect makers?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated permissions, supply, income, and adaptation. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
10. **Basketry: From Plant to Basket**
   - *Question:* Which decisions connect harvest, preparation, weaving, and use?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe with permission and record maker explanations. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Basketry: Demand for Baskets**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured shift in orders?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare household use, substitutes, tourism, price, and data limits. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
12. **Basketry: One Basket Form Across Generations**
   - *Question:* How did material, pattern, and purpose change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use provenanced objects and current maker accounts. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
13. **Basketry: Continuing a Basket Craft**
   - *Question:* What do practitioners need?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare land access, sustainable fiber, teaching, pay, and markets. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
14. **Basketry: Every Basket a Decorative Object?**
   - *Question:* How do practical and ceremonial uses differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask makers and users; avoid gallery-only interpretation. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
15. **Basketry: One Basket's Working Life**
   - *Question:* How was it made, used, repaired, and kept?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace custody and consented user accounts. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Basketry: Making for a Specific Use**
   - *Question:* Which load, size, setting, and client needs shape design?
   - *Key Focus:* Record maker and user decisions. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
17. **Basketry: Why This Plant Fiber**
   - *Question:* How do flexibility, strength, and availability matter?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace source, harvesting rights, and substitutes. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
18. **Basketry: Basket Maker's Sequence**
   - *Question:* Where do preparation, tension, and finishing demand skill?
   - *Key Focus:* Use maker-approved observation. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
19. **Basketry: Pattern Around a Form**
   - *Question:* How does weave structure shape visible motif?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask maker before assigning symbolism. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
20. **Basketry: Who Made the Basket**
   - *Question:* How do gatherer, designer, weaver, and seller contribute?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace credit and pay. Evidence base: provenanced baskets, harvesting and workshop records, material examination, and consented maker and user accounts.
