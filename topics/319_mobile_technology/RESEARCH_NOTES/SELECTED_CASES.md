## BANK-319-042 — AT&T 3G phaseout

- [AT&T's 2021 preparation notice](https://www.att.com/blog/att-is-shutting-down-its-3g-network-heres-how-to-prepare/) states its planned February 2022 3G phaseout and directs customers to device-compatibility information. [AT&T's 4G-device impact brief](https://www.business.att.com/content/dam/attbusiness/briefs/3G_Shutdown_Impact_on_4G_Devices_brief.pdf) identifies certain LTE modules still dependent on 3G voice-service activation or lacking VoLTE. These are carrier documents, and their device lists and service terms have a dated scope.
- An [FCC 2022 waiver record](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-22-1353A1.pdf) describes the distinct schedule of another carrier's 3G retirement, showing why no single national cutoff date should be asserted. Check actual AT&T completion notices, device model, provisioning and location before claiming that a particular phone, alarm or connected device lost service.
- This case narrows BANK-319-033's useful-life question and the archived mobile-network-change pitches. It is different from 320's radio-performance comparison and 318's internet routing case.
