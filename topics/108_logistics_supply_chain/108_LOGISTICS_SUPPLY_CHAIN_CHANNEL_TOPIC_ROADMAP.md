## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Logistics & Supply Chain across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Containerization, Bullwhip Dynamics & Global Chokepoints (Beginner / Scratch)** — Malcolm McLean container revolution, inventory bullwhip math, mega-port gantry mechanics, Toyota JIT principles, and Suez/Panama canal chokepoints.
- **Tier 2: Cold Chains, Automation Robotics & Last-Mile Delivery (Intermediate)** — Ultra-low reefer pharma chains, Kiva robot automated fulfillment, air cargo belly economics, last-mile doorstep density, and freight forwarder Incoterms.
- **Tier 3: Satellite Telematics, Semiconductor Fragility & Nearshoring (Upper Intermediate)** — Real-time IoT control towers, Taiwan semiconductor single-point-of-failure risks, closed-loop circular remanufacturing, hazmat logistics, and Mexico nearshoring.
- **Tier 4: Autonomous Highway Platooning, Green Fuel & Global Operations (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Level 4 autonomous trucking, green methanol container shipping, extreme Antarctic resupply logistics, global cold food networks, and antifragile supply chain architecture.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Box That Changed the World: How Containerization Built Globalization**
   - *Question:* How did Malcolm McLean's standardized steel shipping container slash ocean freight costs by 90%?
   - *Key Focus:* Break-bulk cargo history, intermodal transport, ISO container standards, and modern port cranes.
2. **Supply Chain Fundamentals: The Bullwhip Effect and Inventory Dynamics**
   - *Question:* Why does a 5% bump in retail toilet paper sales cause a 40% manufacturing shock up the supply chain?
   - *Key Focus:* Beer game simulation, demand signal distortion, lead time lags, and safety stock oscillations.
3. **Inside the Mega-Port: How 20,000 TEU Ships Are Unloaded in 48 Hours**
   - *Question:* What coordinated logistical choreography turns around giant container vessels around the clock?
   - *Key Focus:* Gantry crane throughput, berth scheduling, straddle carriers, customs inspection, and drayage trucking.
4. **Just-In-Time (JIT) vs Just-In-Case: The Toyota Production Legacy**
   - *Question:* How did Lean manufacturing eliminate factory warehouses, and why did it backfire during pandemic disruptions?
   - *Key Focus:* Taiichi Ohno's Kanban system, zero-inventory philosophy, supplier co-location, and fragility trade-offs.
5. **The Geography of Global Chokepoints: Suez, Panama, Malacca, and Bab el-Mandeb**
   - *Question:* Why can a single stuck cargo ship or maritime conflict shut down 12% of total global commerce?
   - *Key Focus:* Canal lock systems, transit toll economics, maritime straits, geopolitical vulnerability, and detour costs.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Cold Chain Logistics: How Ice Cream and Vaccines Cross Continents Frozen**
   - *Question:* What happens when temperature-controlled supply chains fail for single-digit minutes?
   - *Key Focus:* Reefer containers, ultra-low temperature dry-ice shipping, IoT temperature loggers, and pharma compliance.
7. **Warehouse Automation: Kiva Robots, AS/RS, and the Automated Fulfillment Center**
   - *Question:* How do robotic fulfillment centers pick and pack an order within 15 minutes of an online purchase?
   - *Key Focus:* Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems (AS/RS), autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), pick-to-light, and WMS software.
8. **Air Freight Economics: Why Putting Cargo on Planes is Worth 5x the Cost**
   - *Question:* When does it make financial sense to fly fresh flowers, smartphones, and auto parts instead of shipping by sea?
   - *Key Focus:* Belly cargo vs dedicated freighters, dimensional weight calculations, speed-to-market depreciation.
9. **Last-Mile Delivery: The Most Expensive and Inefficient Leg of Logistics**
   - *Question:* Why does the final 5 miles from warehouse to residential doorstep consume over 50% of total delivery costs?
   - *Key Focus:* Route density, failed deliveries, porch piracy, driver turnover, urban parking tickets, and parcel lockers.
10. **Freight Forwarding Decoded: The Unseen Middlemen Moving Global Freight**
   - *Question:* How do freight forwarders navigate customs clearance, bills of lading, and multi-modal carrier contracts?
   - *Key Focus:* Incoterms (FOB, CIF, EXW), ocean carrier consortiums, customs brokers, and digital forwarders.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Supply Chain Visibility and Track-and-Trace: Eliminating the Black Hole**
   - *Question:* How do satellite AIS tracking, cellular RFID sensors, and API integrations prevent cargo from disappearing?
   - *Key Focus:* Real-time telematics, exception management, predictive ETA algorithms, and supply chain control towers.
12. **The Semiconductor Supply Chain: The Most Fragile Manufacturing Web on Earth**
   - *Question:* Why does making a single microchip require raw materials and specialized machinery crossing 40 borders?
   - *Key Focus:* ASML lithography monopolies, TSMC fabrication concentration, neon gas and silicon purity supply chains.
13. **Circular Logistics and Reverse Material Flows: Industrial Recycling at Scale**
   - *Question:* How do auto manufacturers and tech giants reclaim lithium batteries and rare earth metals from spent products?
   - *Key Focus:* Closed-loop supply chains, core remanufacturing, scrap collection networks, and regulatory EPR rules.
14. **Dangerous Goods Logistics: Transporting Explosives, Toxins, and Lithium Batteries**
   - *Question:* What rigid international hazmat protocols prevent cargo planes from catching fire and container ships from exploding?
   - *Key Focus:* IMO IMDG Code, IATA DGR, Class 1-9 hazmat placards, packaging certifications, and catastrophic derailments.
15. **Nearshoring and Friendshoring: The Great Supply Chain Decoupling**
   - *Question:* Why are multinational corporations shifting production from China to Mexico, Vietnam, and India?
   - *Key Focus:* Geopolitical risk hedging, Section 301 tariffs, labor cost convergence, and cross-border rail links.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Autonomous Trucking and Freight Drones: The Unmanned Transit Horizon**
   - *Question:* Will Level 4 self-driving semi-trucks solve the chronic global driver shortage on interstate highways?
   - *Key Focus:* Transfer hub models, autonomous highway platooning, regulatory safety approvals, and drone medical deliveries.
17. **Green Shipping Corridors: Decarbonizing Ocean Vessels with Methanol and Ammonia**
   - *Question:* How can the maritime shipping industry, burning 300 million tons of heavy fuel oil, reach net zero?
   - *Key Focus:* Bunker fuel emissions, dual-fuel engines, green hydrogen derivatives, wind-assisted rotor sails, and IMO carbon taxes.
18. **Logistics in Extreme Environments: Supplying Antarctica, Space Stations, and War Zones**
   - *Question:* How do humans deliver food, fuel, and replacement parts to locations where failure means immediate death?
   - *Key Focus:* Icebreaker resupply missions, ISS rocket payload constraints, military forward operating base logistics.
19. **The Global Food Cold Chain: How 8 Billion People Eat Fresh Food Daily**
   - *Question:* How does fresh Chilean salmon reach Tokyo sushi counters in 48 hours without spoiling?
   - *Key Focus:* Controlled atmosphere shipping, nitrogen purge preservation, grain silo networks, and food waste reduction.
20. **Designing the Antifragile Supply Chain: The Global Operations Masterclass**
   - *Question:* How do world-class supply chain architects design networks that grow stronger during global crises?
   - *Key Focus:* Digital twins, multi-echelon inventory optimization, supplier redundancy, and adaptive logistics execution.
