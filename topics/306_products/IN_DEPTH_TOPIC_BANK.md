#### BANK-306-043 — Which products did the EU common charger directive require to support USB-C, and when?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace batch identity, distribution, fault reporting, replacement logistics and communication using a fictional product; exclude claims about a named business or recall compliance.

#### BANK-306-042 — How did the Galaxy Note7 recall change from initial replacement to a full product withdrawal?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare U.S. CPSC September and October 2016 recall notices with Samsung's January 2017 battery investigation. Distinguish incident reports, regulator remedies and manufacturer cause claims without giving repair advice.
