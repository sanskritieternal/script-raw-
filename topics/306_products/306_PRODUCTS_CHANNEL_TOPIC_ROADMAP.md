## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Products across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Products Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Products.
- **Tier 2: Products Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Products.
- **Tier 3: Products Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Products.
- **Tier 4: Products Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Products.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Products: Why a Product Was Created**
   - *Question:* Which user need and constraints shaped its first version?
   - *Key Focus:* Use design briefs, early tests, and alternatives. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
2. **Products: A Breakthrough Product Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What did it improve for defined users?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare performance, price, safety, and access. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
3. **Products: Workers Behind a Product**
   - *Question:* Who designs, sources, assembles, tests, ships, and repairs it?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace credited and outsourced labor. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
4. **Products: Following One Product**
   - *Question:* How does it move from design to end-of-use?
   - *Key Focus:* Map materials, manufacturing, sale, support, and disposal. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
5. **Products: Performance Versus Lifetime Cost**
   - *Question:* When does a higher specification fail to improve user value?
   - *Key Focus:* Test use case, reliability, maintenance, and replacement. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Products: A Product Failure Investigated**
   - *Question:* Which design, use, or manufacturing cause fits evidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Use tests, complaints, lots, and uncertainty. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
7. **Products: A New Feature Tested**
   - *Question:* Does it improve a measurable outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare baseline, user group, cost, and harms. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
8. **Products: A Design Decision**
   - *Question:* Who chose one consequential trade-off?
   - *Key Focus:* Use briefs, testing, and team accounts. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
9. **Products: A Recall or Redesign**
   - *Question:* How did one event change the product?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace official notice, affected models, remedy, and results. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
10. **Products: The Product's Operating System**
   - *Question:* Which components and services deliver its function?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a diagram tied to one version. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Products: Product Demand Over Time**
   - *Question:* What explains sales changes?
   - *Key Focus:* Check price, competitors, distribution, season, and data definition. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
12. **Products: Version One and Today**
   - *Question:* What changed in performance, repair, cost, and safety?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare matched tests and specifications. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
13. **Products: A Product's Conditional Next Version**
   - *Question:* Which needs and constraints would justify change?
   - *Key Focus:* State material, cost, regulation, and user assumptions. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
14. **Products: A Spec Sheet Promise**
   - *Question:* Does one headline metric predict real use?
   - *Key Focus:* Test conditions, variance, durability, and alternatives. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
15. **Products: One Product from Brief to Recall**
   - *Question:* How did decisions and evidence accumulate?
   - *Key Focus:* Build dated timeline, not inventor mythology. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Products: Inputs to End User**
   - *Question:* Where are critical materials, manufacturing, and distribution?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace suppliers and dependencies. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
17. **Products: Designer, Maker, Seller, User**
   - *Question:* Who benefits from a design choice and who bears cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Read warranty, margin, and return terms. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
18. **Products: Profit per Unit**
   - *Question:* How do manufacturing, logistics, returns, and support affect margin?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify volume, period, and cost allocation. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
19. **Products: Does It Work for Users**
   - *Question:* Which measured outcome matters most?
   - *Key Focus:* Define population, comparator, real use, and failure. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
20. **Products: Product Substitutes**
   - *Question:* What alternatives solve the same task?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare total cost and user needs in a defined market. Evidence base: dated design and specification records, independent tests, official recall data, supplier and repair records, and consented user accounts.
