## BANK-306-042: Galaxy Note7 recall sequence

- U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, [15 September 2016 Galaxy Note7 recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2016/Samsung-Recalls-Galaxy-Note7-Smartphones), inspected 2026-10-24. It describes about one million affected U.S. units sold before 15 September, reports overheating/fire incidents and offers replacement or refund remedies at that stage.
- CPSC, [13 October 2016 statement on replacement Note7 devices](https://www.cpsc.gov/about-cpsc/chairman/elliot-f-kaye/statements/statement-from-us-cpsc-chairman-elliot-f-kaye-0?language=en), inspected 2026-10-24. It addresses reports involving replacement phones and the further recall.
- Samsung, [23 January 2017 investigation announcement](https://news.samsung.com/us/Samsung-Electronics-Announces-Cause-of-Galaxy-Note7-Incidents-in-Press-Conference), inspected 2026-10-24, attributes incidents to battery defects and describes process changes.
- Limits: the CPSC notices are U.S. jurisdictional and represent stages in a changing recall, not a standing remedy now. Samsung's root-cause account is the manufacturer's report, not an independently re-analysed engineering record here. Do not reproduce hazardous battery diagnostics or repair instructions; distinguish incident counts, product populations and announcement dates.

## BANK-306-043: EU common charger scope

- European Union, [Directive (EU) 2022/2380](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022L2380), adopted 23 November 2022 and inspected 2026-10-24. It sets USB-C requirements for listed categories if capable of wired charging, with application from 28 December 2024 for categories 1.1-1.12 and 28 April 2026 for laptops (1.13).
- European Commission, [2024 interpretation guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:52024XC02997), inspected 2026-10-24, clarifies scope and that an additional receptacle is not prohibited if a covered device also has the required USB-C receptacle.
- Limits: the rule concerns specified equipment placed on the EU market, not every existing device, all global markets or universal electrical compatibility. Connector shape, cable capability and USB Power Delivery requirements are different questions. Check current national implementation and the exact product category for any consumer-facing claim.
