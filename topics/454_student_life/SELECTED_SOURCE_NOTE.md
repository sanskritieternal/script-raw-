# Selected source note — CUNY ASAP

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-454-044. This is a proposed question, not an approved idea or completed script.

## Primary evidence

- MDRC, *Accelerated Study in Associate Programs (ASAP): A Program That Almost Doubles Three-Year Graduation Rates* (October 2015): https://www.mdrc.org/work/publications/accelerated-study-associate-programs-asap . MDRC reports its random-assignment evaluation of 896 low-income students with developmental education needs, and says the comprehensive CUNY support program almost doubled three-year graduation rates.
- MDRC, *Doubling Graduation Rates: Three-Year Effects of CUNY's Accelerated Study in Associate Programs (ASAP) for Developmental Education Students* (February 2015): https://www.mdrc.org/work/publications/doubling-graduation-rates . Use the full report before quoting a precise effect estimate, eligibility detail or implementation component.

## Claim and access limits

The study is evidence about this 2010-era eligible sample at three New York City community colleges. It does not establish that a single service caused the effect, that every student can enroll, or that a similar program will have the same effect elsewhere or today. The accessible MDRC overview summarizes the trial; full-report tables and current CUNY eligibility require separate checks before a script. Respect MDRC figures/text rights and paraphrase.
