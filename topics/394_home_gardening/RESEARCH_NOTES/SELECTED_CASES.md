## BANK-394-044: spotted lanternfly in a Pennsylvania garden

- [Penn State Extension's 2025 residential-landscape guide](https://extension.psu.edu/what-to-do-about-spotted-lanternfly-on-ornamental-trees-and-plants-in-residential-and-public-landscapes) instructs observation of host, population and risk before choosing a response. Its [2026 FAQ](https://extension.psu.edu/spotted-lanternfly-frequently-asked-questions) distinguishes recorded plant impacts from the unsupported assumption that every sighting kills a mature tree. [USDA APHIS](https://direct.aphis.usda.gov/plant-pests-diseases/slf) supplies federal pest and host context.
- These sources support a dated Pennsylvania decision framework, not diagnosis of a particular plant. A gardener must identify the insect and other stressors, check current local rules and any product label, and assess site conditions. The sources do not justify indiscriminate pesticide use or a universal control recipe.
- The case gives the archived “why did a plant fail?” hook a concrete pest-versus-stress example. It does not duplicate 193's broader insect-history scope or 394's general symptom-triage question.
