## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Home Gardening across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Home Gardening Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Home Gardening.
- **Tier 2: Home Gardening Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Home Gardening.
- **Tier 3: Home Gardening Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Home Gardening.
- **Tier 4: Home Gardening Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Home Gardening.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Home Gardening: A Home Garden Practice in Context**
   - *Question:* Which local climate or household need shaped a dated practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use extension archives and garden records without a universal origin. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
2. **Home Gardening: A Productive Small Garden**
   - *Question:* Which site conditions and choices explain an observed result?
   - *Key Focus:* Record soil, sun, season, water, and harvest method. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
3. **Home Gardening: Labor behind a Garden**
   - *Question:* Who supplies seed, water, soil care, and ongoing maintenance?
   - *Key Focus:* Use grower accounts and cost records. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
4. **Home Gardening: Match Plant to Place**
   - *Question:* What soil, light, space, and season does one plant need locally?
   - *Key Focus:* Use region-specific extension advice and actual site measurements. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
5. **Home Gardening: The Garden as a System**
   - *Question:* How do soil, water, plant health, pollinators, and harvest interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace measured conditions and competing explanations. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Home Gardening: Why Did a Plant Fail?**
   - *Question:* Which causes can testing distinguish and which remain uncertain?
   - *Key Focus:* Check soil, weather, pests, and care records before diagnosis. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
7. **Home Gardening: A Garden Tool Tested**
   - *Question:* Does irrigation or monitoring equipment improve a defined outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare measured water use and plant response. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
8. **Home Gardening: A Gardener Changes Methods**
   - *Question:* What observation prompted a documented change in one plot?
   - *Key Focus:* Use logs and contextual interviews. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
9. **Home Gardening: A Weather Shock in a Garden**
   - *Question:* How did a dated frost, heat event, or flood affect one crop?
   - *Key Focus:* Use local weather and garden records. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
10. **Home Gardening: From Soil Test to Planting**
   - *Question:* How does a site assessment inform one growing decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Show sample method, test limits, and local guidance. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Home Gardening: A Garden Method Reconsidered**
   - *Question:* Which evidence supports or challenges a popular technique?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare trials under comparable conditions. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
12. **Home Gardening: One Plot across Seasons**
   - *Question:* What changed in yield, soil, and labor between two dated seasons?
   - *Key Focus:* Hold measurement and crop definitions steady. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
13. **Home Gardening: Planning Next Season**
   - *Question:* Which uncertain weather or resource factor drives a garden plan?
   - *Key Focus:* Present conditional choices, not harvest promises. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
14. **Home Gardening: A Universal Garden Tip?**
   - *Question:* When does a common rule fail across soil or climate?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare extension guidance by region. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
15. **Home Gardening: A Garden's Year**
   - *Question:* How do preparation, planting, care, and harvest unfold?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated logs and measured outcomes. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Home Gardening: A Season of Gardening**
   - *Question:* How did weather, care, and harvest fit a grower's daily life?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated logs and consented accounts. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
17. **Home Gardening: Access to Growing Space**
   - *Question:* Which lease, allotment, or local rule determines who can garden?
   - *Key Focus:* Read governing documents and actual allocation practice. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
18. **Home Gardening: Why This Plot Is Grown**
   - *Question:* Do food, habitat, enjoyment, or tradition drive the crop choices?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask growers and compare stated goals with practice. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
19. **Home Gardening: Who Can Garden Here?**
   - *Question:* Which cost, reach, path, time, or water barrier limits participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure site and record affected users' views. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
20. **Home Gardening: Gardens for Different Households**
   - *Question:* How do crop and care priorities vary among local growers?
   - *Key Focus:* Use bounded interviews without treating one group as uniform. Evidence base: local extension guidance, soil and water tests, dated weather records, grower logs, plant labels, measured harvests, and bounded comparisons.
