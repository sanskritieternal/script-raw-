## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Climate Science across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Climate Science Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Climate Science.
- **Tier 2: Climate Science Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Climate Science.
- **Tier 3: Climate Science Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Climate Science.
- **Tier 4: Climate Science Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Climate Science.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Climate Science: A Climate Record Begins**
   - *Question:* Which measurements first made a regional or global trend testable?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated station, proxy, and calibration records. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
2. **Climate Science: A Famous Climate Graph Rechecked**
   - *Question:* Which variable, baseline, and uncertainty does it display?
   - *Key Focus:* Read data source and processing method. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
3. **Climate Science: People Maintaining Climate Records**
   - *Question:* Who observes, digitizes, quality-checks, and preserves data?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit technical and local contributors. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
4. **Climate Science: Weather, Climate, and Baseline**
   - *Question:* How does a short event differ from a long-term distribution?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a named variable, place, and time window. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
5. **Climate Science: Detecting and Attributing Change**
   - *Question:* Which observations and model comparisons separate drivers?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare natural variability, forcings, and uncertainty. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Climate Science: A Regional Climate Puzzle**
   - *Question:* Which observations disagree and what could explain them?
   - *Key Focus:* Check coverage, baseline, and model assumptions. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
7. **Climate Science: A New Climate Observation**
   - *Question:* What gap did a satellite or field instrument close?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare calibration and continuity with older data. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
8. **Climate Science: A Climate Assessment Decision**
   - *Question:* Who selected evidence and handled disagreement?
   - *Key Focus:* Review methods, authorship, and documented review. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
9. **Climate Science: A Climate Assessment Milestone**
   - *Question:* What changed between two assessment findings?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace evidence, confidence language, and dates. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
10. **Climate Science: Climate Model and Observation**
   - *Question:* How do physical processes, data, and validation connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one variable and model version. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Climate Science: A Climate Indicator Over Time**
   - *Question:* Does a trend persist across baseline and dataset choices?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare consistent series and uncertainties. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
12. **Climate Science: A Place's Climate Across Decades**
   - *Question:* How did temperature or rainfall distribution change?
   - *Key Focus:* Match season, station coverage, and reference period. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
13. **Climate Science: A Climate Projection**
   - *Question:* Which emissions, model, and adaptation assumptions shape it?
   - *Key Focus:* Present conditional ranges rather than prediction certainty. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
14. **Climate Science: A Cold Day Refutes Warming?**
   - *Question:* What does one event tell us about a long-term distribution?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare temporal scales and variability. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
15. **Climate Science: One Climate Data Product**
   - *Question:* How did raw measurements become a published map?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow collection, adjustment, validation, and review. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Climate Science: Trend to Attribution**
   - *Question:* Which fingerprints link measured change to specific drivers?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare competing explanations and uncertainty. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
17. **Climate Science: Measuring a Climate Variable**
   - *Question:* Which instrument and processing yielded a reported value?
   - *Key Focus:* State units, calibration, coverage, and baseline. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
18. **Climate Science: Global Mean and Local Experience**
   - *Question:* Why can a regional pattern differ from the global mean?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare circulation, land use, and sample scale. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
19. **Climate Science: Models and Field Records**
   - *Question:* What can a simulation reproduce and where does it diverge?
   - *Key Focus:* Use held-out observations and ensemble spread. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
20. **Climate Science: Internal Variability**
   - *Question:* How much change can arise without a new external forcing?
   - *Key Focus:* Use appropriate record and model comparison. Evidence base: IPCC assessment methods, calibrated climate records, proxy and satellite data, model evaluations, field measurements, and local impact studies.
