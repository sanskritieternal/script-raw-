# Selected primary-source note: philanthropy

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed cases BANK-317-041 and BANK-317-042.

The Ford Foundation's BUILD program page describes five years of flexible operating support plus institutional-strengthening support: https://www.fordfoundation.org/work/our-grants/building-institutions-and-networks/ . Its commissioned 2022 NIRAS developmental evaluation reports grantee interviews, surveys, financial analyses and contribution-oriented assessment, and notes that longer-run mission impacts are difficult to observe in the four-year window: https://www.fordfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/build-evaluation-final-report-1.pdf . These are program/commissioned evaluation sources, not a randomized counterfactual or proof of reduced inequality. Date the evaluation, distinguish reported organizational capacity from beneficiary outcomes, and seek grantee/recipient views independently.

Lever for Change's first-party Yield Giving Open Call page describes a March 2023 launch, 6,353 applications, peer/external review and March 2024 awards to 361 US and US-territory organizations: https://leverforchange.org/open-calls/yield-giving-open-call/ . This documents the published selection process and award counts. It does not prove that selection was equally accessible to every eligible group, that all reviewers shared one definition of impact, or that the grants later caused community outcomes. Avoid treating unsuccessful applications as evidence of poor work. Verify program year/eligibility before discussing contemporary opportunities.
