## Primary records

- Harvard CS50x 2025, AI lecture notes: https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/notes/ai/ . CS50 describes its own AI Duck as a debugging and conceptual-help tool, while saying external generative AI was not allowed in that course edition.
- Harvard CS50x 2025, Lecture 0 notes: https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/notes/0/ . The course says CS50.ai could assist but should not give entire problem answers; it states course-specific restrictions on other AI tools.
- Harvard CS50x 2025, Academic Honesty policy: https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/honesty/ . It explicitly permits CS50-owned AI assistance and bars other AI that suggests or completes answers to questions or lines of code in that course edition.
- Harvard CS50x 2025, Lecture 2 debugging notes: https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/notes/2/ . It places the Duck alongside other debugging techniques.
