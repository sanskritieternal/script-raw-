# Digital biology primary-source follow-up — 2026-10-24

## BANK-336-040 — 2023 Human Pangenome Reference Consortium draft

- Primary paper bibliographic record and abstract hosted by NIST, published 10 May 2023: https://www.nist.gov/publications/draft-human-pangenome-reference . Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-05896-x .
- This *draft* comprised 47 phased diploid assemblies. Its authors report added sequence and improved variant discovery against GRCh38 under the study's short-read comparison.
- Limit: the NIST abstract was accessible; the Nature full-text page redirected to a restricted sign-in during this review. The 2023 first release is not a complete census of human variation or a clinical diagnostic standard. Any numerical performance comparison belongs to the study's specified data and workflow, not every population or sequencing method.

## BANK-336-041 — 2022 Tabula Sapiens

- Primary consortium paper, *Science* 376 (13 May 2022), author manuscript hosted by NIH PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9812260/ . DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abl4896 .
- The authors describe nearly 500,000 quality-filtered cells from 24 tissues and organs, with many samples from the same donor, permitting cross-organ comparisons under a coordinated protocol.
- Limit: transcript counts and cell labels reflect sampled donors, collection and quality filters. An atlas cannot record every cell state, donor, disease state or causal mechanism. Respect donor privacy and consent; the paper's public aggregate description does not license individual identification.
