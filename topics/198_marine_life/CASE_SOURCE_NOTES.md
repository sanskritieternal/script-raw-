# Marine-life case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary records inform bounded questions, not certification of the archived trending claims.

| Case | Primary record and inspection | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Turritopsis | [Matsumoto and Miglietta 2021, original transcriptome study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34132809/) reports a medusa-to-cyst-to-polyp reversal and stage-specific gene expression. | Reversal under studied conditions does not show an individual is immortal or suggest a human treatment. Species names in older literature require checking. |
| Nodule mining | [International Seabed Authority exploration contract register](https://isa.org.jm/exploration-contracts/polymetallic-nodules/) lists exploration contractors and dates; its [environmental-impact assessment register](https://isa.org.jm/protection-of-the-marine-environment/environmental-impact-assessments/) describes collector test documents. Accessed 2026-10-24. | Exploration and tests do not demonstrate an approved commercial harvest; contract status and environmental evidence can change. |
| Barreleye | [MBARI 2009 report of its original observation and study](https://www.mbari.org/news/researchers-solve-mystery-of-deep-sea-fish-with-tubular-eyes-and-transparent-head/) identifies rotating tubular eyes in *Macropinna microstoma*. | A few ROV observations do not characterize every individual or species in the family. |

Other new questions are proposal scopes only. Their fossil, toxicology, aging and sardine-run premises require separate primary-source checks before production. The source pages were readable at research time; no full systematic literature review was attempted.
