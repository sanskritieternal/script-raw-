### Water-column conditions and physiology — foundation

Subcategory `CATALOG-198-R01` · foundation.

<a id="bank-198-010"></a>
#### BANK-198-010 — The Vertical Ocean: Journey from the Sunlit Surface to the Challenger Deep

**Video question:** How do temperature, light, and pressure transform marine life as you descend 36,000 feet into the ocean abyss?

**Individual coverage scope:** The five vertical ocean zones: Epipelagic (sunlight), Mesopelagic (twilight), Bathypelagic (midnight), Abyssopelagic (abyss), and Hadalpelagic (trenches), pressure gradients (1 atm every 33 feet), and biological adaptations.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-198-012"></a>
#### BANK-198-012 — Buoyancy Engineering: Swim Bladders, Squalene Livers, and Neutral Equilibrium

**Video question:** How do fish hover motionless at 50 feet without floating to the surface or sinking to the bottom like stones?

**Individual coverage scope:** Gas gland rete mirabile concentrating oxygen into swim bladders against immense pressure gradients, barotrauma stomach eversion when brought up too fast, and sharks using oil-rich squalene livers.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-198-013"></a>
#### BANK-198-013 — Osmoregulation in Marine Fish: Drinking Seawater and Excreting Brine via Chloride Cells

**Video question:** Why would a freshwater goldfish die of dehydration in the ocean, while saltwater tuna drink ocean water continuously?

**Individual coverage scope:** Osmotic pressure gradient (water flowing out of fish into saltier ocean), specialized gill chloride cells actively pumping out sodium and chloride ions, concentrated urine, and salmon transitioning between fresh and saltwater.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-198-026 — The Hadal Zone: Mariana Trench Pressures Exceeding 1,000 Atmospheres and Snailfish
