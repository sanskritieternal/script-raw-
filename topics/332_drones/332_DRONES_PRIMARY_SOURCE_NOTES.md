# Drones primary-source follow-up — 2026-10-24

## BANK-332-040 — 2018 Kīlauea UAS response

- Primary institutional account: US Department of the Interior/USGS, *DOI Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS) Kīlauea Eruption Response*, submitted 7 December 2018: https://eros.usgs.gov/doi-remote-sensing-activities/2018/usgs/doi-unmanned-aircraft-system-uas-kilauea-eruption-response .
- USGS describes authorized coordination with FAA, emergency managers and landowners, and visible/thermal imaging, gas sensors, digital elevation models and orthophotos used for monitoring and situational awareness.
- Limit: the account is the responding agency's description of one 2018 event, not an independent estimate of causal safety outcomes or a current authorization manual. Do not give flight, rescue or eruption-hazard instructions. Protect affected residents' privacy and dignity.

## BANK-332-041 — 2019 UTM TCL4 demonstration

- Primary NASA technical report record: *Flight Demonstration of Unmanned Aircraft System Traffic Management at Technical Capability Level 4*, report ID 20205002447, acquired 19 May 2020, describing May–August 2019 demonstrations: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205002447 .
- NASA reports two FAA-designated test sites and says results indicated viability of its UTM concept while also identifying gaps before routine, safe and efficient operation.
- Limit: the report record/abstract was inspected, not the full PDF. A controlled research demonstration is not certification, broad safety proof, or permission for present-day flight. Rules are jurisdiction- and date-specific; check a dated aviation authority before any operational account.
