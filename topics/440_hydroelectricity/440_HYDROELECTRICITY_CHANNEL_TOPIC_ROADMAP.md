## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Hydroelectricity across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Hydroelectricity Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Hydroelectricity.
- **Tier 2: Hydroelectricity Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Hydroelectricity.
- **Tier 3: Hydroelectricity Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Hydroelectricity.
- **Tier 4: Hydroelectricity Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Hydroelectricity.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

2. **Hydroelectricity: A Hydropower Project in Context**
   - *Question:* What do generation and river records show?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify project, flow period, and license. Evidence base: FERC project licenses and inspections, USGS river data, metered generation, flow and sediment records, environmental monitoring, and affected-community testimony.
3. **Hydroelectricity: Communities around the Reservoir**
