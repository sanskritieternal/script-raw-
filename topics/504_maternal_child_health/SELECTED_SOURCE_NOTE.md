## Bounded premise

E-MOTIVE randomized 80 secondary-level hospitals in Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa and Tanzania; two hospitals did not receive assignments, with analysis from 78 hospitals. Its population was patients having vaginal delivery in participating hospitals. The intervention combined a calibrated blood-collection drape, first-response treatment bundle and implementation strategy. The primary composite was severe postpartum hemorrhage, laparotomy for bleeding or maternal death from bleeding. Reported event proportions among those with outcome data were 1.6% in intervention hospitals and 4.3% in usual-care hospitals (risk ratio 0.40, 95% CI 0.32?0.50). Detection was 93.1% versus 51.1%, and bundle adherence 91.2% versus 19.4%.

## Claim and access limits

The randomized contrast concerns the package, not the independent effect of a drape or any treatment component. The primary endpoint is a composite, not a statistically established effect on maternal death alone; the reported all-cause maternal deaths were 17 versus 28 with a wide interval. Do not generalize to cesarean births, home births, other health-system settings, or current clinical practice without new evidence. No treatment steps, thresholds or patient guidance belong in this proposal.
