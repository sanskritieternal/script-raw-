# Traditional-clothing case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected nomination and statutory records support named textile questions; they do not certify an outfit as timeless or exclusive to one community.

| Case | Primary record inspected | Claim and access limit |
|---|---|---|
| Jamdani | [UNESCO 2013 Jamdani nomination entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-art-of-jamdani-weaving-00879?RL=00879) describes handloom production around Dhaka; [Jamdani Shilpa community consent document](https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/18730.pdf) records artisan representatives’ nomination consent. | The nomination is not a census of all makers or current wages and market conditions; consent to nomination is not consent to film any individual. Public entry and indexed consent text accessible; field practice remains to be checked. |
| Tartan registration | [Scottish Register of Tartans Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2008/7/enacted/data.xht?view=snippet&wrap=true) establishes an electronic preservation/information register; [official Register account](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/aboutUs) dates its launch to 2009. | Registration date is not design origin or proof of exclusive clan ownership. Historical association and present use require separate evidence. |
