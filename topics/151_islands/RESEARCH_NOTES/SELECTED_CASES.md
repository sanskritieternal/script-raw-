# Selected island source cases

Research checked: 2026-10-24. Status: bounded premise research, not script or idea approval.

## BANK-151-042: Hunga Tonga-Hunga Ha'apai

NASA's [2019 island observations](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/145877/making-a-connection-in-the-kingdom-of-tonga) describe land formed in the 2014-15 eruption and studied over several years. The [USGS 2022 eruption account](https://www.usgs.gov/news/featured-story/massive-volcanic-eruption-and-tsunami-informs-plan-future-eruptions-sea-level) says the January 14-15 explosions destroyed much of the island. These are dated agency observations of two separate phases.

**Limit:** The archived hook compresses creation and destruction into 'days'; that is inaccurate for the land NASA studied. 'Much of' is not all land, and this note does not quantify new coastline, land volume or tsunami impact. Use satellite dates, mapped outlines and Tongan authorities for a production account; avoid treating an uninhabited volcanic island as isolated from harms to nearby communities.

## BANK-151-043: Tuvalu digital nation

In a [2023 statement to the UN](https://estatements.un.org/estatements/31.0070/20230309100000000/MJb0P61VYRtc/5axUXzqXgbzf_en.pdf), Tuvalu described its Future Now project as including digital archives of history and cultural practices. The [2023-24 UN country implementation plan](https://pacific.un.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/Tuvalu%20Country%20Implementation%20Plan%202023-2024%20%28Online%29.pdf) describes planned digital administration and cultural continuity. The [Tuvalu initiative's own account](https://www.tuvalu.tv/about) states the COP27 announcement; treat it as the initiative's advocacy, not completed implementation evidence.

**Limit:** These records show plans and institutional framing. They do not establish that all cultural records have been digitized, that islands are already lost, or that a metaverse replica by itself determines international legal statehood. Verify completion milestones with Tuvaluan government records and community control of cultural material before scripting.

**Semantic home:** Niche 151 owns island land change and the relation of Tuvalu's geography to adaptation. Volcanology banks own eruption mechanics; language and global-cultures banks own Ubang language research. Legal statehood and maritime boundaries need their own primary treaty and government records.
