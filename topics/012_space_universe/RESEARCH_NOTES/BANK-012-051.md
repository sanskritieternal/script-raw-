# BANK-012-051 — Pluto and the 2006 IAU classification

Status: bounded primary-resolution review for a topic proposal, not a verified script. Inspected 2026-10-24.

## Primary source inspected

- [International Astronomical Union, 2006 General Assembly resolution results](https://www.iau.org/IAU/Iau/News/PR2006/iau-2006-general-assembly-resolution-votes.aspx). Resolution 5A defines three Solar System categories using orbit around the Sun, approximate roundness and orbital-neighbourhood clearing. Resolution 6A explicitly classifies Pluto as a dwarf planet and prototype of a trans-Neptunian category. The IAU page also records that the proposed 5B “classical planet” amendment did not pass.

## Editorial boundary

“Demoted” is the archived pitch's shorthand for a classification decision, not evidence that Pluto physically changed. The resolution is explicitly about Solar System bodies and is not by itself a definition for exoplanets. A future episode should explain how the competing criteria classify Pluto, why scientists debate those criteria, and what the vote did and did not decide. This note inspects the IAU resolution text, not individual delegates' motives, later planetary-science critiques or current candidate counts. Check any later rule or object-status change before scripting.
