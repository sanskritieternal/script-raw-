## Bounded premise

NIMH describes RDoC as a research framework that organizes investigation around dimensional psychological and biological constructs across conventional diagnosis boundaries and multiple units of analysis. It is intended to support research, not to classify an individual for clinical care. Future assessment or treatment improvement is a research aim, not an outcome established by these institutional descriptions.

## Clinical and evidence limits

NIMH is the framework's sponsor; these pages state its intended use, not independent validation of prediction, diagnostic accuracy or clinical benefit. The matrix can evolve, and specific studies vary in design and result. Do not convert construct labels into self-tests, infer a diagnosis from behavior or neural data, advise medication or psychotherapy, or imply that existing clinical categories have been displaced. Any benefit claim would require separate trial or validation evidence in the specified population and setting.
