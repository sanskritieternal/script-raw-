## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Central Banking across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Central Banking Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Central Banking.
- **Tier 2: Central Banking Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Central Banking.
- **Tier 3: Central Banking Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Central Banking.
- **Tier 4: Central Banking Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Central Banking.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Central Banking: Why a Central Bank Was Established**
   - *Question:* Which problem was one central bank created to address?
   - *Key Focus:* Use the founding law and contemporary debates for a named jurisdiction. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
2. **Central Banking: A Central Bank Decision in Context**
   - *Question:* What did one widely cited policy decision achieve and fail to achieve?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare its stated objective, available evidence, and later outcomes. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
3. **Central Banking: The People Behind a Policy Decision**
   - *Question:* Whose work or experience is absent from the official account of one decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Use attributed staff, legislative, and affected-community records. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
4. **Central Banking: What a Central Bank Is Mandated to Do**
   - *Question:* Which duties does a named central bank have under law?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish its legal mandate from commercial banking and fiscal policy. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
5. **Central Banking: Policy Tradeoffs Under Uncertainty**
   - *Question:* How did a central bank weigh conflicting indicators before one decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare forecasts, minutes, and data available at the time. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Central Banking: Why a Policy Had an Unexpected Effect**
   - *Question:* Which explanations remain plausible for a policy outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate measured effects from competing causal accounts. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
7. **Central Banking: The Infrastructure of Monetary Operations**
   - *Question:* Which operational change altered how a central bank implements policy?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace a dated payment, reserve, or trading-system change. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
8. **Central Banking: Who Had the Vote?**
   - *Question:* How did governance rules shape a consequential central-bank decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Map appointment, voting, dissent, and recorded reasoning. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
9. **Central Banking: A Crisis Response Timeline**
   - *Question:* What did a central bank do at each stage of one financial shock?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated announcements and balance-sheet data; distinguish aims from results. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
10. **Central Banking: From Policy Rate to Borrowing Cost**
   - *Question:* How can a policy-rate change reach households and firms?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace banking, market, and expectation channels in one jurisdiction. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Central Banking: Credibility After a Policy Error**
   - *Question:* How did an institution respond when a policy missed its objective?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare stated target, observed result, and later explanation. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
12. **Central Banking: Two Monetary Frameworks**
   - *Question:* How did one central bank's framework change between two periods?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare mandates, instruments, communication, and outcomes consistently. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
13. **Central Banking: A Conditional Policy Challenge**
   - *Question:* Which constraint would shape a future central-bank response?
   - *Key Focus:* State a dated scenario and alternative responses without prediction. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
14. **Central Banking: Testing a Central-Bank Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a common claim about money creation match institutional records?
   - *Key Focus:* Define the claim and examine balance sheets, law, and operational rules. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
15. **Central Banking: One Decision Through Its Papers**
   - *Question:* What do minutes, speeches, and data reveal about one policy decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated source trail and identify what was unknown then. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Central Banking: The Terms of a Liquidity Facility**
   - *Question:* Which eligibility and collateral terms governed one central-bank facility?
   - *Key Focus:* Use the official facility notice and distinguish it from a consumer product. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
17. **Central Banking: Policy and Household Payments**
   - *Question:* How did a policy-rate move affect a defined household cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a dated, labeled example and actual contract-reset rules. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
18. **Central Banking: Timing in Monetary Transmission**
   - *Question:* Why can a policy change have different effects over different horizons?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare immediate market effects with later lending and price data. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
19. **Central Banking: The Cost of Central-Bank Operations**
   - *Question:* Which operational or interest costs appeared on a central bank's balance sheet?
   - *Key Focus:* Read dated accounts and explain accounting limits; avoid treating them as retail fees. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
20. **Central Banking: The Boundary Between Central Bank and Consumer Regulator**
   - *Question:* Which institution handles a specific banking consumer protection?
   - *Key Focus:* Map legal mandates in one jurisdiction and identify any shared duties. Evidence base: founding law, dated monetary-policy statements and minutes, official facility terms, central-bank accounts, and independently sourced macroeconomic data.
