# Waste management source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-508-043 - New York City curbside organics rollout

- Primary operator service record: New York City Department of Sanitation (DSNY), *Curbside Composting*, https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/collection/residents/curbside-composting.page (inspected 2026-10-24). DSNY says service is citywide and weekly; separation enforcement began April 1, 2025. Service availability and required separation are not proof that all eligible material is collected or composted.
- Primary operator data: DSNY, *Annual Curbside Collection* and *Annual Collection & Diversion Totals*, https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/resources/statistics/dsny-annual-curbside-collection.page and https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/resources/statistics/total-annual-collection-diversion.page (inspected 2026-10-24). These pages describe scaled truck-tonnage measurements and fiscal-year files; the all-material totals include some outside-program reports supplied to DSNY without independent verification.
- Independent city fiscal assessment: New York City Independent Budget Office, *Curbside Composting: Current Trends in New York City's Organics Collection*, April 2026, https://www.ibo.nyc.gov/assets/ibo/downloads/pdf/environment/2026/2026-april-curbside-composting-current-trends-in-nycs-organics-collection.pdf (inspected 2026-10-24). It compares collected organics over fiscal years and notes that FY2024 organics diversion was 1.4% despite recovered collection volume. Read its denominators and definitions before quoting a rate.
- Limits: City service and annual curbside weights are collection measures, not direct evidence that every load became finished compost, avoided landfill methane, or had acceptable contamination. Organics can have multiple processing routes; check facilities and final disposition before making those claims. Program rules and annual data may change. This is a New York City case, distinct from San Francisco household sorting requirements (BANK-396-045). No handling or sanitation instructions.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-508-043 is a dated municipal collection/accounting case. BANK-508-032 covers compost feedstock quality, BANK-508-034 general waste double-counting, BANK-507-042 wastewater reuse and BANK-501-037 wastewater surveillance.
