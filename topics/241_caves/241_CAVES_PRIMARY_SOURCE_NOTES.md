# Selected institutional source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-241-042: Lascaux

- French Ministry of Culture, Lascaux conservation history: https://www.culture.gouv.fr/regions/drac-nouvelle-aquitaine/actualites/la-grotte-de-lascaux-nous-emerveille-depuis-80-ans
- Ministry archaeology portal, original-cave access and replicas: https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/lascaux/fr/informations-pratiques
- The ministry dates the public closure to April 1963 and now distinguishes the restricted original from public facsimiles. Visitor pressure and environmental alterations are part of its account; causal details need conservation records, not a one-factor story. Public permission for a replica is not access to the original or filming permission.

## BANK-241-043: Lechuguilla

- US National Park Service Lechuguilla Cave public profile: https://www.nps.gov/cave/learn/nature/lechuguilla_cave.htm
- NPS says access is limited to approved scientific researchers, survey/exploration teams and management-related trips. The page does not confer permission or describe current individual permit approval. No entrances, route detail or workaround is needed for the editorial case.

## BANK-241-044: Mammoth surveyed length

- US National Park Service Mammoth Cave FAQ, inspected 24 October 2026: https://www.nps.gov/maca/faqs.htm
- NPS reports more than 426 miles surveyed and mapped at the time of access. This is a changing surveyed-passage total, not the cave's proven total extent or a permanent world-record figure; the live figure and survey date need recheck before production. No unpublished passage location should be inferred.
