# Selected primary-source note — metalworking

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-401-044`.

- [US Patent 16,082, Henry Bessemer, “Improvement in the manufacture of iron and steel” (11 November 1856)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US16082A/en): the specification describes claimed decarbonization/refinement of molten crude iron and later says the inventor does not claim air or steam injection into molten iron in general, acknowledging it was known before. It also describes variation by feedstock and practical quality concerns.

The patent is the inventor’s legal and technical account, not independent proof of production scale, industrial success, safety or sole priority. It is OCR-transcribed and image/PDF verification is needed before quotation. The proposed scope is documentary analysis only; molten-metal processing is hazardous and no operational parameters or workshop procedure should be derived from this note.
