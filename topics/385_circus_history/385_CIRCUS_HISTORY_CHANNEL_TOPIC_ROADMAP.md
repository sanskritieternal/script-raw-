## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Circus History: Which Circus Tradition?**
   - *Question:* How did a named troupe and place shape a performance form?
   - *Key Focus:* Avoid single-origin story. Evidence base: Library of Congress circus poster collection, programs, contracts, contemporary press, oral histories, and conservation records.
2. **Circus History: A Circus Poster and Its Act**
   - *Question:* What does advertisement claim and what can corroborate it?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare programs and press. Evidence base: Library of Congress circus poster collection, programs, contracts, contemporary press, oral histories, and conservation records.
3. **Circus History: Workers beyond the Ring**
   - *Question:* Who built, transported, cooked, and maintained a touring show?
   - *Key Focus:* Use payroll and oral history. Evidence base: Library of Congress circus poster collection, programs, contracts, contemporary press, oral histories, and conservation records.
4. **Circus History: A Circus as Performance and Business**
