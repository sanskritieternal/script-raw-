## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Model Building across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Model Building Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Model Building.
- **Tier 2: Model Building Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Model Building.
- **Tier 3: Model Building Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Model Building.
- **Tier 4: Model Building Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Model Building.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Model Building: A Scale-Model Practice**
   - *Question:* Which dated workshop or kit documents a modeling technique?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify subject, scale, maker, and purpose. Evidence base: dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies.
2. **Model Building: A Model beside Its Source**
   - *Question:* Which features of a named model match or simplify the original?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare verified source measurements and model. Evidence base: dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies.
3. **Model Building: Makers of the Model**
   - *Question:* Who researched, drafted, fabricated, painted, and displayed it?
   - *Key Focus:* Check workshop and collection records. Evidence base: dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies.
4. **Model Building: What Scale Means**
   - *Question:* How do model and original dimensions relate?
   - *Key Focus:* State ratio, units, and a measured example. Evidence base: dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies.
5. **Model Building: Accuracy versus Readability**
   - *Question:* When does a scaled detail need exaggeration to be visible?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare viewing distance, medium, and design aim. Evidence base: dimensioned source plans, model and kit versions, workshop notes, maker credits, museum model collections, condition reports, and bounded visitor studies.
