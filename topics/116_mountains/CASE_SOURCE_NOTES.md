# Selected mountain case-source notes

Inspected 2026-10-24. These notes bound premises for proposed questions. They are not a complete expedition, geology, medical or hazard source review; each topic keeps `verified_sources` empty until production research.

## Two mountain-building mechanisms — BANK-116-044

- The [National Park Service Teton Fault page](https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/nature/faults.htm) describes a normal fault formed during regional stretching and movement that raises the range relative to Jackson Hole. The [U.S. Geological Survey Cascade Range account](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/cascade-mountain-range-oregon) describes Oregon’s Cascades as largely volcanic, above Juan de Fuca plate subduction.
- These two named regions show distinct mechanisms. The archived fold/fault-block/dome/volcanic list is a teaching classification, not a claim that every feature has one pure origin. Later glaciers and erosion change the relief in both places.

## K2 in 2008 — BANK-116-045

- The [Norwegian Climbing Federation investigation report](https://klatring.no/siteassets/dokumenter/sikkerhet/ulykker/k2en-1.pdf) reconstructs the events and coordination difficulties from available participant evidence. The [American Alpine Club's 2008 Pakistan overview](https://publications.americanalpineclub.org/articles/12200926001) reports 74 climbers from ten expeditions attempted K2 that season and eleven deaths during or after the 1 August avalanche near the Bottleneck.
- The federation report explicitly limits itself to a chronology as far as events could be known and resists assigning one expedition sole blame. The archive's single-serac-collapse account is too compressed: a script needs timed routes, rope state, separate falls and witness disagreements before making a causal sequence. Counts depend on whether they refer to attempts, summits, one day or the whole season.

## Messner's 1980 Everest ascent — BANK-116-046

- [Messner's own biographical record](https://www.reinholdmessner.com/en/messner-vita/) lists the 1980 first solo Everest ascent and separately lists his 1978 first Everest ascent without supplemental oxygen with Peter Habeler. [His expedition account and route record](https://terra-incognita.page/share/en/everest-1980-exp/) provide a route narrative for the 1980 north-side ascent.
- Define “solo” and “without supplemental oxygen” separately, including approach and base support. A personal account is a primary witness but needs contemporary independent corroboration for contested details. The achievement provides no safe practice advice for others at altitude.

## Matterhorn permafrost — existing BANK-116-026

- [WSL Institute for Snow and Avalanche Research SLF's Matterhorn case report](https://www.slf.ch/en/news/water-causes-rock-to-shift-on-the-matterhorn/) describes a monitored rock pillar on the Hörnligrat that collapsed on 13 June 2023 after measured water ingress and progressive movement. It reports nine years of observations and modeling of water and permafrost effects.
- This is strong evidence for a specific rockfall mechanism, not proof that the entire Matterhorn is “falling” or that all Alpine rockfall has one cause. Local geology, water and monitoring history matter; a current hazard statement needs current authority bulletins.
