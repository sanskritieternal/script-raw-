## BANK-265-042: W. Archer Jacquard hand loom

- Custodian: Science Museum Group, [Jacquard Hand Loom](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8405056/jacquard-hand-loom), object Y1973.43, inspected 2026-10-24. The catalog dates it circa 1910, credits W. Archer of Bolton, calls it hand operated, notes Manchester students' teaching use, and describes cards, pins, warp lifting and the operator's foot lever.
- Access/claim limit: catalog mechanism text describes this specimen and a simplified operation. It does not by itself establish the production history, costs, worker effects or all Jacquard looms. Do not call it a fully automatic loom or equate card coding with removing weaver agency. The catalog warns that records are revised.

## BANK-265-043: UAE Al Sadu

- Institutional record: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, [Al Sadu, traditional weaving skills in the United Arab Emirates](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/al-sadu-traditional-weaving-skills-in-the-united-arab-emirates-02223), nomination 02223, search-indexed text inspected 2026-10-24. It reports urgent-safeguarding inscription in 2011, a 2011-2023 program, and Representative List inscription in 2025. Its account describes women weavers working and teaching in groups, with learners progressing from wool sorting.
- Corroborating UNESCO index: [United Arab Emirates country page](https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/united-arab-emirates-AE), inspected 2026-10-24, lists the 2025 element and safeguarding program.
- Access/claim limit: direct UNESCO page fetch required JavaScript/captcha, so this check relied on UNESCO-indexed text. The listing's continuity and economic-benefit language is institutional reporting, not an independent outcome measurement. Seek current weaver testimony and consent for any personal narrative. Distinguish this UAE element from separately listed Al Sadu nominations in other states and from Bangladesh Jamdani in BANK-247-043.
