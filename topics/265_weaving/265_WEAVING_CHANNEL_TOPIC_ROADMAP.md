## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Weaving across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Weaving Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Weaving.
- **Tier 2: Weaving Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Weaving.
- **Tier 3: Weaving Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Weaving.
- **Tier 4: Weaving Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Weaving.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Weaving: Early Evidence for a Weave**
   - *Question:* What dated cloth documents one community's technique?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish earliest survival from first practice. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
2. **Weaving: A Widely Known Woven Cloth**
   - *Question:* What makes this example distinctive and what varies?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare loom, fiber, maker, use, and local versions. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
3. **Weaving: Preparers Behind the Weaver**
   - *Question:* Who grows fiber, spins yarn, dyes, warps, and finishes?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit labor and income with consent. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
4. **Weaving: How a Loom Makes Cloth**
   - *Question:* How do warp, weft, tension, and pattern interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one named loom type and maker explanation. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
5. **Weaving: Pattern from Loom Constraints**
   - *Question:* How do loom setup and yarn choices shape design?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare drafts, textiles, and practitioner accounts. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Weaving: A Weave's Uncertain Workshop**
   - *Question:* What evidence could place an anonymous cloth?
   - *Key Focus:* Check structure, dyes, provenance, and archival gaps. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
7. **Weaving: A New Loom in a Community**
   - *Question:* How did it change labor, output, and design?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated production and weaver views. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
8. **Weaving: A Master Weaver's Teaching**
   - *Question:* How did one weaver transmit or adapt skill?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace apprentices and work without sole-founder claims. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
9. **Weaving: After a Supply Shock**
   - *Question:* How did one yarn or market change affect workshops?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated material, price, and employment records. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
10. **Weaving: From Yarn to Finished Weave**
   - *Question:* What stages and choices shape a cloth?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow warping, weaving, joining, finishing, and use with consent. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Weaving: A Weaving Workshop's Orders**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured change?
   - *Key Focus:* Check price, labor, fashion, imports, and counting. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
12. **Weaving: One Weave Across Generations**
   - *Question:* How did tools, fibers, patterns, and clients change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated textiles and weaver accounts. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
13. **Weaving: Training Future Weavers**
   - *Question:* Which supports do practitioners choose?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare apprenticeships, pay, materials, space, and markets. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
14. **Weaving: Every Pattern Has One Meaning?**
   - *Question:* How do weavers explain motifs across contexts?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute explanations and note variation. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
15. **Weaving: One Weave from Loom to Wearer**
   - *Question:* How was it made, sold, and used?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace time, cost, credit, and consent. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Weaving: A Woven Commission**
   - *Question:* What size, use, pattern, and budget shaped it?
   - *Key Focus:* Record client and weaver decisions. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
17. **Weaving: Yarn and Loom Selection**
   - *Question:* Why choose particular fiber, dye, and loom?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare properties, supply, cost, and maker preference. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
18. **Weaving: Setting Up a Loom**
   - *Question:* Which preparation decisions determine the result?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe with permission; avoid treating a demo as all practice. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
19. **Weaving: Planning the Pattern**
   - *Question:* How does a weaver place color and structure?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask the maker about revisions and inherited design. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
20. **Weaving: Design and Labor Credit**
   - *Question:* Who conceived, warped, wove, and finished a cloth?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace roles and compensation. Evidence base: provenanced cloth, dated workshop and supply records, structure analysis, and consented weaver and user accounts.
