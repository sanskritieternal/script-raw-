# Case-source note: Fast Transit Interstellar Probe concept

Checked 2026-10-24. NASA’s [2021 NIAC Phase II project page](https://www.nasa.gov/general/extreme-solar-sailing-for-breakthrough-space-exploration/) describes an extreme solar-sailing study, with a proposed Fast Transit Interstellar Probe aiming at 500 AU in ten years. It identifies material, close-solar-pass, mass-budget, control, communications and power work. For scale, NASA JPL?s [Voyager 1 mission page](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/voyager-1/) documents an actual 2012 interstellar-space crossing, already covered by BANK-012-063. NASA [TechPort project record](https://techport.nasa.gov/projects/106030) characterizes the work as a study and materials/architecture investigation.

Limit: This is a funded concept investigation, not a launched, end-to-end tested spacecraft or demonstration of the stated transit. 500 AU is far beyond the heliopause but vastly short of a nearby star; the concept does not transport people or establish an interstellar civilization. Do not infer operational readiness from proposed subsystem testing, or a final mission selection from NIAC funding. TechPort detailed page access was limited in text extraction; the NASA project page is the main inspected evidence.
