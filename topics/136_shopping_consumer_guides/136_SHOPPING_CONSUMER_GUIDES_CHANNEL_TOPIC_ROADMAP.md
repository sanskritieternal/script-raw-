## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Shopping & Consumer Guides across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Cost-Per-Use, Built-to-Last Materials & Consumer Rights (Beginner / Scratch)** — Cost-per-use economics, identifying genuine leather vs bonded vinyl, statutory warranty rights, the Veblen luxury tax, and avoiding fast-fashion traps.
- **Tier 2: Deceptive Pricing, Black Friday Illusions & Affiliate Review Economics (Intermediate)** — CamelCamelCamel price-tracking history, fabricated MSRP markups, the hidden business model of review websites (Wirecutter/affiliate links), and grocery unit-price comparisons.
- **Tier 3: Right to Repair, Warranty Fine Print & Supply Chain Quality Shifting (Upper Intermediate)** — Enshittification of consumer goods, glued batteries and proprietary screws, extended warranty actuarial math, and how brands quietly substitute cheaper parts in second-generation revisions.
- **Tier 4: Algorithmic Price Discrimination, Buy-It-For-Life (BIFL) Curation & The Master Shopper (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Dynamic browser-based surge pricing, private-label manufacturing origins (Costco Kirkland secrets), curating a generational Buy-It-For-Life wardrobe, and consumer defense litigation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Black Friday Illusion: How Retailers Fake Discounts and Inflate Baselines**
   - *Question:* How do major retailers inflate price tags in September just to slap a fake '60% OFF' sticker on them in November?
   - *Key Focus:* Price history tracking algorithms, Black Friday derivative models (cheaper TV panel components made specifically for sales), and psychological anchoring.
2. **Cost-Per-Wear and Cost-Per-Use: The True Mathematics of Smart Buying**
   - *Question:* Why is a $300 Goodyear-welted leather boot drastically cheaper over 10 years than five $60 glued synthetic shoes?
   - *Key Focus:* Cost-per-use calculation formulas, resoleability, maintenance depreciation, and Terry Pratchett's 'Boots Theory' of socioeconomic unfairness.
3. **The Leather Lie: Genuine Leather, Top Grain, and Bonded Vinyl Scams**
   - *Question:* Why does a product labeled '100% Genuine Leather' peel and flake into plastic dust after only six months of use?
   - *Key Focus:* Leather grading hierarchy (Full Grain, Top Grain, Genuine, Bonded leather scrap paste), polyurethane PU coatings, and tactile edge burnishing tests.
4. **The Affiliate Review Conflict: Can You Trust Tech and Mattress Reviews?**
   - *Question:* How do mattress review websites pocket $300 bounties per click, turning independent review sites into corporate shills?
   - *Key Focus:* Affiliate link commission economics, Wirecutter's acquisition by NYT, mattress affiliate lawsuits, and finding genuinely unbiased test labs (Consumer Reports).
5. **Unit Pricing Mastery: How Supermarkets Trick You with Bigger Boxes**
   - *Question:* Why is the giant 'family-size' cereal box frequently more expensive per ounce than buying two smaller boxes on the bottom shelf?
   - *Key Focus:* Price per unit shelf tags, shrinkflation package downsizing, eye-level placement slotting fees, and warehouse club unit math.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Buy It For Life (BIFL): The Science of Unbreakable Kitchenware**
   - *Question:* Why will a $40 raw cast iron skillet or a tri-ply stainless steel pan outlive your grandchildren while Teflon pans die in 2 years?
   - *Key Focus:* PTFE non-stick coating thermal degradation, cast iron polymerized oil seasoning chemistry, 3-ply aluminum-core heat conduction, and rivet construction.
7. **Dynamic Pricing and Browser Tracking: Are You Paying More Because of Your Mac?**
   - *Question:* Do airlines, hotels, and retail sites charge higher prices based on your device, location, and repeated search visits?
   - *Key Focus:* Browser fingerprinting, cookie-based search count triggers, IP geolocation price discrimination, and private browsing test methodologies.
8. **The Kirkland Signature Secret: Who Actually Makes Costco's House Brands?**
   - *Question:* How does Costco negotiate with name-brand giants like Starbucks, Duracell, and Grey Goose to bottle identical goods under their own label?
   - *Key Focus:* Private label contract manufacturing, 14% maximum markup rule, co-packing agreements, and how Costco forces tier-1 quality into discount packaging.
9. **Extended Warranties: Why Financial Planners Call Them Pure Scams**
   - *Question:* Why do electronics retailers push extended protection plans at checkout that carry an 80% profit margin for the store?
   - *Key Focus:* Actuarial failure rate curves, credit card automatic warranty extensions, excluded wear-and-tear clauses, and self-insuring small electronics.
10. **Shrinkflation Unmasked: How Brands Cut 15% of Content Without Lowering Prices**
   - *Question:* How do chip bags add nitrogen gas, chocolate bars widen pyramid indentations, and detergent bottles hide indent bottoms?
   - *Key Focus:* Skimpflation ingredient substitution, subtle container volume reductions, consumer price sensitivity thresholds, and regulatory transparency laws.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Outlet Stores vs Flagship Stores: The Made-for-Outlet Deception**
   - *Question:* Why is 85% of clothing at J.Crew, Coach, and Nike outlet malls manufactured with inferior fabric specifically for discount malls?
   - *Key Focus:* The myth of 'excess inventory clearance', separate manufacturing lines with diamond/dot label markings, polyester blends vs wool, and outlet retail margins.
12. **The Wool, Cashmere, and Synthetic Blend Minefield: Reading Apparel Labels**
   - *Question:* Why does a sweater labeled 'Cashmere Blend' with 3% cashmere feel itchy and pill into balls after two delicate washes?
   - *Key Focus:* Microscopic staple fiber lengths, 100% long-staple virgin wool vs recycled short fiber blends, acrylic cheap synthetics, and fabric burn tests.
13. **The Right to Repair War: Why Apple and John Deere Tried to Outlaw Fixing Your Own Stuff**
   - *Question:* How did software locks and parts pairing make it illegal for farmers to fix their own tractors and consumers to fix broken screens?
   - *Key Focus:* Serialized parts pairing, software diagnostic lockouts, European union repair mandates, and the historic legislative victories of repair advocates.
14. **Car Buying Negotiations: How to Defeat Dealership Finance and Insurance (F&I) Offices**
   - *Question:* How do dealership finance managers quietly add $4,000 in dealer prep, paint protection, and interest rate markups in the back room?
   - *Key Focus:* The four-square sales sheet con, out-the-door (OTD) price negotiations, pre-approved credit union financing, dealer doc fees, and timing end-of-month quotas.
15. **Energy Star and Appliance Longevity: Why New Washers Die in 5 Years While Old Ones Lasted 30**
   - *Question:* Why did modern eco-friendly appliances with sensitive computer motherboards become fragile disposables compared to mechanical timers?
   - *Key Focus:* Electronic control board heat/vibration degradation, plastic water pump impellers, sealed drum bearings, and Speed Queen mechanical simplicity.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Chargebacks, Section 75, and Small Claims: The Consumer Nuclear Options**
   - *Question:* How do credit card dispute protocols legally force merchants to refund your money when customer service ignores you?
   - *Key Focus:* Fair Credit Billing Act, merchant chargeback penalty thresholds, documentation evidence binders, Section 75 UK protections, and small claims court procedures.
17. **The Resale Economy: Grailed, The RealReal, and Poshmark Counterfeit Risks**
   - *Question:* How did fast-growing luxury resale platforms accidentally sell thousands of counterfeit designer bags despite '100% authentic' claims?
   - *Key Focus:* High-volume underpaid authenticator quotas, super-fake Hermès and Chanel manufacturing, digital NFC authentication chips, and secondary market arbitrage.
18. **Minimalism vs Consumerism: The Freedom of Owning Less and Buying Better**
   - *Question:* Can curating an intentional, high-quality capsule wardrobe and durable home eliminate the psychological stress of endless consumption?
   - *Key Focus:* Hedonic treadmill adaptation, visual clutter cortisol reduction, intentional budgeting, and the mental freedom of owning tools that never break.
19. **AI Personal Shoppers and Algorithmic Price Snipers: The Future of Consumer Power**
   - *Question:* Will autonomous AI browser agents crawl dark retail corners, negotiate bulk discounts, and auto-purchase when prices hit historic bottoms?
   - *Key Focus:* Autonomous price tracking bots, API-driven inventory scraping, AI coupon stacking, synthetic review filtering, and the battle between buyer bots and retailer firewalls.
20. **The Master Consumer Architecture: The Lifetime Smart-Purchasing System**
   - *Question:* What complete life operational system unites cost-per-use calculations, material inspection, warranty automation, and intentional ownership?
   - *Key Focus:* The 48-hour purchase delay rule, material specification checklist, warranty digital vault tracking, seasonal clearance calendars, and building generational wealth.
