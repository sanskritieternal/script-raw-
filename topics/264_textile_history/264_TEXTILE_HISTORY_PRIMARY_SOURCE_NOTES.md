## BANK-264-042: Rajah quilt

- Custodian: National Gallery of Australia, [The Rajah Quilt - 1841](https://nga.gov.au/stories-ideas/the-rajah-quilt-1841/), 15 June 2006; search result inspected 2026-10-24. The page names Kezia Hayter and unidentified women of HMS Rajah and describes an inscribed June 1841 patchwork coverlet. It says the quilt's ownership for the following 147 years remains unresolved.
- Supplementary custodian discussion: [The Rajah Quilt Up Close](https://nga.gov.au/on-demand/the-rajah-quilt-up-close/), National Gallery of Australia; search text inspected 2026-10-24. Its speakers explicitly say the overall designer was undocumented and Hayter's design leadership is conjecture.
- Access/claim limit: direct fetch of the first page returned 403 during this check; institution-indexed text was available. Do not identify unnamed makers or treat conjectural leadership, individual motives or later custody as proven. This is stitched patchwork, not a weaving-mechanism case.
