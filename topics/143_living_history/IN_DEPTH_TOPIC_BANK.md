#### BANK-143-042 — How can a Roman domestic-space reconstruction show who moved, worked and ate in different rooms?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare archaeological plans, objects, wear, light, water and textual accounts in a named house or settlement; distinguish modern room labels from demonstrated ancient use.

#### BANK-143-043 — What work happened before a medieval wool garment reached its wearer?
