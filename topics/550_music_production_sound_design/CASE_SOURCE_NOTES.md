# Selected case research: “Tomorrow Never Knows” tape-loop mix

Research checked 24 October 2026 for proposed **BANK-550-051**. This is a scoped research lead, not a verified production-ready source set.

## Sources inspected

- [The Beatles, “Tomorrow Never Knows”](https://www.thebeatles.com/tomorrow-never-knows) (official artist catalog, accessed 24 October 2026): describes tape loops prepared by the band being mixed in and out of the backing. It identifies the song as the final *Revolver* track but first recorded.
- [Abbey Road Studios, “The Beatles' Revolver Special Edition out now”](https://www.abbeyroad.com/news/the-beatles-revolver-special-edition-out-now-3240) (27 October 2022): dates the first *Revolver* session to 6 April 1966 in EMI Studio Three and identifies the producer, engineers, vocal treatment and loops.
- [Abbey Road Studios, Brian Kehew and Kevin Ryan Q&A](https://www.abbeyroad.com/news/brian-kehew-kevin-ryan-answer-your-lectures-questions-2564) (7 June 2019): Ryan describes home-prepared tape loops, separate playback machines, mixer channels and faders used to bring sounds in at different times.

## Claim and access limits

These are official artist/studio pages, but the workflow detail is retrospective historian testimony, not direct inspection of the 1966 multitrack, session logs or original tape loops. The public pages do not establish every loop's creator, exact machine count, signal routing or a first-ever invention claim. The 2022 account also promotes a reissue; its interpretive praise is not evidence of historical priority. No licensed session audio or paid session-sheet replica was inspected. The proposal asks how this particular mix was assembled, not how to duplicate it or infer the precise sound of an isolated channel.
