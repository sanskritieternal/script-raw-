## BANK-358-040 — World Magnetic Model 2025

- [NOAA NCEI's WMM product page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model) identifies WMM2025 as a main-field model released 17 December 2024 with a stated validity period to late 2029 and separately identifies a higher-resolution model. The [WMM2025 metadata record](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ngdc%3AWMM2025) gives its model identity and institutional provenance.
- The model predicts Earth's large-scale main field and its change for a specified date and location. It is not a measurement of a nearby metal structure, a storm-time field, or every short-range anomaly. Check coefficients, altitude/coordinate convention, stated uncertainty and update version before calculating a local declination or suggesting navigation use.
- This narrows BANK-358-033's field-variation question and BANK-358-038's anomaly-separation question. Earth-system observations belong to 348; circuit standards belong to 359.
