## BANK-350-041 — Hershey and Chase, 1952

- The [original 1952 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2147348/), *Independent Functions of Viral Protein and Nucleic Acid in Growth of Bacteriophage*, studies T2 phage infection of *E. coli* using labeled protein and nucleic acid fractions. Read the experimental conditions, recovery fractions and authors' own inference before reducing it to the familiar textbook slogan.
- The study supports a bounded argument about which viral component entered and mattered in that system. It does not alone prove how DNA replicates, establish a mechanism in every organism, or show that proteins have no role in infection. Later studies and organism-specific evidence are needed for broader claims.
