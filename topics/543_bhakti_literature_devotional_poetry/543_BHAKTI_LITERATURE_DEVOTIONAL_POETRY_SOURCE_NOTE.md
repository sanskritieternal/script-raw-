IGNCA's interpretive overview https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/cultural-informatics/gita-govinda/ is institutional commentary, not itself an early manuscript witness.
- Textual, translation and community limits: Edition numbering, sandhi, spelling and variant readings differ. Before close reading, cite the precise song/verse and chosen edition and compare variants; avoid paraphrasing a modern translation as the Sanskrit original. The song's poetic sequence does not establish one universal Vaishnava avatar list or one performance practice across regions and sects. Musical, temple and dance uses require their own dated community/institutional sources and permissions; do not assume public-domain status of modern recordings or translations.
