## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of AI Research across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: AI Research Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in AI Research.
- **Tier 2: AI Research Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of AI Research.
- **Tier 3: AI Research Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in AI Research.
- **Tier 4: AI Research Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for AI Research.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of AI Research: A Research Method Emerges**
   - *Question:* Which dated experiment established a narrow AI technique?
   - *Key Focus:* Read original method, baseline, and replication. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
2. **AI Research: A Benchmark Result Rechecked**
   - *Question:* Does a celebrated score transfer to a useful task?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dataset, contamination risk, metric, and field test. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
3. **AI Research: Research Labor Behind a Model**
   - *Question:* Who labels data, maintains infrastructure, and reproduces results?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit contributors and examine conditions. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
4. **AI Research: From Question to AI Experiment**
   - *Question:* How do hypothesis, data, baseline, model, and evaluation fit?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace a published study without assuming its claim is true. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
5. **AI Research: Generalization Beyond a Test Set**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports performance outside training conditions?
   - *Key Focus:* Use independent split, shift, and uncertainty measures. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **AI Research: A Result That Would Not Replicate**
   - *Question:* Which data, code, compute, or metric differences explain divergence?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare public artifacts and unresolved gaps. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
7. **AI Research: A New Model Architecture**
   - *Question:* Which capability improved under equal evaluation?
   - *Key Focus:* Control data, compute, metric, and baseline. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
8. **AI Research: A Research Dispute**
   - *Question:* How did teams argue over one claim or method?
   - *Key Focus:* Read papers, critiques, responses, and replications. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
9. **AI Research: An AI Paper's Impact**
   - *Question:* Which follow-up studies confirmed, limited, or rejected it?
   - *Key Focus:* Build dated citation and replication trail. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
10. **AI Research: A Research Pipeline**
   - *Question:* How are data, training, validation, and reporting connected?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow a named experiment and versioned artifacts. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **AI Research: A Benchmark Becomes Obsolete**
   - *Question:* Which saturation, leakage, or task mismatch changed its value?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original purpose with later use. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
12. **AI Research: A Capability Across Model Generations**
   - *Question:* Did progress persist under matched tests?
   - *Key Focus:* Control prompt, data access, tools, budget, and scoring. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
13. **AI Research: A Promised Research Direction**
   - *Question:* What falsifiable test would support the proposal?
   - *Key Focus:* State hypotheses, resource assumptions, and failure criteria. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
14. **AI Research: One Score Means General Intelligence?**
   - *Question:* Which tasks and populations lie outside the benchmark?
   - *Key Focus:* Inspect evaluation scope and uncertainty. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
15. **AI Research: A Claim from Preprint to Replication**
   - *Question:* How did the evidence change through review and reuse?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated versions and independent tests. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **AI Research: Model or Whole System?**
   - *Question:* Which results depend on retrieval, tools, humans, or prompt design?
   - *Key Focus:* Draw inference and evaluation boundaries. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
17. **AI Research: One Evaluated Example**
   - *Question:* What input, context, output, and grading produced a score?
   - *Key Focus:* Record protocol while protecting restricted test data. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
18. **AI Research: Scale Versus Data Quality**
   - *Question:* Which investment improved the target outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare controlled experiments and costs. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
19. **AI Research: A Model's Systematic Error**
   - *Question:* What conditions reveal a repeatable failure?
   - *Key Focus:* Use benign, representative tests and report frequency. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
20. **AI Research: The Missing Evaluation Population**
   - *Question:* Who or what was absent from the test?
   - *Key Focus:* Inspect dataset construction and external validity. Evidence base: original papers and revisions, released code and datasets, independent replication, NIST evaluation guidance, and controlled task results.
