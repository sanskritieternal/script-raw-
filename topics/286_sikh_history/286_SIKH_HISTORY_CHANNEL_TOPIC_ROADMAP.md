## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Sikh History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Sikh History Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Sikh History.
- **Tier 2: Sikh History Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Sikh History.
- **Tier 3: Sikh History Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Sikh History.
- **Tier 4: Sikh History Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Sikh History.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

2. **Sikh History: A Sikh Institution in Context**

   - *Key Focus:* Use governance, service, patronage, and community records. Evidence base: dated Gurmukhi, Punjabi, Persian, or other relevant manuscripts and translations, hukamnamas, institutional records, material heritage, and consented community accounts.
3. **Sikh History: People Behind Community Service**
   - *Question:* Whose labor in teaching, langar, care, or preservation is underrecorded?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and dated institutional evidence. Evidence base: dated Gurmukhi, Punjabi, Persian, or other relevant manuscripts and translations, hukamnamas, institutional records, material heritage, and consented community accounts.
4. **Sikh History: A Sikh Historical Timeline in One Place**
   - *Question:* Which events shaped a named community over a defined period?

19. **Sikh History: An Observance in Historical Records**
   - *Question:* What can sources establish about a named community's public observance?

20. **Sikh History: A Punjabi or Gurmukhi Term in Context**
