# Selected source note — person-specific chatbot patent

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-482-044. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA, product launch or personal-continuity claim.

## Primary record

- Microsoft Technology Licensing LLC, U.S. Patent 10,853,717 B2, *Creating a conversational chat bot of a specific person* (published 2020-12-01): https://patents.google.com/patent/US10853717B2 . The patent discloses collecting social and conversational data to build a person-specific personality index; examples include voice, image and written records, and possible past or present entities. The patent text and claims are the evidence, not press descriptions of a 'resurrection' product.
- Patent document PDF: https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/8d/2a/7e/325266284d79df/US10853717.pdf . Use this fixed published record for precise claim language and figures.

## Identity, consent and access limits

A granted patent does not demonstrate that Microsoft built or sold such a chatbot, that its proposed system performs as described, or that a deceased person's consciousness or identity persists. Its source-data list raises consent, shared-message, likeness and third-party rights questions without resolving them. It offers no clinical evidence about grieving users. Do not reproduce private messages or suggest creation of an identifiable person's replica. The patent is a U.S. legal/technical record, not global permission to use personal data; verify current rights and patent status if those become material. Paraphrase and check patent-image rights.
