## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Card Magic across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Card Magic Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Card Magic.
- **Tier 2: Card Magic Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Card Magic.
- **Tier 3: Card Magic Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Card Magic.
- **Tier 4: Card Magic Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Card Magic.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Card Magic: A Card Effect in Print**
   - *Question:* Which dated manual or program documents a named card effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare editions and earlier variants. Evidence base: dated card-magic publications, recorded performances, credited variants, practitioner accounts, and controlled attention studies.
2. **Card Magic: A Card Revelation Revisited**
   - *Question:* What choices make a selection appear free and a reveal surprising?
   - *Key Focus:* Analyze a specified performance's visible sequence. Evidence base: dated card-magic publications, recorded performances, credited variants, practitioner accounts, and controlled attention studies.
3. **Card Magic: Uncredited Card Creators**
   - *Question:* Whose published handling or script shaped a familiar routine?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace manuals, credits, and practitioner testimony. Evidence base: dated card-magic publications, recorded performances, credited variants, practitioner accounts, and controlled attention studies.
4. **Card Magic: What the Audience Tracks**
   - *Question:* Which card identity, location, and choice does a spectator believe is fixed?
   - *Key Focus:* Map information at each beat without teaching an undisclosed method. Evidence base: dated card-magic publications, recorded performances, credited variants, practitioner accounts, and controlled attention studies.
5. **Card Magic: Memory and Card Magic**
   - *Question:* Which details of a routine are remembered differently after the reveal?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare a bounded audience study with performance context. Evidence base: dated card-magic publications, recorded performances, credited variants, practitioner accounts, and controlled attention studies.
