# Selected primary-source note — card magic

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-391-046`.

- [Restored 1902 text of S. W. Erdnase, *Artifice, Ruse and Subterfuge at the Card Table*](https://emend.press/erdnase.html): title page identifies both the gambler and conjurer as audiences and credits drawings to M. D. Smith. The inspected contents and preface divide “Card Table Artifice” from “Legerdemain” and describe the different settings. This is an independent online restoration of a primary text; confirm wording against a scanned original before script quotation.
- [Library of Congress, catalog and digitized 1905 Drake edition](https://www.loc.gov/item/34011788/): metadata identifies S. W. Erdnase, the 1905 Chicago imprint, 205 pages and illustrations, and locates this copy in the Harry Houdini Collection. The catalog result was visible but the item page returned 403 during this inspection, so the page images and edition differences were not verified here.

The evidence establishes stated title-page audiences and a catalogued later edition, not the writer’s civil identity, original invention of any handling, actual readership, or Houdini’s personal use of that copy. No procedural gambling or deception instruction belongs in the editorial scope.
