# Volcano case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary sources inspected for **proposed** cases. These notes test only the stated premises; they do not verify complete scripts or certify current safety conditions.

| Case | Primary record and finding | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Grindavík, 2023–24 | [Icelandic Meteorological Office, Reykjanes eruption chronology](https://en.vedur.is/volcanoes/fagradalsfjall-eruption) describes the 10 November 2023 intrusion beneath Grindavík, evacuation, and later eruptions. | Distinguish a dike intrusion, evacuation, and subsequent lava damage; do not say the entire town was consumed or reuse past notices as live advice. |
| Hunga Tonga, 2022 | [NASA GMAO science snapshot](https://gmao.gsfc.nasa.gov/science-snapshots/unprecedented-amount-of-water-vapor-injected-into-the-stratosphere-by-the-hunga-tonga-eruption/) reports extraordinary stratospheric water-vapor injection; [NASA publication record](https://esdpubs.nasa.gov/content/The_Spread_of_the_Hunga_Tonga_H2O_Plume_in_the_Middle_Atmosphere_Over_the_First_Two_Years) follows its spread. | Atmospheric measurements do not by themselves establish net planetary warming. Quantify only from the underlying study and distinguish water-vapor, aerosol, and dynamical effects. |
| Krafla | [USGS account](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/yvo/news/why-cant-we-drill-yellowstone-stop-eruptions-and-make-power) recounts an accidental 2009 magma encounter; the [Krafla Magma Testbed project](https://kmt.is/) describes planned research drilling. | A proposal is not an achieved observatory; project aspirations do not prove eventual access, energy, or eruption control. |
| Kawah Ijen | [Indonesia's official tourism page](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/destination/java/east-java/ijen-crater) describes blue fire and sulfur work. | Promotional account does not establish individual miners' pay, health outcomes, or current access conditions; seek labor and geological records before scripting these. |
| Pinatubo analogy | [USGS volcanic climate explainer](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP/volcanoes-can-affect-climate) documents natural sulfate-aerosol effects; [USGS Pinatubo analogy discussion](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo/news/volcano-watch-pinatubo-effect-can-geoengineering-mimic-volcanic-processes) discusses uncertainties. | Natural eruption observations do not validate an intentional climate intervention or its safety. No deployment recipe belongs in this topic. |
| Eruption access | [Iceland Environment Agency, 28 August 2024 closure notice](https://www.ust.is/english/the-agency/news/newsitem/2024/08/28/The-eruption-site-is-closed-Park-Rangers-provide-information-at-the-closure-post) records a dated site closure. | This is one decision at one time, not a standing travel guide or support for the archived tour price. |
