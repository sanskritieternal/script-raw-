## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Ayurveda across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Ayurveda Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Ayurveda.
- **Tier 2: Ayurveda Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Ayurveda.
- **Tier 3: Ayurveda Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Ayurveda.
- **Tier 4: Ayurveda Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Ayurveda.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Ayurveda: in Historical Sources**
   - *Question:* How did a named Ayurvedic concept change across dated texts and practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare translations, manuscript dates, and later interpretation without claiming a single origin. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
2. **Ayurveda: A Widely Promoted Ayurvedic Remedy**
   - *Question:* What does the evidence establish about one frequently cited remedy for a defined outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare the exact preparation, population, trial design, harms, and evidence quality. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
3. **Ayurveda: Patients and Practitioners in the Record**
   - *Question:* Whose experience is missing from a familiar history of Ayurvedic care?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and dated institutional records without treating anecdotes as efficacy evidence. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
4. **Ayurveda: Tradition, Product, and Clinical Claim**
   - *Question:* What different meanings does Ayurveda have in a specific care setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish historical framework, practitioner service, manufactured product, and testable outcome claim. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
5. **Ayurveda: Studying a Complex Intervention**
   - *Question:* How can researchers test a multi-component Ayurvedic intervention fairly?
   - *Key Focus:* Define components, comparator, outcomes, follow-up, and limits to generalization. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Ayurveda: A Reported Effect With Uncertain Cause**
   - *Question:* What evidence would separate treatment effect from recovery, placebo, or selection?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a specified claim and examine trial quality and missing data. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
7. **Ayurveda: Testing and Tracking Preparations**
   - *Question:* Can one testing or traceability method improve the safety of Ayurvedic products?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare contaminant detection, batch variation, enforcement, and real-world access. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
8. **Ayurveda: A Practitioner or Researcher's Influence**
   - *Question:* How did one person shape a documented Ayurvedic institution or study?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated publications, institutional records, and critical responses. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
9. **Ayurveda: A Change in Ayurvedic Regulation**
   - *Question:* What changed after one dated safety or practice rule?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare official rule text, implementation, inspections, and outcomes in one jurisdiction. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
10. **Ayurveda: From Product Label to Safety Check**
   - *Question:* What must be checked before a product claim can be evaluated?
   - *Key Focus:* Map ingredient identity, dose, manufacturing, contamination, interactions, and outcome evidence. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Ayurveda: A Treatment's Reputation Over Time**
   - *Question:* Why did one preparation gain or lose clinical credibility?
   - *Key Focus:* Track primary studies, reviews, safety notices, and changing standards. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
12. **Ayurveda: A Historical Claim and a Modern Test**
   - *Question:* How does a traditional use differ from a modern clinical efficacy claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify the historical context and the exact contemporary population and outcome. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
13. **Ayurveda: The Next Evidence Question**
   - *Question:* Which study would most reduce uncertainty about one Ayurvedic claim?
   - *Key Focus:* State comparator, sample, safety monitoring, and prerequisites without predicting success. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
14. **Ayurveda: Natural Does Not Establish Safety**
   - *Question:* Which safety claim about a named product survives product-specific testing?
   - *Key Focus:* Check contaminants, interactions, adverse events, and regulator notices. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
15. **Ayurveda: A Product Claim's Paper Trail**
   - *Question:* How did one Ayurvedic claim move from source text to modern marketing?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace translations, studies, labels, and regulator findings. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Ayurveda: One Claim, One Outcome**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports or weakens a specific Ayurvedic health claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate traditional rationale, laboratory results, and clinical outcomes. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
17. **Ayurveda: Integrative Care Handoffs**
   - *Question:* Where can communication fail when a patient uses Ayurveda alongside conventional care?
   - *Key Focus:* Map disclosure, medication review, referral, and follow-up without giving treatment instructions. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
18. **Ayurveda: Benefits and Harms in One Study**
   - *Question:* What did a trial measure about both benefit and adverse effects?
   - *Key Focus:* Read protocol, denominators, outcomes, and reporting gaps. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
19. **Ayurveda: Plausibility and Patient Outcomes**
   - *Question:* Does a proposed biological mechanism predict a measured clinical benefit?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare mechanistic work with controlled human outcomes and dose. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
20. **Ayurveda: Who Was Actually Studied?**
   - *Question:* Can a result in one study population apply to another?
   - *Key Focus:* Check age, condition, co-treatment, exclusions, and sample size. Evidence base: dated primary texts and translations, registered trials, systematic reviews, current clinical guidance, regulator notices, and consented accounts.
