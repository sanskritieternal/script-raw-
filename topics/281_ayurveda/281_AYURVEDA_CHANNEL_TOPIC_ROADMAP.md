## 1. Channel Core Architecture: Three Pillars

Rather than narrowing the channel to repetitive 'herb benefits' videos, the channel is built around three synergistic editorial pillars:
1. **Botanical Stories** — Plant anatomy, morphology, wild-harvesting vs. cultivation, authentication, and the physical life of medicinal flora.
2. **Practical Lifestyle Education** — Actionable Dinacharya (circadian routines), Ritucharya (seasonal adaptation), Ahara (food principles), home kitchen spices, and wholesome culinary demonstrations.
3. **Tradition Examined Through Research** — Classical Sanskrit treatises (Brihat Trayi) audited against modern double-blind randomized clinical trials, pharmacology, safety pharmacovigilance, and consumer product literacy.

---

## 2. Four-Tier Curriculum Progression

- **Tier 1: Foundations (Scratch / Beginner)** — Core philosophies, Panchamahabhutas (5 elements), Tridosha (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), Agni (digestive fire), Ama (metabolic waste), Dhatus (tissues), and constitutional self-understanding.
- **Tier 2: Daily Lifestyle & Preventive Living (Intermediate)** — Translating principles into actionable circadian habits, seasonal living, Ahara nutritional science, and mental hygiene.
- **Tier 3: Formulations, Herbology & Clinical Therapies (Upper Intermediate)** — Classical monographs, synergistic formulations (Triphala, Trikatu, Chyawanprash), Panchakarma clinical protocols, and pharmacology.
- **Tier 4: Advanced Pathology, Safety & Integrative Horizons (Advanced / Expert)** — Classical pathogenesis (*Samprapti*), 8-fold diagnosis (*Ashtavidha Pariksha*), Rasa Shastra heavy metal toxicology, Ayurgenomics (DNA & Prakriti), and clinical research literacy.
