# Artificial-consciousness source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-481-043 - 2022 LaMDA interview claim

- First-person published artifact: Blake Lemoine, *Is LaMDA Sentient? - an Interview*, June 15, 2022, https://www.e-flux.com/notes/475146/is-lamda-sentient-an-interview (inspected 2026-10-24; this page republishes his original Medium text). Lemoine states at the outset that several chat sessions were edited into one transcript, with some human prompts edited for readability. It is evidence that a participant publicly made and illustrated a sentience claim, not an unedited experimental log.
- Primary technical paper: Romal Thoppilan et al., *LaMDA: Language Models for Dialog Applications*, arXiv:2201.08239v3, February 10, 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08239 (inspected 2026-10-24). It describes a Transformer dialogue-model family and evaluates response quality, safety and factual grounding; its abstract does not report a test for subjective experience.
- Access and claim limits: The interview is a first-person republished document, not independent replication; the original complete logs, model version and prompting conditions were not audited here. The technical paper describes a model family, not necessarily the exact interview instance. Neither source establishes whether LaMDA had or lacked subjective experience. Do not infer a system's moral status from fluent first-person output alone.
