# Case-source note: Sanskrit Heritage Reader

Checked 2026-10-24. The project’s [Hypertext Sanskrit Tools I lesson](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/SKT/COURSE/Lessons/HST_1.html), credited to Gérard Huet (2024), uses a continuous Gaṇeśa invocation, displays a segmentation into *namaḥ, gaṇeśāya,* and *vighneśvarāya*, and shows dative and dictionary links. Its [reference manual](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/SKT/manual.html) describes Reader settings and multiple candidate analyses; the [project home](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/SKT/) identifies the tool and an elementary course.

Limit: These are the developer’s tutorial and documentation, not independent educational evaluation. The lesson’s rapid-fluency rhetoric is not adopted. Machine candidate parses need textual and grammatical checking. The Gaṇeśa invocation is examined as a reading example, not as a claim about devotional truth or a universal Sanskrit register. Tool version and interface may change.
