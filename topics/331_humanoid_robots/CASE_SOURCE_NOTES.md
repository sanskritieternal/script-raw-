It describes planned testing with Hyundai and possible gripper designs. Its comparisons of strength and range of motion are company projections in this announcement.
- [Boston Dynamics, *Atlas' Evolution From Research Robot to Industrial Humanoid*](https://bostondynamics.com/blog/atlas-evolution-from-research-robot-to-industrial-humanoid/) (later retrospective): dates the introduction of the electric Atlas to April 2024. This is retrospective manufacturer context, not independent evaluation.

The accessible manufacturer page establishes the announcement and stated intent, not independently measured performance, proof of daily factory use, worker outcomes or commercial reliability. Product state can change after 2024; any later deployment account needs its own dated source. Do not extrapolate from staged motion clips to unsupervised autonomy.
