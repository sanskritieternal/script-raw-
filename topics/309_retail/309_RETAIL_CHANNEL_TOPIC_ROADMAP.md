## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Retail across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Retail Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Retail.
- **Tier 2: Retail Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Retail.
- **Tier 3: Retail Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Retail.
- **Tier 4: Retail Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Retail.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

1. **The Architecture of Retail: How One Retail Format Emerged**
   - *Question:* Which location, supply, and customer needs shaped it?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated store, planning, and trade records. Evidence base: dated store and firm records, retail statistical definitions, supplier terms, local planning data, and consented worker and shopper accounts.
2. **Retail: A Successful Store Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What explains performance at a named location?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare footfall, assortment, margin, rent, and service. Evidence base: dated store and firm records, retail statistical definitions, supplier terms, local planning data, and consented worker and shopper accounts.
3. **Retail: Work Beyond the Sales Floor**
   - *Question:* Who receives, stocks, cleans, secures, and processes returns?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented worker accounts and schedules. Evidence base: dated store and firm records, retail statistical definitions, supplier terms, local planning data, and consented worker and shopper accounts.

   - *Question:* Why is a product stocked or removed?
