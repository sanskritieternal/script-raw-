# SaaS businesses source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-534-043 - Snowflake FY2026 reporting definitions

- Primary public filing: Snowflake Inc., annual report/Form 10-K for fiscal year ended January 31, 2026, filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1640147/000164014726000008/snow-20260131.htm (inspected 2026-10-24). Snowflake defines remaining performance obligations (RPO) as contracted future revenue not yet recognized, including deferred revenue and non-cancelable contracted amounts. It excludes on-demand arrangements with no minimum purchase commitment and some arrears-billed time-and-materials contracts.
- In the same filing, Snowflake reports approximately $9.8 billion RPO at January 31, 2026 and expects about 46% to be recognized in the following 12 months based on historical consumption patterns. Its net revenue retention rate is a defined capacity-contract customer cohort's second-year product revenue divided by first-year product revenue; zero-use customers stay in the denominator. These are issuer definitions and dated estimates, not audited forecasts of every customer's future use.
- Limits: RPO is neither cash received nor a guarantee of realized revenue or profitability. On-demand consumption may generate revenue outside this metric, and a customer may use less or more than anticipated. Read the filing's risk factors, accounting policy, contract terms and later filings before scripting a trend. This is one U.S.-listed company, not a universal SaaS metric or investment advice.
