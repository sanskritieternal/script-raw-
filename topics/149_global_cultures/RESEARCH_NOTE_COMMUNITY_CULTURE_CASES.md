# Selected primary-source research: community culture cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These checks bound three named proposals only. Other archived claims about cultures, profits, extinction or psychological effects remain unverified.

## Wampanoag language reclamation — BANK-149-043

- Primary community sources: Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe, [About the Wampanoag Language Reclamation Project](https://mashpeewampanoagtribe-nsn.gov/about-wlrp) and [Language Department](https://mashpeewampanoagtribe-nsn.gov/language); Wôpanâak Language Reclamation Project, [project history](https://www.wlrp.org/project-history) and [language requests notice](https://www.wlrp.org/language-requests).
- The tribe describes a community-run intertribal project founded in 1993, drawing on Wampanoag-authored historical texts and teaching within participating communities. The project currently states a moratorium on new outside requests for translations, words, phrases, names and songs.
- Limit: The project defines its own aims, governance and access. Do not call the language extinct, claim AI made the revival, extract vocabulary for a production, or solicit restricted language material. Public description is not consent to record participants, reproduce teaching materials or use community language commercially. Seek direction from the relevant tribal authority before any production.

## Silbo Gomero — BANK-149-044

- Primary heritage record: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, [2009 Silbo Gomero inscription](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/whistled-language-of-the-island-of-la-gomera-canary-islands-the-silbo-gomero-00172) and [committee decision with community-participation findings](https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/06859-EN.pdf).
- The inscription describes whistled communication of the islanders’ habitual Castilian Spanish, with teaching in schools since 1999; the decision records community participation and consent for the nomination.
- Limit: These are nomination and 2009 decision materials, not a current speaker census or proof that every islander can whistle. Whistled Spanish is not a separate universal language, and smartphone use alone does not establish decline. Nomination consent does not authorize recording any individual speaker for a new program.

## Angkor tourism management — BANK-149-045

- Primary planning record: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Angkor Tourism Management Plan](https://whc.unesco.org/document/134601), and [site record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/668/). The plan states aims for local communities to share benefits from tourism and to participate in management.
- Limit: A plan is a commitment, not measured delivery. These sources alone cannot quantify household gains, who profited or a multi-billion-dollar tourism economy. Any outcome episode needs dated visitor/revenue data and community accounts, including residents who did not benefit. Avoid treating the living heritage site as a spectacle detached from the people who maintain it.
