## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Temple Traditions: A Temple's Earliest Record**
   - *Question:* What does the earliest dated evidence establish about one temple?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inscriptions, architecture, texts, and later origin narratives. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
2. **Temple Traditions: A Celebrated Temple Tradition**
   - *Question:* How did one well-known practice develop at a named temple?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated records and practitioner accounts; avoid treating one site as universal. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
3. **Temple Traditions: Caretakers Behind Temple Life**
   - *Question:* Whose work sustains daily temple practice but is absent from standard histories?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts of custodians, artists, and service workers. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
4. **Temple Traditions: A Temple as a Living Institution**
   - *Question:* What functions does one temple serve for its community today?
   - *Key Focus:* Map worship, stewardship, learning, and local variation. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
5. **Temple Traditions: Ritual Authority and Change**
   - *Question:* Who has authority to interpret or change one temple practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare lineage, institutional rules, law, and community perspectives. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Temple Traditions: A Temple Date Under Debate**
   - *Question:* Which evidence supports competing dates for one temple feature?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate inscription, style, reuse, and later tradition. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
7. **Temple Traditions: Documenting a Temple Collection**
   - *Question:* Can digital recording help preserve one collection with custodial consent?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare access, context, sacred restrictions, and preservation value. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
8. **Temple Traditions: A Temple Patron or Custodian**
   - *Question:* What did one documented figure actually contribute to a temple?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish inscriptional evidence from later biography. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
9. **Temple Traditions: A Temple's Turning Point**
   - *Question:* How did one dated event change worship, patronage, or access?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare records before and after and include community memory. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
10. **Temple Traditions: A Day of Temple Practice**
   - *Question:* How do practitioners describe a named sequence at one temple?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute the local protocol and avoid prescribing observance. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Temple Traditions: Patronage and Temple Capacity**
   - *Question:* How did changes in resources affect one temple's functions?
   - *Key Focus:* Use grants, accounts, maintenance records, and local testimony. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
12. **Temple Traditions: Continuity and Change in Practice**
   - *Question:* Which elements of a named practice changed across generations?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated descriptions and present practitioner voices. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
13. **Temple Traditions: A Conservation Choice**
   - *Question:* How might one proposal affect worship and material preservation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare conservation assessments and custodial priorities. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
14. **Temple Traditions: Narrative and Material Evidence**
   - *Question:* How should a temple origin narrative be presented beside dated material evidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute devotional accounts and state what records can establish. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
15. **Temple Traditions: A Temple Through Its Records**
   - *Question:* What do inscriptions, archives, and oral histories reveal about one site?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain provenance, translation, gaps, and living memory. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Temple Traditions: A Text Cited at a Temple**
   - *Question:* How is one text used and interpreted in a named local practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify edition, translation, and practitioner interpretation. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
17. **Temple Traditions: Different Readings of One Practice**
   - *Question:* How do two lineages explain the same temple practice differently?
   - *Key Focus:* Represent each view in its own terms with attributed sources. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
18. **Temple Traditions: Worship as Practiced Here**
   - *Question:* What do participants say matters in one recurring observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented voices and specify place, community, and date. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
19. **Temple Traditions: The Order of an Observance**
   - *Question:* Which steps are public and what meanings do participants assign them?
   - *Key Focus:* Respect restrictions and avoid presenting local sequence as universal. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
20. **Temple Traditions: A Temple Term in Translation**
   - *Question:* What meaning is lost when one ritual term is translated loosely?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original usage, scholarly translation, and living interpretation. Evidence base: original and translated texts, dated inscriptions, conservation records, site rules, archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
