## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Disaster & Survival across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Survival Physiology, The Rule of Threes & Catastrophe Psychology (Scratch / Beginner)** — Rule of threes (air, shelter, water, food), hypothermia dynamics, freeze vs action amygdala responses, and wilderness triage priority.
- **Tier 2: Epic Historic Survival Sagas & Natural Cataclysms (Intermediate)** — The 1972 Andes flight disaster, Shackleton's Endurance ice drift, the 1883 Krakatoa volcanic tsunami, and the 1906 San Francisco earthquake fires.
- **Tier 3: Industrial Failures, Structural Collapses & Extreme Technical Crises (Upper Intermediate)** — The Chernobyl reactor meltdown (1986), Bhopal toxic gas leak (1984), Deepwater Horizon blowout, and the Tham Luang Thai cave rescue (2018).
- **Tier 4: Global Extinction Hazards, Nuclear Winter & Next-Gen Disaster Engineering (Advanced / Expert)** — Supervolcanic winter models, solar coronal mass ejections (Carrington event), deep subterranean fallout bunkers, and disaster robotics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Survival Physiology & Emergency Psychology
1. **The Miracle in the Andes: 72 Days Trapped on a Glacier and the Ultimate Human Will**
   - *Question:* How did 16 Uruguayan rugby players survive 72 days in the freezing Andes at 11,500 feet after their search was called off?
   - *Key Focus:* The fuselage shelter engineering, melting snow with metal sheets, agonizing moral decision of anthropophagy, Nando Parrado and Roberto Canessa's 10-day trek.
2. **The Rule of Threes: The Biomechanics of How the Human Body Dies in the Wild**
   - *Question:* Why does a human body expire in 3 minutes without air, 3 hours without shelter in freezing wetness, 3 days without water, and 3 weeks without food?
   - *Key Focus:* Hypothermia paradox, renal failure in dehydration, metabolic ketoacidosis and starvation stages, shelter insulation physics.
3. **Shackleton's Endurance: 18 Months Stranded on Antarctic Sea Ice Without Losing a Man**
   - *Question:* How did Sir Ernest Shackleton lead 27 men across crushing pack ice, open stormy seas in a 22-foot lifeboat, and uncharted mountain peaks to safety?
   - *Key Focus:* Crushing of the Endurance, camp discipline on drifting ice floes, the 800-mile James Caird open-boat navigation, crossing South Georgia glaciers.
4. **The Psychology of Panic: Why 80% of People Freeze During Catastrophes**
   - *Question:* Why do the majority of airplane crash passengers sit paralyzed in their seats while survivors immediately unbuckle and run toward exits?
   - *Key Focus:* Cognitive paralysis and behavioral freeze, John Leach 10-80-10 rule of disaster behavior, stress inoculation training, situational awareness.
5. **127 Hours: Aron Ralston's Agonizing Choice in Bluejohn Canyon**
   - *Question:* What pushed a solo canyoneer pinned by an 800-pound boulder to snap his own arm bones with torque and sever his limb with a dull pocketknife?
   - *Key Focus:* Crush syndrome toxin buildup, self-amputation torque physics, hallucination and hypothermia, rappelling single-handed to rescue.
### Tier 2: Epic Historic Sagas & Natural Cataclysms
6. **Krakatoa 1883: The Volcanic Detonation That Heard Around the World Four Times**
   - *Question:* How did a volcanic caldera explosion generate a 310 dB sound pressure wave that ruptured eardrums 40 miles away and circled the globe 4 times?
   - *Key Focus:* Pyroclastic flow travel over open seawater, 120-foot tsunamis obliterating coastal Sunda Strait towns, 5-year global temperature drop.
7. **The 1906 San Francisco Earthquake: When Firestorm Smashed a Gilded Age Metropolis**
   - *Question:* Why did ruptured water mains force the US Army to dynamite whole city blocks, accidentally sparking an inferno that destroyed 80% of San Francisco?
   - *Key Focus:* San Andreas fault 20-foot strike-slip displacement, firestorm thermal convection, Ham and Eggs fire origin, refugee tent cities in the Presidio.
8. **Juliane Koepcke: Falling 10,000 Feet from the Sky into the Peruvian Amazon**
   - *Question:* How did a 17-year-old girl strapped to a row of airplane seats survive a two-mile plunge through the clouds and walk out of the jungle 11 days later?
   - *Key Focus:* LANSA Flight 508 thunderstorm breakup, canopy foliage cushioning physics, following downstream water flow rule, treating maggot-infested wounds with gasoline.
9. **The 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami: The 9.1 Megathrust Quake and the Unwarned Coastlines**
   - *Question:* Why did a seafloor rupture off Sumatra unleash a series of ocean surges that claimed 230,000 lives across 14 nations with zero early warning systems?
   - *Key Focus:* Megathrust fault subduction dynamics, deep-ocean wave velocity (500 mph) vs coastal shoaling, Tilly Smith saving a beach, creation of the DART buoy network.
10. **Mount St. Helens 1980: The Lateral Blast That Flattened 230 Square Miles of Forest**
   - *Question:* How did a 5.1 earthquake trigger the largest subaerial landslide in history, venting volcanic pressure sideways at 670 mph?
   - *Key Focus:* Debris avalanche mechanics, volcanologist David Johnston's final radio call ('Vancouver! This is it!'), blowdown zone timber shockwaves.
### Tier 3: Industrial Failures & Extreme Technical Crises
11. **Chernobyl 1986: The 60-Second Turbine Test That Blew Open Reactor No. 4**
   - *Question:* What fatal sequence of human error, xenon gas poisoning, and a graphite-tipped control rod flaw transformed Pripyat into a radioactive ghost town?
   - *Key Focus:* RBMK positive void coefficient design flaw, AZ-5 scram button disaster trigger, three divers in the bubbler pool, 600,000 liquidators sarcophagus construction.
12. **Bhopal 1984: The Deadly Midnight Cloud That Murdered a Sleeping City**
   - *Question:* How did cost-cutting, disabled refrigeration units, and water leaking into Tank 610 release 40 tons of lethal methyl isocyanate gas across Bhopal?
   - *Key Focus:* Exothermic runaway reaction chemistry, pulmonary edema choking victims in their sleep, corporate negligence, ongoing environmental contamination.
13. **The Deepwater Horizon Blowout: 87 Days of Oil Spewing into the Gulf of Mexico**
   - *Question:* Why did faulty cement, misread negative pressure tests, and an uninspected blind shear ram cause the Macondo well to explode in 5,000 feet of water?
   - *Key Focus:* Methane gas kick mechanics, blowout preventer (BOP) hydraulic shear failure, 4.9 million barrels spilled, ROV capping stack success on Day 87.
14. **The Thai Cave Rescue (2018): Anaesthetizing 12 Boys to Swim Them Out of a Flooded Cavern**
   - *Question:* How did an international team of cave divers execute a medical plan considered impossible: ketamine sedation of 12 children through zero-visibility sumps?
   - *Key Focus:* Sump diving navigation in muddy currents, Dr. Richard Harris ketamine dosing protocol, full-face positive pressure masks, Saman Kunan's sacrifice.
15. **Apollo 13: 'Houston, We've Had a Problem' and the Orbital Lifeboat Triumph**
   - *Question:* How did three astronauts and mission control jury-rig carbon dioxide lithium hydroxide scrubbers with duct tape and cardboard 200,000 miles from Earth?
   - *Key Focus:* Teflon wire short circuit in Service Module Oxygen Tank 2, Aquarius lunar module lifeboat power-down, manual alignment burns using the Earth's terminator.
### Tier 4: Global Extinction Hazards & Next-Gen Preparedness
16. **The Carrington Event (1859): The Solar Superstorm That Electrified Telegraph Lines**
   - *Question:* What happens when a direct-hit solar flare creates auroras bright enough to read newspapers at midnight and shocks telegraph operators through disconnected lines?
   - *Key Focus:* Coronal mass ejection (CME) physics, magnetosphere compression, induction currents in copper wire grids, 2012 near-miss STEREO spacecraft data.
17. **Yellowstone and Toba: The Physics and Aftermath of a Supervolcanic Winter**
   - *Question:* Did the eruption of Mount Toba 74,000 years ago reduce the entire human species to fewer than 10,000 breeding individuals in a decade-long winter?
   - *Key Focus:* Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI 8), stratospheric sulfate aerosol solar shielding, continental ash accumulation models, genetic bottleneck controversy.
18. **Nuclear Winter: The Climatic Catastrophe Behind Global Thermonuclear War**
   - *Question:* Why would the true killer in a nuclear conflict not be the initial blast or prompt radiation, but 150 million tons of black carbon soot blocking the sun for a decade?
   - *Key Focus:* Carl Sagan and TTAPS study, modern Robock-Toon climate simulation models, ozone layer destruction, agricultural collapse and global famine.
19. **Bunker Engineering: How Billionaires and Governments Prepare for the Apocalypse**
   - *Question:* How do deep underground facilities like Cheyenne Mountain, Raven Rock, and private billionaire survival complexes maintain independent air, power, and security?
   - *Key Focus:* EMP Faraday shielding, spring-mounted blast dampers, CBRN air filtration scrubbers, geothermal water recycling, Svalbard Global Seed Vault design.
20. **Disaster Robotics and AI First Responders: The Future of Search and Rescue**
   - *Question:* Can autonomous quadruped robots, drone swarms, and snake-bots navigate rubble to find heartbeat signals faster than human sniffer dogs?
   - *Key Focus:* DARPA Subterranean Challenge, UWB through-wall radar respiration detection, autonomous swarm mapping in GPS-denied environments, earthquake response.
