# Selected primary-source checks - Trees
Checked 2026-10-24. Bounded research leads only; original archived claims remain unverified.

- BANK-202-037: Rogers and McAvoy 2018 original Pando plot study, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0203619 . Sixty-five plots compare management regimes and implicate deer browsing. One clone and management contrasts cannot establish every driver or inevitable collapse; no predator-reintroduction result is shown.
- BANK-202-038: USDA collection record, https://www.ars.usda.gov/oc/images/photos/oct16/D3664-1 , and National Bonsai Foundation collection record, https://www.bonsai-nbf.org/yamaki-pine . Yamaki pine was in training since 1625, survived Hiroshima and was gifted in 1976. Training date is not biological age; no millions-dollar value established.
- BANK-202-039: National Park Service warning, https://home.nps.gov/viis/learn/nature/plants-and-trees-to-avoid.htm , and rain-sap discussion, https://parkplanning.nps.gov/showFile.cfm?projectID=19240&sfid=88722 . Rain exposure may blister skin; these sources do not show that all people are blinded.
- BANK-202-040: Africa's Great Green Wall, documented by the UNCCD initiative and 2020 report, https://www.unccd.int/our-work/ggwi and https://www.unccd.int/resources/publications/great-green-wall-implementation-status-and-way-ahead-2030 . This question owns the method for counting and verifying reported restored land, not the program's ecological success. Program effectiveness and the old acacia-wall promise belong to BANK-115-022; China's Three-North program belongs to BANK-205-042. Dated reporting does not establish current survival.
- BANK-202-041: Santos et al. field study, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925857426002569 . Emergence, survival and establishment are separate outcomes. Accessible abstract does not support generalization to all climates or species.
- Overlap: BANK-115-046 owns Hyperion closure. BANK-064-060 owns a home CLT decision. Bonsai technique in 065 is distinct from Yamaki provenance.
