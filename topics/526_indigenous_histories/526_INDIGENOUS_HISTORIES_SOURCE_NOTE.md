# Indigenous histories source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-526-043 - Nisga'a Final Agreement public chronology

- Primary source-community record: Nisga'a Lisims Government, *Timeline*, https://www.nisgaanation.ca/government/history/timeline/ (inspected 2026-10-24). The Nation's public chronology includes the 1890 Land Committee, the 1998 Nisga'a ratification vote and constitutional adoption, Canadian and B.C. legislative steps, and the May 11, 2000 treaty effective date. Retain the Nation's own terminology and full chronology; do not narrate history as beginning with colonial recognition.
- Primary treaty and Nation explanation: Nisga'a Lisims Government, *Understanding the Treaty*, https://www.nisgaanation.ca/government/nisgaa-treaty/understanding-the-treaty/ ; *Treaty Implementation*, https://www.nisgaanation.ca/government/nisgaa-treaty/treaty-implementation/ ; and treaty document index https://www.nisgaanation.ca/government/nisgaa-treaty/treaty-documents/ (inspected 2026-10-24). These distinguish the Nation, Canada and British Columbia as treaty partners and describe Nisga'a Lands and self-government as specific agreement terms.
- Access, attribution and claim limits: These are public Nation-authored pages and treaty documents, not permission to republish restricted cultural knowledge, private testimony, images or oral histories. Treaty legal meaning and present implementation require the current treaty text, Nation sources and possibly specialist legal review; a short editorial question cannot substitute for those. Avoid generalizing this Nation's agreement to all Indigenous peoples, describing sovereignty as granted by Canada, or treating a negotiated settlement as a final resolution of every historical harm. Verify orthography and preferred attribution with Nation materials before scripting.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-526-043 is a named community-led land and governance history. BANK-526-033 covers colonial land-record limits generally; BANK-526-041 covers education; BANK-527-034 concerns individual oral-history consent, not treaty authority.
