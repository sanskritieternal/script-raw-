## BANK-259-039 — 5Pointz work-specific destruction dispute

- The [Second Circuit's 2020 opinion in *Castillo v. G&M Realty*](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/18-498/18-498-2020-02-20.html) is a reproduced primary judicial text addressing destruction of specific aerosol works under the US Visual Artists Rights Act. An [official district-court reconsideration order](https://img.nyed.uscourts.gov/files/opinions/5Pointz%20Reconsideration%20Order%20-%20Final.pdf) provides part of the underlying court record.
- Read the full opinions and work-by-work findings before giving counts, damages or legal tests. The holding is jurisdictional and fact-specific; it neither guarantees preservation of every temporary work nor grants a documentary filmmaker rights to reproduce the images. This named dispute is distinct from BANK-259-033's general preservation ethics and BANK-259-038's anonymous-artist credit question.
