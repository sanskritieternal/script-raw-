# Case-source note: FTC Lumosity case

Checked 2026-10-24. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission’s [Lumos Labs case docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/lumos-labs-inc-lumosity-mobile-online-cognitive-game-timeline-item-2016-01-05) links the 2016 complaint and stipulated judgment. The [FTC case announcement](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) describes allegations about everyday, school/work, age-related and health-condition benefits and the settlement’s future substantiation requirement. The [complaint](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/cases/160105lumoslabscmpt.pdf) is an allegation; the [stipulated order](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/cases/160105lumoslabsstip.pdf) records agreed relief.

Limit: Settlement is not a randomized efficacy trial or a judicial finding that no brain-training method can ever transfer. Do not turn disputed ad claims into established benefits or make treatment advice for dementia, ADHD, stroke or other conditions. Source concerns U.S. marketing and the 2016 order; current products and evidence require separate review.
