# BANK-028-058 — Titan investigation evidence

Research check: 2026-10-24. Editorial proposal only; not a safety certification or approved script.

## Checked premise

The US Coast Guard Marine Board of Investigation's final report, released 5 August 2025, identifies loss of structural integrity of Titan's carbon-fiber hull as the initiating event in the 18 June 2023 implosion. Its causal analysis discusses design and testing, expected hull cycle life, real-time monitoring data that were not meaningfully analyzed, prior incidents and inspections, as well as organizational and oversight factors. The archived pitch's “carbon fiber delamination” wording is too narrow as a full causal explanation. Acoustic logs and monitoring data are evidence to interpret, not a self-contained timestamped proof of one simple failure mechanism.
