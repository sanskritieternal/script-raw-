# BANK-028-056 — filmed hadal snailfish record

Research check: 2026-10-24. Editorial proposal only; this is not an approved script or a claim that every subsequent record has been exhaustively checked.

## Checked premise

The archived trending pitch says the deepest fish was filmed at 8,336 m in the Mariana Trench. The expedition's peer-reviewed report instead locates the 8,336 m video record in the **Izu-Ogasawara Trench**. The animal in that video was a solitary juvenile snailfish; no specimen was collected at that depth, so species identification remains uncertain. Two *Pseudoliparis belyaevi* specimens were collected at 8,022 m in the neighboring Japan Trench, a separate captured-fish record. The university's 2025 update still describes the 2023 filmed and captured records, but publication review is needed again before filming.
