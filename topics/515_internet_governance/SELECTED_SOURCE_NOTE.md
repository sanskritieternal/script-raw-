## Bounded premise

The U.S. NTIA contract for the IANA functions expired on 1 October 2016. ICANN's implementation records document a new arrangement for the IANA functions, including PTI and accountability/service-level mechanisms. The case can distinguish oversight of unique internet identifiers from the technical daily operation and from unrelated governance issues such as content rules, network neutrality or shutdowns.

## Claim and access limits

ICANN and NTIA are parties to the transition; their records establish institutional decisions but do not independently establish equal participation or effectiveness of accountability. The functions include domain names, numbering resources and protocol parameters, with different operational communities. Do not imply ICANN owns or controls the entire internet, or that contract expiration transferred every technical or regulatory power. Governance arrangements and bylaws may have changed since 2016; date each rule or status claim. A separate participation/effectiveness study would be needed for outcome claims.
