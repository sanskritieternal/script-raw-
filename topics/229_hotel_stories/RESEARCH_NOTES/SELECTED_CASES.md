# Selected hotel-case source checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. Operator and agency documents establish different kinds of claims.

## BANK-229-039 — digital key

The [Hilton Digital Key help page](https://www.hilton.com/en/help-center/check-in-and-check-out/how-to-use-your-digital-key/) describes an eligible app workflow and conditions: availability depends on the stay, a replacement device needs re-issuance, and some guests may still need to stop at the desk. It does not measure average check-in time, adoption, failure rate or access outcomes. BANK-229-030 covers room turnover; this case covers one named check-in feature.

## BANK-229-040 — Indian star classification

The [Ministry of Tourism's guidelines index](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/home/page/guidelines) identifies the current guideline collection, while its [2025 written answer](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-04/PIB2118260.pdf) describes voluntary hotel classification and the reviewing committee. The [2018 guideline text](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/1667476361.pdf) is a dated version; check the operative version before citing a requirement. Classification does not establish a particular guest's room condition or predict a stay.

## BANK-229-041 — contracted hotel workers

The [US Department of Labor's 2023 Greenparc enforcement release](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/whd/whd20230222-0) reports an investigation and recovered back wages and damages involving specified workers at New York hotels. The finding is confined to that employer, roles, law and period; it does not establish prevalence across hotels or a guest-service outcome. Existing BANK-229-030 covers operational turnover; this case owns a named labor record.

## BANK-229-042 — Waldorf Astoria restoration

The [New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission's 2017 designation record](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/2591.pdf) states the hotel closed March 1, 2017 for renovation and identifies protected interiors. [Hilton's July 2025 opening-season statement](https://stories.hilton.com/releases/waldorf-astoria-new-york-begins-opening-season) describes guest-room and residence changes and its initial reopening phase. These records separate legal preservation and operator claims from independently measured visitor, labor or financial outcomes. Existing BANK-229-031 covers grand hotels generally; this case owns one closure and change-of-use sequence.
