## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Hotel Stories across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Hotel Stories Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Hotel Stories.
- **Tier 2: Hotel Stories Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Hotel Stories.
- **Tier 3: Hotel Stories Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Hotel Stories.
- **Tier 4: Hotel Stories Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Hotel Stories.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Hotel Stories: How One Hotel Came to Be**
   - *Question:* Which land, finance, and design decisions created this property?
   - *Key Focus:* Use permits, ownership records, plans, and local history. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
2. **Hotel Stories: A Landmark Hotel Revisited**
   - *Question:* What did the celebrated property offer at a specified time?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare archival claims with documented services, labor, and access. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
3. **Hotel Stories: The Unseen Shift**
   - *Question:* Whose work is absent from the hotel's public story?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace housekeeping, maintenance, laundry, security, and suppliers with consent. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
4. **Hotel Stories: What Happens During a Stay**
   - *Question:* How do booking, arrival, service, and checkout connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one guest journey and its staff handoffs. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
5. **Hotel Stories: The Capacity Puzzle**
   - *Question:* How does a hotel balance occupancy, staffing, and service quality?
   - *Key Focus:* Use defined room nights, staffing hours, demand, and complaints. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Hotel Stories: A Hotel Closure Examined**
   - *Question:* Which documented factors explain a property's closure?
   - *Key Focus:* Test debt, demand, repairs, regulation, and owner records. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
7. **Hotel Stories: A Digital Check-In Tested**
   - *Question:* Did a named system improve guest and worker experience?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare wait time, failures, privacy, accessibility, and staff workload. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
8. **Hotel Stories: A Hotelier's Choice**
   - *Question:* How did one manager or worker change a service practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use interviews, records, and outcomes beyond promotional biography. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
9. **Hotel Stories: A Hotel During a Crisis**
   - *Question:* How did one event change staffing and guest care?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated notices, worker accounts, occupancy, and safety records. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
10. **Hotel Stories: The Hotel Behind a Room Key**
   - *Question:* Which teams and systems deliver one occupied room?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace reservation, cleaning, maintenance, front desk, and payment. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Hotel Stories: A Property's Changing Fortunes**
   - *Question:* What drove a measurable occupancy or reputation change?
   - *Key Focus:* Check seasonality, room supply, prices, ownership, and review bias. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
12. **Hotel Stories: One Hotel Across Two Eras**
   - *Question:* How did its rooms, labor, guests, and local role change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable records and distinguish renovation from rebranding. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
13. **Hotel Stories: What Could Change This Property**
   - *Question:* Which scenarios alter its viability?
   - *Key Focus:* State assumptions for demand, labor, energy, water, and capital costs. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
14. **Hotel Stories: Does a Star Rating Predict a Stay?**
   - *Question:* Which services does a rating measure or omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Name rating scheme, inspection date, accessibility, and guest needs. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
15. **Hotel Stories: A Day Inside One Hotel**
   - *Question:* What sequence of work makes a guest stay possible?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented observation and protect guests' and workers' privacy. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Hotel Stories: The Hotel's Actual Location**
   - *Question:* What does a booking map omit about reaching it?
   - *Key Focus:* Check entrances, transit, walking route, barriers, and surroundings. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
17. **Hotel Stories: Getting to a Hotel**
   - *Question:* Which arrival path works for a defined guest?

18. **Hotel Stories: A Hotel's Busy and Quiet Seasons**
   - *Question:* What changes in price, staffing, and services?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated rate offers, occupancy series, and opening schedules. Evidence base: property records and dated offers, planning and ownership documents, relevant labor records, accessible room details, and consented worker, guest, and resident accounts.
19. **Hotel Stories: Neighbors of a Hotel**
