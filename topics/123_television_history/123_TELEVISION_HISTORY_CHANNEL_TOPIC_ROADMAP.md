## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Television History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Mechanical Discs to Cathode Rays: The Invention of the Screen (Beginner / Scratch)** — Nipkow scanning discs, Philo Farnsworth vs Vladimir Zworykin (RCA patent war), early BBC broadcasts, 525-line NTSC vs PAL standards, and the 1939 World's Fair debut.
- **Tier 2: The Network Golden Age & The Living Room Monoculture (Intermediate)** — The Big Three networks (NBC, CBS, ABC), the 1960 Kennedy-Nixon debate, the Quiz Show scandals, I Love Lucy's 3-camera setup and syndication, and color TV transition.
- **Tier 3: Cable TV Revolution, Prestige Drama & Syndication Goldmines (Upper Intermediate)** — HBO satellite launch (Thrilla in Manila), MTV music video culture, Seinfeld syndication economics, The Sopranos and the birth of Peak TV, and Nielsen ratings mechanics.
- **Tier 4: The Streaming Wars, Binge Culture & The Death of Linear TV (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Netflix DVD to streaming disruption, algorithmic recommendation engines, unbundling cable packages, prestige drama economics collapse, and the future interactive screen.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The 1960 Nixon-Kennedy Debate: The Night Television Changed Politics Forever**
   - *Question:* Why did radio listeners believe Richard Nixon won the debate while television viewers were certain JFK dominated?
   - *Key Focus:* Visual optics vs acoustic rhetoric, Nixon's five o'clock shadow and sweating under studio lights, Kennedy's bronzed tan and calm demeanor.
2. **The Battle for the Screen: Philo Farnsworth vs David Sarnoff and RCA**
   - *Question:* How did a 14-year-old farm boy plowing potato fields imagine electronic television scanning lines, and how did RCA try to steal it?
   - *Key Focus:* Image dissector cathode tube, David Sarnoff's patent warfare, Vladimir Zworykin's iconoscope, and the 15-year legal patent victory.
3. **The 1939 World's Fair: When Franklin D. Roosevelt Became the First TV President**
   - *Question:* How did RCA unveil the commercial television set at the New York World's Fair as a futuristic furniture marvel?
   - *Key Focus:* TRK-12 wooden console receivers, FDR opening address, 441-line early broadcast signals, and World War II interruption of TV rollout.
4. **The 1950s Quiz Show Scandals: The Lie That Shattered America's Trust in TV**
   - *Question:* How did producers on the hit show Twenty-One feed Charles Van Doren answers in advance to orchestrate high TV ratings?
   - *Key Focus:* Geritol single-sponsor control, Herbert Stempel whistleblowing, Congressional hearings, and the shift from sponsor-owned to network-controlled programming.
5. **I Love Lucy: How Desi Arnaz Invented the Modern Multi-Camera Sitcom**
   - *Question:* How did Lucille Ball and Desi Arnaz create the 3-camera film setup, live studio audience, and the concept of reruns?
   - *Key Focus:* Karl Freund 35mm film lighting, Desilu studio ownership, retaining film negatives, and the invention of multi-million dollar television syndication.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Color TV Revolution: How RCA Beat CBS and NBC Painted the Peacock**
   - *Question:* Why did the FCC reject CBS's mechanical spinning color wheel in favor of RCA's backward-compatible electronic shadow-mask tube?
   - *Key Focus:* NTSC standard backwards compatibility, phosphor dot triads, shadow mask engineering, and NBC's famous living color peacock bumper.
7. **The Big Three Monoculture: When 90% of America Watched the Same Three Networks**
   - *Question:* What was life like when every citizen in the country watched NBC, CBS, or ABC, and Walter Cronkite was the trusted voice of God?
   - *Key Focus:* Least Objectionable Programming (LOP) philosophy, affiliate station network contracts, 100 million viewer Super Bowls, and shared cultural watercoolers.
8. **The Birth of Cable Television: From Mountain Antennas to HBO and CNN**
   - *Question:* How did Community Antenna Television (CATV) in rural valleys evolve into satellite-beamed pay-TV channels that crushed networks?
   - *Key Focus:* Coaxial cable infrastructure, Gerald Levin beaming the 1975 Ali-Frazier 'Thrilla in Manila' via satellite, and uncompressed multichannel choices.
9. **MTV: The Day Television Swallowed Music Culture**
   - *Question:* How did an unproven 24-hour music video cable channel launch with 'Video Killed the Radio Star' and define Gen-X visual aesthetics?
   - *Key Focus:* 'I Want My MTV' cable carriage campaign, Michael Jackson's 'Billie Jean' breaking the racial barrier, rapid-fire editing grammar, and pop culture dominance.
10. **Nielsen Ratings Decoded: How 40,000 Families Dictate $70 Billion in Ad Spend**
   - *Question:* How do paper diaries and set-top People Meters measure ratings, shares, and key demographic brackets (18-49)?
   - *Key Focus:* Rating point definition (1% of households), share of sets in use, sweeps months, upfront advertising markets, and demographic advertiser premiums.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Golden Age of Syndication: How Seinfeld and Friends Made Billions in Reruns**
   - *Question:* How did reaching the 100-episode milestone allow sitcom creators and stars to earn tens of millions per year in perpetuity?
   - *Key Focus:* Off-network strip syndication, cash-plus-barter deals, creator backend equity, and why sitcoms were the most profitable assets in Hollywood.
12. **The Sopranos and the Birth of Peak TV: When Television Overtook Cinema**
   - *Question:* How did David Chase and James Gandolfini prove that audiences would fall in love with a murderous, depressed mob boss?
   - *Key Focus:* Premium subscription economics (zero advertisers to offend), serialized multi-season character arcs, antihero archetypes, and the path to Breaking Bad and Mad Men.
13. **The Reality TV Gold Rush: How the 2007 Writers' Strike Created Modern Unscripted TV**
   - *Question:* Why did networks rush to fill schedules with cheap unscripted shows like Survivor, The Apprentice, and Keeping Up with the Kardashians?
   - *Key Focus:* WGA strikes, zero guild residual costs, non-union crews, massive product placement opportunities, and high viewer retention.
14. **The Fox Network Gamble: How Rupert Murdoch Used NFL Football to Build a 4th Network**
   - *Question:* How did Rupert Murdoch overpay by $100 million for NFL broadcast rights to force affiliate stations to join the Fox network?
   - *Key Focus:* Loss-leader sports broadcasting, The Simpsons subversive prime-time animation, counter-programming Married... with Children, and breaking the Big Three.
15. **The M*A*S*H Finale: The 106-Million-Viewer Record That Will Never Be Broken**
   - *Question:* How did the 1983 series finale of M*A*S*H capture 77% of all television sets turned on in America?
   - *Key Focus:* 'Goodbye, Farewell and Amen', water supply sewer drops during commercial breaks, television as communal cultural ritual, and peak monoculture.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **The Netflix Disruption: How Mail-Order DVDs Killed Blockbuster and Conquered Streaming**
   - *Question:* How did Reed Hastings transition from no-late-fee red envelopes to a global streaming platform with 250 million subscribers?
   - *Key Focus:* Broadband penetration tipping point, cloud infrastructure on AWS, House of Cards $100M data-driven bet, and the launch of binge-watching.
17. **The Streaming Wars Hangover: Why Hollywood is Losing Billions on Direct-to-Consumer Apps**
   - *Question:* Why did Disney, Warner Bros Discovery, and Paramount pivot from chasing subscriber numbers back to licensing and ad tiers?
   - *Key Focus:* Subscriber acquisition cost inflation, high churn rates, $30B annual content spending sprees, password sharing crackdowns, and ad-supported tiers.
18. **FAST Channels: The Return of Free Ad-Supported Television (Pluto, Tubi, Roku)**
   - *Question:* Why are millions of cord-cutters abandoning paid apps to watch 24/7 linear channels of Gordon Ramsay and Baywatch for free?
   - *Key Focus:* Free Ad-Supported Streaming TV (FAST), decision fatigue relief, programmatic ad insertions, back-catalog monetization, and the cyclical return of cable TV.
19. **The End of the Writers' Room: AI and the 2023 Hollywood Double Strike**
   - *Question:* Why did Hollywood screenwriters and actors shut down the industry for 148 days over streaming residuals and artificial intelligence?
   - *Key Focus:* Mini-rooms and gig-economy casualization, AI script generation bans, digital likeness replication ethics, and the new streaming compensation model.
20. **The Master Screen Architecture: The Future of Global Visual Entertainment**
   - *Question:* What overarching framework unites live sports rights, global IP franchises, creator economy competition, and interactive screens?
   - *Key Focus:* The battle between TikTok/YouTube and Prestige TV, spatial computing living room screens, sports rights bundling, and sustainable production economics.
