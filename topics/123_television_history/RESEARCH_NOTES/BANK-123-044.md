# BANK-123-044: U.S. full-power digital television transition

Research checked: 2026-10-24. Status: bounded historical premise research, not script or idea approval.

The [FCC transition consumer-notice order](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-09-11A1.pdf) states that the nationwide full-power broadcast transition would finish on June 12, 2009 and explains the need for converters for analog-only antenna receivers. The [FCC acting chair's June 13, 2009 remarks](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-291388A1.pdf) describe the end of full-power analog broadcasts the preceding day. The [GAO 2008 readiness report](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-08-510) documents technical and coordination risks before the transition; it reflects an earlier February 2009 statutory deadline, before the later delay.

**Claim boundary:** The June 2009 event concerned full-power U.S. over-the-air analog stations. It did not instantly make every analog TV unusable; low-power services, converters, cable, satellite and external sources have distinct paths. The GAO survey's 66.7 percent response rate and earlier date limit any inference about every station's actual June outcome. Household loss-of-service figures need later measured data. Recheck exact regulatory exceptions before scripting.
