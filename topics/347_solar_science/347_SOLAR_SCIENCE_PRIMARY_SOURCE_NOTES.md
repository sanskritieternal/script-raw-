# Solar science primary-source follow-up — 2026-10-24

## BANK-347-040 — Parker Solar Probe, 2021

- Primary mission account: NASA, *NASA Enters the Solar Atmosphere for the First Time, Bringing New Discoveries*, 14 December 2021: https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/nasa-enters-the-solar-atmosphere-for-the-first-time-bringing-new-discoveries/ . NASA mission page: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/parker-solar-probe/ .
- NASA reports that on 28 April 2021, during its eighth solar flyby, Parker measured particle and magnetic conditions interpreted as a crossing of the Alfvén critical surface, where solar magnetic fields dominate outward flow.
- Limit: this is a mission team's interpretation of a particular encounter, not a fixed spherical boundary around the Sun or proof of a complete corona-heating explanation. The NASA account was inspected; check the linked research paper before quantitative plasma-model claims.

## BANK-347-041 — Solar Orbiter south-pole observations, 2025

- Primary observatory account: European Space Agency, *Solar Orbiter gets world-first views of the Sun’s poles*, 11 June 2025: https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Solar_Orbiter/Solar_Orbiter_gets_world-first_views_of_the_Sun_s_poles%20 .
- ESA shows the south pole observed on 16–17 March 2025 from 15° below the solar equator by multiple instruments, including PHI's magnetic map, and describes mixed magnetic polarities during the then-current solar-cycle phase.
- Limit: these are early high-latitude views at a stated geometry, not a complete map of both poles over a full cycle or a validated forecast of future storms. ESA's narrative and image descriptions were accessible; raw instrument calibration was not assessed.
