# Selected case: DuPont La Porte toxic release

**Proposed question:** `BANK-517-043` — What did the CSB find behind the 2014 DuPont La Porte toxic release?

## Primary records inspected

- [CSB final investigation report](https://www.csb.gov/assets/1/6/dupont_laporte_final_report.pdf), report 2015-01-I-TX, released June 2019. The executive summary on printed pp. 7–8 reports a November 15, 2014 methyl-mercaptan release at the La Porte insecticide unit, four worker deaths, a flawed engineering design and inadequate safeguards, with further management-system and response deficiencies. It distinguishes the release cause from factors that increased severity.
- [CSB case recommendation tracker](https://www.csb.gov/recommendations/?F_InvestigationId=3581). The tracker has separate recommendation statuses, including **Closed — No Longer Applicable** and **Closed — Acceptable Action**. A closed count therefore must never be presented as proof that all proposed controls were installed or tested.

## Bounded premise and gaps

The investigation supports a site- and incident-specific causal analysis: routing and drainage of process piping, toxic-gas detection, ventilation, management of change and emergency response all require careful distinction. The report says the ventilation fans were out of service, but also says working fans probably would not have prevented a lethal atmosphere given the release; a script must not reduce the event to a single failed fan or blame individual operators. The recommendation tracker does not establish successful operation of every control later, injury-free performance, or risk at all chemical facilities. The full 166-page report has not been turned into a claim-level script worksheet.

## Coverage and overlap decision

Documented incident, investigation, hazard identification and corrective-action archive pitches route to this case; broad exposure, contractor and worker-participation topics route to existing `BANK-517-*` owners. Unsupported biographies, technology performance, future prevention and superlatives remain out of scope. `BANK-517-037` is the general distinction between isolation and reporting; `BANK-517-043` is the CSB's named La Porte causal record. General chemical process engineering elsewhere in the bank does not own this worker-safety investigation. The JFK8 topic in `516` concerns a union election, not this incident.
