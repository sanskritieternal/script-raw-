## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Worker Safety across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Worker Safety Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Worker Safety.
- **Tier 2: Worker Safety Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Worker Safety.
- **Tier 3: Worker Safety Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Worker Safety.
- **Tier 4: Worker Safety Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Worker Safety.

1. **The Architecture of Worker Safety: How Worker Safety Developed**
   - *Question:* Which documented hazard or incident shaped a workplace safety system?

Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: published incident reviews, exposure measures, control verification and worker reporting records. Starting framework: ILO occupational safety management guidelines (https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40ed_protect/%40protrav/%40safework/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_107727.pdf).
