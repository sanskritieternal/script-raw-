# Selected forensic-source checks — 24 October 2026

These checks support three bounded scientific or inquiry cases. They do not certify other archive claims, adjudicate an individual case or provide instructions for collecting, altering or evading evidence.

## BANK-100-053 — bitemark source attribution

- [NISTIR 8352, *Bitemark Analysis: A Scientific Foundation Review* (2023)](https://www.nist.gov/publications/bitemark-analysis-nist-scientific-foundation-review) is the primary institutional review of comparisons between marks on human skin and possible biters’ teeth. It identifies weaknesses in the scientific basis for individualizing a source from skin bitemarks and distinguishes foundations from legal admissibility in a particular court.
- **Editorial bound:** State the task being evaluated. Dental radiograph comparisons for identifying remains are a different method; the archive’s “all forensic odontology is junk” framing overgeneralizes the review. Check the exact court record before linking a method critique to a conviction.

## BANK-100-059 — Brandon Mayfield

- The [US Department of Justice Inspector General’s 2006 executive summary](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/legacy/special/s0601/exec.pdf) documents the FBI Laboratory’s erroneous fingerprint identification after the 2004 Madrid train attacks, the Spanish National Police’s contrary information, and withdrawal of the identification. It examines the comparison and verification processes rather than proving that all latent-print work is unreliable.
- The [full OIG report index](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0601/PDF_list.htm) provides chapters and redacted record context. The published executive summary is itself redacted, so documentary claims must respect unavailable material.
- **Editorial bound:** Distinguish an identification error, later investigative decisions and any specific finding about intent or bias. Do not infer guilt from an initial association or allege motives beyond the report.

## BANK-100-063 — Shirley McKie fingerprint inquiry

- [The Fingerprint Inquiry Scotland’s official report page](https://www.thefingerprintinquiryscotland.org.uk/inquiry/3127.html) links the 2011 report. The [inquiry report PDF](https://www.thefingerprintinquiryscotland.org.uk/inquiry/files/TheFingerprintInquiryReport_Low_res.pdf) states its remit: the identification and verification steps around the mark designated Y7 in *HM Advocate v McKie* and procedural recommendations.
- **Editorial bound:** Attribute conclusions to the inquiry, distinguish contested comparison from a finding of dishonest testimony, and verify any later court claim separately. The archive’s $100-billion scandal and categorical “lied in court” wording are unsupported here.
