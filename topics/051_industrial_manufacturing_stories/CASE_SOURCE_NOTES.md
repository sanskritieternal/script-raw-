## BANK-051-061 — Toyota Production System (archive pending 9)

- Toyota’s [production-system history](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/data/automotive_business/production/system/change.html) identifies just-in-time and jidoka as the two core concepts and dates their plant-wide and supplier adoption as a progression over decades. Toyota’s [just-in-time history](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/text/taking_on_the_automotive_business/chapter2/section4/item5.html) describes the later supermarket and kanban methods.
- Editorial boundary: explain the two linked production practices and documented adoption. The archived “crushed Detroit” claim is a competitive conclusion that requires separately chosen measures, dates and comparative evidence.

## BANK-051-062 — ASML EUV lithography (archive pending 10 and trending 22)

- ASML’s [EUV system description](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems) describes laser pulses striking tin droplets to generate 13.5-nm EUV light, multilayer mirrors, a vacuum light path and wafer handling. Its [lithography-principles guide](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers) distinguishes EUV light from earlier lithography sources.
- Editorial boundary: ASML is the primary manufacturer of EUV exposure systems, but a chip requires many tools and process steps. Avoid the archive’s fixed machine-price, “only machine,” flattest-mirror and single-device-completeness claims without model, date and source. The two archived ASML pitches route to this one proposal.

## BANK-051-077 — Seabed nickel and EV batteries (archive trending 27)

- The International Seabed Authority’s [current Q&A](https://isa.org.jm/faq-for-media/) says no commercial exploitation in the international seabed area has been approved while exploitation regulations remain under development. Its [polymetallic nodule contract register](https://isa.org.jm/exploration-contracts/polymetallic-nodules/) concerns exploration. The IEA’s [2026 critical-minerals outlook](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/outlook) treats nickel as a battery mineral but also distinguishes changing battery chemistries.
- Editorial boundary: frame seabed nodules as a prospective nickel supply question with environmental and governance uncertainties. The archived headline implies current EV batteries depend on deep-sea mining, which the cited records do not establish.
