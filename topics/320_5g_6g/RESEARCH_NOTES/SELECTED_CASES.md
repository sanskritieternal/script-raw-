## BANK-320-041 — ITU-R M.2160-0

- [ITU's Recommendation M.2160-0, approved 13 November 2023](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-M.2160-0-202311-I/en), is a framework and set of overall objectives for the future terrestrial component of IMT for 2030 and beyond. The [recommendation text](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/m/R-REC-M.2160-0-202311-I%21%21PDF-E.pdf) describes envisaged use scenarios and capabilities rather than a consumer device specification or achieved network benchmark.
- Check the recommendation's revision and later ITU technical requirements before describing the status at a later date. A research demonstration, framework capability and deployed service are distinct stages. Do not present a headline peak value as a typical user rate.
- The case makes BANK-320-033's framework-versus-product distinction concrete. Mobile device useful life belongs in 319; satellite internet's available capacity in 321 has a different system boundary.
