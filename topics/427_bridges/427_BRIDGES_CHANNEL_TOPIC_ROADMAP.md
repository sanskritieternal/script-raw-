## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Bridges across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Bridges Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Bridges.
- **Tier 2: Bridges Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Bridges.
- **Tier 3: Bridges Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Bridges.
- **Tier 4: Bridges Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Bridges.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Bridges: A Crossing Becomes a Bridge**
   - *Question:* Which geography and travel need shaped an early bridge?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace plans, funding, and prior routes. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
2. **Bridges: A Notable Bridge Reexamined**
   - *Question:* What do its design and inspection records establish?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify original build, modifications, and current condition. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
3. **Bridges: Labor at a Crossing**
   - *Question:* Whose building and maintenance work is absent from the story?
   - *Key Focus:* Use contract, payroll, and oral-history evidence. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
4. **Bridges: How a Bridge Carries Load**
   - *Question:* How do deck, supports, and foundations pass loads to ground?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one structural type and load case. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
5. **Bridges: Scour and Foundation Risk**
   - *Question:* How does water action alter a bridge's support?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare hydraulic model, inspection, and flood history. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Bridges: Uncertain Bridge Damage**
   - *Question:* Which inspection and material evidence could distinguish causes?
   - *Key Focus:* Avoid diagnosing from appearance alone. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
7. **Bridges: A New Bridge Material**
   - *Question:* Did a material improve durability or construction speed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare matched structures and long-term records. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
8. **Bridges: A Bridge Design Decision**
   - *Question:* Who chose a structural scheme, and what alternatives were rejected?
   - *Key Focus:* Read dated drawings and committee records. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
9. **Bridges: A Crossing Opens or Closes**
   - *Question:* How did travel and community access change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare route and traffic evidence before and after. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
10. **Bridges: Inspection to Repair**
   - *Question:* How does a finding become a load decision or repair order?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one documented owner process. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Bridges: A Bridge Replaced**
   - *Question:* Why did repair lose to replacement?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare condition, capacity, cost, and route needs. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
12. **Bridges: One Bridge over Time**
   - *Question:* Which components changed since construction?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated inspection and rehabilitation records. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
13. **Bridges: A Proposed Bridge**
   - *Question:* What tests and approvals would support its planned capacity?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat cost and travel forecasts as uncertain. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
14. **Bridges: Poor Condition Means Collapse?**
   - *Question:* What does an official condition label actually measure?
   - *Key Focus:* Read component ratings and current restrictions. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
15. **Bridges: Crossing from Plan to Service**
   - *Question:* Which design and site findings changed the built bridge?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace drawings, change orders, and inspections. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Bridges: Bridge versus Corridor**
   - *Question:* Which delays or risks belong to approaches and network connections?
   - *Key Focus:* Define segment and time period. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
17. **Bridges: Vehicle Load through Structure**
   - *Question:* How is load distributed to deck, members, and foundations?
   - *Key Focus:* Use design drawings and bounded load cases. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
18. **Bridges: Span, Material, and Cost**
   - *Question:* What did a chosen span arrangement gain and cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare alternatives under same site constraints. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
19. **Bridges: A Documented Bridge Failure**
   - *Question:* Which sequence did official investigation establish?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish initiating failure from contributing conditions. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
20. **Bridges: Model versus Field Behavior**
   - *Question:* Which assumptions in a bridge model need field validation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare sensor, load-test, and inspection evidence. Evidence base: dated design and as-built drawings, FHWA bridge inventory and inspection records, owner load ratings, repair records, official investigations, and community testimony.
