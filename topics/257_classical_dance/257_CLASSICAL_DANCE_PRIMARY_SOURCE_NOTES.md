# Selected institutional source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-257-044: Kalakshetra

- Kalakshetra Foundation history: https://www.kalakshetra.in/college/history/
- Kalakshetra annual report 2021-22: https://www.kalakshetra.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/KF-Annual-Report-2021-22_compressed.pdf
- The foundation dates its initial arts centre to 6 January 1936 and describes its Bharatanatyam and music teaching. This is an institution's account of its own contribution, not proof that one person originated or rescued an entire dance form. Examine earlier dancers, teachers and community perspectives and versioned programs before broader reform claims.

## BANK-257-045: Kerala Kalamandalam

- Kerala Kalamandalam official history: https://kalamandalam.ac.in/about-us/
- Its account of early Kathakali teachers and regional style inclusion: https://kalamandalam.ac.in/vadakkan-kathakali-history/
- The institution dates its founding to 1930 and says it initially trained Kathakali performers; a separate page names early teachers and later addition of a southern acting style. Institutional retrospectives do not establish a complete genealogy, one canonical technique or present-day performer consent. Compare archival programs and living teacher accounts before production.
