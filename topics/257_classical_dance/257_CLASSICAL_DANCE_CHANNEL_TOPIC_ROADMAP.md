## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Classical Dance across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Classical Dance Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Classical Dance.
- **Tier 2: Classical Dance Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Classical Dance.
- **Tier 3: Classical Dance Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Classical Dance.
- **Tier 4: Classical Dance Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Classical Dance.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Classical Dance: A Dance Form's Documented Development**
   - *Question:* Which records show how one named form took shape?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish older movement practice from later institutional naming. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
2. **Classical Dance: A Canonical Dance Reconsidered**
   - *Question:* What does one celebrated performance represent or omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare repertoire, artist choices, venue, and school. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
3. **Classical Dance: Labor Behind the Stage**
   - *Question:* Who trains, accompanies, costumes, lights, and organizes?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit collaborative work and consented accounts. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
4. **Classical Dance: Reading One Dance Form**
   - *Question:* Which movement, rhythm, and story cues matter?
   - *Key Focus:* Use tradition-specific vocabulary and a named performance. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
5. **Classical Dance: Variation Within Technique**
   - *Question:* How do lineages interpret the same movement differently?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare teachers and performances without ranking authenticity. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Classical Dance: A Disputed Choreography Credit**
   - *Question:* Which records establish who created a sequence?
   - *Key Focus:* Check programs, oral accounts, notation, dates, and gaps. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
7. **Classical Dance: Dance on Camera**
   - *Question:* How did recording or projection change choreography?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare framing, timing, audience, teaching, and rights. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
8. **Classical Dance: A Dancer-Teacher's Influence**
   - *Question:* How did one artist shape training or repertoire?
   - *Key Focus:* Use programs, students' accounts, and historical context. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
9. **Classical Dance: A Change in Dance Institutions**
   - *Question:* How did one policy or patronage shift affect dancers?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace employment, venues, repertory, and exclusions. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
10. **Classical Dance: Preparing a Performance**
   - *Question:* How do training, music, costume, and rehearsal connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow a consented production from studio to stage. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Classical Dance: Participation in a Dance Form**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports growth or decline?
   - *Key Focus:* Check school enrollments, professional work, informal learning, and definitions. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
12. **Classical Dance: One Piece Across Generations**
   - *Question:* What changed in gesture, duration, staging, and meaning?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated recordings and performer accounts. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
13. **Classical Dance: Sustaining Dancers and Teaching**
   - *Question:* Which choices matter for viable transmission?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare pay, spaces, access, rights, and practitioner priorities. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
14. **Classical Dance: A Fixed Ancient Dance?**
   - *Question:* Which features are documented earlier and which were standardized later?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated sources without dismissing living continuity. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
15. **Classical Dance: One Dance Work's Journey**
   - *Question:* How did a piece move from teaching to a public stage?
   - *Key Focus:* Use rehearsal notes and consented artist accounts. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Classical Dance: Making a Dance for a Venue**
   - *Question:* How do space, audience, music, and performer needs shape choices?
   - *Key Focus:* Name production, constraints, and collaborators. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
17. **Classical Dance: Costume and Floor as Tools**
   - *Question:* How do clothing, footwear, and surface affect movement?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask artists and makers; compare documented setups. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
18. **Classical Dance: Learning a Sequence**
   - *Question:* What stages move a dancer from demonstration to performance?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe teaching with consent and note adaptation. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
19. **Classical Dance: Why This Gesture Here**
   - *Question:* How do rhythm, narrative, and space shape choreography?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute artist or lineage explanation, not outsider symbolism. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
20. **Classical Dance: Who Made the Dance**
   - *Question:* How do choreographer, dancer, musician, and teacher contribute?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace program credits and agreements. Evidence base: dated programs and notation, complete recordings with permissions, institutional records, and consented dancer, teacher, and audience accounts.
