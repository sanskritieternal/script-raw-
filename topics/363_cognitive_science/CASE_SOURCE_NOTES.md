# Selected primary research source note — cognitive science

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-363-042`.

- [Open Science Collaboration, *Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science*](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/files/21474290/RPP.pdf) (author-accepted manuscript of *Science*, August 28, 2015, DOI 10.1126/science.aac4716): describes 100 completed replications drawn from three 2008 psychology journals, publicly registered protocols and multiple ways to compare original and replication findings. The author manuscript was accessible through the University of Edinburgh repository.
- [Center for Open Science, project release](https://www.cos.io/about/news/massive-collaboration-testing-reproducibility-psychology-studies-publishes-findings) (August 27, 2015): project coordinator account of sampling and the caveat that a nonmatching replication does not automatically prove the original claim false.

The Science publisher DOI page and OSF project landing page returned 403 in this inspection; the repository-hosted author manuscript and Center for Open Science release were accessible. This selected 100-study psychology sample includes areas beyond cognitive science. Different success criteria yield different summaries. A replication outcome may reflect sampling error, conditions or an original false positive; it cannot on its own fix a universal reproducibility rate or diagnose an individual study's cause.
