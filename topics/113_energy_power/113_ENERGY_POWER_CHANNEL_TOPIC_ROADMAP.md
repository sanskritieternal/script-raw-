## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Energy & Power across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Thermodynamics, Grid Physics & Fossil Fundamentals (Beginner / Scratch)** — First and second laws of thermodynamics, AC vs DC War of Currents (Tesla vs Edison), coal and combined cycle natural gas turbines, 50Hz/60Hz frequency balancing, and transformer substations.
- **Tier 2: Nuclear Fission, Hydroelectric Giants & Baseload Reliability (Intermediate)** — Pressurized water reactors (PWR), pumped storage hydro batteries, levelized cost of energy (LCOE), spinning reserves, and high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission lines.
- **Tier 3: The Renewable Inversion: Solar Photovoltaics, Wind & Grid Duck Curves (Upper Intermediate)** — Silicon PV bandgap physics, offshore wind nacelles, California duck curve overgeneration, lithium-ion grid storage, and wholesale nodal pricing markets (ERCOT/PJM).
- **Tier 4: Nuclear Fusion, SMRs & The Net-Zero Supergrid (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), magnetic confinement tokamak fusion, green hydrogen electrolysis, geothermal enhanced systems (EGS), and the master transcontinental supergrid.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The War of Currents: How Tesla and Westinghouse Electrified the Earth**
   - *Question:* How did Nikola Tesla's alternating current defeat Thomas Edison's direct current to build the world's power grids?
   - *Key Focus:* Transformer step-up voltage physics, copper line resistance losses, Niagara Falls power plant, and public electrocution propaganda.
2. **The Physics of the Electric Grid: Why 60 Hertz Frequency Must Never Fail**
   - *Question:* What happens when electricity consumption does not match generation down to the exact millisecond?
   - *Key Focus:* Grid inertia, rotational kinetic energy in turbines, frequency deviations, automatic under-frequency load shedding, and blackouts.
3. **Combined Cycle Natural Gas: The Workhorse of Modern Baseload Power**
   - *Question:* How do combined cycle power plants achieve 60%+ thermodynamic efficiency using waste heat?
   - *Key Focus:* Brayton cycle gas turbines, Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSG), Rankine steam cycles, and peaker plant economics.
4. **Transformer Substations and High-Voltage Transmission: Moving Megawatts Across Continents**
   - *Question:* Why do transmission lines carry 765,000 volts across states only to step it down to 120 volts at your wall socket?
   - *Key Focus:* Ohm's law, Joulean heating losses (I^2*R), step-up/step-down transformers, SF6 switchgear, and coronal discharge.
5. **Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE): How Wall Street Calculates the Cheapest Megawatt**
   - *Question:* How do utility analysts compare the true lifetime cost of solar panels, nuclear reactors, and coal plants?
   - *Key Focus:* Capital expenditures (capex), operating expenses (opex), capacity factors, discount rates, and decommissioning liabilities.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Nuclear Fission Engineering: Inside a Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR)**
   - *Question:* How do uranium fuel pellets generate gigawatts of carbon-free steam for two years straight without refueling?
   - *Key Focus:* U-235 fission chain reactions, neutron moderators, control rod scrams, primary vs secondary coolant loops, and containment domes.
7. **Hydroelectric Giants: The Engineering of Hoover Dam and Three Gorges**
   - *Question:* How does the kinetic weight of billions of gallons of river water spin Francis turbines to power entire nations?
   - *Key Focus:* Gravitational potential energy, penstock water hammer pressure, cavitation wear, and seasonal reservoir management.
8. **The Duck Curve: Why Solar Energy Breaks the Electric Grid at Sunset**
   - *Question:* Why does midday solar overgeneration cause electricity prices to go negative, followed by extreme evening price spikes?
   - *Key Focus:* Net load curves, rapid ramping requirements for gas peakers, solar curtailment, and grid strain.
9. **Pumped Storage Hydropower: The Forgotten Giant of Global Energy Storage**
   - *Question:* Why does 90% of the world's energy storage capacity consist of two water reservoirs at different elevations?
   - *Key Focus:* Reversible pump-turbines, round-trip efficiency (70-80%), black start capability, and topographic site scarcity.
10. **Wholesale Electricity Markets: How ERCOT and PJM Set Power Prices Every 5 Minutes**
   - *Question:* How do algorithmic auctions balance supply, demand, and transmission congestion across thousands of grid nodes?
   - *Key Focus:* Locational Marginal Pricing (LMP), capacity markets vs energy-only markets, reserve margin adequacy, and market manipulation.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Solar Photovoltaic Physics: From Silicon Sand to Free Photons**
   - *Question:* How does the photoelectric effect in doped p-n silicon junctions knock electrons free to generate electric current?
   - *Key Focus:* Shockley-Queisser limit, mono-crystalline vs poly-crystalline, Swanson's law price crashes, and perovskite tandem cells.
12. **Offshore Wind Megastructures: 15-Megawatt Giants Anchored in the Ocean**
   - *Question:* How do engineers erect 850-foot wind turbines with 350-foot carbon fiber blades in tempestuous ocean waters?
   - *Key Focus:* Monopile vs jacket foundations, floating tension-leg platforms, direct-drive permanent magnet generators, and subsea cabling.
13. **Grid-Scale Battery Storage: Lithium Iron Phosphate vs Flow Batteries**
   - *Question:* Can multi-megawatt battery installations provide 4-hour, 8-hour, or multi-day baseload backup for renewable grids?
   - *Key Focus:* LFP thermal runaway resistance, vanadium redox flow batteries, iron-air 100-hour storage, and degradation cycles.
14. **Blackout Forensics: What Caused the 2003 Northeast and 2021 Texas Grid Collapses?**
   - *Question:* How did overgrown tree branches in Ohio and frozen gas wellheads in Texas knock out power to tens of millions?
   - *Key Focus:* Cascading transmission trips, SCADA alarm failure, lack of winterization, islanding failure, and multi-day black start recovery.
15. **Geothermal Energy: Tapping Earth's Superheated Core with Oil Drilling Tech**
   - *Question:* How are startups using hydraulic fracturing horizontal drilling to extract 24/7 clean baseload steam from deep granite?
   - *Key Focus:* Enhanced Geothermal Systems (EGS), binary cycle power plants, supercritical fluids, and seismic monitoring.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Small Modular Reactors (SMRs): Factory-Built Nuclear Power for Data Centers**
   - *Question:* Why are tech giants like Microsoft, Amazon, and Google backing SMRs to power their AI data center campuses?
   - *Key Focus:* Passive safety systems, factory assembly vs on-site capex, high-temperature gas reactors (HTGR), and NRC regulatory hurdles.
17. **Nuclear Fusion: Taming the Power of the Sun in a Magnetic Bottle**
   - *Question:* What physics hurdles stand between scientific net energy gain (Q > 1) and commercially viable grid electricity?
   - *Key Focus:* ITER tokamak, magnetic confinement with REBCO high-temp superconductors, National Ignition Facility laser fusion, and plasma turbulence.
18. **High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC): The Superhighways of the Global Supergrid**
   - *Question:* Why is DC transmission superior to AC for moving gigawatts of wind and solar power across 2,000 miles?
   - *Key Focus:* Voltage Source Converters (VSC), zero capacitive charging losses, undersea cable interties, and continental grid interconnects.
19. **Green Hydrogen: The Clean Molecule for Steel, Fertilizer, and Long-Duration Power**
   - *Question:* Why can't batteries solve industrial emissions, and how does green hydrogen crack the heavy industry decarbonization puzzle?
   - *Key Focus:* PEM vs alkaline electrolyzers, round-trip efficiency penalties, pipeline embrittlement, and salt cavern storage.
20. **The Net-Zero Supergrid Masterclass: Architecting the 100% Clean Energy Civilization**
   - *Question:* What unified energy architecture balances intermittent solar/wind, nuclear baseload, hydro storage, and continental HVDC grids?
   - *Key Focus:* Capacity expansion modeling, grid resilience against solar storms and cyberattacks, sector coupling, and dynamic demand response.
