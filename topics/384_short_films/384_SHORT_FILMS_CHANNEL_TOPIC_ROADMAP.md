## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Short Films across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Short Films Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Short Films.
- **Tier 2: Short Films Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Short Films.
- **Tier 3: Short Films Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Short Films.
- **Tier 4: Short Films Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Short Films.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Short Films: A Short Film in Its Program**
   - *Question:* Which exhibition or production context shaped one short work?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated program, print, and production records. Evidence base: identified films and cuts, scripts, production and sound records, festival or broadcast programs, rights documents, archives, and situated criticism.
2. **Short Films: A Short's Central Choice**
   - *Question:* How does a named film build a complete turn within its runtime?
   - *Key Focus:* Analyze timecoded setup, change, and ending. Evidence base: identified films and cuts, scripts, production and sound records, festival or broadcast programs, rights documents, archives, and situated criticism.
3. **Short Films: Crew behind a Short**
   - *Question:* Whose craft made a low-budget or student film possible?
   - *Key Focus:* Check credits, call sheets, and participant accounts. Evidence base: identified films and cuts, scripts, production and sound records, festival or broadcast programs, rights documents, archives, and situated criticism.
4. **Short Films: A Short Film's Focus**
   - *Question:* Which information can be established with a small number of scenes?

5. **Short Films: Compression without Confusion**
