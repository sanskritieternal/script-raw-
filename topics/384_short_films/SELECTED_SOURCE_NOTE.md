## Primary distributor record

- The Film-Makers’ Cooperative catalog, [*Meshes of the Afternoon*](https://film-makerscoop.com/catalogue/maya-deren-meshes-of-the-afternoon/stills/7), lists the 1943 work under Maya Deren, names Alexandr Hackenschmied as co-director, describes recurring domestic objects and images, and offers 16mm and digital-file rental. Its page also links later Cooperative programs. This establishes how this distributor presently identifies and circulates the film. It does **not** establish which print, restoration, soundtrack or exact runtime every earlier or later audience saw.
- The [Cooperative’s digital streaming catalog](https://film-makerscoop.com/digital-catalogue?page=54) independently exposes the current rental-format listing within the same institution; this is not an independent production-history source.

## Editorial limits and overlap

The catalog labels the film “sound” while dating it 1943. Treat soundtrack and print histories as version-specific questions requiring separate archival verification. Do not infer original-release sound from this present catalog entry. Confirm clip and still reproduction rights before use. Credit both named directors.
