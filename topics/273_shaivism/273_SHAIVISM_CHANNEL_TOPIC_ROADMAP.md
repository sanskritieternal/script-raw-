## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Shaivism across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Shaivism Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Shaivism.
- **Tier 2: Shaivism Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Shaivism.
- **Tier 3: Shaivism Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Shaivism.
- **Tier 4: Shaivism Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Shaivism.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Shaivism: A Śaiva Lineage in Dated Sources**
   - *Question:* Which records show one named Śaiva community or institution taking shape?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inscriptions, texts, sites, and later lineage narratives. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
2. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Site in Its Own Tradition**
   - *Question:* How do custodians explain one prominent site, and what does its history show?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute devotional meaning and use dated material evidence separately. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
3. **Shaivism: People Sustaining a Śaiva Community**
   - *Question:* Whose work in teaching, art, care, or maintenance is absent from familiar histories?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and institutional records. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
4. **Shaivism: Many Śaiva Traditions**
   - *Question:* How do two named Śaiva communities differ in text, practice, or philosophy?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe each on its own terms without a single universal template. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
5. **Shaivism: Śiva, Liberation, and Interpretation**
   - *Question:* How do named Śaiva texts differ over one philosophical question?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare specified passages and commentaries, not generic doctrine. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Text's Attribution**
   - *Question:* Which evidence supports or complicates the attribution of a named text?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine manuscript colophons, citations, and later tradition. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
7. **Shaivism: Digitizing a Śaiva Manuscript**
   - *Question:* Can a new edition clarify one disputed reading without erasing context?
   - *Key Focus:* Record witness provenance, access permission, and editorial choices. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
8. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Teacher in the Sources**
   - *Question:* What can a named teacher's writings and institutional records establish?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate their argument from later devotional biography. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
9. **Shaivism: A Change at a Śaiva Institution**
   - *Question:* How did one dated event alter teaching, worship, or stewardship?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare records before and after with practitioner voices. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
10. **Shaivism: A Local Śaiva Observance**
   - *Question:* How do participants explain the public sequence of one observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify community and place; respect restricted elements. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Shaivism: Patronage of a Śaiva Site**
   - *Question:* Why did resources to one shrine or teaching center change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use grants, accounts, inscriptions, and local history. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
12. **Shaivism: One Śaiva Practice Across Generations**
   - *Question:* What changed in one community's practice or teaching?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated descriptions with consented present accounts. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
13. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Heritage Choice**
   - *Question:* What would one conservation or teaching proposal preserve or alter?
   - *Key Focus:* Assess custodial priorities, access, cost, and living use. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
14. **Shaivism: A Claim About All Śaivas**
   - *Question:* Does one generalization fit distinct Śaiva lineages?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare named texts and communities; attribute their own descriptions. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
15. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Text or Site Through Time**
   - *Question:* How did one source or place acquire its present form?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace manuscripts, patronage, alterations, and living memory. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Passage in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one passage say within its named text and recension?
   - *Key Focus:* Cite original, edition, translation, and neighboring argument. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
17. **Shaivism: Two Śaiva Readings**
   - *Question:* How do two lineages interpret one shared term or practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Represent each account with its own authoritative sources. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
18. **Shaivism: A Community's Everyday Devotion**
   - *Question:* What role does Śaiva practice play in a named community's daily life?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented voices and specify time and place. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
19. **Shaivism: A Rite as Locally Performed**
   - *Question:* Which stages of one public Śaiva rite do participants identify?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish text prescription from observed practice and respect permissions. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
20. **Shaivism: A Śaiva Term in Translation**
   - *Question:* How does translating one Sanskrit or regional term alter interpretation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare usage, grammar, and practitioner explanation. Evidence base: specified Śaiva texts and editions, dated inscriptions and institutional records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
