# Selected bird case checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. These sources bound the selected premises; they do not verify every statement in the preserved archived pitches.

## BANK-196-038 — harpy eagle prey

The [original nest study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10023775/) observed 212 prey deliveries at 10 harpy-eagle nests. Its place, sampling and nest observations can support a specific prey-composition account. The archived grizzly-claw comparison and a universal diet percentage are outside that evidence.

## BANK-196-041 — dodo research

[Colossal's primordial-germ-cell update](https://colossal.com/news-dodo-de-extinction-pigeon-pgc-breakthrough/) describes a technical step toward its project, not a living dodo. This is a company report, so independently published methods and outcomes should be checked before a script asserts a biological milestone or timeline. Existing BANK-196-026 covers extinct island birds; this proposed case owns the present research program, with no claim of restoration.

## BANK-196-044 — condor lead exposure

The [US Fish and Wildlife Service 2024 California Condor Population Status](https://www.fws.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2025-03/2024-california-condor-population-status508-compliant.pdf) reports lead-poisoning mortality over a specified monitoring period. Keep wild versus captive counts and the period explicit; do not imply the cumulative count is an annual rate. The [recovery program](https://www.fws.gov/program/california-condor-recovery) supplies context. The archived claim about a ban requires a jurisdiction and effective date before use.
