# Selected primary-source checks - Fungi
Checked 2026-10-24. Bounded research leads only; no production or health readiness.

- BANK-203-038: Original mechanical and sensory lion mane steak study, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13092705/ . Results apply to tested preparation and panel, not all mycoprotein, nutrition or environmental outcomes.
- BANK-203-039: CDC investigation of 2016 California death-cap poisonings, https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/66/wr/mm6621a1.htm . Fourteen suspected ingestions were investigated and three required liver transplants. RNA polymerase II inhibition is described; archive 48-hour outcome is not universal. No foraging or treatment instruction.
- BANK-203-040: Lorch et al. 2011 infection study hosted by USGS, https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70032551 . Pure-culture exposure caused white-nose syndrome in little brown bats in this experiment; no global mortality inference.
- BANK-203-041: FDA 2020 warning letter for turkey tail and chaga product claims, https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/half-hill-farm-inc-609440-11032020 . This identifies unapproved disease claims for one marketer, not efficacy of all preparations.
- BANK-203-029: NASA ISS experimental summary, https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/exposed-international-space-station-tests-organisms-materials-in-space/ . A melanized fungal layer produced a small radiation reduction; it does not establish astronaut or electronics protection on the Moon.
- Overlap: BANK-484-041 concerns fermentation-derived fibers versus edible proteins. BANK-203-021 covers Oregon Armillaria. Forest mycorrhiza claims require species, site and exchange measurement, not purposeful tree communication.
