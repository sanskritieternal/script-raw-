# Bounded source note: East Palestine train 32N

Accessed 2026-10-24. Primary inquiry: U.S. National Transportation Safety Board, [*Norfolk Southern Railway Derailment and Hazardous Materials Release, East Palestine, Ohio, February 3, 2023*](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/RIR2405%20CORRECTED.pdf), RIR-24-05, issued June 25, 2024 and corrected July 23, 2024. The [NTSB investigation page](https://www.ntsb.gov/EastPalestine/Pages/default.aspx) identifies the case and docket.

The NTSB determined that the L1 bearing on the 23rd car of Norfolk Southern train 32N overheated and failed, leading to axle separation and derailment. Its report also examines limitations in bearing detection, tank-car performance, emergency information and the later decision to vent and burn vinyl chloride cars. The critical detector alarm occurred shortly before the derailment; that sequence does not show the crew had a workable earlier warning. The report treats the derailment cause and subsequent hazardous-material decisions as related but distinct findings.

Limits: this is one U.S. accident, not a failure rate for all freight trains or proof that a particular detector threshold would prevent every derailment. The inquiry is a safety investigation, not a liability finding or an emergency-response manual. Production should use the corrected report and docket, date any later regulatory or environmental claims, and avoid procedural directions for train operation, hazardous-material handling, or emergency response. This note checks one selected premise only.
