## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Mantra across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Mantra Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Mantra.
- **Tier 2: Mantra Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Mantra.
- **Tier 3: Mantra Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Mantra.
- **Tier 4: Mantra Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Mantra.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Mantra: A Mantra in Its Earliest Witness**
   - *Question:* Where does one named mantra appear in surviving sources?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish composition, oral transmission, manuscript copy, and later use. Evidence base: specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts.
2. **Mantra: A Familiar Mantra in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one widely known mantra mean within its source tradition?
   - *Key Focus:* Check exact wording, setting, translation, and practitioner explanation. Evidence base: specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts.
3. **Mantra: Teachers of Recitation**
   - *Question:* Whose teaching work preserves one recitation tradition?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and records of a named community. Evidence base: specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts.
4. **Mantra: What the Word Mantra Covers**

   - *Key Focus:* Specify language, textual source, purpose, and access rules. Evidence base: specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts.
5. **Mantra: Sound, Text, and Meaning**

   - *Key Focus:* Compare their own explanations rather than imposing one theory. Evidence base: specified textual recensions and translations, recordings with custodian permission, manuscript catalogues, teaching records, and consented practitioner accounts.
