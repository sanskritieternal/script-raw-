# Rehabilitation source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-502-043 - AVERT randomized stroke trial

- Primary trial report: AVERT Trial Collaboration, *Efficacy and safety of very early mobilisation within 24 h of stroke onset (AVERT): a randomised controlled trial*, Lancet 2015, DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60690-0, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25892679/ (inspected 2026-10-24). Adults with ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke meeting physiological criteria were randomized at 56 acute stroke units in five countries. The added frequent, higher-dose very early mobilization protocol was compared with usual stroke-unit care. The primary endpoint was modified Rankin Scale 0-2 at three months. The reported favorable outcome was 46% versus 50% (adjusted OR 0.73, 95% CI 0.59-0.90).
- Primary follow-up analysis: *Prespecified dose-response analysis for A Very Early Rehabilitation Trial (AVERT)*, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4898313/ (inspected 2026-10-24). It explores timing, frequency and amount separately; avoid treating the original arm comparison as proof that every early activity dose is harmful.
- Population and claim limits: The trial concerns selected adult acute-stroke patients and a specific more-frequent, higher-dose intervention; usual care itself often included early mobilization. Three-month mRS is not every patient-defined participation outcome, pediatric or chronic rehabilitation, or a clinical instruction for an individual. Check trial protocol, adverse events, subsequent guidelines and current evidence before any practice claim. No individualized therapy advice.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-502-043 is a named clinical trial and endpoint interpretation case. BANK-503-030 covers assistive device plus environment; BANK-501-040 covers population access cost; BANK-489-036 covers occupational devices versus rehabilitation technology.
