## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Rehabilitation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Rehabilitation Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Rehabilitation.
- **Tier 2: Rehabilitation Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Rehabilitation.
- **Tier 3: Rehabilitation Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Rehabilitation.
- **Tier 4: Rehabilitation Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Rehabilitation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Rehabilitation: How Rehabilitation Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a rehabilitation service's goals and professional roles develop?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one rehabilitation service from its original patient need through changes in goals, professional roles and delivery; name the condition and setting. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Rehabilitation: A Documented Rehabilitation Case**
   - *Question:* Which patients and functional outcomes does a prominent rehabilitation evaluation describe?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one documented rehabilitation pathway using eligibility, participation and functional outcome data; state which patients the findings describe. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
3. **Rehabilitation: People behind Rehabilitation**
   - *Question:* What patient and therapist work is omitted from a rehabilitation success story?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow patients' or therapists' overlooked work within one rehabilitation service; use consented accounts and avoid treating one experience as representative. Evidence base: Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
4. **Rehabilitation: Understanding Rehabilitation**
   - *Question:* How does one rehabilitation pathway connect assessment, therapy and follow-up?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain assessment, goal setting, therapy and follow-up in one care pathway; describe roles without prescribing exercises or individual treatment. Evidence base: Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action). 5. **Rehabilitation: Tradeoffs in Rehabilitation**
   - *Question:* Do alternative functional measures tell the same rehabilitation story?

Evidence base: Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: referral criteria, service activity, functional measures and consented patient accounts. Starting framework: WHO Rehabilitation in health systems: guide for action (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/rehabilitation-in-health-systems-guide-for-action).
