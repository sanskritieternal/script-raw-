## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Organization across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Organization Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Organization.
- **Tier 2: Organization Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Organization.
- **Tier 3: Organization Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Organization.
- **Tier 4: Organization Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Organization.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Organization: A Household Storage Practice**
   - *Question:* Which dated housing or work change shaped a storage norm?
   - *Key Focus:* Use plans, catalogs, and household accounts. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
2. **Organization: A Room That Stays Usable**
   - *Question:* What evidence shows an organizing system works after daily use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare before, after, and follow-up with the same tasks. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
3. **Organization: The Labor of Order**
   - *Question:* Who sorts, cleans, labels, and resets a household system?
   - *Key Focus:* Record time and unequal task distribution. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
4. **Organization: Organize for a Task**
   - *Question:* How can items be placed so a specific activity is easier?
   - *Key Focus:* Define user, frequency, reach, and retrieval time. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
5. **Organization: Storage versus Behavior**
   - *Question:* Does adding containers solve a capacity problem or just hide it?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure inventory, space, and repeated use. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Organization: Why Does Clutter Return?**
   - *Question:* Which capacity or routine gap can observation distinguish?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a consented household log rather than diagnosis. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
7. **Organization: A Digital Inventory at Home**
   - *Question:* Does a label or app improve retrieval enough to justify upkeep?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare task time, errors, and maintenance. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
8. **Organization: A Household Negotiates Space**
   - *Question:* Whose preferences changed a shared storage plan?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts without assigning blame. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
9. **Organization: A Move Forces Reorganization**
   - *Question:* Which possessions and routines changed after relocation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inventory and use across dated homes. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
10. **Organization: From Inventory to Placement**
   - *Question:* How does a household decide what belongs where?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace actual tasks, dimensions, ownership, and agreement. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Organization: An Organizing Trend Reconsidered**
   - *Question:* Why did a once-popular container or rule stop working?
   - *Key Focus:* Use long-term use evidence and costs. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
12. **Organization: A Storage System after Six Months**
   - *Question:* Which retrieval, safety, or upkeep outcomes changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Use same measures before and after. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
13. **Organization: Planning for a New Need**
   - *Question:* How can a system adapt when work, care, or household size changes?
   - *Key Focus:* Present adjustable scenarios, not a perfect solution. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
14. **Organization: Tidying Takes One Day?**
   - *Question:* What continued labor does a photo-ready makeover omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Record maintenance over a defined period. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
15. **Organization: One Home's Organization Trial**
   - *Question:* What did a household try, revise, and retain?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated inventory, layout, and resident feedback. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Organization: Finding Things in Daily Life**
   - *Question:* Which tasks became faster or harder after reorganization?
   - *Key Focus:* Use participant logs and specific tasks. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
17. **Organization: Rules in Shared Housing**
   - *Question:* Which lease, building, or workplace rule limits storage choices?
   - *Key Focus:* Read actual policy and approval process. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
18. **Organization: What Counts as Order?**
   - *Question:* How do aesthetics, time, and shared expectations shape a system?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask users before imposing a standard. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
19. **Organization: Storage within Reach**
   - *Question:* Who cannot access a shelf, label, or digital inventory?
   - *Key Focus:* Test with named users and alternatives. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
20. **Organization: Different Homes, Different Order**
   - *Question:* How do family size, routines, and cultural practices affect storage?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare situated examples without moral ranking. Evidence base: measured rooms and inventories, consented task observations, household agreements, itemized costs, follow-up logs, and relevant professional ethics.
