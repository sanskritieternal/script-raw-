## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Rankings & Comparisons across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Quantitative Normalization, Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis & Index Weighting (Beginner / Scratch)** — Standardization (z-score normalization, min-max scaling), multi-criteria decision making (MCDA), identifying subjective vs objective ranking metrics, and creating transparent benchmark scorecards.
- **Tier 2: Benchmarking Consumer Hardware, Infrastructure Capacities & Economic Performance (Intermediate)** — Synthetic vs real-world tech benchmarks, cost-per-unit performance curves, sovereign GDP purchasing power parity (PPP) vs nominal rankings, and consumer product head-to-head testing matrices.
- **Tier 3: Military Capabilities, Megastructure Scales & Sovereign Quality of Life Indices (Upper Intermediate)** — Global Firepower index critique vs logistics reality, Human Development Index (HDI) multi-dimensional health/education/income synthesis, and structural megaproject comparisons (dams, canals, skyscrapers).
- **Tier 4: Algorithmic Ranking Systems, Eliminating Selection Bias & Multi-Dimensional Pareto Frontiers (Advanced / Masterclass)** — PageRank, Elo rating systems and Bradley-Terry probability models, detecting Goodhart's Law metric gaming, multi-objective Pareto optimization frontiers, and building unbiased analytical tier lists.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The World's Top 10 Megaprojects Compared: Cost, Scale, and Real-World Impact**
   - *Question:* How do we compare engineering giants as radically different as the Panama Canal expansion, the International Space Station, and the Three Gorges Dam?
   - *Key Focus:* Normalized capital expenditure (inflation-adjusted billions), concrete and steel tonnage, return on investment (ROI), environmental disruption metrics, and long-term societal utility.
2. **The Science of Comparison: How to Rank Things Without Bias or Apples-to-Oranges Fallacies**
   - *Question:* Why do most internet tier lists and rankings collapse into meaningless subjective shouting matches?
   - *Key Focus:* Min-max and z-score normalization, isolating confounding variables, establishing transparent weighting rubrics, and avoiding the single-metric reductionist trap.
3. **The Elo Rating System: The Math That Ranks Chess Grandmasters, Gamers, and Football Teams**
   - *Question:* How did physicist Arpad Elo design a mathematical formula that dynamically updates skill ratings based on probability and unexpected upsets?
   - *Key Focus:* Logistic distribution expected win formulas (R_new = R_old + K*(S - E)), K-factor volatility tuning, Glicko rating deviation improvements, and online competitive matchmaking.
4. **GDP Nominal vs PPP: Why Russia and China Look Twice as Rich When You Measure Bread Instead of Dollars**
   - *Question:* Which economy is actually bigger: the one with more US dollars at currency exchange rates, or the one that can buy more steel, food, and energy at home?
   - *Key Focus:* The Penn World Table and Big Mac Index, currency exchange rate volatility distortions, Balassa-Samuelson effect on services, and assessing true industrial sovereign capacity.
5. **The Human Development Index (HDI): Moving Beyond Pure GDP to Measure Human Flourishing**
   - *Question:* How did two South Asian economists persuade the United Nations to rank countries using life expectancy and education rather than just corporate profit?
   - *Key Focus:* Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen's capabilities approach, geometric mean calculations preventing metric substitution, Inequality-adjusted HDI (IHDI), and Nordic top-rank consistency.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Military Firepower Rankings: Why Spreadsheet Weapon Counts Lie About Real War**
   - *Question:* Why did popular military ranking sites give armies thousands of paper tanks that turned out to be rusted hulls without crews or fuel in actual combat?
   - *Key Focus:* Active inventory vs rotting depot reserves, logistics replenishment capability, air defense integration, training flight hours, combined-arms doctrine, and Global Firepower index limitations.
7. **The Benchmark Trap: How Tech Companies Cheat Synthetic Speed Tests**
   - *Question:* How do smartphone and GPU manufacturers detect benchmark software and secretly unlock dangerous thermal overclocks to inflate scores?
   - *Key Focus:* Geekbench and AnTuTu whitelist detection scandals, sustained thermal throttling vs 30-second sprint scores, real-world frame-time pacing vs average FPS, and rigorous independent testing.
8. **Top 10 Tallest Buildings: Spire Tricks, Vanity Height, and the Supertall Race**
   - *Question:* Why does a 150-meter empty ornamental radio spire count toward a skyscraper's official world record height, while an occupied observation roof does not?
   - *Key Focus:* Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH) measurement rules, Burj Khalifa vs Merdeka 118, the 'vanity height' percentage controversy, elevator wait-time economics, and megatall engineering limits.
9. **The Global Liveability Index: What Makes Vienna and Melbourne Repeatedly Top the Charts?**
   - *Question:* How does the Economist Intelligence Unit quantify whether a city is good to live in, and why do world financial capitals like New York and London rank outside the top 30?
   - *Key Focus:* Stability, healthcare, culture/environment, education, and infrastructure categories; municipal public transit integration; social housing access in Vienna; and expat vs resident biases.
10. **Goodhart's Law: When a Measure Becomes a Target, It Ceases to Be a Good Measure**
   - *Question:* Why do university ranking systems cause colleges to reject qualified applicants and spend millions on luxury climbing walls rather than professors?
   - *Key Focus:* US News & World Report ranking methodology gaming, Campbell's Law and policing quotas, academic citation cartels and h-index manipulation, and designing counter-incentive systems.
### Tier 3: Advanced Methodologies & Frameworks
11. **Fighter Jet Generations Compared: From Korea's F-86 Sabres to 6th-Gen NGAD**
   - *Question:* What actually separates a 4th-generation F-15 from a 5th-generation F-35: is it speed, dogfighting agility, or stealth and sensor fusion?
   - *Key Focus:* Radar Cross Section (RCS) marble-size stealth, AESA radar arrays, sensor fusion presenting a single tactical picture, internal weapons bays, and drone-wingman 6th-gen capabilities.
12. **The Happiness Index: Can You Mathematically Measure National Well-Being?**
   - *Question:* Why do freezing Nordic countries (Finland, Denmark, Iceland) consistently rank happiest in the world despite dark winters and high tax rates?
   - *Key Focus:* Gallup World Poll Cantril Ladder survey methodology, social trust and safety nets, low corruption, work-life balance, and cultural stoicism vs emotional exuberance across societies.
13. **Top 10 Deepest Oceanic Trenches: Life, Geology, and Exploration Challenges**
   - *Question:* How do the Mariana, Tonga, Philippine, and Kermadec trenches differ in depth, tectonic subduction angle, and endemic hadal biology?
   - *Key Focus:* Oceanic-oceanic plate subduction rates, trench roll-back dynamics, multibeam sonar bathymetry mapping accuracy, hadal zone endemism, and deep submersible exploration rosters.
14. **Electric Vehicles Compared: Range, Efficiency, Charging Curves, and Cold-Weather Degradation**
   - *Question:* Why does a car with a 300-mile EPA window sticker manage only 210 miles at 75 mph in real-world highway winter conditions?
   - *Key Focus:* Aerodynamic drag area (Cd * A), inverter efficiency, EPA test cycle quirks vs real-world highway dynamometer tests, 10-80% charging curve areas, and heat pump efficiency rankings.
15. **The Pareto Frontier: The Mathematical Blueprint for Multi-Objective Trade-Offs**
   - *Question:* When you want something fast, cheap, and good, how does Pareto optimality define the set of choices where you cannot improve one metric without degrading another?
   - *Key Focus:* Dominated vs non-dominated solutions, multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (NSGA-II), plotting 2D and 3D Pareto trade-off curves, and making rational engineering compromises.
16. **The Passport Power Index: What Gives a Little Blue Booklet Visa-Free Access to 190 Countries?**
   - *Question:* Why do Singapore and Japan consistently hold the world's most powerful passports, while other wealthy nations require visas for half the globe?
   - *Key Focus:* Henley Passport Index and Arton Capital methodologies, bilateral visa waiver agreements, geopolitical neutrality, international trade ties, and low illegal immigration overstay rates.
### Tier 4: Enterprise Strategy & Deep Mastery
17. **The World's Deadliest Pandemics Ranked: Death Toll, Transmission R0, and Case Fatality Rates**
   - *Question:* How do epidemiologists rank pandemics when comparing absolute death tolls against world population percentages and basic reproduction numbers?
   - *Key Focus:* The Black Death (1347-1351) killing 50% of Europe vs 1918 Spanish Flu vs COVID-19; Basic reproduction number (R0) vs Case Fatality Rate (CFR); and pandemic preparedness index evaluations.
18. **Algorithmic Recommendations: How PageRank, YouTube, and TikTok Rank the Entire Internet**
   - *Question:* How do search engines and video platforms sort billions of items in 50 milliseconds to decide what appears at the very top of your screen?
   - *Key Focus:* Larry Page's PageRank random surfer Markov chains, two-tower neural network retrieval and ranking stages, collaborative filtering embeddings, watch-time optimization, and engagement click-through weights.
19. **The Cost of Ranking #1: The SEO and PR Industry Built Around Search Position**
   - *Question:* Why is the top spot on Google for keywords like 'auto insurance' or 'mesothelioma lawyer' worth millions of dollars a year?
   - *Key Focus:* Cost-Per-Click (CPC) bidding auctions on Google Ads, organic click-through rate falloff curves (Position 1 gets 30%, Position 10 gets 2%), high-authority backlink outreach costs, and the $80B SEO industry.
20. **The Masterclass on Systems Benchmarking: Designing an Unimpeachable Ranking Framework**
   - *Question:* How does an elite systems analyst design a public benchmark scorecard that cannot be gamed, contested, or dismissed as biased?
   - *Key Focus:* Defining clear operational boundaries, open-source raw datasets, sensitivity analysis testing weight perturbations, double-blind testing procedures, peer review audits, and transparent error bar publication.
