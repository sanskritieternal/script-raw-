# Ranking case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary records inspected for proposed cases. These establish methods or dated measurements, not a current universal league table.

| Case | Primary source | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot Arena | [Zheng et al., 2024 platform paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04132) describes anonymous pairwise human votes and statistical ranking. | Votes are a sample of users and prompts; the rank is conditional on method, period and model availability. It is not a timeless measure of intelligence. |
| Military spending | [SIPRI 2024 fact sheet](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2025/sipri-fact-sheets/trends-world-military-expenditure-2024) and [database methods](https://www.sipri.org/databases/milex/sources-and-methods) define a comparable spending series. | Spending is neither operational capability nor outcome. Fiscal/calendar years, exchange rates, estimated figures and exclusions matter; the archived dollar comparison must be recalculated for a specified year. |
| Passport rankings | [Henley Passport Index methodology](https://www.henleyglobal.com/passport-index/methodology) scores destination access under defined entry rules. | Different indexes and date cuts may rank passports differently; score is not a measure of practical travel freedom for an individual. |
