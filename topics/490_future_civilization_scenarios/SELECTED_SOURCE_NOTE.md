# Selected source note — IPCC AR6 shared socioeconomic pathways

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-490-043. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA or prediction of one inevitable civilization.

## Primary assessment records

- IPCC AR6 Working Group I, Chapter 1, *Framing, Context and Methods* (2021): https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-1/ . Section 1.6 describes the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways framework, illustrative emissions scenarios and their relationship to earlier pathways.
- IPCC AR6 Working Group I, *Summary for Policymakers*: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/ . It assesses conditional climate responses under five illustrative scenarios, including SSP1-2.6 and SSP5-8.5.
- IPCC AR6 Working Group III, Annex III, *Scenarios and Modelling Methods*: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_Annex-III.pdf . It distinguishes socioeconomic narratives and drivers from policy and emissions assumptions incorporated in modeled pathways.

## Model and social-claim limits

SSP labels describe internally specified socioeconomic and emissions pathways, not probabilities that a complete civilization will follow each one. Modelled warming under a scenario is conditional on its inputs and physical-model assessment; it is not an observation of the future. A global aggregate hides local distribution and institutional choices. Do not infer which future is most likely without separate likelihood evidence, or treat IPCC illustrations as exhaustive futures. Note report vintage when comparing with later emissions or policy data. Paraphrase IPCC text and check figure reuse terms.
