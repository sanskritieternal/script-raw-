- **Tier 4: World Geography Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for World Geography.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of World Geography: How a Place Became Mapped**
   - *Question:* Which survey and political decisions shaped one map feature?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated maps, methods, local names, and boundary records. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
2. **World Geography: A Famous Map Claim Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What does one widely shared geographic ranking actually measure?
   - *Key Focus:* Define feature, extent, datum, projection, source, and date. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
3. **World Geography: Who Made the Map?**
   - *Question:* Whose fieldwork and local knowledge appear or disappear in one map?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace surveyors, Indigenous and local contributors, editors, and data sources. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
4. **World Geography: Reading a World Map Critically**
   - *Question:* What do projection, scale, legend, and date change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one map and compare with ground or official data. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
5. **World Geography: Boundaries Are Not One Thing**
   - *Question:* How do physical, administrative, and cultural regions differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Choose one place and attribute each boundary to its source. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **World Geography: Two Maps Disagree**
   - *Question:* Which technical or political choices explain a geographic mismatch?
   - *Key Focus:* Check datum, projection, date, source, resolution, and contested claims. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
7. **World Geography: A New Geospatial Layer Tested**
   - *Question:* Does one satellite or crowdsourced layer improve a place description?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare accuracy, update cycle, bias, licensing, and field verification. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
8. **World Geography: A Place-Name Decision**
   - *Question:* How did one named authority or community change a geographic label?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated records, language, objections, adoption, and continued local use. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
9. **World Geography: A Boundary or Landscape Change**
   - *Question:* What changed on maps after one documented event?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate legal change, physical change, naming, and map updates. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
10. **World Geography: How Coordinates Locate a Place**
   - *Question:* What do latitude, longitude, datum, and precision tell us?
   - *Key Focus:* Show one location across systems and quantify uncertainty. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **World Geography: A Place's Changing Footprint**
   - *Question:* Why did a mapped settlement or habitat expand or shrink?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare classification, land use, population, imagery, and date. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
12. **World Geography: One Place Across Maps**
   - *Question:* Which changes reflect the ground rather than cartographic method?
   - *Key Focus:* Match scale, projection, feature definitions, and sources. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
13. **World Geography: Scenarios for a Changing Place**
   - *Question:* Which plausible processes could alter its geography?
   - *Key Focus:* State land use, coast or river dynamics, governance, and uncertainty. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
14. **World Geography: A Map Is Neutral?**
   - *Question:* Which choices shape what one map emphasizes or omits?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare projection, classification, names, scale, and intended audience. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
15. **World Geography: A Place Through Its Maps**
   - *Question:* How do maps and local accounts record one landscape's change?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated sequence and attribute contested interpretations. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **World Geography: When a Map Misses a Place**
   - *Question:* Which important route, hazard, or use is absent from a map?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare field observation, community account, source date, and access. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.

   - *Key Focus:* Use seasonal data and avoid a year-round claim from one visit. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
19. **World Geography: Names Used by Local People**
   - *Question:* Which locally used names coexist with official labels?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute language, speakers, context, consent, and naming authority. Evidence base: dated authoritative maps and gazetteers, geodetic metadata, satellite imagery with field checks, land and boundary records, and consented local and Indigenous accounts.
