# Case-source note: FTC Twitter account-security data case

Checked 2026-10-24. The FTC [Twitter case docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2023062-twitter-inc-us-v) links the 2022 complaint and stipulated order. The FTC [2022-05-25 announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/05/ftc-charges-twitter-deceptively-using-account-security-data-sell-targeted-ads) states its allegation that Twitter requested phone numbers/email addresses for account security, including two-factor authentication and recovery, then used them for targeted advertising between 2014 and 2019 without appropriate disclosure. The [stipulated order](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2023062TwitterStipulatedOrder.pdf) includes notice and privacy/security obligations, including an MFA route not requiring a phone number.

Limit: FTC complaint claims must be attributed as allegations; the order resolves them and is not proof that MFA itself failed or that every affected user suffered account takeover. This historical Twitter case does not describe current X settings, user-level impact or a universal best authentication method. Do not give individualized account/security instructions from it.
