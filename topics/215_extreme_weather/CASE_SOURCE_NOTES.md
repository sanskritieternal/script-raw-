# Extreme-weather case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected dated primary reports support event-specific questions. They are not storm-chasing or emergency instructions.

| Case | Record inspected | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| El Reno, 2013 | [NWS Norman event survey](https://www.weather.gov/oun/events-20130531) reports a 2.6-mile maximum width and EF3 damage rating. | Width, mobile-radar winds above ground and damage rating measure different things. The survey does not justify the archive’s “monster” inference. |
| Hurricane Patricia, 2015 | [National Hurricane Center tropical-cyclone report](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/EP202015_Patricia.pdf) estimates peak intensity near 185 knots from aircraft and satellite evidence. | Best-track peak is an estimate with uncertainty, not a measured 215-mph surface gust everywhere or at landfall. PDF was accessible through the public search record. |
| Phoenix dust storm, 2011 | [NWS Phoenix event summary](https://www.weather.gov/psr/Jul52011) gives radar-estimated 5,000–6,000-foot peak height and observations from 5 July. | Radar top, ground visibility and storm footprint are distinct; estimate applies to this event only. |
| Texas freeze, 2021 | [FERC–NERC regional-entity inquiry](https://www.ferc.gov/media/february-2021-cold-weather-outages-texas-and-south-central-united-states-ferc-nerc-and) addresses bulk-power outages across Texas and south-central US. | A multicausal grid inquiry is not proof that a single weather feature alone caused every outage; full report detail was not independently reanalysed. |
| 1974 outbreak | [NWS Indianapolis retrospective](https://www.weather.gov/ind/apr31974update) records 148 tornadoes across 13 US states in roughly 24 hours. | Historical event counts and casualty estimates vary by source and reanalysis; Ontario is an additional jurisdiction, not a fourteenth US state. |

Fire-whirl and Gulf wet-bulb pitches route to existing process scopes; their video and sensation claims remain unverified.
