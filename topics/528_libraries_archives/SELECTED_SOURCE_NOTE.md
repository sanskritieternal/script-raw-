## Bounded premise

The October 2023 incident disrupted British Library online systems and access services. The March 2024 review said it believed secure copies of born-digital and digitized collections and descriptive metadata existed, while integrity validation and new infrastructure were needed before restoration. Interim discovery and manual/on-site routes were described separately from full service recovery. This distinguishes preservation of data from usable discovery, requesting and delivery.
