# Selected mission and sample-analysis source note — planetary science

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-345-041`.

- [NASA OSIRIS-REx mission chronology](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/osiris-rex/) records Bennu collection on October 20, 2020 and capsule return on September 24, 2023.
- [NASA, *Surprising Phosphate Finding in NASA's OSIRIS-REx Asteroid Sample*](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/surprising-phosphate-finding-in-nasas-osiris-rex-asteroid-sample/) (June 2024, reporting the team's preliminary *Meteoritics & Planetary Science* analysis, DOI 10.1111/maps.14227): magnesium-sodium phosphate in returned material surprised researchers because it had not appeared in spacecraft remote-sensing data.

Both mission pages were accessible. The second is an agency summary of early team analysis, not an independent full-sample survey. Non-detection in remote data does not prove the mineral absent across Bennu; detection in a returned sample does not establish life, present liquid water, or an ocean-world origin. The proposed parent-body story is an inference requiring further evidence.
