### Sample analysis

Subcategory `CATALOG-345-R04` · advanced.

#### BANK-345-039 — How do laboratory measurements of planetary samples complement remote spectra?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare mineralogy, isotopes, contamination, sample context and geographic representativeness without assuming one sample describes a whole planet.

<a id="bank-345-041"></a>
#### BANK-345-041 — Why did OSIRIS-REx laboratory analysis find a Bennu phosphate signature absent from spacecraft remote measurements?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare 2020 collection, 2023 return and 2024 early sample analysis with remote spectra; describe mineral-detection limits and sampling context without claiming life, liquid water on present Bennu or a confirmed ocean-world parent.
