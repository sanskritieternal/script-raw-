# Selected source and boundary note — 437 Nuclear Engineering

Reviewed 2026-10-24. Proposed **BANK-437-043** traces U.S. NRC docket **05200025** for Vogtle Electric Generating Plant Unit 3, Georgia.

## Primary regulator timeline

- U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, [Vogtle Unit 3 facility page](https://www.nrc.gov/facilities-safety/new-reactors/large-light-water-reactors/combined-license-holders-for-new-reactors/vogtle-electric-generating-plant-unit-3), lists the combined license issued **February 10, 2012** and the **10 CFR 52.103(g)** finding dated **August 3, 2022**. It identifies an AP1000 pressurized-water reactor, licensed thermal power 3,400 MWt and the operating company. Thermal power is not net electrical output.
- NRC, [2022 fuel-loading and operation authorization](https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/news-releases/2022/nrc-authorizes-vogtle-unit-3-fuel-loading-and-operation), describes the regulatory finding and transition toward operation. An [NRC 2024 regulatory summary](https://adamswebsearch2.nrc.gov/webSearch2/main.jsp?AccessionNumber=ML23325A202) records that the operator publicly announced commercial operation on **July 31, 2023** after startup testing. These are different milestones, and the last is explicitly an operator announcement relayed in an NRC record.

## Limits, safety and overlap

A combined license and later finding document compliance with specified regulatory steps; neither guarantees zero risk or substitutes for ongoing NRC inspection and event records. The unit's commercial-operation date does not prove output, cost, schedule merit, safety performance or effectiveness of any particular passive feature.
