## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Nuclear Engineering across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Nuclear Engineering Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Nuclear Engineering.
- **Tier 2: Nuclear Engineering Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Nuclear Engineering.
- **Tier 3: Nuclear Engineering Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Nuclear Engineering.
- **Tier 4: Nuclear Engineering Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Nuclear Engineering.

   - *Key Focus:* Specify reactor design, site, and period. Evidence base: NRC licensing dockets and inspection findings, plant performance indicators, EIA net generation, environmental reviews, public incident records, and operator filings.

4. **Nuclear Engineering: Fission to Grid Power**
