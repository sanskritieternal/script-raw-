# Selected primary-source checks - Island travel
Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed questions only. Access rules, fares, service schedules and storm conditions must be rechecked for any actual journey.

- BANK-226-038: UK National Audit Office progress report on St Helena Airport, https://www.nao.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Realising-the-benefits-of-St-Helena-Airport-a-progress-update.pdf . Wind shear and turbulence delayed scheduled operation after 2016 certification; regular flights began in October 2017. St Helena Government records the RMS ship final 2018 voyage, https://www.sainthelena.gov.sh/rms-st-helena-docks-for-final-time-in-cape-town/ . This describes a 2016-18 transition, not present flight reliability, current freight or resident experience. UK audit and government records have institutional perspectives.
- BANK-226-039: Galapagos National Park arrival instructions, https://galapagos.gob.ec/informacion-de-interes-para-la-llegada-a-las-islas/ , and visit rules, https://galapagos.gob.ec/reglas-de-visita/ . They distinguish transit control, baggage biosecurity and protected-area access; prices and rules are dated and must be rechecked. Administrative procedure alone does not prove ecological effectiveness. Spanish source language should be preserved when translating.
- BANK-226-040: Lakshadweep Administration entry-permit page, https://lakshadweep.gov.in/tourism/entry-permit/ , and current official e-Permit FAQ, https://lak.e-permit.utl.gov.in/faq . The earlier portal, https://epermit.utl.gov.in/ , states availability only until 15 October 2026. This is a current portal transition, so verify the working destination, category, islands and boarding rules directly before publication or travel. No legal or entry guarantee.
