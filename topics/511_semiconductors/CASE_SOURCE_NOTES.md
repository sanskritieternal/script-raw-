# Case-source note: NIST silicon wafer resistivity SRMs

Checked 2026-10-24. NIST's [Special Publication 260-131, 2006 edition](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication260-131e2006.pdf) documents certification of 100 mm silicon-wafer Standard Reference Materials 2541–2547 for sheet resistance and resistivity, including the dual-configuration four-point-probe procedure and uncertainty analysis. The report says these replaced earlier 50.8 mm SRM sets 1521–1523 and describes improved compatibility and characterization in that reference-material context. NIST's [SRM definitions](https://www.nist.gov/srm/srm-definitions) describe a certified reference material and its certificate's values and uncertainties.

Limits: The 2006 report establishes the certification method and intended metrology role of that SRM family. It does not demonstrate a specific fabrication yield improvement, certify all silicon wafers, or establish present product availability or current fab practice. The detailed PDF was accessible through NIST; individual current certificates, inventory and later revisions were not checked. Avoid turning calibration discussion into process instructions.

Overlap: `BANK-511-042` covers general fabrication metrology and `BANK-511-044` yield localization. This case is the named reference-material certification and uncertainty trail.
