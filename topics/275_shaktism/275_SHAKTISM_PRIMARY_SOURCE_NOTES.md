These checks establish candidate premises, not production verification. New proposals retain `status: proposed` and empty `verified_sources`.

## BANK-275-046: Nepal Devi Mahatmya manuscript

- British Library Archives and Manuscripts Catalogue, [EAP1023/8/9: Devi Mahatmya](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003665789), inspected 2026-10-24. The record describes a Sanskrit Devanagari paper witness, 42 folios, catalogued to the 18th century, originating in Nepal with the original in Bhaktapur in a personal collection; it provides [digital copies](https://eap.bl.uk/archive-file/EAP1023-8-9).
- Limit: the catalogue's approximate early date concerns composition of the larger text, while 1700-1799 concerns this physical manuscript. Neither date is independently established here. Images were not collated and no verse was translated in this check. Do not imply that the manuscript is an autograph, earliest witness or complete representation of Shakta practice. Textual narrative belongs to BANK-275-037 and Puranic compilation questions to BANK-272.

## BANK-275-047: Kolkata Durga Puja workshop labor

- UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, [Durga Puja in Kolkata](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/durga-puja-in-kolkata-00703?RL=00703), inscribed 2021; institution-indexed text inspected 2026-10-24. It describes Kolkata's annual public observance and workshops sculpting clay Durga images before the festival. The [2021 nomination evaluation](https://ich.unesco.org/en/8b-representative-list-01191) lists nomination and community-consent materials. UNESCO's [2022 Kolkata visit](https://ich.unesco.org/en/news/unesco-visits-india-ahead-of-the-durga-puja-in-kolkata-13423) also identifies preparatory workshops.
- Limit: direct listing fetch returned a JavaScript/captcha challenge; institution-indexed text and accessible related pages were used. UNESCO's broad public-art description is a nomination perspective, not a census of workshops, gendered work, wages, materials, environmental effects or access today. Seek workshop and community accounts with consent. Keep this Kolkata case distinct from other Durga Pujas, from general domestic/public ritual comparison in BANK-275-038, and from Vaishnava Manipur Sankirtana.
