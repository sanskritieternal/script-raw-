## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Shaktism across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Shaktism Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Shaktism.
- **Tier 2: Shaktism Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Shaktism.
- **Tier 3: Shaktism Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Shaktism.
- **Tier 4: Shaktism Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Shaktism.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Shaktism: A Śākta Tradition's Early Records**
   - *Question:* Which dated sources document one goddess-centered community or site?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inscriptions, texts, images, and later origin stories. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
2. **Shaktism: A Goddess Festival in Local Context**
   - *Question:* How does one community explain a celebrated observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify place and lineage; distinguish public practice from generalized claims. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
3. **Shaktism: Women and Other Stewards of a Tradition**
   - *Question:* Whose labor or authority is overlooked in one Śākta history?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and institutional evidence without assumptions from gender alone. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
4. **Shaktism: Many Ways of Honoring Devī**
   - *Question:* How do two Śākta communities differ in theology, texts, and practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Present each tradition in its own terms. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
5. **Shaktism: Power and the Divine Feminine**
   - *Question:* How do named texts or teachers explain śakti differently?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare passages and historical contexts, avoiding a single doctrine. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Shaktism: A Goddess Site's Contested History**
   - *Question:* Which evidence bears on one disputed site identification or date?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate archaeology, inscription, later narrative, and living belief. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
7. **Shaktism: Documenting a Living Śākta Tradition**
   - *Question:* Can an archive preserve one public repertoire while honoring restrictions?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask custodians about consent, access, and context. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
8. **Shaktism: A Śākta Teacher or Patron**
   - *Question:* What can dated records establish about one named contributor?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate source-backed acts from devotional biography. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
9. **Shaktism: A Shrine or Community Changes**
   - *Question:* How did one dated event affect Śākta worship or access?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare records and affected practitioner perspectives. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
10. **Shaktism: A Public Goddess Observance**
   - *Question:* What do participants say occurs and why in one named observance?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe local stages without revealing restricted practice or instructing worship. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Shaktism: Patronage of a Goddess Site**
   - *Question:* How did resources and custodianship change at one site?
   - *Key Focus:* Use grants, accounts, buildings, and local testimony. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
12. **Shaktism: One Śākta Festival Across Generations**
   - *Question:* Which elements changed and which remain meaningful to participants?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated accounts with consented voices. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
13. **Shaktism: Conserving a Living Shrine**
   - *Question:* What would one preservation plan protect or limit?
   - *Key Focus:* Consider material condition, ritual use, access, and custodians' priorities. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
14. **Shaktism: A Single Goddess Tradition?**
   - *Question:* Does a broad claim fit different Śākta texts and communities?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare named sources without reducing diversity to a stereotype. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
15. **Shaktism: A Goddess Narrative Through Sources**
   - *Question:* How did one story move across texts, images, and performance?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace versions, dates, and local meaning. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Shaktism: A Devī Passage in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one passage say in its named source and recension?
   - *Key Focus:* Cite original, edition, translation, and narrative setting. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
17. **Shaktism: Two Readings of Devī**
   - *Question:* How do two named Śākta lineages interpret one divine figure or concept?
   - *Key Focus:* Use each community's own sources and voices. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
18. **Shaktism: Devī Devotion in One Community**
   - *Question:* How do participants describe their everyday or seasonal practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consent and specify place, date, and community. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
19. **Shaktism: A Locally Documented Rite**
   - *Question:* Which public stages are described by practitioners at one site?
   - *Key Focus:* Respect restricted elements and distinguish text from practice. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
20. **Shaktism: A Goddess Epithet in Translation**
   - *Question:* How does rendering one name or term shift meaning?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original-language context and practitioner explanations. Evidence base: specified Śākta texts and editions, dated inscriptions and object records, site archives, and consented practitioner and custodian accounts.
