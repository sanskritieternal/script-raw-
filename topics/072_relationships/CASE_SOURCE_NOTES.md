# Selected relationship case source checks — 24 October 2026

These checks bound three proposed episodes. They do not certify the other archived claims or provide an assessment of an individual relationship. Source details and transfer limits must be checked again during production.

## BANK-072-049 — Gottman prediction claims

- Original institution account: [University of Washington, 2000, oral-history follow-up](https://www.washington.edu/news/2000/03/27/uw-researchers-can-predict-newlywed-divorce-marital-stability-with-87-percent-accuracy/). The reported 87% classification came from a 95-newlywed-couple follow-up; the account distinguishes an earlier 94% result for 56 couples already married several years with a child. The model was an oral-history interview coding system, not a simple five-positive-to-one-negative interaction rule.
- Original investigators' [14-year marital-dissolution article](https://www.johngottman.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/Timing-of-Divorce-Predicting-When-a-Couple-Will-Divorce-Over-a-14-Year-Period.pdf) analyzes patterns over a defined cohort and time frame. It does not establish the archive's universal single predictor.
- **Editorial bound:** State cohort, measure, outcome and follow-up beside each percentage. Do not use either number or a 5:1 slogan as a personal prediction.

## BANK-072-050 — anxious and avoidant pairings

- Original [longitudinal study of 354 heterosexual dating couples](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8169762/) reported nonrandom attachment-style pairings and relationships between style and later stability. Its sample and categorical measures cannot show that any two styles always attract or explain why an identifiable person acts as they do.
- A later original [dyadic study of 1,036 established couples over 20 months](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40272431/) examined anxiety and avoidance as dimensions and observed little partner codevelopment. This is a different question and sample from who initially chooses whom.
- **Editorial bound:** Present pairing findings as sample specific and distinguish pairing, satisfaction and change over time. Do not use an online label to diagnose a partner or rationalize harm.

## BANK-072-051 — five love languages

- Original [2022 study of 100 heterosexual couples](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9216579/) measured reported affection preferences, behavior and relationship outcomes and found a matching association in that sample. Its self-report, volunteer design cannot prove that everyone has one fixed primary language or that matching causes improvement.
- Original [2018 dyadic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37431256/) examined partners' understanding of affection preferences and reported behavior. It tests a related but different measurement of responsiveness, so results should not be collapsed into a single yes/no verdict on the five-category model.
- **Editorial bound:** Compare operational definitions and sampling before claiming general effectiveness. An individual's desired forms of affection should be discussed directly and consensually, not inferred from a quiz.
