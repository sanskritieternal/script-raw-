# Polar case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary records inspected for proposed questions; these do not certify scripts, current access, or future projections.

| Case | Primary record | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Thwaites | [International Thwaites Glacier Collaboration, 22 May 2024](https://thwaitesglacier.org/news/warm-seawater-speeding-melting-thwaites-glacier) reports satellite-radar evidence of tidal seawater intrusion beneath grounded ice. | This is an observed process, not proof of imminent glacier collapse or a specified sea-level timeline. |
| Endurance wreck | [Falklands Maritime Heritage Trust, 9 March 2022](https://endurance22.org/endurance-is-found) announces finding the ship in the Weddell Sea; [British Antarctic Survey treaty account](https://www.bas.ac.uk/news/reaffirmed-international-commitment-to-antarctic-treaty/) records its historic-site status. | Discovery, imagery, shipwreck protection and Shackleton's 1915 survival are different topics. Do not repeat the archived footage quality/depth/superlative without checking expedition records. |
| Yamal craters | [Morgado et al., *Geophysical Research Letters* (2024)](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2024GL108987) proposes an osmotic pumping mechanism for gas-emission craters. | A modeled mechanism is not a uniquely established cause of every crater; distinguish crater-scale gas release from pan-Arctic methane budgets. |
| Kolyma nematode | [Shatilovich et al., *PLOS Genetics* (2023)](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pgen.1010798) reports revived *Panagrolaimus kolymaensis* and dating evidence, with a linked correction. | Microscopic nematodes are not “giant worms”; an ancient animal study does not establish imminent pathogen release. Review dating, sampling and the correction before scripting. |
| Tourism | [IAATO, 2023–24 season report](https://iaato.org/system/files/2025-01/ATCM46_ip102_rev1_e_IAATO-Overview-of-Antarctic-Vessel-Tourism-The-2023-24-Season-and-Preliminary-Estimates-for-2024-25.pdf) documents vessel tourism for a defined season. | Operator-reported visitor counts cannot establish a generic superyacht price or every environmental impact. |

Semantic review: `BANK-153-015` and `BANK-153-036` cover Thwaites and ice-shelf processes; `BANK-153-017` covers the 1915 Endurance survival, while the new case covers the 2022 archaeological discovery. `BANK-153-020` and `BANK-153-042` cover regional permafrost-carbon mechanisms, while the new Yamal case examines one crater mechanism. `BANK-153-024` concerns speculative ancient-pathogen claims, while the nematode case concerns a published non-pathogen organism.
