## BANK-371-045: WGA theatrical screen credits

- The [WGA Screen Credits Manual](https://www.wga.org/contracts/credits/manuals/screen-credits-manual) defines “Story by,” “Screenplay by,” and “Written by,” and sets out how participating writers' contributions are considered against the final shooting script. The [theatrical credits procedures guide](https://www.wga.org/contracts/credits/manuals/theatrical-credits-procedures) explains notices, protests and arbitration in Guild-covered theatrical projects.
- A writer's name in a finished film alone cannot establish who wrote each draft or why a particular credit was awarded. The manual is a rule source, not a case-specific arbitration record; verify the applicable edition, production's Guild coverage and literary materials before any named-film claim.
- This proposed case covers the archived “writers behind the credit” and “disputed writing credit” pitches at the procedural level. It is separate from 370's actor audition agreement and from script-craft questions already active in this bank.
