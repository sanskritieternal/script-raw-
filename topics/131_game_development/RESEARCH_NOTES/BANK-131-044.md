# BANK-131-044: Nanite geometry and fallback limits

Research checked: 2026-10-24 against Epic's Unreal Engine 5.8 documentation. Status: bounded premise research, not a script or idea approval.

Epic's [Nanite overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/nanite-in-unreal-engine) describes virtualized geometry that selects visible detail. Its [technical details](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/nanite-technical-details) describe fallback mesh settings and uses such as collision and ray tracing. The [versioned feature reference](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/nanite-virtualized-geometry-in-unreal-engine) lists unsupported combinations including stereo rendering for VR in the checked documentation. These are vendor implementation statements for a specific engine version, not benchmarks on a named game.

**Claim boundary:** Nanite changes how suitable geometry is stored and drawn; it does not remove memory, bandwidth, platform, collision, material, or frame-time budgets. Do not claim it renders billions of triangles in every scene or that a source sculpt needs no preparation. Check the engine version, rendering path, hardware and mesh type before scripting a demonstration. Feature support can change with updates.

**Semantic home:** Niche 131 owns engine geometry and fallback behavior. Niche 128 owns VR/AR user experience and device constraints; niche 127 owns film VFX rendering and compositing. A VR support limitation is a cross-bank boundary, not a second VR episode.
