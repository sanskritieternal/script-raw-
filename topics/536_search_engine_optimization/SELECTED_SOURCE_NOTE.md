## Bounded premise

Google announced that its small remaining desktop-crawled set would move to smartphone crawling after 5 July 2024. The announcement says pages entirely inaccessible on a mobile device would cease to be indexable by Google Search, while some desktop Googlebot activity could continue for other Search features. This is a change to one provider's crawling/indexing process, not an observed traffic outcome for a given publisher.

## Claim and access limits

Provider documentation explains intended system behavior; it does not verify any particular website's configuration, ranking, traffic or conversion change. Distinguish crawling, indexing, ranking and referrals. Do not interpret the 2023 ?complete? announcement as proof there were no exceptions before July 2024; Google explicitly described the remaining set. Current Googlebot documentation and exceptions can change, so version and date claims. No promise that changing a page will cause indexing or better ranking.
