## Scope and rights review

Existing 086 topics already cover iron stabilization, furniture joints/veneer/finish, historic-machine replacement, documentation, referral, staged-video disclosure and polishing loss. Repeated archival pitches route there. Neighboring 085 DIY and 087/088 mechanics and manufacturing were reviewed; this bank keeps conservation decisions, provenance, treatment history and handover as its primary purpose. For culturally significant or sacred objects, owner permission alone may be insufficient: identify relevant custodians and access restrictions before examination, imagery or public demonstration. Surface labels, decals, trademarks, artist rights and archival images need separate publication clearance. Dangerous chemical, heat, electrical, abrasive, laser and engine-start claims are framed for trained assessment, with no procedural settings. Each production story requires a named object, condition record, authorized conservator input and a dated source trail.
