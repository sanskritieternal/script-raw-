## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Restoration across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Surface Decontamination, Rust Removal & Disassembly Forensics (Scratch / Beginner)** — Electrolysis rust removal vs. chemical chelators (Evapo-Rust), freeing seized bolts (heat cycling, penetrating fluid), safe screw extraction, and ultrasonic cleaning baths.
- **Tier 2: Mechanical Machining, Surface Reconditioning & Finishing (Intermediate)** — Metal bluing (hot caustic vs. cold chemical), wire wheel and sandblasting media (walnut shell vs. glass bead), hand scraping cast iron ways, and restoring antique motor commutators.
- **Tier 3: Museum Conservation Ethics vs. Functional Rebuilding (Upper Intermediate)** — The Venice Charter of conservation, reversibility of treatments, stabilizing bronze disease (BTA), preserving original factory decals, and conservation-grade microcrystalline waxes.
- **Tier 4: Historic Vehicle, Watch & Fine Mechanical Master Restorations (Advanced / Expert)** — Turning custom watch balance staffs on a watchmaker's lathe, rebuilding antique carburetors and magneto ignitions, lead body solder filling, and hand-rubbed nitrocellulose lacquer finishing.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Surface Decontamination & Rust Removal
1. **The Science of Rust: How Electrolysis and Chemical Chelation Rescue Seized 100-Year-Old Iron**
   - *Question:* How does submerged direct current in a washing soda bath strip heavy iron oxide without removing a single microscopic grain of virgin steel?
   - *Key Focus:* Redox reactions in electrolysis, iron(III) oxide converting to harmless black magnetite, sacrificial rebar vs. graphite anodes, pH-neutral selective chelation molecules (Evapo-Rust), and flash rust prevention.
2. **Freeing Seized Bolts: Thermal Expansion, Induction Heaters, and Capillary Penetrants**
   - *Question:* Why does heating a rusted nut cherry-red with an induction heater break the rust bond when wrench leverage alone snaps the bolt head clean off?
   - *Key Focus:* Differential thermal expansion fracturing brittle iron oxide bridges, capillary draw of penetrating oil into microscopic threads, wax melting trick, manual impact drivers, and left-hand drill extractors.
3. **Ultrasonic Cleaning: How Cavitation Bubbles Blast Grease Out of Hidden Carburetor Passages**
   - *Question:* How can high-frequency 40 kHz sound waves create microscopic vacuum bubbles that implode with thousands of PSI of localized force to clean clogged metal pores?
   - *Key Focus:* Piezoelectric ultrasonic transducers, acoustic cavitation shockwaves, aqueous cleaning solvent temperatures (50–60°C), cleaning brass watch parts, and avoiding hydrogen embrittlement.
4. **Abrasive Blasting Media: Sand vs. Glass Bead vs. Walnut Shell vs. Vapor Honing**
   - *Question:* Why will dry sandblasting warp thin automotive sheet metal and ruin vintage motorcycle engine tolerances, and why is wet vapor honing the gold standard for aluminum?
   - *Key Focus:* Media Mohs hardness (silica vs. glass vs. organic crushed walnut shell), surface peening vs. cutting, wet slurry slurry vapor blasting dampening impact, and protecting machined bearing surfaces.
### Tier 2: Mechanical Machining & Reconditioning
5. **Metal Bluing and Blackening: Hot Caustic Black Oxide vs. Cold Acid Chemistry**
   - *Question:* How do you create that deep, iridescent black-blue protective finish on restored antique tools and firearms that prevents corrosion for decades?
   - *Key Focus:* Traditional hot caustic nitrate salts bath at 140°C converting surface iron to Fe3O4 black oxide, historical slow-rust bluing with steam cabinets and carding wheels, and selenium dioxide cold bluing.
6. **Hand Scraping Machine Ways: The Ancient Lost Art of 0.0001-Inch Precision**
   - *Question:* Why are the world's most accurate machine tool beds still hand-scraped with carbide chisels instead of ground on computerized grinding machines?
   - *Key Focus:* Cast iron wear patterns, granite surface plate reference standards, spotting with Prussian Blue, scraping bearing points per square inch (PPI), and oil-retention flaking patterns.
7. **Restoring Antique Electric Motors: Commutators, Brushes, and Rewinding Armatures**
   - *Question:* How do you bring a smoking 1920s Century induction motor back to silent operation, and why must you undercut the mica insulation between copper commutator bars?
   - *Key Focus:* Turning scored copper commutators on a lathe, mica undercutting depth, carbon brush seating and spring tension, inspecting insulation resistance with a Megger tester, and insulating varnish bake cycles.
8. **Wood Furniture Restoration: Stripping French Polish, Hiding Scratches, and Veneer Patching**
   - *Question:* Why does using denatured alcohol dissolve 150-year-old shellac without sanding away the precious antique wood patina beneath it?
   - *Key Focus:* Identifying finishes (lacquer vs. shellac vs. varnish), re-amalgamating crazed shellac with a Fad pad and raw linseed oil, steam-swelling wood dents, and cutting feathered veneer patches.
9. **Leather Rejuvenation: The Chemistry of Dry Rot, Lanolin, and Stitching Restoration**
   - *Question:* Can stiff, cracked 100-year-old military holster leather actually be softened and revived, or has acid rot permanently severed its collagen proteins?
   - *Key Focus:* Leather tanning chemistry (vegetable vs. chrome tan), collagen fiber degradation, pH-neutral saddle soap cleaning, deep penetration with pure neatsfoot oil and lanolin, and grooving for hand saddle-stitching.
10. **Restoring Antique Gauges and Clocks: Pinion Polishing and Dial Re-Silvering**
   - *Question:* How does a brass Bourdon tube pressure gauge work, and how do you chemically re-silver a tarnished clock face using silver chloride powder and cream of tartar?
   - *Key Focus:* Bourdon tube hairspring calibration, repointing pivot arbors on a jeweler's lathe, friction silvering pastes on brass, wax-filling engraved numerals, and convex glass replacement.
### Tier 3: Conservation Ethics vs. Rebuilding
11. **The Venice Charter: Why Museums Never Make Antiques Look Brand New**
   - *Question:* Why do museum conservators consider making an artifact look 'mint and shiny' an act of historical vandalism that destroys historical truth?
   - *Key Focus:* The 1964 International Charter for the Conservation and Restoration of Monuments and Sites, the principle of reversibility (any repair must be undoable 100 years later), and distinguishing genuine age from damage.
12. **Bronze Disease: The Green Cancer Eating Ancient Relics and How to Cure It**
   - *Question:* How can a powdery bright-green spot on an ancient Roman coin or bronze sword eat through solid metal in weeks if exposed to atmospheric moisture?
   - *Key Focus:* Cuprous chloride reacting with oxygen and water to produce cyclical hydrochloric acid, distinguishing benign noble patina (malachite) from destructive bronze disease, BTA (Benzotriazole) inhibitors, and dry box storage.
13. **Saving Historic Decals: Preserving Waterslide Graphics and Silk-Screened Brand Marks**
   - *Question:* When repainting a vintage sewing machine or cast iron lathe, how do you protect the fragile 100-year-old gold leaf decals from paint stripper?
   - *Key Focus:* Liquid latex masking, micro-sol and micro-set setting solutions, scanning and digitally vectorizing destroyed graphics, custom silk-screening gold metallic ink, and buried clear-coat techniques.
14. **Renaissance Wax: The British Museum's Secret Weapon Against Air and Moisture**
   - *Question:* Why does a tiny pea-sized dab of synthetic microcrystalline wax protect swords, coins, and vintage cars better than any automotive carnauba wax or oil?
   - *Key Focus:* British Museum research in the 1950s, petroleum-derived microcrystalline structure lacking natural acids that oxidize over time, zero optical distortion, fingerprint resistance, and archival reversibility.
15. **Cast Iron Repair: Why Welding Cracks Fails and Cold Stitching Saves Antique Blocks**
   - *Question:* Why does hitting cracked cast iron with an ordinary stick welder cause it to crack right next to the weld when it cools, and what is mechanical metal stitching?
   - *Key Focus:* High carbon content (2–4%) causing brittle martensite phase changes upon fast cooling, nickel rod preheating up to 600°F, and cold mechanical metal stitching (Lock-N-Stitch drilling overlapping threaded keys).
### Tier 4: Master Watch & Vehicle Restoration
16. **Restoring Antique Watch Movements: Pivot Polishing and Balance Staff Turning**
   - *Question:* How does a master watchmaker turn a balance staff thinner than a human hair on a precision lathe to resurrect a pocket watch dead for 80 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Watchmaker's Lorch/Boley lathe, conical pivot burnishing with carbide laps, ruby cap jewel cleaning, poise testing on calipers, hairspring truing with brass tweezers, and epilame anti-spreading fluid.
17. **Vintage Carburetor Rebuilding: Venturi Physics, Float Levels, and Ultrasonic Jet Tuning**
   - *Question:* Why will an engine run rich and foul its spark plugs if your brass carburetor float sits just 1/16th of an inch too high in the fuel bowl?
   - *Key Focus:* Bernoulli's principle in carburetor venturis, ethanol fuel eating vintage rubber diaphragms, unblocking microscopic idle feed restrictors, rebuilding throttle shaft bushings to prevent vacuum leaks, and manometer balancing.
18. **Lead Body Solder vs. Bondo: The Lost Art of Traditional Automotive Coachwork**
   - *Question:* Why did coachbuilders at Aston Martin and Rolls-Royce use wooden paddles dipped in beef tallow to spread molten lead over body seams instead of plastic body filler?
   - *Key Focus:* 70/30 lead-tin plastic state between 361°F and 489°F, tinning acid flux, hardwood paddle sculpting, flexible vixen files, zero shrinkage over 50 years, and modern safety precautions against lead dust.
19. **Hand-Rubbed Nitrocellulose Lacquer: Achieving a Deep Mirror Finish on Classic Guitars and Cars**
   - *Question:* Why does a 1959 Gibson Les Paul or vintage Corvette finish possess an organic depth that modern thick polyurethane clear coats can never duplicate?
   - *Key Focus:* Cotton linter cellulose chemistry, evaporative curing allowing subsequent coats to melt into previous layers, wet-sanding from 800 to 3000 grit, rotary buffing with Tripoli and swirl remover, and thermal weather checking.
20. **The Ultimate Barn Find Restoration: Complete Resurrection Blueprint from Seized to Showroom**
   - *Question:* What is the non-negotiable step-by-step triage protocol when pulling a vehicle or machine that has sat abandoned in a dusty barn for 50 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Photographic disassembly logging, fluid sampling, cylinder borescope inspection, oil priming the oil pump before cranking, static electrical testing, and the sacred ritual of the first start-up.
