# Selected museum and heritage source checks — 24 October 2026

These checks support three bounded case proposals. Museum accounts document their own collections, decisions and policies but do not prove all claims about ownership, market value, culpability or visitor response. Other archived premises need object-level or site-level research.

## BANK-096-055 — Mary Rose conservation

- The [Mary Rose museum’s hull-conservation account](https://maryrose.org/discover/conservation/the-ship/) describes stages from the 1982 raising through water spray, polyethylene-glycol (PEG) treatment, controlled drying and present preventive care. Its [separate artefact-conservation account](https://maryrose.org/discover/conservation/artefacts/) explains that waterlogged wood and other materials need different treatment decisions.
- **Editorial bound:** Date each stage and distinguish the ship’s hull from individual objects. PEG use is a documented choice for this waterlogged archaeological wood, not a universal treatment recipe for antiques or wet wood.

## BANK-096-064 — image access

- The [Met’s 2017 Open Access announcement](https://www.metmuseum.org/ja/press-releases/open-access-2017-news) reported more than 375,000 public-domain artwork images released under CC0 at launch. The museum’s [current image and data policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/it/policies/image-resources) explains that copyright, ownership, donor restrictions, privacy or lack of suitable photography can limit an item’s availability.
- **Editorial bound:** Date counts and verify the rights statement on the specific object record before reuse. The archive’s undifferentiated “millions of high-res masterpieces for free” is not an item-level license or a stable cross-museum total.

## BANK-096-065 — British Museum missing items

- The British Museum’s [2023–24 annual report](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2024-07/British_Museum_Annual_Report_Accounts_2023-24.pdf) states that approximately 2,000 items were found missing, stolen **or damaged**, and describes the recovery program; it reports more than 600 returned by that report date. Its [September 2023 recovery update](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2023-09/Update_on_the_progress_of_the_recovery_programme.pdf) is an earlier snapshot.
- **Editorial bound:** Distinguish missing, stolen and damaged categories, dates and subsequently updated counts. The institutional report does not establish that 2,000 gems were sold on eBay, a $50 unit price or any individual’s guilt. Check police and court records before naming a suspect.
