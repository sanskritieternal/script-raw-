# Selected metro case: New York City Hall station

## BANK-425-046 — passenger station and operating loop

- The [New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission's 1979 designation](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/1096.pdf) documents the station's original loop-track design and later changes to the early IRT plan. The [New York Transit Museum](https://www.nytransitmuseum.org/old-city-hall/) says the first subway ride left City Hall in 1904, passenger station use ended in 1945, and the loop still turns 6-line trains.
- These sources establish design and broad operating dates; neither alone establishes a complete causal account of the 1945 closure, traffic levels or today's safe public access. Museum tour terms are changeable.
- Script gate: examine contemporary transit-board decisions, train-length/platform records and current operator information before explaining closure or advising access. Describe station closure separately from continuing loop operation.
