# Selected source check — Why? Channel

Checked 2026-10-24. These primary records support selected bounded premises, not all 63 active questions or a universal single-cause answer.

| Case | Primary source inspected | Bounded finding and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark night sky (BANK-090-053) | [NASA Goddard Olbers paradox explainer](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/websites/POLAR/cmb.physics.wisc.edu/tutorial/olbers.html) | The paradox depends on idealized assumptions about an infinite, static, eternal distribution of stars. A finite cosmic age and expansion affect observed light; “expanding edge of time” is not a literal observable boundary. |
| Ocean salt (BANK-090-054) | [NOAA National Ocean Service, ocean salinity](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/whysalty.html) | NOAA identifies land-rock weathering and seafloor openings among sources, with ions also removed by biological and geologic processes. Average salinity varies by location and time; it is not just endless accumulation. |
| Dream function (BANK-090-055) | [Original critical review of REM–memory consolidation hypothesis, PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8760621/) | The authors found evidence for a major REM-specific memory-consolidation role weak and contradictory at the time studied. This is a dated review of experiments, not proof that dreaming has no function. Current script requires a current systematic review and stage-specific primary studies. |
| Phantom traffic wave (BANK-090-066) | [Sugiyama et al., 2008 circular-track experiment](https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/93262/NewJPhys_10_033001.pdf) | A wave arose on a controlled closed course without a fixed road bottleneck. It does not establish the cause of any particular real-world traffic jam. |
| Night waking (BANK-090-069) | [Controlled human circadian study of cortisol awakening response](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9669756/) and [NIH NHLBI sleep/wake cycle](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/sleep-wake-cycle) | An experimentally measured circadian phase effect is not a diagnosis or proof that everyone who wakes at 3 a.m. has a cortisol spike. Sleep complaints have multiple causes and merit clinical context. |
| Doorway effect (BANK-090-072) | [Doorways do not always cause forgetting: multimodal experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7938580/) | Replication conditions and memory load matter. A doorway alone is not a universal reset of a person's working memory. |
| Mosquito preference (BANK-090-073) | [Experimental Aedes aegypti odor-receptor study archived by CDC](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/121445/cdc_121445_DS1.pdf) | Attraction differed under tested odor and receptor conditions. Species, experimental setting and human subjects matter; no fixed “400 chemical signatures” rule follows for all mosquitoes or people. |
