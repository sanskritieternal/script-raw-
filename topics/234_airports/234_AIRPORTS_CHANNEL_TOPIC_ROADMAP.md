## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Airports across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Airports Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Airports.
- **Tier 2: Airports Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Airports.
- **Tier 3: Airports Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Airports.
- **Tier 4: Airports Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Airports.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Airports: Why an Airport Was Built Here**
   - *Question:* Which land, route, and political decisions selected one site?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated plans, land records, and competing proposals. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
2. **Airports: A Model Airport Rechecked**
   - *Question:* Which service claims hold for a defined traveler?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare actual transfers, waits, access, costs, and disruptions. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
3. **Airports: Work Behind the Terminal**
   - *Question:* Which workers keep an airport operating beyond the flight crew?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace cleaning, baggage, maintenance, ground handling, and contractors. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
4. **Airports: The Complete Airport Journey**
   - *Question:* What must happen from ground arrival to boarding?
   - *Key Focus:* Map check-in, security, border control where applicable, gate, and assistance. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
5. **Airports: Capacity at the Bottleneck**
   - *Question:* Why can more gates fail to reduce delay?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare runway, airspace, terminal, staffing, and schedule constraints. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Airports: A Persistent Delay Pattern**
   - *Question:* Which records explain recurrent congestion?
   - *Key Focus:* Test weather, schedules, runway use, staffing, and data definitions. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
7. **Airports: A Passenger Technology Tested**
   - *Question:* Did a biometric or self-service system shorten the journey?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure errors, queues, consent, privacy, and accessible alternatives. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
8. **Airports: The Expansion Decision**
   - *Question:* Who shaped a consequential airport project?
   - *Key Focus:* Use board minutes, consultations, alternatives, and outcomes. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
9. **Airports: An Airport During Disruption**
   - *Question:* How did one event change passenger and worker flows?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated timeline from airport and authority records. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
10. **Airports: Airside and Landside Handoffs**
   - *Question:* Which organizations control each part of a journey?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace airport, airline, security, border, and ground transport responsibilities. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Airports: Why Passenger Numbers Changed**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured traffic shift?
   - *Key Focus:* Check routes, airline choices, catchment, counting, and shock periods. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
12. **Airports: A Terminal Across Two Eras**
   - *Question:* How did capacity, access, security, and labor change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable plans, passenger data, and dated rules. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
13. **Airports: An Airport's Conditional Expansion**
   - *Question:* Which assumptions determine a proposal's value?
   - *Key Focus:* State forecast, noise, land, emissions, finance, and alternatives. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
14. **Airports: The Fastest Airport Route?**
   - *Question:* Does a claimed shortcut improve door-to-door time?
   - *Key Focus:* Include ground access, queues, terminal distance, and delay risk. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
15. **Airports: One Passenger Through the System**
   - *Question:* What happened at every handoff on a documented journey?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented records and anonymize personal travel data. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Airports: Terminal Map Versus Actual Transfer**
   - *Question:* Which stairs, distances, queues, or closures are omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Check current maps and site-specific accessibility information. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
17. **Airports: Two Airports for One Destination**
   - *Question:* Which complete journey works for a defined traveler?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare fare, transfer, time, reliability, assistance, and total cost. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
18. **Airports: An Airport in Peak and Off-Peak Periods**
   - *Question:* How do queues, staffing, and routes change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated schedules and comparable passenger counts. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
19. **Airports: Neighbors of an Airport**
   - *Question:* How do nearby communities describe costs and benefits?
   - *Key Focus:* Seek varied accounts and data on jobs, access, noise, and land. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
20. **Airports: Weather and Airport Siting**
   - *Question:* Which wind, terrain, or water factors shaped operations?
   - *Key Focus:* Use meteorological, topographic, and planning records. Evidence base: dated airport plans and passenger data, airline and authority notices, accessibility information, environmental monitoring, and consented worker and resident accounts.
