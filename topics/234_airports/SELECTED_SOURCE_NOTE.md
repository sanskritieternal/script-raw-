# Selected primary-source checks - Airports
Checked 2026-10-24. These are proposed research leads, not live delay, route or access advice.

- BANK-234-040: San Francisco International Airport runway-constraint and operations page, https://www.flysfo.com/about/airport-operations/policies-regulations/runway-constraints , and FAA charted visual-flight procedure notice, https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/notices/media/05_25_17_NTAP.pdf . Weather and approach procedure affect published arrival rates. Airport/FAA descriptions are dated and do not predict an individual flight or assign all delays to fog.
- BANK-234-041: Heathrow 12 July 2022 passenger-cap letter, https://mediacentre.heathrow.com/pressrelease/detail/14051 , and 11 August 2022 update, https://mediacentre.heathrow.com/pressrelease/detail/14114 . Airport statements report handler/airline/airport capacity constraints and subsequent operational indicators; they are institutional accounts, not a controlled causal evaluation or evidence of a current cap.
- BANK-234-042: TSA FY2024-2028 capital plan, https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/tsa-capital-investment-plan-fy-2024-2028.pdf . It describes CAT-2 identity matching, pilot status and voluntary manual alternative. Agency project description does not establish queue reduction, unbiased performance across demographic groups or universal airport rollout. Check current privacy and participation terms separately.
- BANK-234-043: Hartsfield-Jackson airport manager statement of 20 December 2017, https://www.atl.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Press-Release-Power-Outage-Statement-Dec-20.pdf . It documents the outage, communications gaps and agency coordination from airport management's perspective. Do not infer every passenger experience or technical root cause from this statement alone.
- Overlap: BANK-110-011 owns whole-trip timing; BANK-110-055 airline hub scheduling; BANK-215 weather science; BANK-234-033 expansion distribution. The named cases here ask how airport-specific operational records explain passenger consequences.
