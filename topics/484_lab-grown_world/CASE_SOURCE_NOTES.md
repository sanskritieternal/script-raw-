# Case-source note: UPSIDE Foods cultured chicken consultation

Checked 2026-10-24. The U.S. FDA’s [2022-11-16 announcement](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-completes-first-pre-market-consultation-human-food-made-using-animal-cell-culture-technology) states that it had no further questions about UPSIDE Foods’ safety conclusion for its described chicken-cell material and process. FDA expressly says this voluntary consultation was not an approval; it examined cell lines/banks, manufacturing controls and inputs, and said USDA-FSIS inspection requirements still applied. FDA’s [consultation inventory](https://www.fda.gov/food/human-food-made-cultured-animal-cells/inventory-completed-pre-market-consultations-human-food-made-cultured-animal-cells) identifies file 000002 and links response/scientific memo. The [FDA–USDA formal agreement](https://www.fda.gov/food/human-food-made-cultured-animal-cells/formal-agreement-between-fda-and-usda-regarding-oversight-human-food-produced-using-animal-cell) documents divided federal oversight.

Limit: This dated federal consultation covers the specific submitted process/material, not all cultivated foods, commercial-scale output, affordability, taste, life-cycle emissions or absence of every risk. The agency’s no-further-questions response is not an FDA approval or proof of market entry; other requirements and later legal changes need separate checks. This is research scoping, not food-safety advice.
