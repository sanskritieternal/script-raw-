## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Aviation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Lift Physics, Wind Tunnels & The Pioneering Wright Era (Scratch / Beginner)** — Bernoulli principle vs Newton's third law of lift, Kitty Hawk 1903 wing-warping, pitot-static tube flight instruments, and the standard cross-check.
- **Tier 2: The Sound Barrier, The Jet Age & The Queen of the Skies (Intermediate)** — Chuck Yeager's Bell X-1 Mach 1 dive, the Boeing 707 barrel roll, Concorde's Mach 2 ogival delta wing, and the Boeing 747 high-bypass turbofan revolution.
- **Tier 3: Air Disaster Forensics, Windshear & CRM Cockpit Psychology (Upper Intermediate)** — Tenerife 1977 runway disaster, Aloha Airlines 243 fuselage skin peeling, microburst windshear detection, and Cockpit Resource Management (CRM).
- **Tier 4: Stealth Radar Cross-Sections, Hypersonic Scramjets & Autonomous Flight (Advanced / Expert)** — SR-71 Blackbird titanium thermal expansion, B-2 Spirit flying wing radar evasion, scramjet Mach 7 combustion physics, and autonomous eVTOL urban flight.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Lift Physics & The Pioneering Flight Era
1. **Tenerife 1977: The 583-Fatality Disaster That Rewrote Cockpit Psychology Forever**
   - *Question:* How did dense fog, simultaneous radio heterodyne squeals, and hierarchical cockpit deference cause two Boeing 747s to collide on a single runway?
   - *Key Focus:* Captain Jacob van Zanten's departure pressure, Dutch radio phraseology confusion ('We are now at takeoff'), Pan Am Flight 1736 missed exit, birth of modern CRM.
2. **How Wings Actually Generate Lift: Debunking the High School Bernoulli Myth**
   - *Question:* Why does the common 'equal transit time' school explanation fail completely to explain how stunt airplanes can fly upside down?
   - *Key Focus:* Newton's third law and wing downwash momentum, circulation and Kutta condition, angle of attack stall limits, boundary layer airflow.
3. **Kitty Hawk 1903: How Two Bicycle Mechanics Solved the 3-Axis Control Riddle**
   - *Question:* Why did previous aviation pioneers die in crashes while Orville and Wilbur Wright mastered flight using homebuilt bicycle wind tunnels and wing warping?
   - *Key Focus:* Pitch, roll, and yaw 3-axis flight control patent, wind tunnel lift tables correcting Lilienthal's errors, Charles Taylor's custom aluminum engine block.
4. **The Pitot Tube and the Six Pack: How Analog Cockpit Instruments Read the Sky**
   - *Question:* How do ram air pressure and miniature spinning gyroscopes tell a pilot their airspeed, altitude, and artificial horizon in pitch-black cloud cover?
   - *Key Focus:* Pitot-static tube pressure differentials, aneroid wafer altitude contraction, rigidity in space and precession gyroscopic laws, instrument cross-check scan.
5. **Breaking the Sound Barrier: Chuck Yeager, Glamorous Glennis, and the Mach 1 Shockwave**
   - *Question:* Why did wartime aircraft disintegrate when approaching 700 mph, and how did a rocket-powered bullet with an all-moving tailplane conquer compressibility?
   - *Key Focus:* Transonic shockwave formation and shock stall, Yeager flying with broken ribs using a broom handle, all-flying horizontal stabilizer invention.
### Tier 2: The Sound Barrier, Jet Age & Jumbo Era
6. **The Boeing 707: The Jet Airliner That Shrank the Planet and Did a Barrel Roll**
   - *Question:* How did test pilot Tex Johnston shock airline executives by barrel-rolling a 100-ton jet prototype over Seattle's Lake Washington to sell the Jet Age?
   - *Key Focus:* Dash 80 1-G positive maneuver, Pratt & Whitney JT3C turbojets, swept-wing 35-degree cruise efficiency, replacing piston propellers overnight.
7. **The Boeing 747: The Queen of the Skies and the Billion-Dollar Gamble That Saved Boeing**
   - *Question:* How did lead engineer Joe Sutter design the world's first wide-body jumbo jet in just 29 months while Boeing pushed itself to the brink of bankruptcy?
   - *Key Focus:* High-bypass turbofan engines, constructing the world's largest building by volume in Everett, nose cargo door design, democratizing global long-haul travel.
8. **Concorde: The 1,350-MPH Engineering Triumph That Arrived 30 Years Too Early**
   - *Question:* How did British and French engineers pump fuel between trim tanks in flight to compensate for supersonic center-of-lift migration at Mach 2?
   - *Key Focus:* Ogival delta wing aerodynamics, droop snoot cockpit visibility visor, Rolls-Royce Olympus 593 reheat afterburners, sonic boom ban and fuel economics.
9. **The de Havilland Comet: Square Windows and the Deadly Lessons of Metal Fatigue**
   - *Question:* Why did the world's first commercial jetliner suddenly tear apart in mid-air in 1954, and how did water tank testing reveal stress concentrations in window corners?
   - *Key Focus:* Cabin pressurization cycling stress, water submersion fatigue rig tests at Farnborough, why all airplane windows are oval/round today.
10. **The Tupolev Tu-144 'Concordski': The Stolen Blueprints and the Paris Air Show Crash**
   - *Question:* Did Soviet intelligence steal Concorde plans to build the world's first supersonic passenger jet, and why did it disintegrate in front of 250,000 spectators in Paris?
   - *Key Focus:* KGB operation Brunnhilde espionage, retractable front canards, 1973 Paris Air Show crash dodging a French Mirage jet, unreliability as a mail carrier.
### Tier 3: Air Disaster Forensics & Safety Systems
11. **Aloha Airlines Flight 243: The Convertible Boeing 737 at 24,000 Feet**
   - *Question:* How did Captain Robert Schornstheimer land a Boeing 737 with an 18-foot section of the cabin roof ripped clean off over the Pacific Ocean?
   - *Key Focus:* Multi-site fatigue damage (MSD) in lap joints, salty humid Hawaiian coastal corrosion, flight attendant Clarabelle Lansing tragedy, miraculous emergency touchdown at Kahului.
12. **Delta Flight 191 and Microbursts: How a Invisible Weather Monster Revolutionized Radars**
   - *Question:* What happens when a 100-mph downdraft of rain-cooled air strikes the runway threshold right as an airliner slows to landing speed?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. Ted Fujita's microburst discovery, rapid headwind-to-tailwind airspeed collapse, mandatory airborne windshear warning radar installations.
13. **Cockpit Resource Management: How Eliminating Captain Authoritarianism Saved Flying**
   - *Question:* Why did co-pilots in the 1970s watch their captains fly into terrain rather than speak up, and how did NASA and United Airlines fix the hierarchy?
   - *Key Focus:* United Flight 173 Portland fuel starvation crash, steep authority gradients, assertive challenge-response protocols (Two-Challenge Rule), modern airline crew training.
14. **Air France Flight 447: The Frozen Pitot Tubes and the Deep Ocean Stall**
   - *Question:* How did an ultra-modern Airbus A330 fall 38,000 feet into the pitch-black mid-Atlantic while the pilots held the side-stick pulled back the entire time?
   - *Key Focus:* Ice crystal blockage in Thales pitot probes, autopilot disconnect into Alternate Law, dual-input side-stick design debates, deep ocean recovery at 13,000 feet.
15. **The Black Box: Inside the Indestructible Orange Flight Data Recorders**
   - *Question:* How does a brightly painted orange cylinder withstand 3,400 G-forces of impact, 1,100°C kerosene fires, and the crushing pressure of the ocean floor?
   - *Key Focus:* David Warren's 1953 Australian invention, Flight Data Recorder (FDR) vs Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR), titanium armor and thermal insulation, underwater locator beacons (ULBs).
### Tier 4: Stealth, Scramjets & Autonomous Flight
16. **SR-71 Blackbird: The Spyplane That Leaked Fuel on the Runway to Fly at Mach 3.2**
   - *Question:* Why was the SR-71 built with loose-fitting titanium panels that leaked fuel until aerodynamic friction at 2,000 mph heated the airframe to 600°F?
   - *Key Focus:* Kelly Johnson and Lockheed Skunk Works, Soviet CIA titanium procurement shell companies, Pratt & Whitney J58 continuous-bleed turbojet/ramjet engines, evading 4,000 SAM missiles.
17. **The B-2 Spirit and Radar Cross-Section: How a Flying Wing Becomes as Small as an Insect**
   - *Question:* How does a 172-foot-wingspan nuclear bomber appear on enemy radar screens as no larger than a bumblebee or a marble?
   - *Key Focus:* Pyotr Ufimtsev's diffraction equations, continuous curvature and radar-absorbent materials (RAM), serpentine engine intake ducts, fly-by-wire stability on tailless wings.
18. **The Scramjet Frontier: How to Light a Match in a Category 5 Hurricane at Mach 7**
   - *Question:* Why do conventional jet turbines melt at hypersonic speeds, and how does a Supersonic Combustion Ramjet sustain combustion in milliseconds?
   - *Key Focus:* Ram compression vs supersonic flow deceleration, X-43A and X-51 Waverider test flights, thermal barrier materials, hypersonic glide weapons arms race.
19. **The Boeing 737 MAX Crisis: MCAS, Angle-of-Attack Sensors, and Regulatory Capture**
   - *Question:* How did software designed to mimic older flight characteristics rely on a single fragile angle-of-attack sensor, causing two crashes and a worldwide grounding?
   - *Key Focus:* LEAP-1B engine placement pitching moment, Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS) secrecy, FAA delegated certification oversight, 20-month fleet grounding.
20. **eVTOL and Pilotless Commercial Airliners: The Sky in 2040**
   - *Question:* Will electric vertical takeoff air taxis replace highway commutes, and will passenger jets ever fly safely without a human in the cockpit?
   - *Key Focus:* Distributed electric propulsion (DEP) battery density limits, Joby and Archer urban certification, AI autonomy redundancy vs passenger psychological trust.
