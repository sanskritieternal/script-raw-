# Selected institutional source note — Jainism

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-287-049`. This is a catalogue comparison; it is not a folio transcription or critical edition.

- [British Library catalogue, Or 5149, *Kalpasūtra*](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003080823): describes an illustrated 15th-century bound paper poṭhi of 80 folios, a Prakrit Śvetāmbara text, and links digitised content.
- [British Library catalogue, Or 13455, *Kalpasūtra* with Sanskrit glosses](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003080872): describes a 15th-century illustrated but incomplete 90-folio witness with Prakrit text and Sanskrit glosses. The two catalogue records distinguish witness form and contents.

The catalogue pages were accessible; individual manuscript folios and their Prakrit/Sanskrit readings were not examined here. Catalogue date ranges are dates assigned to witnesses, not composition dates for the work. Confirm scripts, foliation, missing leaves, gloss placement and translations with the digital images and specialist editions before narration. These are Śvetāmbara witnesses, not a representative sample of all Jain traditions.
