## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of DIY Mini Worlds across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: DIY Mini Worlds Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in DIY Mini Worlds.
- **Tier 2: DIY Mini Worlds Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of DIY Mini Worlds.
- **Tier 3: DIY Mini Worlds Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in DIY Mini Worlds.
- **Tier 4: DIY Mini Worlds Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for DIY Mini Worlds.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of DIY Mini Worlds: A Handbuilt Tiny World**
   - *Question:* Which dated diorama or dollhouse practice informs a named project?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace documented sources without one origin claim. Evidence base: dated sketches and prototypes, measured builds, material records, credited source designs, display photographs, visitor feedback, and conservation notes.
2. **DIY Mini Worlds: A Mini World with Internal Logic**
   - *Question:* What makes one small scene readable as a place?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine scale, route, inhabitants, and visible clues. Evidence base: dated sketches and prototypes, measured builds, material records, credited source designs, display photographs, visitor feedback, and conservation notes.
3. **DIY Mini Worlds: Who Built the World?**

4. **DIY Mini Worlds: One Scene, One Scale**
