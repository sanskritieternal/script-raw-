## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Archaeology across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Archaeological Methodology, Stratigraphy & Radiocarbon Dating (Scratch / Beginner)** — Law of superposition, Harris Matrix stratigraphy, carbon-14 half-life calibration curves, and survey grid excavations.
- **Tier 2: Monumental Tomb Discoveries & Urban Excavations (Intermediate)** — Tutankhamun's KV62 discovery, Pompeii volcanic pyroclastic casts, Ur Royal Tombs lapis lazuli, and Mohenjo-Daro sanitation engineering.
- **Tier 3: Submerged Underwater Ruins & Lidar Jungle Mapping (Upper Intermediate)** — Thonis-Heracleion sunken Nile ports, airborne LiDAR laser penetration in Mesoamerica, dry-stone bog bodies (Tollund Man), and salvage ethics.
- **Tier 4: Ancient DNA Paleogenomics, Provenance Ethics & The Future of Discovery (Advanced / Expert)** — Svante Pääbo paleogenomics, looting black markets, the Elgin Marbles repatriation debate, and satellite synthetic aperture radar (SAR).

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Methodology, Stratigraphy & Radiocarbon Dating
1. **The Discovery of Tutankhamun's Tomb: Howard Carter, the Sealed Antechamber, and the Curse**
   - *Question:* What did Howard Carter see through the candle-lit chisel hole in November 1922 when Lord Carnarvon asked 'Can you see anything?'
   - *Key Focus:* 'Yes, wonderful things': 5,398 unlooted artifacts, golden death mask, quartzite sarcophagus, debunking the sensational 'Mummy's Curse' deaths.
2. **Radiocarbon Dating: How Willard Libby Won the Nobel Prize for Measuring Radioactive Decay**
   - *Question:* How does measuring the decay of Carbon-14 into Nitrogen-14 in wood, charcoal, and bones establish absolute historical dates?
   - *Key Focus:* Willard Libby 1949 discovery, 5,730-year half-life, tree-ring calibration curves (dendrochronology), AMS (Accelerator Mass Spectrometry) miniaturization.
3. **Stratigraphy & The Harris Matrix: Reading Earth Like the Pages of a Book**
   - *Question:* How do dirt layers, trash pits, and wall foundations allow archaeologists to construct precise relative chronological timelines?
   - *Key Focus:* Nicolas Steno's Law of Superposition, Edward Harris 1973 matrix system, soil discoloration cues, bioturbation anomalies.
4. **The Rosetta Stone: How Jean-François Champollion Deciphered Egyptian Hieroglyphs**
   - *Question:* How did a broken granodiorite slab with the same royal decree in Greek, Demotic, and Hieroglyphs unlock 3,000 years of lost Egyptian records?
   - *Key Focus:* Cartouches of Ptolemy and Cleopatra, phonetic vs symbolic breakthrough, Coptic language phonetic bridge, British Museum acquisition.
### Tier 2: Monumental Tombs & Urban Excavations
5. **Pompeii & Herculaneum: The Day Mount Vesuvius Froze Roman Life in Pyroclastic Ash**
   - *Question:* How did Giuseppe Fiorelli's liquid plaster poured into ash voids reveal the exact facial agony, clothing, and pets of Roman citizens?
   - *Key Focus:* Pliny the Younger eyewitness letters, pyroclastic density currents (PDCs) at 500°F, bakery loaves with stamped names, carbonized Herculaneum wooden beds.
6. **The Royal Tombs of Ur: Leonard Woolley and the Golden Headdresses of Mesopotamia**
   - *Question:* What did the undisturbed 4,500-year-old tomb of Queen Puabi reveal about Sumerian gold craftsmanship and court suicide rituals?
   - *Key Focus:* Lapis lazuli and carnelian beads, the Bull-headed Lyre, the Standard of Ur, 68 court attendants drinking poisoned cups in the death pit.
7. **Mohenjo-Daro & Harappa: The 4,500-Year-Old Indus Cities with Flushing Toilets and Grid Streets**
   - *Question:* Why did a Bronze Age civilization covering 1 million square kilometers build standard-sized bricks and municipal sewers with zero palaces or temples?
   - *Key Focus:* The Great Bath waterproofing with bitumen, underground covered brick sewers, standard weights and measures, the undeciphered Indus script.
8. **The Terracotta Army: The 8,000 Life-Sized Warriors Guarding China's First Emperor**
   - *Question:* Why has no archaeologist dared to open the underground tomb of Qin Shi Huang containing a subterranean empire of mercury rivers?
   - *Key Focus:* 1974 well-digging discovery, individualized clay facial features, purple and green pigments flaking upon air contact, mercury soil vapor anomalies.
### Tier 3: Underwater Archaeology & LiDAR
9. **Thonis-Heracleion: Franck Goddio's Discovery of Egypt's Lost Sunken Port**
   - *Question:* How did an ancient trading metropolis that vanished beneath the Mediterranean in the 8th century get rediscovered under 30 feet of sand?
   - *Key Focus:* Soil liquefaction after earthquakes, nuclear resonance magnetometers, massive 16-foot granodiorite statues of Hapi, 64 ancient shipwrecks.
10. **LiDAR in the Maya Jungle: Peeling Back the Forest to Reveal 60,000 Hidden Structures**
   - *Question:* How did firing 500,000 laser pulses per second from an airplane prove that the Maya civilization was four times larger than textbooks claimed?
   - *Key Focus:* Optical canopy penetration, PACUNAM LiDAR initiative in Guatemala, elevated causeways, raised agricultural wetland terraces, fortress battlements.
11. **Tollund Man: The 2,400-Year-Old Bog Body Preserved Down to His Last Porridge Meal**
   - *Question:* Why did two peat cutters in 1950 think they had stumbled onto a fresh murder victim who actually died during the Iron Age?
   - *Key Focus:* Sphagnum moss acidic water and anaerobic tannins tanning skin like leather, noose around the neck, stomach content analysis (barley porridge).
12. **The Antikythera Shipwreck: The Sponge Divers Who Found the World's First Mechanical Wonder**
   - *Question:* What happened when Greek sponge divers sheltered from a storm in 1900 and descended onto a graveyard of bronze statues and rotting wood?
   - *Key Focus:* Diver Elias Stadiatis discovery, recovery of the Philosopher's head, heavily corroded bronze lump ignored for months, deep diving dangers.
### Tier 4: Ancient DNA, Ethics & Future Discovery
13. **Ancient DNA & The Genetic Revolution: Svante Pääbo Rewriting the Human Family Tree**
   - *Question:* How did extracting micro-fragments of degraded DNA from inside petrous inner-ear bones prove modern humans interbred with Neanderthals?
   - *Key Focus:* Max Planck Institute clean-room protocols, elimination of modern human contamination, Denisovan discovery from a single finger bone, 2022 Nobel Prize.
14. **The Parthenon Marbles: Lord Elgin, the Ottoman Firman, and the Repatriation War**
   - *Question:* Did Lord Elgin rescue Phidias's classical Greek sculptures from Ottoman gunpowder destruction, or did he loot the soul of Athens?
   - *Key Focus:* The contested 1801 Italian translation firman permit, Acropolis Museum waiting gallery, universal museum doctrine vs cultural property repatriation.
15. **The Black Market in Antiquities: How Looters and Smugglers Plunder Ancient Tombs**
   - *Question:* How do looters armed with metal detectors in Egypt, Syria, and Italy funnel looted sarcophagi into prestigious Manhattan museums?
   - *Key Focus:* Giacomo Medici Geneva freeport smuggling ring, forged provenance paper trails, Manhattan DA antiquities trafficking unit seizures.
16. **Satellite Archaeology: Sarah Parcak and Finding Ancient Cities from 400 Miles in Orbit**
   - *Question:* How do near-infrared satellite sensors detect buried mud-brick walls by measuring moisture differences in surface agricultural crops?
   - *Key Focus:* Cropmark and soilmark thermal signatures, finding thousands of forgotten tombs and settlements in Tanis, crowdsourced GlobalXplorer platform.
17. **The Dead Sea Scrolls: The Bedouin Goatherd and the Secret Caves of Qumran**
   - *Question:* How did a shepherd throwing a stone into a limestone cliff cave in 1947 uncover the oldest surviving Hebrew biblical manuscripts?
   - *Key Focus:* Essenes sectarian community, copper scroll treasure map, Great Isaiah Scroll, multi-decade publication delays and international scandal.
18. **Catalhoyuk: The 9,000-Year-Old Rooftop City with No Doors or Streets**
   - *Question:* Why did 8,000 Neolithic people in Anatolia build houses packed together like honeycomb cells entered exclusively through ladders in the roof?
   - *Key Focus:* James Mellaart and Ian Hodder excavations, burial under bedroom floors, bull skull shrines (bucrania), lack of social hierarchy or warfare.
19. **The Piltdown Man Hoax: The 40-Year Scientific Fraud That Fooled the World**
   - *Question:* How did Charles Dawson stain an orangutan jaw and a modern human skull with potassium dichromate to fake the 'Missing Link' in 1912?
   - *Key Focus:* Confirmation bias among British imperial anatomists, Kenneth Oakley's 1953 fluorine absorption dating exposure, lessons for scientific peer review.
20. **The Future of Archaeology: Non-Destructive Muon Scanning, AI, and In-Situ Conservation**
   - *Question:* Why are modern archaeologists putting down picks and shovels in favor of cosmic ray radiography and virtual unwrapping algorithms?
   - *Key Focus:* ScanPyramids cosmic-ray muon detectors revealing the Big Void, AI deciphering fragmented Akkadian cuneiform tablets, leaving sites intact for future tech.
