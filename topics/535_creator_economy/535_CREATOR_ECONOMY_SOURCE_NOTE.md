# Creator economy source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-535-043 - YouTube Shorts Creator Pool

- Primary platform policy: YouTube Help, *YouTube Shorts monetization policies*, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en-EN (inspected 2026-10-24). Eligible monetizing channels must accept the Shorts Monetization Module. The policy says ads between Shorts are pooled, funds are allocated to creators based on eligible engaged views, and music licensing is accounted for before the Creator Pool allocation; creators receive 45% of the revenue allocated to them from that pool.
- Primary platform comparison: YouTube Help, *YouTube partner earnings overview*, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en (inspected 2026-10-24). The page distinguishes Shorts Feed 45% of allocated pool revenue from Watch Page 55% of net ad revenue and membership/fan-funding terms. YouTube's September 2022 announcement https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/supporting-the-next-wave-of-creative-entrepreneurs/ documents the early-2023 introduction; current terms should be taken from the live policy and creator agreement, not the launch promise alone.
- Limits: This is a dated platform rule, not evidence of any creator's actual earnings, a fixed rate per view or a guaranteed income. Country, eligibility, accepted modules, original-content/ad-suitability rules, advertiser demand, music usage, engaged-view definitions and agreement version affect outcome. Terms can change; verify on script date. Do not imply that using music always lowers an individual creator's nominal 45% share of allocated pool revenue, or that revenue share resolves rights for reuse elsewhere.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-535-043 is a named Shorts payout mechanism. BANK-535-035 covers platform-change inequality, BANK-535-036 diversified income and BANK-535-038 licensing/disclosure outside this Shorts formula.
