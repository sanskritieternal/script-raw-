#### BANK-172-011 — The Asch Conformity Experiments: Denying Your Own Eyes to Fit In with the Group

**Video question:** Why will 75% of intelligent people look at three lines on a card and publicly agree with an obviously wrong answer because actors in the room gave it first?

**Individual coverage scope:** Solomon Asch's 1951 methodology, normative vs informational conformity, the power of a single lone dissenter dropping conformity from 37% to 5%, and the neurobiology of social rejection panic.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-172-012 — The Hawthorne Effect: How Being Watched Radically Alters Human Productivity

**Individual coverage scope:** Western Electric's Hawthorne Works experiments (1924-1932), the observer effect in social science, novelty effects, worker feeling of special attention, and controlling for observer bias in modern experiments.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-172-015 — The Robbers Cave Experiment: How Intergroup Conflict is Created and Healed

**Video question:** How did researchers turn 22 well-adjusted boy scouts at an Oklahoma summer camp into bitter, violent tribal enemies in just four days?

**Individual coverage scope:** Muzafer Sherif's Realist Conflict Theory, the Eagles vs the Rattlers, zero-sum competition breeding hatred and cabin raids, failure of simple social contact to bring peace, and the power of superordinate goals (fixing the shared water supply) in uniting warring groups.

#### BANK-172-016 — The Belmont Report: How Unethical Horrors Built the Modern Human Subjects Protection Code

**Video question:** How did the 40-year Tuskegee Syphilis Study force the US government to establish the modern ethical rules governing all human medical and social research?

**Individual coverage scope:** Withholding penicillin from 400 Black men for decades, whistleblower Peter Buxtun, the 1979 Belmont Report's three core ethical principles: Respect for Persons (informed consent), Beneficence (do no harm), and Justice (fair subject selection), and the creation of Institutional Review Boards (IRBs).

**Individual coverage scope:** Dr. Wendell Johnson and Mary Tudor's University of Iowa experiment, relentless negative conditioning berating children that their speech was terrible, lifelong psychological withdrawal, decades of institutional coverup, and modern multi-million-dollar state settlements.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-172-018"></a>
#### BANK-172-018 — The Smoke-Filled Room Experiment: When Pluralistic Ignorance Paralyzes Humans in Real Danger

**Individual coverage scope:** Bibb Latané and John Darley's 1968 experiment, 75% of lone subjects reporting smoke in two minutes vs only 10% reporting it when sitting with passive confederates, pluralistic ignorance dynamics, and overcoming social embarrassment during emergencies.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-172-019 — The Rosenhan Experiment: Being Sane in Insane Places

**Individual coverage scope:** David Rosenhan's 1973 Science paper, average 19-day involuntary hospitalization, normal note-taking labeled as pathological 'writing behavior', the reverse experiment catching imaginary pseudopatients, and the overhaul of the DSM diagnostic manual.
