## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Tiny Houses across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Tiny Houses Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Tiny Houses.
- **Tier 2: Tiny Houses Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Tiny Houses.
- **Tier 3: Tiny Houses Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Tiny Houses.
- **Tier 4: Tiny Houses Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Tiny Houses.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Tiny Houses: A Small Dwelling in Context**
   - *Question:* Which local housing rule or need shaped a dated tiny-house project?
   - *Key Focus:* Use permits, plans, and occupant testimony. Evidence base: current local zoning and adopted code, plans and permits, utility and cost records, product data, measured performance, and consented occupant accounts.
2. **Tiny Houses: A Small Home under Use**
   - *Question:* How does a named design meet sleeping, cooking, hygiene, storage, and exit needs?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare measured plan and lived use. Evidence base: current local zoning and adopted code, plans and permits, utility and cost records, product data, measured performance, and consented occupant accounts.
3. **Tiny Houses: Land and Infrastructure Costs**
   - *Question:* Which utility, transport, site, and permit work is left out of a build story?
   - *Key Focus:* Use itemized records and local rules. Evidence base: current local zoning and adopted code, plans and permits, utility and cost records, product data, measured performance, and consented occupant accounts.
4. **Tiny Houses: Tiny House or Another Type?**
   - *Question:* How is a proposed home classified in a specific jurisdiction?
   - *Key Focus:* Check current definitions, foundation, mobility, and occupancy. Evidence base: current local zoning and adopted code, plans and permits, utility and cost records, product data, measured performance, and consented occupant accounts.
5. **Tiny Houses: Space versus Whole-Life Cost**
   - *Question:* Does a smaller floor area lower total housing cost for one case?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare site, construction, utility, insurance, and upkeep over time. Evidence base: current local zoning and adopted code, plans and permits, utility and cost records, product data, measured performance, and consented occupant accounts.
