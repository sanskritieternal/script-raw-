## BANK-395-043: occupied tiny houses on wheels in Portland

- [Portland's current occupied-RV and tiny-house guidance](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/zoning-land-use/zoning-code-overview/occupied-rvs-and-tiny-houses-wheels) classifies a constructed tiny house on a wheeled trailer as an occupied recreational vehicle when parked on a developed residential lot with a primary dwelling. It describes a one-per-lot limit, spacing and electrical conditions, and distinguishes these vehicles from foundation-built ADUs. The city's [adopted code-amendment record](https://www.portland.gov/sites/default/files/2021/s2hc_as_adopted_volume_1.pdf) documents the policy's adoption context.
- The public page is a current city interpretation and can change. It does not approve a particular parcel, establish that a structure meets construction standards, or replace permits and utility review. Any script about a real address requires the applicable code edition and parcel file; another jurisdiction may classify the same form differently.
- This named jurisdictional case addresses the archived “can this home be occupied here?” question, separate from 395's physical layout and whole-life-cost questions and 386's theme-park land use.
