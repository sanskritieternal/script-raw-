# Selected primary-source premise check ? Earth science (2026-10-24)

## Landsat 9 / Landsat 8 underflight ? BANK-348-042
USGS reports that the early Landsat 9 mission intentionally flew under Landsat 8, yielding near-simultaneous observations of common Earth targets for cross-calibration. Its 2022 publication says fewer scenes were obtained than expected, though sufficient for the planned comparison. This supports an instrument-comparability case, not a claim that the sensors make identical images or that any environmental change follows from the underflight.

Primary agency publication: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/landsat-9-cross-calibration-under-fly-landsat-8-planning-and-execution . Mission context: https://www.usgs.gov/calval/landsat-calibration-and-validation . The 2021 underflight and 2022 paper are historical; calibration and processing versions continue to change.

## Hunga Tonga / Aura MLS ? BANK-348-043
NASA?s Aura mission reports MLS measurements following the 2022-01-15 eruption showing an unusually large stratospheric water-vapor injection; its institutional summary gives an estimate of 146?5 Tg and compares this with the 18-year MLS record. A later NASA Aura analysis follows aerosol and water-vapor plume separation. These records establish a measured atmospheric perturbation with retrieval/model uncertainty; they do not alone establish a specific long-term surface-climate effect or an all-history record.

Primary mission records: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/aura/aura-measurements-of-an-underwater-volcano-eruption/ ; https://aura.gsfc.nasa.gov/science/feature-20230103.html . Source attribution and mass estimate depend on MLS retrieval and trajectory analysis; use the underlying study before a quantitative script.
