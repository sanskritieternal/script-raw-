# Selected case: Wadi el-Jarf logbooks

**Proposed question:** `BANK-544-050` — What do Merer's Wadi el-Jarf papyri show about one Old Kingdom work crew and limestone transport?

## Primary evidence inspected

- [Institut français d'archéologie orientale (IFAO) excavation and papyrus-restoration record](https://www.ifao.egnet.net/recherche/operations/op20452/) states that fragments were found in the port's storage-gallery area in 2013 and comprise boat-team logbooks and accounts of provisions and tools. Its French institutional summary relates the material to the late reign of Khufu and distinguishes logbooks from accounting fragments.
- [Pierre Tallet's 2017 publication record for P. Jarf A and B](https://www.orient-mediterranee.com/les-papyrus-de-la-mer-rouge-i-le-journal-de-merer-p-jarf-a-et-b/) identifies the edited witnesses and offers English and Arabic translation appendices. The publication listing was inspected; the full edition, facsimiles, individual line readings and appendices were not collated for this bank proposal.
- [IFAO Wadi el-Jarf excavation overview](https://www.ifao.egnet.net/archeologie/ouadi-el-jarf/) provides the site context. Treat its summary as archaeological interpretation linked to specific excavated material, not as a complete payroll or building plan.

## Bounded premise and gaps

The record supports a particular boat team's documented transport and provisioning activities and one overseer named Merer. It does not establish how every pyramid stone was moved, the total construction workforce, workers' employment status, a full daily itinerary for the whole site, or the design of the pyramid. Translation choices, broken portions and publication date matter. Exact cargo quantities, trip timing and place-name equivalences require the edition's text and apparatus before narration.

## Coverage and overlap decision

Archived documented-case, labor-logistics and evidence-trail pitches route to this named papyrus corpus. General Old Kingdom administration, worker provisioning and settlement evidence remain with `BANK-544-033`, `BANK-544-037` and `BANK-544-045`; `BANK-544-050` is about what P. Jarf A/B can specifically establish. Tool-performance, biographies, future discoveries and superlatives remain out of scope pending separate evidence. Other archaeology banks may explain dating or excavation methods, but do not own this named crew record.
