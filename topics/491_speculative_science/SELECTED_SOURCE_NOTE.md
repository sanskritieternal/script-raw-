# Selected source note — OPERA neutrino timing anomaly

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-491-044. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA or faster-than-light discovery claim.

## Primary collaboration and laboratory records

- OPERA Collaboration, *Measurement of the neutrino velocity with the OPERA detector in the CNGS beam*, arXiv:1109.4897 with version history: https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.4897 . Compare the 2011 first submission with its later corrected versions; the latest abstract reports a time difference compatible with zero within uncertainty.
- OPERA Collaboration, dedicated 2012 data analysis: https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1276 . This reports the follow-up timing measurement using upgraded/independent systems.
- CERN, *OPERA experiment reports anomaly in flight time of neutrinos from CERN to Gran Sasso*, with 2012-06-08 update: https://home.cern/opera-experiment-reports-anomaly-in-flight-time-of-neutrinos-from-cern-to-gran-sasso/ . CERN records that Borexino, ICARUS, LVD and OPERA measurements were consistent with light speed in the later checks.

## Observation and interpretation limits

The 2011 anomaly was a reported measurement requiring scrutiny, not established superluminal travel or a verified alternative physical theory. Later timing checks and corrected analysis superseded the early claim; do not cite the current arXiv abstract as though it were the 2011 result. Explain that calibration, timing and independent replication matter without inventing a simple one-part failure story or attributing misconduct. The case concerns one neutrino-beam experiment, not every speculative hypothesis. Check exact versions, numerical uncertainties and hardware accounts before scripting them. Paraphrase papers/CERN and respect figure rights.
