## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Cooperatives across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Cooperatives Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Cooperatives.
- **Tier 2: Cooperatives Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Cooperatives.
- **Tier 3: Cooperatives Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Cooperatives.
- **Tier 4: Cooperatives Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Cooperatives.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Cooperatives: How Cooperatives Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a cooperative's founding need shape its ownership and trading rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one cooperative from its founding need through changes in membership, ownership and trading activity; use bylaws and dated business records. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Cooperatives: A Documented Cooperatives Case**
   - *Question:* What do member outcomes and finances establish about a cooperative's performance?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one cooperative's documented performance using member benefits, finances and governance; test whether the case represents its wider sector. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
3. **Cooperatives: People behind Cooperatives**
   - *Question:* Whose voluntary or administrative labor is omitted from a cooperative's account?
   - *Key Focus:* Document overlooked member, administrative or community labor in one cooperative; obtain consent and distinguish paid from voluntary work. Evidence base: Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
4. **Cooperatives: Understanding Cooperatives**
   - *Question:* How do membership, voting, capital and surplus connect in one cooperative?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain membership, voting, capital and surplus through one cooperative's actual rules; distinguish its model from other cooperative forms. Evidence base: Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
5. **Cooperatives: Tradeoffs in Cooperatives**
   - *Question:* Which objectives conflict when judging a cooperative's success?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare methods of measuring cooperative success using financial resilience, member participation and distribution; identify conflicting objectives. Evidence base: Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: actual bylaws, membership rules, audited accounts, votes and member distributions. Starting framework: ILO worker cooperatives (https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
