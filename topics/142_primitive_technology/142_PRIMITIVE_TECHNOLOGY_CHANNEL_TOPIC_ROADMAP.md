## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Primitive Technology across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Flintknapping, Friction Fire & Mud Brick Kilns (Beginner / Scratch)** — Conchoidal fracture percussion flaking, hand drill and bow drill friction fire physics, identifying clay soil deposits, building natural draft charcoal kilns, and stone celt axes.
- **Tier 2: Clay Pottery, Natural Cordage & Foraging Trapping Systems (Intermediate)** — Pit firing earthenware pottery, extracting bast plant fibers (nettle/bark) for 2-ply cordage, bamboo deadfall traps, building wattle-and-daub thatched huts, and making charcoal.
- **Tier 3: Smelting Native Iron, Blower Furnaces & Wattle-and-Daub Shelters (Upper Intermediate)** — Smelting iron from bacterial bog iron and red clay, building reciprocating dual-piston bamboo air blowers, forged primitive iron chisels, and tiled roof kiln firing.
- **Tier 4: Self-Sustaining Stone Age Settlements, Deep Bushcraft Engineering & Primitive Chemistry (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Hydraulic tilt-hammer waterwheels, charcoal gasification, ash lye soap and potash fertilizer chemistry, and establishing multi-year self-sustaining stone-age homesteads.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **Primitive Technology: Smelting Iron from River Bacteria and Red Clay**
   - *Question:* How did John Plant collect orange iron-rich water bacteria, build a clay bloomery furnace, and smelt metallic iron pellets with zero modern tools?
   - *Key Focus:* Bog iron extraction, carbon monoxide chemical reduction (Fe2O3 + 3CO -> 2Fe + 3CO2), bamboo air blower tuyere nozzles, and breaking into the Bronze/Iron Age from pure scratch.
2. **Friction Fire: The Physics of the Bow Drill and Hand Drill**
   - *Question:* How do you grind two sticks together to create an 800°F glowing ember without matches or flint in high humidity?
   - *Key Focus:* Spindle and hearth board wood selection (softwood on softwood), friction coefficient, V-notch dust collection funnel, char dust auto-ignition, and tinder nest blowing.
3. **Flintknapping: The Glass-Fracture Physics of Stone Age Arrowheads and Hand Axes**
   - *Question:* How do hunter-gatherers strike brittle rocks like flint, chert, and obsidian to create edges sharper than modern surgical steel scalpels?
   - *Key Focus:* Conchoidal fracture shockwave propagation, striking platforms, hammerstones vs antler billets (direct percussion), and pressure flaking with deer tine tools.
4. **Finding and Testing Clay: Making Earthenware Pottery from Riverbeds**
   - *Question:* How do you dig muddy river dirt, wash away silt and organic debris, and fire durable cooking pots in a campfire?
   - *Key Focus:* Plasticity ribbon test, sediment settling water jars, grog tempering to prevent shrinkage cracking, coil pottery construction, and campfire pit-firing temperature ramps.
5. **The Stone Axe: Grinding Basalt Celts and Hafting Hardwood Handles**
   - *Question:* How does spending 20 hours rubbing a river rock on sandstone create an axe capable of chopping down 10-inch hardwood trees?
   - *Key Focus:* Hard volcanic basalt vs brittle flint, wet grinding slurry, wooden hafting mortises, resin and plant cordage lashing, and impact wedge physics.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Natural Cordage: Making Indestructible Rope from Bark, Vines, and Nettles**
   - *Question:* How do you strip the inner bast bark of trees and twist it into strong, two-ply cordage that can pull hundreds of pounds?
   - *Key Focus:* Inner bast fibers, retting in water to dissolve pectins, the reverse-wrap thigh-rolling technique, splicing overlapping fibers, and tensile strength comparisons.
7. **Wattle and Daub: The Architecture of Mud and Stick That Built Europe**
   - *Question:* How does weaving flexible hazel rods and smearing them with a mix of clay, sand, and grass straw create durable weatherproof walls?
   - *Key Focus:* Upright stakes and horizontal wattle weaving, daub composite ratio (clay binder + straw tensile reinforcement), roof overhang rain protection, and lime whitewashing.
8. **Charcoal Kilns: Building the Fuel That Powers Primitive Metallurgy**
   - *Question:* Why can wood campfires only reach 1,200°F while pure charcoal burns hot enough to smelt copper and iron at 2,200°F?
   - *Key Focus:* Volatiles driven off without combustion, thermal chimney drafting, mud dome sealing, air intake regulation, and carbon yield optimization.
9. **Primitive Foraging Traps: Deadfalls, Snare Springs, and Fish Weirs**
   - *Question:* How do delicate wooden trigger notches (Figure-4 deadfall) hold 50-pound flat stones ready to drop at the touch of a feather?
   - *Key Focus:* Mechanical advantage in trigger sear notches, spring-pole snares, tidal stone fish weirs, animal behavioral funneling, and ethical survival trapping.
10. **Roof Tile Kilns: Firing 500 Curved Clay Shingles for a Permanent House**
   - *Question:* How did John Plant build an updraft clay kiln with a firebox and subterranean grate to bake waterproof ceramic roof tiles?
   - *Key Focus:* Curved wooden tile-forming molds, preventing tile warping during drying, updraft kiln airflow thermodynamics, vitrification temperatures, and fireproof roofing.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Blower Furnace: Dual-Piston Bamboo Air Bellows for Continuous Draft**
   - *Question:* How do two bamboo cylinders fitted with feathered leather pistons and flap valves produce an unbroken hurricane blast of oxygen into a furnace?
   - *Key Focus:* Double-acting reciprocating piston mechanics, non-return flap valves, tuyere nozzle placement, forced convection combustion rates, and hitting 2,400°F.
12. **Primitive Weaponry: The Atlatl Spearthrower vs The Wooden Self-Bow**
   - *Question:* Why did the flexible lever of the Atlatl launch six-foot darts with enough kinetic force to pierce the thick hide of an ice-age woolly mammoth?
   - *Key Focus:* Leverage extension of the human throwing arm, dart spine spring flex dynamics, tiller wood tillering on hunting self-bows, and kinetic penetration comparisons.
13. **Making Ash Lye Soap: Primitive Chemical Engineering with Wood Ash and Fat**
   - *Question:* How do you leach potassium hydroxide from white hardwood ashes and boil it with animal tallow to synthesize modern antibacterial soap?
   - *Key Focus:* Lye leaching hopper barrels, egg float density test for lye concentration, triglyceride ester saponification chemistry, soft potassium soap vs hard bar soap.
14. **Water Power: Building a Waterwheel and a Primitive Wooden Trip Hammer**
   - *Question:* How can an undershot waterwheel constructed from wood and vines automate the crushing of grain and ore with a 30-pound wooden hammer?
   - *Key Focus:* Stream weir diversion canals, wooden paddle wheel torque, cam lobes converting rotary motion to reciprocating hammer drops, and labor automation.
15. **The Fake Bushcraft Channels Exposed: Secret Bulldozers, Cement, and Crew Scandals**
   - *Question:* Why did dozens of copycat YouTube channels start using hidden diesel excavators, PVC water pipes, and bags of concrete to fake underground villas?
   - *Key Focus:* John Plant's authentic silent single-camera ethos vs Southeast Asian clickbait content farms, structural impossibilities of mud swimming pools, environmental destruction, and audience deception.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Smelting Copper and Bronze: The Malachite Reduction Breakthrough**
   - *Question:* How did ancient potters discover that green malachite rocks heated in charcoal kilns melt into glittering beads of pure malleable copper?
   - *Key Focus:* Oxide copper ores, carbon reduction in clay crucibles, pouring molten metal into soapstone molds, adding tin for bronze alloy hardness (90/10), and casting axes.
17. **Primitive Agriculture: The Milpa Three Sisters and Slash-and-Char Biochar**
   - *Question:* How did indigenous Amazonians turn nutrient-dead jungle clay into fertile Terra Preta black soil that stays rich for 1,000 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Biochar porous micro-structure hosting soil microbes, slash-and-char vs slash-and-burn, companion planting (Corn/Beans/Squash nitrogen fixation and structural support).
18. **Primitive Weaving: Building an Upright Warp-Weighted Loom from Branches**
   - *Question:* How do stones tied to vertical threads allow a primitive weaver to interlace horizontal weft fibers into warm cloaks and blankets?
   - *Key Focus:* Warp-weighted loom mechanics, shed rods and heddle bars creating alternating sheds, bone beaters packing fibers, and wool/flax textile production.
19. **The Psychology of Primitive Solitude: Why 10 Million People Watch Silent Mud Building**
   - *Question:* Why did a channel with zero speaking, zero background music, and only natural forest sounds become one of the most beloved sensations on the internet?
   - *Key Focus:* ASMR acoustic naturalism, vicarious human competence, deep evolutionary connection to survival problem-solving, and mental relief from modern digital noise.
20. **The Master Primitive Civilization Architecture: The Tech Tree of Human Survival**
   - *Question:* If you woke up naked in a primeval jungle with zero modern tools, what exact chronological sequence builds a self-sustaining Iron Age settlement?
   - *Key Focus:* The physical tech tree: Fire -> Cordage -> Stone Axe -> Clay Pottery -> Charcoal Kiln -> Bellows -> Iron Bloomery -> Metal Tools -> Agriculture -> Permanent Shelter.
