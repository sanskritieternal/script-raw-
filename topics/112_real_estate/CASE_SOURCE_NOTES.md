# Selected real estate case-source notes

Inspected 2026-10-24. These are bounded premise checks for proposed topics, not legal, tax or investment advice or a complete production source set. Topic `verified_sources` fields remain empty pending individual episode review.

## NAR practice changes — BANK-112-044

- The [National Association of Realtors practice-change announcement](https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/nar-practice-changes-to-take-effect-august-17) dates the relevant MLS changes to 17 August 2024. It describes removal of buyer-broker compensation offers from the MLS and written buyer agreements before tours for covered MLS participants. [NAR's September 2024 settlement FAQ](https://www.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2024-09/nar-settlement-faq-2024-09-05.pdf) says compensation remains negotiable and may take several forms.
- These are the settling trade association's statements about its practice rules. They do not establish a universal former six-percent rate, how a particular buyer paid, or a causal change in house prices. A production script should inspect the final court order, applicable MLS implementation and measured transaction data for any outcome claim.

## Florida unauthorized-occupant procedure — BANK-112-045

- The [Florida Senate page for 2024 HB 621](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2024/621) links the enrolled bill and legislative history. The [Florida House final bill analysis](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2024/621/Analyses/h0621z1.CJS.PDF) describes an optional sheriff-assisted procedure with specified owner complaint and qualifying conditions, effective 1 July 2024.
- The archive's “squatters hijacking luxury homes” framing does not identify a case or establish prevalence. The statute has fact-dependent exclusions and remedies; it cannot be generalized to tenants, title conflicts, other states or individualized eviction steps. Check current codification before scripting a present-day legal claim.

## Cost segregation — BANK-112-046

- The [IRS Cost Segregation Audit Technique Guide](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5653.pdf), February 2025, explains the evidence and classification issues examiners review in studies of real property components. The [IRS audit-guide index](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/audit-techniques-guides-atgs) identifies its purpose for examiners and preparers.
- The archive's “tax hack saving millionaires millions” is neither a property-specific calculation nor an entitlement. Depreciation timing depends on the component facts, basis, placed-in-service date and applicable law, which may change. The proposed question covers evidence, not a tax strategy or promised savings.

## New York City short-term rentals — BANK-112-047

- The [NYC Office of Special Enforcement registration-law page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/specialenforcement/registration-law/registration.page) explains Local Law 18 registration and booking-platform verification, with initial enforcement beginning 5 September 2023. The [city's host guidance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/specialenforcement/hosting-STRs/what-is-local-law-18.page) lists legal-occupancy and lease checks.
- These rules establish a specific city policy, not the archive's national “Airbnb bust” or panic-selling claim. A market-outcome question would require dated listings, bookings, sales and an appropriate comparison group.
