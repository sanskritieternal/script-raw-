## Scope, claims and rights review

Existing 106 proposals cover positioning, brand versus direct response, attribution, viral-success evidence, AI-assisted campaign accountability and customer economics. Repeated pitches route there. Packaging color routes to verified BANK-105-019; luxury scarcity to BANK-021-038; dark patterns to BANK-105-037. Adjacent retail, workplace, logistics and corporate-history scopes were inspected for campaign overlap. New cases specify dated ads, filings, archived posts or measurable holdouts. A private Liquid Death financing valuation is a dated transaction estimate, not verified profit or proof of marketing causation; obtain primary round documents before naming a figure. Super Bowl spot price varies by year and inventory. Political ad libraries omit some exposure and targeting detail. Campaign images, video, mascots, logos and creator work require appropriate rights for reuse; analysis and a link to the original are not a blanket license. Advertising law, platform rules and cookie settings change, so production must refresh sources and jurisdiction.
