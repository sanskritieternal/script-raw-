## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Consumer Technology across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: The Smartphone Revolution, Touchscreens & Lithium Batteries (Scratch / Beginner)** — Capacitive vs resistive touchscreens, the 2007 iPhone keynote staging, lithium-ion battery dendrites, and cellular 3G/4G/5G radio spectrum bands.
- **Tier 2: Smart Ecosystems, Computational Photography & Wearables (Intermediate)** — Multi-frame computational HDR image stacking, OLED burn-in vs Mini-LED local dimming, smartwatch photoplethysmography (PPG) heart sensors, and Bluetooth LE audio codecs.
- **Tier 3: Right to Repair, Planned Obsolescence & Supply Chain Geopolitics (Upper Intermediate)** — Serialized hardware parts pairing, the Foxconn Zhengzhou iPhone City production hub, e-waste toxic dumping in Guiyu, and European USB-C mandate regulatory battles.
- **Tier 4: Spatial Computing, Foldable Displays & Ambient AI Hardware (Advanced / Expert)** — Ultra-Thin Glass (UTG) hinge mechanical tolerances, micro-OLED passthrough spatial computing (Vision Pro), ambient audio smart glasses, and on-device neural NPU silicon.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: The Smartphone Revolution & Battery Chemistry
1. **The 2007 iPhone Keynote: Steve Jobs' Masterclass in Illusion, Staging, and Golden Phones**
   - *Question:* How did Steve Jobs present a prototype phone that constantly ran out of memory, dropped calls, and froze, by following an exact 'Golden Path' sequence of rehearsed taps?
   - *Key Focus:* The 128MB prototype memory limit, staging Wi-Fi frequencies to hide crowd interference, AT&T portable cell tower rigging, the 'widescreen iPod, phone, internet communicator' triple reveal.
2. **Capacitive vs Resistive Touchscreens: Why Early Smart-Phones Required a Stylus**
   - *Question:* Why did early PDAs and GPS units require hard stylus pressure on flexing plastic layers, and how did Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) grids sense the tiny electrical charge of human skin?
   - *Key Focus:* Pressure-sensitive resistive flexible membranes vs projected capacitive electrostatic field distortion, multi-touch gesture algorithms (pinch-to-zoom), Gorilla Glass chemical strengthening.
3. **Lithium-Ion Batteries: Why Your Phone Battery Dies in 2 Years and What Causes Dendrites**
   - *Question:* How does shuttling lithium ions between graphite anodes and cobalt cathodes degrade over 500 charge cycles, and why can microscopic metallic dendrites cause explosive thermal runaways?
   - *Key Focus:* Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) layer growth, lithium dendrite short circuits, fast charging thermal stress, why 20% to 80% charging habits genuinely prolong battery longevity.
4. **From 1G to 5G: The Invisible Radio Waves Carrying Billions of Gigabytes Through the Air**
   - *Question:* How did mobile communications evolve from crackling 1980s 1G analog voice towers to 5G millimeter-wave beamforming antennas transmitting 1 gigabit per second?
   - *Key Focus:* 2G digital GSM/CDMA, 3G mobile web data packets, 4G LTE OFDM modulation, 5G Massive MIMO beamforming and millimeter wave propagation limits through walls and rain.
### Tier 2: Smart Ecosystems & Computational Displays
5. **Computational Photography: Why a Tiny Phone Lens Takes Better Night Photos Than a DSLR**
   - *Question:* How does a smartphone camera sensor the size of a fingernail take breathtaking astrophotography by burst-capturing 15 underexposed RAW frames and aligning them with machine learning?
   - *Key Focus:* Multi-frame HDR exposure stacking, optical image stabilization gyro tracking, semantic segmentation masks (isolating faces, sky, foliage), synthetic bokeh depth mapping.
6. **OLED vs Mini-LED vs Micro-LED: The Battle for the Perfect Black Pixel**
   - *Question:* Why do organic light-emitting diodes (OLED) deliver infinite contrast ratios by turning individual subpixels completely off, and why is Micro-LED the holy grail that engineers can't mass-produce?
   - *Key Focus:* Self-emissive organic compounds vs Mini-LED thousands of local dimming zones, blue subpixel organic degradation (burn-in risk), Micro-LED pick-and-place manufacturing bottlenecks.
7. **Inside the Smartwatch: How Green LEDs Measure Your Heart Rate and Blood Oxygen**
   - *Question:* How does shining flashing green and infrared light through wrist skin measure blood volume expansion (photoplethysmography) and detect atrial fibrillation heart arrhythmias?
   - *Key Focus:* Hemoglobin green light absorption physics, Photoplethysmography (PPG) waveform filtering, ECG single-lead electrical potential tracking between crown and back glass, FDA medical clearances.
8. **Bluetooth LE and True Wireless Audio: The Miracle of Apple AirPods Synchronization**
   - *Question:* Why did early wireless earbuds suffer audio lag and dropouts between ears until custom silicon chips (H1/W1) broadcast independent synchronized audio streams to each ear?
   - *Key Focus:* Bluetooth Low Energy (LE) sniff subrating, dual-ear snooping protocols, active noise cancellation inverse phase acoustic wave generation, beamforming microphone arrays.
### Tier 3: Right to Repair, Planned Obsolescence & Supply Chains
9. **Right to Repair: How Tech Companies Lock You Out of Fixing Your Own Devices**
   - *Question:* Why does swapping a genuine screen from one brand new iPhone into an identical brand new iPhone disable Face ID, True Tone, and battery health reporting?
   - *Key Focus:* Microcontroller parts serialization and cryptographically paired serial numbers, proprietary pentalobe screws, glue vs pull-tab batteries, Louis Rossmann and iFixit legislative battles.
10. **Foxconn Zhengzhou: Inside the 300,000-Worker 'iPhone City' That Built the Modern World**
   - *Question:* How does a single factory complex in central China produce 500,000 iPhones per day with its own power plants, customs posts, and 300,000 workers living in factory dorms?
   - *Key Focus:* The JIT component supply funnel from Japan/Korea/Taiwan, 400 manual assembly steps per phone, strict security airport scanners, supply chain diversification to India and Vietnam.
11. **Planned Obsolescence: The Lightbulb Cartel and Why Your Old Phone Feels Slow**
   - *Question:* Did the 1924 Phoebus Cartel really fine lightbulb makers whose bulbs lasted over 1,000 hours, and why did Apple secretly throttle older iPhone processors in 2017 (Batterygate)?
   - *Key Focus:* Phoebus Cartel historical documentation, Apple Batterygate CPU power-management throttling to prevent sudden shutdowns on worn batteries, $500M class-action settlement, psychological vs engineered obsolescence.
12. **E-Waste and Guiyu: The Toxic Graveyards Where First-World Gadgets Go to Burn**
   - *Question:* Where do the 50 million tons of discarded laptops and smartphones go every year, and how do informal workers boil circuit boards over lead pots to extract gold?
   - *Key Focus:* Guiyu and Agbogbloshie informal e-waste hubs, toxic heavy metal groundwater contamination (lead, cadmium, mercury), open-air wire burning, modern closed-loop robotic recyclers (Apple Liam/Daisy).
13. **How the European Union Forced Apple to Abandon the Lightning Cable for USB-C**
   - *Question:* How did a single European directive in Brussels compel the world's most valuable tech company to abandon its lucrative proprietary MFi licensing program and adopt USB-C?
   - *Key Focus:* The Common Charger Directive 2022/2380, e-waste reduction estimates (11,000 tons annually), Apple's $100M+ annual Made for iPhone (MFi) royalty revenue loss, the global 'Brussels Effect'.
### Tier 4: Spatial Computing, Foldables & Ambient AI
14. **Foldable Phones: The Ultra-Thin Glass and Water-Drop Hinges Defying Material Physics**
   - *Question:* How can glass bend in half like a piece of paper 200,000 times without shattering, and how do waterdrop hinges eliminate the middle crease?
   - *Key Focus:* Schott and Corning Ultra-Thin Glass (UTG) flexible 30-micron glass sheets, water-drop teardrop hinge radius mechanical cams, dust-sweeper bristle micro-cleaners, polymer layers.
15. **Apple Vision Pro and Spatial Computing: Micro-OLEDs and the 12-Millisecond Passthrough Barrier**
   - *Question:* Why does virtual reality cause motion sickness if latency exceeds 20 milliseconds, and how does custom silicon (R1 chip) stream 12 cameras to 4K postage-stamp displays in 12 milliseconds?
   - *Key Focus:* Micro-OLED on silicon wafers (23 million pixels across two postage stamps), eye tracking using ring LEDs and foveated rendering, the photon-to-motion latency limit, spatial audio ray-tracing.
16. **Smart Glasses and Ambient Audio: Why Big Tech Wants to Replace Your Screen with Your Face**
   - *Question:* Why did Google Glass fail miserably in 2013 while Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses became a mainstream hit in 2024?
   - *Key Focus:* Social acceptability and removing obvious display prisms, open-ear directional audio speakers, multimodal AI cameras answering questions about what you see, privacy LED norms.
17. **On-Device Neural NPUs: Running 7-Billion-Parameter AI Locally on Your Phone**
   - *Question:* How do dedicated Neural Processing Units (NPUs) multiply 4-bit and 8-bit quantized matrix weights at 45 TOPS (trillion operations per second) using under 5 watts of battery power?
   - *Key Focus:* Systolic array tensor matrix multipliers, INT4/INT8 model quantization, private local processing vs cloud server round-trips, the battle between Qualcomm, Apple, and MediaTek.
18. **The Death of the 3.5mm Headphone Jack: The Boldest Corporate Squeeze in Tech History**
   - *Question:* When Phil Schiller claimed it took 'courage' to remove the 100-year-old analog audio jack from the iPhone 7, was it to save space for taptic engines or to force a $30 billion AirPods ecosystem?
   - *Key Focus:* Taptic engine haptic feedback hardware expansion, water resistance IP67 seals, consumer backlash to the $9 Lightning dongle, how every Android competitor copied the move within 2 years.
19. **The Matter Smart Home Standard: How Big Tech Finally Agreed to Talk to Each Other**
   - *Question:* Why did Apple, Google, Amazon, and Samsung spend 15 years trapping customers in walled smart home gardens before unifying under a single open protocol called Matter?
   - *Key Focus:* Thread mesh networking IPv6 radio packets, local network control eliminating cloud lag, eliminating bridge hubs, interoperability allowing Google Assistant to control Apple HomeKit plugs.
20. **Consumer Technology in 2035: Beyond the Glass Rectangle**
   - *Question:* What happens when augmented reality contact lenses, neural wristbands, and ambient home sensors make the smartphone completely obsolete?
   - *Key Focus:* Mojo Vision micro-LED contact lens prototypes, Meta EMG neural motor-neuron wristbands, voice/vision multimodal AI agents, the transition from active device usage to passive ambient intelligence.
