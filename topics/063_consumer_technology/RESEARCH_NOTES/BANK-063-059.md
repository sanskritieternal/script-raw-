# BANK-063-059 — EU common charger and the iPhone 15

**Selected question:** What did the EU common-charger directive require and when did iPhone adopt USB-C?

**Primary records inspected (2026-10-24):**

- [Directive (EU) 2022/2380](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022L2380), adopted 23 November 2022, amends the Radio Equipment Directive. Annex Ia specifies a USB Type-C receptacle for listed categories capable of wired charging, including handheld mobile phones. Article 2 sets application for the phone category from 28 December 2024; laptops have a later 28 April 2026 date. The text also has conditions on charging protocols and consumer information that a script should not reduce to “one cable fits every function.”
- [European Commission interpretive guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A52024XC02997) (2024) clarifies the listed-device scope and dates. It says an additional receptacle is not categorically prohibited if the covered device also has the required USB-C receptacle.
- [Apple's iPhone 15 and 15 Plus announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/09/apple-debuts-iphone-15-and-iphone-15-plus/) (12 September 2023) states those models use a USB-C connector. [Apple's iPhone 15 technical specification](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/111831) lists USB-C charging and USB 2 data transfer up to 480 Mb/s for that model, illustrating that a connector shape does not guarantee every data capability.

**Bounded finding:** The EU enacted a charging-interface rule with a later applicability date for covered phones, and Apple announced USB-C on iPhone 15 in 2023. These records establish chronology and product characteristics. They do not document Apple's internal reasons, so the archived “EU forced Apple” wording should not be used as a proved single-cause explanation.
