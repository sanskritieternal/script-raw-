## Semantic, labor and cultural boundaries

The active bank already covers fiber behavior, seams and fit, bespoke-to-ready-made suits, virtual try-on, secondhand condition, authenticity and brand labor verification. Archived pitches on those themes route there. A luxury-object authentication pitch routes to the verified neighboring BANK-021-039; a claim about Putian being a global counterfeit capital was not accepted. The newly authored cases focus on named dated garments, makers, samples, sites or processes. Neighboring luxury, jewelry, collectibles and shopping banks were inspected for scope. Designer-origin stories need collection dates and object provenance; cross-cultural borrowing needs community and historical context. Do not infer worker pay or conditions from a label, and do not infer demand, wealth or identity from a style photograph. Market prices, retail practices, brand schedules and legal rules require fresh checks before scripting.
