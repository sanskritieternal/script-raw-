# BANK-115-046: Hyperion-area access and visitor impacts

Research checked: 2026-10-24. Status: bounded premise research, not script or production approval.

The [National Park Service visitor guidance](https://www.nps.gov/redw/planyourvisit/hyperion.htm) says off-trail travel toward Hyperion has damaged vegetation and the tree's base. It asks visitors to use designated trails and describes a closure to protect park resources. The [Redwood National Park superintendent's compendium](https://www.nps.gov/redw/learn/management/superintendent-s-compendium.htm) records the closure and attributes it to social trails and resource degradation. These are direct park-management records, suitable for the access-policy premise.

**Claim boundary:** These records support a documented closure and the park's account of visitor impacts. They do not establish that officials concealed the tree's location, that every attempted visit led to a fine, a specific fine amount, or an independent estimate of ecological damage. Do not reproduce the coordinates in the closure order. Recheck the current compendium and conditions page before scripting because access rules can change.
