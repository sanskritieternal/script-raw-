## BANK-461-044 - UK Civil Service Success Profiles

- Primary hiring authority: UK Cabinet Office, *Success Profiles: Civil Service behaviours*, updated January 29, 2025, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/success-profiles/success-profiles-civil-service-behaviours (inspected 2026-10-24). The page distinguishes behaviours from ability, technical skill, strengths and experience. It defines Working together and provides examples by grade; hiring managers select behaviours relevant to each vacancy, and candidates may be asked for examples.
- Version context: GOV.UK *Success Profiles* publication collection, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/success-profiles (inspected 2026-10-24), identifies the current family of guidance. Check the specific vacancy and date before asserting requirements in any selection exercise.
- Limits: This is a UK central-government framework, not a universal workplace competency standard. Published assessment descriptors do not prove predictive validity, fair application or hiring outcomes. Role/grade, accessibility adjustments and local selection practices matter. It is not individualized application or legal advice.
