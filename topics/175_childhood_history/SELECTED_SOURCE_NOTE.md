## BANK-175-045 - French school phone rules

France's official legal database publishes [Law No. 2018-698 of 3 August 2018](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000037284333/) on mobile-phone use in school establishments. The Education Ministry's [2018 implementation circular](https://www.education.gouv.fr/bo/18/Hebdo35/MENE1826081C.htm) explains the amended Education Code article L. 511-5 and school/college application. These are primary legal and administrative texts for France at that date. Check amendments and school-level exceptions before a current-law claim; the texts state the rule and rationale, not causal evidence of improved attention or mental health.

## BANK-175-046 - Adventure playground archives

Copenhagen's municipal image archive holds an [Emdrup junk-playground photograph dated August 1943](https://kbhbilleder.dk/kbh-arkiv/91771). The [London Museum Henry Grant photograph of Lollard Adventure Playground](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/v/object-795231/lollard-adventure-playground-lambeth/) is dated around 1955; its object record places the site on a bombed school and describes loose building materials. These are primary visual objects plus curatorial metadata, limited by camera framing, selection and later cataloging. They show specific spaces but cannot establish how every child played or how frequently injuries occurred. Obtain permission before reproducing image files; a research citation does not grant rights. The separate 1946 Picture Post account by Marjory Allen needs direct edition access before quoting or treating her advocacy as a child's own testimony.
