# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-201-037: Royal FloraHolland Aalsmeer

- Royal FloraHolland, "Reflection on the 2026 Spring Peak," 6 July 2026: https://www.royalfloraholland.com/en/news-2026/week-28/reflection-on-the-2026-spring-peak-2
- Operator reports 102,000 flower transactions on its busiest Aalsmeer day, compared with 93,000 in 2025, and says transaction granularity plus staffing pressure contributed to late delivery on peak days. These are operator-reported figures and explanations for a particular season, not independently audited causal findings or permanent auction throughput. Separate from BANK-201-027 transport and BANK-201-032 cut-flower handling.

## BANK-201-038: ghost orchid

- US Fish and Wildlife Service, 4 June 2025 proposal announcement: https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2025-06/ghost-orchid-proposed-endangered-species-act-protection
- Federal Register proposed rule, 5 June 2025, 90 FR 23869: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-06-05/pdf/2025-10191.pdf
- These sources document a *proposed* listing and threats including poaching and habitat degradation; they do not establish that a final listing is in force on the research date. Recheck the live listing docket before production. Avoid specimen coordinates, collection guidance and claims about all populations from US estimates.
