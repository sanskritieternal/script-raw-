# Selected observing-program source note — oceanography

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-362-040`. Live array counts and quality-control statistics can change.

- [Argo international program, *What makes a float part of Argo?*](https://argo.ucsd.edu/about/what-makes-a-float-part-of-argo/): describes an approximately 4,000-float global network, nominal ten-day profiles from surface to 2,000 m measuring temperature, salinity and pressure, shared data and quality-control expectations. Program extensions use extra sensors or sample deeper water.
- [Argo, implementation status](https://argo.ucsd.edu/about/status/): dates deployments from 2000, notes the July 2024 third-millionth profile, and identifies spatial gaps and the original open-ocean emphasis, with evolving coverage near seasonal ice and marginal seas.

Both program pages were accessible. They are operator descriptions of a changing network, not a guarantee of evenly spaced observations or complete coverage at every depth and time. A float profile is not a direct basin-wide current measurement; gridded fields require quality control and interpolation. Current counts and data-release modes must be rechecked for a production script.
