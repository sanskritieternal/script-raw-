# BANK-006-136 — The Wow! signal as a measured anomaly

[Return to the main niche bank](../IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-006-136).

This is a bounded source note for a proposed episode, not an identification of the signal's origin. The preserved queue title calls it a “72-second deep-space transmission”; the printout alone does not establish a transmitter or a distance.

## Inspected primary records

- [Jerry R. Ehman's Big Ear 30th-anniversary technical report](https://www.bigear.org/Wow30th/wow30th.htm), revised in 2010, reproduces and explains the observatory printout. Ehman says he found `6EQUJ5` in channel 2 during a later review of the August 15, 1977 data, then marked “Wow!” in red. The six characters encode truncated signal-to-noise values in successive approximately 12-second rows, rather than text transmitted by a source. Ehman distinguishes the roughly 72-second sequence from the approximately 37-second half-power beam width, discusses coordinate uncertainty, and reports that the signal did not recur in the reviewed subsequent data.
- [Gray and Ellingsen's published follow-up search, *Astrophysical Journal* 578 (2002)](https://www.bigear.org/Gray-Ellingsen.pdf) reports no similar signal in observations dwelling up to 14 hours at the reported coordinates. The authors state what their sensitivity and dwell time constrain and explicitly leave longer-period, nonperiodic, or out-of-band emission possible. A non-detection in that search does not identify the 1977 source.

## Claim boundary and next research

The episode can show how an instrument-generated code becomes a candidate astronomical anomaly, then how a non-detection narrows specific repeat models. It should not turn the code into a message, claim the signal was recorded as audible sound, or say a celestial or intelligent origin was proved. Before scripting, inspect the original printout image and instrument setup in Ehman's report, the follow-up paper's observing table and frequency coverage, and later independent searches and proposed natural explanations. Date any statement that the signal has “never” repeated.
