# BANK-006-145 — Claimed CMB Hawking points and reanalysis

[Return to the main niche bank](../IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-006-145).

This is a bounded source note for a proposed comparison, not a conclusion that a previous cosmic aeon was observed. The preserved queue title asks what came before the Big Bang; conformal cyclic cosmology is one proposed framework, not an established answer.

## Inspected research

- [An, Meissner, Nurowski and Penrose, “Apparent evidence for Hawking points in the CMB Sky,” *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 495 (2020)](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/495/3/3403/5838759) reports a search for anomalous circular temperature features in CMB maps and interprets them as candidate “Hawking points” predicted within conformal cyclic cosmology. The paper's statistical claim depends on its feature definition, scale choices, sky processing and simulations.
- [Jow and Scott, “Re-evaluating evidence for Hawking points in the CMB,” author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09672) repeats a related Planck-map search and finds that a signal appearing strong at selected ring sizes drops to about 87% confidence, roughly one standard deviation, when the ring-size search is accounted for. They conclude that this does not provide statistically significant evidence for Hawking points. This is an independent analysis of a proposed signature, not a proof that every possible cyclic model is false.

## Claim boundary and next research

The episode can compare what each team counted, which ring sizes and masks they used, and why searching over multiple choices changes a significance statement. It must separate the mathematical CCC model from the empirical claim that a particular CMB feature confirms it. Before scripting, inspect both full methods, figure definitions, simulation inputs, foreground handling and later independent analyses, and date the evidence status. Avoid a visual that labels ordinary CMB fluctuations as images of a previous universe.
