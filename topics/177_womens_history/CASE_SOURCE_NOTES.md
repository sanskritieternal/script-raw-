# Women's-history case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary legal, archival and research records support bounded questions. The original pitches remain archived, including overclaims and retired `BANK-177-013`; these notes do not authenticate every claim in them.

| Case | Primary source | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Credit access | [1974 Congressional Record text](https://www.congress.gov/93/crecb/1974/10/04/GPO-CRECB-1974-pt25-5.pdf) and [Federal Reserve's ECOA text](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/regulations/equal-credit-opportunity-title-v-title-vii.htm) document sex and marital-status protections; [National Archives account](https://prologue.blogs.archives.gov/2023/03/22/on-the-basis-of-sex-equal-credit-opportunities/) provides archival context. | “Women could not get any credit card before 1974” is false as a universal statement. Passage, effective rules, issuer practice and actual access are distinct. |
| Iceland 1975 | [Women's History Archives of Iceland event archive](https://kvennasogusafn.is/en/facts-and-trivia/womens-day-off-1975/) documents organizing, coalition, materials and the 24 October action. Its [50-year retrospective](https://50ar.kvennasogusafn.is/kvennafri/) attributes a 90% estimate to organizers. | The participation share is an organizer estimate, not a census; “paralyzed the nation” and direct causal claims require independent evidence. Distinguish paid and unpaid work. |
| Goldin attribution | [2023 Academy prize decision](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2023/press-release/) recognizes Claudia Goldin's research into women's labor-market outcomes. | A prize summary is not proof of a single universal “real reason” for each wage gap; route to the existing motherhood-penalty question with specific study and population checks. |
| Lizzie Magie / Mosuo overlap | [Magie's 1904 patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US748626A/en) establishes her named game-board design. [Mosuo field study](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12125) examines fathers' roles in one matrilineal population. | The game origin does not alone settle all credit/ownership claims. Mosuo kinship does not mean there are no fathers or husbands; avoid exoticizing the community. |
