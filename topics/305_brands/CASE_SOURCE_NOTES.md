# Selected institutional source note — brands

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-305-042`. This is a bounded U.S. product/brand episode premise, not proof of a universal lesson in consumer behavior.

- [The Coca-Cola Company, New Coke retrospective](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/about-us/history/new-coke-the-most-memorable-marketing-blunder-ever): dates the reformulated launch to 23 April 1985 and original-formula return to 11 July, a 79-day interval. The company's retrospective account is a participant narrative and may emphasize its preferred interpretation of consumer response.
- [The Coca-Cola Company, *A Short History of Coca-Cola* booklet](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/content/dam/journey/us/en/our-company/history/coca-cola-a-short-history-125-years-booklet.pdf): independently within the company chronology identifies the 1985 formula change and subsequent Coca-Cola Classic name.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Coca-Cola Classic can](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_880980): catalogues a material object associated with the Classic relaunch. A museum object and company chronology establish packaging/name evidence, not representative consumer sentiment or causal sales estimates.

The company and Smithsonian pages were accessible. Original 1985 research datasets, advertising briefs and retailer sales records were not checked. Name, product formula, package and consumer preference are different evidentiary claims; mark geography and date for each. This brand-meaning case stays in 305, while product formulation in 306 and advertising execution in 307 require separate questions.
