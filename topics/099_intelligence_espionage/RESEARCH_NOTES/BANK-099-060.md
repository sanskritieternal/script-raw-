# BANK-099-060 — UKUSA source check

Checked 2026-10-24. This note establishes a narrow agreement chronology only.

The [NSA/GCHQ 2010 release announcement](https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/1629812/declassified-ukusa-signals-intelligence-agreement-documents-available/) identifies the March 1946 bilateral BRUSA/UKUSA agreement, followed by Canada in 1948 and Australia and New Zealand in 1956. The [NSA release catalog](https://www.nsa.gov/Helpful-Links/NSA-FOIA/Declassification-Transparency-Initiatives/Historical-Releases/UKUSA/) lists the 5 March 1946 outline, appendices and revisions. The linked scanned outline PDF returned 403 during inspection, so exact clauses were **not read** and should not be quoted from this note.

The archived headline's claim that one postwar five-party pact “monitors global communications” overstates what these released pages prove. A production script would need the original agreement pages, later amendments, applicable law and dated oversight reports before discussing specific sharing powers or current collection. No current operational details or idea readiness are certified; `verified_sources` remains empty.
