# Selected backpacking cases - 2026-10-24

These notes bound proposed case questions. They are not real-time trail conditions or a trip-safety certification.

## BANK-188-038 - Yosemite wilderness food storage

- Yosemite National Park's [bear and food-storage page](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/bears.htm) says bear-resistant canisters are required for overnight hikers throughout Yosemite wilderness and counterbalance hangs are no longer legal there. Its [wilderness-regulation page](https://home.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/wildregs.htm) lists the covered food and scented items and allowed storage categories.
- **Claim limit:** This is a Yosemite rule at the accessed date, not a rule for every national park or a finding that every bear defeats every hang. Check the current superintendent's compendium and official area notices before any route-specific statement. The record supports legal scope, not an experiment comparing container performance.
- **Overlap:** BANK-188-019 owns the general bear-container question; the new case is one park's named regulation and how a general pitch must be localized.

## BANK-188-039 - 2023 Whitewater River notice

- The Pacific Crest Trail Association's [24 March 2023 condition notice](https://www.pcta.org/discover-the-trail/trail-condition/whitewater-river-caution/) identifies the unbridged Whitewater River crossing near PCT mile 220 as running high after winter snow and rain. PCTA's [2023 annual review](https://www.pcta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/PCTA-2023-EOY-Report.pdf) describes high Sierra snowpack and separate Southern California storm damage.
- **Claim limit:** Whitewater is in Southern California, not a Sierra Nevada crossing. The March notice is historical, not a present condition report or risk ranking. The archived “deadliest hazard” hook needs a comparable incident dataset and location-specific dates; do not turn this note into crossing technique.
- **Overlap:** BANK-188-018 covers broader PCT season transitions; this case tracks a named date, drainage and trail notice. BANK-189-034 owns the general changing-crossing principle for day hikes.
