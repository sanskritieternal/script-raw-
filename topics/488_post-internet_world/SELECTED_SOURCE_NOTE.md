# Selected source note — NYC Mesh community network

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-488-043. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA or post-Internet transition claim.

## Primary network records

- NYC Mesh official site: https://www.nycmesh.net/ . The volunteer-run organization describes a community-owned network that connects New Yorkers to the Internet, including rooftop antennas and building connections; this is evidence of its stated model, not independent performance verification.
- NYC Mesh network overview: https://wiki.nycmesh.net/books/1-introduction/page/nyc-mesh-overview . It identifies the project as a community network providing Internet access rather than replacing the global Internet.
- NYC Mesh volunteer documentation: https://www.nycmesh.net/help . It lists maintenance, rooftop-node installation, fiber, routing and monitoring work that makes the alternative operational rather than purely an interface idea.

## Evidence, date and access limits

Ownership of local equipment and volunteer governance do not prove independence from upstream links, guaranteed service during an outage, full neighborhood coverage, affordability for every household, or lack of all centralized dependencies. These pages are operator accounts; an episode claiming reliability, reach or cost effects needs independent dated measurements and local context. Map/price/service details can change. Do not reproduce identifiable member information or network diagrams without checking permissions.
