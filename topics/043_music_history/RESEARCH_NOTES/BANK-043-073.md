# BANK-043-073 — Longplayer's duration and source score

Reviewed 2026-10-24. The preserved `trending_topics/6` calls *Longplayer* a “10-hour song” that will not finish until 2999.

## Inspected creator and custodian records

- The [Longplayer Trust overview](https://longplayer.org/about/overview/) identifies Jem Finer's work as a **1,000-year composition** that began at the 1999/2000 turn and is designed to play without repetition until the end of 2999, then begin its cycle again. Its long-term continuity is a project aim that depends on ongoing stewardship.
- Jem Finer's [2009 composer's note](https://longplayer.org/texts/composers-note-1st-live-performance-12-september-2009/) describes six related pieces derived from an original **20-minute-20-second** source composition. Six two-minute sections, one from each transposition, sound simultaneously, selected by precise rules so the combination does not repeat for 1,000 years.
- The Trust's [survival account](https://longplayer.org/about/survival/) describes live performances and institutional maintenance. A 1,000-minute (16-hour-40-minute) live passage is not the total duration.

## Claim disposition

The archived “10-hour song” description is inaccurate. Distinguish the short source material, a performed excerpt, the rule-generated 1,000-year cycle and the contingent ability of custodians to keep it playing. A selected episode should inspect the graphic score and versioned playback records before claiming continuous performance at a particular location or exact future completion.
