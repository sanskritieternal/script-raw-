#### BANK-206-010 — The Architecture of Aridity: How Atmospheric Physics Creates the World's Great Deserts

**Video question:** Why are almost all of the world's great deserts lined up in two parallel bands around 30 degrees north and south of the equator?

**Individual coverage scope:** Equatorial solar heating, rising moist air creating tropical rainforests, high-altitude cooling, and descending dry air masses (Hadley Cells) creating permanent subtropical high-pressure deserts.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-206-011"></a>
#### BANK-206-011 — Dune Physics: How Wind Sculpts Barchans, Seifs, and Towering Star Dunes

**Video question:** How do wind direction, sand supply, and fluid mechanics shape moving crescent barchans and 1,000-foot multi-armed star dunes?

**Individual coverage scope:** Saltation sand grain hopping physics, the critical angle of repose (34 degrees) on slip faces, unidirectional wind creating crescent barchans, and multi-directional winds forming star dunes.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-206-012"></a>
#### BANK-206-012 — The Rain Shadow Effect: How Mountains Wring Out Moisture to Create Arid Basins

**Video question:** Why is the western side of the Sierra Nevada covered in lush giant sequoias while the eastern side is bone-dry Death Valley?

**Individual coverage scope:** Orographic lifting, adiabatic cooling and condensation on windward slopes, dry air descending leeward slopes undergoing adiabatic warming, and basin-and-range topography.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-206-013"></a>
#### BANK-206-013 — Desert Varnish: The Black Manganese Oxide Glaze Coating Canyon Petroglyphs

**Video question:** What created the shiny, paper-thin dark coating on Southwestern canyon walls that allowed ancient humans to carve petroglyphs?

**Individual coverage scope:** Manganese- and iron-oxidizing rock bacteria, clay particle wind adhesion, micro-lamination growth rates (1 micron per millennium), and dating ancient native rock art.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-206-014"></a>
#### BANK-206-014 — Flash Floods in Dry Washes: Why Dry Soil Resists Water and Creates Lethal Walls

**Video question:** Why do dry desert canyons become deadly torrents of water under clear blue skies when rain fell 20 miles away?

**Individual coverage scope:** Hydrophobic sun-baked soil crusted with clay, lack of absorbing vegetation, rapid runoff into narrow slot canyons, hydraulic bore wave velocities, and arroyo survival rules.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.
