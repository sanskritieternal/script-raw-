## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Economic Crises: The Conditions Before a Crisis**
   - *Question:* Which vulnerabilities were visible before one economic crisis began?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated credit, output, price, and policy records without hindsight. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
2. **Economic Crises: A Crisis Often Used as a Template**
   - *Question:* Which features of a familiar crisis do not generalize to another?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare definitions, institutions, and transmission channels. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
3. **Economic Crises: Households Outside the Aggregate**
   - *Question:* Whose losses disappear inside a national crisis statistic?
   - *Key Focus:* Use disaggregated data and attributed lived accounts. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
4. **Economic Crises: What Counts as an Economic Crisis?**
   - *Question:* How do recession, banking crisis, inflation shock, and sovereign distress differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Apply explicit definitions to one dated case. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
5. **Economic Crises: A Shock Becomes a Feedback Loop**
   - *Question:* How did one initial shock amplify through credit, prices, or expectations?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace a proposed mechanism with dated evidence and alternatives. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Economic Crises: Why Did This Crisis Spread?**
   - *Question:* Which links in a crisis transmission chain remain disputed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare competing explanations and missing data. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
7. **Economic Crises: A Financial Tool in a Crisis**
   - *Question:* Did one financial technology dampen or amplify a shock?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare its actual use, exposures, and measured effects. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
8. **Economic Crises: Decision Makers Under Pressure**
   - *Question:* What did officials know when they chose a crisis response?
   - *Key Focus:* Use contemporaneous minutes, statements, and later reviews. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
9. **Economic Crises: The Week a Crisis Escalated**
   - *Question:* Which events changed expectations during one bounded crisis window?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated timeline from primary records and market data. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
10. **Economic Crises: From Shock to Job Loss**
   - *Question:* How did one shock travel from institutions to employment?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace credit, demand, business decisions, and time lags. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Economic Crises: Recovery and Relapse**
   - *Question:* Why did one economy recover and then weaken again?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare output, jobs, prices, and policy by dated period. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
12. **Economic Crises: Two Crises, Same Measures**
   - *Question:* Which difference matters most when comparing two economic crises?
   - *Key Focus:* Hold definitions and denominators constant. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
13. **Economic Crises: Stress Signals, Not Predictions**
   - *Question:* Which measured vulnerability would worsen a hypothetical future shock?
   - *Key Focus:* State scenario assumptions and avoid predicting a crisis date. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
14. **Economic Crises: Testing a One-Cause Crisis Story**
   - *Question:* Does a popular single-cause explanation fit the evidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare multiple contributing mechanisms and their timing. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
15. **Economic Crises: One Firm Through a Crisis**
   - *Question:* What do records reveal about one firm's response to a downturn?
   - *Key Focus:* Use filings, employment records, and worker accounts in context. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Economic Crises: Contract Terms That Amplified Losses**
   - *Question:* Which loan or funding term worsened a documented crisis exposure?
   - *Key Focus:* Read dated contracts and separate borrower from system effects. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
17. **Economic Crises: Income Shock in a Household**
   - *Question:* How did unemployment or price changes affect a defined household budget?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a labeled illustration supported by period data. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
18. **Economic Crises: The Timing of Crisis Losses**
   - *Question:* Why can short funding and long assets turn a shock into a run?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a simplified balance sheet and an identified crisis case. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
19. **Economic Crises: Fees and Penalties During Distress**
   - *Question:* How did contract charges affect vulnerable borrowers in one crisis?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated terms and measured incidence; avoid generalized advice. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
20. **Economic Crises: Protections Introduced After a Crisis**
   - *Question:* Which rule changed after a crisis, and whom did it cover?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare law, implementation, and documented exclusions. Evidence base: dated macroeconomic series, policy records, financial disclosures, scholarly analyses, and attributed accounts.
