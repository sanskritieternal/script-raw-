## BANK-302-041 — US applications and formations

- The [Census Business Formation Statistics definitions](https://www.census.gov/econ_file/econ/bfs/definitions.html) distinguish a filtered EIN business application, a high-propensity application and an observed employer formation within four quarters. The [Census methodology](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/technicaldocumentation/methodology.html) describes classification, projection and definition revisions.
- Choose a dated vintage, geography, sector and denominator before comparing counts. An application does not prove that a business opened, operated, hired or survived. High propensity is a classification, not an individual's probability. Review Census revisions and underlying data tables before any trend or “wave” claim.
- This is measurement for niche 302's broad entrepreneurship population, distinct from a startup's own cohort and unit economics in 303 or a corporate history in 301.
