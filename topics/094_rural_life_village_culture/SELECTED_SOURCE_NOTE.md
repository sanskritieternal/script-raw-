## Semantic and representation review

Existing 094 proposals cover vernacular houses, shared grazing, craft income, festivals, oral narratives, migration, return enterprise, filming omissions and poverty imagery. Repeated pitches route there. Neighboring 024/091/093/192 banks were inspected for food, farming and hive mechanics; this bank keeps residents, governance, access and practice context primary. The telling-the-bees case treats folklore separately from apiculture. A foraging story does not identify edible species for viewers, and a healing archive does not provide medical advice. Film consent must include intended platform, sacred or private restrictions, fair payment and the right to decline. Municipal prices, housing access, health findings and local policy change; verify them at production.
