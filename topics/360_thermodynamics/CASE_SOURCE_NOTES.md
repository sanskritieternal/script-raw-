# Selected primary metrology source note — thermodynamics

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-360-040`.

- [BIPM, SI kelvin definition](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/kelvin): defines the kelvin by fixing the numerical value of Boltzmann constant `k` to 1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K and explains the link between thermodynamic temperature and energy scale.
- [BIPM, historical perspective on kelvin](https://www.bipm.org/en/history-si/kelvin): contrasts the former water triple-point definition with the 26th CGPM's 2018 resolution. [BIPM SI overview](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/) dates the revised SI's effective start to 20 May 2019.

The BIPM pages were accessible and authoritative for the unit definition. A fixed defining constant does not imply that all practical temperature measurements became exact, that calibration uncertainty disappeared, or that temperature equals total thermal energy. Implementation methods and instrument error need separate metrology records. This is a unit-history case, not a procedure for calibrating or repairing a thermometer.
