## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

   - *Key Focus:* Trace versions and local audience context. Evidence base: identified scripts and recordings, drafts, rehearsal and production records, dated reviews, and bounded audience evidence.
2. **Comedy Stories: A Comic Scene Revisited**
   - *Question:* How does setup, expectation, and turn work in a named scene?
   - *Key Focus:* Analyze exact beats, delivery, and audience information. Evidence base: identified scripts and recordings, drafts, rehearsal and production records, dated reviews, and bounded audience evidence.
3. **Comedy Stories: Collaborators Behind a Joke**
   - *Question:* Whose writing, performance, or edit shaped a familiar comic moment?
   - *Key Focus:* Check drafts, credits, rehearsals, and production records. Evidence base: identified scripts and recordings, drafts, rehearsal and production records, dated reviews, and bounded audience evidence.
4. **Comedy Stories: What Makes a Setup Pay Off?**
