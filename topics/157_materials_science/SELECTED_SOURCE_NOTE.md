# Selected source note - niche 157

Checked 2026-10-24. The following original studies and preprint support bounded evidence questions. They do not approve any BANK proposal or verify all archive claims.

## BANK-157-046 - LK-99

Lee et al., [*The First Room-Temperature Ambient-Pressure Superconductor*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12008) (2023 preprint), is the original claim, not established evidence of a superconducting state. Zhu et al., [*First-order transition in LK-99 containing Cu2S*](https://www.iop.cas.cn/xwzx/kydt/202311/P020231128650554216959.pdf) (Matter, 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.matt.2023.11.001), report original replication measurements and attribute a transition in their samples to a Cu2S impurity. Compare synthesis, phase identification, electrical contacts, field response and sample-to-sample variation before saying exactly which claimed signatures failed. The paper addresses its tested samples and measurements; it does not prove that every imaginable lead-apatite variant is impossible.

## BANK-157-047 - AlON armor

[*Ballistic testing of transparent armour ceramics*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0955221908002033) (Journal of the European Ceramic Society, 2009) reports original ballistic experiments and identifies aluminum oxynitride among transparent ceramic candidates. Access to the full test tables was not confirmed in this pass. Obtain projectile, impact speed, target layering and mass before comparing armor performance. AlON is a ceramic, not transparent metallic aluminum. No sample is universally "bulletproof" absent a specified threat and configuration.

## BANK-157-049 - Clearcoat scratches

[*Fast, Localized, and Low-Energy Consumption Self-Healing of Automotive Clearcoats Using a Photothermal Effect Triggered by NIR Radiation*](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsapm.1c01768) (ACS Applied Polymer Materials, 2022) reports a formulated experimental clearcoat and scratch-healing under focused irradiation; the abstract reports heating around 70 C for one formulation. This is a laboratory surface-scratch result, not proof that ordinary sun exposure repairs deep paint damage on a production car. Check full test protocol, repeated-cycle behavior and coating durability before using the reported recovery figure.
