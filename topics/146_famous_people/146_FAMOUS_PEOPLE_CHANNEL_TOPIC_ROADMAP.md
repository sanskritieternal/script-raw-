## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Famous People across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Defining Fame, Mass Media Evolution & Primary Sources (Beginner / Scratch)** — Mechanisms of public renown from antiquity to print media, distinguishing verifiable public records from press folklore, archival documentation, and early mass-circulation portraiture.
- **Tier 2: The Architecture of Celebrity, Image Making & Public Relations (Intermediate)** — 19th and 20th-century press agentry (P.T. Barnum, Edward Bernays), Hollywood studio star contracts, photojournalism flashbulbs, paparazzi dynamics, and television charisma.
- **Tier 3: Scandals, Re-evaluations & Historical Myth vs Reality (Upper Intermediate)** — The anatomy of the public downfall, unsealing classified government archives, ghostwritten autobiographies vs investigative exposés, and psychological tolls of parasocial idolatry.
- **Tier 4: Cultural Immortality, Algorithmic Virality & Posthumous Estates (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Posthumous licensing rights and synthetic avatar ethics, algorithmic micro-celebrity life cycles, deconstructing great man historical fallacies, and long-term legacy stewardship.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The First Modern Celebrity: How Lord Byron Invented Viral Fame in 1812**
   - *Question:* How did a 24-year-old poet wake up to find himself internationally worshipped, creating Byromania and the template for modern fame?
   - *Key Focus:* Childe Harold's Pilgrimage publishing sensation, Regency salon fandom, merchandising of collars and portraits, scandal management, and the dark cost of persona.
2. **The Mechanics of Renown: From Roman Triumphs to Hollywood Red Carpets**
   - *Question:* How has the staging of public greatness evolved from laurel crowns and coin minting to red carpet paparazzi flashbulbs?
   - *Key Focus:* Imperial Roman triumph processions, Renaissance courtly portraiture, 18th-century theatre handbills, and the golden age studio system glamour apparatus.
3. **Deconstructing the Persona: How Public Relations Invented the Star Identity**
   - *Question:* Where does the human being end and the manufactured commercial archetype begin in public figure branding?
   - *Key Focus:* Edward Bernays and early publicity theory, studio morality clauses, scripted talk show anecdotes, stage names, and controlled leak strategies.
4. **Cleopatra's Propaganda: How Octavian Painted the Queen into History**
   - *Question:* How much of what the modern world believes about Cleopatra VII was Roman imperial wartime smear propaganda?
   - *Key Focus:* Plutarch and Cassius Dio records, Augustan poetry slander campaigns, coinage realism vs Hollywood romanticism, and her intellectual multilingual mastery.
5. **The Fame Machine: How Press Agents Fabricated 19th-Century Folk Heroes**
   - *Question:* How did Ned Buntline turn Buffalo Bill Cody from an ordinary scout into a globally revered Wild West icon?
   - *Key Focus:* Dime novel exaggeration circuits, traveling frontier spectacles, exaggerated shootouts, and the commercialization of living mythologies.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Paparazzi Revolution: The 1960 Flashbulb Moment That Stripped Privacy**
   - *Question:* How did Fellini's film La Dolce Vita name and catalyze a relentless new breed of freelance photojournalists in Rome?
   - *Key Focus:* Tazio Secchiaroli street ambushes, long telephoto lens innovations, tabloid bidding wars, and the legal erosion of public-space privacy.
7. **Steve Jobs vs Thomas Edison: Innovators as Media Mythologies**
   - *Question:* How did both Edison and Jobs stage keynote product reveals to transform technical teamwork into singular visionary wizardry?
   - *Key Focus:* Menlo Park press demonstrations vs Macworld keynotes, reality distortion fields, patent attribution politics, and narrative showmanship.
8. **Marilyn Monroe: The Dangerous Chasm Between Norma Jeane and the Icon**
   - *Question:* What psychological toll occurs when an individual must continuously perform an idealized hyper-sexualized public alter-ego?
   - *Key Focus:* Method acting discipline with Lee Strasberg, studio exploitation, psychological fragmentation, private journal revelations, and media commodification.
9. **Posthumous Commodification: Who Really Owns Dead Celebrities' Images?**
   - *Question:* Can a dead icon rest in peace when corporate estate holding companies license their CGI avatars for commercials and tours?
   - *Key Focus:* Post-mortem right of publicity laws, Marilyn Monroe, Elvis Presley, and Michael Jackson estate monetization, hologram concerts, and AI voice cloning.
10. **The 15 Minutes of Fame: How Warhol Anticipated Algorithmic Virality**
   - *Question:* Why did Andy Warhol's 1968 prediction about democratized fleeting celebrity prove to be the blueprint for the internet era?
   - *Key Focus:* The Factory Superstars phenomenon, silk-screen serial repetition, democratized spotlight economics, and today's TikTok viral influencer turnover.
### Tier 3: Advanced Methodologies & Frameworks
11. **The Fall From Grace: The Anatomy of the Public Downfall**
   - *Question:* What structural dynamics cause public admiration to reverse into collective schadenfreude and ritualistic public shaming?
   - *Key Focus:* The hubris-scandal trajectory, 24-hour cable news feeding frenzies, social media cancelation cascades, corporate sponsor desertion, and redemption tours.
12. **Alexander the Great and the Coins of Immortality: Ancient Image Control**
   - *Question:* Why did Alexander permit only one sculptor, Lysippos, to cast his image across his sprawling empire?
   - *Key Focus:* Anasyrma hairstyle styling, royal tetradrachm currency dissemination, deification iconography (horns of Ammon), and timeless imperial branding.
13. **The Parasocial Bond: Why Strangers Weep for People They Never Met**
   - *Question:* Why did millions globally mourn Princess Diana or Robin Williams with the visceral grief normally reserved for close kin?
   - *Key Focus:* Horton and Wohl's parasocial interaction framework, mirror neuron emotional investment, shared generational milestones, and media intimacy illusion.
14. **Scientific Superstars: Einstein, Curie, and the Myth of the Lone Genius**
   - *Question:* How does mass media elevate individual scientists while erasing the collaborative networks and laboratory teams behind them?
   - *Key Focus:* The 1919 eclipse expedition press circus, Einstein's tongue-out portrait commodification, Marie Curie's American tour, and research community erasure.
15. **The Political Stage: How Television Rewrote Modern Statesmanship**
   - *Question:* Why did radio listeners think Nixon won while TV viewers overwhelmingly gave victory to John F. Kennedy in 1960?
   - *Key Focus:* Sweat, suit contrast, camera angles, makeup refusal vs tan telegenic poise, and the permanent shift from policy oratory to visual optics.
16. **Censorship, NDAs, and Biographers: Uncovering Buried Truths**
   - *Question:* How do non-disclosure agreements, legal injunctions, and protective family trusts suppress the real histories of famous figures?
   - *Key Focus:* Authorized vs unauthorized biographies, FOIA archival requests, cross-verifying tax and court dockets, and breaking through corporate PR walls.
### Tier 4: Enterprise Strategy & Deep Mastery
17. **The Cult of the Anti-Hero: How Infamous Outlaws Became Folk Legends**
   - *Question:* Why do societies romanticize violent figures like Ned Kelly, Billy the Kid, and Bonnie and Clyde into celebrated folk heroes?
   - *Key Focus:* Agrarian discontent, resistance to state authority symbols, ballad traditions, Hollywood mythologizing, and criminal charisma.
18. **Post-Truth Celebrity: Synthetic Avatars and Deepfake Likeness Rights**
   - *Question:* What happens to fame when generative AI can synthesize flawless digital performances of stars living or dead without their presence?
   - *Key Focus:* SAG-AFTRA digital replica contracts, biometric copyright legislation, virtual influencers (Lil Miquela), and the uncoupling of fame from biological mortality.
19. **The Architectural Megastructure of Celebrity Endorsements**
   - *Question:* How did Michael Jordan's 1984 Nike sneaker contract redefine the financial scale and power dynamics of global sports fame?
   - *Key Focus:* Revenue royalties vs flat fee endorsements, sneaker culture economics, equity stakes, brand ambassadorship contracts, and billionaire athlete empires.
20. **The Masterclass on Legacy: How History Truly Decides Who is Remembered**
   - *Question:* Why do 99% of household names vanish within three generations while a tiny fraction echo across centuries?
   - *Key Focus:* Institutions, physical monuments, archival survival, curriculum adoption, literary adaptability, and the ruthless filtering of human memory.
