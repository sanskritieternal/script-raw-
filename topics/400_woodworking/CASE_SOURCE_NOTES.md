# Selected primary-source note — woodworking

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-400-044`.

- [US Patent 19,405, John Henry Belter, “Improvement in the method of manufacturing furniture” (23 February 1858)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US19405A/en): specification and drawings describe a chair back, layered pressed-work staves, shaping and curved assembly. Belter claims improvement in appearance and strength, and expressly distinguishes his claims from simple veneer pressing or cross-grain gluing.

The patent documents Belter’s asserted method and claim boundary, not an independently measured strength result, the first historical use of bentwood, the number of chairs made, or who performed workshop labor. The patent page is OCR-transcribed; verify quotations against the original PDF. This is history and design analysis, not operating instructions for presses, cutting tools or adhesives.
