## BANK-052-058 — Fallingwater’s cantilevers (archive pending 9)

- The Western Pennsylvania Conservancy’s [preservation history](https://fallingwater.org/preservation-history/) describes measured deflection, structural analysis, temporary shoring and post-tensioning of the reinforced-concrete terraces. Its [project timeline](https://fallingwater.org/history/timeline/) records the repair sequence.
- Editorial boundary: describe a monitored structural intervention and its continuing preservation. The archive’s “gamble” wording should not replace engineering evidence or imply the whole house was one failed cantilever.

## BANK-052-061 — Sydney Opera House shells (archive pending 12)

- The Sydney Opera House’s [spherical-solution history](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/the-spherical-solution) describes the roof’s evolution through parabolic and ellipsoid studies to spherical geometry. The institution’s [conservation-management plan](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/sites/default/files/collaborodam_assets/soh-cmp-interactive-1.pdf) dates the refined ribbed scheme and records contributions by Utzon and Arup. The [UNESCO listing](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/166/) identifies the interlocking shell groups and their role in the design.
- Editorial boundary: the repeatable spherical rib geometry was an important construction solution, but the project also involved foundations, interiors, politics and later design changes. Avoid the archived “hack saved the sails” as a complete account.

## BANK-052-072 — Roman concrete and crack repair (archive trending 23)

- Seymour and colleagues’ [2023 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.add1602) analyzes lime clasts in sampled Roman concrete and experiments with modern hot-mixed specimens. The [research-team account](https://news.mit.edu/2023/roman-concrete-durability-lime-casts-0106) explains the proposed self-healing mechanism and experiment.
- Editorial boundary: evidence for a repair mechanism in studied material does not show that every Roman structure, or the Pantheon specifically, gets stronger with each century. BANK-052-051 treats the Pantheon’s dome geometry and support as a separate architectural question.
