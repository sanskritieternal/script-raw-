# BANK-033-058 — K2-18 b atmospheric claim

Research check: 2026-10-24. Editorial proposal only; this note does not certify a biosignature or a life detection.

## Checked premise

JWST transmission-spectrum analyses of K2-18 b have produced disputed inferences about dimethyl sulfide (DMS) and/or dimethyl disulfide (DMDS). The 2025 mid-infrared analysis by Madhusudhan and colleagues reported approximately 3-sigma evidence for one or both candidate molecules. Independent reanalyses and alternative atmospheric models reported insufficient or unreliable evidence. A 2026 published systematic search by members of the original team still says robust gas identification needs more observations and false-positive work. Even confirmed DMS/DMDS would require nonbiological-source analysis before it could support life. The archived “alien gas” premise and prediction of near-term confirmation should not be treated as established.

## Source trail

- Madhusudhan et al., 2025, primary JWST/MIRI analysis, *New Constraints on DMS and DMDS in the Atmosphere of K2-18 b*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12267 . Reports candidate spectral inference at about 3 sigma.
- Schmidt et al., 2025, *Astronomical Journal*, independent NIRISS/NIRSpec reanalysis via the University of St Andrews repository: https://research-repository.st-andrews.ac.uk/handle/10023/33462 . Reports no reliable statistical DMS evidence in its analysis.
- Luque et al., 2025, joint NIRISS, NIRSpec and MIRI reanalysis: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13407 . Reports insufficient evidence for DMS/DMDS in combined analysis.
- Pica-Ciamarra et al., 2026, published systematic molecule search, Cambridge repository: https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/ca5278cc-e551-4ea4-8028-c706bf309e1f . Reports candidate alternatives and need for more observations and false-positive work.
