# BANK-033-056 — AARO historical report claim boundary

Research check: 2026-10-24. Editorial proposal only; no production approval or source-certification implied.

## Checked premise

The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office's *Historical Record Report, Volume I* was released publicly in March 2024. It reports no evidence that a US government investigation, academic research or official review panel has **confirmed** a UAP sighting as extraterrestrial technology. It also reports no empirical evidence for alleged government or private-company reverse engineering of extraterrestrial technology in the information reviewed. The report itself acknowledges unresolved sightings and limited or poor-quality data in many cases. Therefore the archived pitch's “formal verdict on alien tech” is an overstatement if it is presented as proof that extraterrestrial life or technology cannot exist. Its findings are attributed conclusions under a defined historical mandate and evidence set.
