## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Wedding Culture: Tracing One Wedding Custom**
   - *Question:* What can dated sources establish about a specific custom?
   - *Key Focus:* Name community and distinguish surviving evidence from absolute origin. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
2. **Wedding Culture: A Publicly Celebrated Wedding Rite**
   - *Question:* What does one well-known rite mean to current practitioners?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare participant accounts with outside portrayals. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
3. **Wedding Culture: Work Behind a Wedding**
   - *Question:* Whose making, cooking, care, and organizing go uncredited?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and account for paid and unpaid labor. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
4. **Wedding Culture: Reading a Wedding in Context**
   - *Question:* Which acts are legal, religious, familial, or celebratory?
   - *Key Focus:* Define jurisdiction, community, and participants' own terms. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
5. **Wedding Culture: Choice and Family Obligation**
   - *Question:* How do participants negotiate expectations around one practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use varied consented voices without assuming a shared experience. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Wedding Culture: A Disputed Customary Origin**
   - *Question:* Why do two accounts date a wedding practice differently?
   - *Key Focus:* Test archival survival, translation, and later retellings. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
7. **Wedding Culture: A Wedding Goes Online**
   - *Question:* How did streaming or digital booking alter participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine consent, access, cost, privacy, and family roles. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
8. **Wedding Culture: A Maker of Ceremony**
   - *Question:* How did one officiant, artist, or organizer shape a local wedding form?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated evidence and recognize collective authorship. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
9. **Wedding Culture: Wedding Practice After a Disruption**
   - *Question:* How did one law, migration, or crisis alter ceremonies?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate legal change from social practice with dated sources. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
10. **Wedding Culture: A Ceremony's Sequence**
   - *Question:* Who decides the order and meaning of one wedding?
   - *Key Focus:* Use participant-approved observation and explain variation. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Wedding Culture: A Custom's Changing Use**
   - *Question:* What evidence shows more or fewer families choosing it?
   - *Key Focus:* Check sampling, geography, class, and generational context. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
12. **Wedding Culture: One Wedding Practice Across Generations**
   - *Question:* What changed in roles, costs, and meaning?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable accounts without calling either form authentic. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
13. **Wedding Culture: Choices for Wedding Practice**
   - *Question:* Which adaptations do participants want?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare access, expense, belief, and intergenerational priorities. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
14. **Wedding Culture: A Universal Wedding Rule?**
   - *Question:* Is a familiar claim actually shared by all members of a community?
   - *Key Focus:* Test regional, religious, legal, and family variation. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
15. **Wedding Culture: One Wedding, Many Roles**
   - *Question:* How did participants experience a consented event differently?
   - *Key Focus:* Protect intimate details and distinguish observation from interpretation. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Wedding Culture: A Wedding Custom in Print**
   - *Question:* Who first described it for outsiders?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace author, language, date, purpose, and omissions. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
17. **Wedding Culture: Objects in a Wedding**
   - *Question:* What can clothing or gifts reveal about a ceremony?
   - *Key Focus:* Check maker, provenance, use, and participant meaning. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
18. **Wedding Culture: When Did This Rite Enter Weddings**
   - *Question:* Which records support rival dates?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish earlier object use from the named ceremonial form. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
19. **Wedding Culture: The Household Cost of Ceremony**
   - *Question:* How do families and workers bear time and expense?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts, price records, and varied income contexts. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
20. **Wedding Culture: Who Authorizes a Marriage**
   - *Question:* How do law, religious institutions, and families interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Name jurisdiction and date; compare formal rules with lived practice. Evidence base: participant-approved accounts, dated local records, material provenance, and current jurisdiction-specific marriage sources.
