# Research note: community case records

Checked 2026-10-24. These are bounded access notes for proposed BANK questions. They do not verify a complete episode or grant IDEA readiness.

## BANK-169-044 — lower Klamath dams and restoration

- Klamath River Renewal Corporation, [Dam Removal Comes to a Close](https://klamathrenewal.org/klamath-dam-removal-comes-to-a-close/), October 2, 2024. The organization overseeing removal reports completion of the four lower hydroelectric dam removals, identifies the multi-party agreement, and says restoration of former reservoir lands will continue for years.
- Yurok Tribe, [Yurok Tribe Leads Massive Revegetation Project](https://www.yuroktribe.org/post/yurok-tribe-leads-massive-revegetation-project), March 29, 2024. The Tribe describes work led by its Fisheries Department and participating partners, including planting at reservoir sites and projected habitat benefits.

**Claim limit:** Completion of physical dam removal does not prove completed ecological recovery, an exact future salmon population or unanimous support among affected communities. The Yurok account is a participant primary source about its own work; attribute it, and seek other tribal and local perspectives before any film script. Do not use identifiable people, cultural practices, access locations or ceremonial material without permission from the relevant people and authorities.

## BANK-169-045 — Pusher Street decision and closure

- Christiania, [community assembly press statement on Pusher Street](https://www.christiania.org/artikler/pressemeddelelse-christianias-faellesmoede-beslutter-at-pusher-street-skal-lukke/), decision dated August 27, 2023. The community states that its common meeting decided to close the street while seeking to avoid displacement of trade and violence to surrounding areas, and says residents could not secure closure alone.
- City of Copenhagen, [Finance Administration briefing (PDF)](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/d27a47dc-302a-4c67-9312-c1deb82e0367/92d51cb6-293d-4d77-bb23-80f2f7118d21-bilag-19_0.pdf), April 19, 2024. It dates the resident-led closure and initial digging to April 6 and the transformation plan's adoption to March 7, 2024; it identifies municipal and police roles.

**Claim limit:** A resident assembly statement and municipal briefing do not establish every resident's view or the long-term effect on drug trade, violence or neighboring areas. This is a history of governance decisions, not instructions about illicit activity. Report the death and safety context without identifying victims or neighbors unnecessarily; consult residents before filming or reusing accounts, and respect their decisions about representation.
