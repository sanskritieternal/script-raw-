# Sculpture case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected custodial records anchor object-specific questions. They do not settle every attribution or community interpretation.

| Case | Primary record inspected | Claim and access limit |
|---|---|---|
| Rodin cast | [Metropolitan Museum Burghers of Calais object record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/207812) distinguishes 1884–95 modeling from the 1985 Coubertin Foundry cast; the [Met Rodin collection catalogue](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/Rodin_The_B_Gerald_Cantor_Collection.pdf) supplies collection context. | A later authorized cast is not a bronze physically cast by Rodin; object and edition histories require individual records. Public object page was accessible; full foundry archives were not checked. |
| Bristol display | [Bristol Museums 2024 M Shed display account](https://www.bristolmuseums.org.uk/blog/new-display-at-m-shed-the-toppling-of-the-colston-statue/) and [Bristol City Council 2025 plinth record](https://news.bristol.gov.uk/press-releases/507f3e4f-384f-4770-bcb5-84c499bc0e57/new-plaque-installed-on-colston-statue-plinth) document the statue’s move and interpretation. | Consultation participants are not a random sample of all Bristol residents; the museum and council records have institutional perspectives, and other community voices need deliberate inclusion. No endorsement of the statue’s original commemoration is implied. |

Other generic attribution, fabrication and superlative prompts remain unverified proposals.
