# Selected source and boundary note — 419 Supercars

Reviewed 2026-10-24. Proposed **BANK-419-045**: the Porsche 918 Spyder development target and later Nürburgring lap claim.

## Primary manufacturer statements

- [Porsche's July 31, 2012 prototype announcement](https://www.porsche.com/usa/aboutporsche/pressreleases/pag/?id=2012-07-31&pool=international-de) states a development goal of a lap below **7 minutes 22 seconds** for the plug-in hybrid 918 Spyder. A goal is not a measured result.
- [Porsche's 2013 918 Spyder presentation](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/products/918-spyder-10713) reports **6 minutes 57 seconds** on the Nürburgring Nordschleife and describes road homologation. [Porsche's production-end report](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/products/porsche-918-spyder-manufaktory-end-of-production-11123.html) dates the lap to September 2013 and calls the tires production tires. A later [Porsche document](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr%3Ada16c0b9-15c7-452b-bf3e-5643a71182a7/Pressemappe_Mission_X_EN.pdf) specifies the historical **20.6-km** Nordschleife timing configuration. These are manufacturer records of its claim, not an independent timing audit.

## Limits and overlap

Do not compare this time to modern 20.832-km official laps or other cars without harmonizing course distance, start/finish convention, tire, weather, driver, vehicle specification and verification method. The manufacturer's 2015 “never equalled” wording is a dated statement, not a current ranking. Do not infer hybrid energy deployment over the lap from the time alone.
