## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Supercars across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Supercars Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Supercars.
- **Tier 2: Supercars Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Supercars.
- **Tier 3: Supercars Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Supercars.
- **Tier 4: Supercars Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Supercars.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Supercars: A Supercar Lineage**
   - *Question:* Which design and market changes shaped one named lineage?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated model specifications and contemporary accounts. Evidence base: dated factory specifications and drawings, independent instrumented tests, track logs, official approval records, service documentation, and production records.
2. **Supercars: A Celebrated Supercar Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What do independent tests establish about a famous model?
   - *Key Focus:* State year, trim, tires, surface, and test conditions. Evidence base: dated factory specifications and drawings, independent instrumented tests, track logs, official approval records, service documentation, and production records.
3. **Supercars: Specialist Work behind the Badge**
   - *Question:* Which supplier or craft team built a defining system?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace component provenance and production records. Evidence base: dated factory specifications and drawings, independent instrumented tests, track logs, official approval records, service documentation, and production records.
4. **Supercars: What Makes a Supercar?**

5. **Supercars: Performance under Thermal Load**
   - *Question:* How does repeated hard use change brakes, tires, and power delivery?
