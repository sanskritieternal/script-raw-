## BANK-269-050 — IGNCA film's documented branches

- [IGNCA's catalog page for *Oral Tradition of Vedas*](https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/media-centre/outreach/published-dvds/oral-tradition-of-vedas/) names the Jaiminīya and Rāṇāyanīya Sāmaveda branches and Śaunaka and Paippalāda Atharvaveda branches, gives the director and length, and says the film was made for India's UNESCO presentation. This verifies the institution's description of its film, not what each scene or reciter actually demonstrates.
- [IGNCA's Vedic chanting clip page](https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/kalakosa/video-clip-vedic-chanting/) lists a broader set of documented recitation techniques and branches. The two pages describe different media products; do not merge their branch lists. View the actual film with catalog/production records, secure use rights, and consult participating communities before identifying individuals or claiming a branch's present condition. This named documentation project is distinct from BANK-269-035's general recitation mechanism.

## BANK-269-051 — Paippalāda Atharvaveda witnesses

- The [Vedic Heritage Portal's hosted study by Dipak Bhattacharya](https://vedicheritage.gov.in/pdf/Paipalada_Samhita_Atharvaveda.pdf) distinguishes the Paippalāda and Śaunakīya Saṃhitā traditions and discusses lists of recensions, manuscript finds, oral continuity, accent and publication history. It identifies variant traditional enumerations rather than a single simple count.
- This secondary scholarly study is an entry point to primary witnesses. Inspect its citations, manuscripts and edited passages before assigning a date, variant reading or survival statistic. Different evidence for text, practice and publication must remain separate. This is a named recension case within BANK-269-043/044, not a duplicate of niche 270's Upaniṣad interpretation.

## Overlap decision

The film case is about a bounded preservation record; the Paippalāda case is about textual witnesses and oral transmission. General Vedic word translation stays with BANK-269-036, and later philosophical exegesis with niche 268 or 270 as appropriate. The archived “record extremes” and fixed-text hooks remain source-criticism prompts with no oldest or perfectly unchanged claim adopted.
