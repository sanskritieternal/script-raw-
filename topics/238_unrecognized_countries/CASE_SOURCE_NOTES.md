# Recognition-status case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected institutional primary records are used for limited documentary questions. Naming and recognition positions can change; no single source settles all parties’ claims.

| Case | Record inspected | Claim and access limit |
|---|---|---|
| Kosovo advisory opinion | [International Court of Justice, 22 July 2010 advisory opinion](https://api.icj-cij.org/sites/default/files/case-related/141/141-20100722-ADV-01-00-EN.pdf) and [Court summary](https://www.icj-cij.org/node/103912) address the legality of the 17 February 2008 declaration. | The Court answered a specific legal question; it did not require all states to recognize Kosovo or adjudicate every territorial/status issue. Summary was directly accessible; the full opinion was identified in the Court archive but not independently reanalysed paragraph by paragraph. |
| Chinese Taipei WTO status | [WTO member information](https://goods-schedules.wto.org/member/the-separate-customs-territory-of-taiwan-penghu-kinmen-and-matsu-chinese-taipei?page=1) lists the Separate Customs Territory as a WTO member since 2002; [WTO accession record](https://www-server1.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/a1_taipei_chinois_e.htm) gives the accession history. | WTO membership is an institutional fact, not a proxy for UN membership, universal statehood recognition or a finding about sovereignty. The accession page was indexed; full annexes were not reviewed. |
