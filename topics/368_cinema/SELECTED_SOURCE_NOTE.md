## The Great Train Robbery (1903) ? BANK-368-046
The Library of Congress object record identifies the 1903 Edison production, names Edwin S. Porter alongside J. B. Smith and other contributors, describes the paper-print and other holdings, and warns that one viewing copy is incomplete. The film can be studied as a surviving early shot sequence. The record does not establish that this was the first film to invent a technique, nor that every extant copy matches.

Primary archive item: https://www.loc.gov/item/00694220 . The Library says reuse status requires item-specific assessment; its catalog reproduces historic copyright information and notes possible privacy, publicity, licensing and trademark restrictions. Credit LOC and the named creators, and inspect the copy/version used before quoting a cut count or reproducing frames.

## Metropolis reconstruction (2001/2010) ? BANK-368-047
The Friedrich-Wilhelm-Murnau-Stiftung reports that its 2001 reconstruction used stills/text for missing passages; a 2008 Buenos Aires copy filled many gaps, and the restored version premiered in February 2010. The foundation credits the Deutsche Kinemathek and Museo del Cine Pablo C. Ducros Hicken as partners and notes damaged 16 mm material. This is an edition/restoration account, not proof of one completely original or final version.

Primary rights-holder/archival accounts: https://murnau-stiftung.de/metropolis-100 ; https://murnau-stiftung.de/stiftung/projekte/projekt-metropolis-2710 . Consult the specific screening edition and rights holder before using footage, music, stills or promotional materials. The foundation page mentions a future 2027 version, which is not claimed available here.
