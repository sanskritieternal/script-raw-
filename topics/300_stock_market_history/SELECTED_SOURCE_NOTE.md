# Selected primary-source note: stock-market history

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed cases BANK-300-042 and BANK-300-043.

The US Securities and Exchange Commission's FY1975 annual report says it adopted Exchange Act Rule 19b-3 on January 23, 1975, requiring exchanges to end fixed public commission rates effective May 1, 1975: https://www.sec.gov/files/1975.pdf . The SEC's later inspection report also recounts the staged earlier exemptions and statutory codification: https://www.sec.gov/news/studies/softdolr.htm . These are direct institutional records of a rule timeline. They do not quantify every investor's costs, prove liquidity changes or establish that access widened for all groups. Before scripting effects, obtain contemporary fee schedules and participant data. Historic regulation is not current trading guidance.

The SEC's 1997 trading analysis recounts that, after the October 1987 market break, stock and stock-index futures exchanges proposed coordinated circuit-breaker halts in 1988: https://www.sec.gov/news/studies/tradrep.htm . SEC 1989 annual-report material describes initial approvals and period-specific DJIA point thresholds: https://www.sec.gov/file/1989pdf . These retrospective primary regulator records support a response chronology, not a single definitive cause of the crash, proof that halts solved market instability, or present-day threshold values. Confirm the exact approved rule and effective date before numeric narration.
