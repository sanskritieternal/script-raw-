## BANK-321-040 — Starlink RDOF application

- The [FCC's 2023 affirmance order](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-23-105A1.pdf) recounts its review of Starlink's long-form Rural Digital Opportunity Fund application and the 2022 rejection. It discusses the specific modeled locations, program service obligations and submitted technical/financial materials. Read the Bureau's underlying 2022 letter and dissenting views before quoting a contested finding as uncontested fact.
- RDOF eligibility is a US subsidy-program question at a dated application stage. It does not establish that no rural household could buy or use the service, nor that present network performance equals the application projections. Ground stations, capacity, prices, terminal costs and particular household access require separate evidence.
