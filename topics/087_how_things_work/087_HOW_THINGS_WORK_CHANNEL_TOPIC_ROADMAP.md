## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of How Things Work across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Everyday Mechanical Systems & Fluid Dynamics (Scratch / Beginner)** — Flush toilets and siphons, four-stroke internal combustion engines, refrigerator vapor-compression cycles, quartz watch piezoelectricity, and ballpoint pen capillary action.
- **Tier 2: Electromagnetism, Transistors & Power Distribution (Intermediate)** — Electric motors and generators (Faraday's law), silicon semiconductor MOSFET gates, AC power transformers and grid phase balancing, and microwave magnetron physics.
- **Tier 3: High-Tech Sensors, Optoelectronics & Telecommunications (Upper Intermediate)** — Touchscreen capacitive grids, MEMS gyroscopes and accelerometers, fiber optic total internal reflection, laser interferometry in barcode scanners, and GPS relativistic time triangulation.
- **Tier 4: Extreme Engineering, Nuclear Reactors & Quantum Machinery (Advanced / Expert)** — Jet engine turbofan bypass mechanics, nuclear pressurized water reactors (PWR), MRI superconducting magnetic resonance, and quantum computing dilution refrigerators.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Everyday Mechanical Systems & Fluid Dynamics
1. **How a Jet Engine Works: The Terrifying Physics of Compression, Combustion, and Thrust**
   - *Question:* How does a modern turbofan engine suck in 1.2 tons of air per second, compress it to 40 times atmospheric pressure, and generate 100,000 lbs of thrust at 35,000 feet?
   - *Key Focus:* The Brayton thermodynamic cycle ('Suck, Squeeze, Bang, Blow'), high bypass ratio turbofan efficiency, titanium compressor blades, ceramic-coated single-crystal turbine blades surviving 1,700°C temperatures hotter than steel's melting point.
2. **How a Refrigerator Works: The Magic of the Vapor-Compression Cycle**
   - *Question:* How can compressing a gas in a sealed loop make the inside of a metal box cold while pumping blazing heat out the back into your kitchen?
   - *Key Focus:* The second law of thermodynamics, refrigerant phase change (boiling at -26°C), compressor pump, condenser coils dissipating heat, capillary expansion valve dropping pressure, and evaporator coils absorbing heat.
3. **How a Flush Toilet Works: The Ancient Elegance of the Siphon Trap**
   - *Question:* Why does a toilet flush with zero moving parts in the bowl using just water and atmospheric pressure, and how does the S-trap keep deadly sewer gas out of your bathroom?
   - *Key Focus:* The siphon trapway fluid dynamics, atmospheric pressure pushing water over the weir, the fill valve float mechanism, flapper seal timing, and Torricelli's law.
4. **How a 4-Stroke Engine Works: Intake, Compression, Power, and Exhaust**
   - *Question:* What is happening inside an engine cylinder 50 times per second when you step on the gas pedal at 3,000 RPM?
   - *Key Focus:* The Otto cycle four strokes, camshaft timing belts synchronizing intake/exhaust valves, crankshaft converting reciprocating motion to rotary torque, stoichiometric 14.7:1 air-fuel ratio, and spark timing.
5. **How a Quartz Watch Works: Why Vibrating Crystals Beat Every Mechanical Spring**
   - *Question:* Why does every battery-powered quartz watch on Earth use a tiny tuning fork that vibrates exactly 32,768 times per second?
   - *Key Focus:* Piezoelectric effect in quartz crystal, binary frequency divider circuits (dividing 32,768 by 2 fifteen times to get exactly 1 Hz), Lavet-type stepping motor, and the quartz crisis that shook Swiss watchmaking.
### Tier 2: Electromagnetism & Power Grids
6. **How an Electric Motor Works: Faraday's Law, Lorentz Force, and The Rotating Magnetic Field**
   - *Question:* How does sending invisible electrical current through coiled copper wire make a heavy steel shaft spin with hundreds of horsepower without any combustion?
   - *Key Focus:* Lorentz force law (F = q[E + v x B]), Fleming's left-hand rule, DC commutators vs. AC brushless motors, 3-phase rotating magnetic field in stator coils, and permanent magnets vs. induction rotors.
7. **How a Microwave Oven Works: Cavity Magnetrons and Dielectric Water Heating**
   - *Question:* Why does a microwave oven heat cold soup in 60 seconds while leaving the glass plate cool, and why does aluminum foil spark violently inside?
   - *Key Focus:* Cavity magnetron electron resonance generating 2.45 GHz radio waves, dipole rotation of water molecules creating friction heat (dielectric heating), standing wave nodes requiring turntables, and RF arcing on metal edges.
8. **How the Power Grid Works: AC Phase Synchronization, Transformers, and Blackout Defenses**
   - *Question:* How do thousands of power plants across an entire continent spin in perfect synchronization at exactly 60.00 Hz without drifting apart and collapsing the grid?
   - *Key Focus:* Three-phase alternating current efficiency, step-up transformers sending 500,000 volts over power lines to minimize I²R heat losses, substation step-down transformers, grid inertia, and automatic load shedding.
9. **How Transistors Work: Silicon Doping, P-N Junctions, and The Microscopic Light Switch**
   - *Question:* How can adding a few phosphorus or boron atoms to pure silicon allow a microscopic gate to switch electricity on and off billions of times per second?
   - *Key Focus:* Semiconductor band gaps, N-type vs. P-type doping, the depletion zone at P-N junctions, MOSFET (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor) gate voltage creating conductive channels, and CMOS binary logic gates.
10. **How Car Brakes Work: Pascal's Principle, Hydraulic Multiplication, and ABS Valves**
   - *Question:* How can your foot applying 20 lbs of pressure on a brake pedal create 2,000 lbs of clamping force on spinning red-hot brake rotors to stop a 4,000 lb car?
   - *Key Focus:* Pascal's law of fluid hydraulics (force multiplication through piston area ratios), brake booster vacuum assist, kinetic energy conversion into friction heat, and Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) pulsing valves 15 times/sec.
### Tier 3: Sensors, Optoelectronics & Telecom
11. **How Touchscreens Work: Projected Capacitive Grids and Microscopic Charge Sinks**
   - *Question:* Why does your smartphone screen respond instantly to your finger but ignores plastic pens, and why does a single droplet of water make it go crazy?
   - *Key Focus:* Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) transparent conductive diamond grids, human body as an electrical conductor altering local electrostatic field capacitance, multi-touch coordinate triangulation, and hydrophobic oleophobic glass coatings.
12. **How GPS Works: Satellite Atomic Clocks and Einstein's Relativity Corrections**
   - *Question:* Why would your phone's GPS navigation drift by 6 miles every single day if engineers didn't program Einstein's theory of general relativity into the satellites?
   - *Key Focus:* Trilateration using time-of-flight radio signals from 4 satellites, rubidium atomic clocks accurate to nanoseconds, special relativity time dilation (satellites running slow) vs. general relativity gravitational dilation (satellites running fast, net +38 microseconds/day).
13. **How Fiber Optic Cables Work: Total Internal Reflection Carrying Terabits Across Oceans**
   - *Question:* How can a glass fiber strand as thin as a human hair beam the entire Library of Congress under the Atlantic Ocean in half a second without the light leaking out?
   - *Key Focus:* Snell's Law and critical angle, core glass vs. lower refractive index cladding, total internal reflection trapping light pulses, Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing (DWDM), and erbium-doped fiber laser amplifiers on the ocean floor.
14. **How MEMS Gyroscopes Work: The Microscopic Tuning Forks Inside Your Smartphone**
   - *Question:* How does your phone know you rotated it from portrait to landscape using a silicon chip with vibrating mechanical combs smaller than a red blood cell?
   - *Key Focus:* Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS), Coriolis acceleration force acting on vibrating silicon masses, differential capacitive sensing plates measuring picometer movements, and airbag deployment accelerometers.
15. **How Noise-Canceling Headphones Work: Destructive Wave Interference in Real Time**
   - *Question:* How can tiny external microphones listen to airplane jet engine rumble and create an 'anti-noise' sound wave that makes the decibels vanish inside your ear?
   - *Key Focus:* Sound as longitudinal pressure waves, phase inversion (180° out of phase crest-meets-trough cancellation), feedforward and feedback microphone loops, DSP latency processing in microseconds, and why passive isolation still matters for high frequencies.
### Tier 4: Extreme Engineering & Quantum Machines
16. **How a Nuclear Reactor Works: Fission, Control Rods, and The Self-Regulating Neutron Chain**
   - *Question:* How does splitting a microscopic Uranium-235 nucleus with a single neutron produce millions of times more energy than burning coal without releasing a speck of carbon?
   - *Key Focus:* Nuclear fission chain reactions, critical mass, water acting as neutron moderator and coolant, control rods absorbing excess neutrons (boron/cadmium), Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) heat exchangers, and negative void coefficient safety physics.
17. **How an MRI Machine Works: Superconducting Magnets and Proton Spin Resonance**
   - *Question:* How does a 3-Tesla magnetic coil submerged in liquid helium align the hydrogen atoms in your body's water molecules to take high-resolution cross-sectional photos of your brain?
   - *Key Focus:* Larmor precession frequency of hydrogen protons, liquid helium cooling niobium-titanium coils to -269°C for zero-resistance superconductivity, RF pulse tipping proton spins, gradient coils creating slice encoding, and Fourier transform image reconstruction.
18. **How Rocket Engines Work: Cryogenic Turbopumps and The Staged Combustion Cycle**
   - *Question:* How does SpaceX's Raptor engine burn liquid methane and liquid oxygen at 300 atmospheres of chamber pressure without melting its own metal bell nozzle?
   - *Key Focus:* The convergent-divergent de Laval nozzle accelerating gas past Mach 3, regenerative cooling pumping cryogenic fuel through micro-channels in the nozzle walls, full-flow staged combustion cycle, and turbopump impellers delivering tens of thousands of horsepower.
19. **How Quantum Computers Work: Qubits, Superposition, and Dilution Refrigerators**
   - *Question:* How can a superconducting quantum processor evaluate an exponential number of cryptographic possibilities simultaneously at temperatures colder than deep space?
   - *Key Focus:* Classical binary bits vs. quantum qubits in Bloch sphere superposition, quantum entanglement connecting states, helium-3/helium-4 dilution refrigerators cooling chips to 15 millikelvin, quantum decoherence errors, and Shor's algorithm.
20. **How Artificial Neural Networks Work: Matrix Math, Weights, and Gradient Descent**
   - *Question:* When you peel back the hype around Artificial Intelligence, how does multiplying trillions of decimal numbers and calculating slopes create machine intelligence?
   - *Key Focus:* Perceptrons, matrix multiplication on GPU tensor cores, activation functions (ReLU), loss functions measuring prediction error, backpropagation applying the chain rule of calculus, and gradient descent rolling down high-dimensional valleys.
