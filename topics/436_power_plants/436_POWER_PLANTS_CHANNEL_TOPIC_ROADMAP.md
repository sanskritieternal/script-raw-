## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Power Plants across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Power Plants Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Power Plants.
- **Tier 2: Power Plants Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Power Plants.
- **Tier 3: Power Plants Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Power Plants.
- **Tier 4: Power Plants Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Power Plants.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Power Plants: A Power Station Sited**
   - *Question:* Which fuel, water, grid, and demand constraints drove one site?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated plans and community response. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
2. **Power Plants: A Power Plant in Context**
   - *Question:* What do capacity and net generation records actually show?
   - *Key Focus:* Name units, fuel, and reporting period. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
3. **Power Plants: People behind Electric Output**
   - *Question:* Whose operation, maintenance, or neighboring experience is omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Use worker and local evidence. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
4. **Power Plants: Fuel to Net Electricity**
   - *Question:* Where does energy enter, convert, and leave a plant?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow a defined generating unit. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
5. **Power Plants: Dispatch and Efficiency**
   - *Question:* How do load and operating mode alter heat rate and emissions?
   - *Key Focus:* Use matched plant data. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Power Plants: An Output Shortfall**
   - *Question:* Which outage, fuel, water, or grid factor explains a gap?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare operating logs and demand. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
7. **Power Plants: A Plant Retrofit**
   - *Question:* Did one upgrade change net output, fuel use, or pollution?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare before and after with dispatch controls. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
8. **Power Plants: A Generation Decision**
   - *Question:* Who chose a plant design and who bore consequences?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace utility, regulator, and local records. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
9. **Power Plants: A Unit Enters Service**
   - *Question:* Which performance claims were met in the first operating period?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare commissioning and measured generation. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
10. **Power Plants: Plant and Grid Exchange**
   - *Question:* How do generator controls, switchyard, and dispatcher interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Use public system-level records. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Power Plants: A Station Retires**
   - *Question:* Which economics, reliability, or rule changes explain closure?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated cost, output, and retirement filings. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
12. **Power Plants: A Plant before and after Retrofit**
   - *Question:* What changed in generation, heat rate, and emissions?
   - *Key Focus:* Align fuel, demand, and season. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
13. **Power Plants: A Proposed Generator**
   - *Question:* What data would validate its reliability and cost claims?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate plan, permit, build, and operation. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
14. **Power Plants: Capacity Means Constant Power?**
   - *Question:* How does installed capacity differ from actual generation?
   - *Key Focus:* Calculate from metered output and time. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
15. **Power Plants: A Plant through Its Records**
   - *Question:* How did one project change from proposal to retirement?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace permits, construction, operation, and closure. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Power Plants: Gross or Net Generation?**

   - *Key Focus:* State meter and fuel boundary. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
17. **Power Plants: Energy Flow through a Unit**
   - *Question:* How much input becomes net electricity, heat, and waste?
   - *Key Focus:* Use measured annual or test-period balances. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
18. **Power Plants: Efficiency and Flexibility**
   - *Question:* What is gained and lost by ramping a generating unit?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare heat rate, wear, and grid value. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
19. **Power Plants: A Documented Plant Outage**
   - *Question:* Which equipment and organizational factors were found?
   - *Key Focus:* Use official and operator review. Evidence base: EIA generator and plant data, metered output, heat-rate records, grid reports, permits, emissions measurements, outage and maintenance records, and community accounts.
20. **Power Plants: Acceptance Test versus Annual Use**
