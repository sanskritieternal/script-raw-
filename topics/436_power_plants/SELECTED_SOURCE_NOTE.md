# Selected source and boundary note — 436 Power Plants

Reviewed 2026-10-24. Proposed **BANK-436-043** concerns SaskPower's Boundary Dam Unit 3 carbon-capture retrofit in Saskatchewan, Canada.

## Primary operator records

- SaskPower, [BD3 Q4 2024 status update, January 21, 2025](https://www.saskpower.com/about-us/our-company/blog/2025/bd3-status-update-q4-2024), reports **848,388 tonnes of CO₂ captured in calendar 2024**, CCS facility availability of **85%** for that year, and fourth-quarter capture of **172,514 tonnes** with reported emissions intensity of **364 tonnes CO₂/GWh**. These are operator-defined facility metrics and time windows, not direct measures of net station output or lifecycle climate impact.
- SaskPower, [2024 ten-year retrospective](https://www.saskpower.com/about-us/media-information/news-releases/2024/saskpower-marks-10-years-of-operation-at-carbon-capture-and-storage-facility), dates the retrofit startup to fall 2014. Its first/cleanest/record claims are promotional and require defined comparators before use. The Q4 2024 update is the tighter numerical source.

## Limits and overlap

Captured tonnes do not equal avoided lifecycle emissions without accounting for energy penalty, upstream inputs, CO₂ destination and counterfactual generation. Capture-plant availability is not the generating unit's annual capacity factor. Obtain primary station generation and fuel data before answering net-output questions. Figures may be revised; preserve report date and reporting period. Images may have separate rights. **BANK-436-041** treats generic carbon-capture resource tradeoffs, while this named case asks exactly what one operator's 2024 metrics establish. **BANK-437-043** concerns a nuclear regulator's licensing timeline; it is a separate jurisdiction and evidence type.
