# Case-source note: FTC Invitation Homes price disclosures

Checked 2026-10-24. The FTC [case docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/202-3170-invitation-homes-inc-ftc-v) links its [September 24, 2024 complaint](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/1-Complaint_0.pdf) and [September 27, 2024 court-entered stipulated order](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2023170invitationhomesorderenteredbycourt.pdf). The [FTC announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-takes-action-against-invitation-homes-deceiving-renters-charging-junk-fees-withholding-security) describes allegations that advertised rents excluded mandatory monthly fees, including smart-home and utility-management charges; the entered order requires clear rental-price disclosure. A [March 2026 FTC update](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-sends-checks-totaling-more-472-million-consumers-deceived-invitation-homes-undisclosed-fees) reports refund checks, a later remedy milestone rather than proof of every individual loss.

Limits: The complaint pleads the regulator's allegations; the stipulated order is enforceable but not a trial finding on every allegation. This U.S. case covers one company and a specified period, not a universal rental-fee rule, present lease terms, or a measured housing-affordability effect. Do not infer any named tenant's circumstances or give jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Overlap: `BANK-112-027` covers institutional ownership, `BANK-313-036` the general affordability ratio, and `BANK-506-033` generic rental-offer comparison. The named FTC disclosure and remedy record is assigned here.
