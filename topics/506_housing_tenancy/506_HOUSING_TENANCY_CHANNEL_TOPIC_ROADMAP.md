## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Housing & Tenancy across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Housing & Tenancy Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Housing & Tenancy.
- **Tier 2: Housing & Tenancy Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Housing & Tenancy.
- **Tier 3: Housing & Tenancy Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Housing & Tenancy.
- **Tier 4: Housing & Tenancy Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Housing & Tenancy.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Housing & Tenancy: How Housing & Tenancy Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a city's rental rules develop alongside its housing market?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one city's rental or tenancy system through dated housing policies and market conditions; distinguish legal changes from actual access. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Housing & Tenancy: A Documented Housing & Tenancy Case**
   - *Question:* Which households benefited from a documented housing program?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one housing program using eligibility, delivered units, affordability and tenure outcomes; identify which households the evaluation represents. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
3. **Housing & Tenancy: People behind Housing & Tenancy**
   - *Question:* Whose tenant or maintenance work is missing from a housing-policy account?
   - *Key Focus:* Document overlooked tenant, maintenance or organizing work within one housing case; use consented accounts and avoid exposing vulnerable residents. Evidence base: Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: local tenancy rules, housing inspections, household costs and documented program delivery. Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf). Starting framework: UN-Habitat housing approach evaluation (https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2023/12/global_report_evaluation_web.pdf).
