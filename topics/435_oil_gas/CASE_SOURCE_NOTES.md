# Selected primary-source note — oil and gas

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-435-043`.

- [California Geologic Energy Management Division, *Aliso Canyon Storage Facility*](https://www.conservation.ca.gov/calgem/Pages/AlisoCanyon.aspx): regulator chronology dates detection of the Standard Sesnon 25 gas leak to 23 October 2015 and sealing to February 2016. CalGEM summarizes the CPUC/CalGEM-commissioned Blade Energy Partners root-cause analysis published 17 May 2019: an outer casing rupture attributed to external microbial corrosion in contact with groundwater, plus findings about prior casing leaks and risk-management practices. The page also records dated orders and subsequent regulatory changes.
- [Blade Energy Partners, *Root Cause Analysis of the Uncontrolled Hydrocarbon Release from Aliso Canyon SS-25* (16 May 2019), hosted by SoCalGas](https://www.socalgas.com/sites/default/files/CPUC-1000.pdf): direct report link found and indexed; the full PDF did not open in this inspection. A future script should consult its actual pages before detailed causal quotation.

The accessible regulator summary is primary for agency actions and its account of the commissioned findings, but it is not a substitute for full-report examination or community health research. The case is a storage-well failure, not a producing field or pipeline failure. Do not infer current facility status from historical orders, blame individual workers, assert individual health outcomes, or give well-control steps. Incident images and third-party material need separate rights review.
