## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Oil & Gas across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Oil & Gas Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Oil & Gas.
- **Tier 2: Oil & Gas Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Oil & Gas.
- **Tier 3: Oil & Gas Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Oil & Gas.
- **Tier 4: Oil & Gas Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Oil & Gas.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Oil & Gas: A Field Developed**
   - *Question:* Which geology, rights, and demand decisions led to production?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated leases, permits, and records. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
2. **Oil & Gas: A Field's Real Output**
   - *Question:* What do production and impact records show over time?
   - *Key Focus:* State oil or gas, field, and period. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
3. **Oil & Gas: Workers and Host Communities**
   - *Question:* Whose risks and benefits are missing from an energy account?
   - *Key Focus:* Use attributed testimony and public data. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
4. **Oil & Gas: Well to End Use**
   - *Question:* How do production, processing, transport, refining, and consumption differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one product and boundary. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
5. **Oil & Gas: Reservoir Decline and Recovery**
   - *Question:* How do forecasts compare with actual field production?
   - *Key Focus:* Use pressure, output, and uncertainty. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Oil & Gas: An Emissions Discrepancy**
   - *Question:* Which measurements explain differences between inventory and observation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare method, season, and site coverage. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
7. **Oil & Gas: A Leak Detection Method**
   - *Question:* Does it find and reduce emissions in field use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare detection threshold and verified repairs. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
8. **Oil & Gas: A Development Decision**
   - *Question:* Who approved an oil or gas project and who objected?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace official review and affected people. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
9. **Oil & Gas: A Pipeline or Well Incident**
   - *Question:* What did the official investigation establish?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate preliminary cause from final finding. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
10. **Oil & Gas: Gas from Well to Customer**
   - *Question:* How do gathering, processing, storage, and distribution connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one regional flow and measured volumes. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Oil & Gas: A Field Winds Down**
   - *Question:* Which production, price, and policy factors drove closure?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated output and decommissioning records. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
12. **Oil & Gas: An Energy Site after Closure**
   - *Question:* What land, water, leakage, and employment changes are measured?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare baseline and long-term monitoring. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
13. **Oil & Gas: A Proposed Field or Pipeline**
   - *Question:* What demand, safety, and emissions evidence would test the proposal?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat forecasts as scenarios. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
14. **Oil & Gas: Clean Gas?**
   - *Question:* How does a climate comparison change with methane-loss assumptions?
   - *Key Focus:* State time horizon and full lifecycle boundary. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
15. **Oil & Gas: One Barrel or Gas Stream**
   - *Question:* How did a product move from field to market?
   - *Key Focus:* Track custody and transformations without claiming perfect tracing. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms

17. **Oil & Gas: Hydrocarbon and Water Streams**

18. **Oil & Gas: Production and Emissions**

   - *Key Focus:* Compare documented conditions. Evidence base: EIA energy-flow and production data, EPA emissions methods and measurements, PHMSA pipeline records, official incident reviews, permits, and community testimony.
19. **Oil & Gas: A Documented Loss of Containment**
