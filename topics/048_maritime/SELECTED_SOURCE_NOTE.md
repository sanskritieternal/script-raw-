## Vasa — BANK-048-054

The [Vasa Museum's account of the sinking](https://www.vasamuseet.se/en/explore/vasa-history/disaster) describes the 1628 first voyage, top-heavy structure, open gunports and contemporary limits of stability calculation. Its [original-document teaching collection](https://www.vasamuseet.se/skola/i-klassrummet/originaldokument-om-vasa) points to seventeenth-century inquiry records. The episode should separate later hydrostatic analysis from what builders and officials could know at the time. The archive's precise “20 minutes” needs a timed primary account.

## Harrison and longitude — BANK-048-057

The [Royal Museums Greenwich H4 object record](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-79142) identifies the surviving timekeeper; its [H4 Board of Longitude teaching record](https://www.rmg.co.uk/sites/default/files/import/media/pdf/H4.pdf) summarizes sea trials, accuracy, cost and doubts about fleet-wide practicality. Compare the actual timekeeper and Board proceedings before claiming that one carpenter alone solved longitude or counting lives supposedly saved.

## Thresher and SUBSAFE — BANK-048-056

The [U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command's submarine-loss account](https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-019/h-019-3.html) describes the 1963 deep-dive loss, the inquiry's probable sequence, acoustic objections and the resulting SUBSAFE program. The cause is still discussed; treat probable piping failure, reactor shutdown and ballast-system difficulty as a reconstruction, not a proven single chain.

## Ever Given — BANK-048-061

The [International Maritime Organization's incident notice](https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pages/whatsnew-1599.aspx) confirms the March 2021 grounding and refloating and explicitly looked forward to an investigation of contributing factors. This page does not establish bank effect as *the* cause. Source an official casualty investigation, pilotage record and vessel particulars before assigning hydrodynamic causation.
