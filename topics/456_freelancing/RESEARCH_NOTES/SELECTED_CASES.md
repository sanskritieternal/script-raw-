## BANK-456-044 — one jurisdiction's freelance protections

- New York City's [Department of Consumer and Worker Protection guidance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/workers/workersrights/freelancer-workers.page) describes the Freelance Isn't Free Act's written-contract threshold, required terms, timely-payment rule, complaint process and classification caveat. The city page links to the [city code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-130548) and [rules](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCrules/0-0-0-103858); the code-library pages loaded their chapter headings but not provision text in this inspection. The rule concerns covered arrangements in this jurisdiction; a freelancer label alone does not settle worker classification.
- The agency page documents stated rights and procedure, not whether any particular client paid or how a complaint ended. It does not establish a nationwide contract rule. Its linked law, rules and forms can change, so the version and effective date must be checked for a script or any practical guidance.
