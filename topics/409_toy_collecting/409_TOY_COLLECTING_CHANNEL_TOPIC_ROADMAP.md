## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Toy Collecting across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Toy Collecting Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Toy Collecting.
- **Tier 2: Toy Collecting Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Toy Collecting.
- **Tier 3: Toy Collecting Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Toy Collecting.
- **Tier 4: Toy Collecting Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Toy Collecting.
