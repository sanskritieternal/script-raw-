# Selected source note — Canada's automated-decision directive

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-468-044. Proposed question only; no IDEA approval or script readiness is claimed.

## Primary institutional records

- Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat, *Directive on Automated Decision-Making* (2023-04-24 edition): https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=32592 . Its operative text sets duties for covered federal administrative decision systems, including impact assessment, transparency and recourse.
- Government of Canada, *Algorithmic Impact Assessment*: https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/responsible-use-ai/automated-decision-making/algorithmic-impact-assessment.html . This describes the four impact levels, assessment questions, publication and update requirements, and corresponding mitigations.
- Government of Canada, *Guide on the Scope of the Directive*: https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/responsible-use-ai/automated-decision-making/guide-scope-directive-automated-decision-making.html . This distinguishes covered administrative decisions from other uses of automation and identifies department and production conditions.

## Claim and access limits

These are policy texts, not evidence that every covered department complied or that any automated decision was fair. The directive reaches specified federal administrative decision systems, including rule-based systems; calling every covered tool AI would overstate the source. It does not govern all Canadian public bodies or private-sector AI. Check the live policy version and the exact institution/date before scripting an actual deployment.
