## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of What If & Alternative Reality across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Counterfactual Historical Methodology, Divergence Points & Minimum Mutation Rules (Beginner / Scratch)** — Point of divergence (POD) mechanics, the 'plausibility test' and minimum alteration principle, counterfactual historiography standards (Niall Ferguson, E.H. Carr critique), and butterfly effect cascades.
- **Tier 2: Planetary & Environmental 'What Ifs' (Earth Stops Spinning, Oxygen Doubling, Dinosaurs Surviving) (Intermediate)** — Coriolis effect cessation and ocean migration to poles, atmospheric hyperoxia firestorms and giant insect physiology, K-Pg asteroid miss and troodontid evolutionary divergence, and Earth losing its magnetic dynamo.
- **Tier 3: Alternative Geopolitical Timelines (Axis Victory, Roman Industrial Revolution, Space Race Never Ending) (Upper Intermediate)** — Hero of Alexandria's aeolipile steam engine triggering an ancient Roman industrial revolution, Cuban Missile Crisis nuclear exchange aftermath, and uninterrupted continuous Apollo lunar base expansion.
- **Tier 4: Cosmic & Physical Law Alterations (Speed of Light Halving, Gravity Reversing, Many-Worlds Branches) (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Altering fine-structure constants and fundamental physical limits, macroscopic quantum many-worlds branching topologies, vacuum decay speed of light wavefronts, and rigorous speculative worldbuilding.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **What If the Dinosaurs Never Went Extinct? The 66-Million-Year Alternative Evolution**
   - *Question:* What would Earth's biosphere look like today if the 10-kilometer Chicxulub asteroid missed Earth by just 30 minutes in 66 million BC?
   - *Key Focus:* Non-avian dinosaur survival, troodontid encephalization quotient and potential sapience, mammal ecological suppression, survival of ammonites and pterosaurs, and Dale Russell's dinosauroid thought experiment.
2. **The Rules of Alternate History: How to Build Plausible Divergence Points**
   - *Question:* Why do good counterfactuals require minimal historical tampering while bad ones collapse into impossible fantasy?
   - *Key Focus:* The Minimum Mutation Rule, identifying fragile historical choke points vs deep institutional inertia, avoiding 'for want of a nail' exaggerations, and mapping second-order societal ripple effects.
3. **What If the Earth Stopped Spinning? The Physics of a Motionless Planet**
   - *Question:* What happens if Earth's 1,000 mph equatorial rotational velocity instantly grinds to a halt?
   - *Key Focus:* Atmospheric and ocean supersonic kinetic momentum scraping continents clean, centrifugal force collapse draining equatorial oceans to form two polar super-oceans, a 6-month day/night cycle, and magnetic dynamo loss.
4. **What If the Roman Empire Industrialized? The Steam Engine of Hero of Alexandria**
   - *Question:* Why did 1st-century Roman engineers use the Aeolipile steam reaction turbine as a temple novelty toy instead of building railroads?
   - *Key Focus:* Cheap slave labor disincentivizing labor-saving machinery, metallurgy limitations of bronze/iron boiler pressure, deforestation and coal accessibility, and sketching an alternative 3rd-century industrial Rome.
5. **What If Atmospheric Oxygen Doubled? Giant Insects, Wildfires, and Hyperoxia**
   - *Question:* What would happen to animal physiology, mechanics, and human survival if Earth's air jumped from 21% to 42% oxygen?
   - *Key Focus:* Recreating the Carboniferous period (Meganeura dragonflies with 3-foot wingspans via tracheal passive diffusion), unstoppable continent-wide spontaneous forest fires, engine horsepower spikes, and human oxygen toxicity.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **What If the Cuban Missile Crisis Went Hot? Mapping the World of a 1962 Nuclear War**
   - *Question:* How did the world come within seconds of a full thermonuclear exchange on Black Saturday (October 27, 1962), and what would have survived?
   - *Key Focus:* Vasili Arkhipov refusing to launch the nuclear torpedo on submarine B-59, SIOP-62 nuclear war plan targeting 3,000 warheads at the Soviet bloc and China, immediate 100M dead, and multi-year nuclear winter famine.
7. **What If the Moon Didn't Exist? The Chaotic Wobble of a Moonless Earth**
   - *Question:* Why is the Moon the unsung stabilizer of Earth's climate, and what happens to ocean tides and axial tilt without our celestial partner?
   - *Key Focus:* Chaotic obliquity tilt swinging wildly between 0° and 85° (like Mars), extreme runaway ice ages and tropical poles, 6-hour daily rotation speeds causing permanent hurricane winds, and tidal pool origin of life theories.
8. **What If the Library of Alexandria Never Burned? The Myth of Lost Ancient Science**
   - *Question:* Would humanity have reached Mars in the year 1500 if the great scroll collection of Alexandria had survived intact?
   - *Key Focus:* Deconstructing the single cataclysmic fire myth (Julius Caesar, Aurelian, Theophilus, and Arab conquest), papyrus rot decay, the reality that most lost works had few copies, and Islamic golden age preservation.
9. **What If Humans Were Cold-Blooded? The Society of Ectothermic Civilizations**
   - *Question:* How would cities, work schedules, diets, and wars look if humans required only 10% of our daily calories but froze into helpless torpor every night?
   - *Key Focus:* Endothermy metabolic heat costs (eating 10x more food to maintain 37°C), basking architectural design, the impossibility of polar civilization, seasonal hibernation cycles, and energy-conserving social rhythms.
10. **What If the Space Race Never Ended? The Apollo Applications Moon Base Timeline**
   - *Question:* What if NASA's budget was never slashed after 1969, and Apollo 18 through 30 built a permanent nuclear-powered lunar scientific colony?
   - *Key Focus:* Apollo Applications Program (AAP) blueprints, wet workshop Saturn V rocket upper stages, Lunar Base 1978 engineering plans, nuclear NERVA thermal rockets for Mars 1985, and the geopolitical costs of space parity.
### Tier 3: Advanced Methodologies & Frameworks
11. **What If All Mosquitoes Disappeared Overnight? Ecological Chaos or Public Health Triumph?**
   - *Question:* If genetic gene drives wiped all 3,500 species of mosquitoes off the face of the Earth, would ecosystems collapse or simply fill the void?
   - *Key Focus:* Only ~100 species bite humans (Anopheles, Aedes, Culex), tundra migratory bird biomass feeding on Arctic mosquitoes, chocolate pollination by midge mosquitoes, and eradicating malaria, dengue, and yellow fever.
12. **What If the Americas Were Never Colonized? The Alternative Indigenous Modernity**
   - *Question:* What if Eurasian pathogens never decimated 90% of the Indigenous population, allowing the Aztec, Inca, and Haudenosaunee to modernize on their own terms?
   - *Key Focus:* Virgin soil epidemics immunological barrier, trans-Atlantic diplomatic contact and trade treaties, Incan imperial quipu accounting and road networks, Haudenosaunee federal democracy, and a multi-polar global balance.
13. **What If the Speed of Light Was Only 100 Miles Per Hour? Relativistic Daily Life**
   - *Question:* What happens when you ride a bicycle down the street and visual reality warps, Doppler colors shift to ultraviolet, and time slows down dramatically?
   - *Key Focus:* George Gamow's Mr. Tompkins in Wonderland thought experiment, Lorentz contraction warping buildings as you cycle, optical Doppler shifts turning red lights blue, and relativity becoming an intuitive human sensory experience.
14. **What If the Internet Was Destroyed Tomorrow? The 72-Hour Collapse of Modern Civilization**
   - *Question:* If a global cyber attack or solar superstorm permanently took out DNS, BGP routing, and subsea cables, how long until supermarkets ran out of food?
   - *Key Focus:* Just-in-Time (JIT) supply chain dependency on digital telemetry, credit card and digital interbank settlement freeze, water and municipal power SCADA supervisory collapse, and emergency analog civil continuity plans.
15. **What If Gravity Was Twice as Strong? The Physics of a 2G Planet**
   - *Question:* How would trees grow, bones evolve, and skyscrapers stand if Earth had twice its mass, pulling everything down with 19.6 m/s^2 acceleration?
   - *Key Focus:* Square-cube law scaling on animal limbs (squat, multi-legged thick skeletons), blood pressure requirements to pump blood to elevated brains, atmospheric pressure doubling, chemical rocket escape velocity impossibility, and low-rise stone architecture.
16. **What If Germany Won the Battle of Britain? The Infeasibility of Operation Sea Lion**
   - *Question:* Could the German Wehrmacht actually have invaded and occupied Great Britain across the English Channel in 1940 using river barges?
   - *Key Focus:* The 1974 Sandhurst wargame simulation, Royal Navy Home Fleet supremacy crushing unarmored Rhine river barges, Luftwaffe supply drop limitations, and the logistical impossibility of cross-channel amphibious invasion without landing craft.
### Tier 4: Enterprise Strategy & Deep Mastery
17. **What If Earth Lost Its Magnetic Field? The Martian Fate of Our Atmosphere**
   - *Question:* If Earth's molten iron geodynamo froze solid like Mars, how quickly would solar wind strip away our oceans and atmosphere?
   - *Key Focus:* Magnetosphere shielding against coronal mass ejections, solar wind atmospheric sputtering and photo-dissociation of water, surface cosmic ray exposure, aurora borealis vanishing, and the billion-year desiccation timeline.
18. **What If the Many-Worlds Interpretation is True? The Quantum Multiverse We Live In**
   - *Question:* Does every quantum measurement split the universe into parallel branches where every possible outcome actually happens?
   - *Key Focus:* Hugh Everett III's 1957 formulation, wave function collapse vs continuous universal wave function evolution, quantum decoherence, quantum Russian roulette thought experiment, and the nature of personal identity across infinite branches.
19. **The Business of Alternate History: The Economics of Sci-Fi Worldbuilding and IP Franchises**
   - *Question:* How do streaming giants and book publishers monetize speculative 'What If' concepts into billion-dollar media franchises like The Man in the High Castle and Fallout?
   - *Key Focus:* Worldbuilding production design costs, VFX world reconstruction budgets, licensing rights for alternate brand integrations, narrative pacing rules, and intellectual property franchise valuations.
20. **The Masterclass on Counterfactual Worldbuilding: Constructing an Airtight Speculative Reality**
   - *Question:* How does an elite worldbuilder construct a complex, interconnected alternative timeline without logical plot holes or scientific contradictions?
   - *Key Focus:* Establishing primary divergence nodes, tracing cascading economic and geopolitical second-order effects, grounding social changes in historical human psychology, visual and sensory consistency, and maintaining narrative resonance.
