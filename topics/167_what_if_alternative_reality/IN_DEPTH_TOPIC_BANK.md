#### BANK-167-042 — How would a different axial tilt change seasons if Earth's orbit and rotation rate were held fixed?

**Individual coverage scope:** State the hypothetical tilt, illumination geometry, time horizon and climate-model limits; distinguish the controlled premise from changes caused by removing the Moon.

#### BANK-167-043 — What would slower planetary rotation change if it developed gradually rather than stopping Earth instantaneously?
