## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Retirement Education: How Retirement Provision Developed**
   - *Question:* Which documented change made retirement income possible for a particular group?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare the policy or workplace records before and after one dated change. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
2. **Retirement Education: A Retirement Model Under Scrutiny**
   - *Question:* What does a widely cited pension or savings model actually demonstrate?
   - *Key Focus:* Define the country, cohort, period, and outcomes before drawing lessons. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
3. **Retirement Education: Workers Outside the Standard Plan**
   - *Question:* Whose retirement security was omitted from a celebrated scheme?
   - *Key Focus:* Use eligibility records and affected workers' accounts for one system. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
4. **Retirement Education: The Three Retirement Income Sources**
   - *Question:* How do public benefits, employer plans, and personal savings differ in one jurisdiction?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain roles and limits using dated official descriptions rather than a universal formula. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
5. **Retirement Education: The Risk of Outliving Assets**
   - *Question:* How do different assumptions about lifespan change a retirement projection?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare transparent hypothetical scenarios without recommending a withdrawal rate. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Retirement Education: Why Projections Diverge**
   - *Question:* Why do two retirement calculators produce different results for the same inputs?
   - *Key Focus:* Audit assumptions on returns, inflation, taxes, fees, and life expectancy. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
7. **Retirement Education: Digital Retirement Planning Tools**
   - *Question:* Which decision does a planning tool clarify, and where can it mislead?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare one tool's disclosed assumptions with its displayed results. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
8. **Retirement Education: Who Designed the Benefit Rule?**
   - *Question:* Whose choices shaped a consequential retirement benefit rule?
   - *Key Focus:* Use legislative, employer, or plan records to trace one decision and affected groups. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
9. **Retirement Education: A Retirement Rule Changed**
   - *Question:* What changed for a defined group after one dated retirement policy reform?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare the rule text with implementation and measured effects. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
10. **Retirement Education: From Contribution to Retirement Income**
   - *Question:* How does money move through one named retirement arrangement?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace contributions, investment or accrual, fees, payout, and risk allocation. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Retirement Education: When a Pension Promise Came Under Strain**
   - *Question:* What caused a documented retirement plan to become underfunded or curtailed?
   - *Key Focus:* Reconstruct funding assumptions, sponsor obligations, and dated disclosures. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
12. **Retirement Education: Retirement for Two Cohorts**
   - *Question:* How did retirement options differ for two comparable cohorts?
   - *Key Focus:* Hold occupation and jurisdiction steady while comparing rules and costs. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
13. **Retirement Education: A Conditional Retirement Scenario**
   - *Question:* Which demographic or policy assumption most changes future retirement provision?
   - *Key Focus:* Show competing scenarios and label projections as conditional. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
14. **Retirement Education: Testing a Retirement Rule of Thumb**
   - *Question:* Under what assumptions does a familiar retirement rule fail?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace its original context and compare hypothetical cases, without personal advice. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
15. **Retirement Education: One Worker's Retirement Paper Trail**
   - *Question:* What do plan documents reveal about one worker's changing options?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented or anonymized records and distinguish individual from typical outcomes. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Retirement Education: The Clause That Changes the Payout**
   - *Question:* Which plan or annuity term materially changes the illustrated payout?
   - *Key Focus:* Read dated governing documents and compare identical hypothetical inputs. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
17. **Retirement Education: Before and After Paychecks Stop**
   - *Question:* How does a household budget change when earned income ends?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a fictional, labeled budget to show fixed costs, taxes, and uncertainty. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
18. **Retirement Education: Different Risks at Different Horizons**
   - *Question:* Which retirement risk grows or shrinks as the time horizon changes?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare sequence, inflation, liquidity, and longevity risks in bounded scenarios. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
19. **Retirement Education: The Long Run Cost of Fees**
   - *Question:* How does a fee difference alter an otherwise identical retirement illustration?
   - *Key Focus:* Use disclosed fee categories and transparent arithmetic, not product endorsements. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
20. **Retirement Education: What a Retirement Protection Covers**
   - *Question:* Which protection applies to a particular retirement arrangement, and what is excluded?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify jurisdiction, provider type, date, and official guarantee limits. Evidence base: dated plan documents, regulator publications, policy records, and transparent hypothetical calculations.
