# Selected source note — Social Security Fairness Act

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-500-043. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA, financial advice or individual benefit estimate.

## Primary agency records

- U.S. Social Security Administration, legislative bulletin (2025-01-06): https://www.ssa.gov/legislation/legis_bulletin_010624.html . It states that the Social Security Fairness Act was signed on January 5, 2025 and repealed the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) and Government Pension Offset (GPO) for benefits payable after December 2023.
- SSA, *Social Security Fairness Act: WEP and GPO update*, last updated 2025-07-21: https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/retirement/social-security-fairness-act.html . It explains affected noncovered pensions, workers who are not affected, payment timing and agency processing figures.
- SSA, WEP frequently asked question: https://www.ssa.gov/faqs/en/questions/KA-01915.html . This distinguishes a pension from noncovered work from a pension earned where Social Security taxes were paid.

## Legal and financial limits

The repeal is a U.S. rule for specified Social Security retirement, disability and survivor benefits; it does not mean every teacher or public employee gets an increase. The applicable month of benefit is January 2024 and later, which differs from the 2025 enactment and payment dates. SSA’s reported payment total is an implementation count as of its publication date, not an estimate for an individual. Other eligibility rules remain. Verify current SSA records and personal facts with the agency; this note does not calculate a benefit or recommend a retirement choice. Paraphrase agency text and check diagram rights.
