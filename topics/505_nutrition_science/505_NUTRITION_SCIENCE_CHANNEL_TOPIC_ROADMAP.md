## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Nutrition Science across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Nutrition Science Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Nutrition Science.
- **Tier 2: Nutrition Science Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Nutrition Science.
- **Tier 3: Nutrition Science Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Nutrition Science.
- **Tier 4: Nutrition Science Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Nutrition Science.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Nutrition Science: How Nutrition Science Developed**
   - *Question:* Which evidence and methods shaped a nutrition recommendation?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one nutrition concept or recommendation through the studies and methods that shaped it; distinguish historical belief from later evidence. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Nutrition Science: A Documented Nutrition Science Case**
   - *Question:* Does the popular account of a dietary study match its actual design?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one influential dietary study using population, exposure, comparator, outcome and limitations; test whether popular descriptions match its design. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
3. **Nutrition Science: People behind Nutrition Science**
   - *Question:* Whose diets or research contributions are missing from a nutrition study's story?
   - *Key Focus:* Investigate overlooked participant, laboratory or data work in one nutrition study; credit contributions and examine whose diets were underrepresented. Evidence base: Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
4. **Nutrition Science: Understanding Nutrition Science**
   - *Question:* How do studies distinguish a nutrient, a food and a dietary pattern?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain one nutrition-evidence concept through a defined study example; distinguish nutrients, foods and dietary patterns without prescribing a diet. Evidence base: Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
5. **Nutrition Science: Tradeoffs in Nutrition Science**
   - *Question:* Which measurement and confounding choices drive a nutrition estimate?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare causal-inference approaches for one dietary question; examine measurement error, confounding, substitution and uncertainty. Evidence base: Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: original dietary studies, measurement validation, guideline evidence reviews and declared funding. Starting framework: WHO eLENA evidence library (https://www.who.int/tools/elena). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
