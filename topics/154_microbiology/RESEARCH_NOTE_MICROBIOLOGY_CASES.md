# Selected primary-source research: microbiology cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These checks bound three named proposals. They do not validate other archived efficacy, prevalence, risk or engineering claims and provide no clinical or laboratory procedure.

## Personalized phage case, 2017 — BANK-154-045

- Original clinical report: Schooley et al., [Development and Use of Personalized Bacteriophage-Based Therapeutic Cocktails To Treat a Patient with a Disseminated Resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii* Infection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5610518/), *Antimicrobial Agents and Chemotherapy* 61 (2017), e00954-17, DOI 10.1128/AAC.00954-17.
- This is a detailed report of one patient’s course and tailored phage preparations alongside other care. It supports a question about isolate matching, treatment chronology and interpretation, not a general cure rate.
- Limit: A single uncontrolled case cannot separate the phages’ contribution from concurrent antibiotics, procedures, host factors and time. It does not establish routine approval, a standard dose or patient-specific advice. The archive’s “miracle” and all-superbug language is not a source finding.

## *Ideonella sakaiensis* PET study, 2016 — BANK-154-046

- Original research: Yoshida et al., [A bacterium that degrades and assimilates poly(ethylene terephthalate)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad6359), *Science* 351 (2016), 1196–1199; [accessible author-manuscript copy](https://news.schoolsdo.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/accepted_sci_bact_degrades_plastic_keio_jp_2016.pdf).
- The researchers isolated a bacterium from PET-contaminated samples associated with a recycling site and tested degradation under specified laboratory conditions, notably low-crystallinity PET film at controlled temperature.
- Limit: This demonstrates a particular organism and substrate in a lab; it does not show that an engineered strain cleans open oceans, works on every plastic, or succeeds at industrial scale. Engineered enzyme manufacture and lifecycle results have separate biotechnology scopes.

## Louisiana water exposure investigation, 2013 — BANK-154-047

- Primary investigation: Cope et al., [The First Association of a Primary Amebic Meningoencephalitis Death with Culturable *Naegleria fowleri* in Tap Water from a U.S. Treated Public Drinking Water System](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/36349), CDC-hosted article; [article PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/36349/cdc_36349_DS1.pdf).
- Investigators reported a fatal case involving a child with a lawn water-slide exposure supplied by household tap water and detected *N. fowleri* in household and distribution-system samples.
- Limit: This is an unusual case investigation, not a national trend or general claim that drinking tap water causes infection. The exposure route and local water-system conditions matter. Do not infer another person’s diagnosis or provide household disinfection instructions from this account.
