# Capitals source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-448-046 - 1908 Yass-Canberra decision

- Primary law: Commonwealth of Australia, *Seat of Government Act 1908*, No. 24 of 1908, assented to December 14, 1908, https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1908A00024/asmade/1908-12-14/text/original/pdf (inspected 2026-10-24). Section 3 determines the federal seat in the Yass-Canberra district of New South Wales. The act does not select the detailed city plan or establish that the new city was already built.
- Parliamentary record: House of Representatives, *Votes and Proceedings*, October 8, 1908, https://www.aph.gov.au/binaries/house/info/votes/3/3rvpf014.pdf (search-indexed text inspected 2026-10-24; direct parliamentary site access was blocked). Verify the PDF before repeating ballot totals. National Capital Authority's dated chronology, https://www.nca.gov.au/history-national-capital (inspected 2026-10-24), separates 1908 selection from the 1912 plan competition and 1913 naming.
- Limits: The law records the Commonwealth decision, not local consent, construction outcomes or the full history of Ngunnawal and other First Nations people. Any land-impact or cultural account needs community-authored records and careful attribution. This historical legal case does not speak to current capital rankings or present-day planning authority.

## Scope and overlap

This named district-selection decision differs from BANK-448-039 on Brasilia's region, BANK-448-040 on Abuja's relocation rationale, and BANK-218-078 on mapping disputed boundaries. It precedes later Canberra urban planning, which may be covered separately.

The separately integrated 443 flag-count case concerns UN membership and headquarters display conventions. It does not determine the legal location or design of a national capital.
