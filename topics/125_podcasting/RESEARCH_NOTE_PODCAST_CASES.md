# Selected primary-source research: podcast cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These checks bound three proposed questions. They do not certify the other archived claims, popularity figures, production readiness or current market shares.

## WTF episode 613 and the Obama interview — BANK-125-046

- Primary episode record: WTF with Marc Maron, [Episode 613, President Barack Obama](https://www.wtfpod.com/podcast/episodes/episode_613_-_president_barack_obama), published June 22, 2015. Maron’s [contemporaneous account](https://www.wtfpod.com/dispatches/entries/it_was_a_privilege_and_an_honor) dates the garage recording to June 19.
- Primary government account: [Obama White House press briefing, June 22, 2015](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2015/06/23/daily-press-briefing-press-secretary-6222015). The press secretary described an open-ended, roughly hour-long garage conversation, not a single-issue announcement.
- Limit: These sources establish an episode and the administration’s account of its purpose. They do not establish a Secret Service street closure, exact audience size or a general presidential media strategy. The archived “dusty cat garage” wording is colorful source metadata, not a checked finding.

## Spotify’s Rogan distribution terms — BANK-125-047

- Primary platform announcements: Spotify, [May 2020 exclusive partnership](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2020-05-19/the-joe-rogan-experience-launches-exclusive-partnership-with-spotify/) and [February 2024 multiyear renewal](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-02-02/the-art-of-podcasting-with-joe-rogan-and-his-new-multiyear-spotify-partnership/).
- The 2020 announcement described an exclusive licensing deal. The February 2024 announcement said the show would soon be available on additional platforms. This supports a distribution-change question.
- Limit: Spotify’s public announcements are interested company sources and do not disclose full contract economics. They do not prove a stated dollar value, a loss, an admission of defeat or a general industry trend. Renewals and platform availability are date-specific; check the exact comparison period before scripting.

## Podcast downloads under a dated IAB guide — BANK-125-048

- Primary industry-method document: Interactive Advertising Bureau, [Digital Audio Measurement Guide](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/IAB_Audio_Measurement_Guide_Final.pdf), 2022. Its podcast section cites IAB Podcast Measurement Technical Guidelines 2.1 and describes filtering bots, a unique IP-address/user-agent combination in 24 hours and at least 60 seconds of downloaded audio.
- Limit: A legitimate download request is a server-side measure; it does not certify a completed listen, attention, a unique human or agreement with a platform’s first-party audience numbers. Version 2.1 is the guide’s cited standard, so do not project it onto every host or every later specification without checking the applicable version.
