# Research note: disability history and access cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These source-access notes are bounded premises for proposed BANK questions. They are not complete source verification or IDEA readiness.

## BANK-179-045 — 2024 U.S. airline device handling

- U.S. Department of Transportation, [Air Travel Consumer Report: December and full year 2024](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/air-travel-consumer-report-december-2024-full-year-2024-numbers), reports a 1.26% mishandled wheelchair-and-scooter rate among reporting marketing carriers for 2024, versus 1.38% in 2023. For December 2024 it reports 925 mishandled of 76,630 checked devices.
- U.S. Department of Transportation, [2024 final rule on safe accommodations for wheelchair users](https://www.transportation.gov/regulations/federal-register-documents/2024-29731), defines mishandling to include loss, delay, damage or theft and documents the rulemaking scope.

**Claim limit:** This is a checked-device reporting denominator for specified U.S. carriers, not a count of unique passengers or a measure of every country and every airline. A lower aggregate rate does not negate severe individual harm. Check the rule's current effective status and any later amendments before legal or traveler-rights claims. Obtain consent before recounting an individual's injury, equipment damage or care needs.

## BANK-179-046 — Argus II lifecycle

- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, [Argus II HDE record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfhde/hde.cfm?id=h110002), decision February 13, 2013. The indication is specific to adults with severe to profound retinitis pigmentosa who meet listed criteria; it assumes follow-up, device fitting and rehabilitation.
- Second Sight Medical Products, [2019 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1266806/000156459019043531/eyes-10q_20190930.htm), describes a May 2019 decision to accelerate the Orion transition and an impairment of Argus II inventory based on plans to suspend production.
- Second Sight, [2021 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1266806/000175392622000356/g082651_10k.htm), reports cessation of Argus II production and commercial activities, including effects on warranty accounting.

**Claim limit:** FDA authorization and company production filings do not describe each recipient's present device function, clinical care, support access or personal preferences. The archive's claim that implants were universally abandoned or went dark is not established. Seek user-led organizations and consented recipient perspectives; avoid "miracle" and tragedy frames. No treatment or device-operation instructions follow from this history.
