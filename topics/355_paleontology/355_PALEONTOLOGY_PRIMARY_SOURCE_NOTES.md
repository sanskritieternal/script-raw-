## BANK-355-042 — Hallucigenia sparsa

- Primary paper: Smith and Caron, *Hallucigenia’s head and the pharyngeal armature of early ecdysozoans*, *Nature* 523 (2015): https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14573 . Author-university repository record with manuscript: https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/e49a4811-1d34-4ecc-9e33-2c9e877ac4ab .
- The authors reexamined Burgess Shale specimens including Royal Ontario Museum material and described anterior eyes and mouthparts, revising the reconstruction of the head and arguing for deeper anatomical homologies among molting animals.
- Limit: the repository fetch timed out in this review; the indexed original-paper abstract and specimen captions were inspected. The preserved structures and phylogenetic interpretation have different confidence levels; do not infer full feeding behavior, exact colors or a direct ancestry chain.
