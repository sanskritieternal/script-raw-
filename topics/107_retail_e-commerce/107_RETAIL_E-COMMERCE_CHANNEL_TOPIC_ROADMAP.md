## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Retail & E-Commerce across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Retail Foundations, Unit Economics & Store Psychology (Beginner / Scratch)** — Evolution from department store catalogs to 1-click checkout, CAC/LTV mathematics, conversion rate optimization (CRO), business model selection, and supermarket layout psychology.
- **Tier 2: Platform Channels, Reverse Logistics & Algorithmic Pricing (Intermediate)** — Shopify vs Amazon platform dynamics, liquidation of customer returns, dynamic yield pricing engines, subscription box churn curves, and BOPIS omnichannel logistics.
- **Tier 3: Ultra-Fast Supply Chains, Livestreaming & Retail Media (Upper Intermediate)** — Shein/Zara micro-batch agile apparel, Chinese Douyin livestream commerce vs Western friction, marketplace liquidity flywheels, retail media ad networks, and safety stock resilience.
- **Tier 4: Cashierless Automation, Mall Transformations & Global B2B (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Computer vision autonomous stores, suburban mall adaptive reuse, de minimis customs loopholes, multi-trillion B2B procurement portals, and omnichannel enterprise architecture.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Retail Revolution: From Department Stores to 1-Click Checkout**
   - *Question:* How did shopping transform from Montgomery Ward mail-order catalogs to frictionless algorithmic storefronts?
   - *Key Focus:* Sears catalog era, suburban mall boom, barcode scanners, Amazon genesis, and direct-to-consumer waves.
2. **The Economics of E-Commerce: Gross Margins, CAC, and the Returns Crisis**
   - *Question:* Why do digital commerce brands generating $100M in revenue frequently lose millions on the bottom line?
   - *Key Focus:* Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), 30% return rates in apparel, shipping overhead.
3. **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO): The Science of the Add-to-Cart Button**
   - *Question:* How does a 0.5% lift in checkout conversion generate millions in enterprise revenue?
   - *Key Focus:* Cart friction removal, trust badges, sticky add-to-cart buttons, social proof, and mobile UX design.
4. **Dropshipping vs Private Label vs Omnichannel: Business Models Deconstructed**
   - *Question:* Which retail distribution model builds lasting enterprise value versus fleeting cash flow?
   - *Key Focus:* Inventory risk, supply chain moats, white-labeling, wholesale, and direct-to-consumer trade-offs.
5. **Retail Store Psychology: Why Milk is Always at the Very Back of the Supermarket**
   - *Question:* How do physical supermarket floor plans manipulate shopper dwell time and cart value?
   - *Key Focus:* Gruen effect, endcap promotions, eye-level shelf pricing, impulse buy registers, and sensory lighting.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Shopify vs Amazon: The Duel for the Soul of Digital Commerce**
   - *Question:* Should independent brands build on Amazon's walled garden or own their customer data on Shopify?
   - *Key Focus:* FBA logistics vs independent headless commerce, platform take-rates, brand equity ownership.
7. **The Reverse Logistics Nightmare: Where Returned Products Actually Go**
   - *Question:* Why is it often cheaper for retailers to throw returned electronics and clothes in landfills than restock them?
   - *Key Focus:* Pallet liquidation auctions, return processing centers, freight costs, and sustainability impacts.
8. **Dynamic Pricing Algorithms: How Retailers Change Prices While You Browse**
   - *Question:* How do airlines, Amazon, and Uber adjust prices in real time based on your battery life and device type?
   - *Key Focus:* Yield management, competitive scraping, elasticity modeling, and surge price triggers.
9. **Subscription E-Commerce: Churn Rates, Box Fatigue, and Retention Strategies**
   - *Question:* Why did hundreds of meal-kit and beauty box startups collapse after explosive early customer signups?
   - *Key Focus:* Monthly churn cliffs, cohort retention curves, diminishing perceived value, and replenishment models.
10. **Omnichannel Retail: Uniting Physical Stores with Digital Inventory**
   - *Question:* How did Walmart, Target, and Zara survive the 'Retail Apocalypse' by turning stores into fulfillment hubs?
   - *Key Focus:* BOPIS (Buy Online, Pick Up In Store), unified POS systems, ship-from-store, and endless aisle concepts.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Fast Fashion Economics: How Shein and Zara Produce Clothes in 7 Days**
   - *Question:* What agile manufacturing software allows ultra-fast fashion brands to test thousands of micro-batches daily?
   - *Key Focus:* On-demand contract factories, algorithm-driven trend scraping, cross-border de minimis tax exemptions.
12. **Livestream Shopping: Why China Sells Billions on TikTok and the West Still Struggles**
   - *Question:* How did live commerce on Taobao and Douyin reach $500B while Western shoppers resisted video shopping?
   - *Key Focus:* KOL influencer culture, gamified flash discounts, payment ecosystem integration, and Western checkout friction.
13. **Marketplace Network Effects: Why Winner Takes Most in Digital Platforms**
   - *Question:* How do two-sided market dynamics create unassailable moats for platforms like eBay, Etsy, and MercadoLibre?
   - *Key Focus:* Chicken-and-egg launch problem, buyer-seller liquidity, platform governance, and disintermediation defense.
14. **Retail Media Networks: Why Supermarkets are Becoming Ad Agencies**
   - *Question:* Why are Walmart, Target, and Kroger generating billions in pure profit selling ads on their own search bars?
   - *Key Focus:* First-party purchase intent data, offsite audience targeting, in-store digital screens, and 80% ad margins.
15. **Supply Chain Resilience: From Just-In-Time to Just-In-Case Inventory**
   - *Question:* What structural supply chain shifts occurred when global shipping bottlenecks crippled pandemic retail?
   - *Key Focus:* Safety stock calculation, supplier diversification, nearshoring, and container freight volatility.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Autonomous Stores and Cashierless Checkout: The Computer Vision Future**
   - *Question:* How do Amazon Go and smart cart sensors track hundreds of items without barcode scanning?
   - *Key Focus:* Ceiling camera sensor fusion, weight-sensor shelving, shrinkage rates, and implementation capex.
17. **The Death of the Suburban Mall: What Happens to Millions of Square Feet of Dead Retail?**
   - *Question:* Why did iconic shopping malls across America become ghost towns, and what will replace them?
   - *Key Focus:* Anchor tenant bankruptcies, adaptive reuse into data centers/apartments, and experiential luxury hubs.
18. **The Ethics of De Minimis and Counterfeits in Cross-Border E-Commerce**
   - *Question:* How do loophole customs exemptions allow billions in untaxed, counterfeit goods to enter domestic markets?
   - *Key Focus:* Intellectual property enforcement, safety recalls, customs inspection bottlenecks, and unfair domestic competition.
19. **B2B E-Commerce: The Multi-Trillion Dollar Wholesale Digital Modernization**
   - *Question:* Why is business-to-business digital procurement growing twice as fast as consumer retail?
   - *Key Focus:* Custom credit terms, ERP integrations, wholesale catalogs, punchout sites, and complex purchase orders.
20. **Building a Modern Retail Empire: The Omnichannel Architecture Masterclass**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive strategic framework unites product development, digital acquisition, and retail shelf presence?
   - *Key Focus:* Unit economics, supply chain redundancy, unified customer database, and defensive retail moats.
