# BANK-107-069 — Just Walk Out format decisions

Checked 2026-10-24. Selected company-record chronology; not a retailer recommendation or an independent performance audit.

The archived pitch asks whether Amazon “abandoned” Just Walk Out grocery stores. Amazon's [2024 format update, amended in January 2026](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-just-walk-out-dash-cart-grocery-shopping-checkout-stores) says the company shifted large grocery trips toward Dash Cart while continuing to offer Just Walk Out in smaller third-party stores. Amazon's [physical-grocery announcement, updated May 2026](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-fresh-go-stores-closing-expanding-whole-foods) says it decided to close Amazon Go and Amazon Fresh physical stores, convert some to Whole Foods Market, and continue Just Walk Out in third-party venues and some Amazon operations. These pages support a **change in owned grocery format**, not a claim that the technology disappeared.

The pages are Amazon's own account. Their store counts, customer preferences and sales effects need dated independent confirmation before narration. Closure decisions may have further changed since the updates; verify store-level status and worker/customer effects at production time. Keep Dash Cart, Just Walk Out and ordinary staffed checkout distinct.
