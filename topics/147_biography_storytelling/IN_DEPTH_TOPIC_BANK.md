#### BANK-147-034 — How can an interview preserve a subject's voice without adopting every claim?

**Individual coverage scope:** Separate testimony, corroboration, chronology and editorial response with consented examples.

<a id="bank-147-035"></a>
#### BANK-147-035 — Why can two honest witnesses remember the same person differently?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare vantage point, relationship, elapsed time and context; avoid diagnosing motives or memory disorders.

<a id="bank-147-036"></a>
#### BANK-147-036 — How should a biography use a diary written for an imagined future reader?

**Individual coverage scope:** Examine intended audience, revisions, gaps and self-presentation rather than treating privacy as proof of candor.

<a id="bank-147-037"></a>
#### BANK-147-037 — What can financial and household records add to an account of creative work?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace time, resources, dependents and institutions without exposing irrelevant private information.

### Comparative lives and photograph attribution

Subcategory `CATALOG-147-R08` · advanced.

<a id="bank-147-042"></a>
#### BANK-147-042 — How can a parallel biography compare two lives without pretending their opportunities and institutions were equivalent?

**Individual coverage scope:** Define the shared question, comparison dimensions, different periods, source coverage and disconfirming cases; avoid a predetermined moral contest.

<a id="bank-147-043"></a>
#### BANK-147-043 — How can a biographer establish whether an archival photograph shows the claimed person, place and date?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare captions, negatives or contact sheets, provenance, publication history and corroborating records; preserve uncertain identifications and avoid identifying private living people from an image.

## Category: Biography responsibility

<a id="catalog-147-r07"></a>
### Interpretation and publication

Subcategory `CATALOG-147-R07` · advanced.

<a id="bank-147-038"></a>
#### BANK-147-038 — When does a biographical scene become fictionalized rather than reconstructed?

**Individual coverage scope:** Separate exact quotations, paraphrases, inferred actions and invented dialogue in production notes and presentation.

<a id="bank-147-039"></a>
#### BANK-147-039 — How should a living person's right of reply affect a critical documentary?

**Individual coverage scope:** Explain specific allegations, adequate response opportunities, corroboration and editorial independence without publishing unverified claims.

<a id="bank-147-040"></a>
#### BANK-147-040 — How can a biography resist the assumption that success was inevitable?

**Individual coverage scope:** Restore abandoned plans, chance events, alternatives and knowledge available at each decision.

<a id="bank-147-041"></a>
#### BANK-147-041 — What should remain unresolved at the end of a biography?

**Individual coverage scope:** Identify documentary gaps and competing interpretations instead of forcing a moral verdict.

<a id="bank-147-047"></a>
#### BANK-147-047 — What can a documented biography contract establish about advance, royalty and screen rights?

**Individual coverage scope:** Select an accessible, dated agreement or publisher disclosure; distinguish advance recoupment, gross sales, author share and a separately negotiated adaptation option without inventing earnings.
