## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes cold case forensics, criminal profiling, judicial proceedings, systemic institutional accountability, and victim advocacy across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Foundations of Forensic Evidence & Criminal Law (Scratch / Beginner)** — Physical evidence chain of custody, DNA profiling breakthrough history, ballistics vs junk science, and the anatomy of a courtroom trial.
- **Tier 2: Landmark Investigations & Master Sleuthing (Intermediate)** — Tracking digital footprints, cell tower triangulation, handwriting/linguistic analysis, interrogation psychology (Reid technique vs PEACE method), and false confessions.
- **Tier 3: Complex Cold Cases, Heists & White-Collar Frauds (Upper Intermediate)** — Cold cases cracked by genetic genealogy, forensic accounting (Ponzi schemes, embezzlement), art heists, and maritime/jurisdictional loopholes.
- **Tier 4: Systemic Institutional Audits & Miscarriages of Justice (Advanced / Expert)** — Exonerations via The Innocence Project, prosecutorial misconduct, confirmation bias in police departments, forensic pathology errors, and landmark reform legislation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundations of Forensic Evidence & Criminal Law
1. **The Golden 48 Hours: What Crime Scene Investigators Actually Do First**
   - *Question:* How do investigators secure perimeters, preserve touch DNA, and prevent evidence contamination?
   - *Key Focus:* Chain of custody, luminol blood detection, grid search protocols, initial 911 call analysis.
2. **The DNA Revolution: How Alec Jeffreys Rewrote Criminal Justice Forever**
   - *Question:* How did genetic fingerprinting solve the 1983 Narborough murders and clear an innocent suspect?
   - *Key Focus:* STR (short tandem repeat) markers, electrophoresis gel matching, the dawn of forensic CODIS.
3. **Junk Science on Trial: The Flawed Forensics That Put Innocent People on Death Row**
   - *Question:* Why have bite-mark analysis, arson burn patterns, and microscopic hair comparisons been debunked?
   - *Key Focus:* 2009 National Academy of Sciences report, Innocence Project exonerations, cognitive bias in examiners.
4. **The Anatomy of a Murder Trial: Beyond Reasonable Doubt Explained**
   - *Question:* How do circumstantial evidence, direct physical proof, and jury instructions interact in court?
   - *Key Focus:* Grand juries, burden of proof, voir dire jury selection, hearsay exceptions, cross-examination.

### Tier 2: Landmark Investigations & Master Sleuthing
5. **The Golden State Killer: How Investigative Genetic Genealogy Cracked a 40-Year Cold Case**
   - *Question:* How did Paul Holes use GEDmatch public ancestry databases to track Joseph James DeAngelo?
   - *Key Focus:* Familial DNA triangulation, family tree reverse-engineering, discarding discarded DNA forensics.
6. **The Interrogation Room: Why Innocent People Confess to Horrific Crimes**
   - *Question:* How do coercive psychological techniques (Reid Technique) break innocent suspects' minds?
   - *Key Focus:* Central Park Five case, sleep deprivation, false promise of leniency, mandatory video recording laws.
7. **Digital Footprints: How Cell Towers, Apple Watches, and Fitbits Solve Murders**
   - *Question:* How do heart rate spikes, GPS pings, and Wi-Fi handshakes construct minute-by-minute alibis?
   - *Key Focus:* Geofence warrants, telemetry extraction, step-counter timing, metadata timestamps in court.
8. **Forensic Linguistics: How an FBI Agent Caught the Unabomber via Syntax and Spelling**
   - *Question:* How did James R. Fitzgerald match Ted Kaczynski's 35,000-word manifesto to private family letters?
   - *Key Focus:* Idiolect analysis, archaic spelling ('cool-headed logician'), search warrant justification.

### Tier 3: Complex Cold Cases, Heists & White-Collar Frauds
9. **The Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum Heist: $500 Million in Art Gone in 81 Minutes**
   - *Question:* How did two fake Boston police officers steal Rembrandt and Vermeer masterpieces in 1990?
   - *Key Focus:* Motion detector logs, cut canvas frames, Boston mafia connections, federal statute of limitations.
10. **The Bernie Madoff Ponzi Scheme: How Wall Street Regulators Missed a $65 Billion Fraud**
    - *Question:* How did Harry Markopolos spend 8 years mathematically proving Madoff was running a fraud before the crash?
    - *Key Focus:* Split-strike conversion math, SEC institutional blindness, feeder funds, recovering $14 billion for victims.
11. **The Zodiac Killer: Decrypting the 340 Cipher After 51 Years of Silence**
    - *Question:* How did an international team of amateur codebreakers finally crack the killer's 1969 diagonal cipher in 2020?
    - *Key Focus:* Transposition cypher algorithms, FBI verification, remaining identity enigmas.
12. **The Tylenol Murders: The 1982 Poisonings That Changed Every Medicine Bottle in the World**
    - *Question:* How did cyanide tampering in Chicago pharmacies trigger modern tamper-evident safety packaging?
    - *Key Focus:* Product recall economics, FDA triple-seal mandates, uncharged prime suspects, copycat crimes.

### Tier 4: Systemic Institutional Audits & Miscarriages of Justice
13. **The Innocence Project: How DNA Has Exonerated 375+ Wrongfully Convicted People**
    - *Question:* What systemic flaws (eyewitness misidentification, jailhouse informants) cause false convictions?
    - *Key Focus:* Barry Scheck & Peter Neufeld, eyewitness reform (double-blind lineups), post-conviction DNA statutes.
14. **The Darlie Routier Case: Bloodstain Pattern Analysis Under Microscopic Scrutiny**
    - *Question:* Does cast-off blood spatter and vacuum glass evidence prove an intruder or maternal guilt?
    - *Key Focus:* Luminol sock evidence, contradictory medical triage notes, pending appellate DNA tests.
15. **The Atlanta Child Murders: Wayne Williams, Carpet Fibers, and Lingering Doubts**
    - *Question:* How did trilobal green carpet fibers convict one man while leaving dozens of cases open?
    - *Key Focus:* Fiber probability statistics, reopening 1979–1981 cold case files, advanced DNA re-testing.
16. **The McDonald's Monopoly Fraud: How the Mob Stole $24 Million in Winning Game Pieces**
    - *Question:* How did the head of security for Simon Marketing funnel winning Million-Dollar game pieces to accomplices?
    - *Key Focus:* Operation 'Final Answer', postal inspection sting operations, trial of 'Uncle Jerry' Colombo.
17. **The Black Dahlia: Why Hollywood's Most Gruesome Cold Case Will Never Be Solved**
    - *Question:* How did corrupt 1947 LAPD detectives and aggressive sensational journalists ruin the crime scene?
    - *Key Focus:* Pastiche letter hoaxes, surgical bisection techniques, corrupt police archives, false death confessions.
18. **The Enron Scandal: How Accounting Sleight-of-Hand Toppled America's 7th Largest Corporation**
    - *Question:* How did mark-to-market accounting and Special Purpose Entities (SPEs) hide billions in corporate debt?
    - *Key Focus:* Whistleblower Sherron Watkins, Arthur Andersen destruction of shredder files, Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002.
19. **The JonBenét Ramsey Case: 24 Hours of Botched Police Procedure and Media Circus**
    - *Question:* What mistakes during the initial search of the Boulder home permanently compromised the investigation?
    - *Key Focus:* Failure to seal the house, the 3-page ransom note handwriting, touch DNA on waistband evidence.
20. **The Lindbergh Baby Kidnapping: The Trial of the Century and Forensic Wood Anatomy**
    - *Question:* How did Arthur Koehler match wood grain in the kidnapping ladder to Bruno Hauptmann's attic floor?
    - *Key Focus:* Early wood science forensics, marked ransom gold certificates, 1930s federal Lindbergh Law.
