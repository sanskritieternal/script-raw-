# BANK-010-126 — Gardner Museum theft record boundary

Status: bounded public-source review for a case proposal, not a finished case investigation or script. Inspected 2026-10-24.

## Primary public sources inspected

- [FBI case history](https://www.fbi.gov/history/cases-and-criminals/isabella-stewart-gardner-museum-heist): describes the March 18, 1990 theft, two people dressed as police officers, 13 stolen works, the 81-minute interval, and an ongoing investigation. Its value estimate is explicitly tied to the time of the heist.
- [FBI stolen-art notice](https://www.fbi.gov/wanted/seeking-info/stolen-museum-artwork): lists the case as a continuing recovery effort and distinguishes the museum's reward from FBI administration of that reward.
- [Gardner Museum description of the stolen works](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/isabella-stewart-gardner-museum-releases-stolen-first-ever-book): identifies the 13 works and the museum's own account of the theft.

## Editorial boundary

The archived “$500 million in 81 minutes” hook is a dated value estimate and a public timeline, not a solved theory of motive, perpetrators or the artwork's present whereabouts. A future case episode must reconcile the FBI and museum timelines against original public records and any later developments, distinguish physical works from valuations, avoid naming uncharged private people, and confirm case status immediately before publication. No current tip, suspect theory or recovery claim has been independently verified here.
