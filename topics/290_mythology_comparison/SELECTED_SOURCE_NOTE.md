# Selected primary-text note: mythology comparison

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed case BANK-290-043.

The University of Oxford Electronic Text Corpus of Sumerian Literature presents *Inana's descent to the nether world*, c.1.4.1, with line-numbered translation and variant notices: https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/section1/tr141.htm . Its linked transliteration/glossing lets a researcher inspect some source-language decisions: https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/edition2/etcslgloss.php?lookup=c141.5&sn=ON . Perseus at Tufts presents Ovid, *Metamorphoses* book 10 in Hugo Magnus's Latin edition, including Orpheus and Eurydice: https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.02.0029%3Abook%3D10 . These are two specific textual witnesses and editorial presentations, not the entirety of Sumerian or Greek/Roman underworld traditions. Compare conditions, agents and narrative purposes; resemblance alone cannot establish borrowing or universal origins. Identify any English Ovid translator and check Oxford/Perseus text and image reuse terms before extensive quotation.
