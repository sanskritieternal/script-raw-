## Bounded premise

DWP introduced an updated material-deprivation question set from FYE 2024 and a simple-count approach in place of the earlier prevalence-weighted score. The FYE 2025 release explicitly warns that material-deprivation estimates from before FYE 2024 should not be directly compared with those from FYE 2024 onward. For its combined child low-income/material-deprivation measure, the report describes an income threshold below 70% of the median and lacking at least four listed items. This is a defined UK survey metric, not a direct observation of every household's welfare.

## Claim and access limits

A measurement break can produce a misleading apparent trend; it does not prove that deprivation itself improved or worsened. HBAI uses the Family Resources Survey and has sampling/coverage and subgroup uncertainties. The current release states some regional official statistics are in development and advises caution for comparisons; explain the applicable geography and interval. Do not combine before-housing-cost, after-housing-cost, relative, absolute and material-deprivation indicators as if they were interchangeable. Neither the source nor this note establishes the causal effect of a poverty policy. Recheck tables and methodology before scripting or updating figures.
