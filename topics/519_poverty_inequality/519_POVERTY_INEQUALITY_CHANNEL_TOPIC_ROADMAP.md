## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Poverty & Inequality across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Poverty & Inequality Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Poverty & Inequality.
- **Tier 2: Poverty & Inequality Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Poverty & Inequality.
- **Tier 3: Poverty & Inequality Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Poverty & Inequality.
- **Tier 4: Poverty & Inequality Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Poverty & Inequality.

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Poverty & Inequality: How Poverty & Inequality Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a poverty measure's purpose shape its original definition?

Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: household microdata documentation, thresholds, prices, survey weights and original policy evaluations. Starting framework: World Bank poverty measurement methodology (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099101424092027337/pdf/P500855154ddde0b31827a1e9377c43d15e.pdf).
