## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Roller Coasters across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Roller Coasters Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Roller Coasters.
- **Tier 2: Roller Coasters Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Roller Coasters.
- **Tier 3: Roller Coasters Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Roller Coasters.
- **Tier 4: Roller Coasters Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Roller Coasters.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Roller Coasters: A Coaster Design Develops**
   - *Question:* Which documented design change altered a particular coaster type?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated drawings, patents, and ride records. Evidence base: manufacturer plans and publications, operator information, applicable standards and inspection records where public, measured data, and bounded rider accounts.
2. **Roller Coasters: One Ride's Layout**
   - *Question:* How does a named coaster's route create its sequence of sensations?
   - *Key Focus:* Use verified layout and operating version, not promotional superlatives. Evidence base: manufacturer plans and publications, operator information, applicable standards and inspection records where public, measured data, and bounded rider accounts.
3. **Roller Coasters: Engineering and Operations Labor**
   - *Question:* Whose design, inspection, and daily work keeps a coaster running?
   - *Key Focus:* Use manufacturer and operator records where available. Evidence base: manufacturer plans and publications, operator information, applicable standards and inspection records where public, measured data, and bounded rider accounts.
4. **Roller Coasters: From Lift to Brake Run**
   - *Question:* How does a particular train gain, exchange, and lose energy?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a simplified bounded diagram with units and losses. Evidence base: manufacturer plans and publications, operator information, applicable standards and inspection records where public, measured data, and bounded rider accounts.
5. **Roller Coasters: Forces along the Track**
   - *Question:* How do speed, curvature, and rider position affect measured acceleration?
   - *Key Focus:* Use actual or clearly illustrative data; state uncertainty. Evidence base: manufacturer plans and publications, operator information, applicable standards and inspection records where public, measured data, and bounded rider accounts.

### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Roller Coasters: A Coaster Retired**
   - *Question:* What documented condition led to closure or replacement?

   - *Question:* What changed in train, track, restraint, or access?
