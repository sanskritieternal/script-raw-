## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Magic across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Magic Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Magic.
- **Tier 2: Magic Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Magic.
- **Tier 3: Magic Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Magic.
- **Tier 4: Magic Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Magic.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Magic: A Conjuring Tradition Documented**
   - *Question:* Which dated performance or manual records a named effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare primary texts and avoid a single invention claim. Evidence base: dated programs and manuals, recorded performances, credited production artifacts, practitioner testimony, and bounded perception experiments.
2. **Magic: A Classic Effect Revisited**
   - *Question:* What does the audience see, infer, and later remember?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a specified performance and audience evidence if available. Evidence base: dated programs and manuals, recorded performances, credited production artifacts, practitioner testimony, and bounded perception experiments.
3. **Magic: Assistants and Builders in Magic**
   - *Question:* Whose rehearsal, engineering, or stage work shaped an effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Check programs, contracts, and attributed accounts. Evidence base: dated programs and manuals, recorded performances, credited production artifacts, practitioner testimony, and bounded perception experiments.
4. **Magic: Effect versus Method**
   - *Question:* What distinction lets an audience enjoy an impossible-seeming event?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe visible sequence without publishing a protected method. Evidence base: dated programs and manuals, recorded performances, credited production artifacts, practitioner testimony, and bounded perception experiments.
5. **Magic: Attention in a Routine**
   - *Question:* Which cue changes what spectators notice at a critical moment?
