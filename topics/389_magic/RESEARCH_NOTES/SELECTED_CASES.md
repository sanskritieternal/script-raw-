- [The Library of Congress catalog and digitized poster](https://www.loc.gov/item/2014636906/) identify Harry and Beatrice Houdini, Seymour & Muir Printing, and the 1898 Smith's Opera House engagement. The headline advertises them as “original introducers” of *Metamorphosis* and presents a challenge handcuff act. That wording is a promoter's contemporaneous claim, not independent proof of invention or a complete list of collaborators.
- The artifact can support analysis of advertised effect, image, billing and audience expectation. It cannot establish how a performance worked, exactly what happened on stage, or whether rival origin claims were false. Compare other dated posters, programs and performance accounts before attribution.
