# Selected VR and AR case-source notes

Inspected 2026-10-24. These are dated premise checks, not a complete product, human-factors or sales study. The proposed topics retain empty `verified_sources` until individual production review.

## Vision Pro app catalog — BANK-128-043

- [Apple's 1 February 2024 launch-catalog statement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/02/apple-announces-more-than-600-new-apps-built-for-apple-vision-pro/) said more than 600 apps and games were designed for Vision Pro, alongside over one million compatible apps. [Apple's June 2024 visionOS 2 statement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/visionos-2-brings-new-spatial-computing-experiences-to-apple-vision-pro/) said more than 2,000 spatial apps and more than 1.5 million compatible iOS and iPadOS apps.
- These are manufacturer claims about app availability at two dates and two categories. They do not disclose sales, active users, retention or the archive's assertion that “millions stopped using” Vision Pro. A user-behavior episode would require independent, defined engagement data and a comparable baseline.

## Meta's 2024 smart-glasses distinction — BANK-128-044

- [Meta's September 2024 Orion announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/09/introducing-orion-our-first-true-augmented-reality-glasses/amp/) calls the then-current Ray-Ban Meta glasses displayless, while describing Orion as a holographic-display AR prototype made available to employees and selected outside audiences. These primary product statements support a functional and distribution-status comparison for that date.
- The source is the manufacturer's promotional account, not independent measurement of image quality, field of view, popularity or privacy performance. The [September 2025 Meta Ray-Ban Display announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/) shows why model and date must be specified: a later Ray-Ban product includes a display. Neither product's popularity alone establishes that full AR glasses succeeded.
