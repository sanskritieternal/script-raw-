# Selected Indian-city source checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. The archived city formats are generic prompts, not verified case facts or production approvals.

## BANK-220-045 — Mumbai flood and drainage planning

The [Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation 2025 yearbook](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/HomePage%20Data/Whats%20New/English%20Year%20Book%202025%20Portal.pdf) describes the 26–27 July 2005 flood, subsequent fact-finding process and BRIMSTOWAD planning and funding. An [MCGM project terms-of-reference record](https://www.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/Tenders/ETH/ETH_8800646694_290121.pdf) identifies catchment survey and later review work. These are agency accounts of planning and implementation; neither demonstrates that every neighborhood stopped flooding or attributes the entire event to one drainage failure. Existing BANK-220-033 owns Mumbai's long-run shoreline formation; this case owns one dated stormwater-policy response.

## BANK-220-046 — city population boundary

The [Census of India 2011 district handbook definitions](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/1316/download/4278/DH_2011_0511_PART_A_DCHB_NAINITAL.pdf) separate statutory towns, census towns and urban agglomerations. The [Census population finder](https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/population-finder) provides geographic levels down to villages, towns and wards. A reported city population or rank needs its table, year and boundary before comparison. This methods question is distinct from the history of Delhi's older urban centers at BANK-220-037.
