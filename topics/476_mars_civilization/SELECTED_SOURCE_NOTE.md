# Selected source note — CHAPEA Mission 1

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for BANK-476-044. Proposed question only; no approved IDEA or settlement readiness claim.

## Primary agency records

- NASA, *About CHAPEA*: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/about-chapea/ . NASA defines the Crew Health and Performance Exploration Analog as simulated year-long Mars stays with four volunteers in an isolated 1,700-square-foot habitat, including resource, maintenance, communication and workload constraints. It records Mission 1 dates, June 25, 2023 to July 6, 2024.
- NASA, *CHAPEA Mission 1*: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/chapea-mission-1/ . This names the crew and confirms start and end dates.
- NASA, *First Mars Crew Completes Yearlong Simulated Red Planet NASA Mission* (July 10, 2024): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/analog-field-testing/chapea/first-mars-crew-completes-yearlong-simulated-red-planet-nasa-mission/ . NASA reports a 378-day simulated stay and the study purpose.

## Test versus settlement limits

Mars Dune Alpha is at Johnson Space Center in Houston. It did not fly to Mars, face Martian gravity, radiation or a real cargo interruption, or establish that a self-sufficient settlement is feasible. NASA describes aims and activities; public summaries alone do not supply all measured health or performance results. Do not diagnose crew members or infer psychological effects from anecdotes. Check original study outputs before asserting outcomes. Paraphrase NASA text and check rights for crew portraits or habitat imagery.
