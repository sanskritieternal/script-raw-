## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Science Careers: A Scientific Occupation Changes**
   - *Question:* Which tools and institutions created a named role?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated workforce and research records. Evidence base: NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts.
2. **Science Careers: A Science Career Path**
   - *Question:* What work did one scientist or technician actually perform?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented or archival role evidence. Evidence base: NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts.
3. **Science Careers: Technical Workers in Science**
   - *Question:* Whose sampling, instrumentation, and care work is omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Use credits and labor records. Evidence base: NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts.
4. **Science Careers: Many Routes into Science**

   - *Key Focus:* Compare tasks and entry requirements. Evidence base: NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts.
5. **Science Careers: Degree versus Occupation**
   - *Question:* Why do graduates work across different roles?
   - *Key Focus:* Use NSF workforce definitions and pathways. Evidence base: NSF NCSES workforce definitions and survey data, dated occupation profiles, project credits, training outcomes, job postings, and attributed worker accounts.
