# Traditional-instrument case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected institutional heritage records describe named practices. A nomination is not a census of every maker or a guarantee that a craft has never changed.

| Case | Primary record inspected | Claim and access limit |
|---|---|---|
| Balaban/mey | [UNESCO 2023 joint nomination entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/craftsmanship-and-performing-art-of-balaban-mey-01704) describes Azerbaijan/Türkiye double-reed components, regional variation and informal and formal teaching. | Entry reflects the submitting communities and states; it does not quantify all contemporary makers or collapse regional forms into one instrument design. Public entry accessible; individual maker interviews remain to be checked. |
| Morin khuur | [UNESCO traditional music of the morin khuur entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-music-of-the-morin-khuur-00068?lang=en) describes instrument form, oral apprenticeship and adaptation for stage performance; [safeguarding project record](https://ich.unesco.org/en/projects/implementation-of-the-national-action-plan-for-the-safeguarding-of-traditional-morin-khuur-music-of-mongolia-00013) documents an institutional response. | Heritage wording is dated and may simplify material changes; it is not proof that all Mongolian players share one repertoire or sound. Project existence is not measured safeguarding success. |

Sitar, kora, sarangi and Indian percussion scopes remain proposed; no acoustical or attribution claim from the archive was independently verified here.
