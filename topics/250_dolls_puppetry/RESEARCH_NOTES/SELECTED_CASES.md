# Selected puppet cases — source review, 24 October 2026

The bank JSON routes the original 20 pending and seven trending formats to active questions. It preserves their original status text and IDs. These case premises are source-bound proposals, with no performance, community or filming permissions inferred.

## BANK-250-041 — Cambodian Sbek Thom

- [UNESCO's Sbek Thom inscription](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/royal-ballet-of-cambodia-00108) describes the large leather shadow figures, manipulation, ceremonial and theatrical contexts, and disruption and revival after the Khmer Rouge period. A [Cambodian periodic report hosted by UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/Signed%20periodic%20report%20-%20Periodic%20report-67245.pdf) describes later safeguarding activities and troupes.
- These institutional accounts support a question about craft and performance transmission. They are not a complete troupe census, do not validate a simple unbroken ancient lineage, and do not grant permission to reproduce sacred characters. Obtain practitioner testimony, material-object records and report dates before assigning particular changes to a named troupe. The whole-theatre history belongs with niche 249; this case follows figure making and manipulation within the theatre.

## BANK-250-042 — Tay Tham Roc rod puppets

- A [UNESCO documentation account](https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/01858-EN.pdf) describes a Vietnam Museum of Ethnology film project begun in 1999 after village puppets had been stored during decades without performances. It reports intergenerational learning around a return to performance and ceremonial concerns about retrieving the figures.
- The account supports a specific documentation-and-revival question, not a measured long-term revival outcome or permission to disclose ceremonies. View the film under its rights conditions and seek Tham Roc community perspectives before using visual or narrative details. This case is distinct from BANK-250-034's general warning about surviving objects without performance knowledge and BANK-250-040's museum conservation question.
