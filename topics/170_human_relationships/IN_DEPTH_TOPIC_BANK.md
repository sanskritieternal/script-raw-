### Intergenerational support and measurement

Subcategory `CATALOG-170-R08` · advanced.

<a id="bank-170-042"></a>
#### BANK-170-042 — How does a household negotiate support when children, parents and grandparents need different kinds of help at the same time?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace consent, time, money, distance, care work, autonomy and outside services through a fictional or consented bounded household; avoid treating one generation's sacrifice as a universal duty.

<a id="bank-170-043"></a>
#### BANK-170-043 — What can a relationship-quality questionnaire measure when partners, languages and cultural expectations differ?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare the construct, wording, respondent, translation, validation, disagreement and limits on individual interpretation; distinguish research measurement from diagnosis or a prediction about one relationship.

<a id="bank-170-044"></a>
#### BANK-170-044 — What do US age-specific divorce data show about marital dissolution after age 50?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare refined rates, age-group denominators, remarriage history and marriage duration in dated American Community Survey analyses; distinguish a rising share of divorces among older adults from a rising current divorce rate for every older age group. Do not infer why any one couple separates.

**Research follow-up:** [Bounded case and source-access note](SELECTED_SOURCE_NOTE.md). This does not change proposal or production status.

#### BANK-170-045 — What did a 2023 US survey actually ask about partners sleeping in different rooms?

**Individual coverage scope:** Read the survey question, sampling frame and occasional-versus-consistent responses; distinguish sleeping arrangement from relationship happiness, clinical sleep benefit or a global trend. Include partners with caregiving, shift-work and housing constraints without prescribing a choice.
