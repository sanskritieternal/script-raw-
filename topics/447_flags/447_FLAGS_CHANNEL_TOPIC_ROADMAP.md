## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Flags across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Flags Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Flags.
- **Tier 2: Flags Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Flags.
- **Tier 3: Flags Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Flags.
- **Tier 4: Flags Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Flags.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Flags: A Flag's Adoption**
   - *Question:* Which authority adopted one flag and what design preceded it?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated legal and archival records. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
2. **Flags: A Recognizable Flag in Context**
   - *Question:* Which version and use does a famous design represent?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify state, institution, and period. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
3. **Flags: Designers and Communities**
   - *Question:* Who proposed a flag and whose symbolism was excluded?
   - *Key Focus:* Use design record and community accounts. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
4. **Flags: What a Flag Represents**
   - *Question:* How do state, civic, military, and unofficial flags differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare documented uses. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
5. **Flags: Flag and Sovereignty**
   - *Question:* When does a flag's display imply a contested political claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate law, practice, and recognition. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Flags: Uncertain Flag Origin**
   - *Question:* Which evidence supports competing symbolism stories?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare contemporary adoption sources with later tales. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
7. **Flags: Flag Images Online**
   - *Question:* How do digital color and ratio conversions alter an official design?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare authoritative specifications and rendering. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
8. **Flags: A Flag Designer's Role**
   - *Question:* What can be attributed to a named designer?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace proposal, revisions, and formal adoption. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
9. **Flags: A Flag Changes**
   - *Question:* Which event prompted an official redesign?
   - *Key Focus:* Use legislation, public debate, and first use. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
10. **Flags: From Design to Protocol**
   - *Question:* How are a flag's dimensions and official use specified?
   - *Key Focus:* Read governing documents for one jurisdiction. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Flags: A Flag Falls from Official Use**
   - *Question:* Which legal or political change ended its status?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dates and continuing unofficial use. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
12. **Flags: Two Versions of a Flag**
   - *Question:* What changed in symbols, colors, or rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated official images. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
13. **Flags: A Proposed Flag Redesign**
   - *Question:* What process and consent would be required?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat proposals separately from adopted symbols. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
14. **Flags: A Popular Symbol Story**
   - *Question:* Does an origin claim match adoption records?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify earliest attributable explanation. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
15. **Flags: A Flag's Documentary Trail**
   - *Question:* How did proposal, adoption, reproduction, and use unfold?
   - *Key Focus:* Build dated evidence with version labels. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Flags: Flag and Territory**
   - *Question:* Does flag use match effective control or recognized boundaries?
   - *Key Focus:* State place, date, and actor. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
17. **Flags: A Flag across Jurisdictions**
   - *Question:* How does a symbol change meaning across border or diaspora?
   - *Key Focus:* Use documented display contexts. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
18. **Flags: on Commemorative Days**
   - *Question:* When is a flag displayed under a formal calendar?
   - *Key Focus:* Check current official protocol. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
19. **Flags: What the Flag Means Locally**
   - *Question:* How do community accounts differ from official symbolism?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute viewpoints; avoid universal claims. Evidence base: official adoption instruments and flag specifications, UN protocol records, archival designs, dated photographs, community testimony, and accessibility tests.
20. **Flags: Symbol of a Landscape**
   - *Question:* Does a depicted landform match geography or idealized design?
