## BANK-285-045 — Eşrefoğlu Mosque endowment record

- The [UNESCO World Heritage nomination for the wooden hypostyle mosques of medieval Anatolia](https://whc.unesco.org/document/192680) transcribes and discusses an inscription for Eşrefoğlu Mosque at Beyşehir. It reports an endowment dated 696 AH and lists income-bearing property categories, then separately describes a mosque portal inscription dated 699 AH.
- The nomination is an institutional secondary rendering of inscriptions. Inspect the original inscription or high-quality image, the cited epigraphic editions and any surviving waqf deed before quoting wording, translating dates or asserting an exact sequence. An endowed asset list is not proof of actual later receipts, beneficiaries or uninterrupted service. Later Ottoman waqf administration is a different period and would need its own records.
- This case is a concrete test of BANK-285-035's endowment mechanism. Architectural and site preservation belong to separate heritage topics, while this question tracks what a particular record says about support. It does not collapse Islamic legal schools, local worship or all Anatolian mosques into one model.
