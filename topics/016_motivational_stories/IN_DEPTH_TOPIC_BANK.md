### Failure and revision stories

<a id="bank-016-017"></a>
#### BANK-016-017 — How can an apprentice's repeated failures produce a believable learning arc?

**Individual coverage scope:** Give each attempt new information, a different choice and a visible skill change rather than identical failures followed by sudden mastery.

<a id="bank-016-018"></a>
#### BANK-016-018 — What makes a student restarting after an exam failure feel specific rather than generic?

**Individual coverage scope:** Define the mistake, resources, support and revised habits; avoid implying success is guaranteed by positive thinking.

<a id="bank-016-019"></a>
#### BANK-016-019 — How can a small business story reward sustainable choices rather than endless growth?

**Individual coverage scope:** Make tradeoffs, financial constraints and relationships part of the resolution instead of equating worth with revenue.

<a id="bank-016-020"></a>
#### BANK-016-020 — What changes when a runner returns with different goals after a setback?

**Individual coverage scope:** Build agency, adaptation and support rather than treating recovery as proof that limitations can always be overcome.

### Motivation and evidence

Subcategory `CATALOG-016-D03` · intermediate.

Prerequisites: [CATALOG-016-D01](#catalog-016-d01).

<a id="bank-016-021"></a>
#### BANK-016-021 — What does goal-setting research distinguish between intention and behavior?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare task specificity, resources, feedback and measured follow-through rather than promising that writing a goal makes it happen.

<a id="bank-016-022"></a>
#### BANK-016-022 — How can feedback support persistence without encouraging a doomed approach?

**Individual coverage scope:** Separate effort, strategy, changing evidence and opportunity costs in a bounded learning example.

<a id="bank-016-023"></a>
#### BANK-016-023 — Why can a success story's visible habits be unrelated to what caused success?

**Individual coverage scope:** Inspect confounding, selection and imitation costs rather than converting correlation into a universal routine.

<a id="bank-016-024"></a>
#### BANK-016-024 — What is survivorship bias in collections of inspiring biographies?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare the selection process with missing unsuccessful cases and the limits of drawing advice from winners alone.

<a id="bank-016-049"></a>
#### BANK-016-049 — What did the original deliberate-practice studies actually establish about 10,000 hours?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare the 1993 violinist and pianist samples, retrospective practice estimates, the later popular fixed-hour rule and published reanalysis. Explain why an average or association is not a universal threshold or guarantee of mastery.

**Research follow-up:** [Bounded case and source-access note](RESEARCH_NOTES/BANK-016-049.md). This does not change proposal or production status.

#### BANK-016-025 — How can a story show courage through a small consequential action?

<a id="bank-016-026"></a>
#### BANK-016-026 — What makes asking for help a meaningful turning point?

<a id="bank-016-027"></a>
