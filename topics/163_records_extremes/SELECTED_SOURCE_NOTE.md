# Selected source note - niche 163

Checked 2026-10-24. Records below are tied to dated measurements and defined categories. A source can support a particular result without establishing an eternal "world record." All linked BANK topics remain proposed with empty verified_sources.

## BANK-163-043 - Cold Atom Laboratory

The original experiment [*Observation of Bose-Einstein condensates in an Earth-orbiting research lab*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2346-1) (Nature, 2020) reports rubidium condensates in NASA's Cold Atom Laboratory on the ISS, including subnanokelvin states in weak traps. NASA's [mission page](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cold-atom-laboratory/) describes the facility and microgravity setting. The result describes a prepared atomic cloud under an operational definition of temperature, not the ambient temperature of the station or a proof that no colder object exists anywhere in the universe. State species, method and paper date with any value.

## BANK-163-044 - Dickson Fjord wave

Svennevig et al., [*A rockslide-generated tsunami in a Greenland fjord rang Earth for 9 days*](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adm9247) (Science, 2024), combine imagery, seismic evidence and modeling for the September 2023 Dickson Fjord rockslide. The paper estimates an initial tsunami height around 200 m and investigates a persistent fjord seiche. These modeled and localized measures differ from distant runup and open-ocean wave height. The archived "100-meter tall" number is neither the study's headline estimate nor a universal observed height.

## BANK-163-045 - Deepest fish video

The researchers' [*New maximum depth record for bony fish: Teleostei, Scorpaeniformes, Liparidae (8336 m, Izu-Ogasawara Trench)*](https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/publications/new-maximum-depth-record-for-bony-fish-teleostei-scorpaeniformes-/) reports a baited-lander video of a snailfish at 8,336 m, likely a Pseudoliparis species, without a specimen from that depth. The University of Western Australia's [April 2023 field announcement](https://www.uwa.edu.au/news/Article/2023/April/Scientists-break-new-record-after-finding-worlds-deepest-fish) distinguishes filmed and caught records. Check calibration and the full original methods before adopting a precise depth uncertainty or species identification. A 2023 record needs a current comparison before publication.

## BANK-163-046 - Diamond anvils

Ehime University's [February 2026 report on a five-megabar diamond-anvil experiment](https://www.grc.ehime-u.ac.jp/en/archives/news/20260218) links the primary High Pressure Research study, DOI 10.1080/08957959.2026.2627367. It reports a modified diamond tip and an approximately five-million-atmosphere pressure demonstration. Read the paper for pressure markers, error bars, loading and replication before asserting a record. A high pressure alone does not reproduce giant-planet temperature, chemistry or dynamics.
