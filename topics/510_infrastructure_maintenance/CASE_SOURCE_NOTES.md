# Case-source note: I-35W bridge inspection boundary

Checked 2026-10-24. The U.S. National Transportation Safety Board's [I-35W investigation page](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/HWY07MH024.aspx) and [highway accident report HAR-08/03](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/HAR0803.pdf) identify inadequate load capacity of U10 gusset plates due to an original design error as the probable cause of the August 1, 2007 Minneapolis bridge collapse, under accumulated bridge-weight changes and concentrated construction/traffic loads. The NTSB also found inadequate attention to gusset plates in inspection and load rating. Its [January 2008 safety recommendation H-08-1](https://www.ntsb.gov/safety/safety-recs/recletters/H08_1.pdf) explains that routine condition inspections addressed deterioration such as corrosion or cracks and were not intended to uncover original design calculation errors.

Limits: This is the NTSB's case-specific causal finding, not evidence that deferred maintenance caused this collapse or that every visually inspected bridge hides the same defect. A recommendation is not evidence that an intervention was implemented or prevented later incidents. The report is historical, U.S.-specific and cannot be used as operational bridge-safety guidance. Avoid graphic treatment of victims and do not infer individual conduct beyond the investigation.

Overlap: 427 owns bridge form and structural engineering; `BANK-510-036` owns generic inspection-signal comparison. This named question tests the documented boundary between condition inspection and design verification.
