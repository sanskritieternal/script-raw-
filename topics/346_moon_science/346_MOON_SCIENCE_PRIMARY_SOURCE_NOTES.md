# Moon science primary-source follow-up — 2026-10-24

## BANK-346-040 — LCROSS at Cabeus

- Primary mission account: NASA Science, *LCROSS*: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lcross/ . Historical NASA mission chronology: https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-lcross-the-lunar-crater-observation-and-sensing-satellite/ .
- NASA describes the 9 October 2009 impact in the permanently shadowed Cabeus region and the November 2009 announcement that impact observations indicated water near the lunar south pole.
- Limit: mission-site and announcement summaries were inspected, not a full quantitative reanalysis of plume spectra. The finding pertains to an observed impact site and instrument interpretation; it does not establish the extent, purity, extractability or economic value of lunar ice.

## BANK-346-041 — Chang’e-5 basalt dating

- Primary research: *Two-billion-year-old volcanism on the Moon from Chang’e-5 basalts*, *Nature* (2021): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04100-2 . Institutional research account: Chinese Academy of Sciences, 20 October 2021: https://english.cas.ac.cn/newsroom/cas_media/202110/t20211020_285846.shtml .
- The authors analyzed returned basalt fragments from the Chang’e-5 sampling region and report an approximately 2-billion-year crystallization age, extending the directly sampled lunar volcanic record younger than earlier Apollo/Luna basalt samples.
- Limit: site-specific clasts constrain a local volcanic episode and crater-count calibration; they do not date every mare plain or explain the entire heat source. The paper abstract/search record and CAS account were accessible; confirm precise uncertainty and sample selection in the full paper before numeric scripting.
