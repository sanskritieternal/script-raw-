## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Cities & Urbanism across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Urban Evolution, Street Grids & Human Scale (Beginner / Scratch)** — From ancient Jericho and Roman hippodamian grids to modern zoning laws, Jane Jacobs vs Robert Moses street philosophy, pedestrian ergonomics, and superblocks.
- **Tier 2: Infrastructure Systems, Densification & Public Space (Intermediate)** — Barcelona superilles, transit-oriented development (TOD), missing middle housing, urban heat island mitigation, and mixed-use commercial vitality.
- **Tier 3: Housing Crises, Gentrification & Adaptive Reuse (Upper Intermediate)** — Global affordability crunches, speculative property vacancy taxes, land value capture (Henry George), redlining historical impacts, and post-industrial brownfield remediation.
- **Tier 4: Megacities, Climate Resilience & The Future Metropolis (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Sponge cities against flash floods, delta urbanism (Tokyo/Rotterdam), master-planned capitals (Brasilia/Nusantara), smart sensor governance, and sustainable urban biophilia.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Death and Life of Great Cities: Jane Jacobs vs Robert Moses**
   - *Question:* How did a community activist stop a Master Builder from bulldozing Lower Manhattan for an expressway?
   - *Key Focus:* Eyes on the street, short blocks, sidewalk ballet, expressway devastation, and community resistance.
2. **The Origin of the Grid: How Hippodamus and Commissioners Planned New York**
   - *Question:* Why did New York's 1811 Commissioners' Plan carve Manhattan into 2,000 rectangular blocks?
   - *Key Focus:* Real estate speculation, airflow, surveyor logistics, central park omission and correction.
3. **Zoning Laws and Single-Family Mandates: How Laws Shaped the Suburbs**
   - *Question:* Why is it illegal to build a corner cafe or a duplex in 75% of American residential land?
   - *Key Focus:* Euclidean zoning, setback rules, minimum lot sizes, parking minimums, and car dependency.
4. **Barcelona Superblocks: Giving Neighborhood Streets Back to Humans**
   - *Question:* How did Barcelona divert 80% of through-traffic to perimeter streets to create quiet neighborhood plazas?
   - *Key Focus:* Ildefons Cerdà's octagonal blocks, traffic calming, children's play corridors, and noise pollution drops.
5. **The Urban Heat Island Effect: Why Concrete Cities Trap Boiling Temperatures**
   - *Question:* Why are downtown core temperatures 10 degrees hotter than surrounding countryside at night?
   - *Key Focus:* Thermal mass of asphalt, albedo reflection coefficients, green canopy deficit, and air conditioning feedback loops.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Transit-Oriented Development (TOD): How Tokyo and Curitiba Build Around Stations**
   - *Question:* How do private railways fund transit lines by developing towering commercial hubs over train terminals?
   - *Key Focus:* Rail plus property business model, pedestrian shed radiuses, air rights development, and farebox recovery.
7. **The Missing Middle Housing: Why Cities Have Skyscrapers and Mansions but Nothing In Between**
   - *Question:* Why did brownstones, courtyard apartments, and triplexes disappear from modern city construction?
   - *Key Focus:* Two-staircase building codes, construction finance hurdles, community NIMBYism, and density scales.
8. **Sponge Cities: How Wuhan and Copenhagen Absorb 500-Year Floods**
   - *Question:* How can permeable pavements, sunken public parks, and bioswales prevent multi-billion dollar flood disasters?
   - *Key Focus:* Stormwater retention basins, urban wetland restoration, cloudburst boulevards, and green roofs.
9. **The High Line Effect: Park Architecture and the Double-Edged Sword of Gentrification**
   - *Question:* How did an abandoned elevated rail spur generate billions in luxury towers while displacing local residents?
   - *Key Focus:* Adaptive landscape architecture, property value surges, tourism crowding, and wealth concentration.
10. **Parking Minimums: How Empty Spaces for Cars Destroyed Downtowns**
   - *Question:* Why are there 8 parking spots for every car in America while housing remains unaffordable?
   - *Key Focus:* Donald Shoup's high cost of free parking, structured garage costs, retail vacancy, and parking reform.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Land Value Tax: Henry George's 150-Year-Old Solution to Speculation**
   - *Question:* What happens when you tax the unimproved value of land rather than the buildings sitting on top of it?
   - *Key Focus:* Georgism, vacant lot penalty, development incentives, Pennsylvania split-rate experiments, and economic rent.
12. **The Redlining Legacy: How Maps Drawn in 1934 Created Modern Urban Inequity**
   - *Question:* How did the Home Owners' Loan Corporation mortgage grading maps institutionalize racial segregation for a century?
   - *Key Focus:* HOLC color-coded maps, highway demolition through minority neighborhoods, tree canopy disparities, and generational wealth gaps.
13. **Master-Planned Disasters: Brasilia, Chandigarh, and the Failure of Radiant Cities**
   - *Question:* Why did Le Corbusier and Oscar Niemeyer's utopian geometric capitals become lifeless, car-choked wastelands?
   - *Key Focus:* Monumental scale arrogance, lack of spontaneous street life, pedestrian hostility, and satellite slum growth.
14. **Tokyo's Zoning Secret: How Japan Avoided the Housing Affordability Crisis**
   - *Question:* Why does Tokyo build more housing units per year than California while keeping rent affordable for decades?
   - *Key Focus:* Nationalized 12-zone hierarchy, as-of-right development, elimination of local municipal vetoes, and micro-studios.
15. **The Subterranean City: Underground Tunnels, Sewers, and Utilities in Montreal and London**
   - *Question:* How do underground pedestrian cities like Montreal's RESO keep 500,000 citizens moving comfortably in -20°C blizzards?
   - *Key Focus:* Sub-surface right-of-ways, utility conduits, Victorian sewer engineering, and underground mall connectivity.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Delta Megacities: How Tokyo, Rotterdam, and New Orleans Defend Against Sea-Level Rise**
   - *Question:* How does Tokyo's underground G-Cans cathedral discharge 200 tons of floodwater per second into Tokyo Bay?
   - *Key Focus:* Storm surge storm gates (Maeslantkering), levee subsiding, saltwater intrusion, and floating architecture.
17. **Building New Capitals: Nusantara, Egypt's New Administrative Capital, and Songdo**
   - *Question:* Why are nations spending hundreds of billions carving brand-new smart capitals out of jungles and deserts?
   - *Key Focus:* Jakarta sinking dynamics, Cairo congestion relief, IoT sensor infrastructure, and empty luxury city risks.
18. **Smart Cities and Surveillance Capitalism: Did the Sidewalk Toronto Experiment Fail?**
   - *Question:* Why did Google's urban subsidiary abandon its high-tech waterfront smart city in Toronto amidst public outrage?
   - *Key Focus:* Data governance, biometric tracking sensors, intellectual property disputes, and civic privatization fears.
19. **Biophilic Urbanism: Integrating Living Forests into High-Rise Architecture**
   - *Question:* Can Stefano Boeri's Bosco Verticale and Singapore's Supertrees genuinely clean air and lower city temperatures?
   - *Key Focus:* Vertical forest irrigation maintenance, structural root barriers, bird biodiversity corridors, and urban well-being.
20. **The Master Urban Architecture: Designing the 100-Year Resilient City**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive planning framework unites transit, housing abundance, flood resilience, and civic joy?
   - *Key Focus:* Holistic master-planning, circular municipal waste/water loops, human-scale street design, and adaptable zoning codes.
