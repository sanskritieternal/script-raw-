# BANK-111-025 — G-Cans flood channel correction

Checked 2026-10-24. This is a correction to an inherited proposal question, not a complete engineering or flood-protection review.

The archived BANK wording says the Tokyo-area G-Cans facility “discharge[s] 200 tons of floodwater per second into Tokyo Bay.” The operator, Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Kanto Regional Development Bureau), describes the [Metropolitan Outer Area Underground Discharge Channel](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/edogawa/edogawa00576.html) as moving floodwater from named smaller rivers to the **Edogawa River**. Its [Showa pump-station page](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/edogawa/edogawa00734.html) specifies four pumps of 50 **cubic meters of water per second** each, for a maximum capacity of 200 cubic meters per second with all four operating. This is a rated maximum, not an observation of continuous discharge in every storm.

The original imported BANK question and archived pitch are preserved for provenance. Any script, title or idea must use the corrected outlet and unit, identify the scheme by its official name and distinguish capacity from measured operation. The operator attributes a reduction in nearby flooded houses to the completed channel, but that claim needs a dated basin study and rainfall comparison before a quantified benefit is narrated. This note does not certify performance in an extreme future flood or production readiness; `verified_sources` remains empty.
