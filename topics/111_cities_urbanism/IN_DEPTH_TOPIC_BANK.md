**Individual coverage scope:** Compare a grid, cul-de-sac and pedestrian shortcut using actual entrances and crossings; distinguish straight-line distance from usable routes.

#### BANK-111-031 — How does mixing homes and shops change a neighborhood across a day?

**Individual coverage scope:** Map opening hours, deliveries, residences and noise on one block; include residents whose schedules differ from a commuter norm.

#### BANK-111-032 — What is the difference between density and overcrowding?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare dwellings per hectare, people per room, public space and service capacity without treating a single density threshold as ideal.

#### BANK-111-033 — Who can use a public square when its design assumes everyone can stand and climb?

**Individual coverage scope:** Audit seating, gradients, shade, toilets and crossing times through different mobility needs; separate a design review from legal certification.

#### BANK-111-034 — Where does rain go after it lands on a paved neighborhood?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace roof, road, drain, outfall and overflow in one catchment; contrast runoff reduction with moving water downstream.

<a id="bank-111-035"></a>
#### BANK-111-035 — Why can two streets in the same city feel much hotter than each other?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare surface material, shade, wind and measurement time; distinguish satellite surface temperature from pedestrian heat exposure.

<a id="bank-111-036"></a>
#### BANK-111-036 — How does a city decide where its rubbish trucks should go first?

**Individual coverage scope:** Follow collection frequency, narrow streets, transfer sites and worker constraints; compare service reliability rather than shortest distance alone.

#### BANK-111-037 — What happens to a neighborhood when a bus route becomes reliable?

**Individual coverage scope:** Trace waiting time, transfers, operating budgets and reachable destinations; separate accessibility changes from speculative land-value claims.

### Housing, participation and adaptation

Subcategory `CATALOG-111-R07` · advanced.

<a id="bank-111-038"></a>
#### BANK-111-038 — What can a zoning change permit without guaranteeing new homes?

**Individual coverage scope:** Follow one parcel through permitted use, finance, infrastructure and construction; distinguish legal capacity from completed supply.

<a id="bank-111-039"></a>
#### BANK-111-039 — How can upgrading an informal settlement improve services without displacing residents?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare tenure, utility access, rents and residents' participation in a bounded scheme; avoid assuming formalization has a single outcome.

<a id="bank-111-040"></a>
#### BANK-111-040 — Who gets heard at a neighborhood planning meeting?

**Individual coverage scope:** Examine timing, language, childcare, landlord and tenant representation; compare meeting attendance with the affected population.

<a id="bank-111-041"></a>
#### BANK-111-041 — How should a city compare a flood wall with leaving space for water?

**Individual coverage scope:** Define protected assets, residual risk, maintenance, downstream effects and relocation; keep climate scenarios explicit.

<a id="bank-111-045"></a>
#### BANK-111-045 — How did a specified stair and access rule shape small apartment plans?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use a dated city or state code and built examples; distinguish one egress requirement from all causes of missing-middle housing or a universal ban.

<a id="bank-111-046"></a>
#### BANK-111-046 — What do Singapore HDB statistics show about the resident population and tenure?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use dated official household and ownership tables; distinguish residents in HDB flats from citizens, owners, renters and an affordability outcome.
