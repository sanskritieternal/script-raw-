# Selected primary-source note — wind energy

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-439-045`.

- [US Department of Energy, *Experiment Investigates Steering of Wind Farm Wakes for Improved Performance* (24 October 2016)](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/articles/experiment-investigates-steering-wind-farm-wakes-improved-performance): dated experimental plan for Sandia/NREL’s SWiFT research-scale turbines and nacelle lidar; planned comparisons are objectives, not achieved farm-wide gains.
- [US Department of Energy, *Atmospheric Conditions Drive Turbine Wake Behavior at Sandia National Laboratories* (17 May 2017)](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/articles/atmospheric-conditions-drive-turbine-wake-behavior-sandia-national): researchers report lidar velocity profiles from about 27–135 m downstream under different inflow conditions, documenting atmospheric dependence of measured wakes.
- [Sandia National Laboratories, *An Update to the SWiFT V27 Reference Model* (2018)](https://www.sandia.gov/research/publications/details/an-update-to-the-swift-v27-reference-model-2018-10-01/): primary technical publication record describes measured blade geometry and performance inputs from the 2017 campaign; detailed model data would require the full report.

These sources establish experimental setup and selected wake observations at a research site, not a guaranteed output increase for commercial wind farms, annual-energy estimate, community outcome or environmental-impact finding. Weather and operating-state boundaries matter. Turbine operation and control are hazardous and outside the episode scope. DOE/Sandia media reuse rights should be checked per item.
