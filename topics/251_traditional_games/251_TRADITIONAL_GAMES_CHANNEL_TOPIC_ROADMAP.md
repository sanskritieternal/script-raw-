## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Traditional Games across four progressive tiers:

- **Tier 3: Traditional Games Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Traditional Games.
- **Tier 4: Traditional Games Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Traditional Games.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Traditional Games: Early Record of a Local Game**
   - *Question:* What dated source describes its rules or setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate first record from an absolute invention date. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
2. **Traditional Games: A Celebrated Traditional Game**
   - *Question:* How is it actually played by a named group?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe rules, equipment, age, place, and variation. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
3. **Traditional Games: Who Teaches the Game**
   - *Question:* Whose work maintains play spaces and skills?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit children, elders, makers, and organizers with consent. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
4. **Traditional Games: Learning One Game**
   - *Question:* What does a new player need to know to join?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain aim, turn sequence, equipment, boundaries, and local variations. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
5. **Traditional Games: Strategy Within Local Rules**
   - *Question:* Which decisions matter under one rule set?
   - *Key Focus:* Analyze game states and practitioner explanations. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Traditional Games: A Disputed Game Origin**
   - *Question:* Which similarities actually support a shared history?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated rules, travel routes, and evidence gaps. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
7. **Traditional Games: A Digital Version Tested**
   - *Question:* What changes when a physical game moves online?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare access, social play, rules, ownership, and learning. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
8. **Traditional Games: A Game Keeper's Role**
   - *Question:* How did one teacher or organizer expand participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated events and consented player accounts. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
9. **Traditional Games: A Game After Losing Its Space**
   - *Question:* How did one land or policy change affect play?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace venue, rules, players, and community response. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
10. **Traditional Games: A Round from Start to Finish**
   - *Question:* How do moves, scoring, and dispute resolution work?
   - *Key Focus:* Document one locally accepted rule set, not a universal version. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Traditional Games: Participation in One Game**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports a revival or decline?
   - *Key Focus:* Check surveys, events, informal play, and changing labels. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
12. **Traditional Games: Rules Across Two Generations**
   - *Question:* Which rules and materials changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated descriptions and player memories. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
13. **Traditional Games: More Places to Play**
   - *Question:* Which conditions would support continued participation?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask players about space, safety, equipment, time, and teaching. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
14. **Traditional Games: Only One Correct Rulebook?**
   - *Question:* How many legitimate local variants exist?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare practitioner accounts and event rules. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
15. **Traditional Games: One Community Game Day**
   - *Question:* How do newcomers learn and experienced players negotiate?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented observation and protect children. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Traditional Games: Designing a Play Session**
   - *Question:* What must organizers provide for a specific group?
   - *Key Focus:* State space, age, materials, access, supervision, and consent. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
17. **Traditional Games: Equipment of One Game**
   - *Question:* Why do players use a particular board, ball, or marker?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace materials, making, cost, and local substitutes. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
18. **Traditional Games: From Setup to Scoring**
   - *Question:* What sequence makes a match possible?
   - *Key Focus:* Document setup, turns, scoring, disputes, and reset. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
19. **Traditional Games: Layout of the Play Space**
   - *Question:* How do dimensions and markings affect play?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure a local version and ask players about trade-offs. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
20. **Traditional Games: Who Sets the Rules**
   - *Question:* How do players, elders, clubs, and schools negotiate versions?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace decision records and living practice. Evidence base: dated rule descriptions and equipment, consented observation of play, player and organizer accounts, and community safeguarding records.
