## BANK-251-040 — Baga mancala film as a rule source

- The [Smithsonian Human Studies Film Archives catalog](https://collections.si.edu/search/results.htm?q=%22Mancala%20%28Game%29%22) lists a 1960 edited, silent film of Baga children playing with kernels in ground holes. The catalog says access copies require archive contact and original audiovisual material cannot be played. This source verifies an archival item and its description, not the complete moves, scoring or participants' interpretation.
- A script must view the permitted copy, confirm provenance and catalog relationships, seek community interpretation, and distinguish this session from other mancala-family rules. The specific film-evidence problem is separate from BANK-251-035's family-of-games comparison and BANK-251-032's materials question.

## BANK-251-041 — Malawi ludodiversity plan

- [UNESCO's Malawi safeguarding assistance page](https://ich.unesco.org/en/assistances/safeguarding-of-ludodiversity-in-malawi-through-formal-and-non-formal-education-01897) describes a project that plans teacher training, youth apprenticeships, tools and spaces for traditional games. It is a plan record; it does not document completion, who participated or changed play rates.
- Obtain implementation reports, local educator and player accounts, and definitions of each game before judging results. This case concerns transmission infrastructure, distinct from BANK-251-034's standard competition rules and BANK-251-036's street-space change. It should not be collapsed into niche 252's formal traditional-sport governance.
