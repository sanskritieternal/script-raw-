## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of DIY & Making across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Workshop Safety, Core Hand Tools & Fastener Mechanics (Scratch / Beginner)** — PPE protocols, measuring and marking accuracy (tape, square, calipers), hand saw/chisel basics, screw and anchor shear vs. tensile loads.
- **Tier 2: Power Tools, Joinery Techniques & Finishing Chemistry (Intermediate)** — Table saw kickback prevention, pocket holes vs. mortise and tenon joinery, sanding grit progression (80 to 220), and oil vs. polyurethane vs. lacquer finishes.
- **Tier 3: Digital Fabrication: 3D Printing, CNC Routing & Laser Cutting (Upper Intermediate)** — FDM vs. resin SLA 3D printing slicer settings, CNC feeds and speeds, vector laser cutting kerf compensation, and CAD modeling fundamentals.
- **Tier 4: Custom Automation, Microcontrollers (Arduino/ESP32) & Full Maker Systems (Advanced / Expert)** — Microcontroller sensor integration, relay switches for mains power control, custom PCB design, structural welding, and off-grid maker workshop infrastructure.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Safety & Hand Tool Foundations
1. **The Ultimate Beginner Workshop: 10 Essential Tools That Can Build Almost Anything**
   - *Question:* What 10 versatile tools allow a total beginner to build shelves, tables, electronics, and home repairs without spending thousands on fancy equipment?
   - *Key Focus:* Combination square, cordless drill/driver, Japanese Ryoba pull saw, block plane, quality chisels, digital calipers, clamps, multimeter, and personal protective equipment (PPE).
2. **The Physics of Fasteners: Wood Screws, Drywall Anchors, and Shear vs. Tensile Load**
   - *Question:* Why will a drywall anchor hold a 50 lb picture frame hanging down, but pull right out of the wall if you pull it forward, and when should you use nails instead of screws?
   - *Key Focus:* Shear strength (sideways force) vs. tensile strength (pulling force), pilot hole diameter sizing, thread pitch for hardwood vs. softwood, toggle bolts vs. plastic wall anchors.
3. **Measuring and Marking: Why 'Measure Twice, Cut Once' Still Fails Without a Marking Knife**
   - *Question:* Why does a thick carpenter pencil lead introduce a 1/16th-inch error that ruins your joints, and how does a marking knife sever wood fibers for laser-straight cuts?
   - *Key Focus:* Pencil lead kerf error, marking knife registration, reading tape measure hook slack (designed play for inside vs. outside measurements), story sticks, and zeroing calipers.
4. **Japanese Pull Saws vs. Western Push Saws: The Science of Saw Kerf and Tension**
   - *Question:* Why does cutting on the pull stroke allow Japanese saws (Ryoba, Dozuki) to use blades three times thinner than Western push saws, leaving cleaner cuts with half the effort?
   - *Key Focus:* Tensile stress pulling a blade straight vs. compressive stress buckling a pushed blade, crosscut vs. rip cut tooth geometry, kerf waste comparison, and muscle fatigue.
5. **The Art of the Chisel: How to Flatten, Sharpen, and Pare Wood to a Razor Edge**
   - *Question:* Why is a dull tool ten times more dangerous than a razor-sharp one, and how can you sharpen a cheap $10 hardware store chisel until it shaves arm hair cleanly?
   - *Key Focus:* Flattening the back of the blade, primary bevel (25°) vs. secondary micro-bevel (30°), the 'Scary Sharp' sandpaper-on-glass system vs. waterstones, feeling the burr, and leather stropping.
### Tier 2: Power Tools & Joinery Chemistry
6. **Table Saw Safety: The Terrifying Physics of Kickback and How to Prevent It**
   - *Question:* How can a 3-horsepower table saw blade spinning at 4,000 RPM launch a piece of oak backwards at 100 miles per hour in 1/100th of a second?
   - *Key Focus:* The anatomy of kickback (pinching behind the blade), why the riving knife is the single most critical safety device ever invented, push stick ergonomics, and SawStop skin-detection brakes.
7. **Woodworking Joinery: Pocket Holes vs. Mortise & Tenon vs. Dowels vs. Biscuits**
   - *Question:* Are Kreg pocket holes a lazy cheat, or are traditional mortise and tenon joints over-engineered? Scientific destructive strength testing of 5 popular joints.
   - *Key Focus:* End grain gluing failure mechanics, long-grain to long-grain glue shear strength, pocket screw shear limits, dowel alignment jigs, domino joinery, and aesthetic vs. structural balance.
8. **Wood Movement: The Unstoppable Seasonal Expansion That Tears Furniture Apart**
   - *Question:* Why does a solid tabletop glued tightly to a wooden base split in half during winter or bow into a taco during humid summers?
   - *Key Focus:* Cellular moisture equilibrium, tangential vs. radial shrinkage rates, why wood expands across the width but never along its length, figure-8 fasteners, and breadboard end construction.
9. **The Sanding Truth: Why Skipping Grits from 80 to 220 Leaves Ugly Swirls in Your Stain**
   - *Question:* Why does wiping wood with a wet rag ('water popping') before your final sand double the depth of your wood stain and highlight hidden scratches?
   - *Key Focus:* Grit progression rules (80 -> 120 -> 150 -> 180 -> 220), random orbital sander speed and down-pressure, wiping away dust between stages, raising the grain, and inspection raking light.
10. **Finishes Demystified: Boiled Linseed Oil vs. Polyurethane vs. Lacquer vs. Hardwax**
   - *Question:* Which finish should you pick when you want bulletproof waterproof protection versus an all-natural organic matte feel that breathes with the wood?
   - *Key Focus:* Penetrating oils (tung, linseed) vs. film-forming finishes (wipe-on poly, spray lacquer), molecular cross-linking in two-part hardwax oils (Rubio Monocoat), and spontaneous combustion danger of oily rags.
### Tier 3: Digital Fabrication & CAD
11. **3D Printing Foundations: FDM vs. Resin SLA and The Slicer Settings That Actually Matter**
   - *Question:* Why do your FDM prints keep warping off the bed, and when should you switch to an ultraviolet liquid resin printer for microscopic precision?
   - *Key Focus:* Fused Deposition Modeling (PLA, PETG, ABS) thermal adhesion, first layer squish (Z-offset), infill patterns (Gyroid vs. Grid for strength), and stereolithography (SLA) toxic resin washing/curing.
12. **CAD for Absolute Beginners: Designing Your First Functional Part in Fusion 360**
   - *Question:* How can you turn a broken plastic refrigerator clip into a 3D-printable replacement model in Fusion 360 in under 15 minutes using parametric sketches?
   - *Key Focus:* Parametric design principles, fully defining 2D sketches with geometric constraints, extrude/cut operations, shell and fillet tools, exporting clean STLs, and measuring broken parts with calipers.
13. **CNC Routers: Feeds, Speeds, and The Physics of Chip Load**
   - *Question:* Why does running your CNC router spindle too fast without moving fast enough burn your wood and dull your $40 carbide end mill in two minutes?
   - *Key Focus:* Calculating chip load (Feed rate / [RPM x Flutes]), up-cut vs. down-cut spiral end mills, holding workpieces with blue tape and superglue, climb vs. conventional milling, and g-code basics.
14. **Laser Cutters: Kerf Compensation, Vector Cutting vs. Raster Engraving, and Material Safety**
   - *Question:* Why will cutting PVC or vinyl on a laser cutter produce chlorine gas that destroys your lungs and corrodes the machine's steel rails, and how do you compensate for laser beam kerf?
   - *Key Focus:* Diode vs. CO2 vs. Fiber lasers, vector paths vs. raster dithering, kerf calculation for press-fit finger joints in plywood, honeycomb bed maintenance, and exhaust filtration.
15. **Soldering Electronics: The Molecular Science of Intermetallic Bonds and Flux**
   - *Question:* Why is flux the magical ingredient that makes solder flow like liquid silver, and what causes a dull, brittle 'cold solder joint' that fails after a week?
   - *Key Focus:* Thermal mass transfer, iron tip tinning, rosin core flux cleaning oxidation, leaded (60/40) vs. lead-free (SAC305) melting temperatures, desoldering braid, and inspecting shiny concave fillets.
### Tier 4: Microcontrollers, Automation & Full Systems
16. **Arduino vs. ESP32 vs. Raspberry Pi: Choosing the Right Brain for Your Project**
   - *Question:* When should you use a $3 ESP32 with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth instead of a full $60 Raspberry Pi Linux computer for your home automation project?
   - *Key Focus:* Microcontroller (real-time loop execution, low power, GPIO control) vs. Single-Board Computer (full OS, multimedia, network server), GPIO pin voltages (5V vs 3.3V logic danger), and sleep modes.
17. **Controlling Mains Power Safely: Relays, Optocouplers, and Solid State Switches**
   - *Question:* How can a 3.3V microcontroller switch on a 120V/240V workshop vacuum or water pump without frying the computer chip or shocking the user?
   - *Key Focus:* Galvanic isolation using optocouplers, electromechanical relays vs. Solid State Relays (SSR) zero-crossing switching, flyback diodes quenching inductive coil spikes, and fusing protections.
18. **Basic MIG Welding: How to Melt Steel Together Without Blowing Holes Through It**
   - *Question:* How do you set your wire feed speed and voltage so your MIG welder sounds like frying bacon instead of sputtering and spitting hot sparks everywhere?
   - *Key Focus:* Metal Inert Gas (GMAW) principles, 75/25 Argon/CO2 shielding gas, push vs. pull torch angle, cleaning mill scale with an angle grinder, penetration inspection, and safety gear.
19. **Custom PCB Design: From Breadboard Prototype to Finished Printed Circuit Board in 7 Days**
   - *Question:* How do you turn a messy rat's nest of breadboard jumper wires into a sleek, professional two-layer custom green PCB for less than $10 shipped?
   - *Key Focus:* KiCad schematic capture, PCB layout routing, trace width calculators for current carrying, ground planes and decoupling capacitors for electrical noise, and exporting Gerber files.
20. **The Self-Sustaining Maker Workshop: Dust Collection, Solar Power, and System Integration**
   - *Question:* How do you design an airtight 2-stage cyclone dust collection ducting system, clean workshop air down to 1 micron, and power tools with solar batteries?
   - *Key Focus:* Bill Pentz cyclone dust separation physics, static electricity grounding in PVC pipe, automated blast gates with micro-switches, workshop ergonomics, and battery bank sizing.
