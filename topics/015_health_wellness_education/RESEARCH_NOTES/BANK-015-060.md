# BANK-015-060 — Ohsumi's autophagy Nobel and fasting claims

Status: bounded award-source review for a health-education proposal, not a clinical evidence review, medical advice or verified script. Inspected 2026-10-24.

## Primary award sources inspected

- [Nobel Assembly, 2016 medicine prize announcement](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2016/press-release/): states that Yoshinori Ohsumi received the prize for discoveries of mechanisms of autophagy and describes his foundational experiments in baker's yeast.
- [Nobel Assembly, scientific background](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2016/advanced-information/): describes yeast-gene experiments and subsequent work on autophagy machinery.

## Editorial boundary

The archived queue asks what Ohsumi's discovery “proved” about intermittent fasting. These award sources establish discoveries about a cellular recycling pathway, not the safety, optimal duration, clinical benefit or human autophagy response to any popular fasting schedule. A future episode would need a named human population, intervention and clinical outcome, then inspect primary trials, harms and measurement limitations. The Nobel award must not be used as a shortcut to endorse fasting as treatment or give a regimen. No human fasting trial was assessed in this note.
