## BANK-187-039 - Coconino County project

Coconino County, Arizona, publishes its [Alternative Methods and Materials Permit Program](https://www.coconino.az.gov/2173/Alternative-Methods-and-Materials-Permit). The county states that participating projects must still meet applicable setbacks, wastewater-system permits, grading/drainage rules and other county, state and federal requirements. It describes the circumstances in which a project would be off-grid and addresses supporting solar, wind and cistern infrastructure. This is an official program page for one jurisdiction at the checked date, not a guarantee that a particular parcel qualifies, nor a complete cost estimate. Before any current project claim, verify zoning map, parcel status, the permit version and local agency response.

The US Bureau of Land Management's [dispersed camping page](https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/camping) describes a generally limited recreational stay on many BLM lands, with local variations. It does not create a right to build or permanently reside on public land. Keep camping occupancy separate from privately owned off-grid development, and date agency rules. BANK-187-034 already treats restricted/contested route access; this new question is about a named dwelling-permit framework.
