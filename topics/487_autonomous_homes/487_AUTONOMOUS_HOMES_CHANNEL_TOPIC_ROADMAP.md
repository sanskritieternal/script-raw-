## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Autonomous Homes across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Autonomous Homes Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Autonomous Homes.
- **Tier 2: Autonomous Homes Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Autonomous Homes.
- **Tier 3: Autonomous Homes Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Autonomous Homes.
- **Tier 4: Autonomous Homes Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Autonomous Homes.
