- Limits: The complaint contains regulator allegations, not trial findings about every asserted event; the settlement does not license a claim that every allegation was judicially proven. This is a U.S. action about a named camera ecosystem and historical practices, not evidence that all connected homes, local-control devices or automation systems behave alike. Do not reproduce victim footage or identifying details. Check present product practices and case status separately before any current-tense claim.
