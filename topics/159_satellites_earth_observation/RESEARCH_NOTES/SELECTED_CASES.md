# Selected satellite cases - source check (2026-10-24)

These notes delimit proposed questions. They do not verify every claim in an archived pitch or approve an IDEA handoff.

## BANK-159-043 - MethaneSAT observation window

- Mission sponsor EDF's [1 July 2025 statement](https://www.edf.org/media/methanesat-loses-contact-satellite) says contact was lost on 20 June 2025 and the spacecraft had lost power and was likely unrecoverable. EDF says it collected oil-and-gas methane data during its operating year after its March 2024 launch.
- **Claim limit:** Sponsor statements establish the mission status and reported collection window, not independent validation of each emission estimate, instantaneous pipeline identification, or current continuous satellite observation. Retrieve dated data products, uncertainty and ground/air comparisons before a numerical or attribution claim.
- **Overlap:** BANK-159-017 covers hyperspectral methane-sensing mechanisms; BANK-159-041 covers reproducible environmental claims. This question is the dated mission and surviving evidence after loss of contact.

## BANK-159-044 - GRACE-FO and SWOT measure different things

- NASA's [GRACE overview](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/147437/taking-a-measure-of-sea-level-rise-gravimetry) describes satellite-gravity observations used to infer mass change. NASA's [SWOT mission page](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/swot/) describes surface-water extent and height/topography observations. NASA's [GRACE method/limitations explainer](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/Features/WeighingWater/weighing4.php) describes monthly sampling and modeled removal of faster atmospheric/ocean signals.
- **Claim limit:** GRACE/GRACE-FO do not directly image a particular underground aquifer, and SWOT does not weigh groundwater. Regional groundwater inference requires subtraction of other water stores and model/context uncertainty. The archived combined title should not be read as a common instrument or interchangeable output.
- **Overlap:** BANK-159-024 covers the GRACE pair's ranging mechanics; this question compares the missions' measurement types and prevents false cross-attribution.
