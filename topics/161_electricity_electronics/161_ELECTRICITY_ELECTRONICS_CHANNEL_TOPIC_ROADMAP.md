## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Electricity & Electronics across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Voltage, Current, Resistance (Ohm's Law) & Passive Components (Beginner / Scratch)** — Potential difference and electron drift velocity, Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's circuit laws, resistors, capacitors, and inductors in series/parallel, and breadboard circuit prototyping basics.
- **Tier 2: AC vs DC, Electromagnetic Induction & Solid-State Diodes/Transistors (Intermediate)** — Faraday's law of induction and transformer step-up/down ratios, PN junction diode rectification, BJT and MOSFET switching dynamics, and oscilloscope waveform signal analysis.
- **Tier 3: Operational Amplifiers, Integrated Circuit Fabrication & High-Voltage Power Grids (Upper Intermediate)** — Op-amp feedback loops (inverting, non-inverting, filters), photolithography silicon fabrication, switched-mode power supplies (SMPS), and high-voltage AC/DC long-distance power grid transmission.
- **Tier 4: RF Microwave Circuit Design, GaN/SiC Power Electronics & Quantum Computing Hardware (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Transmission line impedance matching (Smith charts), wide-bandgap Gallium Nitride (GaN) and Silicon Carbide (SiC) power efficiency, superconducting Josephson junctions in quantum qubits, and EMC/EMI electromagnetic shielding compliance.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The War of the Currents: Tesla's AC vs Edison's DC That Electrified the Planet**
   - *Question:* Why did George Westinghouse and Nikola Tesla's alternating current defeat Thomas Edison's direct current to power the modern world?
   - *Key Focus:* I^2*R transmission line heat losses, step-up/step-down transformers, Niagara Falls hydroelectric plant, Edison's public electrocution smear campaign, and modern HVDC revival.
2. **Ohm's Law and Circuit Basics: The Holy Trinity of Voltage, Current, and Resistance**
   - *Question:* What is electricity really doing inside a copper wire: water pipe analogies vs electron quantum drift?
   - *Key Focus:* V = I * R mathematical relationships, power dissipation (P = V * I), Kirchhoff's Voltage and Current Laws (KVL/KCL), and reading resistor color bands.
3. **The Transistor: The Microscopic Light Switch That Built the Digital Age**
   - *Question:* How did three Bell Labs physicists in 1947 replace fragile, power-hungry vacuum tubes with a tiny sliver of germanium?
   - *Key Focus:* John Bardeen, Walter Brattain, and William Shockley; N-type and P-type semiconductor doping; BJT base current amplification; and the birth of Silicon Valley.
4. **Capacitors and Inductors: The Magnetic and Electrostatic Energy Storage Duo**
   - *Question:* How do passive ceramic discs and copper coils work together to tune radio waves, smooth power ripples, and store lightning?
   - *Key Focus:* Electric field storage in capacitor dielectrics (Farads), magnetic field storage in inductor chokes (Henries), LC resonant tank circuits, and low-pass/high-pass filtering.
5. **AC vs DC: What Actually Changes When Current Alternates 60 Times a Second?**
   - *Question:* Why does wall socket electricity swing between positive and negative 170 peak volts to deliver 120 volts RMS?
   - *Key Focus:* Sinusoidal waveforms, Root Mean Square (RMS) equivalent heating calculations, 50 Hz vs 60 Hz historical standards, skin effect in conductors, and three-phase rotating magnetic fields.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **How a MOSFET Works: The Field-Effect Transistor Inside Every Modern Computer Chip**
   - *Question:* How does an electric voltage on a gate only a few atoms thick pinch open a conductive channel to let trillions of electrons rush through?
   - *Key Focus:* Gate oxide dielectric capacitance, N-channel and P-channel enhancement mode, gate charge switching losses, CMOS inverter complementary pairs, and FinFET 3D architectures.
7. **Switched-Mode Power Supplies: Why Your Phone Charger Doesn't Weigh 5 Pounds**
   - *Question:* Why did old power adapters feel like heavy, hot iron bricks while modern USB-C chargers deliver 100 watts from a tiny pocket plug?
   - *Key Focus:* Linear voltage regulator heat waste vs high-frequency pulse-width modulation (PWM) switching (100 kHz+), shrinking transformer core size, flyback converters, and 95% electrical efficiency.
8. **The Operational Amplifier: The Swiss Army Knife of Analog Circuit Design**
   - *Question:* How do two simple 'golden rules' allow an 8-pin chip to add, subtract, integrate, and amplify microscopic sensor signals?
   - *Key Focus:* Differential inputs, infinite open-loop gain, negative feedback stabilization, inverting and non-inverting amplifier circuits, comparator thresholds, and active audio filters.
9. **PCB Design and Manufacturing: From Schematic Diagram to Etched Copper Board**
   - *Question:* How does an engineer take a digital circuit diagram and turn it into a multi-layer fiberglass board carrying microscopic copper traces?
   - *Key Focus:* FR4 substrate layers, copper foil photoresist etching, through-hole vias, surface mount technology (SMT) pick-and-place robotics, solder paste reflow ovens, and design for manufacturability (DFM).
10. **High Voltage Safety and Grounding: Why Neutral Isn't Ground and How GFCIs Save Lives**
   - *Question:* How does a Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter detect an electrical leak of just 5 milliamperes and cut power in 25 milliseconds before your heart stops?
   - *Key Focus:* Differential current sensing transformer rings, earth grounding rods, bonded neutral conductors, ventricular fibrillation thresholds (50-100 mA), and arc flash safety gear.
### Tier 3: Advanced Methodologies & Frameworks
11. **Analog vs Digital: What Happens When Continuous Waves Turn into 1s and 0s?**
   - *Question:* How can music, voice, and video be sliced into numbers and reconstructed without losing acoustic warmth or fidelity?
   - *Key Focus:* Nyquist-Shannon sampling theorem, anti-aliasing low-pass filters, quantization noise and bit depth (16-bit vs 24-bit dynamic range), Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADC), and DAC reconstruction.
12. **Electromagnetic Interference (EMI): The Invisible Radio Fog That Crashes Electronics**
   - *Question:* Why do laptop charger cables have mysterious plastic cylinders on them, and why must every electronic gadget pass FCC radiation tests?
   - *Key Focus:* Conducted vs radiated emissions, capacitive and inductive cross-talk, common-mode noise suppression with ferrite bead chokes, Faraday cage enclosures, and EMC certification chambers.
13. **Gallium Nitride (GaN) and Silicon Carbide (SiC): The Wide-Bandgap Semiconductor Revolution**
   - *Question:* Why is 70-year-old silicon hitting its fundamental physical limits, and how do compound crystals switch 1,000 volts at mega-hertz frequencies?
   - *Key Focus:* Wide electronic bandgap physics (3.2 eV vs silicon's 1.1 eV), 10x breakdown electric field strength, slash in switching resistance and heat generation, EV inverters, and high-efficiency solar inverters.
14. **RF and Microwave Engineering: When Wires Become Antennas and Copper Traces Resonate**
   - *Question:* Why does ordinary circuit theory collapse at gigahertz frequencies, forcing engineers to treat printed circuit traces as wave transmission lines?
   - *Key Focus:* Wavelength comparable to trace length, characteristic impedance (50-ohm standard), signal reflection and standing wave ratio (VSWR), quarter-wave stubs, and Smith Chart graphical matching.
15. **The Electrical Grid: Synchronizing Millions of Megawatts Across an Entire Continent**
   - *Question:* How do thousands of spinning steam, gas, and hydro turbines across North America lock their magnetic poles together in perfect 60.00 Hz unison?
   - *Key Focus:* Grid frequency as supply-demand balance speedometer, rotational kinetic inertia, grid interconnection zones, load shedding schemes, and the 2003 Northeast blackout cascade.
16. **High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC): The Underwater Cables Bringing Renewable Power Across Continents**
   - *Question:* Why did Thomas Edison's DC come back from the dead to transport massive offshore wind power across hundreds of miles of ocean floor?
   - *Key Focus:* Overcoming reactive AC cable charging capacitance limits in subsea cables, voltage source converters (VSC) using IGBT valves, 800 kV ultra-long distance lines, and interconnecting non-synchronous grids.
### Tier 4: Enterprise Strategy & Deep Mastery
17. **Quantum Electronics: Superconducting Qubits and the Physics of Cryogenic Computing**
   - *Question:* How do microwave pulses sent through dilution refrigerators at 15 millikelvin control quantum superposition in artificial superconducting atoms?
   - *Key Focus:* Josephson tunnel junctions creating non-linear inductors, Transmon qubit circuits, microwave control line thermal isolation, quantum decoherence mitigation, and dilution refrigerator stages.
18. **Right to Repair: How Manufacturers Lock Down Electronics with Serialized Microchips**
   - *Question:* Why does swapping a genuine camera or battery between two identical smartphones disable features using software cryptographic handshakes?
   - *Key Focus:* Parts pairing algorithms, microscopic schematic access restrictions, independent repair shop legal battles, electronic waste generation, and legislative repair mandates.
19. **The Bill of Materials (BOM): The Cost Breakdown of a $1,000 Smartphone**
   - *Question:* When you pay $1,200 for a flagship phone, how much do the OLED screen, processor, camera sensors, and passives actually cost to manufacture?
   - *Key Focus:* Component pricing teardowns (TSMC 3nm SoC, Samsung OLED panel, Sony CMOS sensors), passive component pennies, assembly and test costs, R&D amortization, and operating margins.
20. **The Masterclass on Hardware Engineering: From Idea to Mass Production**
   - *Question:* How does an electronics team guide a complex hardware product through EVT, DVT, and PVT stages to manufacture a million flawless units?
   - *Key Focus:* Engineering Validation Test (EVT), Design Validation Test (DVT), Production Validation Test (PVT), automated optical inspection (AOI), in-circuit bed-of-nails testing, and supply chain scaling.
