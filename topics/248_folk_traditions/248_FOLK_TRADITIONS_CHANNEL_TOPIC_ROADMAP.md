## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Folk Traditions: Earliest Evidence for a Folk Practice**
   - *Question:* What can a dated source establish about one local practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Name community and avoid a single timeless origin. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
2. **Folk Traditions: A Folk Practice in the Spotlight**
   - *Question:* How does the public version compare with local use?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask practitioners about setting, meaning, and variation. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
3. **Folk Traditions: Unnamed Tradition Bearers**
   - *Question:* Whose teaching and making sustained the practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented accounts and credit collaborative knowledge. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
4. **Folk Traditions: What Folk Means Here**
   - *Question:* Who uses the label and what does it include?
   - *Key Focus:* Define form, community, location, and contested terminology. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
5. **Folk Traditions: Variation as a Feature**
   - *Question:* How do versions change across performers and occasions?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare attributed examples without ranking authenticity. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Folk Traditions: An Uncertain Transmission Path**
   - *Question:* How might a motif have moved between places?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated records and label gaps honestly. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
7. **Folk Traditions: Recording a Living Practice**
   - *Question:* What does digital capture preserve and what does it lose?
   - *Key Focus:* Assess gesture, context, consent, ownership, and access. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
8. **Folk Traditions: A Tradition Bearer's Role**
   - *Question:* How did one named practitioner teach or adapt a form?
   - *Key Focus:* Use permission and locate them in a wider community. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
9. **Folk Traditions: A Break in Transmission**
   - *Question:* How did a documented disruption affect practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace migration, schooling, livelihood, or policy changes. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
10. **Folk Traditions: Learning by Doing**
   - *Question:* How is a folk form taught outside formal classrooms?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow a consented apprenticeship, gathering, or household process. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Folk Traditions: Participation Over Time**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports a decline or revival claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Check venue, generation, survey coverage, and changing labels. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
12. **Folk Traditions: A Folk Form in Two Periods**
   - *Question:* What changed in materials, audience, and purpose?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated examples and practitioner accounts. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
13. **Folk Traditions: Community-Led Transmission**
   - *Question:* Which practical choices could support learning?
   - *Key Focus:* Ask practitioners about space, time, resources, and control. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
14. **Folk Traditions: The Unchanged Tradition Claim**
   - *Question:* Do sources show a single fixed version?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare documented variants and current practice respectfully. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
15. **Folk Traditions: One Teaching Encounter**
   - *Question:* What happens when a skill or story is shared?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented observation and avoid restricted knowledge. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Folk Traditions: A Collector's Folk Account**
   - *Question:* What did an early collector record or omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace methods, language, attribution, and institutional purpose. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
17. **Folk Traditions: A Made Object and Its Use**
   - *Question:* What can an artifact show about a practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Check provenance and compare with living maker knowledge. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
18. **Folk Traditions: Dating a Folk Form**
   - *Question:* Why do archival and oral accounts give different timelines?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare what each source dates and its limits. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
19. **Folk Traditions: The Practice Between Work and Care**
   - *Question:* Where does a folk form fit daily schedules?
   - *Key Focus:* Use varied local accounts, not festival performance alone. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
20. **Folk Traditions: From Local Practice to Official Heritage**
   - *Question:* Who benefits from recognition and who is excluded?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare nomination, funding, access, and practitioner response. Evidence base: community-approved oral and visual records, dated archives, material provenance, and consented practitioner accounts.
