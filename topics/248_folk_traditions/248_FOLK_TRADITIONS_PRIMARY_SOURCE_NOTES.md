# Selected institutional source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-248-041: Ahellil of Gourara

- UNESCO intangible heritage element page, Algeria, inscribed 2008 (original proclamation 2005): https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ahallil-of-gourara-00121
- UNESCO safeguarding project, implementation period 2006-2009: https://ich.unesco.org/en/projects/action-plan-for-the-safeguarding-of-the-ahellil-of-gourara-00032
- The institutional record describes the Zenete-associated poetic, musical and collective form and proposed schools, workshops, inventory and media work. A project plan does not by itself prove transmission outcomes or current participation. Seek named community bearers and consent before recording or interpreting restricted practice. Avoid presenting one administrative heritage description as every performer's account.

## BANK-248-042: Pansori

- UNESCO intangible heritage element page, Republic of Korea, inscribed 2008 (original proclamation 2003): https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/pansori-epic-chant-00070
- The element page describes vocalist and drummer, stylized speech, gesture and improvisation. It is an institutional overview, not a versioned recording or a universal script. A documentary case still needs a named performance, performer/teacher attribution, recording rights and contextual translation. Distinguish storytelling/transmission here from the troupe and scene mechanics of BANK-249 folk theatre.
