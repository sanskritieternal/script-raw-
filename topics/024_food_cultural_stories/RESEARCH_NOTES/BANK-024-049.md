# BANK-024-049 — Neapolitan pizza heritage and recipe standards

Reviewed 2026-10-24. This note tests the preserved queue title that describes “True Neapolitan Pizza” as a “90-Second Wood-Fired Discipline Protected by UNESCO.”

## Inspected primary records

- [UNESCO, Art of Neapolitan ‘Pizzaiuolo’](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/art-of-neapolitan-pizzaiuolo-00722): inscribed in 2017 on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. The named element is the **art and associated social practice of the pizzaiuolo**, not a certification of every pizza, restaurant or 90-second bake.
- [EU Regulation 97/2010, Pizza Napoletana TSG](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32010R0097): a separate product specification that states a 60–90-second baking time under its defined conditions.
- [Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana's current recipe standard](https://www.pizzanapoletana.org/it/ricetta_pizza_napoletana): a distinct association standard also describing a 60–90-second bake at specified oven temperatures.

## Claim disposition

The legacy title fuses three different things: cultural-heritage recognition of practitioner knowledge, a regulated product specification, and an association's certification practice. The time is a **range under specified conditions**, not a universal 90-second rule. A selected episode should date the version of each standard, inspect the UNESCO nomination and EU amendments, and interview practitioners about how they learn and adapt the craft. No restaurant, certification, or consumer quality judgment has been verified here.
