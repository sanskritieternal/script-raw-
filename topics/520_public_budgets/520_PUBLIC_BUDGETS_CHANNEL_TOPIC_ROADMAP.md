## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Public Budgets across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Public Budgets Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Public Budgets.
- **Tier 2: Public Budgets Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Public Budgets.
- **Tier 3: Public Budgets Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Public Budgets.
- **Tier 4: Public Budgets Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Public Budgets.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Public Budgets: How Public Budgets Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a government's budget rules develop and change in practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one government's budget process from its legal and institutional origins through a defined reform; distinguish formal rules from spending practice. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Public Budgets: A Documented Public Budgets Case**
   - *Question:* What did a public spending initiative deliver beyond its appropriation?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one public budget initiative using appropriations, actual spending and service outcomes; state fiscal year, government level and accounting basis. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: published appropriations, execution statements, procurement records and independent public audits. Starting framework: IMF fiscal transparency framework (https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
