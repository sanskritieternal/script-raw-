# Case-source note: GAO SLFRF obligations and spending

Checked 2026-10-24. The U.S. Government Accountability Office's [GAO-24-107301, published September 26, 2024](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-107301), analyzed Treasury project and expenditure reports for the Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds program at the March 31, 2024 reporting cutoff. It states that states including D.C. reported 84% of awards obligated and 60% spent, while localities reported 76% obligated and 60% spent. The [full report](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-24-107301.pdf) explains its data and scope.

Limits: These are recipient-reported U.S. state/local funding data, aggregated at a dated cutoff; they do not describe every tribal or territorial recipient, prove a completed project, measure service outcomes or imply the unspent balance was waste. Later submissions or revisions can change totals. The GAO page and full PDF were accessible, but no independent reanalysis of Treasury microdata was done. Budget and spending rules vary by program and date.

Overlap: `BANK-520-030` is the generic budget-announcement distinction, `BANK-519-038` distributional impact, and `BANK-510-037` maintenance funding. This question stays with the named GAO fiscal-flow snapshot.
