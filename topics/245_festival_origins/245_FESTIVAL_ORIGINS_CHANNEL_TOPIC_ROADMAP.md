## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Festival Origins: Locating the Earliest Evidence**
   - *Question:* What is the earliest secure record for one named festival?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify source, date, place, and what it actually describes. Evidence base: original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations.
2. **Festival Origins: A Famous Founding Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a widely repeated origin survive source checking?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace its earliest version and compare contemporary records. Evidence base: original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations.
3. **Festival Origins: Founders Left Out of the Story**
   - *Question:* Which groups shaped the event before official recognition?
   - *Key Focus:* Use local archives and attributed oral histories. Evidence base: original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations.
4. **Festival Origins: What Counts as an Origin**
   - *Question:* Are we dating a name, ritual, venue, or annual institution?
   - *Key Focus:* Define the feature before comparing claims. Evidence base: original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations.
5. **Festival Origins: Multiple Roots of One Festival**
   - *Question:* How did separate practices become one event?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare chronology, geography, and participants without a single-founder shortcut. Evidence base: original-language dated records, material provenance, attributed oral histories, calendars, and community-approved interpretations.

6. **Festival Origins: A Gap in the Founding Record**

9. **Festival Origins: A Turning Point in Festival History**

11. **Festival Origins: Revival After a Pause**
