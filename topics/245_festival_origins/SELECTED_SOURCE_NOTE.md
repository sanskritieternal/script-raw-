# Selected primary-source checks - Festival origins
Checked 2026-10-24. These notes support bounded proposed histories, not a single universal origin for every practice.

- BANK-245-041: Munich city Oktoberfest chronology, https://www.muenchen.de/en/events/oktoberfest/history-oktoberfest-munich , and municipal city history, https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/stadtgeschichte . The city identifies a wedding horse race in 1810, agricultural association continuation in 1811 and municipal organization by 1819. These dates mark distinct institutional stages; present tents and beer imagery must not be projected back into 1810. Municipal retrospective is not itself an 1810 document.
- BANK-245-042: Edinburgh Festival Fringe Society historical account, https://www.edfringe.com/about-us/history-of-the-fringe/ , and 2017 annual review timeline, https://www.edfringe.com/media/bceaxgdn/fringe-annual-review-2017.pdf . The Society distinguishes eight uninvited theatre groups in 1947 from its 1958 formal creation. The institutional account does not provide all performers' own testimony; archive verification of individual claims remains open. BANK-244-042 separately covers 2024 operations.
- BANK-245-043: National Archives record of original General Order No. 3 dated 19 June 1865, https://www.archives.gov/news/articles/juneteenth-original-document , and US Public Law 117-17 of 17 June 2021, https://www.congress.gov/117/plaws/publ17/PLAW-117publ17.pdf . The order establishes an emancipation announcement in Texas; the statute establishes federal recognition. Neither alone documents the precise first community celebration or speaks for formerly enslaved people. National Archives says Juneteenth was commemorated in Texas by 1866; verify specific local gatherings from local archives before naming founders. Do not confuse 1865, 1866 and 2021.
- Overlap: BANK-244-042/-043/-044 concern documented public festival organization and contemporary practice; BANK-243-030 concerns tradition across generations and BANK-240 sacred landscapes. These origin timelines distinguish dated evidence, institutional claims and later retellings.
