# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-224-040: North Sikkim 2025

- Government of Sikkim, Mangan disruption update, 2 June 2025: https://www.sikkim.gov.in/media/news-announcement/news-info?name=Chief+Secretary+Chairs+Urgent+Meeting+on+Disaster+Situation+and+Evacuation+measures+in++Mangan+District
- Government of Sikkim, tourism reopening announcement, 7 September 2025: https://www.sikkim.gov.in/media/news-announcement/news-info?name=Tourism+Resumes+in+North+Sikkim+with+Key+Destinations+Welcoming+Visitors+from+September
- These are dated official accounts of disruption, restoration and *selected* destination reopening in 2025. They do not describe conditions or permission on a future travel date; local road and permit decisions can change rapidly. The record is about access governance, not advice for bypassing closures or navigating hazardous roads. Verify live authorities before any production that mentions current travel.

## BANK-224-041: Annapurna visitor accounting

- National Trust for Nature Conservation, Annual Report 2024 (published 2025): https://ntnc.org.np/sites/default/files/doc_publication/2025-03/Annual-Report-2024_20_FEB_2025.pdf
- NTNC reports 222,141 international tourists for the Annapurna Conservation Area and describes its visitor posts. Its administrative count needs definitions and denominator checks before comparison; it does not establish unique individuals, all domestic visitors, household benefit, precise trail crowding, or safe carrying capacity.
