## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Himalayan Travel across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Himalayan Travel Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Himalayan Travel.
- **Tier 2: Himalayan Travel Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Himalayan Travel.
- **Tier 3: Himalayan Travel Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Himalayan Travel.
- **Tier 4: Himalayan Travel Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Himalayan Travel.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Himalayan Travel: How One Himalayan Route Developed**
   - *Question:* Which trade, pilgrimage, and land-use histories shaped a corridor?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated maps, local histories, and changing borders. Evidence base: current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts.
2. **Himalayan Travel: A Famous Himalayan Itinerary Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What does a celebrated route require in practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify country, season, permits, elevation, transport, and local services. Evidence base: current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts.
3. **Himalayan Travel: The People Behind Mountain Travel**
   - *Question:* Whose labor makes one journey possible?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace porters, drivers, guides, cooks, road crews, and hosts with consent. Evidence base: current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts.
4. **Himalayan Travel: Reading a Mountain Itinerary**
   - *Question:* Which route facts must a traveler verify before departure?
   - *Key Focus:* Check current permissions, terrain, elevation, weather, transport, and return options. Evidence base: current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts.
5. **Himalayan Travel: Access and Mountain Carrying Capacity**
   - *Question:* How can one route manage visitors without harming communities or habitat?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare services, waste, trail condition, revenue, and local authority. Evidence base: current local route and permit notices, official weather and hazard maps, dated transport and visitor data, georeferenced maps, and consented local and worker accounts.
