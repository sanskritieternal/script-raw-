## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Cloud Computing across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Cloud Computing Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Cloud Computing.
- **Tier 2: Cloud Computing Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Cloud Computing.
- **Tier 3: Cloud Computing Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Cloud Computing.
- **Tier 4: Cloud Computing Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Cloud Computing.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Cloud Computing: From Hosted Servers to Cloud**
   - *Question:* Which changes made one service on-demand and metered?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated provisioning, pooling, elasticity, and billing evidence. Evidence base: NIST definitions, provider specifications and price sheets, contracts, incident reports, workload measurements, and customer records.
2. **Cloud Computing: A Cloud Migration Revisited**
   - *Question:* Did one migration improve its stated outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare matched reliability, cost, latency, and operations before and after. Evidence base: NIST definitions, provider specifications and price sheets, contracts, incident reports, workload measurements, and customer records.
3. **Cloud Computing: People Operating the Cloud**
   - *Question:* Who maintains data centers, networks, support, and customer systems?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace labor and responsibility across organizations. Evidence base: NIST definitions, provider specifications and price sheets, contracts, incident reports, workload measurements, and customer records.
4. **Cloud Computing: What Makes a Service Cloud?**
   - *Question:* Which features distinguish cloud from ordinary remote hosting?
   - *Key Focus:* Test NIST characteristics against a named offer. Evidence base: NIST definitions, provider specifications and price sheets, contracts, incident reports, workload measurements, and customer records.
5. **Cloud Computing: Elasticity Under Load**
   - *Question:* When does autoscaling protect a workload and when does it lag?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure trigger, startup delay, capacity limit, and cost. Evidence base: NIST definitions, provider specifications and price sheets, contracts, incident reports, workload measurements, and customer records.
