## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Shipbuilding across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Shipbuilding Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Shipbuilding.
- **Tier 2: Shipbuilding Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Shipbuilding.
- **Tier 3: Shipbuilding Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Shipbuilding.
- **Tier 4: Shipbuilding Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Shipbuilding.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Shipbuilding: A Ship Design Changes**
   - *Question:* Which cargo, route, or safety need drove one design revision?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated plans, trials, and operating records. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
2. **Shipbuilding: A Famous Vessel Reassessed**
   - *Question:* What do plans and service records show about a named ship?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify build date, refits, and route. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
3. **Shipbuilding: Shipyard Labor**
   - *Question:* Whose fabrication or outfitting work is missing from a vessel's history?
   - *Key Focus:* Use yard records and attributed worker accounts. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
4. **Shipbuilding: Hull, Stability, and Load**
   - *Question:* How do hull form and cargo placement affect a ship's behavior?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one vessel type and loading condition. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
5. **Shipbuilding: Damage Stability**
   - *Question:* How does compartment design limit flooding in a specified case?
   - *Key Focus:* Use approved calculations and test assumptions. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Shipbuilding: An Unexplained Vessel Loss**
   - *Question:* Which physical and voyage evidence remains unresolved?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate investigation findings from hypotheses. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
7. **Shipbuilding: A New Shipyard Method**
   - *Question:* Did a construction method improve quality, time, or repairability?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare documented builds and defects. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
8. **Shipbuilding: A Naval Architect's Choice**
   - *Question:* Who argued for a design decision and who evaluated it?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated drawings and team records. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
9. **Shipbuilding: Launch and Sea Trials**
   - *Question:* What did a vessel's trials demonstrate before delivery?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare test objectives and later service. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
10. **Shipbuilding: Building a Hull**
   - *Question:* How do design, material preparation, assembly, and inspection connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one documented yard program. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Shipbuilding: A Vessel Type Retires**
   - *Question:* Which changes in trade, rules, or operating cost explain decline?
   - *Key Focus:* Use fleet and route evidence. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
12. **Shipbuilding: Two Shipbuilding Eras**
   - *Question:* Which capacity, efficiency, and labor measures can be compared?
   - *Key Focus:* Align vessel type, route, and accounting boundary. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
13. **Shipbuilding: A Proposed Low-Emission Ship**
   - *Question:* Which trials would verify claimed performance?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify fuel supply, route, and lifecycle boundary. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
14. **Shipbuilding: Unsinkable Claim**
   - *Question:* Which flooding conditions did a design actually address?
   - *Key Focus:* Check approved stability basis and damage cases. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
15. **Shipbuilding: Design to First Voyage**
   - *Question:* Which changes occurred between drawing, build, trial, and operation?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated evidence trail. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Shipbuilding: Ship versus Voyage**
   - *Question:* Which outcomes depend on hull, crew, weather, cargo, and port?
   - *Key Focus:* Define system and operating condition. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.

   - *Question:* How do command, steering machinery, hull, and sea state interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Use recorded maneuvering trials. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
18. **Shipbuilding: Capacity and Stability**
   - *Question:* How did a cargo or deck choice affect stability and access?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare approved loading scenarios. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
19. **Shipbuilding: A Documented Structural Failure**
   - *Question:* What initiated damage and how was it contained?
   - *Key Focus:* Use official investigation and survey evidence. Evidence base: dated vessel plans, yard and survey records, IMO instruments, class and flag documentation, sea-trial data, and official investigations.
