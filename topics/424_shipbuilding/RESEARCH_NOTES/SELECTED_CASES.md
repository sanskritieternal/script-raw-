# Selected shipbuilding case: SS John W. Brown

## BANK-424-046 — one Liberty ship's build and later record

- The ship's preservation organization [records](https://www.ssjohnwbrown.org/ship-history) its launch on 7 September 1942 at Bethlehem-Fairfield Shipyard in Baltimore, identifies it as an EC2-S-C1 Liberty ship, and describes its machinery and later service.
- This is the vessel holder's account. It does not supply a full shipyard production log, worker roster, original drawings, sea-trial record or independent condition survey. Its description of the ship's present resemblance to its wartime appearance also includes restored and maintained components.
- Script gate: check yard, Maritime Commission and survey records before precise construction time, labor, design-change or performance claims. Do not project this single ship's experience onto all Liberty ships.
