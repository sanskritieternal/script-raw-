## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Ancient Religions: An Ancient Tradition in Its Own Sources**
   - *Question:* What do the earliest surviving sources show about one named tradition?
   - *Key Focus:* Date sources and distinguish practice from later reconstruction. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
2. **Ancient Religions: A Famous Ancient Ritual in Context**
   - *Question:* What can evidence establish about one widely described ancient ritual?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original text, archaeology, and later retellings. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
3. **Ancient Religions: Ordinary Worshippers in Ancient Records**
   - *Question:* Whose participation is missing from elite accounts?
   - *Key Focus:* Use household finds, inscriptions, and evidence limits. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
4. **Ancient Religions: How We Know About Ancient Religion**
   - *Question:* What kinds of evidence survive for one community's religious life?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain texts, objects, architecture, and gaps without assuming uniform belief. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
5. **Ancient Religions: Reconstructing Practice from Fragments**
   - *Question:* How can scholars infer practice without treating one source as universal?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare independent evidence and alternative readings. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Ancient Religions: A Deity or Site Identification in Dispute**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports competing identifications?
   - *Key Focus:* State uncertainty in inscription, image, date, and later interpretation. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
7. **Ancient Religions: New Methods for Old Evidence**
   - *Question:* What can a new imaging or dating method reveal about one source?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare method, calibration, provenance, and interpretive limits. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
8. **Ancient Religions: A Named Ancient Participant**
   - *Question:* What can a surviving record tell us about one worshipper or patron?
   - *Key Focus:* Distinguish documented role from reconstructed beliefs. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
9. **Ancient Religions: A Religious Change in Dated Records**
   - *Question:* What changed after one documented political or social event?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare source survival and local variation before and after. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
10. **Ancient Religions: Reconstructing One Ceremony**
   - *Question:* Which stages of a named ancient ceremony are actually documented?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate contemporary record from later reconstruction. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Ancient Religions: Changing Visibility of a Tradition**
   - *Question:* Did practice decline, or did its records become less visible?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated evidence, preservation, and continuity. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
12. **Ancient Religions: Ancient Evidence and Living Continuity**
   - *Question:* How do present communities relate to one ancient tradition?
   - *Key Focus:* Let living practitioners define continuity while distinguishing ancient sources. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
13. **Ancient Religions: An Unanswered Research Question**
   - *Question:* Which new evidence could resolve a defined historical disagreement?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify needed excavation, edition, or provenance without predicting discovery. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
14. **Ancient Religions: A Modern Story About Ancient Belief**
   - *Question:* Does a popular claim fit the dated evidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace the claim to source and mark later reinterpretation. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
15. **Ancient Religions: One Ancient Site's Evidence Trail**
   - *Question:* How did scholars identify and reinterpret one religious site?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow excavation records, objects, publications, and challenges. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Ancient Religions: A Religious Text's Transmission**
   - *Question:* What can surviving copies establish about one text's history?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare manuscripts, language, date, and editorial choices. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
17. **Ancient Religions: Later Readings of an Ancient Source**
   - *Question:* How have different communities read one inherited text?
   - *Key Focus:* Attribute each reading and distinguish historical setting. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
18. **Ancient Religions: Practice Beyond Written Doctrine**
   - *Question:* What evidence reveals everyday devotion in one community?
   - *Key Focus:* Use domestic objects, local inscriptions, and limits of inference. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
19. **Ancient Religions: A Documented Ritual Sequence**
   - *Question:* Which steps of one ancient rite are attested and by whom?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate prescriptions from evidence of performance. Evidence base: dated archaeological reports, primary texts and manuscript witnesses, inscriptions, provenance records, translations, and consented community accounts.
20. **Ancient Religions: A Key Term Across Languages**
   - *Question:* How does translation change a religious term's apparent meaning?
