- **Tier 4: Unsolved Science Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Unsolved Science.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Unsolved Science: How a Question Became Open**
   - *Question:* Which observation or proof gap first defined a specific unresolved problem?
   - *Key Focus:* Date the original formulation and subsequent revisions. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
2. **Unsolved Science: A Famous Open Problem Precisely Stated**
   - *Question:* What is the exact unresolved claim and what has already been proved?
   - *Key Focus:* Use authoritative current status and explicit definitions. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
3. **Unsolved Science: Work Behind an Open Question**
   - *Question:* Whose measurements, failed tests, or partial results changed the problem?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace credited publications and available records. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
4. **Unsolved Science: What Unsolved Means**
   - *Question:* Is a named question unmeasured, unexplained, unproved, or underdetermined?
   - *Key Focus:* Define the claim and the evidence needed to resolve it. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
5. **Unsolved Science: Competing Explanatory Models**
   - *Question:* Which predictions differ between two live models of an unresolved phenomenon?
   - *Key Focus:* State assumptions, parameters, and testable range. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Unsolved Science: An Open Case, Not a Mystery Label**
   - *Question:* What observation is established, and which explanation remains unverified?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate anomalous datum, replication, and inference. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
7. **Unsolved Science: A New Instrument Changes the Question**
   - *Question:* Which detection limit or resolution has a new tool changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare calibration, sensitivity, and coverage before and after. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
8. **Unsolved Science: A Claim and Its Critics**
   - *Question:* How did named researchers revise a proposed answer after criticism?
   - *Key Focus:* Read original claim, response, replication, and correction. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
9. **Unsolved Science: A Result That Reframed an Open Problem**
   - *Question:* Which dated finding ruled out or weakened a proposed explanation?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace protocol and independent follow-up. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
10. **Unsolved Science: Testing an Unresolved Claim**
   - *Question:* What observation would distinguish the leading explanations?
   - *Key Focus:* Design a falsifiable comparison with explicit controls. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Unsolved Science: A Once-Promising Explanation**
   - *Question:* Why did support for a proposed answer weaken?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare initial evidence with later replication or counterexample. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
12. **Unsolved Science: An Open Question Across Decades**
   - *Question:* Which part is still open after new data or proofs?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated authoritative statements of the same claim. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
13. **Unsolved Science: The Next Decisive Test**
   - *Question:* What feasible measurement or proof step could reduce uncertainty?
   - *Key Focus:* State required precision, constraints, and alternative outcomes. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
14. **Unsolved Science: Does Unsolved Mean Unknowable?**
   - *Question:* What is unknown versus merely untested or disputed in a named case?
   - *Key Focus:* Use current authoritative status and avoid certainty claims. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
15. **Unsolved Science: The Evidence Trail of an Open Problem**
   - *Question:* How did evidence, criticism, and revision change one research question?
   - *Key Focus:* Build a dated source trail, including null results. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Unsolved Science: From Anomaly to Hypothesis**
   - *Question:* When does an unusual observation warrant a new explanation?
   - *Key Focus:* Check replication, calibration, and known mechanisms. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
17. **Unsolved Science: Measuring the Unknown**
   - *Question:* Which instrument and operational definition produce the key observation?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify units, sensitivity, uncertainty, and calibration. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
18. **Unsolved Science: A Result at Another Scale**
   - *Question:* Does a laboratory effect persist across relevant distances or times?
   - *Key Focus:* Check scaling assumptions and available measurements. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
19. **Unsolved Science: Controlled Test and Natural Setting**
   - *Question:* Which confounders change when a result leaves the lab?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare protocols, selection, and environmental variation. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
20. **Unsolved Science: Could Variation Explain the Signal?**
   - *Question:* How wide is the baseline variation in comparable observations?
   - *Key Focus:* Describe sampling frame, spread, and missing cases. Evidence base: peer-reviewed original studies, datasets and methods, independent replication, calibration records, current scientific assessments, and exact theorem statements where relevant.
