# Selected subject: USDA 2023 Farm to School Census

**Proposed question:** `BANK-524-043` — What can the USDA 2023 Farm to School Census say about local procurement and school meals?

## Primary records inspected

- [USDA Food and Nutrition Administration, 2023 Census release](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/research/f2s/census2023). The report describes school food authority (SFA) activity in school year 2022–23. It reports 74% of SFAs did at least one farm-to-school activity, 63% said they served local foods, and participating SFAs reported almost $1.8 billion in local purchases. Those are different denominators and self-reported categories, not a single measure of meals served.
- [USDA methodology FAQ](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/f2s/census/results-faqs). Invitations went to 18,833 SFAs; 12,559 submitted and 11,803 supplied usable responses for national/state findings. USDA weights national/state estimates for nonresponse. SFAs use their own definitions of “local”; earlier 2013/2015 waves are not directly comparable to 2019/2023 because the instrument changed.
- [USDA report page and downloadable dataset](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/research/f2s/census2023) expose the underlying report, codebook and national/state workbook. The individual-response dataset has not been reanalysed for this topic.

## Bounded premise and gaps

The evidence supports a dated, survey-based picture of local procurement and reported farm-to-school activity in the United States. An SFA is an administrative authority, not one school, child or meal. The survey does not by itself show improved dietary quality, reduced waste, stronger shock resilience, farmer income or a causal effect of local sourcing. USDA reports perceived benefits, which should be attributed as respondent reports. The 2023 versus 2019 comparison requires the report's weighting and question definitions before an exact trend claim enters a script.

## Coverage and overlap decision

Archive pitches about a documented food-system case, purchasing governance and evidence trails route here; broad loss, logistics, retailer, seed and kitchen questions route to existing `BANK-524-*` owners. Unsupported biographies, technology outcomes, forecasts and superlatives await separate evidence. `BANK-524-038` asks how procurement may protect diversity during shocks; `BANK-524-043` asks what this named survey actually measures and cannot prove. The nutrition trial in `BANK-505-044` measures short-term intake under controlled diets, not school procurement.
