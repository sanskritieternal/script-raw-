# Writing source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-452-043 - Declaration rough draught and adopted text

- Primary manuscript and institutional description: Library of Congress, *Declaring Independence: Drafting the Documents*, https://wwws.loc.gov/exhibits/declara/declara4.html (inspected 2026-10-24). The exhibited original Rough draught bears revisions associated with Jefferson, the Committee of Five and Congress. The library notes an earlier composition fragment and later adopted text; visible changes alone do not securely assign every hand or motive.
- Primary manuscript context: Library of Congress, *Creating the Declaration of Independence*, https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/creating-the-united-states/interactives/declaration-of-independence/overview.html (inspected 2026-10-24). The online exhibit identifies the rough draft as a combined record of Jefferson's drafting and subsequent committee/congressional changes. National Archives institutional history, https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-history (inspected 2026-10-24), cautions that a possible fair copy has not survived.
- Limits: This is one political document's visible revision trail, not proof that collective editing has a general writing effect. Distinguish manuscript transcription, reconstruction and adopted printing; verify individual alterations on the image before attributing them. The Declaration's claims, omissions and historical violence need separate historical and descendant-community treatment. Image rights and reproduction terms require separate review.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-452-043 studies revision and editorial attribution. BANK-451-038 studies literary editions' reception; BANK-177-021 studies the later Declaration of Sentiments rewriting. Neither is a duplicate of the 1776 rough-draught workflow.
