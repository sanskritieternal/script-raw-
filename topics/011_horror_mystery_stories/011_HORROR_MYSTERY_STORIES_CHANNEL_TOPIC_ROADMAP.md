## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Horror / Mystery Stories across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Foundations of Dread & Suspense (Scratch / Beginner)** — Information withholding, atmospheric pacing, psychological uncanny valley triggers, and foundational ghost/haunted house archetypes.
- **Tier 2: Folk Terrors, Urban Legends & Cryptids (Intermediate)** — Regional cryptid lore, unexplained cold cases, liminal space psychology, sleep paralysis, and folklore evolution.
- **Tier 3: Unexplained Disappearances & Cold Anomalies (Upper Intermediate)** — Historical vanishings, isolated lighthouse enigmas, mountain tragedies, and forensic timeline discrepancies.
- **Tier 4: Cosmic Dread, ARG Worldbuilding & Unreliable Narrators (Advanced / Expert)** — Lovecraftian horror craft, ergodic literature, analog horror mechanics, ARG worldbuilding, and perceptual deception.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundations of Dread & Suspense
1. **The Anatomy of Suspense: How Hitchcock Manipulated Information**
   - *Question:* How does revealing a hidden danger to the audience while hiding it from the characters create agonizing suspense?
   - *Key Focus:* The Hitchcock bomb-under-the-table axiom, dramatic irony, pacing, sound design, information control.
2. **The Uncanny Valley: Why Almost-Human Things Terrify Our Brains**
   - *Question:* Why do wax figures, mannequins, porcelain dolls, and humanoid robots trigger an evolutionary revulsion response?
   - *Key Focus:* Masahiro Mori's hypothesis, pathogen-avoidance theory, cognitive dissonance, facial symmetry anomalies.
3. **Ghost Ships & Ocean Phantoms: The Mystery of the Mary Celeste**
   - *Question:* What caused Captain Briggs, his family, and seven crewmen to abandon a perfectly seaworthy brigantine in 1872?
   - *Key Focus:* Alcohol cargo vapor theory, pump malfunction, seaquake acoustics, Admiralty court records.
4. **The Gothic Archetype: Haunted Houses, Isolation, and Decaying Legacies**
   - *Question:* How did Castle of Otranto, Shirley Jackson, and The Shining transform architecture into a predatory character?
   - *Key Focus:* Gothic spatial psychology, claustrophobia, labyrinthine floor plans, internal psychological breakdown.
### Tier 2: Folk Terrors & Cryptids
5. **The Mothman Prophecies: Cold War Hysteria, Bridge Collapse, and the Silver Bridge Disaster**
   - *Question:* How did 13 months of winged humanoid sightings in Point Pleasant intersect with a tragic structural failure?
   - *Key Focus:* Sandhill crane misidentification, TNT area folklore, John Keel's reporting, eyebar 330 stress corrosion.
6. **Dyatlov Pass Incident: Hypothermia, Infrasound, or Military Testing?**
   - *Question:* What drove nine experienced Soviet hikers to cut open their tent from the inside in sub-zero Ural winds?
   - *Key Focus:* Slab avalanche physics, paradoxical undressing, Karman vortex infrasound, 2020 EPFL simulation models.
7. **Black Eyed Children & Liminal Spaces: The Psychology of Backrooms and Modern Creepypastas**
   - *Question:* How did internet folklore evolve from email chain letters into infinite yellow-carpeted alternate dimensions?
   - *Key Focus:* Brian Bethel's 1996 account, Kane Pixels' Backrooms ARG, architectural nostalgia, kenopsia.
8. **The Shadow People Phenomenon: Sleep Paralysis, Hypnagogic Hallucinations, and Ancient Myths**
   - *Question:* Why do cultures across six continents independently report the exact same silhouette wearing a fedora at bedside?
   - *Key Focus:* REM atonia spillover, temporal parietal junction stimulation, the 'Old Hag' syndrome, hyperactive agency detection.
### Tier 3: Unexplained Disappearances
9. **The Flannan Isles Lighthouse Mystery: Three Keepers Vanish Without a Trace**
   - *Question:* How did three seasoned lighthouse keepers disappear from Eilean Mòr in 1900 leaving an uneaten meal and a clocked log?
   - *Key Focus:* Rogue wave oceanography, western landing cliff damage, Northern Lighthouse Board logs, isolation psychosis.
10. **The Yuba County Five: California's Unsolved Frozen Mountains Tragedy**
   - *Question:* Why did five young basketball fans drive 70 miles in the wrong direction into snowdrifts, abandoning a working car?
   - *Key Focus:* Forest Service trailer provisions, Gary Mathias mystery, hypothermia confusion, mountain pass searches.
11. **The Hinterkaifeck Murders: Footprints in the Snow Leading Only In**
   - *Question:* Who lived in the attic of a secluded Bavarian homestead for six days before murdering six people with a mattock?
   - *Key Focus:* Attic footsteps, stolen keys, missing money untouched, Munich Police cold case re-investigation 2007.
12. **Roanoke: The Lost Colony and the Cryptic Word 'Croatoan'**
   - *Question:* Did 115 English colonists vanish into thin air in 1590, or did archaeology reveal their true survival fate?
   - *Key Focus:* Tree bark carving, John White's relief voyage, Hatteras Island assimilation, drought tree-ring data.
### Tier 4: Cosmic Dread & Advanced Craft
13. **Cosmic Horror & The Fear of the Unknown: Lovecraftian Dread Without Cheap Monsters**
   - *Question:* How do masters of cosmic horror evoke deep existential terror without resorting to rubber monster tropes?
   - *Key Focus:* Human insignificance, non-Euclidean geometry, color out of space, linguistic breakdown when confronting the infinite.
14. **The Mandela Effect & False Collective Memories: Parallel Timelines or Cognitive Glitches?**
   - *Question:* Why do millions of strangers share identical false memories of movie lines, logos, and world maps?
   - *Key Focus:* Schema theory, post-event misinformation effect, confabulation, Berenstain vs Berenstein test studies.
15. **The Bloop and Julia: Deep Ocean Hydrophone Signals That Defied Biology**
   - *Question:* What produced ultra-low frequency acoustic rumbles detected 5,000 kilometers apart across the Pacific Basin in 1997?
   - *Key Focus:* NOAA equatorial hydrophone arrays, blue whale decibel thresholds, Antarctic icequake calvings.
16. **Analog Horror: How VHS Artifacts, Emergency Broadcasts, and Low-Fi Dread Captured Gen Z**
   - *Question:* How did Kris Straub (Local58) and Alex Kister (The Mandela Catalogue) pioneer a revolutionary new horror genre?
   - *Key Focus:* CRT scanlines, EAS audio screech, institutional authority mimicry, uncanny facial distortion.
17. **The Lead Masks Case: Mystery on Vintém Hill, UFOs, and Radioactive Raincoats**
   - *Question:* Why were two electronic technicians found dead in Rio de Janeiro wearing suits, waterproof coats, and eye lead masks?
   - *Key Focus:* Cryptic written schedule notes ('wait for mask signal'), spiritualist scientific sects, toxicology limitations.
18. **House of Leaves: Ergodic Literature, Labyrinthine Architecture, and Endless Corridors**
   - *Question:* How does a house that measures 1/4 inch larger on the inside than the outside produce profound psychological terror?
   - *Key Focus:* Mark Z. Danielewski's typographic maze, the Minotaur myth, spatial impossibility, epistolary fragmentation.
19. **The Ourang Medan: The Ghost Freighter Whose Crew Died with Open Eyes**
   - *Question:* Did a Dutch freighter truly drift through the Strait of Malacca with corpses frozen pointing towards the sun in 1948?
   - *Key Focus:* Morse code distress call ('I die'), Silver Star rescue attempt, declassified CIA correspondence, chemical contraband.
20. **Found Footage Cinematography: The Illusion of Raw Authenticity from Blair Witch to REC**
   - *Question:* How do shaky handheld cameras, imperfect framing, and missing footage trick audiences into suspending disbelief?
   - *Key Focus:* Cinéma vérité techniques, 1999 Blair Witch guerilla marketing, diegetic audio, digital vs tape aesthetics.
