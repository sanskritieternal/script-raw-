## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Roads & Highways across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Roads & Highways Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Roads & Highways.
- **Tier 2: Roads & Highways Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Roads & Highways.
- **Tier 3: Roads & Highways Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Roads & Highways.
- **Tier 4: Roads & Highways Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Roads & Highways.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Roads & Highways: A Highway Alignment Chosen**
   - *Question:* Which travel and political assumptions drove one route?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace maps, alternatives, and decision records. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
2. **Roads & Highways: A Widely Cited Road Project**
   - *Question:* Which measured outcomes followed opening?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare promised congestion, safety, and access effects. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
3. **Roads & Highways: Communities in the Right of Way**
   - *Question:* Whose homes, businesses, or lands were affected?
   - *Key Focus:* Use acquisition records and local accounts. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
4. **Roads & Highways: What Makes a Road Work?**
   - *Question:* How do geometry, pavement, drainage, and operations interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one bounded road segment. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
5. **Roads & Highways: Safety and Capacity Tradeoffs**
   - *Question:* How does a design change affect crash severity and throughput?
   - *Key Focus:* Use site-calibrated evidence and multiple modes. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Roads & Highways: A Crash Pattern without Clear Cause**
   - *Question:* Which road, traffic, and reporting data would distinguish explanations?

7. **Roads & Highways: A New Pavement or Control System**
   - *Question:* Did it improve durability, safety, or travel time?

8. **Roads & Highways: A Route Decision**
   - *Question:* Who selected one alternative, and who opposed it?

9. **Roads & Highways: A Road Opens or Closes**
   - *Question:* What changes in traffic and access followed?

10. **Roads & Highways: Road from Design to Maintenance**
   - *Question:* How do traffic estimates, geotechnics, drainage, and upkeep connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow a project file. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Roads & Highways: A Highway Replaced**
   - *Question:* Why was a road removed, bypassed, or rebuilt?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare condition, costs, and community effects. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.

   - *Question:* How did crashes, traffic, and nearby land use change?
   - *Key Focus:* Control boundaries, exposure, and reporting changes. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
13. **Roads & Highways: A Proposed Road Expansion**
   - *Question:* What evidence would test its claimed benefits?

14. **Roads & Highways: More Lanes End Congestion?**

   - *Question:* How did alternatives become the built alignment?

### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Roads & Highways: Segment or Network?**

17. **Roads & Highways: Storm to Road Closure**
   - *Question:* How does rainfall pass through drainage and affect access?
   - *Key Focus:* Use design storm and observed incidents. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
18. **Roads & Highways: Speed and Street Access**
   - *Question:* What did wider lanes or fewer crossings change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare travel, crash severity, and pedestrian access. Evidence base: dated agency designs and assessments, traffic and crash data, FHWA safety methods, pavement inspections, contracts, and affected-community accounts.
19. **Roads & Highways: A Pavement or Drainage Failure**
   - *Question:* What evidence explains a documented closure?
