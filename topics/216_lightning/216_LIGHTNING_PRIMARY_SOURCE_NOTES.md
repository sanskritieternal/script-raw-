# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-216-040: WMO megaflash

- WMO, 31 July 2025 certification announcement: https://public.wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-certifies-megaflash-lightning-record-usa
- WMO weather/climate extremes table, as of 31 July 2025: https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Table_Records_25Jul2025.pdf
- WMO certified 829 +/- 8 km for a flash in a 22 October 2017 US storm. The 2017 event and 2025 certification are different dates. This is an instrument- and archive-dependent observed record, not a proof of the maximum possible flash distance or a public safety distance. Check archive revisions before production.

## BANK-216-041: Apollo 12

- NASA original incident analysis: https://sma.nasa.gov/SignificantIncidents/assets/analysis-of-apollo-12-lightning-incident.pdf
- NASA Saturn V flight evaluation, triggered-lightning section: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/afj/ap12fj/pdf/a12-saturn-v-fer.pdf
- NASA reports electrical disturbances at about 36.5 and 52 seconds after the 14 November 1969 launch. Flight analysis attributes the first discharge to vehicle-triggered lightning; evidence for the second is less definitive. Launch-specific rule changes should not be recast as generic public lightning instructions.
