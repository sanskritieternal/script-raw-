# Selected primary-source note — tunnels

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-428-043`.

- [Crossrail Learning Legacy, “The use of InSAR to complement control of construction and protect third party assets”](https://learninglegacy.crossrail.co.uk/documents/use-insar-interferometric-synthetic-aperture-radar-complement-control-construction-protect-third-party-assets/): the project’s Case Study 2 compares Farringdon conventional UCIMS monitoring and satellite InSAR. It reports rapid settlement in August 2014 coincident with sprayed-concrete-lining tunnel enlargement and attributes approximately 10–20 mm settlement to those works. It also documents InSAR phase-unwrapping errors above about 10 mm between readings in that case and different sensor positions and reference datums.

This is a project-authored technical case study, not an independent safety certification. The numerical and causal interpretation applies to the stated instruments, places and period; satellite readings did not replace frequent site monitoring during fast construction movement. Source illustrations need rights review before reuse. This note is analysis of a historical monitoring record, not geotechnical field instructions or a claim that tunnel depth assures safety.
