## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Pottery across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Pottery Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Pottery.
- **Tier 2: Pottery Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Pottery.
- **Tier 3: Pottery Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Pottery.
- **Tier 4: Pottery Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Pottery.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Pottery: Early Pottery in One Community**
   - *Question:* What dated vessels document a local practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate archaeological survival from first making. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
2. **Pottery: A Celebrated Vessel Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What does one pot reveal about use and craft?
   - *Key Focus:* Check maker, clay, firing, decoration, and provenance. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
3. **Pottery: Work Behind the Kiln**
   - *Question:* Whose clay gathering, fuel, firing, selling, and teaching is omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit paid and unpaid labor with consent. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
4. **Pottery: How a Pot Takes Shape**
   - *Question:* How do clay, forming, drying, firing, and use connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one named workshop and local terms. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
5. **Pottery: Clay and Fire Trade-offs**
   - *Question:* How do recipe and firing conditions affect a pot?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare measured examples and potter explanations. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Pottery: An Unknown Pot's Origin**
   - *Question:* What evidence could identify workshop or date?

7. **Pottery: A New Kiln or Glaze**

10. **Pottery: A Pot from Clay to Use**
   - *Question:* Which decisions shape a vessel's function?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow consented making and actual use. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Pottery: Why Pottery Demand Changed**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured shift?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare household use, restaurants, tourism, substitutes, and price. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
12. **Pottery: A Workshop Across Generations**
   - *Question:* How did clay, forming, firing, and buyers change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated objects and potter accounts. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
13. **Pottery: Keeping Pottery Viable**
   - *Question:* Which resources do potters prioritize?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare clay access, fuel, apprenticeships, pay, and markets. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
14. **Pottery: A Single Authentic Pot?**
   - *Question:* How much variation exists among makers and uses?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare provenanced vessels and practitioner definitions. Evidence base: dated vessels with context and provenance, clay and workshop records, conservation assessment, and consented potter and user accounts.
15. **Pottery: One Vessel's Working Life**
   - *Question:* How was it made, sold, used, broken, or kept?

16. **Pottery: A Vessel for a Purpose**
