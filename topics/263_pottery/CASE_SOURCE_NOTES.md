# Selected case-source note — pottery

Inspected 2026-10-24. This is a bounded premise check for `BANK-263-041`, not full episode verification.

- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art, *Storage jar*, object 2020.7](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/747045): catalogues the 1858 jar as by Dave (later recorded as David Drake), made at Stony Bluff Manufactory, with signature, date and verse; records the dimensions and acquisition. The jar inscription is object evidence, while the catalog's reading of its significance is curatorial interpretation.
- [Smithsonian Institution, *Stoneware storage jar by David Drake*, NMAAHC 2011.69](https://www.si.edu/object/stoneware-storage-jar%3Anmaahc_2011.69): independently catalogues a different signed and dated jar from 1852 and summarizes Edgefield clay/glaze context. The two object records should not be conflated.

Both institutional object records were accessible. They establish inscriptions and catalogued provenance, not a complete account of kiln labor, compensation, Drake's intentions or every vessel he made. Further study needs dated workshop records and specialist scholarship; the episode should handle enslavement without romanticizing it.
