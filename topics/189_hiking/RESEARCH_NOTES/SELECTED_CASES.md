# Selected hiking cases - 2026-10-24

These notes bound proposed case questions. They are not an itinerary, current permit advice, medical guidance or an all-routes risk ranking.

## BANK-189-038 - Angels Landing permit pilot

- Zion National Park's [visitor-use planning page](https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/management/visitor-use-research-and-planning.htm) says the Angels Landing pilot began in 2022 to address congestion and access certainty. Its [April 2024 two-year update](https://home.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/zion-national-park-marks-two-years-of-the-angels-landing-pilot-permit-program.htm) describes ranger and visitor reports of reduced crowding.
- **Claim limit:** Pilot issuance and reported crowding are not comparable injury/fatality denominators and do not prove a universal safety benefit. The archived grouping with Half Dome and Huashan cannot be called the world's most dangerous hike without a defined population and exposure period. Check current NPS rules before a route-specific statement.
- **Overlap:** BANK-189-029 covers social-media crowding broadly; this is a named NPS visitor-allocation intervention. Half Dome's [separate Yosemite permit page](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/hdpermits.htm) shows why those two sites should not be treated as one permit program.

## BANK-189-039 - Nepal trekking-route comparison

- The Nepal Tourism Board's [TIMS guidance](https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/tims-card) lists Everest Base Camp and Annapurna Circuit among routes covered by its stated 2023 revised guide/card provision. Its [protected-area entry page](https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/park-entry-fees) separates national-park and conservation-area entry fees, while its [trekking-permit page](https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/trekking-permit) discusses restricted-area permits.
- **Claim limit:** Different agencies and route segments can have different requirements. Accessed tourism pages differ in fee figures and may be updated at different times; no exact current fee, total trek length, difficulty ranking or safety conclusion is certified here. Check the responsible office and date for a script.
- **Overlap:** BANK-189-025 through 027 cover other named trekking regions; this is a Nepal route/permit comparison, not another generic regional journey.

## BANK-189-016 - lightning framing

- The National Park Service's [general lightning guidance](https://www.nps.gov/articles/lightning-safety.htm) prioritizes going indoors or to a hard-topped enclosed vehicle and says tents are not protection. Its [Rocky Mountain page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/lightning-safety.htm) discusses location-specific exposed-terrain contingencies.
- **Claim limit:** The archived “1:00 PM” and crouch wording is not a universal forecast or a guarantee. Do not present a last-resort posture as equivalent to reaching safe shelter; check current local weather and official park guidance.
