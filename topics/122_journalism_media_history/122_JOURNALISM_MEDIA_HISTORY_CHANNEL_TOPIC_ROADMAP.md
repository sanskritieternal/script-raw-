## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Journalism & Media History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Gutenberg, The Penny Press & Yellow Journalism (Beginner / Scratch)** — Movable type printing press revolution, the John Peter Zenger libel precedent, penny press democratization, Pulitzer vs Hearst yellow journalism, and the creation of objective wire services (AP).
- **Tier 2: Muckrakers, War Correspondents & The Golden Age (Intermediate)** — Ida Tarbell dismantling Standard Oil, Edward R. Murrow broadcasting from the London Blitz, Cronkite and the Vietnam turning point, and Pentagon Papers supreme court battles.
- **Tier 3: Watergate, Cable News 24/7 & Media Consolidation (Upper Intermediate)** — Woodward and Bernstein's Deep Throat investigation, Ted Turner's CNN Gulf War debut, Fox News vs MSNBC partisan divide, Telecommunications Act of 1996, and the death of local papers.
- **Tier 4: Digital Algorithmic Disruption, Citizen OSINT & Future Newsrooms (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Bellingcat open-source intelligence (OSINT), WikiLeaks and Snowden whistleblowing, algorithmic ad duopoly (Google/Meta) destroying newspaper ad revenues, and the decentralized future of journalism.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **Watergate: How Woodward and Bernstein Brought Down a Presidency**
   - *Question:* How did two junior metro reporters follow a seemingly routine DNC burglary to unmask the Oval Office cover-up?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow the money, Deep Throat garage meetings, Ben Bradlee's editorial courage, subpoena battles, and Nixon's resignation.
2. **The Printing Press Revolution: How Gutenberg Smashed the Information Monopoly**
   - *Question:* How did movable metal type undermine the Catholic Church's Latin monopoly and unleash the Protestant Reformation?
   - *Key Focus:* Movable type metallurgical casting, vernacular Bible translations, pamphleteering, censorship bans, and the birth of public sphere debate.
3. **The Penny Press: How 1-Cent Papers Created Mass Culture and Sensationalism**
   - *Question:* How did the 1830s New York Sun and Herald shift newspaper business models from partisan subscriptions to mass advertising?
   - *Key Focus:* Steam-powered rotary presses, street-corner newsboys, crime reporting, human interest stories, and the Great Moon Hoax of 1835.
4. **Pulitzer vs Hearst: How Yellow Journalism Manufactured the Spanish-American War**
   - *Question:* Did William Randolph Hearst's sensationalist tabloid headlines really force America into war over the sinking of the USS Maine?
   - *Key Focus:* The Yellow Kid comic strip wars, banner scare headlines, fabricated atrocities, jingoism, and Citizen Kane's real-life inspiration.
5. **The Muckrakers: How Progressive Reporters Destroyed Gilded Age Monopolies**
   - *Question:* How did magazine exposés by Ida Tarbell and Upton Sinclair lead to the breakup of Standard Oil and the creation of the FDA?
   - *Key Focus:* McClure's Magazine, Tarbell's forensic accounting of Rockefeller's secret rail rebates, Sinclair's The Jungle meatpacking horrors, and antitrust legislation.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Edward R. Murrow and the London Blitz: The Birth of Broadcast Radio Journalism**
   - *Question:* How did rooftop audio broadcasts of falling Nazi bombs across London persuade isolationist Americans to enter World War II?
   - *Key Focus:* 'This is London' sign-on, natural ambient soundscapes, vivid descriptive restraint, the 'Murrow Boys', and CBS news standards.
7. **Murrow vs McCarthy: How a 30-Minute TV Broadcast Smashed the Red Scare**
   - *Question:* How did See It Now use Senator Joe McCarthy's own recorded words to expose his anti-communist witch hunts?
   - *Key Focus:* Television as an editorial instrument, fearless corporate confrontation (Alcoa sponsorship), the famous closing monologue, and McCarthy's censure.
8. **The Pentagon Papers: The Supreme Court Battle for the Free Press**
   - *Question:* How did Daniel Ellsberg's leaked 7,000-page top-secret history of the Vietnam War establish the constitutional ban on prior restraint?
   - *Key Focus:* New York Times and Washington Post defiance, Attorney General injunctions, 6-3 Supreme Court ruling, and the Espionage Act.
9. **The 24-Hour News Cycle: How Ted Turner's CNN Changed the World with the Gulf War**
   - *Question:* How did live tracer-fire broadcasts from Baghdad's Al-Rashid Hotel turn global news into an around-the-clock spectacle?
   - *Key Focus:* Satellite flyaway packs, live unedited transmission, Peter Arnett and Bernard Shaw, the 'CNN Effect' on foreign policy, and the hunger for constant updates.
10. **The Fairness Doctrine Repeal: How Talk Radio and Fox News Polarized America**
   - *Question:* How did the FCC's 1987 decision to eliminate mandatory contrasting viewpoints create partisan media empires?
   - *Key Focus:* Public airwave trustee doctrine, Rush Limbaugh AM radio boom, Roger Ailes and Fox News 'Fair and Balanced' strategy, and MSNBC counterpart.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Death of the Local Newspaper: How Craigslist and Google Swallowed the Ad Moat**
   - *Question:* Why did profitable local gazettes collapse when Craig Newmark gave away classified ads for free online?
   - *Key Focus:* Classified ad profit engine (unbundled print), private equity hedge fund gutting (Alden Global Capital), news deserts, and local municipal corruption rises.
12. **Whistleblowers and Digital Leaks: WikiLeaks, Julian Assange, and Edward Snowden**
   - *Question:* How did encrypted dropboxes and mass digital leaks replace envelope exchanges in dark parking garages?
   - *Key Focus:* Collateral Murder cockpit footage, NSA PRISM surveillance revelations, Laura Poitras and Glenn Greenwald, and modern Espionage Act prosecutions.
13. **Bellingcat and OSINT: How Amateur Internet Sleuths Solved Global War Crimes**
   - *Question:* How did Eliot Higgins and volunteer researchers use Google Earth, dashcam videos, and social media geotags to identify Russian missile units?
   - *Key Focus:* Downing of Malaysia Airlines MH17, Skripal poisoning identification, digital shadow analysis, open-source verification tools, and citizen journalism.
14. **The Spotlight Team: How The Boston Globe Exposed the Catholic Church Abuse Scandal**
   - *Question:* How did a dedicated investigative unit cross-reference directories and legal files to expose systemic abuse across decades?
   - *Key Focus:* Court seal unsealing, institutional cover-up tracking, victim interview trauma sensitivity, editorial independence against powerful civic institutions.
15. **The Paywall Revolution: How The New York Times Survived the Digital Transition**
   - *Question:* How did legacy print publishers pivot from collapsing print ads to 10 million digital subscriptions powered by Wordle and Cooking?
   - *Key Focus:* Metered vs dynamic paywalls, subscriber ARPU economics, lifestyle ecosystem bundling, and surviving the ad-supported digital collapse.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **The Dangers of War Reporting: Syria, Ukraine, and the Most Deadly Era for Journalists**
   - *Question:* Why has the historic 'PRESS' flak jacket transformed from a badge of neutral protection into a deliberate sniper target?
   - *Key Focus:* Targeted assassinations, drone surveillance risks, local 'fixer' exploitation and protection, PTSD trauma, and Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) data.
17. **Algorithmic News Feeds and Rage Engagement: How Social Media Broke Public Discourse**
   - *Question:* How did Facebook, Twitter, and TikTok feed algorithms optimize for moral outrage, creating tribal echo chambers and killing nuance?
   - *Key Focus:* Outrage dopamine feedback loops, engagement optimization math, viral misdirection, zero-click platforms, and political radicalization.
18. **AI in the Newsroom: Automated Financial Reports, Deepfake Anchors, and Synthetic Journalism**
   - *Question:* Will large language models replace beat reporters writing earnings summaries, or will AI flood the web with synthetic clickbait spam?
   - *Key Focus:* Automated earnings and sports wire generation (AP/Bloomberg), synthetic voice cloning for news bulletins, hallucination liabilities, and editorial guardrails.
19. **Substack and the Creator Journalist: The Rise of Independent Solo Newsrooms**
   - *Question:* Why are top investigative writers abandoning established magazines to build multi-million dollar direct newsletter businesses?
   - *Key Focus:* Direct subscriber monetization, unbundled talent economics, editorial autonomy vs lack of institutional legal backing, and media decentralization.
20. **The Master Newsroom Architecture: The Future of Investigative Journalism**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive institutional blueprint unites cryptographic whistleblower channels, open-source satellite tools, and sustainable subscriber models?
   - *Key Focus:* End-to-end investigative protocol, cross-border collaborative consortiums (ICIJ / Panama Papers), legal defense shields, and verifiable factual integrity.
