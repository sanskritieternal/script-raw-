# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-209-036: Demak, Central Java

- Wetlands International, 2025 implementer account of Building with Nature Indonesia (2015-2021): https://www.wetlands.org/publication/wetlands-for-resilience-securing-eroding-delta-coastlines-of-the-northern-coastal-java/
- Wetlands International, original four-year monitoring report on permeable structures: https://www.wetlands.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Ecological-mangrove-restoration-by-use-of-permeable-structures.pdf
- The report examines sediment capture and mangrove colonization behind structures at Demak. These are site-specific implementer and project-linked records, not independent proof of a universal survival rate, a government-wide project failure percentage, or guaranteed protection under all storm conditions. Verify monitoring methods and local community accounts before production.

## BANK-209-037: Laguna Grande, Fajardo

- Para la Naturaleza, reserve landscape page: https://www.paralanaturaleza.org/veredas/laguna-grande
- Para la Naturaleza, bioluminescence educational brochure: https://www.paralanaturaleza.org/dl/Brochure-Bioluminiscencia-2016.pdf
- The reserve manager identifies a mangrove-fringed lagoon and the bioluminescent dinoflagellate Pyrodinium bahamense. The light is not produced by mangrove trees. The brochure describes suitable lagoon conditions, not a controlled causal test that mangroves alone create or maintain bioluminescence. Nightly visibility is variable; avoid tourism or paddling advice.
