#### BANK-209-010 — The Salt Survivors: How Mangroves Stand Between Ocean Waves and Inland Continents

**Video question:** How do woody land trees stand in corrosive ocean brine, thrive in toxic mud, and protect tropical coastlines from catastrophic storms?

**Individual coverage scope:** Convergent evolution across 70 distinct plant species sharing halophytic adaptations, intertidal ocean boundary physics, stabilizing shifting coastlines, and ecological connectivity.

**Neighboring scope:** [BANK-202-024](../202_trees/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-202-024) — Mangrove ecology overview versus tree adaptation across all trees.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-209-011"></a>
#### BANK-209-011 — Red, Black, and White Mangroves: Zonation Patterns Across the Intertidal Boundary

**Video question:** Why do different mangrove species arrange themselves in strict horizontal bands from low tide mudflats to high dry shorelines?

**Individual coverage scope:** Rhizophora mangle (red mangrove with seaward stilt prop roots), Avicennia germinans (black mangrove with vertical pencil snorkel pneumatophores), and Laguncularia racemosa (white mangrove with upland salt glands).

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-209-012"></a>
#### BANK-209-012 — Ultrafiltration vs Salt Secretion: How Roots Desalinate Seawater at 40 Atmospheres

**Video question:** How do red mangrove roots extract pure freshwater from the ocean using physical reverse osmosis membranes with zero external electrical power?

**Individual coverage scope:** Negative hydrostatic tension (40 atmospheres) in xylem pulling water across suberized root membranes excluding 99% of sodium ions, black mangroves actively excreting salt crystals onto leaves, and salt leaf shedding.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-209-013"></a>
#### BANK-209-013 — Pneumatophores and Lenticels: The Underwater Snorkel Roots Breathing Air in Black Mud

**Video question:** Why do black mangrove roots project thousands of 10-inch woody pencils out of the mud, and how do they breathe when high tides submerge them?

**Individual coverage scope:** Aerenchyma spongy tissue, hydrophobic lenticel pores allowing air in while excluding seawater, negative pressure created as oxygen is consumed sucking fresh air down at low tide, and surviving hydrogen sulfide mud.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-209-014"></a>
#### BANK-209-014 — Viviparous Propagules: Seeds That Germinate on the Mother Tree and Float for Miles

**Video question:** Why do red mangrove seeds grow into 12-inch green torpedoes while still hanging on the branch before dropping like darts into the mud?

**Individual coverage scope:** True vivipary in plants, developing green photosynthetic hypocotyls, surviving months floating horizontally in saltwater ocean currents, shifting vertically when encountering freshwater brackish mud, and anchoring.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-209-016 — The Coral-Mangrove Nursery: How 75% of Tropical Fish Spend Their Youth in Roots

**Video question:** Why would open-ocean coral reef fish like snappers, barracudas, and groupers travel miles to lay eggs inside murky mangrove creeks?

**Individual coverage scope:** Structural shelter preventing large ocean predators from entering shallow tangled prop roots, abundant plankton and detritus food supply, gradual salinity acclimatization, and migrating out to coral reefs as adults.

**Individual coverage scope:** Mudskipper cutaneous respiration and specialized gill chambers holding water bubbles, visual courtship leaps, fiddler crab ecological bioturbation oxygenating anaerobic sediment, and organic detritus consumption.

#### BANK-209-019 — Epibiont Communities: Oysters, Sponges, and Tunicates Encasing Mangrove Roots

**Individual coverage scope:** Root encrustation ecology, sponges protecting roots from wood-boring isopod crustacean parasites, mutualistic nutrient exchange (sponges providing inorganic nitrogen for mangrove carbon), and filter feeding.

**Individual coverage scope:** Local community ownership, building freshwater wells and school classrooms with carbon sale profits, women leading seedling nurseries, and ending illegal charcoal cutting through economic empowerment.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-209-029 — Mangrove Expansion Under Climate Warming: Moving North into Salt Marshes as Frost Declines

**Video question:** What happens when rising winter minimum temperatures allow red and black mangroves to march north into Texas, Louisiana, and Florida salt marshes?

**Individual coverage scope:** Poleward range expansion, frost threshold sensitivity (killing freezes below 28°F becoming rare), replacing herbaceous Spartina salt marshes with woody shrub canopies, and changing coastal carbon dynamics.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.
