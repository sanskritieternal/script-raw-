## BANK-398-043: SHARE Frome library of things

- [Frome Town Council's project page](https://www.frometowncouncil.gov.uk/share-a-library-of-things/) identifies SHARE as a partnership launched in 2015. It links a 2018/19 impact assessment and reports modeled greenhouse-gas, material and community-money savings from borrowing. The underlying assessment's borrowing counts, replacement assumptions, lifecycle boundaries and uncertainty need review before exact savings are quoted.
- This is a named example of service access in place of individual ownership. Existence of a borrowing service does not show that every borrower would otherwise buy the item, that the same item is always available, or that a particular household saves money after travel and borrowing costs.
- The case addresses 398's borrow-versus-own question. It differs from 396's municipal recycling and composting service case and from 397's household record organization.
