# Air quality source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-522-043 - Port of Los Angeles 2023 inventory and monitoring

- Primary operator inventory: Port of Los Angeles, *Annual Inventory of Air Emissions*, https://portoflosangeles.org/environment/air-quality/air-emissions-inventory (inspected 2026-10-24). The port links its 2023 technical inventory and earlier annual baselines; the 2023 report is https://kentico.portoflosangeles.org/getmedia/3fad9979-f2cb-4b3d-bf82-687434cbd628/2023-Air-Emissions-Inventory . The inventory estimates emissions associated with maritime-industry sources under stated activity and methodology assumptions; its 2023 report was issued in August 2024.
- Primary operator monitoring: Port of Los Angeles, *Air Quality Monitoring*, https://portoflosangeles.org/environment/air-quality/air-quality-monitoring (inspected 2026-10-24). The port says real-time ambient monitoring began in 2008 and covers several pollutants at selected locations. Monitoring records can check local conditions but also include non-port sources and meteorology.
- Limits: Emissions inventories estimate source output, whereas ambient monitors sample concentrations at their sites. Neither alone proves an individual's inhaled dose, observed disease burden, or a causal effect of one clean-air measure. Year comparisons require matched inventory methodology, activity and pollutant definitions; the port's press claims are operator interpretation. Current inventory editions and monitoring-site uptime need separate verification. No individualized health advice.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-522-043 is the named port evidence comparison. BANK-522-033 covers general attribution; BANK-522-037 monitoring placement; BANK-501 covers population health rather than personal dose or clinical causation.
