## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Traditional Skills across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Timber Framing, Blacksmithing & Stone Masonry Fundamentals (Beginner / Scratch)** — Mortise and tenon wood joinery, charcoal forge heat colors and metallurgy, dry-stone wall gravity mechanics, axe hewing logs into square beams, and natural lime mortars.
- **Tier 2: Wheelwrighting, Cooperage & Wool Spinning (Intermediate)** — Shrinking red-hot iron tires onto wooden wagon wheels, stave steaming and trussing oak barrels, drop-spindle raw wool carding, pit-tanning raw hides with tree bark, and slate roofing.
- **Tier 3: Traditional Joinery, Leather Tanning & Heritage Seed Saving (Upper Intermediate)** — Japanese interlocking temple carpentry (Kigumi), vegetable bark tanning chemistry, blacksmith fire welding, open-pollinated heirloom grain stewardship, and historic horse plowing.
- **Tier 4: Intangible Cultural Heritage, Master Guild Apprenticeships & Ancient Craft Preservation (Advanced / Masterclass)** — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage treaties, European journeyman Wandergeselle traditions, restoring medieval cathedral timber roofs (Notre Dame), and establishing sustainable craft guilds.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Lost Art of Cooperage: How Wooden Barrels Built Global Trade**
   - *Question:* How did ancient coopers bend stiff white oak staves with fire and steam to create watertight containers that carried world trade for 2,000 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Stave bevelling angles, iron hoop tensioning, charring and toasting chemistry for whiskey/wine aging, and the decline of the cooper trade to steel shipping containers.
2. **Dry Stone Walling: Building 200-Year Fences Without a Single Drop of Mortar**
   - *Question:* Why do mortar-less stone walls in the Scottish Highlands survive frost and centuries of sheep while concrete block walls crack and fall?
   - *Key Focus:* Friction, gravitational batter slope (taper), hearting packing stones, through-stones anchoring both faces, and coping stones capping the wall.
3. **The Village Blacksmith: Charcoal, Air Blast, and the Colors of Steel**
   - *Question:* How does a smith judge steel temperature down to 50 degrees using only the ambient shadow color of glowing iron?
   - *Key Focus:* Charcoal vs coal combustion chemistry, hand-cranked bellows, cherry red to straw yellow tempering colors, quenching mediums, and hammer anvil bounce physics.
4. **Timber Framing: The Ancient Joint That Holds Barns for 500 Years**
   - *Question:* How do mortise and tenon joints held together by dried white oak pegs lock massive green timbers into earthquake-resistant cathedral frames?
   - *Key Focus:* Drawboring offset hole physics, broadaxe log hewing, beetle mallets and framing chisels, bents raising rituals, and barn preservation.
5. **The Wheelwright's Miracle: Shrinking Red-Hot Iron Tires Onto Oak Wheels**
   - *Question:* What happens when a red-hot 1,000°F iron ring is lowered onto a wooden wagon wheel and instantly quenched with buckets of cold water?
   - *Key Focus:* Thermal expansion and contraction mechanics, wheel dishing against cornering lateral forces, hub mortising, felloes and spokes carving.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Vegetable Bark Tanning: Turning Stiff Rawhide into Supple Leather with Oak Bark**
   - *Question:* How do tannins extracted from ground oak and chestnut bark preserve animal collagen without toxic chromium chemicals?
   - *Key Focus:* Hydrated lime de-hairing pits, progressive tannin pit vats (12-month soak), currying with tallow and cod oil, and historical tanneries.
7. **Scything vs String Trimmers: The Quiet Physics of Cutting Hay by Hand**
   - *Question:* Why can an experienced mower with a razor-sharp Austrian scythe cut an acre of grass faster and quieter than a gas-powered weed whacker?
   - *Key Focus:* Ergonomic snath balance, thin hand-peened blade edges, slicing motion vs chopping impact, whetstone field honing, and hay curing.
8. **Thatching Roofs: 300 Years of Water Shedding with Water Reed and Straw**
   - *Question:* How does a 12-inch layer of dried river reed or wheat straw keep English cottages bone-dry during torrential rainstorms for 50 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Steep 50-degree roof pitch capillary shedding, hazel spar fixing, ridging patterns, natural thermal insulation values, and fire-retardant sprays.
9. **Raw Wool to Tweed: The Spinning Wheel and the Gaelic Waulking Song**
   - *Question:* How did crofters on the Outer Hebrides turn oily sheep fleece into weatherproof, indestructible Harris Tweed on pedal-powered loom shuttles?
   - *Key Focus:* Lanolin preservation, drop spindle twist mathematics, natural lichen and peat bog dyeing, waulking communal fabric fulling, and protected origin laws.
10. **Lime Mortar vs Modern Portland Cement: Why New Mortar Destroys Old Brick**
   - *Question:* Why does pointing historic 18th-century masonry with modern Portland cement cause old bricks to crack, spall, and turn to dust?
   - *Key Focus:* The lime cycle (limestone calcination, slaking, carbonation), vapor permeability ('breathability'), sacrificial sacrificial mortar concept, and historic preservation.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Japanese Kigumi: Interlocking Wooden Temples Built Without a Single Nail**
   - *Question:* How do 1,000-year-old wooden pagodas in Kyoto flex and sway during violent 8.0 earthquakes without breaking or collapsing?
   - *Key Focus:* Shinbashira central pendulum pillar, Kanawa Tsugi scarf joints, seasonal wood expansion tolerances, pull-saw (Nokogiri) precision, and Master Miya-daiku carpentry.
12. **Charcoal Burning: The Ancient Colliers Who Powered the Iron Age**
   - *Question:* How did forest colliers stack 20 cords of hardwood under turf and dirt and burn it without oxygen for a week to make pure carbon?
   - *Key Focus:* Pyrolysis anaerobic decomposition, volatile smoke color reading, turf mound venting, danger of internal hollow flare-ups, and smelting fuel purity.
13. **Green Woodworking and Bodging: Turning Fresh Logs into Chairs on Pole Lathes**
   - *Question:* Why is freshly felled green timber easier to shape with drawknives and foot-powered pole lathes than kiln-dried lumber?
   - *Key Focus:* Shaving horses, split-ring cell structure shrinkage locking round mortises, reciprocating spring pole lathe mechanics, and Windsor chair construction.
14. **Draft Horses and Traditional Plowing: Farming with Living 2,000-Pound Engines**
   - *Question:* Why are modern organic vineyards and smallholder farms trading 200-horsepower diesel tractors for teams of Percheron and Suffolk Punch horses?
   - *Key Focus:* Soil compaction elimination, horse collar harness draft line mechanics, moldboard plow furrow slicing, voice command steering, and closed-loop farm fertility.
15. **Heritage Seed Saving: Guarding 10,000 Years of Open-Pollinated Crop Genetics**
   - *Question:* Why are seed-savers freezing jars of heirloom tomato and grain seeds to prevent corporate hybrid seeds from monopolizing food supplies?
   - *Key Focus:* F1 hybrid seed sterility traps, open-pollination genetic diversity, isolation distance cross-pollination prevention, seed vaults (Svalbard), and nutritional density.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Notre Dame Cathedral Roof: Rebuilding the Medieval Forest with Hand Hewn Oak**
   - *Question:* Why did French master carpenters refuse modern electric sawmills to recreate the burned 13th-century 'Forest' roof truss by hand?
   - *Key Focus:* Following natural grain curvatures, doloire broadaxe hewing marks, plumb bob drafting, medieval plumb-line scribing, and the rebirth of French guild pride.
17. **The Journeyman Tradition: The Wandergeselle Secret Guilds of Europe**
   - *Question:* Why do young European carpentry apprentices still wear flared corduroy suits, carry spiral staves, and wander the continent for 3 years without phones?
   - *Key Focus:* The 3 years and 1 day travel rule, 50-kilometer home banishment radius, hitchhiking only, guild secret greetings, and mastering regional building techniques.
18. **Intangible Cultural Heritage: UNESCO's Battle to Save Dying Human Knowledge**
   - *Question:* What happens when the last human who knows how to weave a Peruvian rope bridge or blow Venetian glass passes away?
   - *Key Focus:* The Q'eswachaka annual Incan grass rope bridge rebuild, Living Human Treasure designations, economic viability challenges, and digital archiving vs muscle memory.
19. **The Modern Homestead Renaissance: Why Software Engineers Are Learning to Blacksmith**
   - *Question:* Why are knowledge workers staring at computer screens burning out and turning to wood-chopping, sourdough bread, and manual forging?
   - *Key Focus:* Matthew Crawford's Shop Class as Soulcraft, cognitive alienation from abstract work, the dopamine of physical tangible outputs, and sovereign self-reliance.
20. **The Master Traditional Guild Architecture: Preserving Craft for the Next 500 Years**
   - *Question:* What operational framework ensures the intergenerational survival of hands-on traditional craftsmanship in the 21st century?
   - *Key Focus:* Apprenticeship economic subsidies, heritage craft certification standards, toolmaking toolmaker networks, living village museums, and sustainable artisan enterprise.
