# Case-source note: Jackson drinking-water interim order

Checked 2026-10-24. The EPA's [Jackson drinking-water case page](https://www.epa.gov/ms/jackson-ms-drinking-water) says that a federal district court entered an interim stipulated order on November 29, 2022, agreed by EPA, DOJ, the Mississippi State Department of Health and Jackson. It set a Priority Projects List and appointed an interim third-party manager, operating through JXN Water, to manage and operate the drinking-water system and carry out that list. The page links the [court-entered order](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-08/ISO%20entered%20by%20court_11292022.pdf) and subsequent reports. EPA's [2024 Office of Inspector General review](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/oig/documents/full_report_-_24-e-0055_errata.pdf) examined the earlier enforcement timeline and found opportunities for earlier action.

Limits: The court order describes legal duties and an interim arrangement; it does not itself establish full completion, current service reliability, individual exposure, or health outcomes. EPA's page includes later progress statements and changing litigation dates, so production must recheck dated court and utility records before claiming an outcome. The OIG review assesses oversight and does not determine each resident's experience. This is a U.S. Mississippi system case, not a universal water-treatment design or operational instruction.

Overlap: `BANK-501-037` is wastewater epidemiology, while `BANK-507-035`, `BANK-507-033` and `BANK-507-041` address general finance, household burden and emergency planning. This topic is the named 2022 court-management assignment.
