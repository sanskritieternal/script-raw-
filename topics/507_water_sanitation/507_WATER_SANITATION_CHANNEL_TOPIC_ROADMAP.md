## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Water & Sanitation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Water & Sanitation Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Water & Sanitation.
- **Tier 2: Water & Sanitation Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Water & Sanitation.
- **Tier 3: Water & Sanitation Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Water & Sanitation.
- **Tier 4: Water & Sanitation Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Water & Sanitation.
