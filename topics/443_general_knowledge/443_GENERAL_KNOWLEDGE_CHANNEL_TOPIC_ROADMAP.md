## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of General Knowledge across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: General Knowledge Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in General Knowledge.
- **Tier 2: General Knowledge Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of General Knowledge.
- **Tier 3: General Knowledge Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in General Knowledge.
- **Tier 4: General Knowledge Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for General Knowledge.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of General Knowledge: How a Fact Entered Common Knowledge**
   - *Question:* When and through which sources did one claim become widely taught?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated publications and corrections. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
2. **General Knowledge: One Familiar Fact Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What does its best primary source actually establish?
   - *Key Focus:* Define place, date, and wording. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
3. **General Knowledge: Knowledge beyond the Canon**
   - *Question:* Whose evidence was left out of a standard overview?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare source selection and contributors. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
4. **General Knowledge: From Question to Reliable Answer**
   - *Question:* Which source and definition must a learner check first?
   - *Key Focus:* Work through one bounded example. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
5. **General Knowledge: Facts with Conditions**
   - *Question:* How does a true statement change with scale or definition?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare two valid frames and limits. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **General Knowledge: A Question without a Settled Answer**
   - *Question:* What is known, disputed, or unmeasured?
   - *Key Focus:* Show evidence gaps rather than force certainty. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
7. **General Knowledge: A Tool for Finding Answers**
   - *Question:* Does a search or reference tool help verify one claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare source traceability and error rate. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
8. **General Knowledge: Who Defined the Standard Answer?**
   - *Question:* Which people and institutions selected a familiar formulation?
   - *Key Focus:* Use curriculum and reference history. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
9. **General Knowledge: A Fact Gets Revised**
   - *Question:* Which new evidence changed a textbook answer?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated editions and studies. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
10. **General Knowledge: Checking a General-Knowledge Claim**
   - *Question:* How do definition, source, date, and counterexample affect confidence?
   - *Key Focus:* Demonstrate a transparent verification path. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **General Knowledge: A Once-Common Answer Fades**
   - *Question:* Why was a popular answer dropped?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original evidence and later correction. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
12. **General Knowledge: The Same Question across Time**
   - *Question:* How did a time-sensitive answer change?
   - *Key Focus:* Include both as-of dates and scope. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
13. **General Knowledge: Questions Likely to Change**
   - *Question:* Which facts need a dated update before reuse?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify changing institutions or measurements. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
14. **General Knowledge: A Plausible False Fact**
   - *Question:* Which part of a familiar claim fails?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare exact wording with primary evidence. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
15. **General Knowledge: One Answer's Source Trail**
   - *Question:* How did a claim travel from record to classroom and media?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow citation chain and revisions. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **General Knowledge: Build a Knowledge Map**
   - *Question:* Which prerequisite ideas make a broad topic understandable?
   - *Key Focus:* Sequence three claims with explicit links. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
17. **General Knowledge: An Overgeneralized Rule**
   - *Question:* What example reveals a common learner misunderstanding?
   - *Key Focus:* Use pre-response and counterexample. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
18. **General Knowledge: Verify a Number**
   - *Question:* How can a learner reproduce one published statistic?
   - *Key Focus:* Show denominator, unit, year, and source. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
19. **General Knowledge: Recall with Context**
   - *Question:* Does revisiting a fact after delay improve accurate recall?
   - *Key Focus:* Use equivalent items and feedback. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
20. **General Knowledge: Explain an Incorrect Answer**
   - *Question:* Which explanation identifies the mistaken assumption?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare feedback quality, not just score. Evidence base: primary source records, dated reference and curriculum editions, evidence reviews, learner responses, and education practice guides.
