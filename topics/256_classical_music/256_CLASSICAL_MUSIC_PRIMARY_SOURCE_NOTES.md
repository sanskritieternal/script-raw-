# Selected primary and institutional checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-256-041: Beethoven Ninth sources

- Beethoven-Haus Bonn digitized autograph of added trombone parts for movements two and four, catalog HCB Mh 28: https://www.beethoven.de/en/media/view/5626809903218688/scan/1
- Beethoven-Haus digitized autograph coda leaves, catalog HCB Mh 2 and HCB BMh 5/45: https://www.beethoven.de/en/media/view/5593271610900480/scan/1
- The archive describes late additions around April-May 1824 and warns no single surviving manuscript reflects all final intentions. The catalog description and facsimile establish document identity, not every interpretive or performance choice; compare source chronology and scholarly edition before production. Image reproduction rights require checking.

## BANK-256-042: Gould's Goldberg recordings

- Library of Congress 2003 National Recording Registry entry for Glenn Gould's 1955 recording: https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/registry-by-induction-years/2003/
- LOC expanded registry essay: https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/GoldbergVar.pdf
- LOC confirms the 1955 recording and 1981 rerecording. Registry inclusion and an essay are institutional context, not a substitute for licensed listening, versioned score/audio comparison or complete session archives. Claims about tempo, touch, edits, sales and reception need independent primary evidence. No definitive-performance ranking follows from selection.
