## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Global Economy across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Global Economy Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Global Economy.
- **Tier 2: Global Economy Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Global Economy.
- **Tier 3: Global Economy Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Global Economy.
- **Tier 4: Global Economy Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Global Economy.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Global Economy: A Global Economic Link Forms**
   - *Question:* Which institutions and trade or capital flows connected economies?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated national accounts and agreements. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
2. **Global Economy: A Cited Growth Miracle Rechecked**
   - *Question:* Which measures support the label and who benefited?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare GDP per person, wages, distribution, and baselines. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
3. **Global Economy: Workers Behind Global Growth**
   - *Question:* Whose paid and unpaid labor disappears in headline aggregates?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare sector, gender, migration, and care evidence. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
4. **Global Economy: Reading a World-Economy Claim**
   - *Question:* What does one indicator measure and omit?
   - *Key Focus:* Define geography, year, currency, price basis, and source. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
5. **Global Economy: Nominal and Real Comparisons**
   - *Question:* How do exchange rates and purchasing power change rankings?
   - *Key Focus:* Use matched series and explain conversion choices. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Global Economy: Why Forecasts Diverge**
   - *Question:* Which assumptions explain two economic projections?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare model, policy, oil, trade, and update date. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
7. **Global Economy: Technology and Productivity**
   - *Question:* Did adoption raise measured output per hour?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare sectors, timing, diffusion, and labor effects. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
8. **Global Economy: A Policy Maker's Choice**
   - *Question:* How did one decision affect an economy?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace proposal, implementation, counterfactual limits, and outcomes. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
9. **Global Economy: A Global Shock**
   - *Question:* How did one event travel through trade, finance, and households?
   - *Key Focus:* Build dated sequence with comparable indicators. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
10. **Global Economy: Flows Around the World**
   - *Question:* How do goods, income, capital, remittances, and debt interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Map one bounded chain across named economies. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Global Economy: A Growth Cycle Compared**
   - *Question:* What drove a measured expansion or contraction?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate price, volume, population, and revision. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
12. **Global Economy: World Output Across Decades**
   - *Question:* Which structural changes are robust to measure choice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use constant prices, weights, and consistent country coverage. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
13. **Global Economy: A Conditional World Outlook**
   - *Question:* Which assumptions change a forecast?
   - *Key Focus:* State model vintage, rates, trade, energy, and uncertainty. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
14. **Global Economy: One Number Describes Prosperity?**
   - *Question:* Why can GDP rise while households struggle?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare median income, price, employment, and services. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
15. **Global Economy: One Shock Through a Supply Chain**
   - *Question:* How did policy and prices reach people?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated firms, workers, households, and markets. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Global Economy: A Product Across Economies**
   - *Question:* Where is value added and who captures income?
   - *Key Focus:* Use input-output and firm evidence. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
17. **Global Economy: Borrowers, Lenders, States, Workers**
   - *Question:* How do responses to a shock differ?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace contracts, policy, and distributional effects. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
18. **Global Economy: A Producer Under Currency Change**
   - *Question:* How do imported inputs and export receipts affect margin?
   - *Key Focus:* Define currency, timing, hedges, and volume. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
19. **Global Economy: Prices in Household Budgets**
   - *Question:* Which global change altered a defined basket?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare local pass-through, substitutes, and income. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
20. **Global Economy: Global Concentration in One Input**
   - *Question:* Which suppliers constrain a defined market?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure capacity, geography, and realistic alternatives. Evidence base: dated national accounts and WEO series with definitions and revisions, trade and labor data, policy records, and attributed household and worker accounts.
