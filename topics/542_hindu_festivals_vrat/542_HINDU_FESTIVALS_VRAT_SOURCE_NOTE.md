## BANK-542-054 - Bihar Tourism Chhath account

- Primary regional institutional account: Government of Bihar Tourism, *Chhath Puja*, https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/en/experiences/festivals-and-fairs/festivals/chhath-puja (inspected 2026-10-24). It describes Chhath as a major festival in Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh, observes it over four days, situates a Kartik sixth-day calendar reference, and distinguishes offerings to the setting sun and the following dawn sun. It also names household preparation and public riverside gatherings.
- Parallel government-language version: Bihar Tourism, Hindi *Chhath Puja*, https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/hi/experiences/festivals-and-fairs/festivals/chhath-puja (inspected 2026-10-24). Compare wording before quoting local terms; the tourism summary is not an exhaustive ethnography, community testimony or a mandate for every household.
- Regional, date and care limits: Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh are the page's stated focus; practices, naming, participation and dates can vary by locality, calendar authority, year and diaspora setting. Do not repeat the page's uniqueness rhetoric as a global comparative fact without evidence. Avoid prescribing abstinence/fasting or river entry; health needs and water conditions differ. For a specific community documentary, obtain local participants' accounts and permission rather than stage or infer ritual from the tourism page alone.
