## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Public Health across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Public Health Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Public Health.
- **Tier 2: Public Health Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Public Health.
- **Tier 3: Public Health Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Public Health.
- **Tier 4: Public Health Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Public Health.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Public Health: How Public Health Developed**
   - *Question:* Which documented problem led a community to establish a public-health program?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one population-health program from the problem it addressed to its first implementation; distinguish contemporary records from later success stories. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
2. **Public Health: A Documented Public Health Case**
   - *Question:* What does a prevention program's evaluation establish beyond its success story?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine one documented prevention program, its target population, evaluation design and measured outcomes; test whether its results apply elsewhere. Evidence base: Read the original evaluation and its population, comparator, outcomes and limitations. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
3. **Public Health: People behind Public Health**
   - *Question:* Whose community work is missing from an account of a public-health response?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow an overlooked contribution by community workers or residents to one public-health response; use attributed accounts and program records. Evidence base: Use attributable contribution records and consented participant accounts. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
4. **Public Health: Understanding Public Health**
   - *Question:* How do surveillance, prevention and service delivery connect in one local program?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain surveillance, prevention and service delivery through one local program; distinguish population measures from individual diagnosis or treatment. Evidence base: Use primary definitions and a documented end-to-end example. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
5. **Public Health: Tradeoffs in Public Health**
   - *Question:* How does an evaluation separate a program's effects from other changes?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare methods used to estimate one program's effect, including comparison groups, confounding, missing data and unequal exposure. Evidence base: Compare original methods, assumptions, measures and uncertainty. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Public Health: An Open Public Health Question**
   - *Question:* Which competing explanations remain for a program's unexpected outcome?
   - *Key Focus:* Investigate one unresolved program-outcome question using competing explanations and available surveillance records; identify what evidence would distinguish them. Evidence base: Compare competing explanations and specify missing discriminating evidence. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
7. **Public Health: A Tool for Public Health**
   - *Question:* Did a public-health tool improve the outcome it was introduced to change?
   - *Key Focus:* Evaluate one public-health tool in a defined deployment; compare uptake, data quality and measured outcomes with the earlier process. Evidence base: Compare validation, deployment and follow-up against a stated baseline. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
8. **Public Health: Decisions in Public Health**

Evidence base: Use decision records, assigned authority and attributed accounts. Case records: program protocols, disaggregated surveillance data and original evaluations. Starting framework: WHO Health Inequality Monitor (https://www.who.int/data/inequality-monitor). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
