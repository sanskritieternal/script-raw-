# BANK-022-013 — Model T and the moving assembly line

**Proposal:** How did assembly-line production change more than the speed of car building?  
**State:** bounded institutional archive review, 2026-10-24. This is not a script or production approval.

## Bounded finding

The preserved queue pitch folds the Model T and moving assembly line into one invention story. The Model T was introduced in **1908**; Ford dates the automobile moving line at Highland Park to **1913**. The available collection records show an evolving production system involving workers, plant layout, machinery and methods borrowed from other industries. An episode can compare these dated stages and ask what changed for price, labor, repair, suppliers and consumers; it should not claim that Henry Ford alone invented the automobile or assembly-line work.

Before a script, inspect the 1914 worker letter and the actual plant production and wage records, then attribute any exact productivity or price figures to their year and source. The active BANK question remains a proposal, and no idea readiness or production status was changed.
