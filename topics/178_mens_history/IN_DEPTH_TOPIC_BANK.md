#### BANK-178-010 — Shell Shock in the Trenches: How World War I Shattered the Victorian Myth of Unbreakable Men

**Individual coverage scope:** Dr. W.H.R. Rivers and Craiglockhart War Hospital, poets Siegfried Sassoon and Wilfred Owen, initial accusations of malingering and firing squad executions, the discovery of psychological combat exhaustion, and the birth of PTSD.

**Individual coverage scope:** The Church's Pax Dei (Peace of God) and Truce of God decrees, restricting violence against non-combatants, the transition from savage warlords to Christian knights, tournament pageantry, and courtly love poetry.
