# Venus cloud settlement: bounded premise and source-access note

Linked proposal: [BANK-053-055](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-053-055). Research checked 2026-10-24. This is a source trail for a conditional scenario, not evidence that a Venus settlement is feasible or planned.

## Demonstrated environment versus proposed vehicle

[NASA's Venus facts](https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/) describe an extremely hot, high-pressure surface and sulfuric-acid clouds. A [NASA technical concept paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/20160006329.pdf) places a proposed airship at roughly 50 km altitude, where pressure and temperature are comparatively benign. It examines a **30-day, two-person exploration mission**, not a permanent city. The paper identifies aerocapture, airship deployment and sulfuric-acid protection among technical challenges. NASA's [concept overview](https://www.nasa.gov/general/havoc/) presents habitable airships as an artist's conceptual rendering.

## Checks for a researched scenario

1. Use the technical paper's stated mission duration, crew size, altitude and vehicle assumptions as the bounded starting point. Label a permanent settlement as an extrapolation.
2. Build an explicit mass and maintenance ledger for breathable gases, water, food, spare materials, acid-resistant surfaces and ascent vehicles. No claim of local self-sufficiency follows from a suitable pressure layer.
3. Keep atmospheric data separate from vehicle survivability: a benign temperature and pressure band does not remove cloud chemistry, winds, entry and return requirements.
4. Compare the conditional concept with [BANK-053-033](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-053-033) on off-world dependence. State which imports and Earth services the imagined community still requires.

## Limits

The NASA sources support the atmospheric setting and existence of a mission concept. They do not establish a tested crewed Venus airship, a city design, safe long-term exposure, an economy or a settlement timetable. Other 053 megastructure proposals remain conceptual and have not gained verified-source status through this note.
