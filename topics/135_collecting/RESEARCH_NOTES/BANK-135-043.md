# BANK-135-043: 1952 Topps unsold-card disposal story

Research checked: 2026-10-24. Status: bounded source audit, not script or idea approval.

Topps' [2026 historical account](https://ripped.topps.com/history-1952-topps-baseball-mickey-mantle-75th-anninversary/) treats disposal of unsold later-series inventory as an oral-history story, quotes Sy Berger's son, and says the exact waterway is uncertain. Another [Topps card entry](https://ripped.topps.com/trading-cards/1952-topps-baseball/311-311/) says the cards were dumped into the Hudson River. Those two first-party publisher pages conflict on the location. They are retrospective accounts, not manifests or contemporary disposal records.

**Claim boundary:** The accounts support that Topps itself recounts unsold inventory disposal, with a location dispute. They do not establish how many Mantle #311 cards were destroyed, a precise Atlantic Ocean dumping site, or a counterfactual link to any later auction price. Confirm an auction price from its dated auction lot and distinguish hammer price from buyer's-premium total before using it. The inherited question and trend remain preserved as archived pitches, with their stronger claims unverified.
