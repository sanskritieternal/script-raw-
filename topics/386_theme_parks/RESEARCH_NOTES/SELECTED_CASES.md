## BANK-386-043: Tokyo DisneySea Fantasy Springs

- [Oriental Land's opening presentation](https://www.olc.co.jp/en/ir/library/presentations/2023/main/02/teaserItems1/00/tableContents/03/multiFileUpload5_0/link/sp2024ex-04e.pdf) lists a 6 June 2024 opening, the themed lands, attractions and hotel, plus an approximately ¥320 billion investment estimate and a projected ¥75 billion annual consolidated sales increase under stable full-year operation. The latter is a forecast with stated assumptions, not a realized outcome.
- [The operator's later fiscal 2025 results presentation](https://www.olc.co.jp/en/ir/library/presentations/main/02/teaserItems1/00/tableContents/03/multiFileUpload5_0/link/sp2025ex-04e.pdf) reports an expected investment of approximately ¥301 billion versus the initial ¥320 billion projection. [The operator history page](https://www.olc.co.jp/en/company/history/history09.html) gives a later approximate ¥307 billion total. These figures reflect different reporting dates and definitions; identify the date and metric if using any one figure.
- Operator documents establish plans and self-reported financial figures. They do not by themselves prove visitor experience, access equity, labor conditions, local economic benefit or exact attraction throughput. An independent impact study and operational data would be needed for those claims.
- This named park-expansion case is distinct from 387's coaster controls and from 386's general guest-flow questions.
