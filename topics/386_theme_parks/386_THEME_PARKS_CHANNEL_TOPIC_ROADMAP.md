## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Theme Parks across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Theme Parks Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Theme Parks.
- **Tier 2: Theme Parks Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Theme Parks.
- **Tier 3: Theme Parks Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Theme Parks.
- **Tier 4: Theme Parks Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Theme Parks.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Theme Parks: A Park's Design Context**
   - *Question:* Which dated plan or local condition shaped a named theme park?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare master plans, opening records, and later accounts. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
2. **Theme Parks: One Park Land Examined**
   - *Question:* How do paths, facades, sound, and queues establish a themed place?
   - *Key Focus:* Walk a specified route using dated plans and recordings. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
3. **Theme Parks: Workers behind the Experience**
   - *Question:* Whose operations, maintenance, and service labor makes a land function?
   - *Key Focus:* Use staffing records and worker accounts with context. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
4. **Theme Parks: From Gate to Attraction**
   - *Question:* How does a guest find, queue for, and experience a named attraction?
   - *Key Focus:* Map the actual journey, signage, and access conditions. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
5. **Theme Parks: Capacity and Guest Flow**
   - *Question:* How do path width, ride throughput, and dwell time interact?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated operating data and disclose measurement limits. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Theme Parks: A Park Plan Never Built**
   - *Question:* Which unbuilt feature can surviving plans actually confirm?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate concept art, approved project, and speculation. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
7. **Theme Parks: A New Park System in Practice**
   - *Question:* Which operational or storytelling problem did a documented tool address?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare before and after with reliability and labor effects. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
8. **Theme Parks: A Designer's Documented Choice**
   - *Question:* Which named decision shaped a visitor route or attraction?
   - *Key Focus:* Use plans, design notes, and collaborator testimony. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
9. **Theme Parks: A Park Expansion Opens**
   - *Question:* What changed in capacity, access, or visitor experience?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare plans and operating evidence across dated periods. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
10. **Theme Parks: The Attraction as a System**
   - *Question:* How do entry, queue, dispatch, ride, exit, and reset connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one published or observed workflow without exposing restricted details. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Theme Parks: A Park Land Rethemed**
   - *Question:* Why was one themed area altered or removed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare official plans, maintenance context, and dated visitor accounts. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
12. **Theme Parks: A Park Route Across Decades**
   - *Question:* Which paths, uses, and access options changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated maps and accessibility information. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
13. **Theme Parks: A Proposed Park Project**
   - *Question:* What permits, funding, capacity, and operating choices remain?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat proposal as conditional and date every source. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
14. **Theme Parks: An Opening-Day Legend**
   - *Question:* Does a popular claim about a park match contemporary records?
   - *Key Focus:* Find earliest telling and compare opening documents. Evidence base: dated park maps and plans, attraction briefs, official operating and access information, worker accounts, maintenance and archive records, and bounded visitor research.
