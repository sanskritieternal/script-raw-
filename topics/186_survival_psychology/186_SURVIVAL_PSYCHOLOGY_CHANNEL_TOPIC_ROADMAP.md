## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Survival Psychology across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: The Will to Live: Neurobiology of Extreme Stress, Freezing & The 10/80/10 Rule (Beginner / Scratch)** — The psychological foundations of survival, sympathetic nervous system response, amygdala hijack under mortal threat, freezing behaviors, and the universal distribution of panic vs calm reactions.
- **Tier 2: Mental Traps & Cognitive Bias in Catastrophe: Denial, Pluralistic Ignorance & Bending Reality (Intermediate)** — Disaster cognitive traps: normalization bias in building fires, pluralistic ignorance in crowds, disorientation cognitive maps when lost, and the Stockdale Paradox in long-term captivity.
- **Tier 3: Extreme Isolation & Hostile Environments: Solitary Endurance, Sensory Deprivation & The Third Man Factor (Upper Intermediate)** — Solitary confinement and sensory deprivation hallucinations, the Third Man factor during hypothermic exhaustion, taboo moral dilemmas (cannibalism survival), and psychogenic 'give-up-itis' death.
- **Tier 4: The Post-Survival Mind: Resilience, Post-Traumatic Growth & The Psychology of the Survivor Mission (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Neuroplastic resilience conditioning in elite military special operations, polar winter-over syndrome, survivor guilt resolution, and post-traumatic growth frameworks.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The 10/80/10 Rule: Why Most People Freeze During Fatal Emergencies**
   - *Question:* Why do only 10% of people stay calm during a sudden catastrophe, while 80% freeze and 10% panic uncontrollably?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. John Leach's survival research, cognitive paralysis under mortal danger, working memory collapse, and conditioning rapid motor responses.
2. **The Neurobiology of Survival: Amygdala Hijack, Cortisol Floods, and Tunnel Vision**
   - *Question:* What chemical and neural cascades shut down rational calculation when the brain perceives an existential threat?
   - *Key Focus:* Sympathetic adrenal-medullary axis, auditory exclusion, tachypsychia (time dilation), vasoconstriction, and loss of fine motor dexterity.
3. **The Will to Live: How Viktor Frankl's Logotherapy Explains Extreme Survival**
   - *Question:* How did psychiatrist Viktor Frankl discover in Auschwitz that having a clear future purpose is the decisive factor in surviving mortal conditions?
   - *Key Focus:* Man's Search for Meaning, finding meaning in suffering, future-oriented hope vs immediate despair, and psychological anchoring.
4. **Panic vs Inaction: The Bizarre Instinct to Collect Luggage During Plane Evacuations**
   - *Question:* Why do airline passengers in smoke-filled cabins stop to open overhead bins for luggage instead of running for emergency slides?
   - *Key Focus:* Behavioral inertia, script preservation theory, cognitive disbelief in impending mortality, and passenger briefing compliance psychology.
5. **The Survival Rule of Threes: Psychological Triage and Decision Hierarchy**
   - *Question:* How does framing survival priorities into 3 seconds, 3 minutes, 3 hours, and 3 days prevent fatal cognitive overwhelm?
   - *Key Focus:* Cognitive load reduction, 3 seconds to control panic, 3 minutes of air, 3 hours without shelter in cold, 3 days without water, and decision tree simplicity.
### Tier 2: Cognitive Biases & Mind Traps
6. **The Third Man Factor: The Mysterious Guardian Presence Felt in Extreme Solitude**
   - *Question:* Why do solo mountaineers, polar explorers, and ship-wreck survivors repeatedly report a benevolent unseen companion guiding them to safety?
   - *Key Focus:* Ernest Shackleton's Antarctic crossing, bicameral mind theory, neuro-sensory coping mechanisms in hypoxia, and neurological theories of phantom presences.
7. **Denial in Disaster: Why Titanic Passengers Ignored Lifeboats for 45 Minutes**
   - *Question:* Why did early Titanic lifeboats launch half-empty while passengers casually socialized in brightly lit first-class lounges?
   - *Key Focus:* Normalization bias, architectural illusion of safety, the reluctance to trade a warm deck for a cold dark rowboat, and social consensus traps.
8. **Pluralistic Ignorance and the Bystander Effect: Waiting for Someone Else to Scream**
   - *Question:* How does looking around at a calm crowd convince rational people to ignore smelling smoke until it is too late?
   - *Key Focus:* Bibb Latané and John Darley's smoke-filled room experiments, social proof traps, diffusion of responsibility, and overcoming social hesitation to flee.
9. **Cognitive Map Breakdown: Why Lost People Walk in Circles and Discard Gear**
   - *Question:* What psychological break causes disoriented hikers to deny compass readings, strip off essential clothing, and walk in blind loops?
   - *Key Focus:* Hippocampal cognitive mapping failure, biomechanical spiral-walking without visual horizons, 'woods shock', and paradoxical discarding of survival gear.
10. **The Psychology of Captivity: Stockdale Paradox and Surviving Hostage Situations**
   - *Question:* How did Admiral James Stockdale survive 7 years of torture in Vietnam by balancing brutal realism with unwavering faith?
   - *Key Focus:* The Stockdale Paradox (confronting harsh reality without losing ultimate faith), optimism traps that lead to broken hearts, mental tap-codes, and prisoner solidarity.
### Tier 3: Extreme Isolation & Endurance
11. **The Donner Party & Andes Flight 571: The Agonizing Psychology of Taboo Survival**
   - *Question:* How do human beings psychologically cross moral and cultural barriers to consume human flesh to survive freezing starvation?
   - *Key Focus:* Cognitive dissonance reduction, religious sanctification of survival pacts, communal consensus building, and mental compartmentalization during starvation.
12. **Solitary Confinement and Sensory Deprivation: How the Mind Hallucinates in Total Silence**
   - *Question:* What happens to the human brain when external auditory and visual stimuli drop to near-zero for weeks on end?
   - *Key Focus:* Spontaneous neural firing in sensory cortices, loss of circadian rhythm anchors, cognitive disintegration, phantom sounds, and psychological preservation strategies.
13. **S.O.S. and Goal Framing: How Small Daily Routines Keep Castaways Sane**
   - *Question:* Why did Poon Lim survive 133 days on a life raft while others died of despair in two weeks?
   - *Key Focus:* Rigid daily scheduling, physical exercise drills, micro-goals (catching fish, counting knots), maintaining agency, and combatting depressive lethargy.
14. **Hypothermia's Final Trick: Paradoxical Undressing and Terminal Burrowing**
   - *Question:* Why do freezing victims in the final stages of hypothermia tear off their clothes and cram themselves into tiny crevices to die?
   - *Key Focus:* Vasomotor paralysis causing sudden subjective hot flashes, hypothalamic temperature regulation failure, primitive brainstem burrowing instincts, and forensic crime confusion.
15. **The 'Give-Up-Itis' Phenomenon: Psychogenic Death When the Will to Live Evaporates**
   - *Question:* Can a person with no physical wounds or terminal illness simply choose to curl up and die of psychological hopelessness in three days?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. John Leach's five-stage model of psychogenic death (demoralization, aboulia, akinetic mutism, death), dopamine pathway inhibition in the anterior cingulate cortex, and early intervention.
### Tier 4: Frontiers & Post-Survival Psychology
16. **Elite Resilience Training: How Navy SEALs and Astronauts Reprogram Fear Responses**
   - *Question:* How do military special operations and NASA train recruits to maintain cognitive function during simulated drownings and capsule depressurization?
   - *Key Focus:* The Big Four mental skills (goal setting, visualization, self-talk, arousal control / box breathing), stress inoculation training (SIT), and neurofeedback.
17. **Survivor Guilt: The Crushing Mental Burden of Being the Only One Who Made It**
   - *Question:* Why do sole survivors of plane crashes and battlefield ambushes frequently suffer more from survival than from their physical trauma?
   - *Key Focus:* Counterfactual thinking ('If only I had done X'), existential arbitrariness of death, moral injury, and therapeutic modalities for reconciling unearned survival.
18. **Post-Traumatic Growth: How Surviving Disaster Can Forge Indomitable Mental Toughness**
   - *Question:* How do some survivors emerge from catastrophic ordeal with higher life appreciation, stronger relationships, and deeper personal strength?
   - *Key Focus:* Richard Tedeschi and Lawrence Calhoun's Post-Traumatic Growth (PTG) inventory, cognitive rumination turning constructive, psychological antifragility, and renewed purpose.
19. **The Psychology of Polar Madness: Winter-Over Syndrome in Antarctic Research Stations**
   - *Question:* What happens to the minds of 50 scientists trapped together in total darkness and -100°F isolation for eight consecutive months?
   - *Key Focus:* The 'Antarctic Stare', T3 thyroid hormone syndrome in extreme cold, interpersonal friction without exit routes, seasonal affective disorder, and space mission analog studies.
20. **The Ultimate Survivor Mindset: Deconstructing Laurence Gonzales' 'Deep Survival' Principles**
   - *Question:* What common cognitive personality traits unite wilderness survivors, downed pilots, and disaster escapees across centuries of recorded crises?
   - *Key Focus:* Laurence Gonzales' Deep Survival framework: perceive and accept reality quickly, remain playful and curious, surrender pride, control emotional reactivity, and celebrate small victories.
