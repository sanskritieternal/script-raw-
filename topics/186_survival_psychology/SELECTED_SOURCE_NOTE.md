# Selected source note - niche 186

Checked 2026-10-24. These sources bound selected proposed questions; none is an emergency protocol or a diagnosis. New BANK topics remain proposed with empty verified_sources.

## BANK-186-038 - Alone production and withdrawal

The program's own [interview with lead survival and safety consultant Dave Holder](https://www.history.com/shows/alone/articles/alone-insider-interview-with-lead-survival-and-safety-consultant-dave-holder) describes site-specific risk plans, communication devices, 24-hour monitoring, periodic medical checks and examples of medical withdrawal. It is a first-party production account, not independent access to unedited footage, every contestant's reason for leaving, or a clinical assessment. Use one identified season and documented exit records before making frequency claims. Respect participants' privacy and avoid turning a television edit into a psychological diagnosis.

## BANK-186-039 - Breathing study

Balban et al., [*Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/) (Cell Reports Medicine, January 2023), report a remote randomized study of daily five-minute breathing exercises, including box breathing, compared with mindfulness over one month. Outcomes include reported mood and physiological measures. The abstract emphasizes an exhale-focused exercise; it does not test the archived 4-7-8 claim, acute panic-attack termination or survival performance. Review recruitment, attrition, conflicts of interest and full methods before citing effect sizes. This note provides no treatment advice.

## BANK-186-040 - Preparedness attitudes

FEMA's [2024 National Household Survey on Disaster Preparedness findings](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_icpd_2024-national-household-survey-on-disaster-preparedness-findings_05072025.pdf) is the original US survey summary. The PDF was indexed in search but direct retrieval was blocked during this pass, so no unverified table values are quoted. A [FEMA-hosted 2023 survey summary](https://fema-community-files.s3.amazonaws.com/2023-National-Household-Survey.pdf) is a dated comparator; verify its survey wording against 2024 before trend claims. Survey responses can describe perceived risks, actions and stated barriers, not clinical anxiety, private bunker motives or an inherent prepper personality.

## Archive and semantic boundaries

BANK-186-023 and -015 already hold hypothermia behavior and unusual survival accounts, respectively, without assigning a neural cause to named individuals. Bunker-owner mind reading, DMT certainty for near-death experiences and a Stockholm-syndrome diagnosis from hostage anecdotes are documented out of scope. The outdoor-life and camping banks cover physical trip systems; this bank examines interpretation of decisions and evidence, not instructions for fire, shelter, medical treatment or hostage response.
