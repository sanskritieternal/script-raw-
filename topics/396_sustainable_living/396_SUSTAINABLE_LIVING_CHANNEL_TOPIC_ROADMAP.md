## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Sustainable Living across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Sustainable Living Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Sustainable Living.
- **Tier 2: Sustainable Living Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Sustainable Living.
- **Tier 3: Sustainable Living Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Sustainable Living.
- **Tier 4: Sustainable Living Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Sustainable Living.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Sustainable Living: A Household Practice Changes**
   - *Question:* Which dated policy or technology altered a particular home's resource use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare records before and after; avoid a single origin story. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
2. **Sustainable Living: A Low-Impact Home Claim Tested**
   - *Question:* Which measured outcome supports a named household practice?
   - *Key Focus:* State baseline, boundary, period, and comparison. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
3. **Sustainable Living: Labor behind a Green Routine**
   - *Question:* Whose time, money, or public infrastructure makes a practice possible?
   - *Key Focus:* Include household and service-provider accounts. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
4. **Sustainable Living: What Is Being Reduced?**
   - *Question:* Does a change target energy, material use, water, waste, or cost?
   - *Key Focus:* Define metric and full life-cycle boundary. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
5. **Sustainable Living: Tradeoffs across Impacts**
   - *Question:* Does reducing one impact raise another elsewhere in the life cycle?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare quantified alternatives with uncertainty. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Sustainable Living: An Unverified Eco Claim**
   - *Question:* Which data are missing to assess a product or habit?
   - *Key Focus:* Name functional unit, baseline, and measurement method. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
7. **Sustainable Living: A Home Efficiency Device**
   - *Question:* What measured change follows its installation in one setting?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare meter data, weather, occupancy, and costs. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
8. **Sustainable Living: A Household Changes a Habit**
   - *Question:* Which constraint led to a documented sustainable choice?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented interviews and actual bills or logs. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
9. **Sustainable Living: A Local Program Begins**
   - *Question:* What changed after an energy, waste, or water policy took effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Check eligibility, participation, and measured outcomes. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
10. **Sustainable Living: Following a Product's Life**
   - *Question:* Where do extraction, production, use, and disposal impacts arise?
   - *Key Focus:* Define product, functional unit, and geography. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Sustainable Living: A Green Product Reassessed**
   - *Question:* Which evidence challenged an earlier environmental claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated assessments and their boundaries. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
12. **Sustainable Living: One Household over Time**
   - *Question:* Did measured consumption change after an intervention?
   - *Key Focus:* Normalize for household, weather, and service level. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
13. **Sustainable Living: A Conditional Home Upgrade**
   - *Question:* Which assumptions control projected savings or impacts?
   - *Key Focus:* Show scenario range and upfront burden. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
14. **Sustainable Living: Recyclable Means Recycled?**
   - *Question:* What local collection and processing actually happen to a material?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace accepted items and downstream route. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
15. **Sustainable Living: A Home Resource Audit**
   - *Question:* How did measurement change a household's priorities?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow baseline, intervention, and follow-up evidence. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Sustainable Living: Living with a New Routine**
   - *Question:* What time, comfort, or access burdens accompany an impact reduction?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated logs and consented accounts. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
17. **Sustainable Living: Who Enables the Change?**
   - *Question:* Which landlord, utility, council, or provider decision matters?
   - *Key Focus:* Map actual approval and service pathway. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
18. **Sustainable Living: Why Households Adopt It**
   - *Question:* How do price, norms, convenience, and eligibility shape uptake?
   - *Key Focus:* Use bounded evidence, not assumed motives. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
19. **Sustainable Living: Who Cannot Make This Choice?**
   - *Question:* Which capital, tenure, mobility, or infrastructure barrier excludes households?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify affected groups and feasible alternatives. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
20. **Sustainable Living: Different Homes, Different Results**
   - *Question:* How do climate, building, and household size alter performance?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare matched metrics across settings. Evidence base: EPA life-cycle guidance, meter and utility data, product declarations, local service and policy records, itemized costs, and consented household logs.
