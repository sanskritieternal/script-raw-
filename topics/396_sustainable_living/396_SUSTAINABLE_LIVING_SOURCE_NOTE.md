## BANK-396-045 ? San Francisco's 2009 sorting ordinance

- Primary government record: San Francisco Board of Supervisors, Ordinance No. 100-09, *Mandatory Recycling and Composting*, adopted June 2009, https://www.sfenvironment.org/sites/default/files/policy/sfe_zw_sf_mandatory_recycling_composting_ord_100-09.pdf (inspected 2026-10-24). The text requires source separation of recyclables, compostables and landfill trash, property collection subscriptions and implementation/enforcement provisions.
- City implementation context: San Francisco Environment Department policy page, https://www.sfenvironment.org/policy/mandatory-recycling-composting-ordinance ; city outreach sheet, https://www.sfenvironment.org/files/fliers/files/sfe_zw_foodservicewaste_faq.pdf , describes labelled bins, education and assistance for businesses and property managers. The latter is a later outreach document, not the ordinance itself.
- Limit: this is a San Francisco 2009 policy case. Amendments, current local rules, accepted materials and operator practices require dated recheck before practical guidance. Mandatory separation and adequate service do not by themselves establish actual material recovery, carbon savings, equal access or household compliance. Avoid adopting city promotional superlatives.
