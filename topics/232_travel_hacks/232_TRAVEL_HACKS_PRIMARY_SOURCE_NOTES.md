# Selected primary and institutional checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-232-039: booking weekday

- Google Flights, 31 August 2022 operator analysis and footnoted methodology: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/travel/how-to-find-the-best-deal-on-your-next-flight/
- The platform sampled observed round-trip fares, August 2017 to August 2022, for two trip-length bands in its top 4,000 US markets. It reports a small average booking-weekday difference, distinct from departure weekday. This operator-selected historical sample cannot predict one future fare, prove a universal booking day, or rule out personalized pricing.

## BANK-232-040: US 24-hour rule

- US Department of Transportation consumer refund guidance, accessed 24 October 2026: https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds
- For airline tickets purchased at least seven days before departure, DOT says airlines must offer either a 24-hour unpaid hold or a 24-hour cancellation/full-refund option; both are not required. This airline requirement does not apply to online-travel-agency or other third-party bookings and does not itself grant free ticket changes. US-specific, time-sensitive guidance; verify current law and terms before production.
