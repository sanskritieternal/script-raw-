## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Travel Hacks: Where a Travel Tip Came From**
   - *Question:* When and why did one common tactic become popular?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace original terms, dated advice, and later rule changes. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
2. **Travel Hacks: A Viral Travel Hack Tested**
   - *Question:* Does a widely shared tip work for a defined itinerary?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare total cost, restrictions, failure risk, and source date. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
3. **Travel Hacks: The Cost Shift Behind a Tip**
   - *Question:* Who bears work or loss when a traveler saves money?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace staff, provider, local business, and other customers. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
4. **Travel Hacks: Checking a Travel Tip**
   - *Question:* What must a traveler verify before trying a tactic?
   - *Key Focus:* Name jurisdiction, provider, date, contract, accessibility, and backup. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
5. **Travel Hacks: Expected Value of a Booking Tactic**
   - *Question:* When does an apparent saving survive risk and fees?
   - *Key Focus:* Model probability, penalties, time, changes, and traveler preferences. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Travel Hacks: Why a Tip Sometimes Fails**
   - *Question:* Which terms or data gaps explain mixed outcomes?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare provider policy, inventory, dates, and anecdotal selection bias. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
7. **Travel Hacks: A Fare Alert Audited**
   - *Question:* Does an alert find usable savings for a specific trip?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare all-in fares, timing, data coverage, privacy, and false alerts. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
8. **Travel Hacks: The Source of a Travel Tip**
   - *Question:* How did one advisor or company popularize a tactic?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace dated claims, incentives, corrections, and outcomes. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
9. **Travel Hacks: When a Rule Changed a Hack**
   - *Question:* How did one documented policy change affect a tactic?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare rule text and applicable travel dates before and after. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
10. **Travel Hacks: From Tip to Completed Trip**
   - *Question:* What steps must work for a booking tactic to succeed?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace search, purchase, documentation, journey, and remedies. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Travel Hacks: Why a Tip Lost Value**
   - *Question:* What market or rule change made a tactic less useful?
   - *Key Focus:* Check price dispersion, fees, inventory, and provider terms. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
12. **Travel Hacks: One Tip Across Two Booking Eras**
   - *Question:* Does an older saving still exist?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare all-in offers and rules using matched trips and dates. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
13. **Travel Hacks: A Tactic Under New Conditions**
   - *Question:* What changes would make it useful or obsolete?
   - *Key Focus:* State pricing, distribution, regulation, and traveler assumptions. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
14. **Travel Hacks: Always Book on One Day?**
   - *Question:* Is a simple timing rule supported for a defined market?
   - *Key Focus:* Test comparable routes, booking windows, total prices, and variability. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
15. **Travel Hacks: A Tip Tried End to End**
   - *Question:* What happened when a consented traveler used a tactic?
   - *Key Focus:* Keep dated screenshots, terms, costs, and outcome without extrapolation. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Travel Hacks: Shortcut Versus Actual Journey**
   - *Question:* Does a mapped shortcut reduce door-to-door time?
   - *Key Focus:* Include transfers, queues, opening hours, access, and walking. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
17. **Travel Hacks: Cheap Fare, Expensive Connection**
   - *Question:* When is a lower fare a worse complete itinerary?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare total price, delay exposure, protected connections, and needs. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
18. **Travel Hacks: A Tip by Travel Season**
   - *Question:* Does it work during peaks and off-peaks alike?
   - *Key Focus:* Match destination, lead time, inventory, and service schedule. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
19. **Travel Hacks: Local Costs of a Visitor Tip**
   - *Question:* How do residents and workers view a popular shortcut?
   - *Key Focus:* Seek consented accounts and measurable traffic or service effects. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
20. **Travel Hacks: Terrain Limits a Shortcut**
   - *Question:* Which physical barrier defeats an apparent route hack?
   - *Key Focus:* Check grades, water, weather, and actual legal access. Evidence base: dated provider terms and all-in offers, official consumer guidance, matched itinerary comparisons, performance data, and consented traveler accounts.
