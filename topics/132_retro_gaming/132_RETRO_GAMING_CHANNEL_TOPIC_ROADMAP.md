## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Retro Gaming across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: The 8-Bit Revolution, 240p CRT Physics & ROM Hardware Constraints (Beginner / Scratch)** — Atari 2600 TIA register limits, NES Picture Processing Unit (PPU) sprite limits, 240p scanline CRT beam physics, tilemap scrolling tricks, and the 1983 video game crash.
- **Tier 2: 16-Bit Console Wars, Custom Sound Chips & Blast Processing (Intermediate)** — Sega Genesis Motorola 68000 vs SNES Ricoh 5A22, Sony SPC700 audio chip sample compression, Super FX 3D coprocessors (Star Fox), and marketing wars (Blast Processing).
- **Tier 3: The 3D Polygon Leap, CD-ROM Audio & Early 64-Bit Memory Bottlenecks (Upper Intermediate)** — Sony PlayStation CD-ROM audio vs Nintendo 64 cartridge silicon, N64 Reality Coprocessor 4KB texture cache nightmare, analog stick evolution, and memory card saves.
- **Tier 4: FPGA Hardware Emulation, CRT Preservation & Speedrunning Glitch Forensics (Advanced / Masterclass)** — MiSTer FPGA cycle-accurate hardware simulation vs software emulation, CRT phosphor RGB beam calibration, arbitrary code execution (ACE) speedruns, and game ROM archival ethics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The 1983 Video Game Crash: How E.T. and Atari Buried an Industry in the Desert**
   - *Question:* How did a 6-week programming deadline for E.T. and an avalanche of retail shovelware wipe out 97% of the American video game industry?
   - *Key Focus:* Atari 2600 glut of unlicenced games, Howard Scott Warshaw's 5-week crunch, millions of cartridges buried in Alamogordo landfill, and the Japanese rescue by Nintendo.
2. **The NES Picture Processing Unit (PPU): Squeezing Miracles from 2 Kilobytes of RAM**
   - *Question:* How did Shigeru Miyamoto create Super Mario Bros when the console had only 2KB of work RAM and could only draw 8 sprites per scanline?
   - *Key Focus:* Ricoh 2C02 PPU architecture, 8x8 background tile palettes, sprite flickering limits, horizontal nametable scrolling, and cartridge mapper chips (MMC1/MMC3).
3. **The Magic of 240p CRT Scanlines: Why Retro Games Look Terrible on Modern 4K TVs**
   - *Question:* Why do 16-bit pixel art games look like blurry, jagged messes on modern OLED screens while glowing seamlessly on a cathode ray tube?
   - *Key Focus:* Cathode ray electron beam scanning, non-interlaced 240p trick, phosphor persistence blending adjacent pixels, zero input latency, and retro upscalers (Retrotink).
4. **The Console Wars: 'Genesis Does What Nintendon't' and the Blast Processing Myth**
   - *Question:* How did Tom Kalinske's edgy marketing and Sonic the Hedgehog's blazing speed allow Sega to steal 55% of the US market from Nintendo?
   - *Key Focus:* Motorola 68000 7.6MHz CPU vs SNES 3.58MHz, Blast Processing DMA data transfer trick, Sonic speed momentum physics, and aggressive teen marketing.
5. **The SPC700 Sound Chip: How Sony Sneaked Modern Music into the Super Nintendo**
   - *Question:* How did Ken Kutaragi secretly design an 8-channel digital audio synthesizer for Nintendo that outclassed all arcade hardware?
   - *Key Focus:* Bit Rate Reduction (BRR) sample compression, built-in digital echo/reverb DSP, 64KB dedicated sound RAM, and legendary scores (Donkey Kong Country, Chrono Trigger).
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Super FX Chip: How British Hackers Brought 3D Polygons to the 16-Bit SNES**
   - *Question:* How did Argonaut Games solder an auxiliary math RISC coprocessor directly inside a Star Fox cartridge to render 3D space battles?
   - *Key Focus:* Mario Chip / Super FX architecture, frame buffer rendering in cartridge RAM, flat-shaded polygon rasterization, and launching 3D console gaming.
7. **Cartridges vs CD-ROMs: Nintendo's Fatal Mistake and the Birth of the PlayStation**
   - *Question:* Why did Nintendo publicly humiliate Sony at the 1991 CES, causing Ken Kutaragi to build the PlayStation out of pure revenge?
   - *Key Focus:* Nintendo-Sony CD-ROM partnership betrayal, cartridge licensing profit margins, CD-ROM 650MB capacity vs 64MB cartridges, Square jumping ship for Final Fantasy VII.
8. **The N64's Fatal Flaw: The 4 Kilobyte Texture Cache Nightmare**
   - *Question:* Why did Nintendo 64 games have famously muddy, blurry textures compared to the crisper (yet wobbly) PlayStation games?
   - *Key Focus:* Silicon Graphics Reality Coprocessor (RDP), tiny 4KB TMEM texture memory limit, aggressive bilinear filtering, and microcode programming hurdles.
9. **PlayStation's Wobbly Polygons: The Lack of Subpixel Precision in PS1 Hardware**
   - *Question:* Why did 3D walls and character faces warp, bend, and swim across the screen when you walked in PS1 games like Silent Hill?
   - *Key Focus:* Lack of floating-point arithmetic (fixed-point integer math), missing subpixel coordinate precision, affine texture mapping vs perspective correction.
10. **Arbitrary Code Execution (ACE): How Speedrunners Reprogram Games with Controller Inputs**
   - *Question:* How can a player beat Super Mario World in 41 seconds or code Flappy Bird into Pokemon Yellow using only gamepad button presses?
   - *Key Focus:* Memory overflow exploits, writing payload instructions into RAM via player coordinates and inventory slots, jumping the program counter (PC), and Tool-Assisted Speedruns (TAS).
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Game Boy's Immortal Battery: Gunpei Yokoi and Lateral Thinking with Withered Technology**
   - *Question:* Why did an ugly monochrome olive-green screen completely destroy the technologically superior color backlit Atari Lynx and Sega Game Gear?
   - *Key Focus:* 30-hour battery life on 4 AA batteries, cheap reflective STN LCD, durable drop-resistant chassis, Tetris bundling genius, and Yokoi's design philosophy.
12. **Arcade Hardware and the Neo Geo: The $650 Super-Console for Millionaire Kids**
   - *Question:* How did SNK put exact 100% arcade cabinet hardware into a home console with 330-Megabit cartridges costing $300 each?
   - *Key Focus:* MVS arcade board vs AES home console parity, dual Motorola 68000 and Z80 CPUs, massive sprite multiplexing (380 sprites), and the cult luxury gaming status.
13. **Software Emulation vs FPGA Hardware Clones: The MiSTer Revolution**
   - *Question:* Why are purists abandoning PC emulator software to simulate vintage microchips on Field-Programmable Gate Arrays?
   - *Key Focus:* CPU instruction cycle inaccuracies, operating system input lag, FPGA logic-gate hardware reconfiguration (DE10-Nano), and sub-millisecond controller response.
14. **The Sega Dreamcast: The Tragic Masterpiece Ahead of Its Time**
   - *Question:* How did a console with a built-in 56k modem, online multiplayer, and groundbreaking 128-bit graphics kill Sega's hardware business in 2 years?
   - *Key Focus:* The 9/9/99 launch triumph, VMU memory card screens, lack of DVD playback, Sony's devastating PS2 hype machine, and Sega's final exit from hardware.
15. **Save Batteries and Flash Memory: How Zelda Saved Your Game Progress Forever**
   - *Question:* How did a tiny CR2032 lithium coin battery keeping static RAM powered inside an NES cartridge end the nightmare of 24-character passwords?
   - *Key Focus:* The Legend of Zelda gold cartridge, battery-backed SRAM circuits, memory degradation over 30 years, EEPROM saves, and modern FRAM battery-less mods.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Capcom's CP System (CPS-1 & CPS-2): The Hardware That Built Street Fighter II**
   - *Question:* How did Capcom's custom arcade boards deliver fluid 60 FPS fighting animations and booming QSound audio that dominated 90s arcades?
   - *Key Focus:* QSound spatial stereo processing, hardware sprite scaling, the CPS-2 suicide battery encryption chip anti-piracy trap, and the fighting game arcade boom.
17. **Sony's BVM and PVM Monitors: Why Retro Gamers Pay Thousands for Medical CRT Displays**
   - *Question:* Why did discarded broadcast studio and hospital surgical monitors become the most coveted displays in the retro gaming hobby?
   - *Key Focus:* Trinitron aperture grille technology, 800+ TV Line (TVL) resolution, pure RGB analog video signals (SCART/BNC), and factory calibration precision.
18. **The Disappearing History of Video Games: ROM Archival, Bit Rot, and Legal Warfare**
   - *Question:* Why are 87% of classic games out of print and legally unplayable, while Nintendo sues ROM preservation archives into oblivion?
   - *Key Focus:* Silicon bit rot and disc rot degradation, DMCA anti-circumvention exemptions, Video Game History Foundation findings, and museum preservation rights.
19. **The Minus World and Glitch Ecology: The Accidental Secrets Hidden in 8-Bit Code**
   - *Question:* How did jumping through a brick wall at the end of World 1-2 transport players into an endless underwater prison labeled World -1?
   - *Key Focus:* Uninitialized memory registers, tile index reading errors, MissingNo in Pokemon Red/Blue, kill screens in Pac-Man (split-screen level 256), and digital folklore.
20. **The Master Retro Hardware Architecture: The Complete Restoration & Preservation Blueprint**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive technical framework covers electrolytic capacitor replacement, RGB video modding, optical drive emulators (ODEs), and CRT maintenance?
   - *Key Focus:* Capacitor leaking remediation, recapping motherboards, custom clean digital video output mods (DCDigital/N64RGB), ODE flash solutions, and tube degaussing.
