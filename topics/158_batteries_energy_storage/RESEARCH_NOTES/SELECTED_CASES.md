# Selected battery cases - source check (2026-10-24)

These notes delimit proposed questions. They do not verify every claim in an archived pitch or approve an IDEA handoff.

## BANK-158-043 - CATL Shenxing PLUS

- CATL's [25 April 2024 announcement](https://www.catl.com/en/news/6239.html) says its LFP Shenxing PLUS system reaches 205 Wh/kg, above 1,000 km range and 600 km of added range in ten minutes. This is the manufacturer's product-launch description, with performance stated for its system and unspecified vehicle/test conditions in the page reviewed.
- **Claim limit:** The archived “1 km per second” framing is CATL's range-per-charge-time arithmetic. It is not an independent road test, a charging-rate guarantee, or evidence that every Shenxing PLUS vehicle achieves it. Identify the test cycle, vehicle, pack configuration, temperature and charger before a numerical script claim.
- **Overlap:** BANK-158-012 covers LFP tradeoffs, BANK-158-017 fast-charge taper, and BANK-158-029 pack architecture. This case is the named April 2024 product claim and should not be retitled as another general LFP explainer.

## BANK-158-044 - Toyota/Idemitsu all-solid-state target

- Toyota and Idemitsu's [12 October 2023 joint announcement](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/corporate/39865919.html) describes a 2027-28 commercialization/market-launch target for BEVs using sulfide solid electrolytes, pilot-facility steps, and subsequent study of full-scale mass production. Toyota's [2025 integrated report](https://global.toyota/pages/global_toyota/ir/library/annual/2025_001_integrated_en.pdf) still frames 2027-28 as targeted commercialization.
- **Claim limit:** These sources document plans and development claims, not completed mass-market deliveries, doubled range, or zero fire risk. Recheck dated launch, independent safety testing and production volume before an episode.
- **Overlap:** BANK-158-020 addresses general solid-electrolyte engineering; this case examines a specific company's dated milestone and evidence threshold.

## Other archived pitch cautions

- Moss Landing has had separate operators, facilities and incidents. The preserved “Megapack fire” pitch is routed to BANK-158-021 and BANK-158-041 for site and battery-safety scope, while the particular operator, event date, failure cause and redesign await official incident records. Do not infer that one company's pack caused another facility's fire.
- The sodium-ion vehicle price, Tesla 4680 dry-electrode yield, DLE water benefit and annual home-arbitrage income are preserved as pitches, not certified facts. Product, region, tariff and incident-specific records are required.
- **Neighbor review:** BANK-113-022 owns grid reliability and duration adequacy at the energy-system level. This bank owns cell, pack and storage-technology behavior; do not clone that grid-service question into a new battery case.
