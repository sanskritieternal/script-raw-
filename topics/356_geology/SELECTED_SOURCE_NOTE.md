## 2019 Ridgecrest surface rupture ? BANK-356-043
USGS authors documented field observations after the 2019-07-04/05 Ridgecrest earthquake sequence, supplemented by optical and geodetic imagery. The 2020 paper reports thousands of ground observations and more than 68 km of mapped fault rupture. Another USGS study found pre-event landscape evidence of previous faulting even along parts not previously mapped as faults. This supports a retrospective field-to-map question, not a prediction or a claim that every mapped line ruptured.

Primary agency research/data: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/documentation-surface-fault-rupture-and-ground-deformation-features-produced-4-and-5 ; https://www.usgs.gov/data/surface-displacement-observations-2019-ridgecrest-california-earthquake-sequence ; https://www.usgs.gov/publications/evidence-previous-faulting-along-2019-ridgecrest-california-earthquake-ruptures . Date, map scale, displacement measurement and completeness matter; no hazard or travel advice follows from these historical records.

## Grand Canyon geologic map ? BANK-356-044
USGS Geologic Investigations Series I-2688 is a mapped Grand Canyon quadrangle with accompanying stratigraphic and structural pamphlet. It supports a case on how a contact between mapped rock units can indicate missing strata and deformation history. The map?s scale and revisions bound local interpretation; do not infer an exact duration of missing time from a line alone.

Primary map and source pamphlet: https://pubs.usgs.gov/imap/i-2688/ . A separate USGS map pamphlet discusses the Great Unconformity and unit context: https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3227/sim3227_pamphlet.pdf . Keep publication/area differences explicit when comparing them.
