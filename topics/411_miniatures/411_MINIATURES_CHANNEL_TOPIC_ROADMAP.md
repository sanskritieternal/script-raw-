## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Miniatures across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Miniatures Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Miniatures.
- **Tier 2: Miniatures Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Miniatures.
- **Tier 3: Miniatures Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Miniatures.
- **Tier 4: Miniatures Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Miniatures.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Miniatures: A Miniature Tradition in Context**
   - *Question:* Which dated object documents a particular miniature-making practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Identify maker, function, scale, and collection history. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
2. **Miniatures: A Miniature Room Examined**
   - *Question:* Which details create a believable sense of size and use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare object dimensions, source references, and viewing position. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
3. **Miniatures: Makers of Tiny Objects**
   - *Question:* Whose carving, sewing, painting, and collecting shaped a miniature set?
   - *Key Focus:* Check maker notes and provenance. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
4. **Miniatures: Scale in a Small Object**
   - *Question:* How does a chosen ratio govern furniture and figure dimensions?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one measured model and explicit units. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
5. **Miniatures: Selective Detail at Scale**
   - *Question:* Which features must be exaggerated or omitted for viewers to read them?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare physical size, viewing distance, and goal. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Miniatures: An Unattributed Miniature**
   - *Question:* Which marks, materials, or records support a maker claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate dating from attribution. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
7. **Miniatures: New Tools for Tiny Parts**
   - *Question:* Which task changed with laser cutting or 3D printing?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare actual tolerances, finishing, and maker labor. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
8. **Miniatures: A Miniaturist's Revision**
   - *Question:* Why did a maker alter a tiny object or room?
   - *Key Focus:* Use sketches, scrapbooks, and attributed accounts. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
9. **Miniatures: A Miniature Collection Opens**
   - *Question:* How did display alter interpretation of objects made for private use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare donor intent, labels, and visitor access. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
10. **Miniatures: Building a Miniature Scene**
   - *Question:* How do research, scale, fabrication, assembly, and lighting connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one documented build. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Miniatures: A Miniature Style Changes**
   - *Question:* Which materials or audience expectations altered a named form?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated objects and catalogs. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
12. **Miniatures: One Miniature after Conservation**
   - *Question:* What is original, altered, or replaced?
   - *Key Focus:* Use condition and treatment records. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
13. **Miniatures: A Planned Miniature Exhibit**
   - *Question:* Which research and conservation questions remain?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat design proposal as conditional. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
14. **Miniatures: Are Exact Copies?**
   - *Question:* Which details are symbolic or deliberately adjusted?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare source and measured object. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
15. **Miniatures: A Dollhouse's Making**
   - *Question:* How did one miniature world grow through additions and revisions?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated inventory and maker notes. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Miniatures: The Scene's Story**
   - *Question:* What place, era, or invented household did the maker aim to show?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare brief or scrapbook with display. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
17. **Miniatures: Tiny Materials under Stress**
   - *Question:* Which paper, wood, fabric, or adhesive choices affect detail and survival?
   - *Key Focus:* Use object examination and conservation records. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
18. **Miniatures: A Tiny Prototype**
   - *Question:* Which fit or visibility test changed a component?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare sketches, test piece, and final. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
19. **Miniatures: A View into the Room**
   - *Question:* How do front opening, light, and sightline guide attention?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure viewer position and scene layout. Evidence base: measured objects and plans, maker scrapbooks, dated catalogs, collection provenance, exhibition records, visitor evidence, and conservation reports.
