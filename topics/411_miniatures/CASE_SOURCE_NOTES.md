# Selected primary-source note — miniatures

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-411-045`.

- [Royal Collection Trust, *Queen Mary's Dolls' House*, RCIN 231999](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/queen-marys-dolls-house): collection holder identifies the 1921–24 construction, Sir Edwin Lutyens, Princess Marie Louise’s committee, one-twelfth scale, and working electricity, water and lifts. It describes broad categories of contributors and the 1924 Wembley display, with move to Windsor in 1925.
- [Royal Collection Trust, Lutyens letter from the Dolls' House archive](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/royal-writing-throughout-the-ages/dolls-house-letter-from-sir-edwin-lutyens): institutional record identifies an archival letter and Princess Marie Louise’s organizing role.

These are holder-curated descriptions and one selected archival item, not a complete design archive or task ledger for all contributors. “Working” miniature systems should not be equated with safe full-size systems. The collection page’s “largest and most famous” wording is not independently tested here; no record-superlative claim is adopted. Object photographs carry Royal Collection copyright and need rights clearance. No electrical or water-system build advice is implied.
