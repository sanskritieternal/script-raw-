# Selected primary-source note — solar technology

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-438-043`.

- [NIST, Matthew Boyd, *Performance Data from the NIST Photovoltaic Arrays and Weather Station* (2017)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/122/jres.122.040.pdf): original research data report describes three campus PV arrays completed in 2012, onsite meteorological instruments and extended high-resolution performance datasets.
- [NIST, *High-Speed Monitoring of Multiple Grid-Connected Photovoltaic Array Configurations and Supplementary Weather Station* (21 March 2017)](https://www.nist.gov/publications/high-speed-monitoring-multiple-grid-connected-photovoltaic-array-configurations-and): institutional publication record identifies three instrumented arrays of 73–271 kW with different tilts, orientations and configurations; electrical, irradiance, weather and shading-image measurements support model validation.

These are research installations and measurement descriptions, not a comparison that isolates every design variable or a consumer production guarantee. The data interval and instrument uncertainty must accompany any numerical comparison; nameplate rating alone is not field energy. PV equipment and grid connections pose electrical hazards, so this note is not installation or troubleshooting guidance. Data and imagery may have separate terms of reuse.
