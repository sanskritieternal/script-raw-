# Selected marine-mammal case checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. Original trending wording is preserved in the source snapshot; these are narrower research questions.

## BANK-197-039 — Iberian orca interactions

[Recorded interactions](https://www.orcaiberica.org/en/copia-de-interacciones-registradas-1) can identify dates and sites but require individual-level observational records before drawing a social-learning inference. [Spanish Maritime Rescue's May 2024 notice](https://www.salvamentomaritimo.es/sala-de-comunicacion/sala-de-prensa/transportes-actualiza-las-recomendaciones-a-navegantes-en-caso-de-encuentros-con-orcas) documents navigational advice, not animal motive. Neither source establishes revenge, an organized attack motive or a single named leader. BANK-140-045 owns vessel incidents and navigation response; existing pod-culture BANK-197-021 has a broader biological remit. This proposed case remains source-pending for behavioral inference.

## BANK-197-040 — 52-hertz call

The [Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution account](https://www.whoi.edu/oceanus/feature/a-lone-voice-crying-in-the-watery-wilderness/) traces the unusual hydrophone detections. A frequency does not establish that other whales cannot hear it or that the animal is lonely. Existing song and acoustic topics are broader; this proposed case owns the recorded signal and how the myth grew.

## BANK-197-041 — sperm-whale codas

The [2024 Nature Communications study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47221-8) analyzes contextual and combinatorial patterns in recorded codas. The finding concerns call structure, not a semantic translation or proof of conversations. Keep sample and inference separate from the archived 'AI cracked the code' premise.

## BANK-197-042 — vaquita survey

The [official 2025 CONANP visual/acoustic survey report](https://www.conanp.gob.mx/vaquita_marina/crucerovaquita2025/ReporteCruceroObservacionVaquita2025.pdf) gives an expert-elicited probability for **distinct animals observed within the study area**, with detection limits. Its 7–10 range is not an exact worldwide population count or a trend from prior surveys. Use the report's window and observation method whenever quoting it.

## BANK-197-044 — walrus tusks

[NOAA's walrus overview](https://oceantoday.noaa.gov/animalsoftheice_walruses/) describes tusks in ice access and social/defensive contexts. The archived sled-runner phrasing is metaphorical. Further field evidence is needed to assign prevalence to each function.
