## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Career & Jobs across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Resume Algorithmic Parsing, Interview Psychology & Negotiation (Scratch / Beginner)** — Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) keyword parsing, the STAR interview behavioral framework, anchoring bias in salary negotiation, and LinkedIn algorithmic search optimization.
- **Tier 2: The Peter Principle, Corporate Politics & The 80/20 Leverage Rule (Intermediate)** — Laurence Peter's rising to level of incompetence rule, corporate organizational silos, Pareto principle 80/20 productivity leverage, and managing up effectively without burning bridges.
- **Tier 3: Remote Work Dynamics, Burnout Neuroscience & High-Income Skill Stacking (Upper Intermediate)** — Asynchronous communication documentation culture, dopamine chronic workplace burnout stages, Scott Adams' skill-stacking talent formula, and golden handcuffs equity vesting cliff strategies.
- **Tier 4: AI Job Displacement, Fractional Executive Careers & The Future of Work (Advanced / Expert)** — Tasks vs jobs displacement models in white-collar automation, the fractional C-suite executive economy, sovereign solopreneur workflows, and post-degree meritocratic skill validation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Resume Parsing, Interviews & Negotiation
1. **The Peter Principle: Why Everyone Gets Promoted to Their Level of Incompetence**
   - *Question:* Why does a brilliant software engineer get promoted into a terrible manager, ensuring that every role in a corporate hierarchy eventually fills with someone who can't do the job?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. Laurence J. Peter's 1969 satire turning into management science, promoting for current success rather than future skill fit, the dual career ladder solution (individual contributor vs manager).
2. **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS): Why 75% of Resumes Are Rejected by Robots Before a Human Sees Them**
   - *Question:* How do corporate hiring portals like Taleo, Greenhouse, and Workday score and discard PDF resumes using keyword density algorithms, and how do you format a resume to pass?
   - *Key Focus:* Optical character parsing failures with multi-column fancy templates, hard vs soft skill keyword matching, reverse-chronological standard formatting, beating the robotic recruiter filter.
3. **The STAR Method: How to Answer Any Behavioral Interview Question Without Rambling**
   - *Question:* When an interviewer says 'Tell me about a time you handled a difficult conflict', how does structuring your answer into Situation, Task, Action, and Result guarantee an offer?
   - *Key Focus:* Situation context (10%), Task responsibility (10%), Action personal leadership (60%), Result quantifiable business metrics (20%), eliminating rambling narrative traps.
4. **Salary Negotiation Psychology: Why Whoever Mentions a Number First Loses (Anchoring Bias)**
   - *Question:* How does Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman's cognitive anchoring bias dictate whether you leave $20,000 on the table, and how do you deflect early salary expectation questions?
   - *Key Focus:* Anchoring effect on compensation bands, the psychological power of silence, negotiating total compensation (equity, bonuses, remote flexibility) beyond base salary, tactical scripts.
5. **LinkedIn Algorithmic Optimization: How to Turn Your Profile into an Inbound Recruiter Magnet**
   - *Question:* How does LinkedIn Recruiter's backend search engine index job titles, skill badges, and active engagement to rank candidates on recruiter search screens?
   - *Key Focus:* SEO-optimized headline formulas (Role + Impact + Target Keyword), the 'Open to Work' recruiter toggle signal, skills endorsement indexing, creator mode engagement multipliers.
### Tier 2: Corporate Politics & The 80/20 Leverage Rule
6. **Managing Up: How to Handle a Bad Boss Without Ruining Your Reputation**
   - *Question:* How do you align with a micromanager, an absent boss, or a credit-stealer by understanding their incentives, communication preferences, and corporate pressures?
   - *Key Focus:* Understanding your boss's KPIs and stressors, asynchronous status documentation preventing micromanagement, paper trails protecting against scapegoating, emotional intelligence at work.
7. **The 80/20 Rule at Work: How Doing Less Work Makes You Look More Productive**
   - *Question:* Why do 20% of your daily workplace tasks generate 80% of your recognition and promotions, while the other 80% is low-value busywork you should aggressively delegate or eliminate?
   - *Key Focus:* Vilfredo Pareto's law applied to corporate productivity, high-leverage vs low-leverage activities, saying 'no' to non-promotable office housework, strategic visibility over grinding hours.
8. **Parkinson's Law: Why Work Expands to Fill the Time Allotted for Its Completion**
   - *Question:* Why does a task given a 2-week deadline take 2 weeks of stress, while the exact same task given a 2-hour deadline gets completed in 2 hours with identical quality?
   - *Key Focus:* C. Northcote Parkinson's 1955 British Admiralty bureaucracy study, artificial constraint engineering, time-boxing techniques (Pomodoro), corporate meetings expanding to fill calendar slots.
### Tier 3: Remote Work, Burnout & Skill Stacking
9. **The Skill Stack: Scott Adams' Formula for Becoming the Top 1% Without Being a Genius**
   - *Question:* Why is it almost impossible to become the #1 best programmer or salesperson in the world, but extraordinarily easy to become the top 5% at three overlapping complementary skills?
   - *Key Focus:* The Dilbert creator's talent stack concept, combining mediocre drawing + corporate humor + business writing, combining engineering with public speaking and design, building a unique career moat.
10. **Workplace Burnout Neuroscience: What Chronic Cortisol Does to the Prefrontal Cortex**
   - *Question:* Why does prolonged workplace stress literally shrink the brain's prefrontal cortex, enlarge the fear-driven amygdala, and turn high performers into paralyzed, cynical husks?
   - *Key Focus:* Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal (HPA) axis dysregulation, chronic cortisol neurotoxicity, Herbert Freudenberger's 12 stages of burnout, setting psychological detachment boundaries.
11. **Remote Work and Asynchronous Culture: Why Real-Time Slack Messages Kill Deep Work**
   - *Question:* How do fully remote companies like GitLab and Basecamp operate across 60 time zones with zero meetings by treating long-form writing as their primary workplace culture?
   - *Key Focus:* Synchronous interruptions vs asynchronous deep work (Cal Newport), public documentation handbooks, eliminating instant-response Slack anxiety, remote performance evaluation metrics.
12. **Golden Handcuffs and the Vesting Cliff: How Silicon Valley Keeps Engineers Trapped**
   - *Question:* How do 4-year Restricted Stock Unit (RSU) vesting schedules with 1-year cliffs and refresher grants make it psychologically impossible for miserable engineers to leave tech giants?
   - *Key Focus:* RSUs vs ISO stock options, the 1-year cliff trap, refresher grant overlapping schedules, lifestyle inflation matching stock compensation, calculating your real 'walk-away number'.
13. **The Impostor Phenomenon: Why the Highest Achievers Suffer the Deepest Self-Doubt**
   - *Question:* Why do 70% of successful professionals secretly live in terror that someone will find out they don't know what they're doing, and why is it the inverse of the Dunning-Kruger effect?
   - *Key Focus:* Pauline Clance and Suzanne Imes 1978 origin, pluralistic ignorance (everyone looks calm on the outside), externalizing success to luck while internalizing failure, reframing self-doubt as growth.
### Tier 4: AI Displacement, Fractional Executives & The Future
14. **AI and White-Collar Job Displacement: Tasks vs Roles in the Automation Wave**
   - *Question:* Will generative AI eliminate entire job titles like paralegals, copywriters, and analysts, or will it unbundle repetitive sub-tasks while 10x-ing the output of senior practitioners?
   - *Key Focus:* David Autor's automation economic models, the 'Lump of Labor' fallacy vs Jevons Paradox (cheaper services exploding demand), prompt engineering as a transient skill vs deep domain mastery.
15. **The Fractional Executive: Why Top Directors Are Ditching 9-to-5 for 5 Clients at Once**
   - *Question:* How are seasoned CMOs, CTOs, and CFOs earning $300,000+ a year working 10 hours a week for three different startups without corporate HR politics or 60-hour burnout?
   - *Key Focus:* The economics of fractional leadership for early-stage companies, retainer contracts vs hourly billing, managing multiple executive relationships, building personal brand authority.
16. **Quiet Quitting and the Great Resignation: The Generational Collapse of Corporate Loyalty**
   - *Question:* Why did millions of workers in their 20s and 30s refuse to go the extra mile, strictly working their contract hours after realizing loyalty rarely pays off in layoffs?
   - *Key Focus:* Historical 'Work-to-Rule' labor union roots, post-pandemic existential perspective shifts, the death of corporate pensions and the rise of at-will corporate layoffs, boundary defense.
17. **The Sovereign Solopreneur: How One-Person Companies Leverage AI to Rival 10-Person Agencies**
   - *Question:* When Sam Altman predicted the first one-person billion-dollar company, how are solo founders using AI code generators, automated marketing, and Stripe to run lean empires?
   - *Key Focus:* Permissionless leverage (code and media vs capital and labor: Naval Ravikant), micro-SaaS businesses, automated customer support pipelines, sovereign career freedom.
18. **How to Pivot Careers in Your 30s, 40s, or 50s: The Bridge Skill Framework**
   - *Question:* How do you transition from an unfulfilling career into tech, design, or consulting without starting at the bottom as an intern or taking a 70% pay cut?
   - *Key Focus:* Herminia Ibarra's 'Working Identity' research, identifying transferable 'bridge skills' (project management, data storytelling), side-project validation before quitting, informational interviewing.
19. **The Death of the 5-Day Work Week: The 4-Day Work Week Global Trials**
   - *Question:* What happened when 61 companies in the UK gave employees 100% pay for 80% time on the 100-80-100 model, and 92% of the companies refused to ever go back to 5 days?
   - *Key Focus:* The 100:80:100 rule (100% pay for 80% time, maintaining 100% productivity), cutting unnecessary meetings, sick day reductions by 65%, employee retention and revenue gains.
20. **Work in the Year 2040: Meritocratic Proofs, DAO Guilds, and Post-Job Identity**
   - *Question:* When AI handles administrative and analytical execution, how will humans define professional dignity, earn livelihoods, and find purpose in post-job societies?
   - *Key Focus:* Decentralized talent guilds, algorithmic reputation scores replacing degrees, human-centric emotional and philosophical care work, separating personal self-worth from labor productivity.
