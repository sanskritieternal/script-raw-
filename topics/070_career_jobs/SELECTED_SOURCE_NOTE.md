# 070 selected source inspection — 2026-10-24

These are bounded research leads for proposed career episodes, not individual legal, financial or medical advice. Name the country, state, occupation, contract and data vintage before making a decision claim.

## Promotion and the Peter Principle — BANK-070-052

[Benson, Li and Shue's original study](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24343) analyzes sales workers at 214 firms and finds patterns consistent with promoting strong individual contributors over workers with better observable managerial signals. It does not establish that *everyone* is promoted to incompetence or that a particular boss is incompetent. Separate one occupation's measured promotion incentives from a universal personality judgment.

## Applicant tracking systems — BANK-070-022

The [U.S. EEOC's worker guidance on automated employment tools](https://www.eeoc.gov/sites/default/files/2024-04/20240429_Employment%20Discrimination%20and%20AI%20for%20Workers.pdf) describes varied resume-screening uses. It does not support the archive's “75% rejected by robots” or “PDF instantly rejected” rates. ATS behavior depends on employer configuration, job requirements, parser and file guidance. Test an actual application route rather than invent a universal format trick.

## Salary history and negotiation — BANK-070-053

The [California labor agency's Equal Pay Act guidance](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/california_equal_pay_act.htm?refPageViewId=4fe2a096654537fc) says covered California employers generally may not seek applicant salary history, while they may ask salary expectations; voluntary disclosure has distinct treatment. That rule is jurisdiction-specific and dated. No evidence here establishes that mentioning a number first always loses. An episode should compare a named location, role range, offer terms and applicable rule before advising what to disclose.

## Four-day-week trial — BANK-070-056

The [University of Cambridge's researchers' report on the UK pilot](https://www.sociology.cam.ac.uk/news/new-results-worlds-largest-trial-four-day-working-week) identifies a June–December 2022 trial involving 61 organizations and reports continuation intentions and outcome data for a subset. This is a voluntary pilot in participating organizations, not proof that all industries can move to four days or that the five-day week has ended. Preserve denominator, recruitment and outcome measurement.

## Occupation forecasts — BANK-070-038 and BANK-070-040

The [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' employment-projection data overview](https://www.bls.gov/emp/documentation/data-overview.htm) identifies its current projection vintage and cautions that forecasts for individual occupations are uncertain and should not determine a career alone. The archive's AI displacement and 2040 claims need task, place and timeframe, and should distinguish changing duties from eliminated positions. Recheck the page's vintage at production time.
