# BANK-018-054 — A Gilgamesh version for animation

**Proposal:** How can one Gilgamesh version be animated without claiming it was the world's first written quest?  
**State:** bounded source-access review, 2026-10-24. The BANK topic remains a proposal; no edition, translation or script has been approved.

## Editorial finding

The archived pitch calls Gilgamesh “the world's first written quest for immortality.” Surviving witnesses show a changing textual tradition, not one recoverable original screen story. “First” would require a definition of quest, writing, survival and comparison with other texts; the inspected records do not support that rank. The later epic's mortality question is a viable bounded premise, with the chosen version and tablet gaps named.

For a script, select a translated edition and a short passage, inspect its tablet basis and editorial restorations, then storyboard only what that source supports. The [Mesopotamian civilizations bank](../../545_mesopotamian_civilizations/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md) is a neighboring historical home; this BANK question concerns what an animation can responsibly show from one chosen witness. No narration, visual design or source-use right is approved here.
