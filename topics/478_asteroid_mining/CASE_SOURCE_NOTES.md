# Case-source note: Hayabusa2 Ryugu sample

Checked 2026-10-24. JAXA ISAS’s [2021 initial Ryugu sample description](https://www.isas.jaxa.jp/en/topics/002893.html) reports two Hayabusa2 touchdown sites in 2019 and 5.4 g of material returned in December 2020; initial optical and near-infrared analyses found water-bearing and organic-related signatures. JAXA’s [Ryugu curation record](https://curation.isas.jaxa.jp/en/sample-curation/ryugu/) describes sample handling, measured material and research allocation. These are mission and custody records relevant to prospecting evidence.

Limit: A few grams from two sites are not a quantified asteroid-wide ore reserve. Water-related mineral signatures are not freely extractable water, and scientific sample return is not industrial excavation, processing, storage, transport, sale or profitability. JAXA’s scientific aim does not establish a mining plan. Check later laboratory publications for any specific compositional claim.
