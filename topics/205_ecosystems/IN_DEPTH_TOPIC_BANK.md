**Individual coverage scope:** The cradle of Chinese civilization ruined by centuries of overgrazing, banning goats on hillsides, building terraced check-dams, restoring perennial vegetation, lifting 2.5 million people out of poverty, and hope for global drylands.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.
