# Selected ecosystem case checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. A project launch, a site measurement and a landscape outcome are different claims.

## BANK-205-038 — Pleistocene Park

The [field and model study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7078274/) compares grazing and nearby nongrazing conditions and models snow and soil-temperature effects. Another [field study](https://bg.copernicus.org/articles/19/1611/2022/index.html) reports warmer topsoil and greater thaw depths at some grazed sites while noting carbon-cycle changes. Designs, season, depth and site differ; neither is proof that introduced herbivores stop Siberian permafrost thaw. Existing BANK-205-026 covers rewilding generally; this case owns the named permafrost experiment.

## BANK-205-039 — beaver-dam analogs

The [USGS-indexed Price River trial](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70254900) found that dam analog installation did not improve apparent survival or site fidelity of translocated beavers in its desert-river study. Its endpoint is not the full set of potential stream-restoration effects. The [USGS New Mexico monitoring project](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/fs20263019/full) describes sensors and an ongoing assessment; it is not a completed benefit result. Existing BANK-205-016 concerns beaver ecosystem engineering, not an installed analog's evaluated outcome.

## BANK-205-040 — Colorado wolves

The [Colorado Department of Natural Resources performance plan](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/DNR_Performance%20Plan_FY25-26.pdf) documents the state's dated release program and monitoring. Releases and reproduction are not evidence of changed rivers or vegetation. Separate wildlife and livestock-conflict indicators, and distinguish Colorado from the existing broad predator-cascade question BANK-205-010.

## BANK-205-041 — Chernobyl wildlife

An [original forest-bird survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2394539/) found lower bird abundance along its measured radiation gradient. This site/taxon association does not describe every species throughout the exclusion zone. An [original mammal re-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7442794/) illustrates the continuing methodological debate. Reduced human land use and radiation exposure must not be collapsed into the archived 'wildlife Eden' slogan.

## BANK-205-043 — ghost forests

The [USGS coastal-transition project](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/ecosystems-land-change-science-program/science/impacts-coastal-and-watershed-changes-upper?field_pub_type_target_id=All&field_release_date_value=&field_science_type_target_id=All&items_per_page=6&page=2&qt-science_center_objects=2&tltagv_gid=133) identifies salinity-driven forest transitions while listing sea level and human watershed changes as interacting influences. A [USGS field account](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/ecosystems-land-change-science-program/news/a-temporary-boost-surviving-trees-tidal) addresses named Chesapeake rivers. The archived Atlantic-wide single-cause claim remains unverified. Tree physiology belongs to niche 202; this question owns wetland ecosystem transition.
