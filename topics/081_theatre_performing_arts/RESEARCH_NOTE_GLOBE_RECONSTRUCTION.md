# Shakespeare's Globe reconstruction: bounded venue note

Linked proposal: [BANK-081-052](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-081-052). Research checked 2026-10-24. This note concerns the current Globe Theatre as a reconstruction and research setting, not an exact recording of the 1599 playhouse.

## Holding institution's record

[Shakespeare's Globe's venue history](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/discover/about-us/globe-theatre/) distinguishes its company, the historical Globes and the present Globe Theatre. It dates the current venue's official opening to 1997 and describes the open-air yard, galleried seating and stage relationship. Its [playhouse overview](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/discover/shakespeares-world/playhouses/) summarizes evidence for early modern outdoor playhouses, including a projecting stage and tiring house. The institution's [Third Globe account](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/discover/shakespeares-world/the-third-globe/) notes changes required by modern fire and audience-safety rules. The [performance and institutional archive description](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/learn/research-and-collections/archive-collections/) identifies records that can support a production-level inquiry.

## Research boundaries

1. Choose one dated contemporary production and record its stage configuration, cast, script edition and audience position. Compare those choices with evidence for the historical playhouse instead of presenting a modern performance as an unmediated Elizabethan event.
2. Distinguish documented architecture from acoustic inference. The modern building and audience can demonstrate possibilities; they cannot reproduce the exact soundscape, crowd behavior or acting conventions of 1599.
3. Document reconstruction differences such as exits, stewardship and audience capacity. These are safety and access features, not errors to erase in a period reenactment.
4. Check permission before reproducing archive photographs, production video, scripts or substantial performance excerpts. An accessible catalogue entry is not a redistribution license.

## Limits

The Globe's own pages support venue identity, design and archive leads. Specific claims about an early modern staging practice still need dated primary evidence and scholarship; specific production claims need that production's programme and records. No technical stage effect or performer-safety procedure is supplied here. The BANK case remains proposed and source-unverified beyond this bounded note.
