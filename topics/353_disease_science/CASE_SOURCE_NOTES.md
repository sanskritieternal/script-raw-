# Selected public-health research source note — disease science

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-353-041`. Historical US EVALI outbreak evidence only; this note gives no individual diagnosis or treatment advice.

- [CDC MMWR, *Evaluation of Bronchoalveolar Lavage Fluid...*](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6845e2.htm) (November 15, 2019): CDC reported vitamin E acetate in all 29 EVALI case-associated lung-fluid specimens in an initial convenience sample across 10 states. Its authors explicitly said additional comparisons and controlled work were needed for causality and that other toxicants had not been ruled out.
- [CDC archived outbreak investigation summary](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/tobacco/basic_information/e-cigarettes/severe-lung-disease.html) (later 2019–20 update): reported vitamin E acetate in 48 of 51 patient samples and not in a healthy comparison group, alongside surveillance peak and subsequent decline. The later summary reflects more evidence than the first 29-sample report.

Both CDC pages were accessible. Case definitions, convenience sampling, exposure reporting and testing changed through the outbreak. These population and laboratory findings do not establish that every lung injury has the same cause or that a test result alone diagnoses an individual. Recheck current public-health guidance separately if any health advice is contemplated.
