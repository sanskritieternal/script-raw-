# Selected remote-work case: GAO's 2025 federal review

## BANK-457-043 — a dated remote-work survey and agency interviews

- The [GAO report GAO-25-107363](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107363/index.html), published 17 June 2025, surveyed the 24 U.S. Chief Financial Officers Act agencies about remote workers as of June 2024. It separately examined hiring postings and documents/interviews at four selected agencies. GAO explicitly distinguishes remote work, with no regular agency-site reporting, from telework, which has recurring onsite work.
- The report describes reported recruitment/retention effects and office-space actions, while recommending better evaluation of outcome and cost effects. It also documents 2025 policy changes after the June 2024 count. The snapshot is federal and dated; it does not establish all-sector prevalence, current permission or a causal productivity gain.
- Script gate: retain the agency, occupation, survey-date and policy-date denominators; inspect individual tables and methods before numeric claims. Compare later OPM/agency policy and outcomes if discussing present-day arrangements.
- Semantic boundary: BANK-457-033 asks generally why activity measures can mislead. This case examines what one audit's survey and interviews can establish. Freelance contracts in 456 concern worker/client arrangements rather than agency remote-employee policy.
