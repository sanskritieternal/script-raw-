# Selected institutional source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-240-041: Kii Mountains

- UNESCO World Heritage Committee decision 28 COM 14B.28 (2004): https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/111/
- UNESCO component and boundary maps: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1142/maps/
- The decision inscribed Japanese sacred sites and pilgrimage routes as a cultural landscape, describing Shinto-Buddhist interaction and requesting stronger management planning. An inscription map defines a heritage property, not all sacred practice, oral geography or current route permission. The UNESCO record is institutional; seek current local custodians' perspectives before narrating living traditions. Do not disclose nonpublic sensitive locations.

## BANK-240-042: Mawphlang

- Meghalaya Tourism, Mawphlang Sacred Groves public page: https://www.meghalayatourism.in/experiences/nature-%26-wildlife/biodiversity-%26-nature-trails/mawphlang-sacred-groves/
- Meghalaya Government, Shillong cultural overview: https://meghalaya.gov.in/index.php/node/34292
- These public government-facing sources identify Mawphlang and Khasi sacred-grove association. Tourism prose is not direct authorization from all custodians and does not prove biodiversity outcomes or one uniform rule across Khasi communities. Obtain community-led interpretation and consent for on-site filming, ritual detail and access claims.
