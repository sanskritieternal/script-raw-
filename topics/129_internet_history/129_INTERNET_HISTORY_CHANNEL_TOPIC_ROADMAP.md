## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Internet History across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: ARPANET, Packet Switching & TCP/IP Protocol Foundations (Beginner / Scratch)** — Paul Baran and Donald Davies packet switching, the 1969 first ARPANET message ('LO'), Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn designing TCP/IP, the 1983 Flag Day transition, and DNS domain naming.
- **Tier 2: The World Wide Web, Browser Wars & The Dot-Com Mania (Intermediate)** — Tim Berners-Lee at CERN inventing HTTP/HTML, Marc Andreessen and Mosaic/Netscape, Microsoft crushing Netscape with Internet Explorer, and the 2000 dot-com stock market bubble crash.
- **Tier 3: Web 2.0, Social Platforms, Mobile Ubiquity & Cloud Infrastructure (Upper Intermediate)** — Tim O'Reilly's Web 2.0 user-generated content, the iPhone and mobile broadband explosion, AWS utility cloud computing, the death of Flash, and algorithmic ad surveillance networks.
- **Tier 4: The Open Web Resistance, Sovereign Intranets & Future Internet Architecture (Advanced / Masterclass)** — ICANN governance battles, the Great Firewall of China's packet inspection, net neutrality struggles, IPFS peer-to-peer storage, and the architectural defense of the decentralized open web.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Mother of All Demos: Doug Engelbart's 1968 Prophecy of the Modern Computer**
   - *Question:* How did a 90-minute live presentation in 1968 introduce the computer mouse, hypertext, windows, and collaborative real-time editing?
   - *Key Focus:* Augmenting human intellect, the wooden mouse prototype, NLS system, interactive video conferencing, and inspiring Xerox PARC.
2. **Packet Switching vs Circuit Switching: The Nuclear-Proof Network That Built the Web**
   - *Question:* Why did AT&T engineers tell Paul Baran that cutting messages into digital packets and reassembling them was impossible?
   - *Key Focus:* Cold War survivability, Paul Baran (RAND) and Donald Davies (NPL), telephone switchboard limitations, store-and-forward routing, and decentralized topologies.
3. **October 29, 1969: When ARPANET Crashed on the Word 'LOGIN'**
   - *Question:* How did Charley Kline transmitting from UCLA to Stanford type 'L', 'O', and crash the entire system before typing 'G'?
   - *Key Focus:* BBN Interface Message Processors (IMPs), Leonard Kleinrock, 50 kbps telephone leased lines, and the birth of interconnected computers.
4. **TCP/IP: How Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn Created the Internet's Universal Language**
   - *Question:* How do transmission control protocols ensure zero lost packets across completely different hardware networks?
   - *Key Focus:* Network of networks concept, IP addressing, TCP packet sequencing and acknowledgments, end-to-end principle, and the 1983 Flag Day switchover.
5. **The Invention of the World Wide Web: Tim Berners-Lee's Gift to Humanity**
   - *Question:* Why did an English physicist at a Swiss particle collider release HTTP, HTML, and URLs for free without patenting a single line?
   - *Key Focus:* Information management at CERN, NeXT computer server, the first web browser, Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP), and the decision to keep the web open and royalty-free.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The First Browser War: Netscape vs Microsoft Internet Explorer**
   - *Question:* How did Bill Gates crush Netscape by bundling Internet Explorer for free inside Windows 95, sparking the biggest antitrust trial in history?
   - *Key Focus:* Marc Andreessen and Mosaic, Netscape's 1995 IPO gold rush, 'cutting off Netscape's air supply', US v. Microsoft DOJ antitrust verdict, and the birth of Mozilla.
7. **The Dot-Com Bubble Crash: When Pet Food Companies Were Worth Billions**
   - *Question:* Why did investors pour billions into unprofitable websites with zero revenue models, leading to a $5 trillion market wipeout?
   - *Key Focus:* Pets.com puppet commercials, eyeball metrics vs cash flow, Super Bowl ad frenzies, NASDAQ 78% collapse, and the survival of Amazon and eBay.
8. **The Morris Worm of 1988: The Accidental Virus That Paralyzed the Early Internet**
   - *Question:* How did a Cornell graduate student crash 10% of all computers connected to the internet using a 99-line C script?
   - *Key Focus:* Buffer overflow vulnerabilities, finger daemon exploits, unintended infinite replication loops, Robert Tappan Morris's conviction, and the founding of CERT.
9. **Usenet and Eternal September: When the Internet Lost Its Quiet Innocence**
   - *Question:* Why did September 1993 mark the permanent death of early academic internet etiquette (Netiquette)?
   - *Key Focus:* Usenet newsgroups, college freshmen onboarding cycles, America Online (AOL) opening the floodgates to millions of casual users, and culture clashes.
10. **The Napster Earthquake: How Two College Kids Destroyed the Music Industry's Business Model**
   - *Question:* How did Shawn Fanning and Sean Parker's peer-to-peer MP3 file-sharing software trigger the collapse of CD sales?
   - *Key Focus:* Centralized P2P indexing servers, MP3 acoustic compression codecs, RIAA lawsuits, Metallica vs Napster, and paving the way for iTunes and Spotify.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Invention of Search: How PageRank and Google Conquered the Information Universe**
   - *Question:* Why did Larry Page and Sergey Brin treat web links like academic paper citations, making Yahoo and Altavista obsolete overnight?
   - *Key Focus:* Random surfer model, eigenvector centrality math, web crawler indexing, clean white homepage design, and AdWords keyword auction monetization.
12. **Web 2.0: When Consumers Became the Content Creators**
   - *Question:* How did static, read-only HTML pages transform into dynamic, interactive databases driven by user-generated content?
   - *Key Focus:* AJAX asynchronous javascript, XMLHttpRequests, Wikipedia crowdsourced truth, Flickr, YouTube upload explosion, and O'Reilly's manifesto.
13. **The Cloud Revolution: How Amazon Turned Unused Servers into AWS**
   - *Question:* How did an online bookstore build a utility computing business that powers half the modern internet?
   - *Key Focus:* S3 simple storage service, EC2 elastic compute cloud, virtualization hypervisors, eliminating server closets for startups, and high-margin B2B dominance.
14. **Steve Jobs and Thoughts on Flash: The Day Mobile Killed Adobe Flash**
   - *Question:* How did an open letter by Steve Jobs in 2010 single-handedly execute the most popular multimedia plugin on the web?
   - *Key Focus:* Touch interface incompatibility, battery drain, security zero-day vulnerabilities, proprietary lock-in, and the accelerated triumph of open HTML5 video.
15. **The Great Firewall of China: Building the World's Most Sophisticated Censorship Machine**
   - *Question:* How does an authoritarian government monitor and filter the internet traffic of 1 billion connected citizens in real time?
   - *Key Focus:* Deep packet inspection (DPI), DNS poisoning, BGP border routing filtering, domestic ecosystem cloning (WeChat/Baidu), and VPN cat-and-mouse games.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Undersea Fiber Optic Cables: The Invisible Glass Threads Carrying 99% of Internet Data**
   - *Question:* Why is the internet not in the sky or satellites, but carried inside 1-inch-thick glass cables laid across the ocean floor?
   - *Key Focus:* Subsea cable-laying ships, erbium-doped optical amplifiers (EDFA), shark bite shielding, anchor snags, and Big Tech (Google/Meta) cable consortia.
17. **The Domain Name System (DNS) and the 7 Cryptographic Key Holders**
   - *Question:* Who are the 14 trusted individuals who hold physical physical metal keys to reboot the entire internet's address book?
   - *Key Focus:* ICANN Key Signing Ceremony, 13 root DNS server clusters, DNSSEC cryptographic authentication, and geopolitical battles over global internet governance.
18. **Net Neutrality: The War Over Internet Fast Lanes and Utility Regulation**
   - *Question:* Should broadband internet service providers be allowed to throttle competitor video traffic and sell paid fast lanes?
   - *Key Focus:* Title II common carrier regulation, Comcast vs BitTorrent throttling, zero-rating deals, FCC regulatory swings, and the open internet principle.
19. **The Decentralized Web: IPFS, Tor, and the Fight Against Digital Feudalism**
   - *Question:* Can peer-to-peer content-addressed storage and onion routing liberate the internet from AWS, Google, and corporate control?
   - *Key Focus:* InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) content-addressing vs location-addressing, Tor layered onion routing, censorship resistance, and decentralized identity.
20. **The Master Internet Architecture: The Blueprint of Global Digital Civilization**
   - *Question:* What unifying design principles allowed an experimental military network of 4 computers to scale to 5 billion people without collapsing?
   - *Key Focus:* Layered abstraction model (OSI / TCP-IP), the end-to-end principle, rough consensus and running code (IETF), and architecting for the next 100 billion nodes.
