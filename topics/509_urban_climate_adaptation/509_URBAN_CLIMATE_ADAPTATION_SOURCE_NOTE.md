# Urban climate adaptation source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-509-043 - Rotterdam Benthemplein water square

- Primary municipal project page: Municipality of Rotterdam, *Benthemplein*, https://www.rotterdam.nl/benthemplein (inspected 2026-10-24; Dutch-language page). Rotterdam says the three basins can store approximately 1.7 million litres of water. This is stated storage capacity, not an observed reduction in flood damage.
- Primary municipal system description: Municipality of Rotterdam, *Toekomstbestendige wateropvang*, https://www.rotterdam.nl/toekomstbestendige-wateropvang (inspected 2026-10-24; Dutch-language page). It describes nearby roof/street runoff entering water squares via gutters, pipes and changes in elevation, and water slowly infiltrating after rain. Benthemplein is one example in a broader network.
- Claim and access limits: The municipal pages describe design and function but do not provide controlled before/after damage, water-level or downstream impact measurements for a specified storm in this review. The 1.7 million-litre number is approximate design capacity, not proof of filling, drainage efficacy or citywide climate resilience. A script needs dated monitoring, maintenance records, storm context and accurate translation before a performance claim. Avoid advising how to use flood-prone public space during an event. Local benefits and burdens should be checked with affected residents.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-509-043 is a built Rotterdam stormwater-storage case. BANK-509-033 asks generic downstream drainage tradeoffs; BANK-507-042 covers treatment and reuse of wastewater; BANK-501-037 covers surveillance, not stormwater storage.
