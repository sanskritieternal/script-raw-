## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Urban Climate Adaptation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Urban Climate Adaptation Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Urban Climate Adaptation.
- **Tier 2: Urban Climate Adaptation Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Urban Climate Adaptation.
- **Tier 3: Urban Climate Adaptation Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Urban Climate Adaptation.
- **Tier 4: Urban Climate Adaptation Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Urban Climate Adaptation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Urban Climate Adaptation: How Urban Climate Adaptation Developed**
   - *Question:* How did a city's risk assessment become an adaptation project?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one city's adaptation measure from documented hazard assessment through planning and delivery; distinguish historical conditions from projected risk. Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: local hazard assessments, project designs, maintenance records and observed event performance. Starting framework: IPCC AR6 urban adaptation chapter (https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGII_Chapter06.pdf). Select and verify the dated underlying case sources before scripting..
