# Selected film case source checks — 24 October 2026

These checks bound three production and labor cases. A platform, supplier, effects house or union has first-hand information about its own work or agreement, but its promotional or advocacy wording does not prove every viewer reaction or production-wide claim. Other archived stories still require credit, edition and rights checks.

## BANK-080-071 — Oppenheimer presentation formats

- [Kodak’s interview with credited cinematographer Hoyte van Hoytema](https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/blog-post/oppenheimer/) describes 65mm negative capture, color and black-and-white stock, and 2023 print counts: 30 IMAX 70mm, 113 standard 70mm and about 80 35mm prints. These are supplier and production claims tied to release period, not a census of all later screenings.
- [IMAX’s 2023 format notice](https://www.imax.com/news/oppenheimer-in-imax-70mm) specifies that sequences shot with IMAX film cameras expand to 1.43:1 on 15-perf/70mm film projection. It does not establish that every frame or every IMAX auditorium used that ratio.
- **Editorial bound:** Identify the screened version and projection format before a frame comparison. Travel behavior, universal superiority and a simple pixel-equivalence claim are not established by these sources.

## BANK-080-072 — The Mandalorian and StageCraft

- [Industrial Light & Magic’s original StageCraft production account](https://www.ilm.com/groundbreaking-led-stage-production-technology-created-for-hit-lucasfilm-series-the-mandalorian/) names The Mandalorian team’s LED-stage workflow and describes virtual environments combined with physical set pieces and props. ILM is a participant and a technology vendor, so its claims about appearance are not independent audience tests.
- [ILM’s later stage expansion account](https://www.ilm.com/3-new-ilm-stagecraft-stages-coming-soon-global-trainee-program-announced/) identifies the first season’s 2018 volume and later expanded facilities. It does not imply that every Star Wars or Marvel production used the same stage, settings or visual strategy.
- **Editorial bound:** Attribute VFX, cinematography and production-design credits for the selected episode and distinguish photographed imagery, practical pieces and finishing. A general “flat” appearance claim needs a specified shot and comparison.

## BANK-080-076 — 2023 WGA generative-AI terms

- The [WGA’s 2023 Memorandum of Agreement](https://www.wga.org/uploadedfiles/contracts/2023_MBA_MOA.pdf) is the negotiated primary document; Article 72 addresses generative artificial intelligence. Its precise definitions, disclosure requirements and covered projects govern a script, not all entertainment work.
- The guild’s [current rights explanation](https://www.wga.org/contracts/know-your-rights/artificial-intelligence) describes the 2023 MBA protections for writers and links to the underlying provision. It is a party’s interpretation, so the agreement text controls a clause-specific claim.
- **Editorial bound:** Date the agreement and distinguish WGA writer terms from SAG-AFTRA performer digital-replica terms and later contracts. Do not present a strike outcome as proof that AI use ended or all jobs were protected.
