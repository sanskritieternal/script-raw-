## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Logic & Critical Thinking across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Formal Syllogisms, Cognitive Biases & Fallacy Detection (Beginner / Scratch)** — Aristotelian syllogistic logic, modus ponens/modus tollens, ad hominem and straw man fallacies, confirmation bias, and Daniel Kahneman's System 1 vs System 2 thinking.
- **Tier 2: Probabilistic Reasoning, First Principles & Mental Models (Intermediate)** — Charlie Munger's latticework of mental models, Elon Musk's first principles physics deconstruction, Occam's Razor, Hanlon's Razor, and base rate neglect.
- **Tier 3: Epistemology, Dialectics & Propaganda Deconstruction (Upper Intermediate)** — Popperian falsificationism, Socratic dialogue questioning method, red teaming and steel-manning opposing arguments, cognitive dissonance mitigation, and manufactured consent media analysis.
- **Tier 4: Formal Symbolic Logic, Meta-Rationality & Master Cognitive Architecture (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Propositional calculus and Boolean truth tables, Bayesian cognitive calibration, superforecasting methodology (Philip Tetlock), algorithmic cognitive debiasing, and the master thinker's toolkit.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Art of the Steel Man: How to Win Arguments by Making Your Opponent Stronger**
   - *Question:* Why is articulating the absolute strongest version of your opponent's belief the ultimate cognitive superpower?
   - *Key Focus:* Straw man vs steel man methodology, Daniel Dennett's Rapoport rules, intellectual charity, and dialectical synthesis.
2. **System 1 vs System 2: Thinking, Fast and Slow with Daniel Kahneman**
   - *Question:* How does your automatic, emotional brain constantly trick your lazy analytical executive mind into making irrational decisions?
   - *Key Focus:* Heuristics and biases, substitution questions, cognitive ease vs cognitive strain, ego depletion, and anchoring effects.
3. **The Top 10 Logical Fallacies: How Everyday Arguments Deceive You**
   - *Question:* How do politicians and media pundits weaponize Ad Hominem, False Dilemma, Slippery Slope, and Whataboutism?
   - *Key Focus:* Informal vs formal fallacies, Tu Quoque, Begging the Question, Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc, and spotting manipulative debate tactics.
4. **Confirmation Bias: Why We Only See Evidence That Proves We Are Right**
   - *Question:* Why does presenting stubborn people with objective, factual counter-evidence often cause them to double down on falsehoods?
   - *Key Focus:* The Backfire Effect, motivated reasoning, identity-protective cognition, search algorithm filter bubbles, and actively open-minded thinking.
5. **Occam's Razor vs Hanlon's Razor: The Essential Heuristic Blades**
   - *Question:* When should you cut away complex conspiracy theories, and why should you never attribute to malice what is explained by stupidity?
   - *Key Focus:* Parsimony in scientific explanation, Chatton's anti-razor, malice vs incompetence in bureaucracy, and heuristic efficiency.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **First Principles Thinking: How Aristotle, Descartes, and Elon Musk Solve Impossible Problems**
   - *Question:* How do you break complex problems down to their fundamental atomic truths and build up solutions without reasoning by analogy?
   - *Key Focus:* Cartesian doubt, physics raw material cost breakdowns, analogical thinking traps, and creative engineering breakthroughs.
7. **Charlie Munger's Latticework: The Multidisciplinary Mental Model Machine**
   - *Question:* Why does a person with only a hammer treat every problem like a nail, and how do you build a latticework of 100 mental models?
   - *Key Focus:* Man-with-a-hammer syndrome, inversion thinking ('Invert, always invert'), critical mass, compounding, and multi-disciplinary synthesis.
8. **The Socratic Method: How to Question People into Discovering the Truth**
   - *Question:* Why did Socrates never lecture or assert facts, yet could lead anyone to realize their own foundational contradictions?
   - *Key Focus:* Elenchus questioning sequence, uncovering hidden presuppositions, productive cognitive dissonance, and educational inquiry.
9. **The Dunning-Kruger Effect: Why Incompetent People Are the Most Confident**
   - *Question:* Why does learning 5% of a topic create sky-high arrogance on 'Mount Stupid' while true experts suffer from imposter syndrome?
   - *Key Focus:* Metacognitive incompetence, confidence vs competence curves, Valley of Despair, and calibrating self-awareness.
10. **Correlation vs Causation: The Spurious Statistics Deceiving the Public**
   - *Question:* Why does ice cream consumption correlate 98% with drowning deaths, and how do confounding variables distort news headlines?
   - *Key Focus:* Confounders and lurking variables, reverse causality, selection bias, Judea Pearl's causal calculus, and randomized controlled trials (RCTs).
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Popperian Falsification: What Makes a Claim Truly Scientific?**
   - *Question:* Why did Karl Popper declare that astrology and psychoanalysis are pseudosciences while Einstein's relativity is genuine science?
   - *Key Focus:* The Demarcation Problem, black swan logic (no amount of white swans proves all swans are white), risky predictions, and unfalsifiable dogmas.
12. **Cognitive Dissonance and Self-Justification: The Architecture of Excuses**
   - *Question:* What happens when our deep self-image clashes with undeniable proof that we did something terrible or foolish?
   - *Key Focus:* Leon Festinger's cult prediction failure study, 'Mistakes Were Made (But Not by Me)', pyramid of choice, and rationalization mechanics.
13. **Red Teaming and Pre-Mortems: Stress-Testing Plans Before Disaster Strikes**
   - *Question:* How do military strategists and NASA engineers assume a mission has completely failed before it even launches to identify fatal flaws?
   - *Key Focus:* Gary Klein's pre-mortem protocol, devil's advocacy institutionalization, adversarial red teams, and groupthink immunization.
14. **Superforecasting: How Ordinary People Beat the CIA at Predicting the Future**
   - *Question:* How did Philip Tetlock's amateur 'Good Judgment Project' volunteers consistently out-predict intelligence agency analysts?
   - *Key Focus:* Brier scores, probabilistic updating in micro-increments, fox vs hedgehog mindsets, Fermi estimation, and epistemic calibration.
15. **Manufactured Consent: How Mass Media Filters Frame Reality**
   - *Question:* How do Noam Chomsky's five propaganda filters shape public consensus without overt government censorship?
   - *Key Focus:* Corporate ownership, advertising revenue reliance, official source sourcing, flak pushback, and common enemy ideology.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Symbolic Logic and Propositional Calculus: Truth Tables and Boolean Algebra**
   - *Question:* How do AND, OR, NOT, and IMPLIES logic gates form the bridge between human philosophical deduction and silicon microchips?
   - *Key Focus:* Truth tables, De Morgan's laws, modus tollens proofs, tautologies vs contradictions, and computer logic gate synthesis.
17. **Algorithmic Cognitive Debiasing: Can AI Help Humans Think Rationally?**
   - *Question:* Can large language models act as real-time dialectical sparing partners that detect our subconscious cognitive fallacies during writing?
   - *Key Focus:* Automated fallacy detection, synthetic counter-argument generation, blind-spot mapping, and human-AI epistemic teaming.
18. **The Epistemic Commons: Fighting Truth Decay in the Age of Generative AI**
   - *Question:* What happens to democratic self-governance when synthetic deepfakes and AI hallucinations make objective truth unverifiable?
   - *Key Focus:* Infocalypse, cryptographic content provenance (C2PA), cognitive security (CogSec), and institutional trust reconstruction.
19. **Wittgenstein's Language Games: The Limits of Words and the Unspeakable**
   - *Question:* Why did Ludwig Wittgenstein declare that most philosophical problems are just linguistic misunderstandings and semantic confusion?
   - *Key Focus:* Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus, 'Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent', language-games, and beetle-in-a-box.
20. **The Master Thinker's Toolkit: The Complete Cognitive Architecture**
   - *Question:* What unified daily operational system combines Bayesian updating, first principles, emotional detachment, and dialectical synthesis?
   - *Key Focus:* Daily epistemic checklist, cognitive debiasing rituals, decision journals, probabilistic calibration exercises, and intellectual integrity.
