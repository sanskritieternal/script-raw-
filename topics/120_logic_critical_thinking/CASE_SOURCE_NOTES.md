## Plato's *Euthyphro* — BANK-120-043

- The [primary dialogue in the Perseus edition, section 10a](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0170%3Atext%3DEuthyph.%3Asection%3D10a) presents Socrates’ question about whether the pious is loved by the gods because it is pious, or pious because it is loved. The question tests a proposed account rather than supplying a fixed recipe for winning disputes.
- The surviving text is Plato's authored dialogue in translation, not a verbatim recording of a historical conversation. Follow the surrounding argument and translation choices before claiming exactly what Socrates believed or how every modern discussion should proceed.

## Good Judgment Project scoring — BANK-120-044

- [IARPA's Aggregative Contingent Estimation program page](https://www.iarpa.gov/research-programs/ace) describes its effort to elicit and aggregate probabilistic judgments. The [project team's original methods paper](https://cdn.aaai.org/ocs/5570/5570-23868-1-PB.pdf) reports recruiting more than 2,000 forecasters and evaluating probability estimates with a Brier score, the squared difference between assigned probability and a binary outcome. A [research-team tournament paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0963721414534257) describes training, teams and aggregation in the winning project.
- Scoring depends on the selected questions, resolution criteria, comparison group and period. The archive's claim that volunteers consistently beat all intelligence analysts or could predict “the future” exceeds what these sources alone establish. An episode should report the actual tournament comparison and uncertainty in generalizing beyond it.
