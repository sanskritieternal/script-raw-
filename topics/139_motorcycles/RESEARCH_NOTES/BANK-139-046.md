# BANK-139-046: optional motorcycle ABS and fatal-crash involvement

Research checked: 2026-10-24. Status: bounded source review, not riding instruction, script or idea approval.

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety's 2021 study account](https://www.iihs.org/news/detail/largest-study-of-its-kind-strengthens-argument-for-motorcycle-abs) reports an analysis of 65 motorcycle models on which ABS was optional. It says equipped models had 22 percent fewer driver fatal-crash involvements per 10,000 registered vehicle years than comparable models without ABS. The same account describes different associations by motorcycle type and notes that models with standard ABS were outside the sample. [NHTSA's motorcycle safety page](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/motorcycles) supplies the broader U.S. road-safety context, with a different denominator for its overall risk comparisons.

**Claim boundary:** The IIHS estimate is an observational association in selected U.S. models and years, not a guarantee for an individual rider or proof that ABS prevents every crash. Registered vehicle years are not miles ridden; model, rider and use differences may remain. The NHTSA rate comparison is not interchangeable with IIHS's matched-model ABS estimate. Neither source supports the archived claims that a specific braking technique is always safer or that electronics make mid-corner full throttle safe. Do not convert this research note into maneuver instruction.
