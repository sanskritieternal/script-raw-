## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Motorcycles across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Two-Wheel Gyroscopic Physics, Countersteering & Motorcycle Ergonomics (Beginner / Scratch)** — Countersteering physics (push right to go right), gyroscopic precession, rake and trail geometry, friction circle traction budgets, and clutch friction zone control.
- **Tier 2: Engine Architectures, Carburetor vs EFI & Suspension Geometry (Intermediate)** — V-Twin vs Inline-4 vs Parallel-Twin vs Boxer torque curves, telescopic fork damping rods vs cartridge valves, chain drive vs shaft drive, and setting suspension sag.
- **Tier 3: Track Cornering Dynamics, Trail Braking & High-Speed Safety Electronics (Upper Intermediate)** — Trail braking past the turn-in point, highside vs lowside crashes, 6-axis Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs), cornering ABS, and quickshifter ignition cuts.
- **Tier 4: Adventure Touring Logistics, Isle of Man TT Danger & Electric Moto Futures (Advanced / Masterclass)** — The lethal Isle of Man TT course physics (135 mph average on country roads), round-the-world ADV logistics, electric motorcycle torque-density, and custom chassis fabrication.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **Countersteering Explained: The Counter-Intuitive Physics of Turning a Motorcycle**
   - *Question:* Why must you push the right handlebar forward (turning the front wheel left) to initiate a high-speed right turn?
   - *Key Focus:* Out-tracking roll moment, gyroscopic precession of front wheel, tire contact patch displacement beneath center of mass, and Keith Code's No-BS Bike experiments.
2. **Engine Character: V-Twin vs Inline-4 vs Parallel Twin vs Boxer**
   - *Question:* Why does a Harley 45-degree V-Twin rumble with low-end torque while an R1 Inline-4 screeches like a Formula 1 car at 14,000 RPM?
   - *Key Focus:* Crankshaft pin offsets (270-degree crossplane firing orders), primary and secondary dynamic balancing, low-end torque vs high-RPM horsepower, and center of gravity.
3. **The Traction Budget: The Friction Circle and Why Panicking Ruins Turns**
   - *Question:* Why does grabbing the front brake in the middle of a lean angle overload your front tire and cause an instant lowside crash?
   - *Key Focus:* Kamm's friction circle, 100% traction budget split between lean force and braking/acceleration, tire contact patch squirm, and progressive control.
4. **Clutch Friction Zone Mastery: Low-Speed Maneuvers and Police Rodeo Riding**
   - *Question:* How do motor officers whip 900-pound Harley-Davidsons through tight 18-foot figure-8 cones without tipping over?
   - *Key Focus:* Wet multi-plate clutch slipping, dragging the rear brake against engine torque (drivetrain tensioning), head-and-eyes target fixation, and counter-balancing.
5. **Target Fixation: The Deadly Neurological Trap That Pulls You Toward Hazards**
   - *Question:* Why do motorcyclists staring at a pothole or guardrail steer directly into the very obstacle they are desperate to avoid?
   - *Key Focus:* Survival reactions (SRs), vestibular-ocular coupling, looking through the turn to the exit, panoramic peripheral vision, and panic reflex management.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Motorcycle Suspension: Setting Sag, Compression, and Rebound Damping**
   - *Question:* How does spending an hour measuring spring preload and adjusting clicker valves transform a harsh, skittering bike into a glued cornering machine?
   - *Key Focus:* Static and rider sag calculations (30-35mm target), shim stacks in cartridge forks, high vs low-speed compression, and packing down vs pogo rebound damping.
7. **Trail Braking Masterclass: Why Modern Riders Brake All the Way to the Apex**
   - *Question:* Why did riding schools teach 'finish all braking in a straight line' for 30 years, and why is trail braking actually safer?
   - *Key Focus:* Front fork compression steepening rake/trail for faster steering, trailing off brake pressure as lean angle increases, adjusting turn radius with front brake, and emergency stopping mid-corner.
8. **Highside vs Lowside Crashes: The Violent Physics of Losing Grip**
   - *Question:* What happens when a spinning rear tire breaks traction, steps out sideways, and suddenly bites into pavement?
   - *Key Focus:* Lowside sliding friction loss, highside kinetic energy catapult (suspension unloading like a springboard), chassis rotational momentum, and avoiding panic throttle chops.
9. **Chain Drive vs Belt vs Shaft Drive: The Power Transfer Battle**
   - *Question:* Why do sportbikes use greasy roller chains, cruisers use carbon belts, and BMW adventure tourers use heavy enclosed shafts?
   - *Key Focus:* Mechanical power efficiency (98% for roller chains), un-sprung weight penalties, BMW Paralever anti-jacking shaft geometry, belt longevity without oiling, and maintenance trade-offs.
10. **The 1969 Honda CB750: The Four-Cylinder Machine That Invented the Superbike**
   - *Question:* How did Soichiro Honda's transverse inline-4 with electric start and front disc brake kill the British motorcycle industry in one year?
   - *Key Focus:* Oil-tight reliability, overhead camshaft smooth power, push-button electric start vs finicky British kickstarters, affordable performance, and the Universal Japanese Motorcycle (UJM).
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **The Electronics Revolution: 6-Axis IMUs, Cornering ABS, and Wheelie Control**
   - *Question:* How do gyro-sensors calculating roll, pitch, and yaw 1,000 times a second allow a 200-horsepower superbike to pin the throttle mid-corner without crashing?
   - *Key Focus:* Bosch Motorcycle Stability Control (MSC), lean-angle sensitive ABS pressure modulation, ride-by-wire throttle mapping, slide control algorithms, and fly-by-wire rider aids.
12. **The Quickshifter and Autoblipper: Shifting Gears at Wide-Open Throttle in 50 Milliseconds**
   - *Question:* How does a piezoelectric strain gauge on the shift rod allow clutchless upshifts and automatic throttle-blipped downshifts?
   - *Key Focus:* Drivetrain dog-ring gear un-loading, ignition cut micro-bursts (40-60ms), electronic ride-by-wire throttle auto-blips on downshifts, and gearbox synchronization.
13. **The Isle of Man TT: The Most Dangerous Sporting Event on Earth**
   - *Question:* Why do racers willingly blast past stone walls, lampposts, and village curb edges at 200 MPH where over 260 competitors have lost their lives?
   - *Key Focus:* The 37.73-mile Snaefell Mountain Course, average lap speeds exceeding 135 MPH, jumping crests at Ballagarey (The Central Nervous System), mental memorization, and fatal risk philosophy.
14. **Adventure Bike Logistics: How to Prepare a Motorcycle to Cross Continents**
   - *Question:* How do you outfit a BMW R1250GS or Yamaha Ténéré 700 to survive 20,000 miles of gravel, rivers, and zero mechanic support?
   - *Key Focus:* Crash bar reinforcement, aluminum pannier weight balance, tire selection (50/50 knobbies), field tubeless tire plug kits, fuel filtration for bad gas, and Carnet de Passages border customs.
15. **Cafe Racers and Custom Culture: Stripping Down Vintage Iron in the Ace Cafe Era**
   - *Question:* How did British 'Ton-Up Boys' in the 1960s strip down Triumph and Norton motorcycles to race between London transport cafes at 100 MPH?
   - *Key Focus:* The Ton-Up 100 MPH milestone, clip-on handlebars, rearset footpegs, sweeping exhaust pipes, Triton hybrid builds (Norton featherbed frame with Triumph Bonneville engine), and custom culture.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Motorcycle Safety Gear Science: CE Armor, Kevlar Abrasion, and Wireless Airbag Vests**
   - *Question:* How do standalone autonomous airbag vests inflate in 25 milliseconds before your body hits the pavement in a crash?
   - *Key Focus:* D3O non-Newtonian shear-thickening impact armor, slide abrasion ratings (AA/AAA cordura and aramid), accelerometer crash detection algorithms, and CO2 canister deployment.
17. **Electric Motorcycles: Instant Torque vs The Highway Battery Range Problem**
   - *Question:* Why do electric bikes like the Zero SR/F offer blistering supercar acceleration in town, but drain their batteries in 45 minutes on the interstate?
   - *Key Focus:* Zero-RPM peak torque dynamics, regenerative braking, lack of fairing aerodynamics at 75 MPH, thermal throttling in air-cooled packs, and fast-charging weight trade-offs.
18. **Chassis Geometry and Handling: Rake, Trail, Swingarm Angle, and Anti-Squat**
   - *Question:* How does dropping the front triple clamps by 3 millimeters completely transform how aggressively a motorcycle tips into corners?
   - *Key Focus:* Steering head angle (rake), trail dimensions, offset triple clamps, swingarm pivot location relative to countershaft sprocket, and squat dynamics under acceleration.
19. **The Philosophy of the Open Road: Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance**
   - *Question:* Why did Robert Pirsig use tuning a motorcycle's tappets as an inquiry into the nature of Quality and human technology?
   - *Key Focus:* Classical rational understanding vs romantic sensory experience, gumption traps, mechanical empathy, and the meditative mindfulness of solo riding.
20. **The Master Rider Architecture: The Complete High-Performance & Survival System**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive daily riding framework unites visual scanning habits, trail braking execution, emergency swerves, and survival mental discipline?
   - *Key Focus:* System of motorcycle control, pre-ride T-CLOCS mechanical inspection, SIPDE (Scan, Identify, Predict, Decide, Execute), weather contingency planning, and lifetime safety mastery.
