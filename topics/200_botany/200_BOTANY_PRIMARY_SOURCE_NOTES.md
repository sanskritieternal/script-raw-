# Selected primary-source checks (2026-10-24)

## BANK-200-036: Light Bio petunia

- USDA APHIS, "APHIS Issues Regulatory Status Review Response: Light Bio Petunia," 6 September 2023: https://www.aphis.usda.gov/print/pdf/node/6100
- APHIS says the modified petunia was unlikely to pose increased plant-pest risk compared with cultivated petunia and was not subject to regulation under 7 CFR part 340. This is a particular US regulatory finding, not a comprehensive safety endorsement, a global authorization, or evidence of ecological benefit. The APHIS PDF endpoint returned HTTP 403 on direct open here; its indexed agency text was inspectable. APHIS later states the RSR-enabling regulations were vacated on 2 December 2024 (https://www.aphis.usda.gov/biotechnology/petitions), so confirm current rules and sales status before production.

## BANK-200-037: plant ultrasound

- Khait et al., "Sounds emitted by plants under stress are airborne and informative," *Cell* 186 (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2023.03.009: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0092867423002623
- The original study reports ultrasonic recordings of tomato and tobacco under dehydration and cutting in controlled acoustic and greenhouse settings, and classification by condition. It does not show subjective distress, language, a receiver response in nature, or a field-ready monitoring system. Separate this emitting case from BANK-204-018, which asks about response to recorded sound.
