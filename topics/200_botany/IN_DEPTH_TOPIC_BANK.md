## Category: Plant structure, growth and energy

<a id="catalog-200-r01"></a>
### Plant structure, growth and energy — foundation

Subcategory `CATALOG-200-R01` · foundation.

<a id="bank-200-010"></a>
#### BANK-200-010 — The Solar Engine: How Chloroplasts Transformed Sunlight into All Terrestrial Life

**Video question:** How does a green leaf convert photons from 93 million miles away into glucose sugars that power every animal on Earth?

**Individual coverage scope:** Thylakoid membranes, chlorophyll a and b photon absorption, the water-splitting photolysis reaction generating oxygen, and the Calvin-Benson cycle fixing carbon via RuBisCO enzymes.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-011"></a>
#### BANK-200-011 — The Vascular Highway: Xylem Cohesion-Tension Transporting Water 300 Feet Up Redwoods

**Video question:** How do 350-foot coast redwoods pull thousands of gallons of water from soil up to canopy needles without a mechanical pump?

**Individual coverage scope:** Cohesion-Tension theory, hydrogen bonding between water molecules creating unbroken tensile columns, negative pressure (tension) generated by leaf transpiration, and phloem sugar translocation.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-012"></a>
#### BANK-200-012 — Stomata and Guard Cells: Osmotic Pores Balancing CO2 Ingestion with Water Evaporation

**Video question:** How do microscopic microscopic dumbbell-shaped cells open and close millions of leaf valves in response to sunlight and drought?

**Individual coverage scope:** Potassium ion (K+) pumping driving osmotic water swelling of guard cells, microfibril radial cellulose rings causing outward bending, abscisic acid (ABA) drought signaling, and transpiration tradeoffs.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-013"></a>
#### BANK-200-013 — The Five Plant Groups: Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, and Angiosperms

**Video question:** How did plants evolve from non-vascular creeping mosses into naked-seed conifers and spectacular flowering fruit trees?

**Individual coverage scope:** Bryophytes (mosses without vascular tissue), Pteridophytes (ferns with spores), Gymnosperms (naked-seed pines and cycads), and Angiosperms (flowering plants with encased ovaries), alternation of generations.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

Subcategory `CATALOG-200-R03` · intermediate.

<a id="bank-200-014"></a>
#### BANK-200-014 — Plant Communication and VOC Alarms: Chewed Leaves Signaling Neighbors

**Video question:** When a caterpillar chews on a tobacco leaf, how does the plant warn downwind neighbors to produce bitter nicotine toxins before they are attacked?

**Individual coverage scope:** Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs), methyl jasmonate signaling through air currents, recruiting parasitoid wasps to attack chewing herbivores, and systemic acquired resistance (SAR).

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-015"></a>
#### BANK-200-015 — The Wood Wide Web: Mycorrhizal Fungal Networks Trading Phosphorus for Plant Sugars

**Video question:** How do underground fungal hyphae connect hundreds of individual trees into an interconnected resource-sharing subterranean network?

**Individual coverage scope:** Ectomycorrhizal vs arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi, trading soil mineral nutrients (phosphorus, nitrogen) for 20% of the tree's photosynthetic carbon, nutrient routing, and mother tree theories.

**Neighboring scope:** [BANK-204-024](../204_plant_intelligence/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-204-024) — Fungal-network mechanism versus claims about plant communication.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-016"></a>
#### BANK-200-016 — Allelopathy and Chemical Warfare: Black Walnuts and Eucalyptus Poisoning Soil

**Video question:** Why does almost nothing grow beneath a black walnut tree, and how do eucalyptus trees use flammable volatile oils to torch competitors?

**Individual coverage scope:** Juglone hydroquinone exudation inhibiting seed germination and enzyme respiration in neighboring plants, terpenes in eucalyptus needles promoting intense wildfires to which the trees are fire-adapted.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-018"></a>
#### BANK-200-018 — Thigmonasty: Rapid Action Potentials Snapping Venus Flytraps and Mimosa Shut

**Video question:** How can a plant with no nerves or muscles trigger electric action potentials and snap a leaf shut in 100 milliseconds?

**Individual coverage scope:** Venus flytrap mechanosensory trigger hairs requiring two mechanical touches within 20 seconds, calcium ion influx, sudden loss of turgor pressure in pulvinus cells, and Mimosa pudica sensitive leaf folding.

**Neighboring scope:** [BANK-204-012](../204_plant_intelligence/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-204-012) — Electrical trap closure versus historical root-tip interpretation.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-200-037"></a>
#### BANK-200-037 — Ultrasonic sound from stressed tomato and tobacco

**Video question:** What did the 2023 tomato and tobacco recordings establish about airborne ultrasound from stressed plants?

**Individual coverage scope:** Examine Khait et al. chamber and greenhouse recordings after dehydration or cutting, classification of signals, controls and uncertainty; sound emission alone does not establish pain, speech or ecological communication.

**Research follow-up:** [Bounded case and source-access note](200_BOTANY_PRIMARY_SOURCE_NOTES.md). This does not change proposal or production status.

**Neighboring scope:** [BANK-204-018](../204_plant_intelligence/IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-204-018) — Acoustic emission experiment versus plant response to an acoustic recording.
