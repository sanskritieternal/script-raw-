# Selected primary-source note — character design

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-380-045`.

- [Nintendo, *Iwata Asks: New Super Mario Bros. Wii*, “The Reason Mario Wears Overalls”](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-New-Super-Mario-Bros-Wii/Volume-1/2-The-Reason-Mario-Wears-Overalls/2-The-Reason-Mario-Wears-Overalls-210759.html): Shigeru Miyamoto recalls the early 16×16 character image, too few pixels to draw a mouth, a moustache instead, a cap to simplify hair animation, and overalls to distinguish moving arms from the body. The page is an official published creator interview; no publication date was established in this inspection.

This is a retrospective account, not a dated original design sheet or an exhaustive credit history. The claim is limited to design choices Miyamoto describes and does not establish sole authorship, exact first-use chronology, or hardware limits of later *Super Mario Bros.* releases.
