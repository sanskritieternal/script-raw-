# Language-learning source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-449-044 - CEFR Companion Volume mediation

- Primary framework: Council of Europe, *Common European Framework of Reference for Languages: Learning, teaching, assessment - Companion volume* (2020), https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4 (inspected 2026-10-24). Section 3.4 describes mediation as constructing or conveying meaning across people, languages or modalities and distinguishes mediating texts, concepts and communication.
- Institutional context: Council of Europe CEFR page, https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages (inspected 2026-10-24), identifies the 2020 Companion Volume and its extended descriptors for mediation, online interaction, plurilingual/pluricultural competence and sign languages.
- Limits: Descriptors are a published assessment/teaching reference, not a measured causal effect of mediation tasks on learning. Levels and examples need adaptation to age, language, modality, disability access and classroom context; they cannot diagnose an individual's proficiency from one task. Do not imply uniform adoption or a universal curriculum requirement.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-449-044 names a 2020 framework and its mediation construct. BANK-449-043 covers fluency assessment generally, BANK-449-037 covers listening and conversation practice, and BANK-069-037 covers multilingual students demonstrating subject knowledge. It does not duplicate Sanskrit-specific grammar in 450.
