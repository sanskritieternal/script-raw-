# Selected primary-source checks - Rivers
Checked 2026-10-24. These checks bound proposed questions; archived numerical and causal claims remain unverified.

- BANK-210-037: UN Security Council presidential statement of 15 September 2021, https://press.un.org/en/2021/sc14637.doc.htm . It recognizes the 2015 Declaration of Principles and calls for Ethiopia, Egypt and Sudan to resume talks on GERD filling and operation. This is a dated diplomatic record, not proof of present agreement, actual flow impacts or any party's exclusive legal position. The existing BANK-210-036 discusses transboundary agreements generally.
- BANK-210-038: Diringer et al. 2015 original Madre de Dios sampling study, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25573610/ . Samples from 2013 across a 560 km reach found elevated mercury downstream of known artisanal mining in some matrices. A later original study found high sediment mercury at a mining-affected tributary but no corresponding elevation in fish versus two comparator rivers, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30049961/ . Site, season, matrix and food web differ. Neither study alone proves individual illness or all Amazon rivers are affected.
- BANK-210-024: US Army Corps of Engineers describes Old River Control structures and the Mississippi-Atchafalaya flow allocation, https://www.mvn.usace.army.mil/Missions/Recreation/Old-River-Control/ . An infrastructure description does not predict exact future avulsion timing.
- BANK-210-026: Bureau of Reclamation 2024 update on lower Klamath removal and habitat plans, https://www.usbr.gov/newsroom/news-release/5068 . Opened passage and planned restoration are not equivalent to a demonstrated recovered salmon population.
