**Video question:** Why does a straight artificial river canal naturally begin to snake into curves, and how do winding loops pinch off into crescent oxbow lakes?

**Individual coverage scope:** Helicoidal secondary flow, thalweg (deepest fastest flow line) scouring outer cut banks, centrifugal deceleration depositing point bars on inner bends, neck cutoffs during floods, and oxbow sloughs.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

#### BANK-210-013 — Sediment Transport Mechanics: Bedload, Suspended Silt, Saltation, and Alluvial Fans

**Video question:** How does a river roll massive multi-ton granite boulders along its bed while carrying billions of tons of fine silt in suspension to the sea?

**Individual coverage scope:** The Hjulström diagram (velocity vs particle size erosion, transport, and deposition), dissolved load vs suspended load vs rolling/sliding bedload, saltation bouncing, and braided river fans.

Source: preserved local roadmap proposal. Its original framing is not a verified finding.

<a id="bank-210-014"></a>
#### BANK-210-014 — Stream Order Classification: Horton-Strahler Ordering from Tiny Brooks to Mighty Rivers

**Video question:** How do hydrologists classify a tiny headwater spring as a 1st-order stream and trace its branching hierarchy until it becomes a 12th-order Amazon?

**Individual coverage scope:** The Strahler stream order mathematical rules (two 1st-order streams meet to form a 2nd-order; two 2nd-order form a 3rd), dendritic drainage basin patterns, and the River Continuum Concept.
