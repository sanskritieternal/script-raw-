## BANK-173-042 - 1950 U.S. census release

- The [National Archives' 1950 guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/census/1950) says population schedules were released on 1 April 2022 after 72 years and are freely searchable, alongside enumeration district maps and descriptions. Its [official search site](https://1950census.archives.gov/) offers image access, name search and transcription correction.
- **Claim limit:** Indexing or a name match is a finding aid, not proof of a family relationship. Consult the schedule image, household context, enumeration district and corroborating records; protect living relatives when connecting the entries to a contemporary tree.
- **Overlap:** BANK-173-011 covers the access-rule mechanism and BANK-173-034 general census interpretation; this case is the dated release and discovery process.

## BANK-173-043 - 23andMe bankruptcy and custody

- The [FTC's 31 March 2025 letter](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/23andme-letter-ferguson.pdf) raised concerns about a possible transfer of sensitive customer information and reminded the court of privacy promises. The company's [30 June 2025 announcement](https://investors.23andme.com/news-releases/news-release-details/23andme-receives-court-approval-sale-ttam-research-institute/) says the court approved a sale of substantially all assets to TTAM, subject then to closing conditions. Its [July 2025 SEC filing](https://investors.23andme.com/static-files/c086ab99-9b49-4062-b39f-c1fde20699b4) reports consummation of the transaction on 14 July 2025.
- **Claim limit:** Distinguish prospective privacy concern, stated buyer commitments, court approval and closing. These records do not independently prove every customer's data disposition, continued consent, compliance or future ownership. Recheck current account controls and jurisdiction before advising an individual; do not give blanket legal/privacy reassurance.
- **Overlap:** BANK-173-028 covers ancestry-industry economics and BANK-173-041 family-archive access; the new case is a dated consumer-genomics custody event.

## BANK-173-044 - Francis Lupo identification

- A [2006 U.S. Army account](https://www.army.mil/article/433/1st_division_soldier_identified_laid_to_rest) names Pvt. Francis Lupo, missing since July 1918, and describes the discovery of remains in 2003, subsequent identification work and 2006 burial. It is a public affairs account, not the laboratory or service dossier.
- **Claim limit:** The inspected source does not state that forensic genetic genealogy or a particular DNA technique made this identification. Obtain the underlying identification report before claiming a method. The archived generic WWI-heroes premise cannot be generalized from this one case, and relatives' privacy should be respected.
- **Overlap:** BANK-173-021 covers military pension files and BANK-173-038 DNA relationship interpretation; this case concerns a named military identification and its evidence chain.
