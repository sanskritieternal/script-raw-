# Selected institutional source note — currency

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-312-041`. This is a bounded 2002 euro-area cash-history premise, not current currency-exchange advice.

- [European Central Bank, 2002 initial cash changeover](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/2002/html/index.en.html): reports banknotes and coins introduced in 12 countries on 1 January 2002, pre-distribution to banks/retailers from September 2001, dual circulation and sole euro legal-tender status by 1 March 2002. It is an institutional summary with euro-area aggregates.
- [ECB press release, 28 February 2002](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2002/html/pr020228.en.html): contemporaneous statement on the end of dual circulation and the then-applicable exchange arrangements, which varied by national central bank and denomination. This dates the stated rules; it is not a present-day guide.
- [ECB, *Evaluation of the 2002 cash changeover* (April 2002)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/other/cashchangeoverreport2002en.pdf): an institutional assessment of planning and execution. Its aggregate success measures cannot establish every household's experience or explain price changes without separate evidence.

The public ECB pages and report were accessible. Verify country-level legal dates, rounding and conversion records before narration. The euro had already been introduced for noncash use before physical notes and coins; do not treat 2002 as creation of every monetary function. This belongs in currency transition in 312, while monetary origins in 295 and central-bank policy in 298 are separate questions.
