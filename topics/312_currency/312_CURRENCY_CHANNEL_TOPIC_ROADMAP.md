## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Currency across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Currency Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Currency.
- **Tier 2: Currency Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Currency.
- **Tier 3: Currency Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Currency.
- **Tier 4: Currency Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Currency.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Currency: How a Currency Was Established**
   - *Question:* Which authority and rules created one named currency?
   - *Key Focus:* Read founding law, issue records, and contemporary exchange practice. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
2. **Currency: A Currency Reform in Context**
   - *Question:* What did one famous redenomination or currency change accomplish?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare stated aims, transition terms, and measured outcomes. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
3. **Currency: Who Could Not Use Official Currency?**
   - *Question:* Whose payment practices were excluded from the official account?
   - *Key Focus:* Use denominations, geography, and local testimony. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
4. **Currency: , Cash, and Bank Money**
   - *Question:* How does a banknote differ from a bank deposit in one jurisdiction?
   - *Key Focus:* Define issuer, claim, legal status, and use. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
5. **Currency: Nominal and Real Exchange Rates**
   - *Question:* Why can a currency move in price without the same change in purchasing power?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a dated two-country example and consistent price measures. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Currency: An Exchange-Rate Move Explained**
   - *Question:* Which causes of one currency move remain uncertain?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare policy, trade, capital flows, and contemporaneous data. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
7. **Currency: A New Currency Form**
   - *Question:* Which practical payment problem did one currency design address?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare security, access, durability, and cost after adoption. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
8. **Currency: The Decision to Change Currency**
   - *Question:* Who had legal authority over one currency decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace official minutes, statutes, and affected groups. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
9. **Currency: A Currency Conversion Timeline**
   - *Question:* What happened during one dated changeover?
   - *Key Focus:* Map announcement, conversion rule, circulation, and withdrawal. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
10. **Currency: Paying Across Currencies**
   - *Question:* How does a payment convert between two currencies?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace quoted rate, spread, fees, settlement, and timing. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Currency: A Currency's Loss of Acceptance**
   - *Question:* What evidence shows a currency losing practical use?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare prices, payment records, and policy actions. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
12. **Currency: A Currency Across Two Regimes**
   - *Question:* How did its issuance or exchange rule change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare law, monetary operations, and household use. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
13. **Currency: A Conditional Currency Proposal**
   - *Question:* Which legal and technical requirements would govern a proposed form?
   - *Key Focus:* State assumptions and avoid claiming adoption is certain. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
14. **Currency: Testing a Currency Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a popular claim about backing or value match official terms?
   - *Key Focus:* Check legal redemption, reserves, and actual practice. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
15. **Currency: One Banknote's Journey**
   - *Question:* What can an issued note reveal about a monetary transition?
   - *Key Focus:* Use design, serial, issue, and withdrawal records. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Currency: Terms of Conversion**
   - *Question:* Which official conversion rule determined the value of old units?
   - *Key Focus:* Read dated legal notices and actual exchange procedures. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
17. **Currency: Change at the Household Level**
   - *Question:* How did a changeover affect a defined household's payments?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a labeled example with prices and wages. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
18. **Currency: Timing a Cross-Currency Payment**
   - *Question:* Which risks arise between agreeing and settling a foreign-currency payment?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate rate, settlement, and counterparty exposure. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
19. **Currency: Exchange Rate Versus Conversion Cost**
   - *Question:* How much of a conversion cost comes from spread and explicit fees?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated quoted rates and transparent arithmetic. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
20. **Currency: Rights During a Currency Change**
   - *Question:* Which exchange rights or deadlines applied to holders?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify authority, jurisdiction, and dates. Evidence base: dated issuer notices, monetary law, exchange rates, price data, provider terms, and payment records.
