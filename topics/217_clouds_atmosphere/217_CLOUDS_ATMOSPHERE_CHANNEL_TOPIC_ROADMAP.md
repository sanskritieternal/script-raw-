## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Clouds & Atmosphere: How Clouds Became Classified**
   - *Question:* Which observation rules made cloud descriptions comparable?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace historical atlases, names, photographs, and revisions. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
2. **Clouds & Atmosphere: An Unusual Cloud Rechecked**
   - *Question:* What evidence supports a claimed rare cloud form?
   - *Key Focus:* Check image time, location, viewing angle, WMO criteria, and weather setting. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
3. **Clouds & Atmosphere: The Observers Behind Cloud Records**
   - *Question:* Whose work turns sky views into consistent data?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace surface observers, satellite teams, pilots, forecasters, and archivists. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
4. **Clouds & Atmosphere: Reading a Cloud Layer**
   - *Question:* Which features identify one cloud genus and what cannot be inferred?
   - *Key Focus:* Use official criteria, height, texture, precipitation, and uncertainty. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
5. **Clouds & Atmosphere: Clouds and Radiation**
   - *Question:* How can a cloud both reflect sunlight and trap outgoing heat?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare height, thickness, particle type, day or night, and measured flux. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.

6. **Clouds & Atmosphere: Why One Cloud Layer Persisted**

8. **Clouds & Atmosphere: A Cloud Classification Choice**

9. **Clouds & Atmosphere: Clouds During a Weather Event**
   - *Question:* How did cloud structure evolve before one storm?
   - *Key Focus:* Match satellite, radar, surface observations, and timing. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
10. **Clouds & Atmosphere: From Vapor to Droplets**
   - *Question:* Which conditions cause a cloud to form and persist?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace cooling, saturation, aerosols, mixing, and measured evidence. Evidence base: WMO cloud classification, calibrated surface and satellite observations, radar and lidar records, weather archives, and peer-reviewed field or model studies.
