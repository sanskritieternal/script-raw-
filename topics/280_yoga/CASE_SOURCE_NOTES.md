The description lists postures, meditation, breath, chant and transmission settings; these are the nomination's representation, not universal characteristics of every practitioner.

The decision documents a heritage-list process; it does not test health outcomes, settle historical origins or certify any teaching program.

The live UNESCO pages were accessible on the inspection date. Compare the nomination dossier, consent documentation and diverse community accounts before interpreting representation.
