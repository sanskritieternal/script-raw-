# Selected primary-source checks - Festivals around the world
Checked 2026-10-24. These are proposed documentary questions, not current event access or filming permissions.

- BANK-244-042: Edinburgh Festival Fringe Society 2024 review, https://www.edfringe.com/about-us/news-and-blog/weve-published-our-review-of-the-year-2024/ , and Society financial statements, https://www.edfringe.com/media/nbflgmkf/effs-financial-statements-2024-v5-compressed.pdf . The Society reports 3,746 shows across 262 venues and ticketing service counts. These are organizer records, not independent verification of all artists' work, unique visitors or a future year's capacity. The 1947/1958 founding history is BANK-245-042, not this operational case.
- BANK-244-043: Burning Man Project 2023 after-action account, https://journal.burningman.org/2023-afterburn/ , and BLM land-use/environmental review, https://eplanning.blm.gov/public_projects/nepa/93518/175007/212561/Burning_Man_Event_SRP-Final_EIS_Vol1.pdf . Organizer account documents gate and information changes during rain; it is a participant institution's perspective and must not be generalized to every person's experience or future emergency guidance.
- BANK-244-044: Ethiopia-submitted UNESCO Timkat heritage record, https://f5vip11.unesco.org/en/RL/ethiopian-epiphany-01491 , and nomination form, https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/39370.pdf . They describe procession, clergy and community roles; inscription does not make practice uniform across Ethiopian communities or confer filming permission. Sacred objects and restricted rites require community authority and context.
- Overlap: BANK-243-030 concerns traditions across generations, BANK-240-031 sacred landscape, and BANK-245-041/-042 historical beginnings. This bank asks about a dated public festival organization and representation. No fabricated local or worker voices.
