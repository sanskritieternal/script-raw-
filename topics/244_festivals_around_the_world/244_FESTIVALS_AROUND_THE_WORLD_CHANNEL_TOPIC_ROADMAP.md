- **Tier 2: Festivals Around the World Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Festivals Around the World.

- **Tier 4: Festivals Around the World Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Festivals Around the World.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Festivals Around the World: One Festival's Documented Beginning**
   - *Question:* What do local sources establish about its early form?
   - *Key Focus:* Name community and distinguish legend from dated evidence. Evidence base: dated programs and organizer records, participant-approved interviews, local archives, attendance methods, and relevant permissions.
2. **Festivals Around the World: A Festival Beyond the Spectacle**
   - *Question:* What does a famous event mean to participants?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare public parade with preparation, ritual, and local accounts. Evidence base: dated programs and organizer records, participant-approved interviews, local archives, attendance methods, and relevant permissions.
3. **Festivals Around the World: Who Makes a Festival Possible**
   - *Question:* Which organizers, makers, and cleaners are overlooked?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace labor, payment, volunteers, and permissions. Evidence base: dated programs and organizer records, participant-approved interviews, local archives, attendance methods, and relevant permissions.
4. **Festivals Around the World: Reading a Festival in Context**
   - *Question:* Who hosts it, when, where, and for whom?
   - *Key Focus:* Use organizer and community explanations, not tourist summaries alone. Evidence base: dated programs and organizer records, participant-approved interviews, local archives, attendance methods, and relevant permissions.
5. **Festivals Around the World: Festival, Ritual, and Tourism**
   - *Question:* How do participant and visitor purposes intersect?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare spaces, access rules, commercialization, and governance. Evidence base: dated programs and organizer records, participant-approved interviews, local archives, attendance methods, and relevant permissions.
