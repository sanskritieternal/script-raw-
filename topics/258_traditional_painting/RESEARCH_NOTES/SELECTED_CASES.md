## BANK-258-042 — Madhubani geographical indication

- [Intellectual Property India's application record 37](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/37) lists Madhubani Paintings as a registered handicraft GI, with a 2007 certificate and later renewal documents. This is primary registry evidence of a product designation, not authentication of every work sold under the name.
- Before scripting, inspect the certificate, specification and renewal documents, producer eligibility and current status. The current registry page displays multiple dates; one page view alone is insufficient for precise present validity. Seek makers' accounts of production, pricing and attribution. This question concerns designation rules, distinct from BANK-258-038's wall-to-portable-art history or BANK-258-035's single-work authentication.

## BANK-258-043 — Nainsukh and Majnu attribution

- The [Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art object page](https://asia.si.edu/interactives/dutta/S2018_1_61.html) attributes a painting of Lakshmi and Vishnu to joint work and reports a Takri inscription on the reverse naming Nainsukh and Majnu. Its interpretation assigns composition and painting roles with a stated degree of likelihood.
- This supports a case about how an inscription and museum interpretation bear on workshop attribution. The reverse text and its reading, object condition, provenance and comparative scholarship need inspection before asserting exactly who did which stroke. The question is a named evidence test within BANK-258-040's broader workshop method and BANK-258-041's shared-credit issue, with no new claim that all Pahari work was collaborative.
