## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Traditional Painting across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Traditional Painting Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Traditional Painting.
- **Tier 2: Traditional Painting Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Traditional Painting.
- **Tier 3: Traditional Painting Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Traditional Painting.
- **Tier 4: Traditional Painting Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Traditional Painting.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Traditional Painting: A Painting Tradition's Early Record**
   - *Question:* What dated works document a named local form?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate earliest surviving object from absolute origin. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
2. **Traditional Painting: A Celebrated Painting in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one work reveal and what is exceptional?
   - *Key Focus:* Check maker, purpose, audience, technique, and provenance. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
3. **Traditional Painting: Painters and Preparers**
   - *Question:* Whose grinding, surface making, teaching, and repair are omitted?
   - *Key Focus:* Credit collaborative labor and consented accounts. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
4. **Traditional Painting: Reading One Painting Form**
   - *Question:* How do surface, pigment, motif, and use fit together?
   - *Key Focus:* Name community and setting; ask practitioners about meaning. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
5. **Traditional Painting: Variation Within a Tradition**
   - *Question:* How do painters change form without losing continuity?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated works and maker explanations. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Traditional Painting: An Uncertain Attribution**
   - *Question:* Which evidence might identify the painter or workshop?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare provenance, materials, style, inscriptions, and limits. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
7. **Traditional Painting: A Material or Reproduction Shift**
   - *Question:* How did a new pigment, surface, or print process alter work?
   - *Key Focus:* Test cost, durability, access, and maker control. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
8. **Traditional Painting: A Painter-Teacher's Contribution**
   - *Question:* How did one artist pass on techniques?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace students and work within the wider community. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
9. **Traditional Painting: A Collection or Market Turning Point**
   - *Question:* How did one documented event change making or ownership?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated sales, exhibitions, laws, and maker accounts. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
10. **Traditional Painting: From Surface to Finished Work**
   - *Question:* Which choices shape one painting?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe preparation, drawing, pigment, layering, and finishing with consent. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Traditional Painting: Demand for a Painting Form**
   - *Question:* What explains a measured shift in production?
   - *Key Focus:* Check commissions, materials, markets, and survey coverage. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
12. **Traditional Painting: A Form Across Generations**
   - *Question:* How did technique, patronage, and subject change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare provenanced works and practitioner views. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
13. **Traditional Painting: Supporting Living Painters**
   - *Question:* Which resources do makers prioritize?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare training, materials, fair pay, rights, and display. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
14. **Traditional Painting: A Single Authentic Style?**
   - *Question:* What documented variations exist among makers and periods?
   - *Key Focus:* Avoid ranking one market-approved version as true. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
15. **Traditional Painting: One Painting's Journey**
   - *Question:* How was it made, sold, displayed, or returned?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace custody and consented maker and owner accounts. Evidence base: provenanced dated works, material examination, maker records, conservation reports, and consented practitioner and audience accounts.
