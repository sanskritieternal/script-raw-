## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Then vs Now across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Historical Baseline Setting, Visual Retrospectives & Primary Metric Tracking (Beginner / Scratch)** — Archival photo matching and spatial rephotography, inflation-adjusted cost comparisons, primary demographic records (life expectancy, infant mortality, literacy), and baseline historical methodology.
- **Tier 2: Daily Life, Domestic Technologies & Workplace Ergonomics Across Decades (Intermediate)** — Home appliances freeing domestic labor hours (refrigeration, washing machines), changes in workplace safety and 40-hour workweeks, food preservation and nutritional diversity, and personal transport evolutions.
- **Tier 3: Urban Morphologies, Industrial Transformations & Information Consumption Shifts (Upper Intermediate)** — De-industrialization and urban renewal of major riverfronts, from landline party-lines to ubiquitous 5G smartphones, the collapse of local newspapers into social algorithm echo chambers, and consumer retail shifts.
- **Tier 4: Generational Paradigm Shifts, Long-Wave Kondratiev Cycles & Future Trajectory Extrapolation (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Tracking long technological innovation waves (steam, electricity, semiconductors, AI), housing affordability-to-income multiples across generations, shifting definitions of privacy, and synthesizing centenary historical arcs.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **London 1924 vs London 2024: How One Century Re-engineered a Global Megacity**
   - *Question:* What happens when you compare the same street corners of the British capital across exactly 100 years of technological, social, and architectural evolution?
   - *Key Focus:* Pea-souper coal fogs vs Ultra Low Emission Zones (ULEZ), dockyard industrial warehouses turned into Canary Wharf financial skyscrapers, horse carts to electric buses, and population demographics.
2. **The 1950s Kitchen vs Today: The Technology That Freed 30 Hours of Human Labor a Week**
   - *Question:* How did automated washing machines, frost-free refrigerators, and microwave ovens quietly revolutionize gender equality and domestic life?
   - *Key Focus:* Pre-war manual laundry (mangle wringers, coal boilers taking 12 hours), the mass electrification of domestic appliances, food storage shelf life, and the economic entry of women into the paid workforce.
3. **Flying in 1970 vs Flying Today: How Air Travel Traded Caviar and Legroom for Democratization**
   - *Question:* Why was the 'Golden Age of Flying' actually an unaffordable luxury that cost an entire month's salary for an ordinary worker?
   - *Key Focus:* The Airline Deregulation Act of 1978, smoking on planes, crash fatality rates falling by 95% today, seat pitch compression, and budget airlines democratizing global travel for billions.
4. **How We Bought Things: Sears Catalogs to Next-Day Amazon Prime Delivery**
   - *Question:* How did mail-order paper catalogs that sold complete kit houses evolve into robotic fulfillment centers that deliver goods within hours?
   - *Key Focus:* The 1890s Sears, Roebuck & Co. postal logistics revolution, the suburban shopping mall golden age, barcodes and automated warehouse picking robots, and the death of department stores.
5. **Childhood in 1980 vs Childhood Today: Street Freedom vs The Screen Age**
   - *Question:* Why did 1980s children roam miles away on bicycles until streetlights came on, while modern children spend 7 hours a day on digital screens?
   - *Key Focus:* Parental surveillance and stranger danger perceptions vs declining violent crime rates, organized extracurricular scheduling, playground safety litigation, and the mental health impacts of smartphones.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Office Desk: How 40 Physical Objects Were Swallowed by a Single Glass Screen**
   - *Question:* Where did the Rolodex, typewriter, filing cabinets, fax machine, tape recorder, and encyclopedia go?
   - *Key Focus:* Step-by-step dematerialization of the physical office desk from 1980 to 2024, software applications replacing hardware tools, open office floor plans, and remote telecommuting culture.
7. **Automobile Safety: Crashing a 1959 Chevrolet Bel Air into a 2009 Chevrolet Malibu**
   - *Question:* Why did heavy, solid steel vintage cars kill their passengers in crashes, while modern plastic-bumpered cars crumple to pieces and let drivers walk away?
   - *Key Focus:* IIHS 50th anniversary offset crash test, rigid steering column impalement vs collapsible steering wheels, rigid passenger safety cells with sacrificial crumple zones, seatbelt physics, and modern curtain airbags.
8. **How Music Was Consumed: Wax Cylinders, Vinyl, Cassettes, CDs, and MP3 Streaming**
   - *Question:* How did music transition from an ephemeral live event to a physical physical disc in a store, and finally to 100 million songs floating invisibly in the cloud?
   - *Key Focus:* Thomas Edison's phonograph cylinders, 33 1/3 RPM vinyl microgrooves, Phillips compact cassettes creating portable walkman personal audio, CD digital optical audio, MP3 compression, and streaming royalty economics.
9. **Housing Affordability: Why One Blue-Collar Income Bought a Home in 1960 That Requires Three Incomes Today**
   - *Question:* Did your grandparents have it easier: what does the ratio of median home prices to median household incomes reveal over the past 60 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Price-to-income multiples (2.5x in 1960 vs 6.5x+ today), square footage expansion, zoning laws and suburban land artificial scarcity, double-income household baseline shifts, and mortgage interest rate histories.
10. **The News: From Walter Cronkite's 30 Minutes to the 24/7 Algorithmic Rage Machine**
   - *Question:* Why did entire nations trust three nightly news anchors in 1970, and how did cable news and social media fracture consensus reality?
   - *Key Focus:* The FCC Fairness Doctrine repeal (1987), CNN and the Gulf War 24-hour cable cycle, advertising incentives shifting from broad credibility to hyper-partisan tribal engagement, and clickbait economics.
### Tier 3: Advanced Methodologies & Frameworks
11. **Medicine in 1924 vs Medicine Today: When an Infected Scratch Was a Death Sentence**
   - *Question:* What was a hospital visit like before antibiotics, MRI scanners, blood typing, and sterile disposable syringes existed?
   - *Key Focus:* Pre-antibiotic mortality from simple streptococcus and dental abscesses, open-air tuberculosis sanatoriums, modern robotic laparoscopic surgery, survival rates of childhood leukemia (5% in 1950 vs 90% today), and medical longevity.
12. **How Hollywood Made Movies: Hand-Painted Glass Matte Paintings to 100% CGI Virtual Sets**
   - *Question:* How did classic films create alien planets and grand castles using optical glass and paint, and why does modern CGI sometimes feel less authentic?
   - *Key Focus:* Practical optical compositing printers, hand-drawn rotoscoping, miniature pyrotechnics, the Jurassic Park digital CGI watershed, and modern LED volume virtual production (The Mandalorian).
13. **Farming: 100 Farmers Feeding 100 People vs 1 Farmer Feeding 150 People Today**
   - *Question:* How did agriculture shift from employing 60% of the entire human workforce in 1900 to less than 2% in developed nations today?
   - *Key Focus:* Horse-drawn plows to GPS-guided autonomous 500-horsepower combines, the Haber-Bosch synthetic nitrogen process, Norman Borlaug's Green Revolution dwarf wheat genetics, and precision satellite yield mapping.
14. **Privacy: From Small-Town Gossip to Planetary Digital Surveillance Capitalism**
   - *Question:* Did humans ever really have privacy, or did we trade intimate neighborhood gossip for faceless corporate ad-tracking algorithms?
   - *Key Focus:* Victorian village social surveillance, 20th-century anonymous urban freedom, digital breadcrumbs (cookies, GPS location telemetry, facial recognition), and Shoshana Zuboff's Surveillance Capitalism thesis.
15. **Tokyo 1945 vs Tokyo 2024: Rising from Ash to the World's Cleanest Megacity**
   - *Question:* How did a metropolis flattened by firebombing in World War II rebuild itself within three decades into an economic powerhouse with the world's most punctual railway system?
   - *Key Focus:* Post-war wooden shantytowns, 1964 Tokyo Olympics infrastructure modernization, the Shinkansen bullet train launch, seismic zoning innovation, and maintaining safe, affordable urban density.
16. **Dating and Romance: Calling the Family Landline vs Swiping on Tinder**
   - *Question:* How did courtship evolve from supervised porch visits and calling a prospective partner's father to an algorithmic supermarket of infinite digital choices?
   - *Key Focus:* Calling a family landline and overcoming social gatekeepers, local social circles vs hypergamy on dating apps, paradox of choice, declining marriage rates, and the modern loneliness epidemic.
### Tier 4: Enterprise Strategy & Deep Mastery
17. **Manufacturing: From Thousands of Men in Steel Mills to Three Engineers in Automated Plants**
   - *Question:* What happens when physical industrial manufacturing output hits record all-time highs while factory floor employment drops to historical lows?
   - *Key Focus:* 1940s Pittsburgh open-hearth steel mills employing 30,000 workers, modern electric arc mini-mills, FANUC and KUKA six-axis industrial robotics, lights-out manufacturing, and labor productivity curves.
18. **The Extinction of Boredom: What Constant Digital Stimulation Did to the Human Attention Span**
   - *Question:* What happened to the empty, quiet moments of waiting in line, sitting on buses, and daydreaming without a dopamine-slot-machine in our pockets?
   - *Key Focus:* Default Mode Network (DMN) brain activation during daydreaming and creativity, dopamine loop micro-rewards, continuous partial attention, and the neurobiology of chronic digital overstimulation.
19. **The True Cost of Living: What a Dollar Bought in 1924, 1974, and Today**
   - *Question:* When inflation is accounted for, why did manufactured goods (TVs, clothes, toys) become dirt cheap while life essentials (housing, college, healthcare) skyrocketed?
   - *Key Focus:* The Consumer Price Index (CPI) basket evolution, Baumol's cost disease in non-automatable services, globalization deflation of manufactured goods, and real median wage purchasing power stagnation.
20. **The Masterclass on Historical Analysis: How to Dissect 'Then vs Now' Without Rose-Tinted Glasses**
   - *Question:* How do elite historians and cultural analysts separate nostalgic mythologizing from verifiable historical facts across generations?
   - *Key Focus:* Survivorship bias in historical artifacts (only the best buildings and furniture survived), adjusting for inflation and purchasing power, examining marginalized perspectives omitted from old archives, and synthesizing long-term progress metrics.
