# Then-versus-now case-source notes (2026-10-24)

Selected primary records inspected for proposed comparisons. A before-and-after pair is a research frame, not proof of one cause or a population trend.

| Case | Primary record | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Sears Modern Homes | [Smithsonian collection record of Sears's 1913 catalog](https://collections.si.edu/search/results.htm?q=%22Sears%2C+Roebuck+and+Co.%22) and [Smithsonian Libraries' 1930 catalog discussion](https://blog.library.si.edu/blog/2010/12/02/1930s-diy/) establish actual catalog artifacts. | Catalog list prices omit land, labor, finance and later changes; a surviving house is not automatically authenticated as a specific Sears model. Archived $1,500 and 115-year claims remain unverified. |
| Grocery prices | [US Bureau of Labor Statistics average-price methods](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/average-prices.htm) and [published selected series](https://www.bls.gov/charts/consumer-price-index/consumer-price-index-average-price-data.htm) support a defined-item comparison. | Average prices are not an exact receipt for the same 20 products. Sample rotation, item definitions, missing observations, packaging and location can change results; CPI indexes answer a different question. |
| Dubai's change | [Dubai Statistics Center 2024 population bulletin](https://www.dsc.gov.ae/Publication/Population%20Bulletin%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai%20-%202024.pdf) and [Dubai government urban-plan account](https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/mohammed-bin-rashid-launches-dubai-2040-urban-master-plan/) supply dated definitions and plans. | Historical and current boundary/estimate methods need matching. Official planning descriptions do not prove a single cause of growth; the archived “quiet desert creek” narrative erases existing communities and commerce. |
