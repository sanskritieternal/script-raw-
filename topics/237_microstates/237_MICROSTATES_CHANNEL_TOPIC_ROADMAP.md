## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Microstates: A Small State's Political Formation**
   - *Question:* Which dated acts shaped one sovereign small state's institutions?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace constitution, independence or continuity, and external agreements. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
2. **Microstates: A Small State's Governance Choice**
   - *Question:* What can one celebrated institutional example actually show?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure capacity, costs, services, and conditions of transferability. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
3. **Microstates: Public Workers in a Small State**
   - *Question:* Whose work sustains a service often described only through leaders?
   - *Key Focus:* Use staffing records and consented worker and resident accounts. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
4. **Microstates: What Counts as a Microstate?**
   - *Question:* Which explicit population or area rule defines the comparison?
   - *Key Focus:* State operational definition, date, and why other small-state groupings differ. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
5. **Microstates: Scale and State Capacity**
   - *Question:* Where does small population change the cost of providing a public function?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare fixed costs, workforce, shared arrangements, and service quality. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Microstates: A Surprising Small-State Outcome**
   - *Question:* Why did one small state outperform or lag a comparator on a measure?
   - *Key Focus:* Test data quality, geography, income, institutions, and time. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
7. **Microstates: Digital Government at Small Scale**
   - *Question:* Did one digital system improve a small state's service delivery?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare implementation cost, access, cybersecurity, and maintenance. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
8. **Microstates: A Small-State Negotiator**
   - *Question:* What did a named leader achieve in one international negotiation?
   - *Key Focus:* Use official records and distinguish personal role from institutional work. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
9. **Microstates: A Shock to a Small State**
   - *Question:* How did one dated event affect budget and service capacity?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare exposure, response, external support, and recovery evidence. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
10. **Microstates: Providing a Specialized Service**
   - *Question:* How does a small state deliver a service it cannot staff entirely at home?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace local capacity, cross-border agreement, costs, and resident access. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Microstates: One Industry Grows and Contracts**
   - *Question:* How did dependence on one sector affect a small state's finances?
   - *Key Focus:* Use long-run output, employment, fiscal, and external data. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
12. **Microstates: Public Services Across Two Periods**
   - *Question:* Did access to one service improve as population or revenue changed?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable definitions, budgets, and outcomes. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
13. **Microstates: A Diversification Proposal**
   - *Question:* What capacities and risks would one public proposal need to address?
   - *Key Focus:* State assumptions rather than predicting growth. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
14. **Microstates: Small Does Not Mean Uniform**
   - *Question:* Does a popular claim hold across small states with different geography?
   - *Key Focus:* Define population threshold and compare like measures. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
15. **Microstates: A Small State's Policy Record**
   - *Question:* How did one policy move from proposal to measurable result?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow parliamentary record, budget, implementation, and evaluation. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Microstates: Using a Small-State Service**
   - *Question:* What does a resident's account reveal about access and informal networks?
   - *Key Focus:* Pair consented testimony with service-wide evidence. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
17. **Microstates: Few Agencies, Many Roles**
   - *Question:* How do institutions divide responsibilities for one policy?
   - *Key Focus:* Map legal mandates, shared staff, contractors, and oversight. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
18. **Microstates: Close Networks and Accountability**
   - *Question:* How did social proximity affect one documented public decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Use public records and multiple perspectives; avoid stereotypes. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
19. **Microstates: Who Falls Outside a Small Service System?**
   - *Question:* Which residents face distance, cost, language, or eligibility barriers?
   - *Key Focus:* Use disaggregated data and named service rules. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
20. **Microstates: Main Island and Outer Areas**
   - *Question:* How does service access differ across a state's settlements?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare travel, connectivity, staffing, and demand where relevant. Evidence base: constitutions, treaties, national budgets and statistics, audits, multilateral records, and consented resident accounts.
