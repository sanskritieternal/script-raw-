# Selected primary-source note — rocket engineering

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-423-046`.

- [NASA, *SLS Core Stage Green Run Tests Critical Systems for Artemis I*](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-sls-core-stage-green-run-tests-critical-systems-for-artemis-i/): dated pretest agency description lays out modal, avionics, fail-safe, propulsion, thrust-vector, countdown, wet-dress and hot-fire checks. These are planned test objectives, not results.
- [NASA Office of Inspector General, *Artemis Status Update*, IG-21-018 (2021)](https://oig.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ig-21-018.pdf): independent agency oversight reports that the initial January 2021 hot fire ended after 67 seconds rather than the planned eight minutes. It documents the status at its report date, not the final outcome.
- [NASA, *Artemis I Core Stage Being Readied for Shipment to Kennedy* (13 April 2021)](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/missions/2021/04/13/artemis-i-core-stage-being-readied-for-shipment-to-kennedy/): NASA reports review and inspection after the second, full-duration hot fire. [NASA Artemis I mission timeline](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/artemis-i-mission-timeline/) records the 16 November 2022 launch and timed ascent events.

Ground-run success supports core-stage test objectives under test-stand conditions; integrated flight adds different evidence and does not certify every later mission or subsystem. Agency summaries and OIG oversight have different dates and purposes. This is a historical evidence comparison, with no hazardous test, propellant or launch procedure. NASA image rights should be checked individually.
