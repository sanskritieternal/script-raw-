## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Knitting across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Knitting Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Knitting.
- **Tier 2: Knitting Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Knitting.
- **Tier 3: Knitting Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Knitting.
- **Tier 4: Knitting Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Knitting.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Knitting: A Knitted Object in Context**
   - *Question:* Which surviving garment documents a specific knitting technique?
   - *Key Focus:* Check fiber, dating, provenance, and later repair. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
2. **Knitting: A Knitted Fabric Examined**
   - *Question:* How do loop structure and yarn choice shape an object's use?
   - *Key Focus:* Study gauge, fiber, wear, and intended function. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
3. **Knitting: Knitters behind the Pattern**
   - *Question:* Whose design, sample knitting, spinning, or finishing work is recorded?
   - *Key Focus:* Use labels, archives, and maker accounts. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
4. **Knitting: A Loop Becomes Fabric**
   - *Question:* How do stitch, row, gauge, and yarn form a piece?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain one bounded sample and measured size. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
5. **Knitting: Drape and Stretch**
   - *Question:* How do stitch structure and fiber alter recovery or warmth?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare controlled swatches and use conditions. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Knitting: A Pattern with Unclear Origin**
   - *Question:* Which archive or object supports an attribution?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate traditional motif from named modern design. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
7. **Knitting: A Knitting Machine's Impact**
   - *Question:* Which task or fabric structure changed with a documented machine?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated objects and labor. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
8. **Knitting: A Designer's Swatch Revision**
   - *Question:* What test led to changed stitch count or fiber?
   - *Key Focus:* Use versioned swatches and maker notes. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
9. **Knitting: A Pattern Enters Print**
   - *Question:* What changed when instructions moved from teaching to publication?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace editions, charts, and readership. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
10. **Knitting: From Gauge to Garment**
   - *Question:* How do swatch, measurements, shaping, and finishing connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow one documented pattern and sample. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Knitting: A Knitting Technique Revisited**
   - *Question:* Why did a stitch or garment form become less common or return?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated collections and maker context. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
12. **Knitting: An Old Pattern Reworked**
   - *Question:* Which yarn, sizing, and notation changes affect a modern version?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare original object or text with remake. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
13. **Knitting: A New Fiber Blend**
   - *Question:* Which care and durability claims need testing?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure washed and worn swatches. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
14. **Knitting: One Gauge Fits Every Yarn?**
   - *Question:* How do tension, fiber, needle, and washing change dimensions?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare measured swatches. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
15. **Knitting: One Garment's Making**
   - *Question:* How did design, sampling, knitting, and blocking evolve?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated maker artifacts. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Knitting: What the Knit Must Do**
   - *Question:* Which warmth, fit, movement, and care requirements shaped a design?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare brief with actual wearer use. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.

   - *Question:* How do fiber, thickness, and tool size affect the fabric?
   - *Key Focus:* Measure gauge and behavior rather than label alone. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
18. **Knitting: A Swatch Changes the Pattern**
   - *Question:* Which measurement or wash result prompted revision?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare before-and-after samples. Evidence base: dated knitted objects and patterns, measured swatches, yarn specifications, maker records, V&A textile collections, and conservation reports.
19. **Knitting: Motif across a Form**
   - *Question:* How is a repeat placed around shaping and seams?
