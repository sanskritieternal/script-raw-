# Selected primary-source research: repair-access policy

Checked 2026-10-24. These sources bound two policy cases only. They do not verify the archived technical percentages, component failure diagnoses, alleged illegality or safety procedures.

## Apple’s April 2024 used-parts announcement — BANK-137-045

- Primary manufacturer source: Apple, [11 April 2024 announcement on used genuine parts](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2024/04/apple-to-expand-repair-options-with-support-for-used-genuine-parts/) and its [June 2024 Longevity by Design report](https://www.apple.com/environment/pdf/Longevity_by_Design-June_2024-Apple.pdf).
- Apple announced a process, beginning with selected iPhone models later in 2024, for calibration of new or used genuine parts on device. It also described Parts and Service History and an Activation Lock restriction for parts associated with lost or stolen devices.
- Limit: These are Apple’s dated descriptions of its own products and rationale. Announced support was model, part, software and region dependent; check the exact device and implementation date before scripting a real swap. The sources do not establish that every pair of identical screens disables Face ID or True Tone, or that swapping parts is illegal. Security and environmental claims require independent outcome evidence if quantified. No repair procedure is provided here.

## FTC warranty warning letters, 2018 — BANK-137-046

- Primary regulator source: Federal Trade Commission, [10 April 2018 announcement of warning letters](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/04/ftc-staff-warns-companies-it-illegal-condition-warranty-coverage-use-specified-parts-or-services), [FTC staff explanation](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2018/04/ftc-staff-sends-warranty-warnings), and [published letter set](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/foia_requests/Warning-Letters.pdf).
- FTC staff warned six U.S. companies that generally conditioning warranty coverage on specified branded parts or authorized service was prohibited unless the company supplied the part/service free or obtained an FTC waiver. The staff also distinguished damage caused by unauthorized repair from blanket loss of warranty coverage.
- Limit: The letters are warnings about cited warranty language, not final findings that every company violated every provision. This is U.S. federal consumer-warranty context, and product terms, damage causation and state law may matter. It is a historical policy case, not individualized legal advice.
