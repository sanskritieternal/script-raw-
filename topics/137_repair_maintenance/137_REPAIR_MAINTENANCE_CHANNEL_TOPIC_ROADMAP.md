## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Repair & Maintenance across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Diagnostic Fundamentals, Hand Tool Mastery & Electrical Safety (Beginner / Scratch)** — Multimeter voltage/continuity testing, proper screwdriver bit sizing (avoiding stripped screws), safe capacitor discharge, mechanical diagnostic trees, and basic lubricant physics (WD-40 vs grease).
- **Tier 2: Small Engine Carburetors, Household Appliances & Soldering Circuits (Intermediate)** — Small 2-stroke/4-stroke carburetor rebuilds, washing machine pump drain unclogging, through-hole and basic surface-mount soldering, copper pipe sweating, and drywall patching.
- **Tier 3: Planned Obsolescence Teardowns, Proprietary Fasteners & Schematics Reverse Engineering (Upper Intermediate)** — Defeating security Torx/pentalobe fasteners, troubleshooting blown electrolytic capacitors on power supply boards, schematic reading, multimeter diode check mode, and thermal paste reapplication.
- **Tier 4: Right-to-Repair Legislation, Component-Level Micro-Soldering & The Master Repair Workshop (Advanced / Masterclass)** — BGA chip reballing and hot-air rework stations, oscilloscope signal tracing, right-to-repair legal precedents (Magnuson-Moss Act), 3D printing custom replacement gears, and workshop tooling ergonomics.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **Planned Obsolescence: How Lightbulb Cartels and Glue Encapsulation Killed Repair**
   - *Question:* How did the 1924 Phoebus Cartel secretly fine manufacturers whose lightbulbs lasted longer than 1,000 hours, creating modern disposable culture?
   - *Key Focus:* The Centennial Light in Livermore (burning since 1901), Phoebus Cartel fines, glued smartphone enclosures, proprietary pentalobe screws, and engineered product lifespans.
2. **The Digital Multimeter Masterclass: Voltage, Resistance, and Continuity Testing**
   - *Question:* How does a $25 multimeter allow you to diagnose 90% of electrical and appliance failures without guessing?
   - *Key Focus:* Continuity beeps for broken traces, AC vs DC voltage safety, resistance testing heating elements, diode checks, and never blowing internal meter fuses.
3. **The Lubricant Lie: WD-40 vs White Lithium Grease vs Silicone Spray**
   - *Question:* Why does spraying standard blue-can WD-40 into door hinges and bicycle chains attract dirt and ruin bearings?
   - *Key Focus:* WD-40 as a solvent water-displacer, penetrating oils (Kroil/PB Blaster) for seized bolts, high-pressure lithium grease, silicone on rubber, and dry PTFE lubricants.
4. **Fasteners Decoded: Why Stripped Screws Happen and How to Extract Them**
   - *Question:* Why do Phillips screwdrivers deliberately 'cam out' and strip Japanese motorcycle and electronics screws?
   - *Key Focus:* JIS (Japanese Industrial Standard) vs Phillips vs Torx vs Pozidriv, screw extractor drill bits, left-handed drill bits, rubber band tricks, and impact drivers.
5. **The Capacitive Death Trap: Safely Discharging Power Supply and Microwave Capacitors**
   - *Question:* Why can an unplugged microwave oven or old CRT TV store 2,000 lethal volts for weeks after being disconnected from the wall?
   - *Key Focus:* Capacitor dielectric energy storage, bleed resistors, building a safe ceramic resistor discharge probe, one-hand rule safety, and high-voltage warnings.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Small Engine Rescue: Cleaning Gunked Carburetors on Lawnmowers and Generators**
   - *Question:* Why does letting ethanol gas sit over the winter plug microscopic carburetor jets, and how do you clean it with a guitar string?
   - *Key Focus:* Ethanol fuel phase separation, needle and seat valves, float bowl gunk, main jet cleaning with wire, ultrasonic carburetor baths, and fuel stabilizers.
7. **Washing Machine Surgery: Clearing Clogged Drain Pumps and Faulty Pressure Switches**
   - *Question:* Why does a front-load washer throw an error code and refuse to spin because of a single baby sock or quarter in the coin trap?
   - *Key Focus:* Drain pump filter cleanout, testing pump impeller motors with multimeters, pressure switch air hoses, door latch interlocks, and replacing drive belts.
8. **Soldering Fundamentals: How to Solder Wires and Replace Blown Circuit Board Components**
   - *Question:* Why does cold solder look dull and break apart, and why is rosin flux the magical secret to perfect shiny joints?
   - *Key Focus:* Lead vs lead-free solder alloys, temperature-controlled irons (650°F-750°F), flux chemistry, desoldering braid/pumps, and through-hole capacitor swaps.
9. **The Capacitor Plague: How Stolen Chemical Formulas Bricked Millions of Motherboards**
   - *Question:* Why did computer motherboards and flat-screen TVs from 1999-2007 bulge, leak brown crust, and die by the millions?
   - *Key Focus:* Industrial espionage theft of incomplete water-based electrolyte formulas, hydrogen gas bulging, ESR (Equivalent Series Resistance) meters, and recapping boards.
10. **Plumbing Rescues: Sweating Copper Pipes, PEX Crimp Rings, and Running Toilets**
   - *Question:* How do you torch-sweat a leak-free copper pipe joint and fix a phantom-flushing toilet in 15 minutes?
   - *Key Focus:* Propane torch soldering, water draining before heat, PEX tubing vs copper pros/cons, toilet flapper valve degradation, and shutoff valve emergencies.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Refrigerator Diagnostics: When It's Warm on Top and Freezing on the Bottom**
   - *Question:* Why does ice buildup on the back freezer wall indicate a failed defrost thermostat rather than a dead compressor?
   - *Key Focus:* Vapor compression refrigeration cycles, defrost heater element testing, bimetal defrost thermostats, evaporator fan motors, and condenser coil cleaning.
12. **Drywall Surgery: Patching Doorknob Holes and Seamless Mud Blending**
   - *Question:* How does the 'California Patch' use the drywall's own paper backing to create an invisible patch without wooden backing studs?
   - *Key Focus:* California drywall patch technique, mesh tape vs paper tape, setting-type hot mud (20-minute) vs premixed joint compound, feathering edges, and sanding flush.
13. **Hot Air Micro-Soldering: Surface Mount (SMD) Rework and USB-C Port Replacement**
   - *Question:* How do board technicians replace 24 microscopic USB-C pins soldered beneath a phone motherboard without melting nearby chips?
   - *Key Focus:* Hot air rework stations, solder paste stencils, kapton tape thermal shielding, microscope inspection, and surface tension self-alignment.
14. **3D Printing Replacement Parts: Rebuilding Obsolete Plastic Gears with CAD**
   - *Question:* How can a $200 3D printer save a vintage KitchenAid mixer or car dashboard vent when the manufacturer discontinued the part in 1995?
   - *Key Focus:* Digital caliper measurement precision, FreeCAD/Fusion 360 involute gear generation, PETG and Nylon filament strength vs brittle PLA, and print layer orientation.
15. **Louis Rossmann and the Right to Repair: The Legal Battle Against Board-Level Monopolies**
   - *Question:* How did an independent MacBook repair shop owner in Manhattan build a national political movement with millions of supporters?
   - *Key Focus:* Independent repair shop economics, customs border seizures of replacement glass, Apple Authorized Service Provider (AASP) restrictions, and passing state repair bills.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Oscilloscope Diagnostics: Tracing Power Rails and Clock Signals on Dead Laptops**
   - *Question:* When a laptop draws zero amps from the charger, how do you trace sequential power rails (PPBUS_G3H) to find the single shorted capacitor?
   - *Key Focus:* Boardview software (.fz / .brd), PDF schematic signal tracing, power sequence timing diagrams, short-circuit thermal cameras, and bench power supply current injection.
17. **Rust Remediation and Metal Preservation: Electrolysis and Chemical Conversion**
   - *Question:* How does submerged sacrificial iron and washing soda in water use 12 volts of DC current to gently strip 100 years of iron oxide rust?
   - *Key Focus:* Electrolytic rust removal chemistry, sacrificial anodes, phosphoric acid iron phosphate conversion, Evapo-Rust chelating agents, and boiled linseed oil finishes.
18. **Parts Pairing and Serialization: The Cryptographic Anti-Repair Nightmare**
   - *Question:* Why does swapping two genuine, identical screens between two brand-new iPhones disable FaceID and True Tone on both devices?
   - *Key Focus:* Cryptographic EEPROM handshake pairing, secure enclave authentication, blocking third-party repair shops, and EU eco-design regulatory bans on serialization.
19. **The Repair Café Movement: Community Fixing vs Disposable Society**
   - *Question:* How are volunteer workshops in 3,000 cities across the globe bringing neighbors together to fix toasters and sew torn coats for free?
   - *Key Focus:* Martine Postma's Amsterdam origins, knowledge transfer between retired tradespeople and youth, reducing municipal landfill e-waste, and community resilience.
20. **The Master Repair Workshop Architecture: The Complete Professional Blueprint**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive operational framework organizes diagnostic stations, electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection, tool layout, and inventory?
   - *Key Focus:* ESD mat grounding protocols, fume extraction ventilation, 5S workshop organization, standardized hardware bin inventory (metric/imperial), and safe chemical storage.
