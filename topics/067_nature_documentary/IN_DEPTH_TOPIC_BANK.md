**Individual coverage scope:** Develop an original wetland-season outline with defined habitat, timescale and observable events; avoid turning a location montage into evidence of an ecosystem process.

<a id="bank-067-014"></a>
#### BANK-067-014 — What does a film need to establish before calling a landscape untouched?

**Individual coverage scope:** Investigate land-use history, Indigenous stewardship and current management from traceable sources; treat pristine language as a claim rather than a visual impression.

<a id="bank-067-015"></a>
#### BANK-067-015 — How can a single tree become a documentary subject without implying it represents an entire forest?

**Individual coverage scope:** Plan coverage of age evidence, associated organisms, seasonal events and local context; distinguish the selected tree's story from population-level conclusions.

<a id="bank-067-021"></a>
#### BANK-067-021 — What must a river documentary show between headwaters and estuary?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare flow, sediment, habitats, human use and seasonal change at selected reaches; avoid portraying a sampled route as a complete watershed inventory.

#### BANK-067-022 — How can a coral-reef episode distinguish bleaching, mortality and later recovery?

**Individual coverage scope:** Require identified sites, dates, monitoring definitions and follow-up evidence; use marine research sources and avoid diagnosing reef condition solely from color grading.

<a id="bank-067-023"></a>
#### BANK-067-023 — Why does filming a wildfire's aftermath require more than contrasting blackened ground with green shoots?

<a id="bank-067-024"></a>
#### BANK-067-024 — How can soil life be made visible without turning magnified imagery into a misleading scale comparison?

<a id="bank-067-053"></a>

<a id="bank-067-055"></a>

**Individual coverage scope:** Specify coast, season, sardine movement and predators from field records; avoid a greatest-ever superlative.

<a id="bank-067-056"></a>
#### BANK-067-056 — How can a reef film test what happens at a named cleaner-fish station?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use repeated observations and species identities; avoid a blanket claim that predators never eat cleaners.

<a id="bank-067-058"></a>
#### BANK-067-058 — How can an Arctic ice-edge film distinguish seasonal hunting from long-term habitat change?

**Individual coverage scope:** Identify region, species, ice metric and time series; do not attribute an individual filmed behavior to a trend without evidence.

<a id="bank-067-060"></a>
#### BANK-067-060 — What is documented about a particular Siberian permafrost crater's formation?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use site-specific imagery, ground and gas measurements; distinguish a proposed mechanism from a filmed explosion.

<a id="bank-067-062"></a>
#### BANK-067-062 — How did Christmas Island red-crab migration timing and road protection work in a named season?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use park observations, rainfall/moon timing and dated barriers or road closures; avoid a fixed 50-million count for every year.

**Individual coverage scope:** Use repeated camera visits and dated biological samples; do not turn a decades-scale potential into a measured 100-year lifespan.

### Camera, sound and time

Subcategory `CATALOG-067-D04` · intermediate.

Prerequisites: [CATALOG-067-D01](#catalog-067-d01).

<a id="bank-067-025"></a>
#### BANK-067-025 — What does a macro lens change about the apparent world of a small animal?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare field of view, depth of field, scale cues and lighting using controlled non-disturbing subjects; avoid treating cinematic closeness as an animal's actual visual experience.

<a id="bank-067-026"></a>
#### BANK-067-026 — How should a time-lapse disclose the relationship between screen time and real time?

**Individual coverage scope:** Record frame intervals, omitted periods and environmental conditions; distinguish continuous observation from an assembled sequence of separate events.

<a id="bank-067-027"></a>
#### BANK-067-027 — Why can slow-motion footage create a false impression of routine movement?

**Individual coverage scope:** Identify capture rate, playback speed and the event context; compare real-time footage before drawing conclusions about speed or intention.

<a id="bank-067-028"></a>
#### BANK-067-028 — How can location sound be distinguished from sound added during editing?

**Individual coverage scope:** Plan field recordings, sound-library attribution and disclosures for recreated effects; do not imply that an enhanced soundtrack is an unaltered acoustic record.

<a id="bank-067-051"></a>
#### BANK-067-051 — How can deep-ocean footage show an animal's pressure environment honestly?

**Individual coverage scope:** Specify camera depth, pressure rating, lighting and habitat; do not use one atmosphere value for every deep-sea sequence.

<a id="bank-067-054"></a>
#### BANK-067-054 — How can a camera reveal a bird-of-paradise's dark plumage without inventing its appearance?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use named species, illumination and camera response; separate optical measurements from mate-choice behavior.

<a id="bank-067-066"></a>
#### BANK-067-066 — What evidence documents a particular milky-sea event?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare satellite observations, ship logs and biological sampling; distinguish observed glow area from a proved single-species cause.

<a id="bank-067-067"></a>
#### BANK-067-067 — How was the Planet Earth II iguana-and-snake sequence filmed and edited?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use attributable production records, camera positions and edit chronology; avoid an unsupported single-take or greatest-scene claim.

<a id="bank-067-029"></a>
#### BANK-067-029 — What evidence is needed before narration says one environmental event caused another?

**Individual coverage scope:** Build a claim map using field data, alternative explanations and relevant studies; separate visible sequence, correlation and causal inference.

<a id="bank-067-030"></a>
#### BANK-067-030 — How can an animated reconstruction explain a hidden process without inventing certainty?

**Individual coverage scope:** Annotate observed inputs, inferred mechanisms and unknown details using expert-reviewed references; label reconstruction and preserve uncertainty in narration.

<a id="bank-067-031"></a>
#### BANK-067-031 — When should a documentary show a scientist's method rather than only their conclusion?

**Individual coverage scope:** Choose an observable sampling or identification task, describe its limitations and retain conflicting findings; exclude staged laboratory results presented as original discoveries.

<a id="bank-067-032"></a>
#### BANK-067-032 — How can climate trends be discussed in a film centered on a single unusual season?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare the local event with official long-term datasets and attribution evidence; avoid declaring one season proof of a trend or assigning causation without research.

<a id="bank-067-057"></a>
#### BANK-067-057 — How should a golden-poison-frog film handle toxin and exposure claims?

**Individual coverage scope:** Use named population, toxin chemistry and measured dose route; avoid a generic human-death calculation or handling advice.

<a id="bank-067-064"></a>
#### BANK-067-064 — What did a named Mariana-Trench plastic-sampling study find?

**Individual coverage scope:** Specify species, depth, material identification and contamination controls; avoid generalizing one sample to all deep-sea animals.

**Individual coverage scope:** Connect proposed actions to official local opportunities and evaluated outcomes; distinguish awareness, donations, policy and habitat work rather than inventing impact figures.
