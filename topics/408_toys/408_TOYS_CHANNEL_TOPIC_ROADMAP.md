## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Toys across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Toys Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Toys.
- **Tier 2: Toys Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Toys.
- **Tier 3: Toys Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Toys.
- **Tier 4: Toys Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Toys.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Toys: A Toy in Its Childhood Context**
   - *Question:* Which dated social or manufacturing change shaped a named toy?
   - *Key Focus:* Use catalog, patent, object, and family records. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
2. **Toys: A Toy's Actual Play**
   - *Question:* How did children use a named toy beyond its advertised purpose?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare product design with consented observation or oral history. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
3. **Toys: Makers and Players behind a Toy**
   - *Question:* Whose labor and play experiences are absent from its brand story?
   - *Key Focus:* Use manufacturing and family evidence. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
4. **Toys: Toy, Child, and Setting**
   - *Question:* What makes a toy suitable for a particular child and play context?
   - *Key Focus:* Check current age guidance, abilities, interest, and actual components. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
5. **Toys: Open-Ended Play Claims**
   - *Question:* Does a toy permit multiple child-led uses and what evidence supports learning claims?
   - *Key Focus:* Use bounded observations, not developmental promises. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Toys: An Unverified Toy Origin**
   - *Question:* Which early design or manufacturer claim can be dated?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare patents, catalogs, packaging, and surviving examples. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
7. **Toys: Electronics in a Toy**
   - *Question:* Which play action changed when a toy gained sensors or software?
   - *Key Focus:* Test released version and data/privacy requirements. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
8. **Toys: A Toy Designer's Revision**
   - *Question:* What documented feedback altered shape, materials, or interaction?
   - *Key Focus:* Use prototypes, test records, and attributed accounts. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
9. **Toys: A Toy Recall**
   - *Question:* What defect and affected production dates did an official notice identify?
   - *Key Focus:* Use current regulator notice and product identifiers. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
10. **Toys: From Concept to Plaything**
   - *Question:* How do design, prototype, age determination, testing, and sale connect?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace a real product and jurisdiction. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Toys: A Toy Leaves Shelves**
   - *Question:* Was a product withdrawn for safety, demand, or licensing?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate documented reasons from collector lore. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
12. **Toys: One Toy across Versions**
   - *Question:* Which size, material, or rules changed over time?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare identified production runs and labeling. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
13. **Toys: A Proposed Connected Toy**
   - *Question:* Which safety, privacy, and repair questions remain?
   - *Key Focus:* Treat concept claims as unverified until tested. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
14. **Toys: Educational on the Box?**
   - *Question:* What learning outcome was measured, for which children and conditions?
   - *Key Focus:* Examine original study and marketing language separately. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
15. **Toys: A Toy's Design Trail**
   - *Question:* How did prototype, test, and production change a play object?
   - *Key Focus:* Follow dated artifacts and credited makers. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Toys: The Intended Play Experience**
   - *Question:* What child age and interaction did a design brief specify?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare brief, actual product, and observed play. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
17. **Toys: What a Toy Is Made Of**
   - *Question:* Which material and component choices affect use and durability?
   - *Key Focus:* Use product and test data for the specified version. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
18. **Toys: Testing a Toy Prototype**
   - *Question:* What did playtesting and safety assessment reveal?
   - *Key Focus:* Separate observation, compliance test, and design response. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
19. **Toys: Shapes Invite Play**
   - *Question:* How do size, color, and moving parts cue an action?
   - *Key Focus:* Observe a bounded child group with consent. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
20. **Toys: Many Makers of a Toy**
   - *Question:* Who designed play, mechanism, artwork, packaging, and testing?
   - *Key Focus:* Check credits and product records. Evidence base: identified product versions, manufacturer and test records, current CPSC safety and recall information, museum toy collections, and consented age-specific play observation.
