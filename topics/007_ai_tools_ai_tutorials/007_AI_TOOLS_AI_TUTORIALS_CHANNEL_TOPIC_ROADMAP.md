## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes practical AI tooling, prompting engineering, workflow automation, multimodal creative generation, and local open-source deployments across four progressive learning tiers:
- **Tier 1: Foundations of Everyday AI (Scratch / Beginner)** — Core conversational tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), prompt engineering fundamentals (zero-shot, few-shot, role priming), and avoiding hallucination traps.
- **Tier 2: Creative & Multimodal AI Tools (Intermediate)** — Image synthesis (Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion), video generation (Runway Gen-3, Kling, Luma), audio/voice cloning (ElevenLabs), and design integrations.
- **Tier 3: Productivity, Workflows & Automation (Upper Intermediate)** — Autonomous research tools (Perplexity, NotebookLM), workflow automation (Make, Zapier, n8n), custom GPTs, and document ingestion (RAG).
- **Tier 4: Advanced Local AI, Open-Source & Developer Tools (Advanced / Expert)** — Running local models with Ollama & LM Studio, Cursor & Copilot coding workflows, prompt injection security, and building autonomous agent stacks.
