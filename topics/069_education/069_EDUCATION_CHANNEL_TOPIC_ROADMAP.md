## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Education across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Cognitive Load Theory, Spaced Repetition & The Forgetting Curve (Scratch / Beginner)** — Hermann Ebbinghaus 1885 forgetting curve, John Sweller's Cognitive Load Theory (intrinsic vs extraneous), Anki SM-2 spaced repetition algorithms, and active recall testing effects.
- **Tier 2: The Feynman Technique, Bloom's Taxonomy & Deliberate Practice (Intermediate)** — Richard Feynman teaching-to-a-child mental model, Bloom's Revised Taxonomy cognitive hierarchy, Anders Ericsson's deliberate practice myth vs reality, and the Leitner flashcard box system.
- **Tier 3: The Prussian School Model, Alternative Pedagogy & Standardized Testing (Upper Intermediate)** — The 19th-century Prussian military origin of factory school bells and grade batches, Montessori self-directed learning methods, SAT/ACT predictive validity controversies, and Finnish education reforms.
- **Tier 4: Bloom's 2-Sigma Problem, AI Socratic Tutors & Unbundled Universities (Advanced / Expert)** — Benjamin Bloom's 2-Sigma mastery tutoring problem, LLM interactive Socratic dialogue agents, neuroplasticity critical learning windows, and the unbundling of the $100k college degree.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Cognitive Load & Spaced Repetition
1. **The Feynman Technique: How Explaining Difficult Concepts to a 10-Year-Old Unlocks True Mastery**
   - *Question:* How did Nobel physicist Richard Feynman master complex quantum electrodynamics by systematically stripping away technical jargon until a sixth-grader could understand it?
   - *Key Focus:* The 4 steps (Choose concept, Explain to a child, Identify knowledge gaps, Simplify and analogize), illusion of explanatory depth, removing vocabulary crutches to expose real understanding.
2. **The Forgetting Curve: Hermann Ebbinghaus and the Mathematics of Memory Loss**
   - *Question:* Why does the human brain discard 70% of new information within 24 hours, and how does reviewing at expanding mathematical intervals flatten the forgetting curve permanently?
   - *Key Focus:* Hermann Ebbinghaus 1885 nonsense syllable experiments, exponential memory decay function, the spacing effect (massed vs distributed practice), long-term synaptic potentiation.
3. **Cognitive Load Theory: Why Cluttered PowerPoints Make Students Dumber**
   - *Question:* Why can human working memory only hold 4 chunks of information at once, and how do bad classroom slides overload students with useless extraneous cognitive load?
   - *Key Focus:* John Sweller's 1988 theory, Intrinsic vs Extraneous vs Germane load, split-attention effect, modality effect (combining audio narration with clean graphics rather than dense text).
4. **Active Recall vs Re-Reading: The Testing Effect That Cuts Study Time in Half**
   - *Question:* Why does highlighting textbooks and re-reading notes feel satisfying while producing near-zero retention compared to closed-book practice retrieval tests?
   - *Key Focus:* Henry Roediger and Jeffrey Karpicke 2006 testing effect studies, desirable difficulties (Robert Bjork), retrieval practice strengthening neural retrieval pathways, metacognitive illusions.
5. **Anki and the SM-2 Algorithm: How Medical Students Memorize 20,000 Flashcards**
   - *Question:* How does a mathematical algorithm developed in Poland in 1987 calculate the exact day you are about to forget a fact to prompt you with a review flashcard?
   - *Key Focus:* Piotr Wozniak's SuperMemo SM-2 interval algorithm, ease factor calculations, active recall flashcard design principles (minimum information principle), med school USMLE dominance.
### Tier 2: The Feynman Technique & Bloom's Hierarchy
6. **Bloom's Taxonomy: Why Memorization Is Just the Basement of Human Intelligence**
   - *Question:* How did Benjamin Bloom organize human thinking into a 6-tier pyramid from basic remembering and understanding to analyzing, evaluating, and creating?
   - *Key Focus:* The 2001 revised cognitive hierarchy (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create), higher-order thinking skills (HOTS), designing curriculum questions that force critical thought.
7. **The 10,000-Hour Rule: The Malcolm Gladwell Myth vs Anders Ericsson's Real Deliberate Practice**
   - *Question:* Why did Anders Ericsson publicly repudiate Malcolm Gladwell's 'Outliers', proving that 10,000 hours of mindless repetition makes you mediocre, not a master?
   - *Key Focus:* Deliberate practice components (immediate feedback, expert coaching, operating at the edge of comfort zone, mental representations), genetic variability, quality of practice vs raw volume.
8. **The Leitner Box: The 1970s Cardboard Box System That Tamed Long-Term Memory**
   - *Question:* How did German science journalist Sebastian Leitner build an analog mechanical system out of a shoebox and index cards that automatically prioritized difficult facts?
   - *Key Focus:* The 5-compartment expanding interval box mechanics, moving cards forward on success and sending them back to Box 1 on failure, low-tech tactile spaced repetition for any subject.
### Tier 3: The Prussian Model, Montessori & Standardized Tests
9. **The Prussian School Model: Why Modern Schools Look and Act Like 19th-Century Factories**
   - *Question:* Why do schools ring bells every 45 minutes, group students strictly by manufacturing age batches, and punish divergence like a 19th-century military recruit depot?
   - *Key Focus:* Prussia's 1806 Battle of Jena defeat driving compulsory state schooling to produce obedient soldiers and factory workers, Horace Mann importing the model to Massachusetts, standardization vs creativity.
10. **Montessori Education: Why Google and Amazon Founders Grew Up in Self-Directed Classrooms**
   - *Question:* How did Italy's first female physician design a classroom with zero grades, zero tests, mixed-age classrooms, and tactile wooden puzzles that produced Larry Page, Sergey Brin, and Jeff Bezos?
   - *Key Focus:* Prepared environment, auto-education (self-correcting materials), three-hour uninterrupted work cycles, intrinsic motivation over extrinsic gold stars, longitudinal executive function data.
11. **The Finnish Miracle: Why Finland Gives Zero Homework, Has No Standardized Tests, and Tops PISA**
   - *Question:* How did a Nordic nation transform its school system into the highest-performing in the Western world by starting formal school at age 7, giving 15-minute outdoor breaks every hour, and banning inspections?
   - *Key Focus:* Master's degree requirement for all teachers (teaching as prestigious as medicine/law), high trust and teacher pedagogical autonomy, comprehensive equity over competitive school ranking.
12. **The SAT and Standardized Testing: The Eugenics Origin of America's College Gatekeeper**
   - *Question:* Did the Scholastic Aptitude Test originate as a democratic tool for social mobility, or was it adapted by eugenicist Carl Brigham from WWI military IQ tests to restrict immigrant admissions?
   - *Key Focus:* Army Alpha mental tests, Carl Brigham's 1923 book 'A Study of American Intelligence', correlation between test scores and household parental wealth, the modern test-optional university revolt.
13. **The Learning Styles Myth: Why You Are NOT a 'Visual' or 'Auditory' Learner**
   - *Question:* Why do 90% of teachers and students still believe in the VARK (Visual, Auditory, Read/Write, Kinesthetic) learning styles model when cognitive psychology has repeatedly debunked it?
   - *Key Focus:* Pashler et al. 2008 systematic review finding zero experimental evidence that matching presentation style improves retention, the meshing hypothesis failure, why dual coding (audio + visual) benefits everyone.
### Tier 4: Bloom's 2-Sigma, AI Tutors & Unbundled Degrees
14. **Bloom's 2-Sigma Problem: Why One-on-One Tutoring Produces Miracles Classrooms Cannot Match**
   - *Question:* Why did Benjamin Bloom prove in 1984 that an average student tutored one-on-one with mastery learning outperforms 98% of students in a traditional 30-person classroom?
   - *Key Focus:* The 2-Sigma effect size (two standard deviations improvement), immediate feedback and clearing foundational misconceptions before moving on, the economic impossibility of 1-to-1 human tutors.
15. **AI Socratic Tutors: Can Large Language Models Finally Solve the 2-Sigma Problem?**
   - *Question:* Can conversational AI models like Khanmigo deliver infinite patience, customized analogies, and Socratic questioning to give every child on Earth a private personal tutor?
   - *Key Focus:* Sal Khan's vision, Socratic prompt scaffolding (refusing to give the answer, guiding inquiry), multimodal voice interfaces, hallucination risks and student dependency concerns.
16. **Neuroplasticity and Critical Windows: Why Children Learn Languages in Months While Adults Struggle**
   - *Question:* How does synaptic pruning and the development of the brain's perineuronal nets lock in adult neural circuits, and how can adults re-open neuroplastic learning windows?
   - *Key Focus:* Critical periods in sensory and language cortex development, acetylcholine and noradrenaline release during focused deliberate attention, sleep's role in memory consolidation.
17. **The Unbundling of the $100,000 College Degree: Micro-Credentials and Proof-of-Skill**
   - *Question:* Why are top tech and finance employers dropping college degree requirements in favor of verified skill portfolios, coding challenges, and specialized micro-credentials?
   - *Key Focus:* The $1.7 trillion student debt crisis, signaling theory of higher education (Bryan Caplan's 'The Case Against Education'), apprenticeship revivals, the bifurcation between elite ivy networks and skills.
18. **Sugata Mitra's Hole in the Wall: The Astonishing Power of Minimally Invasive Education**
   - *Question:* What happened when an Indian researcher placed a high-speed computer into a brick wall in a New Delhi slum with zero instructions, and poor children taught themselves biotechnology?
   - *Key Focus:* Self-Organized Learning Environments (SOLE), peer collaboration dynamics, children teaching each other across language barriers, TED Prize 2013 'School in the Cloud' initiative.
19. **Gamification in Learning: How Duolingo Uses Video Game Psychology to Hook 100 Million Users**
   - *Question:* How do streak freezes, leaderboards, variable dopamine reward schedules, and push notifications turn foreign language vocabulary into an addictive mobile game?
   - *Key Focus:* B.J. Fogg behavioral model, loss aversion (fear of losing a 500-day streak), gamified micro-learning vs deep communicative fluency efficacy, the dark patterns of engagement metrics.
20. **Education in 2040: Neural Direct Knowledge Downloads or Collaborative Socratic Guilds?**
   - *Question:* If brain-computer interfaces allow direct declarative knowledge uploads like the Matrix, what will human teachers actually teach in the mid-21st century?
   - *Key Focus:* Declarative knowledge vs tacit wisdom, emotional empathy, ethics, creative synthesis, physical embodied apprenticeship, the ultimate return to Socratic human dialogue.
