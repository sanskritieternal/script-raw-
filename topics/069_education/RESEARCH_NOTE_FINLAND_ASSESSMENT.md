# Finnish basic education and PISA: bounded source note

Linked proposal: [BANK-069-059](IN_DEPTH_TOPIC_BANK.md#bank-069-059). Research checked 2026-10-24. This is a source trail for a descriptive comparison, not a causal evaluation of Finnish education policy.

## Primary institutional records

The [Finnish National Agency for Education's basic-education guide](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/bacic-information-about-primary-and-lower-secondary-education) says there are no **national tests** in primary and lower-secondary schools; teachers assess pupils' learning and competence. Its [national curriculum page](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/national-core-curriculum-primary-and-lower-secondary-basic-education) describes a national framework and varied assessment methods. “No national tests” does not mean “no tests” or “no assessment.” An [agency overview](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/Primary%20and%20lower%20secondary%20education%20in%20Finland-2022-Net-RGB_0.pdf) says the amount of homework is low compared with other countries, rather than zero.

The OECD's [PISA 2022 Finland country note](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_ed6fbcc5-en/finland_6991e849-en.html) reports performance above the OECD average in mathematics, reading and science and a decline since 2018 in all three. PISA samples **15-year-olds**, rather than all pupils, and the country note reports uncertainty and participation caveats. Compare years and domains before describing any trend.

## Episode method

Pick a school level, year and outcome. Quote the Finnish agency on its actual assessment rules and the OECD on measured PISA results. Examine the outcome distribution and sampling notes, not only the national average. Do not infer that low homework volume or absence of national basic-school tests caused a PISA score; that would require a credible comparison design controlling for other differences. Do not generalize Finland's arrangements to every grade or country.

## Limits

These sources establish published policy descriptions and a bounded test result. They cannot isolate the effect of homework, teacher autonomy, resources or assessment policy. The active BANK proposal remains source-unverified and not ready for production; current rules and later PISA cycles require new date-specific checks.
