# Research note: fire incident records

Checked 2026-10-24. These are bounded source-access notes for proposed BANK questions, not verified_sources entries or IDEA approval.

## BANK-184-046 — 2018 Camp Fire records

- [CAL FIRE Camp Fire incident page](https://www.fire.ca.gov/incidents/2018/11/8/camp-fire), incident beginning November 8, 2018, provides dated status and incident-document links.
- National Institute of Standards and Technology, [Camp Fire preliminary reconnaissance](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-4f30911c5c2d271654aa5a81f01d2d1d/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-4f30911c5c2d271654aa5a81f01d2d1d.pdf), records what was available near the event, including an initially unknown cause; it is a preliminary survey, not the final origin finding.
- Butte County District Attorney, [2020 Camp Fire public investigation report](https://www.buttecounty.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1881/Camp-Fire-Public-Report---Summary-of-the-Camp-Fire-Investigation-PDF), reconstructs ignition near a PG&E transmission tower and the prosecution record. The [county case page](https://www.buttecounty.ca.gov/342/Camp-Fire) states the company pleaded guilty to the charged counts.

**Claim limit:** An incident status, a preliminary reconnaissance and a later legal investigation have different evidentiary dates and purposes. Do not present the early unknown-cause entry as a contradiction of the later finding or imply every harm had one simple cause. Avoid operational tactics, graphic victim detail and unconsented survivor stories. Climate attribution needs a separate study; this single event cannot establish it.

## BANK-184-047 — FDNY micromobility bulletin

- New York City Fire Department, [2023-2024 Fire and Emergency Preparedness Bulletin (PDF)](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/fdny/downloads/pdf/codes/2023-2024-fep-annual-bulletin.pdf), reports 214 lithium-ion-battery structural fires, 14 deaths and 116 injuries through October 16, 2023, in its section on e-bikes, e-scooters and related micromobility devices.
- U.S. Fire Administration, [2024 EV and energy-transition workgroup report](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/summit/2024/2024-summit-ev-energy-transition-workgroup-report.pdf), frames batteries across micromobility, passenger vehicles and other storage systems as distinct scales for fire-service evidence and policy.

**Claim limit:** FDNY's cutoff figures are not a complete calendar-year total or a passenger-EV denominator. The bulletin cannot validate the archived universal "30,000 gallons" suppression claim, a particular incident cause, or a safe response method. No battery handling, firefighting or evacuation instructions are offered. Respect those injured and families; source any personal story separately and with consent.

## Scope

BANK-185-033 addresses hindsight-aware rescue review. BANK-063-052 concerns consumer battery aging. These do not duplicate fire incident reporting.
