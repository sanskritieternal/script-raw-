# Selected source and boundary note — 432 Heavy Equipment

Reviewed 2026-10-24. Proposed **BANK-432-045** concerns the Volvo EC230 Electric excavator in a named Bristol Airport trial.

## Primary manufacturer/project account

- Volvo Construction Equipment, [*First Volvo EC230 Electric in UK powers up airport project*](https://www.volvoce.com/europe/en/about-us/news/2024/flying-start-first-volvo-ec230-electric-in-uk-powers-up-airport-project/), describes a 2024 airport project trial involving Chepstow Plant. Volvo says a heavier task than the site's expected duty cycle required a recharge after about **2.5 hours**, and reports use of a **150-kW** fast charger during breaks plus overnight AC charging. This is a participant/manufacturer report, not an independently instrumented fleet trial.
- Volvo CE, [charging FAQ](https://www.volvoce.com/en-us/discover/guides-and-advice/2021/charging-electric-construction-equipment-faq/), says midsize/large electric machines such as EC230 require site electrical planning. Region, machine version and charging hardware matter; do not transpose one region's nominal voltage or a later product-guide runtime onto the 2024 UK trial.

## Limits, safety and overlap

The account supports a duty-cycle and infrastructure question, not a claim that every EC230 runs for 2.5 hours or that electric operation has zero lifecycle emissions. The airport and contractor statements may be promotional; obtain actual task logs and energy measurements before quantitative comparisons. No high-voltage maintenance or machine-operation instructions belong in the case.
