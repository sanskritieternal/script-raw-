# Selected institutional source note — human anatomy

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-352-041`.

- [US National Library of Medicine, *The Visible Human Project*](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html): NLM describes cross-sectional cryosection, CT and MRI images derived from one male and one female cadaver, with public releases in 1994 and 1995. The project supplied reference material and a test bed for image libraries and imaging algorithms.
- [NLM technical account, Visible Human image datasets and software](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1480240/) (2003): an NLM author recounts the releases and the distinction between source images and derived segmentations or interfaces.

Both accessible institutional accounts establish dataset scope and purpose. Two donors cannot represent the full distribution of anatomy, development, health status or body variation. Section images and reconstructions are processed records, not proof of live physiology or a patient-specific diagnosis. Source access does not settle donation-consent history or all reuse ethics; those need dedicated records.
