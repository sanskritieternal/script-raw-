# Selected primary-source note: corporate empires

Checked 2026-10-24. Proposed cases BANK-301-042 and BANK-301-043.

The US Supreme Court's official *United States Reports*, volume 221, contains *Standard Oil Co. of New Jersey v. United States*, 221 U.S. 1 (1911), with the court's reasoning on consolidated control and the ordered remedy: https://www.supremecourt.gov/pdfs/USReports/USREPORTS-221_PDFA.pdf . It is primary evidence of the adjudicated US case, not a neutral census of the entire oil business, a proof of later consumer outcomes, or present-day antitrust advice. Pinpoint pages and distinguish party allegations, findings, decree and subsequent implementation.

The US Department of Justice case file for *United States v. The Walt Disney Company and Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc.* links the June 2018 complaint and proposed remedy, competitive impact statement, public-comment response and September 2019 final judgment: https://www.justice.gov/atr/case/us-v-walt-disney-company-and-twenty-first-century-fox-inc . DOJ's contemporaneous announcement specifies a proposed divestiture of 22 regional sports networks and the local cable sports programming concern: https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/walt-disney-company-required-divest-twenty-two-regional-sports-networks-order-complete . State which document is allegation, settlement proposal or final order; none alone proves the eventual price, worker or viewer effects. The analysis is US market- and date-specific. Reuse of company marks/video needs separate rights review.
