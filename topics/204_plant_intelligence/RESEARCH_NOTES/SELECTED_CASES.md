# Selected plant-response claim checks

All linked questions remain **proposed**. An observed plant response does not by itself establish subjective experience.

## BANK-204-026 — anesthetized flytrap

The [original comparative experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6215046/) reports that several anesthetics affected movements in studied plants and that diethyl ether exposure blocked recorded Venus-flytrap action potentials. The treatment and measured responses are specific to the experiment. This does not establish sleep, consciousness or equivalence to human anesthesia. A [follow-up Venus-flytrap experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6948209/) investigates jasmonate signaling; it is not a test of subjective sensation.

## BANK-204-036 and BANK-204-034 — pea learning and Mimosa habituation

An [independent pea-plant replication attempt](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7311169/) found no evidence of the earlier associative-learning effect under its tested conditions and questioned a growth-direction scoring premise. This does not prove all plant learning impossible; it makes the archived 'anticipate trellis' conclusion unsupported. A separate [Mimosa response study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5541926/) did not find habituation in its tested individual-leaf design and cautioned that stimulus and scale differ from earlier dropping protocols. Production research must compare designs before claiming replication or failure to replicate.

## BANK-204-029 — engineered spinach sensor

The [MIT team's 2016 institutional account](https://news.mit.edu/2016/nanobionic-spinach-plants-detect-explosives-1031) describes carbon-nanotube-enhanced spinach sensing certain explosive-associated compounds and an engineered wireless readout. The claim is a laboratory sensor demonstration; landmine detection in the field and a naturally emailing plant are not established.

## Scope and overlap

The slime-mold Tokyo-network pitch routes to preserved active BANK-204-027, a cross-domain placement that needs microbial-network review: slime mold is a protist, not a plant. The electrical-music pitch routes to a proposed sonification-method question and existing signal-measurement scope; no audible plant melody is verified. The pain pitch routes to concepts and ethics questions, neither of which licenses a subjective-suffering claim. No idea entered Phase 0–5 production.
