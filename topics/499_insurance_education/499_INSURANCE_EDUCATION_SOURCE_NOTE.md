# Insurance education source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-499-044 - NFIP Dwelling Form basement terms

- Primary federal policy artifact: U.S. Federal Emergency Management Agency, *National Flood Insurance Program Flood Insurance Manual*, October 2025 edition, Appendix A, https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_rsl_national-flood-insurance-manual_06032025.pdf (inspected 2026-10-24 via indexed text). It contains the Standard Flood Insurance Policy Dwelling Form and describes coverage, limits and exclusions. FEMA's current-manual index https://www.fema.gov/flood-insurance/work-with-nfip/manuals/current identifies the applicable edition for new business by policy effective date.
- Primary consumer claims explanation: NFIP/FEMA, *Start a Claim*, https://www.floodsmart.gov/recover/start-a-claim (inspected 2026-10-24). FEMA states that basement coverage is limited and depends on the purchased coverage type; some installed building equipment may be covered under building coverage, while contents are treated differently.
- Limits: This is U.S. federal NFIP policy education, not a determination on a particular address, policy form, loss, coverage election or claim. The exact policy period, endorsements, definitions, flood cause, insured property, limits and deductibles govern; private flood policies may differ. Full Appendix A clause-by-clause review is needed before quoting any exclusion or asserting whether a named household item is payable. Consumer guidance is explanatory and cannot override policy text. No individual insurance advice.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-499-044 is a specific NFIP Dwelling Form coverage distinction. BANK-499-030 and BANK-499-037 cover general payment and property-valuation questions; BANK-498-043 concerns FDA recall remedies, not insurance claims.
