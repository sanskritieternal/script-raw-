## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Insurance Education: How a Risk Pool Emerged**
   - *Question:* Which need and rule shaped one early insurance arrangement?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated policy, mutual, or regulator records. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
2. **Insurance Education: An Insurance Product in Context**
   - *Question:* What does one widely cited policy actually cover?
   - *Key Focus:* Read the dated contract and compare claims with exclusions. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
3. **Insurance Education: Who Could Not Get Covered?**
   - *Question:* Whose risks or communities were excluded from a scheme?
   - *Key Focus:* Use eligibility, underwriting, and affected-group records. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
4. **Insurance Education: Premium, Deductible, and Limit**
   - *Question:* What does a policyholder pay before and after a covered loss?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a fictional claim with one dated policy form. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
5. **Insurance Education: Pooling Risk and Adverse Selection**
   - *Question:* How can who enrolls change an insurance pool's costs?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a simplified model and state its assumptions. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Insurance Education: Why a Claim Was Denied**
   - *Question:* Which policy facts and records determine one disputed claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare contract language, evidence, and adjudication. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
7. **Insurance Education: Digital Claims Handling Tested**
   - *Question:* Did one claims tool improve speed without reducing fairness?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare measured processing and appeal outcomes. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
8. **Insurance Education: Who Sets Coverage Rules?**
   - *Question:* Which insurer or regulator decision changed one coverage boundary?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace filings, consultations, and policy text. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
9. **Insurance Education: A Disaster and Insurance Capacity**
   - *Question:* How did one event change claims, prices, or availability?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated loss and renewal data for one area. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
10. **Insurance Education: From Premium to Claim Payment**
   - *Question:* What steps connect enrollment to a paid claim?
   - *Key Focus:* Map underwriting, premium, event, documentation, adjustment, and appeal. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Insurance Education: Coverage That Became Unavailable**
   - *Question:* Why did insurers withdraw or restrict one product?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare losses, reinsurance, regulation, and prices. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
12. **Insurance Education: One Policy Across Two Periods**
   - *Question:* How did coverage or exclusions change between policy versions?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare forms, limits, and effective dates. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
13. **Insurance Education: A Conditional Coverage Gap**
   - *Question:* Which conditions would make a risk harder to insure?
   - *Key Focus:* State loss, capital, regulation, and adaptation assumptions. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
14. **Insurance Education: Testing a Coverage Assumption**
   - *Question:* Does a common belief about what insurance covers match the contract?
   - *Key Focus:* Read definitions, exclusions, deductibles, and limits. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
15. **Insurance Education: One Claim's Paper Trail**
   - *Question:* What do records show about a specific claim decision?
   - *Key Focus:* Use consented or anonymized documents and appeal history. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Insurance Education: An Exclusion That Changes Protection**
   - *Question:* Which wording determines whether one hypothetical event is covered?
   - *Key Focus:* Read the exact dated policy and define the peril. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
17. **Insurance Education: Premium and Out-of-Pocket Costs**
   - *Question:* How do premium, deductible, and uncovered loss affect a household?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a labeled illustrative budget. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
18. **Insurance Education: Waiting Periods and Renewals**
   - *Question:* When does coverage begin, end, or change?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated policy terms and renewal notices. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
19. **Insurance Education: Total Cost of a Policy**
   - *Question:* Which charges appear beyond the headline premium?
   - *Key Focus:* Read fees, taxes, installments, and cancellation terms. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
20. **Insurance Education: Appealing a Claim Decision**
   - *Question:* What review or complaint process applies to one policy?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify jurisdiction, insurer type, and deadlines. Evidence base: dated policy forms, insurer filings, regulator guidance, claims data, and consented accounts.
