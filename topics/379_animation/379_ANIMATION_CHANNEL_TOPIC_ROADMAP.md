## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Animation across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Animation Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Animation.
- **Tier 2: Animation Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Animation.
- **Tier 3: Animation Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Animation.
- **Tier 4: Animation Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Animation.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

1. **The Architecture of Animation: An Animation Method Takes Shape**
   - *Question:* Which dated production technique changed movement in a named work?

2. **Animation: A Sequence Frame by Frame**
   - *Question:* Which timed choices make a celebrated animated action legible?
   - *Key Focus:* Analyze exposure, poses, staging, and sound in a bounded sequence. Evidence base: identified films and versions, storyboards, exposure sheets, cels or digital assets, production records, credits, technical publications, and archive catalogs.
3. **Animation: Artists Behind the Moving Image**

4. **Animation: From Drawing to Motion**
