# Animation: selected production cases, 24 October 2026

All 20 pending and seven trending archived pitches have routes to active questions. Their original records and IDs are preserved. The new cases remain `proposed` with empty `verified_sources`.

## BANK-379-044: Garity's animated-photoplay patent

- [US 2,281,033, *Method of producing animated photoplays*](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2281033A/en), filed in 1939 by William E. Garity and assigned to Walt Disney Productions, describes imagery on spaced planes and coordinated camera/background movement across successive exposures. The claims and drawings are primary evidence of the described method.
- A patent shows what was disclosed and claimed. It does not prove first invention of every multiplane method, use in a particular *Snow White* shot, or the scope of any team's actual workflow. [The Library of Congress's 1937 *Snow White* poster record](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2004673418/) supports contemporaneous promotion of “multiplane technicolor,” but is advertising, not a production log.
- This named method gives the archived tool-history pitch a bounded case distinct from 379's general camera-layout question.

## BANK-379-045: *Toy Story* and RenderMan

- [Pixar's RenderMan history](https://renderman.pixar.com/the-evolution-of-renderman) and a [Pixar-authored rendering paper](https://graphics.pixar.com/library/RendermanTog2018/paper.pdf) identify the 1995 *Toy Story* as rendered with an early RenderMan version based on the Reyes approach. [Pixar's studio timeline](https://www.pixar.com/our-story) dates the film's 1995 release.
- These sources establish a rendering role and dated studio account. The 2018 architecture paper describes a later path-tracing system; its current design must not be projected backward onto 1995. Rendering does not by itself describe modeling, rigging, animation, editorial or story decisions. Shot-level workflow claims need production records.
- This case differs from 368's film preservation cases and from 379's active general pipeline questions.
