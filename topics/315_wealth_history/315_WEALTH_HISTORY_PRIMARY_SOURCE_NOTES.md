## BANK-315-040: Mount Vernon list, 1799

- Mount Vernon Ladies' Association, [George Washington's List of Enslaved People, 1799](https://www.mountvernon.org/education/primary-source-collections/primary-source-collections/article/george-washington-s-list-of-enslaved-people-1799), inspected 2026-10-24. The institution provides a primary-document image/transcript and distinguishes people Washington held in slavery, people held as Custis dower property and people rented from a neighbor.
- Mount Vernon, [Census of the Enslaved Population at Mount Vernon, 1786 and 1799](https://www.mountvernon.org/library/digitalhistory/digital-encyclopedia/article/census-of-the-enslaved-population-at-mount-vernon-1786-and-1799), inspected 2026-10-24, explains the estate's ownership columns, locations and work assignments.
- Limits: the list is an enslaver's administrative record, not the voices or full lives of those named. Legal property categories were coercive and do not define people. Counts at a specific date should not be mistaken for a monetary fortune, stable household composition or complete family network. Check name spellings and reuse permissions before reproducing pages; center dignity and kinship.

## BANK-315-041: Carnegie Corporation founding records, 1911

- Carnegie Corporation/Andrew Carnegie Foundation, [1911 act of incorporation (charter)](https://media.carnegie.org/filer_public/a8/01/a801e25b-4e2f-42a8-b580-59ac85a1ab37/ccny_other_19111110_charter.pdf), inspected 2026-10-24. The New York act became law 9 June 1911 and created a corporation with powers to hold and apply funds for named purposes.
- Institution's [Andrew Carnegie Deed of Gift](https://carnegie.org/publication/deed-of-gift/), 1911, inspected 2026-10-24. This is a separate founding gift document, not interchangeable with the charter. The institution's [history](https://carnegie.org/about/history/) says $125 million was transferred by 1912 and notes a 2026 name change.
- Limits: incorporation authority, donor commitment, later transfer and current asset value are separate measures. Neither a charter nor an institutional retrospective alone verifies exact personal net worth, public benefit or the full funding path. Consult the gift text, dated accounts and recipient/community perspectives before making those claims. This is a named asset-to-institution transition rather than a richest-person ranking.
