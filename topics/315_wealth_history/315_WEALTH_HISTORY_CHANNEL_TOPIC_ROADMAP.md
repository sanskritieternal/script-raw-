## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Wealth History: How Wealth Was Recorded**
   - *Question:* What is the earliest usable record of wealth for one society?
   - *Key Focus:* Define assets and liabilities before comparing households. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
2. **Wealth History: A Celebrated Fortune in Context**
   - *Question:* How large was one famous fortune relative to its economy?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated asset estimates and transparent denominators. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
3. **Wealth History: Assets Missing From the Archive**
   - *Question:* Whose property or debt is absent from standard wealth histories?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare formal records with community and household evidence. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
4. **Wealth History: Income Is Not Wealth**
   - *Question:* How did a historical record distinguish earnings from net assets?
   - *Key Focus:* Use one estate, survey, or tax source and explain its limits. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
5. **Wealth History: Comparing Wealth Across Centuries**
   - *Question:* Which valuation and currency choices change a historical comparison?
   - *Key Focus:* Test multiple deflators, asset types, and denominators. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Wealth History: The Missing Top of the Distribution**
   - *Question:* How do absent wealthy households affect a historical wealth estimate?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare source coverage and correction methods. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
7. **Wealth History: A New Asset Changes Wealth Records**
   - *Question:* How did one financial innovation alter what could be owned or measured?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace legal rights, valuation, and access. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
8. **Wealth History: A Fortune's Institutional Roots**
   - *Question:* Which laws or institutions enabled one documented fortune?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace ownership, inheritance, market power, and public records. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
9. **Wealth History: A Wealth Shock**
   - *Question:* How did one war, reform, or crisis change measured wealth?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable before-and-after data and causal caution. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
10. **Wealth History: From Estate Inventory to Wealth Estimate**
   - *Question:* How can an inventory be converted into a net-wealth measure?
   - *Key Focus:* Show assets, debts, valuation date, and missing items. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Wealth History: A Family Fortune Across Generations**
   - *Question:* Which transfers and losses explain a fortune's change?
   - *Key Focus:* Use estate and business records across named generations. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
12. **Wealth History: Two Wealth Distributions**
   - *Question:* How did wealth concentration change between two comparable periods?
   - *Key Focus:* Match units, coverage, valuation, and asset definitions. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
13. **Wealth History: Past Predictions About Wealth**
   - *Question:* Which historical prediction of wealth distribution proved inaccurate?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare dated forecasts with later survey or tax data. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
14. **Wealth History: Testing a Richest-Person Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a historical ranking survive a consistent valuation method?
   - *Key Focus:* Check source estimates and denominator choices. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
15. **Wealth History: One Estate's Paper Trail**
   - *Question:* What does an estate file show and hide about wealth?
   - *Key Focus:* Read assets, debts, beneficiaries, and excluded property. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Wealth History: Ownership Rights Behind an Asset**
   - *Question:* What rights did holders of one historical asset actually possess?
   - *Key Focus:* Read title, company, or legal records for a defined period. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
17. **Wealth History: Income and Asset Accumulation**
   - *Question:* Did a household's income translate into lasting wealth?
   - *Key Focus:* Use longitudinal records and account for debt and transfers. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
18. **Wealth History: Preserving Wealth Across Time**
   - *Question:* Which asset risks mattered to a historical household?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare inflation, default, property loss, and liquidity. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
19. **Wealth History: Costs of Holding Wealth**
   - *Question:* Which taxes and management costs reduced a historical asset's value?
   - *Key Focus:* Use dated contracts, tax rules, and transparent arithmetic. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
20. **Wealth History: Property Rights and Their Limits**
   - *Question:* Who could legally own or inherit assets in one period?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify jurisdiction, group, and legal change. Evidence base: estate and tax records, asset valuations, household surveys, legal texts, and documented methodology.
