#### BANK-315-039 — How can probate records understate wealth held outside the estate they record?

**Individual coverage scope:** Compare jointly owned property, trusts, debts, jurisdiction, household membership and record survival in a defined historical system; avoid allegations about named people.

#### BANK-315-040 — What does Washington's 1799 Mount Vernon list reveal about legal ownership and its limits?

**Individual coverage scope:** Examine the June 1799 list of enslaved people and institutional transcription for Washington-owned, Custis dower and rented categories.
