## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Bicycles & Cycling across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Bike Geometry, Drivetrain Ratios & Tire Pressure Physics (Beginner / Scratch)** — Head tube angles and trail stability, gear inches and cadence mathematics, Silca rolling resistance tire pressure curves, chain lubrication physics, and basic bike fit.
- **Tier 2: Frame Material Dynamics (Steel, Aluminum, Carbon, Titanium) & Disc Brakes (Intermediate)** — Yield strength and fatigue limits of 4130 chromoly vs 6061-T6 aluminum vs unidirectional carbon fiber vs Grade 9 titanium, hydraulic disc brake fluid dynamics, and wheel truing.
- **Tier 3: Aerodynamics, Marginal Gains, Tubeless Tech & The Tour de France (Upper Intermediate)** — Wind tunnel drag coefficient (CdA), marginal gains in pro peloton, tubeless sealant chemistry and bead hooks, aerodynamic wheel depth trade-offs, and power meter FTP pacing.
- **Tier 4: E-Bike Transits, Urban Cycling Infrastructure & The Master Custom Frame Blueprint (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Torque sensor mid-drive motors vs hub motors, Dutch protected junction civil engineering, custom frame jig welding/brazing, and the future of human-electric micromobility.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **Steel is Real vs Carbon Fiber: The Great Frame Material Showdown**
   - *Question:* Why do bespoke frame builders swear by 4130 Chromoly steel while modern Tour de France pelotons race exclusively on carbon fiber?
   - *Key Focus:* Tensile strength to weight ratios, fatigue limits (infinite fatigue endurance of steel/titanium vs aluminum), vibration damping, and catastrophic carbon failure modes.
2. **The Physics of Bicycle Stability: Why a Moving Bike Doesn't Fall Over**
   - *Question:* Is it gyroscopic wheel precession or fork caster trail that keeps an unmanned moving bicycle perfectly upright?
   - *Key Focus:* The 1970 David Jones unrideable bike experiments, head tube angle, fork rake, trail calculation, and forward center of mass self-steering.
3. **Gear Inches and Cadence: The Mechanical Advantage of the Bicycle Drivetrain**
   - *Question:* Why is grinding at 60 RPM worse for your leg muscles than spinning smoothly at 90 RPM in a lower gear ratio?
   - *Key Focus:* Gear inch formulas, chainring-to-cassette ratios, 1x vs 2x drivetrains, derailleur parallelogram mechanics, and neuromuscular efficiency.
4. **Tire Pressure and Rolling Resistance: Why High Pressure Actually Makes You Slower**
   - *Question:* Why did pro cyclists spend 50 years pumping tires rock-hard to 120 PSI, and why does 75 PSI roll significantly faster?
   - *Key Focus:* Casing hysteresis loss vs suspension impedance losses on rough asphalt, Silca breakpoint pressure curves, wider 28mm/30mm tire science, and contact patches.
5. **Bike Fit Ergonomics: Solving Knee Pain, Numb Hands, and Saddle Sores**
   - *Question:* How does raising your saddle by 5 millimeters eliminate anterior knee pain and unlock 20 extra watts of pedaling power?
   - *Key Focus:* Knee flexion angle at bottom dead center (25-35 degrees), saddle setback, reach and stack geometry, sit bone measurement, and ulnar nerve pressure.
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **Hydraulic Disc Brakes vs Rim Brakes: The Stopping Power Revolution**
   - *Question:* How does Pascal's law of fluid pressure give index-finger braking power in pouring rain down a 15% alpine descent?
   - *Key Focus:* Mineral oil vs DOT fluid, master cylinder to slave caliper piston area multiplication, rotor thermal dissipation, wet braking rim delamination, and thru-axle stiffness.
7. **The Art of Wheel Truing: Spoke Tension, Radial Runout, and Dishing**
   - *Question:* How do 32 thin wires under 120 kilograms of tension suspend a 200-pound rider without buckling over potholes?
   - *Key Focus:* Tensioned spoke wheel physics (standing on the hub vs hanging from upper spokes), truing stands, spoke nipple keys, tensiometer readings, and asymmetric rear dishing.
8. **Tubeless Tires: How Liquid Latex and Rim Hooks Eliminated Pinch Flats**
   - *Question:* How does liquid latex seal a puncture from a thorny nail while riding at 25 MPH without losing tire pressure?
   - *Key Focus:* Coagulation chemistry, Stan's NoTubes origins, hookless carbon rim blow-off controversies, lower operating pressures, and tubeless rim tape installation.
9. **Aerodynamics on Two Wheels: CdA, Wind Tunnels, and 80% Power Resistance**
   - *Question:* Why is 80% of a cyclist's power spent pushing air aside at 20 MPH, and why does rider body position dwarf a $10,000 aero bike?
   - *Key Focus:* Coefficient of drag area (CdA), deep-section carbon aero wheels (50mm vs 80mm crosswind stability), skinsuits, narrow handlebars, and aero tucks.
10. **The Doping Dark Ages: EPO, Blood Bags, and the Lance Armstrong Era**
   - *Question:* How did synthetic erythropoietin (EPO) and refrigerated blood transfusion centrifuges turn cycling into a pharmaceutical arms race?
   - *Key Focus:* Hematocrit 50% limit, nighttime heart attacks from sludge-thick blood, Michele Ferrari's protocols, USADA's Reasoned Decision report, and stripping 7 Tour titles.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Mountain Bike Suspension Kinematics: Horst Link vs VPP vs Single Pivot**
   - *Question:* How do four-bar linkages prevent pedaling forces from compressing the rear shock while keeping suspension active under hard braking?
   - *Key Focus:* Instant centers, leverage rate curves (progressive vs linear), anti-squat percentages, anti-rise brake jack, and pedal kickback physics.
12. **The Chain Waxing Obsession: Why Molten Paraffin Beats Wet Lubes**
   - *Question:* Why are competitive cyclists cleaning brand-new chains in mineral spirits and slow-cooking them in crockpots of paraffin wax?
   - *Key Focus:* Factory grease removal, friction loss testing (Zero Friction Cycling data), avoiding grinding paste abrasive wear, and extending drivetrain lifespan by 400%.
13. **The Hour Record: The Purest, Most Brutal Endurance Test in Sports**
   - *Question:* How did Filippo Ganna ride 56.792 kilometers in exactly 60 minutes on an indoor velodrome track with zero gears or brakes?
   - *Key Focus:* Fixed gear track bikes, aerodynamic 3D-printed titanium frames, metabolic threshold pacing (lactate clearance), and the historic Merckx vs Boardman equipment battles.
14. **The 1817 Dandy Horse to Safety Bicycle: The Two-Wheeled Technological Leap**
   - *Question:* How did Karl von Drais's wooden balance bike and the terrifying high-wheel Penny Farthing evolve into the chain-driven Safety Bicycle?
   - *Key Focus:* Year Without a Summer horse famine, high-wheel rollover header crashes, John Kemp Starley's 1885 Rover safety bike, and John Boyd Dunlop's pneumatic tire.
15. **E-Bikes: Torque Sensors vs Cadence Sensors and the Transit Revolution**
   - *Question:* Why does a mid-drive motor with a strain-gauge torque sensor feel like having bionic legs, while cheap hub motors feel like a scooter throttle?
   - *Key Focus:* Piezoelectric torque sensing math, brushless DC motor integration, planetary gear reduction, battery fire safety standards (UL 2849), and Class 1-3 regulations.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **The Dutch Cycling Miracle: How the Netherlands Banned Cars in the 1970s**
   - *Question:* How did grassroots protests over child traffic deaths and the 1973 oil crisis turn a car-centric country into a cycling paradise?
   - *Key Focus:* 'Stop de Kindermoord' civic protests, continuous separated cycle tracks, protected intersections, roundabouts with cyclist priority, and multimodal train commuting.
17. **Frame Building: The Alchemy of TIG Welding and Silver Brazing Tubing**
   - *Question:* How do artisan builders mitre paper-thin Columbus and Reynolds steel tubes and braze them into ornate cast lugs with silver flux?
   - *Key Focus:* Heat-affected zones (HAZ), frame alignment jigs, tube butting profiles (double-butted walls), brass vs silver brazing temperatures, and liquid metal capillary flow.
18. **Ultra-Endurance Bikepacking: The Transcontinental Race and Self-Supported Survival**
   - *Question:* How do cyclists race 4,000 kilometers across Europe sleeping 3 hours a night in bank ATM vestibules with zero outside support?
   - *Key Focus:* Mike Hall's legacy, dynamo hub lighting and USB charging, bivvy bag sleep setups, caloric fueling strategies (6,000 kcal/day), and mental hallucination management.
19. **The Death of the Front Derailleur: The Rise of 1x Drivetrains in Gravel and Mountain**
   - *Question:* How did narrow-wide chainring tooth profiles and clutch rear derailleurs allow riders to ditch front shifters forever?
   - *Key Focus:* Alternating tooth thickness preventing chain drops, 10-52T wide-range cassettes, eliminating chain rub, frame tire clearance gains, and gear step gaps.
20. **The Master Bicycle Fleet Architecture: Maintenance, Geometry & Fleet Management**
   - *Question:* What comprehensive mechanical system covers torque wrench specifications, bearing overhauls, cable routing, and fleet longevity?
   - *Key Focus:* Sealed cartridge vs cup-and-cone bearings, bottom bracket standards (threaded vs press-fit), systematic 500-mile maintenance checkouts, and pro shop workflow ergonomics.
