# Moon civilization source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-475-043 - NASA Moon Base 2026 phases

- Primary agency phase plan: NASA, *Moon Base Development*, page last updated October 8, 2026, https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase-phases/ (inspected 2026-10-24). It labels Phase One now-2029 as robotic access, tests and site learning; Phase Two 2029-2032 as infrastructure buildout; Phase Three 2032-beyond as assembly of a permanent outpost. Page images are conceptual and listed missions/vehicles are plans unless later flight records prove completion.
- Primary agency systems page: NASA, *Moon Base Systems*, https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase-systems/ (inspected 2026-10-24). It describes intended transport, power, communications and JAXA pressurized-rover interfaces. Stated performance targets and expected deployments are requirements or plans, not flight results.
- Earlier architecture comparison: NASA, *Lunar Living: NASA's Artemis Base Camp Concept*, October 28, 2020, https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/artemis/2020/10/28/lunar-living-nasas-artemis-base-camp-concept/ (inspected 2026-10-24). It documents an earlier habitat/vehicle concept; the 2026 phase page should be used for the currently published sequence. The comparison does not prove a formal cancellation of any older element.
- Limits: Moon Base is a program plan, not an inhabited base or civilization. Dates, contractors, named assets and schedules can change; verify each mission's current status before a script. A robotic precursor or contract award does not prove a crewed habitat, self-sufficiency, extractable water, safe long stays or governance. No flight/operations instructions.

## Scope and overlap

BANK-475-043 addresses dated settlement architecture and readiness gates. BANK-346-038 addresses scientific extractability of lunar materials, while 345 covers planetary science. BANK-475-035 asks what would demonstrate less Earth dependence. No 'colony' outcome is asserted from the NASA plan.
