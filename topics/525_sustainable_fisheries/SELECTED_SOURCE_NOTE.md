# Selected case: Bering Sea snow-crab 2022–23 closure

**Proposed question:** `BANK-525-043` — Why was the Bering Sea snow-crab fishery closed for the 2022–23 season?

## Primary records inspected

- [Alaska Department of Fish and Game 2022–23 shellfish management report](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/FedAidPDFs/FMR25-08.pdf). The official indexed report states the 2022–23 Bering Sea snow-crab fishery was closed after the 2022 NMFS trawl survey and stock-assessment estimate of mature-male abundance fell below the state harvest threshold. Direct PDF fetch failed in this review, so the underlying table, exact threshold and regulatory text need inspection before numerical narration.
- [NOAA Fisheries, 2022 survey results](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/feature-story/preliminary-survey-results-2022-eastern-bering-sea-crab-survey) describes survey observations provided to stock scientists and managers. A survey estimate is not the same thing as landed catch or the state's final season decision.
- [NOAA Fisheries, snow-crab management FAQ](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/alaska/bycatch/frequent-questions-snow-crab-and-salmon-declines-alaska) describes federal/state roles and distinguishes **overfished** stock size from **overfishing** harvest rate. It also cautions against treating bycatch alone as an established explanation of the observed decline.
- [NOAA 2022 stock-assessment compilation](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2023-06/2022-crabsafe.pdf) records the 2021 overfished designation, sharply reduced 2021–22 catch limit, 2022–23 state closure and later rebuilding-plan process. This compilation should be read at the relevant snow-crab section before a script uses model estimates.

## Bounded premise and gaps

The closure is a dated management response to low assessed abundance under the applicable harvest rule. It does not by itself establish that fishing, bycatch or the marine heatwave alone caused the collapse. NOAA has separately investigated climate-related mortality; causal estimates need their own study review. This proposal does not assert that the fishery remains closed in 2026. No fisher interview, community-loss estimate or post-reopening outcome was inspected.
