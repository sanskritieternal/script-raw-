# Fusion energy source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-441-043 - Wendelstein 7-X 2023 discharge

- Primary experiment institution: Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics, *Wendelstein 7-X reaches milestone*, February 22, 2023, https://www.ipp.mpg.de/5322229/01_23 (inspected 2026-10-24). IPP reports a February 15, 2023, eight-minute plasma discharge with 1.3 GJ energy turnover, after water cooling of wall elements and an upgraded heating system. Energy turnover is the coupled heating power integrated over discharge duration.
- Primary institutional press PDF: https://www.ipp.mpg.de/5322276/PI01_2023-02-22_1GJ_Wendelstein_7-X_EN.pdf (inspected 2026-10-24). It supplies experiment configuration context and should be compared with later peer-reviewed diagnostics before precise physics or reproducibility claims.
- Limit: IPP describes an experimental hydrogen-plasma run. This energy turnover is energy delivered to the plasma, not fusion energy gain, net facility energy, heat for a turbine, or electricity sent to a grid. It is a dated 2023 result, not a current record or plant-readiness claim. No operational plasma or high-voltage instructions.
