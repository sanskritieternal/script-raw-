This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Fusion Energy across four progressive tiers:

- **Tier 4: Fusion Energy Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Fusion Energy.
