## BANK-288-042: British Library MSS Avestan 26

- British Library Archives and Manuscripts Catalogue, [MSS Avestan 26](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003719703), inspected 2026-10-24. The catalog lists Pahlavi, Avestan/Persian-glossed and Pazand components; the Persian colophon in one section is dated 1106 AY (1737 CE), and the record locates origin in India. It says Samuel Guise acquired the codex in India, possibly from the widow of Dastur Darab Kumana, before the East India Company Library acquired his collection in 1812.
- Limits: a 1737 colophon dates this copy/section, not the composition of every text it contains. Guise's immediate source is explicitly possible, not proven. Digital images were unavailable on the catalogue page at inspection, so no folio collation or translation was attempted. The mixed Avestan, Pahlavi, Pazand, Persian and Gujarati annotations should not be flattened into one language or one ritual practice.

## BANK-288-043: Arab-Sasanian coin, Bishapur, 696

- British Museum Collections Online, [coin 1930,0708.4](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/C_1930-0708-4), institution-indexed record inspected 2026-10-24. It catalogs a silver drachm dated 696 CE / AH 77, minted at Bishapur under governor al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, with a fire altar and two attendants on its reverse.
- Context: British Museum [Arab-Sasanian collection term](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/x115535), institution-indexed text inspected 2026-10-24, describes the continuing Sasanian-style designs under Arab and Muslim authorities after the Sasanian empire.
- Limits: direct object-page fetch failed during this check; indexed institutional catalog text was available. A coin design does not establish the issuing governor's religion, the holders' worship, an operating fire temple or uninterrupted Sasanian political rule. Verify legend reading and mint/dating against a specialist numismatic edition before production. This post-Sasanian evidence is distinct from the ancient-religions bank's broad reconstruction question.
