# BANK-034-057 — Finding *Endurance* in 2022

## Decision and question

The archived pitch describes underwater drones finding Shackleton's ship at “10,000 feet.” The bounded question is how the Endurance22 team combined a historical position, an ice-access window, underwater survey tools and wreck identification. `BANK-034-057` is a proposed expedition reconstruction. This note does not certify a script or media.

## Inspected direct and institutional records

- Falklands Maritime Heritage Trust, [“On this day in history – 5th March 2022: Shackleton's Endurance found”](https://fmht.co.uk/news/on-this-day-in-history-5th-march-2022-shackletons-endurance-found/) (2024), inspected 2026-10-24. The organizing trust says the wreck was located on 5 March 2022 at **3,008 m** depth, about four miles from Captain Worsley's last recorded position. It identifies the *S. A. Agulhas II* and Sabertooth underwater vehicles. Its retrospective page contains an apparent typo giving the Elephant Island rescue year as **2016**; that line is excluded from this note's evidence.
- Historic England, [“Historic England Partners with UK Antarctic Heritage Trust on the Future Protection of Shackleton's Endurance”](https://historicengland.org.uk/whats-new/news/shackleton-endurance-protection/) (21 November 2022), inspected 2026-10-24. It independently records the 5 March discovery, **3,008 m** depth and the wreck's protected historic-site designation. It concerns protection after discovery, not a detailed search log.
- The [Endurance22 scientific cruise report](https://endurance22.org/uploads/2022/06/Science_Report_Endurance22_final_version.pdf) was located but not inspected in full here; its size defeated browser extraction. Its survey tracks and site-identification documentation remain required before a minute-by-minute reconstruction.

## Claim boundaries and overlap

- “10,000 feet” is a rounded conversion of roughly 9,869 feet from the reported 3,008 m, not a precise measured foot depth. “Underwater drones” compresses a hybrid autonomous/operated vehicle and its shipboard survey crew into one phrase. Do not imply the machine located and authenticated the wreck without human search planning and review.
- The organizing trust says “about four miles” from Worsley's position. The exact coordinate, uncertainty and search area need the cruise report and historical navigation record. Discovery does not establish every historical event aboard the ship.
- `BANK-034-019` covers Shackleton's original expedition and collective work; `BANK-153-017` covers the polar survival history; `BANK-028-025` covers ROV/AUV technology. This proposed case is the **2022 expedition search and evidentiary identification**.
