## Planning, access and claim limits

Agency announcements establish plans, described infrastructure and selected milestones, but do not independently prove energy savings, affordability, equity, all projected jobs, or residents' lived experience. A pilot using live operations is still a test. Distinguish a facility's opening from full district completion and state each record's date. Current access, tenants and system performance may change.
