# Research note: documented searches

Checked 2026-10-24. These are bounded source-access notes for proposed BANK questions, not complete episode verification or IDEA readiness.

## BANK-185-038 — Titan public search chronology

- U.S. Coast Guard unified command, [June 21, 2023 search update](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/3435166/joint-search-continues-for-missing-submersible-titan/), reports the overdue notification, participating assets and detected underwater sounds being assessed at that date.
- U.S. Coast Guard unified command, [June 22, 2023 debris update](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/3436499/unified-command-locates-debris-field-near-titanic-continues-rov-search-efforts/), reports ROV discovery and ongoing assessment.
- U.S. Coast Guard Marine Board of Investigation, [2025 report release](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4265651/coast-guard-marine-board-of-investigation-releases-report-on-titan-submersible/) and [full report](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/DCO%20Documents/5p/CG-5PC/INV/MBOIs/TITAN_7724663_MBIR_Redacted.pdf), supply later findings and a search-and-rescue section. The 2025 board report was under Commandant review when released.

**Claim limit:** A June 21 sound report was a search lead, not proof people survived or that the sounds came from Titan. The June 22 discovery and 2025 investigation are later knowledge; do not retroactively ascribe them to the earlier search team. BANK-162-046 is the primary design and oversight failure question. Do not sensationalize deaths, show graphic debris or infer family reactions without consent. No deep-sea search instructions.

## BANK-185-039 — Loudoun County Project Lifesaver deployment

- Loudoun County Sheriff's Office, [September 15, 2017 archived announcement](https://sheriff.loudoun.gov/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/4447?arcId=11844), describes acquisition of a drone equipped with a Project Lifesaver radio antenna for its enrolled-person program.
- U.S. National Institute of Justice, [March 2018 account](https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/loudoun-county-using-project-lifesaver-suas), describes the county implementation and combined search sensors.
- Loudoun County Sheriff's Office, [August 2018 program account](https://www.sheriff.loudoun.gov/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/4329?arcId=9360), describes a December 2017 search for a 92-year-old hunter using a drone and thermal equipment. That account does not establish that the hunter was enrolled in Project Lifesaver or located by the wristband signal.

**Claim limit:** Program adoption and a reported search are not a controlled estimate of search-time improvement, universal coverage or a guarantee. Distinguish radio tracking of an enrolled participant from thermal search of any missing person. Do not disclose frequencies, personal health details, home locations, live tactics or names without clear consent. Discuss participant autonomy and family perspectives without assuming all people with dementia or autism have the same needs.
