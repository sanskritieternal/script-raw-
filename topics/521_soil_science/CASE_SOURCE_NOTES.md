# Case-source note: Broadbalk soil-carbon evidence trail

Checked 2026-10-24. Rothamsted Research's [2024 Broadbalk handout](https://www.era.rothamsted.ac.uk/metadata/rbk1/Broadbalk_handout_2024.pdf) describes a long-running wheat experiment and soil organic matter changes in the plough layer, 0–23 cm, under different treatments. The institutional [2018/2019 guide to long-term experiments](https://www.rothamsted.ac.uk/sites/default/files/national-capability/long-term-experiments/Web_LTE%20Guidebook_2019%20Final2.pdf) documents modifications to classical experiments. Rothamsted's [Broadbalk soil chemical-properties dataset catalogue](https://www.era.rothamsted.ac.uk/dataset/rbk1/01-BKSOIL) describes plot data from 1865–2021 and estimated 1843 starting values, with topsoil sampled every 10–20 years before 1987 and about every five years from 1992; subsoil and plots were not equally sampled throughout.

Limits: The catalogue landing page returned a 502 error on direct fetch during this review, although its official indexed metadata was visible; the downloadable workbook was not inspected. The accessible Rothamsted PDF handout and guide support the experiment and depth description, not an independently recalculated carbon trend. The historical treatment history, changing sampling intervals, estimated baseline and 0–23 cm focus prevent a simple annual, whole-profile or universally causal reading. No field intervention or farmer advice follows from this note.

Overlap: `BANK-521-033` is a generic depth-versus-stock question and `BANK-521-042` a general attribution question. This proposal is the named Broadbalk sampling and treatment-history case.
