## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Photography across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Exposure Triangle, Optical Physics & Darkroom Chemistry (Beginner / Scratch)** — Aperture f-stops, shutter speed motion blur, ISO sensor noise, focal length perspective compression, depth of field, silver halide chemical emulsions, and camera obscura origins.
- **Tier 2: Compositional Grammar, Lighting Mastery & Street Documentation (Intermediate)** — Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing within frames, the Golden Ratio, three-point studio lighting, inverse-square light falloff, and Henri Cartier-Bresson's 'Decisive Moment'.
- **Tier 3: Medium Format, Color Grading & Conflict Photojournalism (Upper Intermediate)** — Large format tilt-shift view cameras, Ansel Adams' Zone System, RAW sensor dynamic range, chromatic aberration correction, and the ethical hazards of war photojournalism.
- **Tier 4: Computational Photography, Sensor Physics & Master Visual Signature (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Bayer matrix demosaicing, dual-gain ISO architectures, computational smartphone multi-frame HDR stacking, generative neural image synthesis, and curating an enduring visual body of work.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Decisive Moment: Henri Cartier-Bresson and the Art of Street Photography**
   - *Question:* How did Cartier-Bresson use a silent 35mm Leica to capture the split second where form, light, and geometry align perfectly?
   - *Key Focus:* The decisive moment concept, Leica M rangefinder ergonomics, 50mm natural eye perspective, zero cropping philosophy, and Magnum Photos.
2. **The Exposure Triangle: Aperture, Shutter Speed, and ISO Physics**
   - *Question:* How do three interrelated physical parameters balance incoming light photons without blowing out highlights or muddying shadows?
   - *Key Focus:* Aperture f-stop fractions (light entry area), shutter curtain mechanics, sensor gain amplification (ISO), and reciprocal exposure balancing.
3. **Camera Obscura and Daguerreotypes: The Alchemy That Captured Light**
   - *Question:* How did Niépce and Louis Daguerre turn toxic mercury vapors and silver-plated copper sheets into permanent mirrors with memory?
   - *Key Focus:* Pinhole optical projections, bitumen of Judea long exposures, Daguerreotype chemical development, and the 1839 public gift of photography.
4. **The Zone System: Ansel Adams and the Science of Black-and-White Exposure**
   - *Question:* How does dividing scene brightness into 10 distinct tonal zones allow photographers to visualize prints before snapping the shutter?
   - *Key Focus:* Zone 0 (pure black) to Zone X (pure white), spot metering 18% middle gray (Zone V), contrast expansion/contraction in darkroom chemical baths.
5. **Focal Length and Perspective Compression: How Lenses Alter Reality**
   - *Question:* Why does a 24mm wide-angle lens stretch human faces like a funhouse mirror while a 200mm telephoto flattens cityscapes?
   - *Key Focus:* Angle of view, subject-to-background spatial compression, barrel and pincushion optical distortion, and selecting portrait lenses (85mm vs 50mm).
### Tier 2: Practical Mechanics & Channels
6. **The Inverse-Square Law of Light: Studio Lighting Mathematics**
   - *Question:* Why does moving a softbox from 2 feet to 4 feet away cut illumination by 75% rather than 50%?
   - *Key Focus:* Geometric light dispersion (1/d^2), light falloff rates, feathering key lights, dramatic Rembrandt shadows vs flat high-key lighting.
7. **Robert Capa on Omaha Beach: The Blurry Masterpieces of D-Day**
   - *Question:* How did 11 grainy, heat-damaged 35mm film negatives become the most visceral war photographs ever taken?
   - *Key Focus:* 'If your pictures aren't good enough, you aren't close enough', Contax II underwater surf, London darkroom drying cabinet melting controversy.
8. **Color Theory in Photography: How William Eggleston Made Color Respected as Fine Art**
   - *Question:* Why did the art world reject color photography as vulgar commercial trash until Eggleston's 1976 MoMA exhibition?
   - *Key Focus:* Dye-transfer printing saturation, democratic mundane subject matter, Kodachrome slide aesthetics, and color harmony vs contrast.
9. **RAW vs JPEG: Sensor Bit Depth, Dynamic Range, and Highlight Recovery**
   - *Question:* Why is an uncompressed 14-bit RAW file capable of pulling details out of pitch-black shadows that an 8-bit JPEG destroys forever?
   - *Key Focus:* Unprocessed sensor voltage data, linear gamma vs sRGB curve, highlight clipping thresholds, white balance non-destructive flexibility.
10. **The Afghan Girl Portrait: Steve McCurry and the Kodachrome 64 Legend**
   - *Question:* How did a Nikon FM2 and Kodachrome 64 slide film capture Sharbat Gula's piercing green eyes for National Geographic's most famous cover?
   - *Key Focus:* Kodachrome K-14 chemical complexity, narrow latitude of reversal slide film, ethical consent in photojournalism, and finding her 17 years later.
### Tier 3: Strategic & Advanced Architectures
11. **Medium Format vs Full Frame: Why High-End Fashion Shoots Use Hasselblad and Phase One**
   - *Question:* Why do commercial billboard and fine-art photographers spend $40,000 on giant 54x40mm digital back sensors?
   - *Key Focus:* Sensor surface area, shallow depth-of-field transition roll-off, 16-bit color depth, leaf shutter flash sync at 1/1600s, and resolving power.
12. **The Golden Hour vs Blue Hour: Sun Angles and Atmospheric Rayleigh Scattering**
   - *Question:* Why does sunlight skimming through miles of Earth's atmosphere turn harsh noon contrast into soft golden velvet?
   - *Key Focus:* Rayleigh scattering of blue wavelengths, warm directional sidelight, civil twilight blue hour color contrast, and landscape timing apps.
13. **Photo Manipulation Before Photoshop: Darkroom Dodging, Burning, and Stalin's Airbrushing**
   - *Question:* How did Soviet censors physically scrape Nikolai Yezhov and Leon Trotsky off historic photographs using scalpels and ink?
   - *Key Focus:* Chemical dodging/burning with cardboard wands, composite negative sandwiching, retouching paste, and the illusion of photographic objectivity.
14. **Bayer Sensors vs Foveon: The Physics of How Digital Cameras See Color**
   - *Question:* Since silicon photodiodes can only measure light intensity (grayscale), how does a green-red-green-blue grid produce full-color photos?
   - *Key Focus:* Bayer color filter array (2x green for human eye luminance sensitivity), demosaicing interpolation algorithms, moiré patterns, and anti-aliasing optical filters.
15. **Ethics in Photojournalism: Kevin Carter and the Vulture and the Little Girl**
   - *Question:* When does the duty to document human famine and suffering cross into voyeuristic complicity?
   - *Key Focus:* The Bang-Bang Club in South Africa, 1993 Sudan famine, Pulitzer Prize backlash, moral injury, and the tragic psychological toll on war photographers.
### Tier 4: Future Horizons & Masterclasses
16. **Computational Photography: How Smartphones Defeat the Laws of Physics**
   - *Question:* How does an iPhone with a tiny lens smaller than a dime produce images competing with large mirrorless cameras?
   - *Key Focus:* Multi-frame burst buffer, HDR image alignment stacking, semantic scene segmentation, synthetic portrait bokeh blur, and Night Sight long exposures.
17. **Global Shutter vs Rolling Shutter: The End of Jello Effect in Mirrorless Cameras**
   - *Question:* Why does Sony's global shutter read out every pixel simultaneously at 1/80,000s, eliminating rolling shutter distortion forever?
   - *Key Focus:* Rolling shutter skew (bent golf clubs/propellers), simultaneous photodiode capacitor readout, zero flash sync speed limits, and sensor base ISO trade-offs.
18. **AI Image Synthesis and the Death of Evidentiary Photography**
   - *Question:* When Midjourney can generate photo-realistic images indistinguishable from real life, what is the value of a physical camera?
   - *Key Focus:* Diffusion models, Boris Eldagsen refusing the Sony World Photography Award, C2PA cryptographic camera credentials, and the return to analog authenticity.
19. **The Analog Film Renaissance: Why Gen-Z is Buying 35mm Point-and-Shoots**
   - *Question:* Why are digital natives who grew up with smartphones paying $20 a roll for grain, light leaks, and 24-exposure limits?
   - *Key Focus:* Tactile intentionality, delayed gratification, escaping clinical digital sharpness, Kodak Portra 400 skin tone aesthetics, and camera mechanical repair.
20. **The Master Photographic Architecture: Developing an Unmistakable Visual Signature**
   - *Question:* How do masters like Peter Lindbergh, Cindy Sherman, and Sebastiao Salgado create images instantly recognizable within 0.1 seconds?
   - *Key Focus:* Cohesive visual vocabulary, editing curation (kill your darlings), long-term documentary project development, print gallery presentation, and photographic voice.
