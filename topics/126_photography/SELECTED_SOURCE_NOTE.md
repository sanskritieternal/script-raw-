## Sharbat Gula and the 1985 cover

National Geographic, the original publisher, records the portrait's 1984 making in a Pakistani refugee camp, its June 1985 cover use, and the 2002 search that publicly identified the subject as Sharbat Gula in [“A Life Revealed”](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/article/afghan-girl-revealed) (April 2002). The article gives the publisher's reconstruction and includes accounts attributed to Gula and photographer Steve McCurry. National Geographic's [2016 report](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/afghan-girl-sharbat-gula-arrested) says she had not known of the photograph's worldwide circulation and quotes a Society representative that she had not sought notoriety.

**Limits:** These are first-party publisher records, but they are not a substitute for a full, independently sourced account from Gula, contemporaneous consent documentation, the 1985 editorial decision record, or applicable law. Do not convert the archive hook “what National Geographic hid” into a claim of deliberate concealment.
