## BANK-359-040 — SI ampere, 2019

- The [BIPM definition of the ampere](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/ampere) fixes the numerical value of the elementary charge in coulombs (ampere-seconds). [NIST's redefinition chronology](https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/ampere-introduction) dates the new basis to 20 May 2019, after its approval in 2018. [BIPM practical-realization guidance](https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/mises-en-pratique) distinguishes a definition from methods laboratories can implement.
- The fixed definition does not imply that a current reading is exact, that every measurement counts individual electrons, or that device power or safety rules changed on that date. Read the relevant practical-realization edition and uncertainty budget before describing an instrument.
- This narrows BANK-359-030's current question and the archived electrical-standard pitch. A device's operation remains in BANK-359-032 onward; niche 358 owns magnetic-field models and niche 161 owns applied electronics cases.
