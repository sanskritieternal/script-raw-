## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Banking across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Banking Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Banking.
- **Tier 2: Banking Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Banking.
- **Tier 3: Banking Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Banking.
- **Tier 4: Banking Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Banking.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Foundational Frameworks
1. **The Architecture of Banking: How a Banking Institution Began**
   - *Question:* Which need and rule shaped the founding of one bank?
   - *Key Focus:* Use charter, founders' records, and the original operating context. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
2. **Banking: A Bank That Shaped the Debate**
   - *Question:* What does a widely cited bank actually reveal about banking design?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare its documented practices with claims made about it. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
3. **Banking: The Workers Behind a Bank**
   - *Question:* Whose labor or exclusion is missing from a bank's official history?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace one role or affected group through records and accounts. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
4. **Banking: What a Bank Does With a Deposit**
   - *Question:* What happens after a customer deposits money at a bank?
   - *Key Focus:* Explain a dated jurisdiction's accounts, lending, liquidity, and protections. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
5. **Banking: Liquidity and Solvency**
   - *Question:* How can a bank face a cash shortage while its assets exceed liabilities?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a simplified balance sheet and distinguish timing from asset quality. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
### Tier 2: Methodological Deep Dives
6. **Banking: Why a Bank Failed**
   - *Question:* Which causes of one bank failure remain disputed after investigation?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare official findings with alternative explanations and evidence gaps. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
7. **Banking: When Banking Went Digital**
   - *Question:* Which task changed when one bank introduced a digital channel?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare access, cost, reliability, fraud exposure, and exclusions. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
8. **Banking: The Decision That Changed a Bank**
   - *Question:* Which documented leadership decision altered a bank's risk or service?
   - *Key Focus:* Trace authority, incentives, contemporaneous records, and outcomes. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
9. **Banking: A Banking Rule After Crisis**
   - *Question:* What did one dated banking reform change in practice?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare enacted rule, compliance evidence, and intended result. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
10. **Banking: A Payment From Start to Finish**
   - *Question:* How does a customer payment travel through one banking system?
   - *Key Focus:* Map participants, settlement, delays, fees, and failure points. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
### Tier 3: Advanced Analytical Paradigms
11. **Banking: Growth and Failure of One Bank**
   - *Question:* Which balance-sheet changes preceded one bank's expansion or failure?
   - *Key Focus:* Use comparable dated disclosures and avoid hindsight certainty. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
12. **Banking: Across Two Periods**
   - *Question:* How did access to a common banking service change over time?
   - *Key Focus:* Hold jurisdiction and customer group steady for comparison. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
13. **Banking: A Conditional Banking Scenario**
   - *Question:* Which rule or technology could change banking access or risk?
   - *Key Focus:* State assumptions and current constraints; do not forecast as fact. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
14. **Banking: Testing a Banking Claim**
   - *Question:* Does a popular claim about bank deposits match the relevant rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Check dated legal terms and regulator explanations for one jurisdiction. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
15. **Banking: One Customer, One Banking Process**
   - *Question:* What do records show about a customer's route through a bank process?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a consented or anonymized case and distinguish general rules. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
### Tier 4: Frontier Systems & Interdisciplinary Paradigms
16. **Banking: Reading an Account Agreement**
   - *Question:* Which clause changes an account's cost or access?
   - *Key Focus:* Compare account terms, fee schedule, and effective dates. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
17. **Banking: When a Payment Arrives Late**
   - *Question:* How does settlement timing affect a household's available funds?
   - *Key Focus:* Use an illustrative cash-flow timeline and disclosed bank rules. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
18. **Banking: A Bank's Maturity Mismatch**
   - *Question:* How do short-term liabilities and long-term assets create risk?
   - *Key Focus:* Use a simplified balance sheet with a dated institutional case. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
19. **Banking: The Total Cost of an Account**
   - *Question:* How do recurring account and transaction fees add up?
   - *Key Focus:* Show transparent examples based on a dated fee schedule. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
20. **Banking: What Deposit Protection Covers**
   - *Question:* Which deposits are protected under one jurisdiction's rules?
   - *Key Focus:* Specify institution type, limits, ownership, and exclusions. Evidence base: dated account agreements, bank disclosures, regulator records, legislation, and attributed customer accounts.
