## BANK-293-041 — Silicon Valley Bank resolution

- The [FDIC's 13 March 2023 announcement](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23019.html) describes its bridge bank and depositor transfer following the closure of Silicon Valley Bank. The [12 March joint statement](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23017.html) records the exceptional depositor-protection decision. Read these as dated US agency actions, not a standing guarantee for every uninsured deposit.
- The [Federal Reserve's 2023 supervisory review](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-April-SVB-Federal-Reserve-Supervision.htm) analyzes management, interest-rate and liquidity risks and its own supervisory failures. Separate that retrospective analysis from the FDIC's resolution steps and from what depositors experienced on particular dates.
- The case makes BANK-293-031, -033 and -035 concrete. Wider economic-crisis narratives belong in niche 299. US legal boundaries, balance-sheet chronology, workers and customer accounts remain research tasks.

## BANK-293-042 — FedNow launch

- The [Federal Reserve's 20 July 2023 launch announcement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20230720a.htm) identifies FedNow as an instant-payment infrastructure available through participating financial institutions. Verify service rules and the chosen bank's participation before describing a specific customer transfer.
- An interbank settlement service is a narrower mechanism than an assertion that all payments are instant. Contrast notification, availability to the customer, and final settlement in BANK-293-032. Credit-card authorization and settlement belong to niche 294.
