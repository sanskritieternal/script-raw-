# Autonomous transportation source follow-up - 2026-10-24

## BANK-486-044 - Tempe developmental automated vehicle test

- Primary completed investigation: U.S. National Transportation Safety Board, *Collision Between Vehicle Controlled by Developmental Automated Driving System and Pedestrian*, investigation HWY18MH010 (2018 event; completed 2019 report HAR-19-03), https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/hwy18mh010.aspx (inspected 2026-10-24). NTSB describes a test vehicle with a human operator and a fatal collision with a pedestrian. It identifies the operator's inattention as probable cause and contributing factors including the developer's risk assessment, operator oversight and safety culture, plus state oversight.
- Full report and docket route: https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/HAR1903.pdf and https://data.ntsb.gov/Docket?ProjectID=96894 . The NTSB page links findings and recommendations; a script should check the original report and current recommendation status for exact wording.
- Limits: This was developmental public-road testing in Tempe, Arizona on March 18, 2018, with a human operator; it was not a driverless commercial passenger service. One investigated crash cannot establish comparative crash rates or all autonomous-system safety. Present the deceased pedestrian with dignity; avoid assigning all causation to her conduct or giving operational driving instructions. Recommendations are not proof of implementation.
