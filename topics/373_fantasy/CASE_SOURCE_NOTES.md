# Selected author-archive source note — fantasy

Inspected 2026-10-24 for `BANK-373-048`.

- [The Tolkien Estate, *Maps*](https://www.tolkienestate.com/painting/maps/): reproduces and describes Tolkien's own maps, including an earliest Shire map around 1937, a growing map drafted while he wrote *The Lord of the Rings*, a circa-1948 Rohan/Gondor/Mordor map used to plot Book 5, and a Mordor sketch where relative positions were later altered.
- [The Tolkien Estate, *The Lord of the Rings*](https://www.tolkienestate.com/writing/the-lord-of-the-rings/): describes the difficulty of coordinating scattered character chronologies with redrafted maps and notes multiple Shire map drafts.

The estate pages were accessible and show selected author artifacts with curatorial description. They do not provide a complete manuscript chronology, every route calculation or an independent account of all revisions. The maps are copyrighted; source inspection does not authorize reproducing them in an episode. Statements about an author's private intent beyond documented drafts need separate evidence.
