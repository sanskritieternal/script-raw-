## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Gardening & Plants across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Soil Microbiomes, Photosynthesis & Watering Physics (Scratch / Beginner)** — N-P-K nutrient macronutrients, mycorrhizal fungal networks, capillary action and root rot hypoxia, and USDA plant hardiness zones.
- **Tier 2: Propagation Secrets, Companion Planting & Pruning Biomechanics (Intermediate)** — Auxin rooting hormone cloning, Three Sisters intercropping (corn, beans, squash), apical dominance node pruning, and heirloom seed saving.
- **Tier 3: Hydroponics, Soil Remineralization & Carnivorous Flora (Upper Intermediate)** — Deep Water Culture (DWC) nutrient electrical conductivity (EC), biochar carbon sequestration, Venus flytrap action potential mechanics, and fruit tree grafting.
- **Tier 4: Plant Neurobiology, CRISPR Crop Editing & Hyperaccumulating Phyto-Mining (Advanced / Expert)** — Volatile organic compound (VOC) airborne pest defense signaling, CRISPR drought-tolerant crops, hyperaccumulating nickel phyto-mining plants, and vertical aeroponic skyscraper farms.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

### Tier 1: Soil Microbiomes, Photosynthesis & Watering
1. **The Wood Wide Web: How Trees Secretly Talk and Share Food Through Underground Fungal Networks**
   - *Question:* How do dying mother trees send carbon, nitrogen, and chemical alarm signals to neighboring saplings through an underground fungal internet miles long?
   - *Key Focus:* Dr. Suzanne Simard's carbon-isotope tracking experiments, mycorrhizal fungal hyphae symbiosis (sugar for minerals), fungal hub mother trees, forest cooperative ecology.
2. **Why You Are Killing Your Houseplants: The Physics of Overwatering and Root Rot**
   - *Question:* Why does overwatering not mean giving a plant too much water at once, but watering too frequently and drowning the roots of oxygen?
   - *Key Focus:* Soil pore air-space physics, the perched water table myth of gravel in bottom of pots, root rot anaerobic fungal pathogens (Pythium/Phytophthora), bottom-watering mechanics.
3. **The N-P-K Code: What Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium Actually Do Inside Leaves**
   - *Question:* What do the three numbers on every fertilizer bag mean: why does Nitrogen build green leaves, Phosphorus build flowers and roots, and Potassium build cellular walls?
   - *Key Focus:* Nitrogen in chlorophyll and amino acids, Phosphorus in ATP energy transfer and DNA, Potassium in stomata guard cell osmotic pressure, fertilizer salt burn hazards.
4. **Plant Hardiness Zones: The Temperature Map That Dictates What Lives and What Dies**
   - *Question:* How did the USDA map North America into 10-degree average extreme winter minimum temperature bands, and how is climate change shifting the botanical map north?
   - *Key Focus:* Extreme winter minimum temperature thresholds, permafrost and frost dates, microclimates in urban heat islands and sheltered brick walls, 2023 USDA map revision data.
### Tier 2: Propagation, Companion Planting & Pruning
5. **Companion Planting: The Three Sisters Method That Kept Native Americans Fed for Millennia**
   - *Question:* How does planting corn, pole beans, and squash together in the same mound create a self-supporting, nitrogen-fixing, pest-deterring agricultural miracle?
   - *Key Focus:* Corn providing natural bean trellises, Rhizobium bacteria in bean roots fixing atmospheric nitrogen, broad spiny squash leaves shading soil to retain moisture and deter raccoons.
6. **The Auxin Secret: How to Clone Any Plant from a Single Stem Cutting**
   - *Question:* Why does cutting a stem right below a node trigger undifferentiated stem cells to transform into brand new water-seeking roots?
   - *Key Focus:* Indole-3-butyric acid (IBA) synthetic and willow bark auxin hormone, nodal meristematic tissue activation, callous tissue formation, preventing bacterial rot in water propagation.
7. **Apical Dominance: Why Chopping Off the Top of Your Plant Makes It 3 Times Bushier**
   - *Question:* How does the lead central bud on a branch produce hormones that actively suppress all side branches from growing, and what happens when you pinch it off?
   - *Key Focus:* Auxin transport down the phloem suppressing cytokinin lateral bud growth, 'pinching' basil and tomatoes, pruning branch collar angles preventing tree bark tear, fruit production maximization.
8. **Heirloom Seeds vs F1 Hybrids: Why Seeds Saved from Grocery Tomatoes Never Grow True**
   - *Question:* Why does planting seeds from a supermarket hybrid tomato produce weird, unpredictable franken-fruit, while open-pollinated heirloom seeds stay true for 150 years?
   - *Key Focus:* Mendelian genetics and hybrid vigor (heterosis) in F1 crossings, genetic segregation in F2 generations, historical heirloom varieties (Cherokee Purple, Brandywine), Svalbard seed vault.
### Tier 3: Hydroponics, Biochar & Carnivorous Plants
9. **Deep Water Culture: How to Grow Massive 10-Foot Tomatoes in Bubbling Water with Zero Dirt**
   - *Question:* Why do plants grow 30% faster in aerated water tanks than in the richest garden compost on Earth?
   - *Key Focus:* Direct uninhibited nutrient salt absorption, dissolved oxygen saturation via air stones, Electrical Conductivity (EC) ppm meters, pH 5.8 sweet spot for nutrient availability.
10. **Biochar and Terra Preta: The 2,000-Year-Old Amazonian Charcoal That Supercharges Soil**
   - *Question:* How did pre-Columbian indigenous farmers turn poor Amazonian acid clay into pitch-black, hyper-fertile 'Terra Preta' soil that remains rich 1,000 years later?
   - *Key Focus:* Low-oxygen biomass pyrolysis, microscopic charcoal porous honeycombs housing billions of soil microbes, permanent cation exchange capacity (CEC), locking carbon in soil for millennia.
11. **Venus Flytraps: The Electrical Action Potentials of Carnivorous Plants**
   - *Question:* How does a brainless bog plant count to two before snapping its trap shut in 100 milliseconds, and count to five before releasing digestive acids?
   - *Key Focus:* Dionaea muscipula sensory trigger hair bending, mechanical-to-electrical calcium ion wave signaling, turgor pressure snap-buckling mechanics, nitrogen acquisition in nutrient-poor bogs.
12. **Tree Grafting: How to Grow Apples, Pears, and Peaches on a Single Frankenstein Tree**
   - *Question:* How does aligning the green microscopic vascular cambium layer of two completely different fruit varieties allow them to heal into a single thriving tree?
   - *Key Focus:* Rootstock (controlling dwarf size and disease resistance) vs scion (flavor/fruit variety), cambium cell division and xylem/phloem reconnection, grafting wax sealing, Van Aken's 'Tree of 40 Fruit'.
### Tier 4: Plant Neurobiology & Future Agriculture
13. **Plant Communication: How Attacked Leaves Scream with Airborne Chemicals to Warn Neighbors**
   - *Question:* When a caterpillar chews on a tomato leaf, how does the plant release airborne chemicals that warn downwind plants to produce toxins and summon parasitic wasps?
   - *Key Focus:* Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) such as jasmonic acid and green leaf volatiles, systemic acquired resistance (SAR), predator-recruitment chemical distress calls.
14. **Hyperaccumulators: The Rare Plants That Eat Toxic Heavy Metals and Mine Nickel from Dirt**
   - *Question:* Why does a tree in New Caledonia bleed turquoise-blue latex sap containing 25% nickel, and can we farm plants to mine metals from toxic industrial soil?
   - *Key Focus:* Phytoremediation and phytomining, Pycnandra acuminata, hyperaccumulation adaptation against insect herbivores, cleaning contaminated Superfund sites with sunflowers.
15. **The Stomata Valve: The Microscopic Mouths That Regulate the Planet's Breath**
   - *Question:* How do billions of microscopic doughnut-shaped valves on the underside of leaves inflate and deflate with water pressure to balance carbon dioxide intake against dehydration?
   - *Key Focus:* Guard cell potassium ion osmotic pumping, water transpiration pull pulling moisture 300 feet up redwoods against gravity, stomatal density adapting to global CO2 levels.
16. **CRISPR Crops: The Gene-Edited Plants Built to Survive Severe Global Droughts**
   - *Question:* How did inserting a sunflower gene into wheat allow crops to survive 3 months without rain while yielding 20% more grain in arid soil?
   - *Key Focus:* HB4 drought-tolerant wheat, CRISPR/Cas9 targeted knocking out of water-wasting stomatal density genes, non-transgenic gene editing regulatory approvals.
17. **Vertical Aeroponics: Farming Without Soil, Sun, or 95% of Normal Farm Water**
   - *Question:* How do skyscraper warehouse farms mist bare plant roots hanging in air with nutrient fog every 3 minutes under tuned pink LED light recipes?
   - *Key Focus:* High-pressure aeroponics (droplet size 30-50 microns), customized LED photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD) spectra, zero pesticides, energy costs vs farm logistics.
18. **The Corpse Flower (Amorphophallus titanum): The 10-Year Wait for a 48-Hour Stench of Rotting Meat**
   - *Question:* Why does a 10-foot tropical flower store energy in a 200-pound underground tuber for a decade just to bloom for two days, heat itself to 98°F, and smell like rotting dead flesh?
   - *Key Focus:* Endothermic metabolic heat generation (thermogenesis), dimethyl trisulfide and putrescine chemical bouquet mimicking dead flesh, deceptive pollination of carrion beetles and flesh flies.
19. **Allelochemical Warfare: How Black Walnut Trees Poison Every Plant Around Them**
   - *Question:* Why will tomatoes, potatoes, and hydrangeas instantly wither and die if planted within 50 feet of a Black Walnut tree?
   - *Key Focus:* Allelopathy, Juglone chemical respiration inhibitor secreted through roots and decaying leaves, evolutionary territorial competition among flora.
20. **Bonsai Masters: The 500-Year Discipline of Miniaturizing Living Trees**
   - *Question:* How do generations of Japanese bonsai masters keep 400-year-old pine trees thriving in shallow ceramic pots no larger than a dinner plate?
   - *Key Focus:* Root pruning balance preventing pot-bound suffocation, copper wire directional branch training, deadwood jin and shari preservation, Yamadori wild mountain tree collection.
