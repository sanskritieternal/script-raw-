## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of AI Music across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: AI Music Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in AI Music.
- **Tier 2: AI Music Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of AI Music.
- **Tier 3: AI Music Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in AI Music.
- **Tier 4: AI Music Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for AI Music.
