## BANK-351-040 — FlyWire adult fly brain

- The [2024 primary reconstruction paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y) reports a synapse-resolution wiring diagram from one adult female *Drosophila melanogaster* brain, with 139,255 reconstructed neurons and roughly 50 million chemical synapses under the paper's counting scheme. The [companion annotation paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07686-5) provides cell-type context and should travel with claims about identified neuron classes.
- Inspect segmentation, proofreading, synapse-confidence and specimen methods before quoting exact counts or calling it complete in a way that includes every possible connection type. A static map is not a simultaneous activity recording, a causal demonstration of behavior, an estimate of variation across flies or a human-brain map.
- This narrows BANK-351-036's small-sample recording question while keeping structural reconstruction distinct from live neural recording. Niche 333 owns a participant-specific brain-computer interface, and 350 owns broad model-organism transfer.
