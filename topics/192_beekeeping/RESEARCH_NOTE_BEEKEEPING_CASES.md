# Research note: managed honey bee cases

Checked 2026-10-24. These are bounded source-access notes for proposed BANK questions. They do not populate verified_sources or authorize practical disease treatment or hornet handling.

## BANK-192-037 — colony collapse disorder

- USDA Agricultural Research Service, [Colony Collapse Disorder research page](https://www.ars.usda.gov/oc/br/ccd/index/), accessed 2026-10-24. ARS distinguishes CCD's characteristic adult-bee disappearance with a live queen and remaining brood and food from all managed-colony losses. It says no single scientific cause has been proven and that CCD-attributed losses waned while other colony losses continued.
- USDA Agricultural Research Service, [early CCD progress report (PDF)](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/oc/br/ccd/ccdprogressreport.pdf), prepared in response to the 2008 Farm Bill and describing the winter 2006-2007 losses and subsequent research priorities.

**Claim limit:** The agency's webpage includes older survey snapshots and a changing notion of "today." Do not carry its colony totals or loss percentages into 2026 without a current, same-method survey. CCD is a specific syndrome, not a synonym for every Varroa, pesticide, winter or nutrition loss. No hive diagnosis or chemical treatment instructions.

## BANK-192-038 — Washington northern giant hornet surveillance

- Washington State Department of Agriculture and USDA, [eradication announcement](https://agr.wa.gov/about-wsda/news-and-media-relations/news-releases?article=41658), December 18, 2024. Agencies describe Washington detections starting in 2019, one nest in 2020 and three in 2021, subsequent trapping, and a declaration after three years without confirmed detections. A 2024 Kitsap County image was not confirmed by a specimen.
- Washington State Department of Agriculture, [northern giant hornet FAQ](https://agr.wa.gov/departments/insects-pests-and-weeds/insects/hornets/faq-s/northern-giant-hornet), accessed 2026-10-24, supplies the state's surveillance and declaration context.

**Claim limit:** A Washington/U.S. eradication declaration is geographic and based on surveillance, not a worldwide species extinction or a guarantee against reintroduction. Do not turn potential hive-attack capability into an observed loss count, and do not publish capture or nest-removal instructions. Use the scientific/common name without sensational nicknames. The general insect-bank ecology scope is separate.
