# Case-source note: NASA's 2012 Black Marble image provenance

Checked 2026-10-24. NASA Earth Observatory's [December 6, 2012 Black Marble image page](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/night-lights-2012-the-black-marble-79803/) says the view is a composite from Suomi NPP VIIRS data acquired across nine days in April and thirteen days in October 2012, then mapped over existing Blue Marble imagery. NASA's [night-light flat-map page](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/) distinguishes 2012 and 2016 products and processing versions.

Limits: NASA's caption documents the intended provenance of its published visualization; this is not a single-exposure photograph of all Earth at one time, nor a complete census of human activity or electricity access. Visual brightness alone cannot attribute a specific local change without sensor, cloud, compositing and calibration context. The institutional pages were accessible; raw VIIRS observations and image-processing code were not independently reprocessed. Do not relabel a source-data composite as a fabricated scene.

Overlap: `BANK-530-030` is generic authenticity/context, `BANK-530-032` reverse-image-search limits, and `BANK-530-037` geolocation. This is the named NASA acquisition-and-compositing trail.
