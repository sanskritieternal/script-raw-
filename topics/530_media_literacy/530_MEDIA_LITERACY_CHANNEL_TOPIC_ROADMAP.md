## 1. Channel Progression Strategy

This curriculum organizes the core disciplines of Media Literacy across four progressive tiers:
- **Tier 1: Media Literacy Foundations & Core Mechanisms (Beginner / Scratch)** — Core physical and conceptual fundamentals, terminology, primary mechanisms, and foundational observation methods in Media Literacy.
- **Tier 2: Media Literacy Methodological Deep Dives & Empirical Evidence (Intermediate)** — Detailed instrumentation, measurement metrics, classification systems, and documented case studies of Media Literacy.
- **Tier 3: Media Literacy Advanced Systemic Paradigms & Comparative Dynamics (Upper Intermediate)** — Complex systemic interactions, historical shifts, anomalous patterns, and comparative analyses across different contexts in Media Literacy.
- **Tier 4: Media Literacy Frontier Technologies, Global Impact & Future Horizons (Advanced / Masterclass)** — Emerging technological breakthroughs, policy frameworks, environmental and human impact, and long-term evolutionary projections for Media Literacy.

## 3. Topic Catalog (Scratch to Advanced Curriculum)

1. **The Architecture of Media Literacy: How Media Literacy Developed**
   - *Question:* Which communication problem shaped a media-literacy teaching approach?

Evidence base: Trace dated origin records and subsequent interpretations. Case records: original claims and records, dated redistribution traces, lesson designs and original learning evaluations.
