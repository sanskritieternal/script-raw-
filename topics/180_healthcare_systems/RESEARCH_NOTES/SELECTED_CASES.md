## BANK-180-044 - Cost Plus Drugs formula

- The company's [pricing explanation](https://www.costplusdrugs.com/mission/index.html) states a supplier cost plus 15% markup, a pharmacy fee if applicable, shipping and location-varying taxes. The published formula is the company's description of its offering.
- The FTC's [January 2025 interim PBM report](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-releases-second-interim-staff-report-prescription-drug-middlemen) describes markups in the studied specialty generic transactions under the Big Three PBMs. That scoped finding is not a head-to-head price test against every drug on Cost Plus.
- **Claim limit:** A 90% saving, PBM bypass or individual out-of-pocket benefit needs the same named drug, dosage, date, fulfillment location, shipping, coverage and comparator price. Do not recommend switching a patient's prescription from this bank entry.
- **Overlap:** BANK-180-014 owns PBM structure; this case is a named transparent-pricing claim and how a final patient cost would be compared.

## BANK-180-045 - Change Healthcare incident

- A [6 March 2024 CMS notice to health plans](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/hpmsmemoonchangehealthcarecyberattack03062024.pdf) says the late-February attack on UnitedHealth Group's Change Healthcare subsidiary affected pharmacy, hospital and physician-office operations and some access to prescriptions/care. [CMS's same-day statement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-statement-change-healthcare-cyberattack) specifically discusses provider financial and claims-processing strain.
- **Claim limit:** These are contemporaneous operational notices, not a final attack-forensics report. They do not prove every pharmacy stopped dispensing, a specific patient's denied care, a final loss amount, or direct compromise of bedside devices. Obtain a dated incident report and affected-organization records before assigning technical cause.
- **Overlap:** BANK-180-025 covers record interoperability; this question is a clearinghouse dependency and incident. Hospital-level cyber preparedness belongs in niche 181 if a separate named hospital event is documented.

## BANK-180-046 - England dental access

- [NHS England's 7 February 2024 plan](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/our-plan-to-recover-and-reform-nhs-dentistry/) announces dental-access measures. The [NHS Business Services Authority's 2024/25 statistics](https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/statistical-collections/dental-england/dental-statistics-england-202425) report completed treatment and people seen, with a March 2026 correction to workforce and some patient-location data. [NHS England's evaluation](https://www.england.nhs.uk/primary-care/dentistry/dental-commissioning/dental-recovery-plan-evaluation/) says several impacts cannot be isolated against the counterfactual because the system was changing for other reasons.
- **Claim limit:** Completed treatments, dentist activity, survey-reported attempts and unmet need have different denominators. The plan is an intention, activity figures are measured service, and neither alone establishes a universal collapse, all self-extraction anecdotes, or the plan's causal effect.
- **Overlap:** BANK-180-012 covers the wider NHS funding history; BANK-180-039 addresses people absent from waiting lists. This case is a dated dental-access measurement question.
