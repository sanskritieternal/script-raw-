**Video question:** Why does the United States spend $13,000 per person annually—double any other developed nation—while having lower life expectancy and higher infant mortality?

**Individual coverage scope:** Commonwealth Fund comparative rankings, administrative waste consuming 25% of health expenditures, high prices for identical procedures and drugs (Uwe Reinhardt's 'It's the Prices, Stupid'), fragmented multi-payer insurance, and lack of universal primary care access.
