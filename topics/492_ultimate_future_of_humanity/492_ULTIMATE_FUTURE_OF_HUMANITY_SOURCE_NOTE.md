## BANK-492-044 - UN Declaration on Future Generations

- Primary adopted instrument: United Nations, *Pact for the Future*, General Assembly resolution A/RES/79/1, Annex II, *Declaration on Future Generations*, adopted at the September 2024 Summit of the Future, https://www.un.org/pact-for-the-future/en/annex-ii-declaration-future-generations (indexed text inspected 2026-10-24). The text describes future generations as people not yet existing, calls for long-term policy consideration, intergenerational participation, and use of foresight and evidence. It notes a proposed Special Envoy and plans for later review; it does not establish that all such mechanisms have been implemented.
- Primary official overview: https://www.un.org/en/summit-of-the-future/pact-for-the-future (inspected 2026-10-24); this identifies the adoption and the annex. Official final PDF is linked from https://www.un.org/pact-for-the-future/en/ .

This is a political commitment and governance document, not a prediction, binding universal enforcement scheme, proof of action by every state or a single representative voice for people who do not yet exist. It does not select one ultimate human destiny.
