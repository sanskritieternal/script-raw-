**Rules:** A source title alone is not verification. The source cell needs a working URL, DOI or stable archival identifier plus a page or section locator, and the access date must be exact. Record enough detail to inspect the supporting evidence. If a claim is disputed, write the competing evidence and qualified script wording. Do not pass Phase 1 while a material scripted claim remains unverified. Recheck time sensitive figures before Phase 4 compilation.
