# Character appearance lock

## Purpose and applicability

Control recurring visual identities where characters are depicted. Text-only narration or an episode without depicted characters may justify Not applicable. This record defines visual design, not character biography.

## Design status and reference assets

For each character ID record design version, proposed/approved status, approval evidence when it exists, and reference asset IDs. Specify required front/side/back views, expression sheet, relative-height sheet and reference-image paths. Planned images remain planned in ASSET_REGISTER.md; do not claim that a missing sheet was approved or generated.

## Immutable features

Specify face shape, eyes, hair texture, skin treatment, body proportions, identifying markings and relative scale. Separate evidence-backed appearance from reconstruction or fictional design. Make choices specific to the episode's visual style rather than prescribing realism to every format.

## Variants and timeline

Assign variant IDs for age stages, clothing, accessories, hair changes, injuries and other permitted differences. For each variant record effective story date/event and scene range, allowed changes and persistent features. Scars cannot appear before their story event; damage cannot reset without a stated transition. Link costume definitions and chronology instead of duplicating them.

## Generation description and exclusions

Provide reusable identity wording, reference inputs and format-appropriate visual exclusions. Negative wording must not forbid an expression or action required by the scene. Describe intended consistency; prompts alone do not guarantee it across generators.

## Review and revision

Record design changes, reason, affected scenes/assets and regeneration needs in REVIEW_REVISION_LOG.md. A lock requires actual approval evidence. Link [main](MAIN_CHARACTERS.md)/[supporting profiles](SUPPORTING_CHARACTERS.md), [costumes](PROPS_COSTUMES.md), [timeline](RELATIONSHIPS_TIMELINE.md) and [assets](ASSET_REGISTER.md).

## Detailed identity and model-sheet worksheet

For each depicted character record face/body silhouette; eyes/brows; nose/muzzle; ears; hair/fur texture and arrangement; skin/material treatment; distinguishing features; proportions; posture; costume/prop references; expression range and scale relationship. Separate proposed visual choices from known source descriptions. Specify front/three-quarter/profile/back, neutral/expression/action and shared-scale references as needed.

Map each permitted variant to an event/scene range. State what may change, what must stay stable and one concrete failure example with its correction. Include reusable identity wording alongside actual scene action, never as a substitute for action. Record reference version/path/status and genuine approval evidence only when it exists. A completed proposed design must not be relabelled approved to close a checklist.

## Required editorial depth

Before filling this document, read the shared source `CORE/PIPELINE/03_Phase2_PRODUCTION_BIBLE/PRODUCTION_DETAIL_STANDARD.md`. Write actual episode decisions, individual entries, representative scene use, examples, exceptions and acceptance checks relevant to this file. Do not submit a short summary or a list of instructions as completed production content. Keep genuinely inapplicable fields explicitly justified; do not invent factual detail, assets or approvals to increase length. Structural validation alone does not verify editorial depth.

## File-specific completion evidence

Individual identity features, sheets/scale/expressions, allowed variations, scene-state/variant matrix, reference versions and genuine proposed/approved distinction.

Record actual section/example/reference evidence in the per-file editorial depth review before marking complete. A word count, present heading or generic continuity warning is not proof that the decisions above have been supplied.
