# Visual Style Quick Reference Card

> One-page decision aid for choosing the right visual style for a business documentary. Used in Phase 2 `04_Visual_Style_Guide.md`.

---

## How to Choose in 3 Steps

1. **What is the primary storytelling need?** (See table below.)
2. **What is the target audience?** (General → simpler; Professionals → data-heavy; Students → animated explainers.)
3. **What is the video generation method?** (Veo3 favors shorter, stock-heavy styles; standard animation allows any style.)

---

## Style Matrix

| Style | Best For | Pacing | Color Mood | Data Load | Example Channels |
|-------|----------|--------|------------|-----------|------------------|
| **Stock Footage + Graphics** | General business stories, market news, company updates | Moderate | Blues, grays, whites | Light | CNBC, Bloomberg Quicktake |
| **Data Visualization Heavy** | Economic analysis, crises, market history | Slow–medium | Red/green/blue/gold data coding | Heavy | 3Blue1Brown, FT DataViz |
| **Archive Footage** | Company histories, luxury brands, industry evolution | Slow, contemplative | Sepia, faded, period colors | Light | History Channel, documentary features |
| **Documentary Realism** | Scandals, investigations, people-focused stories | Natural, interview-driven | Realistic, neutral | Medium | Netflix documentaries, Frontline |
| **Animated Explainers** | Business models, financial concepts, marketing psychology | Medium, teaching | Bright, clear | Medium | CrashCourse, CGP Grey |
| **Timeline Visualization** | Company rise/fall, startup journeys, IPOs | Medium | Professional highlights | Medium | ColdFusion, Business Casual |
| **Infographics + Text Overlay** | Statistical analysis, marketing psychology | Slower (reading) | Clean, minimal, corporate | Heavy | Vox, data journalism channels |
| **Documentary + Interview Hybrid** | Billionaire stories, founder journeys, corporate culture | Conversational | Natural, professional | Light | Netflix, YouTube interview channels |
| **Animation + Data Visualization Hybrid** | Financial analysis, market movements | Medium | Data-driven palette | Heavy | FT graphics, Visual Capitalist |
| **Minimalist Aesthetic** | Serious analysis, corporate news, short-form | Fast, clean | White/black/gray + 1 accent | Light | Financial channels, Kurzgesagt-style explainers |

## Domain-to-Style Defaults

| Business Domain | Recommended Style | Why |
|-----------------|-------------------|-----|
| Company Rise & Fall | Timeline Visualization + Archive Footage | Clear chronology, emotional weight |
| Billionaire Stories | Documentary + Interview Hybrid or Archive Footage | Personal story + credibility |
| Startup Stories | Animated Explainers or Timeline Visualization | Innovation energy, growth pacing |
| Failed Businesses | Data Visualization Heavy + Documentary Realism | Explain failure with data |
| Brand Wars | Stock Footage + Graphics or Infographics | Competitive comparison |
| Corporate Scandals | Documentary Realism | Investigative tone, legal gravity |
| Business Mysteries | Documentary Realism + Infographics | Evidence + clarity |
| Famous Product Histories | Archive Footage + Timeline Visualization | Product evolution over time |
| Luxury Brands | Archive Footage + Documentary Realism | Heritage, exclusivity |
| Financial Crises | Data Visualization Heavy | Numbers tell the story |
| Marketing Psychology | Animated Explainers or Infographics | Teach concepts visually |
| Economic History | Data Visualization Heavy + Timeline Visualization | Macro trends + chronology |
| Cryptocurrency/Blockchain | Animated Explainers + Data Visualization | Complex concepts + volatility |
| IPO Stories | Data Visualization Heavy | Financial journey |
| Mergers & Acquisitions | Timeline Visualization + Infographics | Deal chronology + numbers |
| Workplace Culture | Documentary + Interview Hybrid | Human stories |
| Corporate Fraud & Crime | Documentary Realism | Serious, legal-safe tone |

## Color Rules (Universal)

| Color | Meaning | Use For |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Red** | Loss, decline, danger, warning | Falling stock, bankruptcy, failure |
| **Green** | Profit, growth, success | Revenue up, market share gain |
| **Blue** | Neutral, trust, professional | Backgrounds, corporate identity |
| **Gold** | Premium, luxury, wealth | Luxury brands, billionaires, high value |
| **Gray/Black** | Serious, neutral, crisis | Scandals, market crashes, somber moments |
| **Orange** | Urgency, caution | Warning, risk, attention-grabbing |

---

## Typography Rules

- **Title font:** Bold serif (documentary authority) or bold sans-serif (modern business).
- **Body / data labels:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, Helvetica).
- **Date markers:** Large, faded serif numbers for historical context.
- **Minimum readable size:** Test at 100px width for thumbnail and mobile viewing.

## Quick Decision Flow

```
Need to explain a concept? → Animated Explainers or Infographics
Need to show change over time? → Timeline Visualization or Data Visualization Heavy
Need credibility from real people? → Documentary + Interview Hybrid
Need emotional/historical weight? → Archive Footage or Documentary Realism
Need fast, platform-native content? → Minimalist Aesthetic or Stock Footage + Graphics
Need to compare competitors or markets? → Data Visualization Heavy or Infographics
```
