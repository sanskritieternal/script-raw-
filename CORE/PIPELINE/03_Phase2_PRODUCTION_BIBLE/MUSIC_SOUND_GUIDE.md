# Music and sound guide

## Purpose and applicability

Map music/SFX/silence to scene ranges. Keep speech intelligible; choose engineering targets explicitly as project specifications. Record narrator rights and actual media licenses before execution. Do not fabricate evidentiary recordings or clone a real voice without authorization. Silence is a valid production choice; record it rather than inventing a track.
