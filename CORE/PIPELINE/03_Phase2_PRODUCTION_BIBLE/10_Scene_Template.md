## Design references and continuity states

In applicable Phase 3 scenes include **Character IDs:**, **Location ID:**, **Prop / Costume IDs:** and **Continuity State:** fields. Reference authoritative profiles and the variant active at that point in story time. State intended **Start State:** and **End State:** for generated clips; no silent resets of age, damage, clothing, possession or geography. These supporting references do not replace protected Characters/Location fields, narration, claims, citations or timing.

Use **Reference Inputs:** for actual reference asset IDs/paths or explicitly planned inputs, and **Asset IDs:** for scene assets. Keep precise text/graphics in **Editorial Overlay Instructions:**. Phase 3 owns the scene index/batch plan; production decisions link to it rather than maintaining a second scene order.
