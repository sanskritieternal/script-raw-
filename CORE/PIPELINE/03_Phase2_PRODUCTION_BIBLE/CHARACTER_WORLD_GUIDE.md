# Character and world guide

## Purpose and applicability

Use this as the episode's cast/world overview and navigation record. For nonfiction describe narrator, sourced entities, periods and depiction limits. For fiction describe cast, setting and world rules. Mark reconstruction and invention explicitly. Individual records may be Not applicable with an episode-specific manifest reason; do not invent a cast for a technical explainer.

## Roles and entities

Summarize the relevant entities and their narrative importance. Link authoritative profiles in [MAIN_CHARACTERS.md](MAIN_CHARACTERS.md) and [SUPPORTING_CHARACTERS.md](SUPPORTING_CHARACTERS.md). Assign each individual one ID and one profile, including aliases. Refer to the [appearance lock](CHARACTER_APPEARANCE_LOCK.md) for visual identity and variants.

## Locations and periods

Summarize world rules, geographical scope and relevant periods. Link [locations](LOCATIONS_ENVIRONMENTS.md), [props/costumes](PROPS_COSTUMES.md) and [relationships/timeline](RELATIONSHIPS_TIMELINE.md). This overview must not duplicate detailed profiles or create a competing chronology.

## Continuity boundaries

Record persistent rules, approved variation and evidence/fiction boundaries. Preserve protected scene fields after approval. Record changes and affected scenes in [the revision log](REVIEW_REVISION_LOG.md); downstream text packages must be rebuilt when inputs change.

## Depiction and permissions

Record what the episode depicts and how sourced reality differs from reconstruction or fiction. No fabricated documentary testimony. Link evidence and actual permissions; pending rights and reference generation must remain pending.

## Production navigation

List actual episode-relative paths for CONFIG, STATUS/PROGRESS (whichever exists), Phase 1 evidence, story beats, the authoritative Phase 3 scene file or batch list, and the compiled package when available. Identify the next unfinished decision and current phase by referencing existing status records. Do not create another independently maintained project-state file. Scene ordering, batch plans and scene index belong in Phase 3.

## Required editorial depth

Before filling this document, read the shared source `CORE/PIPELINE/03_Phase2_PRODUCTION_BIBLE/PRODUCTION_DETAIL_STANDARD.md`. Write actual episode decisions, individual entries, representative scene use, examples, exceptions and acceptance checks relevant to this file. Do not submit a short summary or a list of instructions as completed production content. Keep genuinely inapplicable fields explicitly justified; do not invent factual detail, assets or approvals to increase length. Structural validation alone does not verify editorial depth.

## File-specific completion evidence

Concise scope/world rules/entity overview, authoritative navigation/ownership, actual scene/status references and boundaries; do not duplicate detailed dossiers.

Record actual section/example/reference evidence in the per-file editorial depth review before marking complete. A word count, present heading or generic continuity warning is not proof that the decisions above have been supplied.
