# Editorial depth review — reusable worksheet

Copy this source into an episode's `06_Phase5_FINAL_AUDIT/EDITORIAL_DEPTH_REVIEW.md` when reviewing editorial readiness. It can be prepared during Phase 2 and revisited during the final audit; it is supporting review evidence, not a 33rd required production-manifest document. Do not treat this source worksheet as a completed episode review.

## Review rules

For every applicable file record concrete section/entry/example/scene evidence, not merely the filename. Verdicts: Needs work, Ready as text, Not applicable with episode-specific reason, or Not reviewed. Pending external execution/approval must be described separately from missing editorial decisions. Link the manifest's applicability and the actual document. Preserve previous reviews when revising findings.

Check shared IDs, source/interpretation/design labels, actual versus planned assets, scene timing/state and references across documents. Confirm each required prompt is actually written and each individual profile/object/location is usable. A short overview/justified inapplicability may be appropriate; a detailed dossier cannot be replaced by warnings or boilerplate. This is editorial judgement, not an automated prose-quality certificate.

## Cross-file review

- Identity/version/profile ownership and active versus mention-only cast agree.
- Location anchors, camera axis, costume/prop appearance and before/after states agree with actual scenes.
- Script/narration, chapters/planner/index and claim/source boundaries agree.
- Full prompts do not smuggle in unplanned figures, objects, text or events.
- Source facts, artistic proposals, pending selections and real execution/approval records are clearly distinct.
- Changed inputs have been revalidated/recompiled; actual reviewer evidence is not invented.

Record findings and affected files, the correction made, and what was actually rechecked. Structural validation and current package hashes supplement this review; they do not replace it.
